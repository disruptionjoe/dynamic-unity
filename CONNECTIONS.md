# Connections — open, sovereign, self-checking

This repo **connects to everything and is bounded by nothing** except its own
self-verification duty. It draws on and contributes to the sibling repos as *sources and
sinks of learnings*, not as walls.

**The one rule that makes the open boundary safe:** *ingest freely, verify everything.* The
repo is fully responsible for checking every learning — its own or borrowed — against what it
knows, at honest grade. That responsibility, not a boundary, is what keeps it disciplined.

## On verification — sovereign, not dependent

The repo verifies with its **own full adversarial apparatus** — hostile-verify, planted
controls, the rigged-ledger control, honest grading — never a naive self-audit, and **never a
dependency on another repo to clear its work.** It can research *anything*, including the
cross-repo identity, reconstruction, and remainder questions the sibling division leaves open;
it is not bounded by
any one approach and it does **not** send packets elsewhere for permission. The concern that an
interested self-audit cannot be trusted on a merger it would want to cohere is real, and is
answered by the **rigor** of the verification, not by deference: a claim about the identity
must clear the *same hostile-verification bar* as any other claim, and until it does it is held
at honest grade — a hypothesis, not load-bearing. Verification is a standard the repo enforces
**on itself**, not clearance it must request. (There are always other ways to verify than
handing the question to the repo that owns it.)
