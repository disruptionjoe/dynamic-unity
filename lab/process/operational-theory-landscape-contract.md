# Operational theory-landscape contract

## Purpose

Locate an operational behavior among declared rival theory classes without
confusing:

- existence with non-exclusion;
- exclusion with metaphysical interpretation;
- a theorem under frozen assumptions with unconditional nonexistence;
- a representation change with a physical difference; or
- a permissive completion with an explanation.

This instrument complements the
[conditional and abductive research contract](conditional-and-abductive-research-contract.md).
It is not a standing verification gate and does not privilege derivation over
construction, abduction, or experiment.

The motivating landscape view is standard generalized-probabilistic-theory
terrain. Barrett gives an operational framework containing classical,
quantum, local, and arbitrary no-signalling theories and explicitly asks what
additional axioms select quantum theory
([source](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0508211)). Spekkens' toy theory shows
that many effects informally called “quantum” occur in a local
knowledge-restricted model, while Bell and Kochen--Specker phenomena do not
([source](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0401052)). Popescu and Rohrlich's
no-signalling super-quantum foil shows that no-signalling and Bell violation do
not select quantum theory
([source](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02058098)).

These are inputs and calibration terrain, not Dynamic Unity results.

## Common-interface rule

A nested landscape exists only after freezing one common operational
interface:

1. systems or parties;
2. inputs, controls, or interventions;
3. outputs and complete conditional tables;
4. cross-context occurrence identities;
5. causal separation and setting-independence assumptions;
6. access and provenance registers;
7. resource and adversary boundaries; and
8. the transformations treated as representation gauge.

Changing any of these can change the theory class rather than move one
behavior inside a fixed landscape. Every inclusion, no-go, or separation
receipt is therefore relative to the frozen interface.

## Identity comes before membership

The compiler must not infer which occurrences in different contexts are “the
same” from the obstruction produced after identifying them.

Use this typed ladder:

```text
same label
    != same outcome effect
    != same selective instrument
    != same multi-time continuation
    != same provenance/value
    != same declared upper action.
```

The level required depends on the claim. Equality of POVM effects can be
enough for a single-time outcome-statistics comparison while remaining
insufficient for a history, capability, or recursive-finality claim.

An identity receipt must be fixed before landscape classification and state:

- the level of identity being certified;
- the operational comparisons supporting it;
- the representation quotient;
- a context/permutation/disturbance rival;
- a held-out sequential or causal-break discriminator; and
- the class relative to which the receipt is complete.

## Required receipts

Keep the following separate even when one fixture supplies several:

| Receipt | Positive result | Negative or incomplete result |
|---|---|---|
| Conditional validity | complete, nonnegative, normalized behavior | invalid or incomplete table |
| Causal admissibility | declared no-signalling or causal constraints hold | signalling or contract violation |
| Restrictive-class membership | explicit realization or exact primal certificate | exact dual/no-go certificate |
| Target-class membership | explicit construction, complete theorem, or converged certificate with its assumptions | unresolved; not merely “not excluded” |
| Target-class exclusion | a bound or no-go applies under the frozen interface and is violated | not excluded; never automatic membership |
| Permissive-class membership | explicit admissible realization | incomplete or outside the declared landscape |
| Selection principle | independently motivated condition excludes both foils while retaining the target | principle inserted, circular, or still open |
| Held-out discriminator | finite intervention separates adjacent classes | observational equivalence or incomplete intervention class |

## Force, permit, and unresolved vocabulary

Use these dispositions:

- `CONSTRUCTIVELY_REALIZED`: an explicit object in the class reproduces the
  complete behavior.
- `DERIVED_MEMBERSHIP`: a complete theorem or certificate establishes
  membership under stated assumptions.
- `EXCLUDED_UNDER_DECLARED_ASSUMPTIONS`: a substituted no-go or separating
  witness excludes the class.
- `PERMITTED_NOT_PROVED`: tested necessary conditions hold but no positive
  membership certificate exists.
- `UNDERIDENTIFIED`: supplied observations or interventions do not decide
  between declared rivals.
- `OUTSIDE_FROZEN_LANDSCAPE`: validity, causal, identity, or interface
  assumptions fail.

“We have not found a model” is none of these until a search class and
exhaustive certificate are supplied.

## Two-sided foil requirement

Every central candidate names:

1. a **restrictive foil** that is scientifically plausible but cannot express
   the target behavior if the claimed residue is real; and
2. a **permissive foil** that can express the behavior too easily.

The target advance is not merely escaping the restrictive foil. It is an
independent principle that also rules out or prices the permissive foil.

For the frozen CHSH calibration:

```text
local behaviors
    subset ordinary quantum behaviors
    subset no-signalling behaviors
    subset valid conditional behaviors.
```

The CHSH local bound is `2`; the ordinary quantum bound is
`2 sqrt(2)`; the algebraic/no-signalling maximum is `4`. The original CHSH
test is [here](https://journals.aps.org/prl/pdf/10.1103/PhysRevLett.23.880);
Tsirelson's original result is
[here](https://www.ma.huji.ac.il/~ohadfeld/Tsirelson/download/qbell80.html).

That nesting must not be copied to a different operational scenario without
rederiving its classes and maps.

## Positive and negative certificate asymmetry

For a finite local polytope:

- a primal hidden joint distribution certifies membership; and
- a substituted Bell/Farkas functional certifies exclusion.

The general quantum set is not a finite polytope. Therefore:

- an explicit state, instrument, and Born table is a positive quantum
  membership certificate;
- violation of a valid quantum bound is a negative certificate;
- satisfying one bound is normally only non-exclusion; and
- membership in a finite outer relaxation is not quantum membership.

The Navascués--Pironio--Acín hierarchy supplies convergent outer conditions and
can sometimes reconstruct a realization, but finite-level survival alone is
not a universal positive certificate
([source](https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.4290)).

## Constructive causal exit

When a classical common-cause class is excluded, retain at least three
distinct possibilities:

1. change the causal influence structure;
2. change the state/realism structure while retaining a generalized
   common-cause explanation; or
3. leave the operational result underinterpreted.

Quantum causal models provide a concrete version of the second route by
generalizing conditional independence and common-cause factorization rather
than simply inserting superluminal signals
([source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.09487)).

A Dynamic Unity causal reconstruction must still:

- reproduce the ordinary quantum and classical limits;
- state which causal assumptions changed;
- exclude PR-like no-signalling behaviors for an independent reason;
- preserve covariance/no-signalling where claimed; and
- add a new operational constraint or prediction before being counted as a
  physical theory.

A 2026 result derives the CHSH quantum bound from a particular causally local,
indivisible stochastic-process framework
([source](https://journals.aps.org/pra/accepted/10.1103/xfpf-734d)).
Any DU proposal to derive a “quantum-strength” bound from causal locality must
collide directly with that and adjacent reconstruction programs.

## Landscape card

Every active application records:

```yaml
card_id:
operational_object:

restrictive_foil:
target_class:
permissive_foil:
membership_receipt:
exclusion_receipt:
selection_principle:

```

The card is complete when these fields are populated. Completeness means the
comparison is legible, not that the target is true, important, novel, or ready
to bank.

## Dynamic Unity application order

### 1. `HC-DU-035C` / `H-CCR-16` — overlap identity

- Restrictive foil: one context-independent completed biography.
- Current target: context-indexed quantum or provenance-bearing process with
  independently warranted overlaps.
- Permissive foil: split every occurrence by context.
- Open selection principle: certify exactly the cross-context identities
  required for multi-time and upper-action claims without importing a
  classical global assignment.

This is the immediate research use of the contract.

### 2. `H-CCR-15` — physical remainder after completion

- Restrictive foil: fixed classical oracle or too-small retained-memory model.
- Current target: implementation-complete ordinary quantum process.
- Permissive foil: arbitrary signalling/nonlinear or after-the-fact
  completion.
- Open discriminator: a post-causal-break residual surviving reset of every
  admitted quantum and classical memory while preserving no-signalling.

### 3. `HC-DU-035B` / `H-CCR-03,04,07` — public finality

A possible long-horizon question is whether an independently selected
finality quantity has:

- a classical/local limit;
- an ordinary quantum achievable region;
- a more permissive no-signalling or GPT foil; and
- a resource-sensitive upper bound.

Calling this a “finality Tsirelson bound” is analogy only until the operational
quantity, theory classes, resource ledger, and collision audit exist.

## Executable calibration

`tests/du_operational_theory_landscape_probe.py` supplies:

- deterministic-local and isotropic local-boundary controls;
- an exact rational Bell-state realization with `CHSH=14/5`;
- an exact algebraic Tsirelson-saturating realization;
- an isotropic post-quantum behavior and a PR box;
- signalling and invalid controls;
- a withheld-realization control proving that non-exclusion is not positive
  membership evidence; and
- unchanged landscape cards for authenticated provenance and complete
  Mermin--Peres instruments.

`tests/du_certified_overlap_identity_probe.py` supplies:

- identical-instrument and implementation-gauge positive controls;
- a context-dependent post-record flip with identical outcome effects but a
  held-out continuation difference;
- consistent, equivocated, and cross-epoch authenticated occurrences; and
- the typed identity ladder required before marginal descent.

Neither probe establishes new Bell/GPT/contextuality mathematics, a physical
identity selector, a generalized causal theory, a public-finality bound, or
an ontology.
