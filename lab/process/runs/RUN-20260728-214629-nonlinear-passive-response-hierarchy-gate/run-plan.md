# Nonlinear passive-response hierarchy gate

## Cold-start contract

Dynamic Unity's purpose is to make physical reality intelligible as one
coherent, evidence-accountable whole. Its North Star is to determine whether
independently selected observer-indexed certified causal records reconstruct
all observer-accessible time, geometry, fields, and capability, or expose a
finite physical remainder.

The repository begins quiescent at revision 50. `HC-DU-094` imports a serious
positive: a sufficiently rich nonlinear source-to-solution operator can
reconstruct hidden Lorentzian conformal geometry. `HC-DU-095--098` state the
compactness, finite-packet, and stochastic observability requirements.
`HC-DU-099` shows that equilibrium fluctuation-dissipation structure can
replace an externally designed source at linear order after independent
thermal and force calibration, but does not select the state or interface.

This swing tests the exact handoff:

> Can a passive equilibrium fluctuation hierarchy recover enough of the
> nonlinear response operator used by Lorentzian inverse geometry, and can a
> finite part of that hierarchy ever be a sufficient certified record?

The evidence boundary is local exact proof, exact counterexample, and primary
literature. No hardware, simulation, metaphor, or fitted numerical example
may decide the gate.

Keep independent:

- **scope:** one stationary physical arena, one observer worldtube, a passive
  multi-time record hierarchy, a counterfactual source-response map, and a
  held-out causal or conformal target;
- **status:** theoretical equilibrium correlation functions, physically
  measured sequential records, and inferred response operators are not the
  same object; and
- **formation:** ordinary disclosure under a frozen dynamics, not source
  issuance.

## Cheapest positives and kills

Cheapest exact kill:

- for any finite \(N\), compare
  \(L_0(s)=s\) and \(L_c(s)=s+c\,s^{N+1}\). They have the same derivatives
  through order \(N\) at zero but different held-out response at every
  nonzero \(s\). A finite passive hierarchy that recovers only those
  derivatives cannot identify the full response.

Cheapest constructive positive:

- if \(L(s)=\sum_{n\geq0}a_ns^n\) has a physically warranted common bound
  \(\|a_n\|\leq M R^{-n}\), then for \(|s|\leq r<R\) the order-\(N\) remainder
  is bounded by
  \[
  M\frac{(r/R)^{N+1}}{1-r/R}.
  \]
  Compose this with a target-separation margin to obtain a finite response
  order sufficient at fixed resolution.

Cheapest typing kill:

- exhibit two measurement instruments that estimate different sequential
  quantum correlation objects for the same algebraic state, or show that the
  imported theorem assumes a theoretical correlation distribution rather
  than a formed, retained, complete-attempt observer record.
