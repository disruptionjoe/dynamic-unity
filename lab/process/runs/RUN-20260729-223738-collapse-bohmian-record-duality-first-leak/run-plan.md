# PRRF-06 — collapse/Bohmian record duality and first leak

## Cold-start contract

Dynamic Unity's Purpose is to make physical reality intelligible as one
coherent, evidence-accountable whole. Its North Star asks whether
independently selected, observer-indexed certified causal records reconstruct
all observer-accessible time, geometry, fields, and capability, or leave a
finite physical remainder.

The repository is quiescent at revision 94. `HC-DU-142` proved that record
formation, one actual outcome, and retained accessible provenance are separate
physical joints, and proposed an unchanged-apparatus comparison across
standard unitary, Bohmian, and objective-collapse descriptions as an exact
reopener. Tilloy and Wiseman supply a stronger published comparison:
non-Markovian collapse trajectories and a tailored deterministic Bohmian
system-plus-bath construction coincide pathwise at the conditioned system
wavefunction and a natural beable.

The compared system, bath, conditioned wavefunction, beable/noise path,
downstream record instrument, archive, access, and task are ontic or

epistemic scientific representations. “Stochastic issuance” and

neither label is promoted to global ontology by record equivalence.

## Exact question

Given the Tilloy--Wiseman pathwise map between:

1. a non-Markovian stochastic collapse model; and
2. a deterministic Bohmian system-plus-bath completion,

does every passive formed record, archive, and downstream target that factors
through the matched conditioned wavefunction and beable/noise history remain
identical? For active source interventions, does the correspondence extend to
one intervention-natural family rather than a separately refitted map? What is
the smallest admissible structure through which the two accounts first differ?

## Candidate theorem

Let \(Z_C\) be a collapse-model process and \(Z_B\) its mapped Bohmian process.
Suppose a measurable path isomorphism \(F\) satisfies

\[
Z_C = F(Z_B)
\quad\text{in law,}
\]

including the conditioned wavefunction and declared local-beable path. For
every unchanged passive downstream record/archive channel \(K\),

\[
R_C=K(Z_C),\qquad R_B=K(F(Z_B))
\]

have the same law. Every target or passive capability that factors through
\(R\) is therefore equal across the two representations. An active
intervention claim additionally requires a single correspondence natural
across the frozen intervention family; the source theorem does not supply that
for free. A discriminator must therefore use a failure of intervention
naturality or structure not preserved by \(F\), such as direct bath access,
the global joint wavefunction, different symmetry/covariance commitments, a
singular Markovian limit, or a different resource contract.
