# Material gauge-boundary formation mechanism

## Objective

Carry the `HC-DU-040C` law-only/record-assisted/first-leak trichotomy through
one target-independent finite gauge Hamiltonian and a neutral material
detector. Determine whether the physical constraints select:

1. no record coupling;
2. one effective formation orbit;
3. one oriented operational instrument; or
4. one unique microscopic detector Hamiltonian.

The run must not count a chosen regional center, pointer labeling, decoder,
or Hamiltonian logarithm as physical selection.

## Frozen arena

Use the smallest closed `Z3` lattice-gauge region carrying the prior
three-valued boundary flux:

- two hard-core matter sites with occupations \(n_1,n_2\in\{0,1\}\);
- one oriented internal `Z3` link and one oriented boundary `Z3` link;
- two exact Gauss constraints whose product gives
  \(F=n_1+n_2\in\{0,1,2\}\);
- a gauge-invariant interior hopping Hamiltonian that preserves \(F\);
- one neutral three-state material pointer and one neutral archive; and
- a boundary-local coupling only.

The region, inside/outside partition, material surface, ready state, pointer
carrier, and finite action horizon are explicit premises.

## Formation criteria

The candidate coupling must satisfy, before its result is inspected:

1. exact commutation with both local Gauss generators;
2. locality on the boundary link plus pointer;
3. exact nondemolition of the boundary-flux projectors;
4. perfect distinguishability of all three flux values from one blank
   minimal pointer;
5. no extra source evolution on the blank write subspace;
6. additive/group-covariant record semantics under composition;
7. explicit pointer-to-archive transduction and blank-memory cost;
8. a material orientation/calibration receipt;
9. a declared perturbation norm and finite horizon; and
10. one interior-sensitive and one charge-crossing first-leak witness.

## Predeclared outcomes

- `NO_FORMATION_COUPLING`: no local gauge-invariant exact QND write satisfies
  the criteria.
- `FORMATION_ORBIT_ONLY`: the criteria determine an operational orbit but
  leave orientation or decoder freedom.
- `ORIENTED_INSTRUMENT_ONLY`: a material reference selects one operational
  write, while phases, off-ready-subspace action, Hamiltonian logarithms, or
  inaccessible dilations remain.
- `UNIQUE_MICROSCOPIC_SELECTOR`: one physical detector Hamiltonian survives
  all declared equivalences.

The strongest positive result is the third outcome. The fourth must not be
claimed from accessible-instrument data alone.
