### Mid-run correction to the constructive expectation

The final sentence above was the pre-registered positive and is **rejected**.
A Weyl displacement is a bounded unitary, but the parameterized displacement
is generated by an unbounded field. For a non-null source direction,

\[
\|\operatorname{Ad}W(\lambda f)^*-\operatorname{id}\|=2
\qquad(\lambda\neq0).
\]

The automorphism family is therefore not norm-continuous and does not inherit
`HC-DU-101`'s bounded-generator tail. The displayed Weyl identity supplies an
exact packet-specific bypass on every fixed Weyl readout, not a uniform
bounded continuum bridge. The published diagonal-Weyl result is also
asymptotic and presupposes the spacetime, net, localization, and instrument.
