# Forecastability if issuance rather than disclosure is real

## Question

Conditionally assuming that reality contains genuine issuance, what can
forecastability mean without turning the issued content back into something
already encoded by a fixed richer source?

The run also audits the supplied proposal that forecasting is the forward twin
of settlement/finality and that Local Friendliness independently selects an
observer-relative ontology.

## Frozen contract

- **Lane:** 1, supported by 2, 4, 5, and 7.
- **Channels:** formal, synthesis, and collision.
- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 conditional necessity/type-separation
  result; no physical evidence for issuance.
- **Strongest absorbers:** fixed ambient type spaces, fixed stochastic
  processes, Bayesian nonparametric schema growth, completed histories,
  Blackwell comparison on fixed decision spaces, and the Local Friendliness
  conjunction.
- **Cheapest kill:** if a fixed antecedently admissible model already
  individuates every later type while preserving the frozen operational
  trace, the apparent extension is disclosure relative to that model.
- **Local-model boundary:** exact factorization and non-injectivity decide the
  gate; do not build an illustrative simulation.
- **Hardware:** unavailable and irrelevant.

## Assumption labels

- `STANDARD`: factorization through a statistic, non-injectivity, conditional
  distributions on fixed type spaces.
- `PROJECT_NATIVE`: formed record, access, settlement, capability, and
  issuance remain distinct.
- `CONDITIONAL_POSIT`: the source can physically add a distinction absent from
  its prior admissible type.
- `IMPORTED`: Temporal Issuance completion/hyperprior absorbers and Time as
  Finality's proposed type-extension surface are hostile comparators only.

## Method

1. Separate fixed-type outcome occurrence, type issuance, and generator/law
   issuance.
2. Define the minimum old-vocabulary forecast with a typed novelty symbol.
3. Prove that novelty mass cannot identify exact new content.
4. Reapply the record-fibre factorization criterion to exact preforecasting.
5. State the fixed-richer-interface and hyperprior disclosure absorbers.
6. Define a non-scalar issuance-compatible forecast profile.
7. Test whether forecastability and settlement are one axis.
8. Scope the first-person/public-composability and Local Friendliness claims.

## Falsifier and stop

The conditional claim is falsified by an exact-content forecast that uses no
prior representation of the new distinction, no richer fixed interface, and
no semantic refit. A fixed ambient representation does not falsify the result;
it assigns the case to disclosure.

Stop after the exact boundary. Do not infer that issuance occurs, that
evolution optimizes forecastability, that public settlement is quantum
measurement, or that Local Friendliness uniquely selects relational ontology.
