# Quantum-gravity cumulant rival-prediction tournament

## Cold-start contract

- **Purpose/North Star:** require a selected material interface and a finite
  no-refit rival discriminator before assigning a response to new physics.
- **Current state:** no scientific program is executable. `HC-DU-191` leaves
  one exact reopener: compare same-mean/different-cumulant sources across the
  full live rival set.
- **Bounded question:** what is the weakest statistic that distinguishes
  expectation-value semiclassical closure, stochastic/profile rivals,
  separable classical channels, direct action, and field mediation?
- **Perspective/status:** model systems and interactions are ontic; acquired
  knowledge is epistemic; endpoint and mediator access are separately typed.
- **Lanes/channels:** Lane 1 with Lanes 2, 3, 6, and 7 support;
  `CH-FORMAL`, `CH-MODEL`, `CH-COLLIDE`, and `CH-EMPIRICAL`.
- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 observable/rival hierarchy. Grade 5 only
  after a physical no-refit packet survives the complete rival class.

Test mean phase, higher cumulants/visibility, complementary source coherence,
matter entanglement, and mediator-facing multi-time response separately.

## Preregistered returns

- **Unique finite statistic:** one target differs for quantized field
  mediation and every frozen rival.
- **Rival hierarchy:** successively stronger statistics exclude narrower
  rival classes, but no endpoint statistic uniquely identifies mediation.
- **Complete absorption:** classical stochastic/direct models reproduce every
  admitted target.
- **Scope exit:** current source and interaction class is too small; preserve
  one exact reopener without claiming a universal no-go.

- Stop before magnitude optimization, apparatus design, hardware search, or

- Exact local calculation is admissible only for characteristic functions,
  cumulant order, a separability witness, and endpoint channel equivalence.
- A profile lottery reproducing the diagonal characteristic kills any claim
  that visibility alone certifies source coherence or quantum mediation.
- Equal endpoint channels kill endpoint-only field-versus-direct attribution.
