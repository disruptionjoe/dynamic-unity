# Oriented material-trace and head-tail gate

## Cold-start contract

- **Purpose:** make physical reality intelligible as one coherent,
  evidence-accountable whole.
- **North Star:** determine what target-blind physical antecedents, if any,
  select material, provenance-bearing, observer-indexed record interfaces;
  then test reconstruction, operational duality, and finite remainder.
- **Evidence boundary:** formed detector matter and acquired signals are
  physical, not constituted by an observer's belief. A local or
  observer-indexed record is not subjective; a statistical source inference
  is not an ontic event identity; and a physical asymmetry is not automatically
  source issuance or public finality.
- **Starting state:** explicit no-ready quiescence after `HC-DU-179`.
  Nuclear-emulsion matter retains a local marked-site field and spatial
  pattern but generally not path edges, temporal orientation, event time, or
  source identity.
- **Bounded role:** test whether a source-pinned directional detector adds a
  physically formed orientation coordinate, and distinguish that coordinate
  from calibration, time sampling, deconvolution, classification, and a known
  source direction. This is not an activated successor or Wave 3.
- **Owning lane/channels:** Lane 3 with Lanes 1, 4, and 7 support;
  `CH-EMPIRICAL`, `CH-FORMAL`, `CH-MODEL`, and `CH-COLLIDE`.

## Pre-registered positive, kill, and repair

- **Material-response positive:** a reflection-asymmetric primary ionization
  law can carry physical information about track orientation before an
  observer infers it.
- **Exact orientation positive:** for an unordered path with scalar profile
  \(q(s)\), reflection asymmetry \(q(s)\ne q(L-s)\) permits a noiseless
  orientation tag in a frozen model.
- **Statistical boundary:** under equal priors, the best head-tail error is
  \((1-\mathrm{TV}(\mu_+,\mu_-))/2\). Nonzero total variation gives useful
  orientation information; zero error requires mutually singular laws.
- **Cheapest kill:** the orientation-conditioned record laws overlap, or the
  reported sign is established only from a known source direction,
  calibration, analysis threshold, or truth-labelled simulation.
- **Readout boundary:** deterministic diffusion, amplification,
  digitization, and reconstruction cannot increase total variation between
  orientation laws. They may preserve, expose, or lose a physical tag but
  cannot create one absent upstream.
- **Advance gate:** no later causal-response wave activates without an
  invariant, unabsorbed response-order question and finite held-out
  consequence.

## Grade, absorbers, dependencies, and local boundary

- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 for exact factorization and
  data-processing boundaries; conditional Grade 3 for bounded-error
  orientation reconstruction; Grade 2 for the primary-source detector audit.
- **Strongest absorbers:** stopping-power/Bragg physics, detector
  directionality, binary hypothesis testing, total variation and
  data-processing, TPC drift/diffusion, detector calibration, deconvolution,
  and source classification.
- **Internal dependencies:** `HC-DU-178` material event formation and
  `HC-DU-179` spatial-trace/lineage boundary.
- **External dependencies:** only the cited primary detector literature.
  No sibling-repo theorem supplies the record.
- **Nonblocking:** Geometric Unity, Time as Finality, Temporal Issuance,
  external hardware, and publication work.
- **Local computation:** one exact finite probability-law and
  data-processing fixture. No detector simulation, provider, or hardware.
