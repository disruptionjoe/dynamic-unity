# Exact question

Can Dynamic Unity turn the `HC-DU-223--225` sequence into one exact theorem
that distinguishes:

1. selection of a source response;
2. physical formation of a material record value; and
3. selection of the record carrier's transition/admissibility rule;

and state necessary and sufficient conditions for those layers to compose
into an exact target-preserving handoff?

# Formal objects

Freeze a finite completion/history space `X`, target `t:X->T`, selected
response `r:X->Q`, a response-conditioned stochastic write/process channel
`W:Q->Delta(Z)`, and, when needed, a response-selected transition kernel
`K_q` on a material carrier.

The composite endpoint experiment is

```text
P(z | x) = W(z | r(x)).
```

For a full process record, replace `z` by the finite path
`(z_0,...,z_n)`. Provenance is a separately declared target on histories, not
something inferred from an endpoint label.

# Exact tests

1. Prove that an exact decoder `d:Z->T` exists iff every two completions whose
   output supports overlap have the same target.
2. Prove the response-to-record composition corollary: if `t` factors through
   `r`, then the stochastic writer preserves exactly the target quotient iff
   target-distinct response classes have disjoint output support.
3. Exhibit a target-sufficient selected response with a constant blank writer:
   response selection does not imply material record formation.
4. Exhibit a stable formed carrier independent of the target: formation does
   not imply target sufficiency.
5. Exhibit two target-indexed transition kernels with distinct transition
   graphs but overlapping endpoint and finite-path support: transition-rule
   selection does not imply value selection or zero-error certification.
6. Exhibit a disjoint-support positive stochastic writer and reconstruct its
   exact decoder.
7. Locate the first two leaks in `HC-DU-223--225`: total charge is lost at the
   charge-parity quotient; charge parity is not zero-error recoverable from a
   finite fluxoid endpoint/path while the path-law supports overlap.
8. Show that two formation histories with the same response-conditioned path
   law remain provenance-indistinguishable.
9. Exhaustively verify the finite binary-target/three-output support theorem,
   rather than relying on selected examples.
10. Collide the result with Blackwell sufficiency, Shannon zero-error
    confusability, quantum instruments, process tensors, decoherent records,
    spectrum broadcasting, and instrument post-processing.

support/confusability criterion plus previously banked DU typing. The theorem
claim is killed if any layer implication holds without its declared
factorization or support hypothesis.

- a selected law or kernel is called a realized material value;
- statistical identifiability is called blank-to-written formation;
