coherent, evidence-accountable whole. Its North Star asks whether independently
selected, observer-indexed certified causal records reconstruct all
observer-accessible time, geometry, fields, and capability, or leave a finite
physical remainder.

The current authority is quiescent at revision 83. `HC-DU-131--132` established
that nuclearity, an unspecified split interpolation, and a Hamiltonian cutoff
can constrain or approximate local phase space without selecting a finite
formed record. The next question is whether **standard** split-inclusion
machinery selects more than those results credited.

## Pre-registered theorem spine

### Proposition 1 — split existence is weaker than standard-split canonicity

The split property alone supplies existence of an intermediate type-I factor.
A standard split inclusion with a distinguished standard vector supplies a
canonical intermediate factor by modular construction. Verify the exact
standardness conditions and formula from primary sources before banking.

### Proposition 2 — canonicity is relative to a typed triple

The selected factor is canonical under equivalences preserving the inclusion
and distinguished vector/state. It does not thereby select the net,
representation, nested regions, collar, or vector/state from laws alone.

### Proposition 3 — type I does not imply a finite record

The canonical intermediate factor is generally type \(I_\infty\), not a
finite matrix algebra. A tensor-product subsystem boundary therefore does not
by itself supply finitely many coordinates, a detector, a record write, or a
certified archive.

### Proposition 4 — the distinguished state selects finite compressions

The faithful normal state on the canonical type-\(I_\infty\) factor has an
infinite-rank trace-class density \(\rho_\Lambda\). Every declared threshold
\(\tau>0\) selects the finite basis-free projection
\(P_\tau=\mathbf1_{[\tau,\infty)}(\rho_\Lambda)\), with rank at most
\(1/\tau\) and exact trace-distance control for the normalized truncation.
This is reference-state-relative, not uniform over all normal states, and the
finite corner is not generally an intermediate factor containing \(M_1\).

### Proposition 5 — composition with phase-space control is conditional

Composing a canonical split factor with the Hamiltonian/nuclearity results of
`HC-DU-131--132` can define a subsystem-relative finite-resolution
reconstruction problem. It still requires supplied scale, tolerance, target,
probe realization, lineage, archive, access, and resource contract.

### Proposition 6 — the existing DU statement needs a typed correction

Replace the broad statement “the split property does not canonically choose
one intermediate type-I factor” with:

```text

a standard split triple can choose one canonically relative to its
distinguished vector/state;
neither fact selects a finite formed record from the physical laws alone.
```
