   \(\mathcal A\) iff all its projectors lie in \(Z(\mathcal A)\).
2. The minimal central projections give the unique finest such sharp record,
   up to relabeling.
3. The corresponding pointer/archive isometry forms that central label
   without disturbing \(\mathcal A\), but its existence does not select the
   coupling, archive, decoder, or action algebra.
4. For an admitted finite event/next-record effect family inside
   \(\mathcal A\), the central record is statistically autonomous iff every
   joint effect is central.
5. The central record is sufficient for every \(\mathcal A\)-effect and
   every state iff \(\mathcal A\) is commutative. A noncommutative block gives
   a same-central-record/different-action-response witness; a factor has no
   nontrivial action-internal nondisturbing sharp record.

If any implication fails, return the smallest exact counterexample and revise
the package rather than weakening the definitions after seeing the result.
