  evidence-accountable whole.
- **North Star:** determine which target-blind physical antecedents select
  material, provenance-bearing, observer-indexed record interfaces, then test
  their reconstruction and smallest finite remainders.

  finality rule, or global actuality.

  scope; indistinguishability is operational, not subjective or merely
  epistemic.

  proper-coalition threshold, local-measurement-plus-parity readout, and

  This bounded formal continuation tests the shared-reference premise hidden

  external-custody QEC branch.
- **Owning lanes/channels:** Lanes 1, 4, and 5; `CH-FORMAL`, `CH-COLLIDE`, and
  one minimal exact `CH-MODEL` regression.

## Frozen specimen

\[
\rho_\eta=\frac12\left(
|0^n\rangle\!\langle0^n|+|1^n\rangle\!\langle1^n|
+\eta|0^n\rangle\!\langle1^n|
+\bar\eta|1^n\rangle\!\langle0^n|
\right)
\]

with its fully dephased completion \(\rho_0\), where
\(|\eta|\le1\). Treat each qubit as one physical regional carrier and give
region \(i\) an equatorial measurement angle \(\theta_i\) defined relative
to its local reference. Freeze:

1. the already-banked proper-coalition indistinguishability result;
2. local equatorial measurements plus later classical aggregation;
3. aligned, independently misaligned, and certified-relative-reference
   contracts;
4. independent reference-angle errors and independent local dephasing; and
5. a fixed two-setting phase-tomography control.

Do not call a coordinate angle physical, the reference selected, the
carriers selected records, the coalition a consensus protocol, or the
alignment loss new dynamics.

   \(\operatorname{Re}[\eta e^{i\sum_i\theta_i}]\) and prove covariance when

## Grade, absorber, kill, and stop

- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 reference/access necessity.
- **Strongest absorber:** quantum reference frames, superselection and
  asymmetry resource theory, phase-reference alignment, GHZ stabilizer
  correlations, local tomography, and standard dephasing.
- **Cheapest kill:** independently twirled local reference frames preserve a
  nonzero product-parity response, or the claimed response fails covariance
  when state and measurement coordinates are transformed together.
- **Local-model disposition:** formal proof first; a small exact regression
  only verifies generated finite cases and the resource/noise boundary.
- **Stop:** no provider or hardware; no consensus, cryptographic, collapse,
  ontology, empirical-excess, or paper claim; no activation of a successor.
