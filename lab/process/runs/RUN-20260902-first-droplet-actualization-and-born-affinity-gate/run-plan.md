# Exact question

Does a metastable first-droplet mechanism close the orbit-member and single-run
selection gaps left by `HC-DU-227`, and does its proposed low-amplitude cutoff
have empirical content beyond an ordinary nonideal quantum detector?

# Cold-start contract

- **Purpose / North Star:** locate target-blind physical antecedents that select
  material, provenance-bearing, observer-indexed record interfaces, then test
  their reconstruction or finite-remainder boundary.
- **Starting state:** revision 177 is quiescent `no_ready`. Joe's direct `Go`
  authorizes one bounded reopener audit; it does not preselect a successor.
- **Evidence consumed:** `HC-DU-142`, `HC-DU-156`, `HC-DU-178`, and
  `HC-DU-227` already separate invariant laws, samplers, one-run actuality,
  material amplification, archives, and provenance.
- **Perspective/status:** droplets, chamber microstates, couplings, and tracks
  are physical. Probability models and inferences about them are epistemic
  unless an ontology says otherwise. Observer-indexed does not mean subjective.
- **Formation:** pre-existing microscopic asymmetry, objective stochastic
  innovation, branch-relative unitary records, and monitored trajectories are
  separate actualization accounts.
- **Lane/channel:** Lane 3, supported by Lanes 1 and 7; `CH-FORMAL`,
  `CH-COLLIDE`, and `CH-EMPIRICAL`.
- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 for the exact attribution and
  convex-affinity boundary; the physical source audit is Grade 2. Grade 5
  requires a no-refit all-attempt residual and is not presumed.
- **Absorbers:** hidden chamber microstates, unitary entanglement/decoherence,
  fixed POVMs, detector inefficiency, finite-horizon event probabilities,
  postselection, and incomplete attempt census.
- **Cheapest kill:** the first direction is inherited from an assumed classical
  droplet configuration, or the cutoff is a calibrated apparatus/selection
  effect rather than a non-affine state response.
- **Dependencies:** all repository dependencies are internal and completed.
  Primary literature is nonblocking evidence. No GU or sibling result is
  imported.
- **Local-model disposition:** formal analysis decides the question. The finite
  executable is a minimal proof-checking regression under `LMLG-01`, not a
  nucleation simulation or a new-learning model.
- **Hardware:** unavailable and unnecessary for the theorem boundary. A future
  experiment is only an awareness path after its preparation, instrument, and
  all-attempt contract is frozen.

# Tests

1. Identify where Schonfeld's proposal places randomness and whether that
   object is derived, supplied, classical, or quantum.
2. Separate a particular microscopic seed from a symmetric probability law
   over seeds and from a physical sampler that realizes one seed.
3. Test the fully quantum, unitary reading against the coherent-carrier versus
   dephased-shadow boundary.
4. Test whether metastable amplification selects a seed or only copies and
   retains it.
5. Test whether a realized track identifies its actualization route.
6. Derive the convex-affinity condition obeyed by every fixed quantum effect.
7. Apply it to the proposed offset cutoff
   `f_c(q)=max(q-c,0)` and identify the smallest exact mixture witness.
8. Separate a density-level nonlinear law from a branchwise,
   preparation-contextual rule and from fixed detector inefficiency.
9. Audit the published cloud-chamber evidence and qubit extrapolation without
   treating either as a confirmed Born-rule violation.
10. Decide whether a prediction-shaped seam, paper, successor, local build, or
    hardware action is earned.
