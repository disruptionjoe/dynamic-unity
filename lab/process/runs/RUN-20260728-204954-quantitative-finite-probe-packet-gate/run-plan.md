# Quantitative finite-probe packet gate

## Purpose and North-Star relation

`HC-DU-095` proves existence of a finite robust target-resolution certificate
on a compact target-separating capability surface. `HC-DU-096` gives one
conditional physical route to that compactness through a regularity gap.

This run tests the remaining constructive question:

> What additional physical and quantitative structure bounds the number of
> probes, their required precision, and the retained record size?

## Frozen typed objects

Let:

- \((\Theta,d)\) be the compact physical completion class already admitted by
  `HC-DU-095/096`;
- \(T:\Theta\to Z\) be the held-out target;
- \(K_\delta\subset\Theta^2\) be the pairs separated by target distance at
  least \(\delta\);
- \(\mathcal M\) be a target-independent family of physically admissible
  scalar probes;
- \(\nu\) be a physically defined sampling/design distribution over probes
  or complete compatible campaigns;
- \(L\) bound probe Lipschitz sensitivity in the compactness topology;
- \(a_\delta\) be a declared response separation;
- \(p_\delta\) be a uniform lower bound on the \(\nu\)-mass of probes achieving
  that separation for every pair in \(K_\delta\);
- \(\eta\) be total retained error;
- \(A\) be the maximum retained alphabet size per probe; and
- \(P_\delta(T(\Theta))\) be the target packing number.

Keep distinct:

1. topological existence of some finite subcover;
2. a computable deterministic probe selector;
3. a random-design success probability;
4. packet coordinate count;
5. repetitions/copies per coordinate;
6. finite-bit archive size;
7. source energy, duration, bandwidth and control cost; and
8. joint physical realizability and complete acquisition.

## Exact questions

1. Which uniform observability or small-ball modulus turns the compactness
   proof into a quantitative random probe bound?
2. What deterministic bound follows from an effective pair-space net and
   separator oracle?
3. Which target entropy and per-probe alphabet bounds are unavoidable?
4. Can packet size and margin be arbitrarily bad despite compactness and
   pointwise target separation?
5. Does a regularity-compact Sobolev class have finite intrinsic dimension?
6. Which low-rank, sparse, manifold or attractor restrictions permit a fixed
   finite stable embedding, and which merely shrink the completion class?
7. Does any active-wave arena supply a physical probe distribution,
   observability modulus, joint campaign, and no-refit resource bound?

## Assumption and warrant discipline

- `STANDARD`: covering/packing numbers, union bounds, Lipschitz continuity,
  Sobolev Fourier entropy, finite box-counting dimension, and finite-alphabet
  capacity.
- `IMPORTED`: generic finite-dimensional embeddings of compact sets with
  finite box dimension, stable manifold embeddings, compressed sensing, and
  finite-dimensional tomography.
- `PROJECT_NATIVE`: physically selected completion and probe classes, formed
  certified records, complete acquisition, no-refit held-out transfer, and
  evidence grades.
- `CONDITIONAL_POSIT`: uniform physically admissible small-ball separation,
  effective covers, repeatable or jointly compatible campaigns, calibrated
  error, and finite source/readout resources.

## Strongest absorbers

- metric entropy and rate-distortion theory;
- finite-dimensional embedding and Mané projection theorems;
- compressed sensing and restricted-isometry theory;
- observability inequalities and optimal experimental design; and
- finite-measurement inverse problems on parametric model classes.

## Cheapest positives and kills

Cheapest constructive positive:

- prove a union-bound packet-size theorem from a finite net of \(K_\delta\),
  uniformly Lipschitz probes, and a uniform small-ball probability
  \(p_\delta>0\).

Cheapest count kill:

- a finite compact \(n\)-point target class with only singleton indicator
  probes requires \(n-1\) coordinates although a rich binary code would need
  only \(\lceil\log_2n\rceil\) bits.

Cheapest margin kill:

- rescale one separating probe by an arbitrary \(\varepsilon>0\); topology
  and packet size remain unchanged while the noise tolerance collapses.

Cheapest fixed-dimension kill:

- show that a compact Sobolev ball in a weaker norm has entropy growing
  faster than every power of \(1/\epsilon\), hence infinite box dimension.

## Stops

- do not call a finite-subcover existence proof an algorithm;
- do not infer precision, copies, energy, duration, or bandwidth from probe
  count;
- do not use a target-dependent probe distribution as a universal physical
  interface;
- do not use independent random probes when the physical campaign is not
  jointly compatible or repeatably preparable;
- do not import finite-dimensional manifold or low-rank bounds into a full
  regularity ball;
- do not hide completion-class narrowing inside a sparsity, rank, attractor,
  or parametric assumption;
- do not build a local sampling model when the exact entropy and union-bound
  arguments decide the gate; and
- do not activate a successor without a physical observability modulus and
  resource-accounted packet.

## Durable outputs

- one refined physical active-wave reopener and successor disposition;
- the minimum authority, concept, orientation, counter-assumptive, and
