# Time-resolved TPC record-packet gate

## Cold-start contract

- **Purpose:** make physical reality intelligible as one coherent,
  evidence-accountable whole.
- **North Star:** determine what target-blind physical antecedents, if any,
  select material, provenance-bearing, observer-indexed record interfaces;
  then test reconstruction, operational duality, and finite remainder.
- **Starting state:** no-ready quiescence after `HC-DU-181`. Detector
  positives remain split among persistent spatial trace, transient physical
  orientation, and separately supplied acquisition.
- **Bounded role:** test one real time-resolved detector architecture for a
  joined acquired packet and locate the first exact leak. Adjacent detector
  results may collide with the result but may not be stitched into one
  imaginary apparatus.
- **Owning lane/channels:** Lane 3 with Lanes 1, 4, and 7 support;
  `CH-EMPIRICAL`, `CH-FORMAL`, `CH-MODEL`, and `CH-COLLIDE`.

## Frozen question

> Does one existing source-pinned detector physically join retained spatial
> provenance, timing, polarity or charge, event association, and accessible
> acquisition strongly enough to close Dynamic Unity's material-record
> packet; and if not, what is the first exact leak?

## Pre-registered theorem, kills, and repairs

- **Clock-depth obstruction:** one carrier records
  \(t_{\rm arr}=t_0+z/v\), not \(t_0\) and \(z\) separately.
- **Two-carrier repair:** two correctly associated species with calibrated
  velocities \(v_1\ne v_2\) give a full-rank two-equation system.
- **Association kill:** a missing or misassociated minority peak returns the
  one-carrier ambiguity.
- **Event-building kill:** one raw hit packet may admit multiple physical
  event partitions. A clustering rule selects an estimate, not necessarily
  occurrence identity.
- **Repairs:** retain a physical event/trigger tag, or independently justify
  a single-event completion class. Keep record refinement and completion
  narrowing distinct.
- **Archive boundary:** lossless storage preserves the acquired packet but
  cannot add an absent carrier, path polarity, event identity, or source
  provenance.

## Grade and absorber contract

- **Maximum:** scoped Grade 4 exact rank and factorization boundary.
- **Conditional:** Grade 3 reconstruction inside a calibrated,
  same-event, common-clock two-carrier class.
- **Source audit:** Grade 2.
- **Absorbers:** TPC transport, negative-ion drift, linear inverse problems,
  triggering, DAQ, event building, clustering, calibration, timewalk,
  diffusion, detector efficiency, and classification.
- **No novelty credit:** for the two-carrier depth formula, detector
  engineering, or event-building ambiguity as such.
