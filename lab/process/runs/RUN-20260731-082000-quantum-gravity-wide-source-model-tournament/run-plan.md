# Quantum-gravity wide-source model tournament

## Cold-start contract

- **Purpose/North Star:** require a target-blind physical antecedent, formed
  interface, and finite no-refit target before treating a response formula as
  a physical remainder.
- **Current state:** no scientific program is executable. `HC-DU-190` parks
  the Chen--Giacomini wide-source branch behind an explicit source-model and
  smeared-stress spectral-realizability reopener.
- **Bounded question:** do any of the smallest one-particle, condensate,
  fragmented-number, solitonic, or free-field source classes supply a broad,
  normalizable, preparable, sufficiently sharp local energy profile whose
  gravitational response is not already the Newton functional of an extended
  classical density?
- **Lanes/channels:** Lane 1 with Lanes 2, 3, 6, and 7 support;
  `CH-FORMAL`, `CH-MODEL`, `CH-COLLIDE`, and `CH-EMPIRICAL`.
- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 source-class trilemma, approximate
  concentration boundary, and corrected finite reopener.

## Frozen tournament

Evaluate without source-specific refitting:

1. a delocalized one-particle/tight-binding state;
2. an $N$-boson Hartree condensate number state;
3. a fragmented local-number Fock state;
4. a translated or delocalized solitonic collective coordinate; and
5. a free scalar coherent/Fock-sector analogue.

For every candidate, distinguish:

- exact joint local-profile sharpness;
- approximate relative concentration;
- quantum coherence across profile sectors;
- phase and visibility under a density--density kernel;
- equivalence to the classical extended-density Newton functional; and
- which controller, localization, or reference structure was supplied.

## Preregistered returns

- **Exact escape:** one normalizable, preparable, nonclassically delocalized
  source has a broad joint-sharp local stress profile and a nonabsorbed
  operator-valued phase.
- **Concentration-only repair:** macroscopic sources approach a mean profile,
  but the same limit is an ordinary mean-field/semiclassical absorber; the
  first honest residual moves from mean phase to stress cumulants.
- **Universal finite no-go:** every admitted model provably fails exact
  broad-profile realizability or becomes Newton-equivalent.
- **Scope exit:** the tournament is too small to settle QFT; preserve a
  continuum source construction reopener without claiming universality.

## Strongest absorbers and stops

- Absorbers: multinomial/Poisson concentration, Gross--Pitaevskii and
  coherent-state limits, stochastic-gravity noise kernels, standard
  density--density interactions, and extended-body Newton gravity.
- Cheapest kill: exact broad profiles occur only in commuting occupation
  eigenstates and their phase is the classical extended-density functional;
  coherent wide states have profile fluctuations.
- Stop before apparatus optimization, magnitude forecasts, hardware search,
  or treating stress variance as a uniquely quantum-gravity prediction.
- A finite source tournament cannot prove a universal QFT no-go.

## Local-model learning gate

Local computation is admissible only for exact occupation statistics,
characteristic functions, visibility, cumulants, and finite scaling laws that
decide the reopener. It is not a gravity simulation.

Disposition: `CUMULANT_REOPENER_AFTER_EXACT_PROFILE_ABSORPTION`.
