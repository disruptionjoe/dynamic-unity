# Risk-indexed operational separation

## Question

Does `HC-DU-069`'s absence of a zero-error finite separator imply that a
physical theory has no finite empirical content, or can two theory classes
have identical finite-transcript support while a finite, preregistered
statistical test separates their probability laws at controlled error?

The swing must state the exact relation among:

```text
support exclusion
finite-confidence statistical exclusion
asymptotic separation
global law-family nonmembership.
```

## Why this is live

`HC-DU-069` correctly classifies exact zero-error escape by operational
closure, but explicitly leaves probability measures outside its theorem.
Dynamic Unity already owns:

- a complete exact CHSH/Tsirelson/PR-box landscape calibration;
- bounded-score convex separation from a frozen null class;
- finite-shot Hoeffding certificates for physical-sufficiency packets; and
- a program charter that permits typed error bounds in physical
  certification.

Those pieces have not been composed. The current physical reopener therefore
risks being too strict if it requires a support-disjoint fibre where a
physically selected probability-law family and positive robust score gap
would already yield a legitimate finite discriminator.

## Scope and controls

- **Owning lane:** Lane 1.
- **Supporting lanes:** Lanes 4 and 7.
- **Channels:** formal proof, empirical discrimination, and literature
  collision.
- **Maximum return:** scoped Grade-4 exact separation ladder and current-

  record ontology, Grade-5 remainder, or prediction can be earned.
- **Strongest absorber:** Neyman--Pearson testing, total variation, convex

  and DU's own `HC-DU-036D`/bounded-score compiler.
- **Cheapest kill:** the distinction is already explicitly composed in

  grade boundary.
- **Local computation:** no new model. Use symbolic proof and the existing
  exact CHSH calibration. Arithmetic checks may verify sample counts but
  cannot raise the grade.
- **Hardware:** unavailable and unnecessary. This run specifies evidentiary
  logic, not a new Bell experiment.
- **Cross-repository posture:** no sibling result is required.

## Frozen specimen

Use the binary-input, binary-output CHSH game already frozen by
`lab/process/operational-theory-landscape-contract.md`:

- settings \(x,y\in\{0,1\}\) are sampled uniformly and independently of the
  devices and past trial history;
- outputs \(a,b\in\{0,1\}\);
- a win is \(a\oplus b=xy\);
- the local ceiling is \(3/4\);
- the ordinary quantum ceiling is
  \[
  p_Q=\cos^2(\pi/8)=\frac{2+\sqrt2}{4};
  \]
- a PR box wins with probability \(1\); and
- every attempted setting and output is retained, with no postselection.

The null may carry arbitrary memory between rounds, provided each
history-conditioned round remains in the declared null class and the setting
independence/causal contract remains fixed.

## Pre-registered targets

### T1 — support-level kill

Prove that the quantum null contains a full-support behavior. Therefore every
nonempty finite CHSH transcript event has positive probability under at least
one quantum model, and no zero-error finite record excludes the full quantum
class.

### T2 — risk-indexed separator

For a finite transcript space \(Z\), null law family \(\mathcal N\),
alternative family \(\mathcal A\), and test
\(\phi:Z\to[0,1]\), type the worst-case errors

\[
\alpha(\phi)=\sup_{P\in\mathcal N}\mathbb E_P[\phi],
\qquad
\beta(\phi)=\sup_{Q\in\mathcal A}\mathbb E_Q[1-\phi].
\]

Show that zero-error support exclusion is the special case \(\alpha=0\), not
the complete notion of finite empirical content.

### T3 — robust bounded-score theorem

If a frozen score \(S_i\in[0,1]\) has conditional null ceiling \(b\) and
conditional alternative floor \(a>b\) on every admitted trial history, derive
a finite sample gate with type-I error at most \(\alpha\) and type-II error at
most \(\beta\):

\[
n\ge
\frac{
 \left(\sqrt{\log(1/\alpha)}+\sqrt{\log(1/\beta)}\right)^2
}{2(a-b)^2}.
\]

The memory-robust proof must use conditional expectations, not an undeclared
IID assumption.

### T4 — CHSH controls

Prove:

1. \(n\) consecutive CHSH wins have quantum-null probability at most
   \(p_Q^n>0\);
2. the ideal PR alternative produces that transcript with probability \(1\);
3. finite confidence is therefore available for every declared
   \(\alpha>0\), while zero-error exclusion is unavailable for every finite
   \(n\); and
4. local-versus-quantum separation obeys the same risk-indexed logic without
   treating Bell nonlocality as DU novelty.

### T5 — capability and reopener correction

Show that passive post-processing cannot increase total-variation or robust
score separation, while a richer intervention can. Then decide whether the
physical reopener must accept either:

- a support-disjoint finite fibre; or
- a physically selected law family with a positive robust score gap,
  implementation-complete acquisition, and preregistered error budget.

## Required stops

Do not:

- call a nonzero-error rejection logical impossibility;
- report \(1-\alpha\) as the probability that the null is false;
- infer quantum-class membership from satisfying one Bell bound;
- assume IID when the conditional null contract is enough;
- ignore setting independence, postselection, acquisition visibility,
  calibration, drift, or the admitted memory class;
- relabel known Bell or statistical mathematics as new physics; or
- activate a physical successor merely because the calibration succeeds.
