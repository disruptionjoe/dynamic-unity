# Subsystem factor selection and quantum-mereology gate

## Question

Can the source law that Dynamic Unity treats as antecedent select the
subsystem or observable algebra that physically carries a record, or does a
Hilbert-space factorization enter as another supplied interface?

## Why this is live

`HC-DU-075` requires a selected physical output carrier and accessible
algebra. That result did not close whether an abstract Hamiltonian, channel,
or Stinespring factor selects the relevant physical subsystem.

Quantum mereology contains both constructive and adversarial results:

- observable algebras can induce tensor-product structures;
- Hamiltonian locality, quasi-classicality, and minimal scrambling are
  proposed selection criteria;
- Hamiltonian symmetries can generate multiple candidate factorizations; and
- a 2025 invariant-theory theorem says a nondegenerate Hamiltonian plus a
  full-support state can be paired with a complete entropy profile that
  uniquely characterizes a factorization.

The run separates classification from physical selection and carries the
result into the quantum-record passport.

## Scope and controls

- **Owning lane:** Lane 3.
- **Supporting lanes:** Lanes 1, 4, and 7.
- **Channels:** formal proof, primary-source collision, exact finite control,
  and synthesis.
- **Maximum return:** scoped Grade-4 necessity/nonselection theorem and
  carrier-passport refinement. All tensor-product, invariant-theory,
  observable-algebra, and quantum-mereology component mathematics remains
  absorbed.
- **Strongest absorbers:** Zanardi observable-induced subsystems and
  operational quantum mereology; Cotler locality-from-spectrum; Carroll--Singh
  quasi-classical factorization; Soulas--Franzmann--Di Biagio invariant
  classification; Stoica's no-go program.
- **Cheapest kill:** a fixed bare source law has a symmetry that moves the
  candidate factorization while preserving every antecedent datum.
- **Mandatory positive control:** a pair of commuting full matrix
  subalgebras with trivial intersection and full generated algebra
  reconstructs a tensor-product structure.
- **Mandatory hostile control:** adding a full-support state breaks the
  particular Hamiltonian symmetry, so the Hamiltonian-only witness may not
  be broadened to every Hamiltonian--state pair.
- **Local computation:** one exact four-dimensional matrix certificate.
- **Hardware:** unavailable, unnecessary, and unauthorized.
- **Cross-repository posture:** Dynamic Unity only.

## Pre-registered targets

1. Prove the stabilizer-orbit limit for any natural source-only structure
   selector.
2. Construct the smallest nondegenerate Hamiltonian whose stabilizer moves a
   two-qubit factorization and changes the entanglement status of one fixed
   state.
3. State the exact algebraic data sufficient to reconstruct a factorization.
4. Audit the 2025 Hamiltonian-plus-state theorem without denying its actual
   existential classification result.
5. Determine whether minimal-scrambling or quasi-classical criteria already
   supply a physical record carrier.
6. Refine `HC-DU-075` from a bare Hilbert factor to a selected physical
   observable algebra, net region, or equivalent access-preserving carrier.
7. Activate no successor unless a source selects that carrier together with
   formation, retention, access, provenance, and a held-out consequence.

## Required stops

Do not:

- claim that no Hamiltonian--state pair can characterize a factorization;
- call an entropy table derived from a target factorization a source-selected
  law;
- treat a TPS orbit as a point without stating the equivalence;
- call a selected factorization a formed record;
- infer spacetime, locality, classicality, ontology, or new physics;
- simulate many-body dynamics or use external hardware; or
- activate the next campaign swing from an algebraic classification alone.

## Required outputs

1. exact theorem and four-dimensional certificate;
2. primary-source collision and fair classification;
3. quantum-record carrier-passport refinement;
4. concept, orientation, counter-assumptive, and current-authority updates;
5. governed receipt and memory update; and
6. validation, explicit-path commit, push, and session finish.
