# Inverse chirality and three-generation retrodiction

## Frozen conditional target

Assume:

> The three observed matter generations form the protected chiral low-energy
> kernel of one source-derived physical operator, with a consistent local and
> global anomaly/mirror completion and source-derived restricted flavor
> breaking that yields nondegenerate masses, nontrivial mixing and a nonzero
> rephasing-invariant CP witness.

Every phrase is conditional. This run asks what that endpoint forces
backward; it does not treat the endpoint as evidence that one such operator
exists.

## Questions

1. Which parts of the endpoint constrain the content of one family, and which
   can constrain the number of families?
2. Does low-energy access to three chiral families identify a global chiral
   index, a triplet factorization, or the ultraviolet completion?
3. What does observed CKM-like CP violation force about family dimension and
   flavor algebra?
4. Which candidate source mechanisms remain after anomaly, completion,
   provenance, symmetry and held-out-prediction controls?

## Controls

- Treat anomaly cancellation as a homogeneous constraint and explicitly test
  more than three family counts.
- Admit arbitrary hidden complete-family and conjugate-family modes in the
  broad finite completion class; do not infer physical realizability from the
  combinatorial countermodel.
- Keep global index, accessible chiral rank and total zero-mode count distinct.
- Keep a CP lower bound distinct from an exact family count.
- Mark a flux, defect number, rank or rectangularity chosen to equal three as
  target import.
- Require any flavor mechanism to generate a noncommutative projected algebra
  and at least one held-out relation under a frozen budget.
- Preserve `gu-formalization` ownership of the actual carrier, source,
  operator/domain, anomaly analysis, chirality and generation verdict.

## Stop and reopen

Stop if the endpoint is simply encoded into a defect charge, family-copy
number, projector or generic Yukawa matrices.

Reopen at physical-construction grade only with a source-derived operator and
domain. Reopen at prediction grade only after a held-out mass, mixing, CP,
running or partner relation survives the strongest effective-three and generic
flavor rivals.
