## What was learned

factors. The three-width contrast is therefore not failing because of an
algebraic or implementation error.

It fails locally on conditioning. Around preparation ratios still hundreds
to thousands above the weak trap, the breathing curve is almost flat and its
affine-quotiented curvature is tiny. The source-model optimistic five-sigma
shot diagnostic exceeds $10^{19}$ baseline shots and $10^{17}$
aggressive shots for the source-near fixtures.

A much wider $q=(10,20,50)$ redesign increases the curvature. Even then the
same optimistic diagnostic is about $5.67\times10^7$ total baseline shots
and $1.62\times10^7$ aggressive shots at the source's quoted single-point
minimum rates. More importantly, it lowers the preparation trap by two to
three orders relative to the analyzed $10^7\,\mathrm{s}^{-1}$ setting.
The source has not established the timing, endpoint-overlap, preparation, or
noise contract for that redesign.
