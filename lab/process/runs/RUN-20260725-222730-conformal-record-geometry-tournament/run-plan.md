# Conformal record-geometry reconstruction tournament

Execute the next `HC-DU-038` gate rather than preparing a paper. Determine
exactly what a finite set of causal, volume, clock and reference records can
reconstruct inside a declared smooth conformal family, then defeat any
universal reading with the smallest admissible completion mode.

## Frozen arena

Use the time-oriented strip

\[
M=[0,1]_t\times[-1,1]_x
\]

with anchored left and right observer worldlines and the smooth Lorentzian
family

\[
g_u=u(x)(-dt^2+dx^2),\qquad u(x)>0.
\]

The coordinates, strip, anchors and conformal family are supplied comparison
scaffolding. They are not reconstructed and may not be reported as selected

## Training records

Reveal in order:

1. complete causal order and null reachability;
2. total spacetime volume over the strip;
3. the left observer's unit-interval proper time and one left-anchored radar
   return;
4. volume of the anchored region `x in [-1,0]`; and
5. volume of the overlapping anchored region `x in [-1,1/2]`.

Reserve the right observer's unit-interval proper time and scalar curvature at
an anchored interior point as held-out geometric observables.

## Candidate and adversary classes

### Declared two-mode family

\[
u_{a,b}(x)=1+a f_1(x)+b f_2(x)
\]

with

\[
f_1=(x+1)(x-1/3),\qquad
f_2=(x+1)(x^2-1/3),
\]

and `|a|+|b| <= 1/2`.

Prove:

- positivity throughout the strip;
- causal-order, total-volume, left-clock and radar equivalence across the
  family;
- one regional volume leaves a nontrivial exact fibre;
- two overlapping regional volumes identify `(a,b)` exactly;
- the identified parameters predict the held-out right clock; and
- a nonflat member is not isometric to the flat member because an anchored
  scalar-curvature invariant differs.

### Hostile smooth completion

Add

\[
h(x)=(x+1)(1-3x-6x^2+10x^3).
\]

It must lie in the kernel of every supplied training volume and left-clock
functional while changing the held-out right clock and curvature. A small
coefficient must preserve positivity.

This completion is the strongest local absorber: it demonstrates that the
two-volume positive reconstruction is conditional on the supplied
finite-dimensional mode class, not a universal geometry theorem.

## Theorem targets

1. **Finite-family rank theorem.** For
   `u_theta = u_0 + sum theta_k f_k`, finite linear volume and
   clock-squared records identify `theta` exactly iff their measurement
   matrix has full column rank.
2. **Held-out predictability criterion.** A linear held-out observable is
   fixed on every training fibre iff its mode row lies in the row span of the
   training matrix.
3. **Finite-record smooth nonuniqueness.** In the unrestricted smooth
   conformal class, whenever a held-out functional is outside the span of the
   finitely many training functionals, a sufficiently small smooth conformal
   perturbation preserves all training records and changes the held-out
   observable.
4. **Refinement naturality.** Splitting a measured region into additive
   subregions and reaggregating its volume preserves the measurement row and
   reconstruction rank; raw region count is not physical information.
