## Frozen lattice arena

Let \(G\) be a nontrivial finite Abelian group and let \(C_n\) be an oriented
cycle with \(n\geq2\) vertices and links. Use additive notation.

The kinematic registers are:

\[
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{kin}}
=
\bigotimes_{v\in V}\mathbb C[G]_{\phi_v}
\otimes
\bigotimes_{e=(v,w)\in E}\mathbb C[G]_{U_e}.
\]

The regular-representation matter coordinate and link coordinate transform
under \(\lambda\in G^V\) as

\[
\phi_v\mapsto\phi_v+\lambda_v,
\qquad
U_{vw}\mapsto U_{vw}+\lambda_v-\lambda_w.
\]

Define the dressed link variable

\[
Y_{vw}=-\phi_v+U_{vw}+\phi_w.
\]

It is gauge invariant. Gauge fixing every \(\phi_v\) to zero gives a unique
representative of every orbit, so the physical orbit basis is canonically
indexed by \(G^E\):

\[
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{phys}}
\cong
\bigotimes_{e\in E}\mathbb C[G]_{Y_e}.
\]

This explicit matter-completed factorization is part of the frozen arena. It
must not be silently transferred to pure gauge theory or to a different
regional-algebra convention.

The Wilson holonomy is

\[
H_C=\sum_{e\in C}U_e=\sum_{e\in C}Y_e,
\]

because the matter coordinates telescope around the closed cycle.
