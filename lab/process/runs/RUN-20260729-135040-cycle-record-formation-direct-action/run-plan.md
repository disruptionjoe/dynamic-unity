## Candidate A — one homomorphic record instrument

\[

\]

be a finite product of local finite-Abelian data groups and let

\[

\[

\frac1{\sqrt{|K|}}

\[

\mapsto

\(z\in D\).

### Target A1 — exact instrument

The predicted law is

\[

\begin{cases}
|K|^{-1},&Q(z)=Q(y),\\

\[

the selective Lüders instrument

\[
