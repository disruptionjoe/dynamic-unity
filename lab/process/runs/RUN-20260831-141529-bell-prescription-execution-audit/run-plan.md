# Frozen question

Does the source-pinned heralded Bell apparatus physically consume its formed
record to enact correction capability, or does it establish a correction
prescription and compatible terminal evaluation only? What exact continuation
contract separates those claims?

# Typed contract

- **Carrier:** the source-reported three-bit/eight-outcome Bell record.
- **Prescription:** the two-bit quotient selecting `I`, `X`, `Y`, or `Z`.
- **Execution:** a physical operation conditioned on that record.
- **Deferred correction:** record-conditioned adaptation of later gates,
  measurement frames, or terminal result interpretation.
- **Capability claim:** must declare whether the target is a corrected live
  quantum state, one terminal statistic, or an arbitrary future action family.
- **Status:** apparatus-local, ontically formed carrier; operational
  prescription and evaluation; disclosure only.

- **Lane:** 1, supported by 3, 4, and 7.
- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 necessity/type boundary.
- **Strongest absorber:** standard teleportation, Pauli-frame tracking,
  quantum instruments, and adaptive measurement-based computation.
