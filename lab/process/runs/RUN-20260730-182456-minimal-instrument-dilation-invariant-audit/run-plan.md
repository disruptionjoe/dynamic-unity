# Minimal instrument-dilation invariant audit

## Cold-start contract

- **Purpose:** make physical reality intelligible as one coherent,
  evidence-accountable whole.
- **North Star:** determine which target-blind physical antecedents select
  material, provenance-bearing, observer-indexed record interfaces, then test
  reconstruction and finite remainder.
- **Earned input:** `HC-DU-164` separates complete reconstruction of a
  declared reduced quantum instrument from selection of its material
  dilation and archive.
- **Current authority:** the flagship is parked with no executable successor.
  This run is one bounded formal audit. It does not activate the next
  empirical campaign card.
- **Owning lane/channel:** Lane 3 with Lane 4 support; `CH-FORMAL`,
  `CH-COLLIDE`, and one exact `CH-MODEL` control.

## Frozen question

> What is the strongest apparatus structure forced by a finite-dimensional
> labelled quantum instrument itself, and does that forced structure select a
> material archive?

The audit must keep apart:

1. the rank of an outcome map as a linear superoperator;
2. the rank of its positive Choi matrix, equivalently its minimal Kraus
   multiplicity;
3. the abstract minimal instrument dilation;
4. a normal unitary measurement model;
5. an actual material apparatus, archive, provenance chain, reset semantics,
   and access boundary.

## Pre-registered theorem targets

1. **Two-rank separation.** For outcome map \(\mathcal E_x\), define

   \[
   \ell_x=\operatorname{rank}L(\mathcal E_x),
   \qquad
   r_x=\operatorname{rank}J(\mathcal E_x).
   \]

   `HC-DU-164` gives operational label sufficiency iff \(\ell_x=1\).
   Test whether \(r_x\) instead fixes the minimal normal apparatus-sector
   multiplicity.
2. **Minimal-dilation theorem.** In a finite-dimensional, finite-outcome
   instrument, test the standard construction

   \[
   \mathcal K_{\min}=\bigoplus_x\mathbb C^{r_x},
   \qquad
   \dim\mathcal K_{\min}=\sum_xr_x,
   \]

   with the pointer PVM selecting each summand.
3. **Gauge-fixed archive algebra.** Let

   \[
   G=\prod_xU(r_x)
   \]

   act by within-outcome Kraus-basis changes. Determine the pointwise
   fixed algebra in \(\mathcal B(\mathcal K_{\min})\).
4. **Material-selection test.** Decide whether minimality and uniqueness up
   to unitary equivalence identify a physical carrier, basis, location,
   archive policy, provenance, retention, reset, access, or realized outcome.

## Exact controls

- Ideal projective outcome maps: \((\ell_x,r_x)=(1,1)\).
- Coin-labelled identity maps: \((\ell_x,r_x)=(4,1)\) for a qubit.
- One-outcome completely depolarizing replacer:
  \((\ell,r)=(1,4)\).
- A rational rotation of two dephasing Kraus operators leaves the map
  unchanged.
- For sector profile \((1,2)\), exact commutant equations for a sector sign,
  swap, and phase generator leave a two-dimensional fixed algebra: one scalar
  per outcome sector.
- A redundant Kraus list realizes the same CP map with more apparatus
  coordinates than its Choi rank.

## Absorber and novelty contract

Strongest absorbers:

- Choi's theorem and minimal Kraus rank;
- Stinespring uniqueness;
- Ozawa's instrument/measurement-process correspondence;
- Pellonpää and Tukiainen's minimal normal measurement models; and
- fixed-point/commutant algebra under unitary conjugation.

The mathematical core is expected to be known. Dynamic Unity may bank only
the typed relation between operational sufficiency, minimal representation,
and material selection. It may not claim a new measurement theorem or new
physics.

## Grade, kills, and stops

- Maximum Grade 4 for a scoped necessity/nonselection boundary.
- Cheapest positive: the instrument fixes an abstract minimal
  outcome-sector multiplicity profile and normal-model dimension floor.
- Cheapest kill: the only pointwise gauge-invariant apparatus observables are
  functions of the supplied outcome sectors, while physical carrier and
  provenance remain unselected.
- Stop on infinite-dimensional generalization, a claim that effective
  minimality is physical ontology, hardware, provider execution, a fitted
  rank claim, or a later campaign swing.
- Literature and exact finite algebra only. No simulation or external
  hardware is authorized.
