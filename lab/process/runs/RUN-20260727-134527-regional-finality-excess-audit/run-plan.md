# Regional finality and excess-content hostile audit

## Objective

Determine whether any of three prepared regional-finality candidates has
meaning-preserving empirical or mathematical content beyond standard physics:

1. a finality-rate bound distinct from causal propagation;
2. complementarity derived from physical record mergeability; and
3. universal critical exponents for decoherence/finality thresholds.

The exactly compensated continuous preferred foliation remains an ungraded
conditional premise. It may be inert, but it must not be silently replaced by
a leaking or discrete branch.

## Shared typed contract

For each candidate freeze:

- the physical system and dynamics;
- the observer/access and record interface;
- the action/tester family;
- the regional-finality or response predicate;
- the resource, error, and horizon contract;
- the matched standard-physics null;
- the first omitted field that could reopen the response class;
- the kill condition; and
- the retreat cost.

Delete distributed-systems vocabulary before judging excess content. Native
computer-science results may constrain an adapter only when their synchrony,
fault, task, adversary, randomness, and participant-knowledge assumptions are
preserved.

## Candidate 1 — finality-rate bound

### Physical proposition

There exists an observer-accessible rate or speed limiting formation of a
regional action-safe public record that is neither causal propagation at
\(c\) nor an ordinary system-dependent relaxation, mixing, decision, error,
or communication timescale.

### Matched null

Relativistic causal reach plus ordinary Hamiltonian/open-system dynamics,
finite communication, a declared error tolerance, and a declared decision or
fault protocol.

### Pre-registered kill

Return no excess if every proposed rate is:

1. a causal cone;
2. a spectral-gap, mixing, decoherence, metastable-escape, quantum-speed-limit,
   communication, or protocol hitting time;
3. adjustable by a law-compatible local coupling or waiting rule while
   leaving \(c\) fixed; or
4. operationally inaccessible under exact compensation.

An observable preferred-leaf rate that is not Lorentz-covariant exits the held
assumption and returns `LEAKING_BRANCH_REQUIRED`, not a result inside this
series.

### Retreat cost

The universal distinct-rate claim is withdrawn. A surviving rate is typed as
a model/contract-dependent ordinary timescale, an inaccessible hidden
parameter, or a separately parked leaking-branch conjecture.

## Candidate 2 — complementarity from record mergeability

### Physical proposition

Two source alternatives admit one jointly formable, provenance-preserving
physical record interface exactly when their associated observables are
compatible; quantum complementarity is therefore the obstruction to that
joint formation.

### Matched null

Standard joint measurability and compatibility of quantum effects,
instruments, channels, and sharp observables, followed by ordinary classical
merging of already formed records.

### Pre-registered kill

The broad claim is killed by any one of:

1. noncommuting observables that are jointly measurable;
2. commuting observables whose selected instruments are not jointly
   implementable or nondisturbing under the frozen resource contract;
3. classical mergeability of records formed in distinct incompatible
   contexts; or
4. dependence on a supplied pointer/archive/decoder rather than source
   physics.

A narrowed sharp-PVM theorem survives only as
`KNOWN_THEOREM_SPECIALIZATION` unless it yields a new physical adapter,
coefficient, or held-out response beyond standard quantum theory.

### Retreat cost

Replace “CRDT merge derives complementarity” with a typed distinction among
source measurement compatibility, joint record formation, sequential
disturbance, and downstream classical archive merge.

## Candidate 3 — universal critical exponents

### Physical proposition

An independently selected regional-finality order parameter has the same
critical exponents across microscopically distinct physical record-forming
systems, beyond exponents already predicted by their standard open-system or
statistical-mechanical universality classes.

### Matched null

Ordinary finite crossovers, exponential decoherence, dissipative phase
transitions, percolation, absorbing-state transitions, finite-size scaling,
and model-dependent metastable first-passage dynamics.

### Pre-registered kill

Return no excess if:

1. no thermodynamic/scaling family and operational order parameter are fixed;
2. the exponent changes under an admissible reparameterization of the proposed
   “finality” score;
3. symmetry, dimension, conservation, interaction range, bath, or dynamical
   generator selects an existing universality class; or
4. only a finite knee, threshold crossing, or exponential timescale is shown.

### Retreat cost

Withdraw universal-finality language. Retain only a finite operational
threshold or a standard universality-class assignment until a selected order
parameter and cross-platform no-refit exponent are supplied.
