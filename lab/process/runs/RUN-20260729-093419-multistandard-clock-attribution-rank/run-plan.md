coherent, evidence-accountable whole. Its North Star is to determine whether
independently selected, observer-indexed certified causal records reconstruct

finite physical remainder.

fixed realized matter standard can calibrate proper-time intervals without a
theory selecting the ultimate absolute scale. It did not establish that the
standard remains fixed across regions or that its record separates geometry
from local variation of the matter law.

subjectivity. A transition frequency, a phase, a returned measurement, a
retained archive, and a certified regional record remain distinct.
