# Causal-priority intervention ladder

## Question

`HC-DU-072` proves that terminal physical-operational and certified-record
presentations can be mutually sufficient without identifying ontological
priority. What is the minimum additional intervention structure needed to
distinguish:

1. a physical source writing a record;
2. a record/controller producing the matched physical state; and
3. a common cause producing both?

The swing asks for causal formation direction, not metaphysical substrate
priority.

## Why this is live

Dynamic Unity already owns:

- endpoint operational duality and action-relative first leaks
  (`HC-DU-072`);
- observational/channel/interventional boundary relocation grades
  (`HC-DU-031C`);
- certificate semantics versus physical feedback (`HC-DU-055`);
- response-equivalence selection and capability refinement (`HC-DU-056`);
  and
- source-bound versus preloaded authenticated controls (`HC-DU-051`).

Those results do not yet state one minimum, cross-platform causal-priority
test or identify which intervention family distinguishes direct formation
from a common-cause explanation.

## Scope and controls

- **Owning lane:** Lane 1.
- **Supporting lanes:** Lanes 3, 4, 5, and 7.
- **Channels:** formal proof, exact finite counterexamples, synthesis, and
  literature collision.
- **Maximum return:** scoped Grade-4 causal-orientation necessity/minimality
  theorem, quantum/distributed transfer, and corrected physical reopener. It
  cannot establish an ontology, new causal-inference theorem, new quantum
  law, physical interface selection, Grade-5 remainder, prediction, paper,
  model, or hardware result.
- **Strongest absorber:** structural causal models and intervention calculus,
  Markov equivalence, process tensors/quantum combs, quantum causal models,
  no-signalling, system identification, and DU's `HC-DU-031C/051/055/056/072`.
- **Cheapest kill:** the minimum intervention ladder is already explicit in
  current DU evidence; the claimed two-intervention minimum fails; or the
  quantum/distributed interpretations require changing object roles.
- **Local computation:** exact finite enumeration and two-qubit linear
  algebra only. It must validate a proof boundary, not simulate a proposed
  universe.
- **Hardware:** unavailable and unnecessary.
- **Cross-repository posture:** no sibling theorem is required or imported.

## Frozen classical models

Let \(U\sim\operatorname{Bernoulli}(1/2)\), physical-source variable
\(P\in\{0,1\}\), and record variable \(R\in\{0,1\}\).

Three candidate formation models share the same passive endpoint law
\(P=R=U\):

```text
M_PR: U -> P -> R
M_RP: U -> R -> P
M_CC: U -> P and U -> R.
```

The admitted arrow-breaking interventions are:

- `do(P=p)` followed by readout of \(R\); and
- `do(R=r)` followed by readout of \(P\).

Diagonal interventions setting both variables together are retained as a
negative control.

## Pre-registered targets

### T1 — endpoint nonorientation

Prove that every terminal-only observation or postprocessing has the same law
in all three models. Terminal operational duality therefore does not orient
the formation arrow.

### T2 — two-intervention minimum

Compute the response signatures under `do(P=p)` and `do(R=r)`. Prove:

- each single intervention separates one direct-arrow model but leaves the
  other direct-arrow model confounded with the common-cause model; and
- the pair of interventions distinguishes all three.

Return exact total-variation margins and the minimum intervention cover.

### T3 — access obstruction

Prove that if the admitted action family contains only passive observation,
terminal postprocessing, or diagonal joint setting, causal orientation
remains unidentified. Independent addressability is a physical premise, not
something recoverable from more endpoint samples.

### T4 — unchanged quantum and distributed transfers

Quantum:

- compare `S -> A` and `A -> S` CNOT formation circuits that produce the same
  Bell endpoint from oppositely located \(|+\rangle\) roots;
- verify that terminal tomography cannot distinguish them; and
- use fixed local pre-formation phase interventions to identify direction.

Distributed/physical:

- interpret the same three-model schema as source-write, certificate-driven
  actuation, and common-source formation;
- keep authentication, physical source binding, and public action distinct;
  and
- locate the same two-intervention minimum without claiming consensus creates
  matter.

### T5 — ontology and portfolio disposition

State exactly what an oriented causal arrow earns. A formation arrow within a
typed process is not proof that its upstream variable is the fundamental
substrate. Decide whether the sharpened physical reopener activates any
current arena.

## Required stops

Do not:

- infer causal direction from endpoint equality, correlation, or mutual
  sufficiency;
- treat a semantic relabeling as a surgical physical intervention;
- identify source writing, certificate authentication, controller action,
  and ontological creation;
- use a post-formation operation to claim a pre-formation causal arrow unless
  the complete process and recoupling are frozen;
- infer retrocausality from later record feedback;
- claim a universal quantum causal-discovery theorem from a two-qubit control;
  or
- activate hardware or a physical successor without an independently
  realizable intervention and complete acquisition.

## Required outputs

1. exact causal-priority ladder and proof;
2. executable classical and quantum certificate;
3. unchanged distributed interpretation and hostile controls;
4. corrected ontology and physical-reopener statement;
5. durable exploration, concept/orientation correction, current-authority
   transition, governed receipt, and memory update; and
6. validation, explicit-path commit, push, and session finish.
