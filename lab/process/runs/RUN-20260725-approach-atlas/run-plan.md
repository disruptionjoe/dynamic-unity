# Near-term research-swing approach atlas

## Purpose

Make every plausible near-term Dynamic Unity swing easier to execute well.
Attach the most efficient first attack, cheapest discriminator, reusable
output, scale-up rule, and stop condition without reranking, deleting, or
scientifically promoting any candidate.

## Five lenses

1. counterfactual statistician: estimand, rival contrast, identification,
   controls, uncertainty, and sequential stopping;
2. category theorist: typed objects and maps, equivalence/gauge, naturality,
   composition, obstruction, and smallest diagram;
3. adversarial experimentalist/metrologist: complete specimen, calibration,
   systematics, strongest null, held-out intervention, and feasibility;
4. formal proof engineer: theorem kernel, assumption minimization,
   counterexample synthesis, proof certificates, and independent verifier;
5. sequential value-of-information strategist: dependency-aware first move,
   reusable artifact, scale-up trigger, and avoidance of irreversible work.

## Coverage rule

- Begin from the active Lane objectives and the complete 27-paper portfolio.
- Give a direct approach card to every paper family with a plausible
  source-owned next swing or bounded clarification.
- Give explicit gate-only dispositions to post-ready, merge-only,
  transfer-owned, and dependency-blocked families so future agents do not
  mistake preparation for authorization.
- Reuse the existing 36-hypothesis method registry instead of copying it.
- Record approach, not worthiness: no vote, score, rank, grade, or Factory
  state changes.

## Required card

Each live swing records:

```text
home and current gate
exact decision or claim
counterfactual contrast
typed object, map, equivalence, and composition question
smallest proof/model/physical specimen
strongest null or absorber
first decisive move
artifact useful under every outcome
condition for increasing cost or scale
kill, stop, and reopen condition
maximum evidentiary grade
```

## Durable outputs

- one concise human-readable near-term swing atlas;
- one deterministic coverage/contract probe and artifact;
- cold-start and Lane routing pointers;
- no paper draft, TeX/PDF, experiment, submission, or external action.
