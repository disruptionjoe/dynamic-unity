carrying one independently selected physical record from antecedent physics
through formation, observer access, certification, lawful completion
restriction, and held-out reconstruction or physical remainder?

- **Primary lanes:** Lanes 1 and 3.

  counterexamples, and empirical discrimination.

- **Current-authority change:** none.

1. Use ten divergent method lenses in a triple-diamond pass:
   problem, architecture, and assurance.
2. Select a funnel rather than ten parallel programs.
3. Make Swing 1 an antecedent/reopener admission tournament, not another
   generic selector audit.
4. Prepare every later swing with its exact activation gate, parallel arms,
   return states, cheapest kill, retreat, grade ceiling, and hardware boundary.
5. Provide an explicit transition automaton and cold-start activation packet.
6. Preserve quiescence until a later direct-chat authorization activates
   Swing 1.
