# Commutative-record modular-time reconstruction gate

## Question

Can a complete classical certified-record algebra and its state reconstruct
nontrivial Tomita--Takesaki modular time, or must any modular reconstruction
retain a noncommutative ambient algebra, its state, and additional inclusion
or localization structure?

## Why this is the next unbanked seam

`HC-DU-076` sharpened a physical quantum carrier from a bare Hilbert factor
to a selected observable algebra or net region. Modular theory is one of the
strongest known bridges from an algebra--state pair to an intrinsic
one-parameter automorphism group, and in special AQFT settings that group has
geometric meaning.

Dynamic Unity has oriented to modular time but has not yet banked the exact
boundary between:

```text
classical certified records
  != noncommutative ambient algebra
  != modular automorphism group
  != calibrated physical time
  != reconstructed spacetime symmetry or geometry.
```

The swing tests that boundary without assuming record-first ontology or
borrowing Time as Finality's preferred-foliation work.

## Scope and controls

- **Owning lane:** Lane 6.
- **Supporting lanes:** Lanes 1, 3, 4, and 7.
- **Channels:** formal proof, exact finite control, primary-source collision,
  and synthesis.
- **Maximum return:** scoped Grade-4 necessity/non-reconstruction theorem and
  a typed positive modular-reconstruction ladder. Tomita--Takesaki,
  Bisognano--Wichmann, half-sided modular-inclusion, and locally covariant QFT
  mathematics remain absorbed.
- **Strongest absorbers:** standard modular theory; thermal time;
  Bisognano--Wichmann geometric modular action; modular-inclusion
  reconstruction; Fewster--Verch preferred-state nonselection.
- **Cheapest kill:** one faithful state on a commutative von Neumann algebra
  with a nontrivial modular automorphism.
- **Mandatory positive control:** a faithful nontracial state on \(M_2\)
  generates a nontrivial modular flow.
- **Mandatory hostile control:** two faithful ambient states agree on the
  complete classical record algebra while inducing different ambient modular
  dynamics.
- **Local computation:** one exact two-dimensional matrix certificate. It
  validates the proof boundary rather than simulating physics.
- **Hardware:** unavailable, unnecessary, and unauthorized.
- **Cross-repository posture:** Dynamic Unity only. Time as Finality owns its
  dedicated foliation exploration; no sibling result is used as evidence.

## Pre-registered targets

1. Prove that every faithful normal state on a commutative von Neumann
   algebra has trivial modular automorphism group.
2. Construct the smallest noncommutative positive control with explicit
   modular phases.
3. Construct same-record/different-modular-flow ambient states.
4. Separate modular parameter, calibrated physical time, and geometric
   modular action.
5. State the exact antecedents used by Bisognano--Wichmann and by
   modular-inclusion reconstruction.
6. Audit whether a locally covariant preferred state is physically selected.
7. Determine the minimum enriched record object that leaves modular
   reconstruction open without defining the target into the record.
8. Activate no successor unless a physically selected algebra--state--
   inclusion structure also supplies record formation, access, and a held-out
   consequence.

## Required stops

- claim that classical records cannot reconstruct any notion of time;
- identify a dimensionless modular parameter with proper time without a
  calibration theorem;
- use a supplied spacetime wedge as evidence that records derived geometry;
- treat a noncommutative algebra or faithful state as a formed record;
- infer ontology from operational or modular reconstruction;
- broaden a finite-dimensional witness into a Type-III AQFT theorem; or
- activate a local model, external hardware action, paper, or sibling work.
