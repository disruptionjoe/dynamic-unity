# Exact question

What does the Mott cloud-chamber construction physically select between a
spherically symmetric quantum source and one visible particle track, and which
additional record duties remain unselected?

The run must distinguish:

1. a law-selected family of correlated excitation patterns;
2. a retained quantum carrier whose alternatives may remain coherent;
3. a decohered pointer distribution;
4. one material track in one run; and
5. provenance for the upstream source and actualization mechanism.

  locating target-blind antecedents that select material, provenance-bearing,
  observer-indexed records and their reconstruction/remainder boundaries.

- **Lane/channel:** Lane 3, supported by Lanes 1, 4, and 7; `CH-FORMAL`,

# Tests

1. Reconstruct Mott's actual result: joint excitation is strongly suppressed
   unless source and excited atoms are approximately collinear; do not call
   this a single-outcome theorem.
2. Prove that a symmetric response can select the matched-pair relation while
   every one-site marginal remains uniform.
3. Prove that no deterministic equivariant selector can choose one direction
   from a fully symmetric antecedent when the direction orbit has no fixed
   point.
4. Exhibit orthogonal direction-conditioned quantum record sectors and show
   that their coherent superposition and dephased mixture have the same
   pointer-basis track statistics.
5. Exhibit a global coherent measurement that distinguishes those two states,
   proving that material track statistics do not identify the prior
   actualization ontology.
6. Apply `HC-DU-226`: a realized ideal track can reconstruct its own direction
   when direction sectors have disjoint support, while a coarse straight-track
   label and response-identical source routes cannot reconstruct direction or
   provenance.
7. Audit amplification, retention, access, reset, and first-droplet claims
   against the already banked detector results rather than rebuilding them.

decoherence and decoherent histories, quantum instruments, symmetry breaking,
cloud-chamber detector physics, and statistical sufficiency.

The novelty claim is killed if the result is standard correlation geometry,
symmetry nonselection, and quantum/classical-shadow typing. Stop if:

- collinearity is called selection of one ray;
