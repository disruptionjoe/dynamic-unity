# Center-screening regional finality and first-leak localization

## Objective

Consume `HC-DU-033D` rather than repeating it. Compose formed central records
across regions while retaining each noncommutative within-sector fibre. Prove
or refute the exact condition under which regional hardening screens those
fibres from every future public record, and locate the smallest map at which a
hidden fibre can re-enter the public center.

The swing asks:

> Is a layered regional fact final because the hidden quantum state was
> destroyed, or because every admitted future selective map closes on the
> public center?

## Frozen formal object

For every layer \(k\), freeze:

- a finite-dimensional regional action algebra \(\mathcal A_k\);
- its public center \(Z_k=Z(\mathcal A_k)\);
- the minimal central record formed before this swing;
- every acquisition, route, response, validity, provenance, and next-record
  selective CP map;
- the admitted future action policy and reset boundary;
- the representation group of common *-isomorphisms;
- the regional cover and occurrence identity; and
- all noncommutative fibres rather than tracing them out by definition.

For a selective map \(\mathcal J_\alpha\) from layer \(k\) to \(k+1\), its
Heisenberg adjoint is center-screening when

\[
\mathcal J_\alpha^*(Z_{k+1})\subseteq Z_k.
\]

## The theorem package to prove or kill

1. **Regional center descent.** The complete public event/next-record row
   factors through the current center iff every selective map pulls the next
   center into the current center.
2. **Sequential and parallel closure.** Tensor products and compositions of
   selectively center-screening maps remain center-screening.
3. **First-leak localization.** If a final public record depends on a hidden
   fibre, the backward pullback of one final central effect has a first layer
   at which it leaves the center. Two states with the same central record at
   that layer furnish a finite future-record witness.
4. **Exact leakage margin.** For a Hermitian pulled-back public effect \(E\),
   the maximum probability gap across same-central-record states is the
   maximum spectral diameter of its compression to one minimal central
   block.
5. **Screening is not destruction.** Noncommutative fibres may remain fully
   present while being screened from the public process. Conversely, a later
   noncentral coupling can convert retained fibre population or phase into a
   public record.
6. **Selective qualification.** A nonselective channel may preserve the
   center while a retained route or acquisition stratum depends on the fibre;
   `HC-DU-036H` therefore requires selective, not aggregate, center closure.

If any implication fails, return the smallest exact counterexample and revise
the package without changing the center or future action class after seeing
the result.
