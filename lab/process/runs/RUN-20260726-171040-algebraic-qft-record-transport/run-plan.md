# Algebraic-QFT record transport and capability-stable finality

## Objective

Attack four assumptions on which the completed finite five-swing spine might
silently depend:

1. a nontrivial center survives passage from finite quantum systems to local
   QFT algebras;
2. small one-step screening error implies durable approximate finality;
3. finality under the current action class survives future capability growth;
   and
4. physical selection must return one point interface rather than an
   equivariant orbit plus a reference-selected section.

The swing must reuse, not restate, `HC-DU-033C`, `HC-DU-033D`, and
`HC-DU-035D`.

## Frozen typed objects

- a unital von Neumann action algebra \(\mathcal A\);
- a finite sharp PVM \(\{P_q\}\subseteq\mathcal A\);
- its nonselective Lüders adjoint
  \(\Lambda^*(X)=\sum_qP_qXP_q\);
- an increasing family of admitted action generators;
- a binary public algebra and same-public-record coherent state pair;
- a finite future horizon \(H\);
- exact or norm-bounded selective screening maps;
- a symmetry group acting on candidate interfaces;
- an interface orbit and a physical reference reducing its stabilizer; and
- AQFT local-factor and split-inclusion literature as collision terrain, not
  as a supplied Dynamic Unity record theory.

## Theorem package to prove or kill

1. **Von Neumann transport.** A finite internal PVM preserves every element of
   \(\mathcal A\) under its Lüders channel iff all of its projections lie in
   \(Z(\mathcal A)\).
2. **Factor-internal record no-go.** If \(\mathcal A\) is a factor, the only
   exact action-internal nondisturbing finite sharp record is trivial.
3. **AQFT corollary.** A net of factor local algebras has no nontrivial
   bounded-region record net formed solely from local centers. A record must
   instead restrict the action algebra, use an enlarged apparatus/environment
   interface, invoke sector/global/asymptotic structure, or become
   approximate and horizon-indexed.
4. **Capability monotonicity.** The disturbance/screening defect of a fixed
   record is monotone under enlargement of the admitted action class.
5. **Generator-to-word bound.** For a binary record projection,
   one-generator commutator bounds add at most linearly with action-word
   length. Exact zero composes indefinitely; nonzero local error earns only a
   declared finite-horizon result absent contraction, summability, or reset.
6. **Approximate regional composition.** For contraction maps between
   Hilbert operator spaces with orthogonal public projections, the composite
   public-to-hidden defect is at most the sum of the step defects.
7. **Orbit correction.** The existing stabilizer theorem already permits a
   selected interface orbit with no selected point. A concrete observer needs
   a physical reference/section; covariance alone still does not select it.
