# PRRF-05 — thermodynamic quantum-record packet selector audit

## Cold-start contract

Dynamic Unity's Purpose is to make physical reality intelligible as one
coherent, evidence-accountable whole. Its North Star asks whether
independently selected, observer-indexed certified causal records reconstruct
all observer-accessible time, geometry, fields, and capability, or leave a
finite physical remainder.

The repository is quiescent at revision 93. `HC-DU-141` proves that a supplied
hybrid apparatus gives an exact finite-time capacity, while exposing two
missing physical arrows: reversible thermodynamic typing and one actual
retained outcome. This run searches a bounded primary-source class for the
smallest published model that supplies both.

The apparatus, bath, trajectory, detector, and memory are treated as ontic
physical model objects. Probability distributions and reconstruction
calculations are epistemic tools. A conditioned trajectory is not cast as an
observer-independent ontology unless the source theory says so, and a
measurement current is not cast as a retained or certified record without a
physical archive.

## Exact question

Does any bounded, physically explicit quantum-measurement model jointly
select, rather than merely assume:

1. the system--apparatus coupling or measured observable;
2. reversible pointer dynamics with thermodynamic typing;
3. a trajectory-level actual outcome;
4. retention, reset, and provenance;
5. observer access and a conditional source-to-record channel; and
6. a no-refit capacity or held-out reconstruction statement?

## Candidate classes

1. quantum-jump and continuously monitored stochastic thermodynamics;
2. autonomous or metastable quantum measurement apparatuses;
3. thermodynamically consistent classical pointers coupled to quantum
   systems; and
4. objective-trajectory or measurement-record models only where the record
   ontology is stated rather than inferred.

## Conditional/abductive warrants

- `STANDARD`: quantum instruments, stochastic master equations, local
  detailed balance, metastability, and channel capacity.
- `IMPORTED`: each primary source's apparatus, bath, detector, and trajectory
  interpretation.
- `PROJECT_NATIVE`: formed-record, provenance, access, and no-refit capacity
  requirements.
- No `CONDITIONAL_POSIT` may silently supply a missing selector and then count
  it as a result.

## Preregistered returns

### Complete packet

One model selects all six arrows and permits an exact finite-time
record-capacity calculation.

### Partial physical typing

The dynamics selects measurement formation and thermodynamic reliability but
still supplies the unravelling, detector, pointer basis, archive, or access.

### Trajectory representation boundary

A stochastic trajectory is conditional on a chosen monitoring/unravelling
and therefore is not a uniquely selected physical record.

### Record-without-capacity boundary

One actual detector transcript is physically formed, but the source
encoding, task, observer access, or retained sufficient statistic remains
external.

### Clean null

No bounded source satisfies the complete packet. Bank the minimum common
missing interface and keep the branch parked.

## Controls

1. Compare different unravellings of the same unconditional master equation.
2. Separate a detector click/current from a durable archive that a later task
   can access.
3. Separate local detailed balance of apparatus transitions from arbitrary
   measurement correctness odds.
4. Check whether the measured observable, detector efficiency, threshold,
   read time, and coarse graining are derived or supplied.
5. Require a held-out target or capacity calculation with no interface refit.

## Strongest absorber, cheapest kill, and stop

- **Strongest absorber:** quantum trajectory/unravelling theory, continuous
  measurement, metastable pointer models, stochastic thermodynamics, and
  ordinary quantum instruments.
- **Cheapest kill:** one unconditional physical dynamics admits inequivalent
  monitoring schemes or records, or the source explicitly starts from a
  supplied measurement operator and detector.
- **Positive control:** a source-selected metastable pointer plus reversible
  bath dynamics, an actual retained trajectory, and an exact conditional
  channel.
- **Stop:** after a bounded primary-source audit and the smallest exact
  selector/nonselector proof. Do not build a model, generalize the literature
  survey, or activate hardware unless a source crosses the complete-packet
  gate.
