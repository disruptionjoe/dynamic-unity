# Exact question

Can spinor or twistor transport provide the physical connection and
intertwiner needed by `HC-DU-220`, and under what conditions does the resulting
holonomy become an observer-accessible record rather than a latent response?

# Scope and authority

Dynamic Unity is the only write scope. Joe's direct `Go` reopens the typed
connection--soldering--readout seam for one bounded exact wave. GU and every
sibling repository remain read-only and are not required. No provider,
hardware, or external data acquisition is in scope.

# Frozen distinctions

The run keeps separate:

1. a spin or twistor carrier;
2. a connection and physical path;
3. its same-fibre holonomy;
4. gauge-conjugacy invariants versus a matrix in one frame;
5. a spinor vector versus its physical ray/density operator;
6. an independent reference arm and recombination rule;
7. the measurement basis and material archive;
8. a same-bundle endpoint versus a distinct target spin frame;
9. a selected soldering/intertwiner; and
10. twistor incidence/conformal transport versus a material detector coupling.

# Tests

1. **Spin-holonomy orbit.** Enumerate the quaternion/Pauli central and
   noncentral holonomies under the finite octahedral spin-frame shadow; keep
   central sign and noncentral axis/frame data typed separately.
2. **Projective erasure.** Prove that `+I` and `-I` give identical single-spinor
   rays and density operators.
3. **Reference-arm reopener.** Place the holonomy on one arm of a coherent
   two-path control and prove that an `X`-basis recombination distinguishes
   `+I` from `-I` perfectly.
4. **Readout-basis control.** Show that a path `Z` measurement cannot
   distinguish the same two states; visibility requires the selected coherent
   reference and readout basis.
5. **Reference deletion.** Trace out/delete the path and require the central
   sign difference to vanish from the spin state again.
6. **Distinct-frame obstruction.** Enumerate finite Pauli-frame alignments
   under independent source and target frame changes; require no invariant
   intertwiner before a coupling is supplied.
7. **Intertwiner control.** Verify in the two-dimensional irreducible carrier
   that a linear map commuting with the generating `X` and `Z` actions is a
   scalar, leaving projective phase versus relative-phase use distinct.
8. **Twistor type transfer.** State the local-twistor incidence/connection
   extension and identify which maps remain antecedent; do not claim a full
   twistor construction from the finite spinor control.

# Absorbers and maximum grade

The strongest absorbers are spin-bundle parallel transport, SU(2)/Spin
holonomy, projective quantum states, Ramsey/interferometric phase readout,
Schur's lemma, tetrad/solder-form geometry, local twistor incidence, quantum
instruments, and ordinary open-system measurement.

Maximum grade is scoped Grade 4 for exact necessity/nonselection boundaries
and a faithful typed extension. It cannot establish a physical twistor
substrate, derive a spin/twistor connection from nature, select a material
instrument or observer, transfer GU, establish issuance or a remainder, or
produce new physics.

# Cheapest kill and stop rules

Cheapest kill: the central spin holonomy `-I` is a global phase on one isolated
spinor ray and becomes visible only after adding a coherent reference arm,
recombination, and measurement basis.

Stop if:

- a frame-dependent spinor matrix is called gauge-invariant;
- a global phase on one ray is called an observable record;
- an interferometer or detector basis is inferred from the connection alone;
- a same-bundle spin transport is promoted to a distinct-system coupling;
- twistor incidence is called a selected material interface;
- the finite Pauli/quaternion control is called a full Lorentzian twistor
  theory; or
- any repository other than Dynamic Unity is written.
