## Science Council

### Orthodox professor

Every live program needs a premise status, earned grade, maximum grade, and
authoritative evidence pointer. A label or narrative priority is not enough.

### Heterodox professor

Parked ideas must keep exact reopeners, and divergent anomalies need a
non-WIP intake path so the one-program limit does not become intellectual
closure.

### Wild-frontier scientist

Do not preselect the next moonshot. The current cross-branch adjudication may
redraw the program map, so successor selection must wait for its result.

### Commercial scientist

Use one active scientific program, at most one decision-changing bounded side
probe, and one separately typed publication extraction. Publication should
consume banked work rather than compete with knowledge generation.

### Philosopher of science

Dependencies must be typed. Logical prerequisite, conditional premise,
empirical test, extraction, external custody, and nonblocking adjacency are
not the same relation. Repository ownership is not evidence.
