# Risk-indexed capability frontier

## Question

`HC-DU-070` proves that finite confidence can survive when exact support
exclusion fails. Does that broaden Dynamic Unity's North-Star reconstruction
route, or does it merely lower error for targets already present in the
complete record law?

The swing will build one target-relative risk object and apply it unchanged
to the strongest existing physical candidates. It must distinguish:

```text
empirical separation of theory-law families
    != target reconstruction from a record
    != physical selection of the record interface
    != formation of the complete record
    != capability at a declared risk and resource budget.
```

## Why this is live

The repository already owns:

- exact target-relative factorization and finite total-variation deficiency
  (`HC-DU-036C`);
- exact capability--record Galois closure (`HC-DU-064`);
- formation profiles and higher-order joint-realizability controls
  (`HC-DU-065--067`);
- a physical gauge-boundary specimen with exact reliability and first leaks
  (`HC-DU-040D/051`);
- selection-versus-transfer non-unification (`HC-DU-054`); and
- support-versus-probability-law non-unification (`HC-DU-070`).

Those results have not yet been composed into a single risk-indexed
capability frontier. Without that composition, agents may infer that a
positive statistical margin expands the scope of a record, or conversely
that a nonzero error makes a physically useful record non-final.

## Frozen formal object

Let:

- \(H\) be a nonempty finite completion/history class;
- \(Q_R(z\mid h)\) be the complete law of one target-independent formed
  record \(R\);
- \(A\) be a frozen query/action family;
- \(P_a(y\mid h)\) be the held-out response law for \(a\in A\); and
- \(\ell_a\) and \(\varepsilon_a\) be independently fixed loss and acceptable
  risk.

Define the per-action reconstruction deficiency

\[
d_R(a)
=
\inf_{K_a}
\sup_{h\in H}
\operatorname{TV}
\left(
P_a(\cdot\mid h),
\sum_z Q_R(z\mid h)K_a(\cdot\mid z)
\right).
\]

The risk-feasible capability frontier is

\[
\mathcal C_\varepsilon(R)
=
\{a\in A:d_R(a)\le\varepsilon_a\}.
\]

This is a target-relative statistical experiment. It is not a scalar
property of the bare state, record carrier, or observer.

## Pre-registered targets

### T1 — risk-indexed closure

For a fixed error contract, prove the antitone polarity between action sets
and record families:

\[
\Phi_\varepsilon(S)
=
\{R:\sup_{a\in S}d_R(a)\le\varepsilon_a\},
\qquad
\Psi_\varepsilon(\mathcal R)
=
\{a:\sup_{R\in\mathcal R}d_R(a)\le\varepsilon_a\}.
\]

Show that

\[
S\subseteq\Psi_\varepsilon(\mathcal R)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathcal R\subseteq\Phi_\varepsilon(S),
\]

so every fixed risk contract has closed capability--record pairs while
different risk contracts need not collapse into one scalar chain.

### T2 — no-scope-from-confidence

Prove:

1. exact factorization is \(d_R(a)=0\);
2. common postprocessing cannot decrease deficiency relative to the original
   record;
3. if two histories have identical complete joint record laws and different
   deterministic binary targets, every record-only decoder has worst-case
   error at least \(1/2\), at every repetition count; and
4. repetition or redundancy may reduce error only when the complete joint
   record law already differs across the target classes.

### T3 — three independent margins

Separate:

1. a robust margin between competing physical law families;
2. a record-to-target reconstruction deficiency; and
3. an antecedent-to-interface selection fibre.

Construct minimum controls showing that any one can be positive or closed
while another remains open.

### T4 — unchanged physical audit

Apply the same frontier without semantic refit to:

- the material `Z3` gauge-boundary record;
- the stabilizer-syndrome record;
- the closed metastable host;
- the causally closed gravitational-wave intensity record;
- finite-time infrared memory;
- the proper-time coherent-history specimen;
- the provider QND packet; and
- CHSH as a theory-separation control rather than a record-reconstruction
  specimen.

For each, return the target scope, risk behavior, formation/selection status,
complete-acquisition status, and exact reopener disposition.

### T5 — portfolio decision

Decide whether the broadened controlled-risk route activates a real physical
successor. A positive requires all of:

- physically warranted law family or record process;
- complete acquisition and frozen error model;
- target-independent formed interface;
- independently held-out target;
- positive robust reconstruction or theory-separation margin after the
  strongest legitimate class enlargement; and
- no interface, source, decoder, or resource refit.

If none qualifies, keep the portfolio quiescent and state the exact missing
premise rather than launching another host.

## Required stops

- treat confidence as new target information;
- scalarize action-specific losses, resources, or thresholds;
- infer interface selection from record sufficiency;

- count a supplied detector, code, probe, or decoder as endogenous;
- rerun an already banked gauge/QEC/metastable fixture as a new model; or
- promote a physical successor merely because the formal frontier exists.
