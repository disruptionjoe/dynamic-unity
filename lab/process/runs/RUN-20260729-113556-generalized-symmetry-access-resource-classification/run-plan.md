# Generalized-symmetry access and regional-QND resource classification

## Cold-start contract

Dynamic Unity asks whether independently selected, observer-indexed certified
causal records reconstruct all observer-accessible time, geometry, fields, and
capability, or expose a finite physical remainder. The program remains neutral
among record-first reconstruction, operational duality, and a physics-first
remainder. Local formation, regional aggregation, observer access, and global
completion are scope distinctions; none makes the record epistemic or proves
record fundamentality.

`CURRENT-RESEARCH.yaml` is quiescent after `HC-DU-119`. That result established
that one Wilson-loop target can be nondemolition-recordable, destructively
reconstructible, changeable but unmeasurable, measurable but locally
unchangeable, or causality-forbidden depending on gauge group, matter,
topology, resources, and demolition semantics.

This bounded successor tests the highest-ceiling reopener:

> Does generalized-symmetry structure determine physical access, or does it

> envelope?

slice, not another parity illustration.

## Primary-source boundary

- Casini and Magán,
  [*A generalization of the DHR theorem for higher form
  symmetries*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21810), prove under their
  \(\pi_0\)-complete, finite-index, transportable AQFT assumptions that
  higher-form loop sectors in \(D>2\) have finite Abelian-group fusion rules;
  dual sectors carry the Pontryagin-dual group and linked loop commutators are
  fixed by the character pairing and linking number.
- Shao, Sorce, and Srivastava,
  [*Additivity, Haag duality, and non-invertible
  symmetries*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20863), show in scoped 1+1D CFT and
  lattice examples that invertible and non-invertible symmetry sectors
  produce different additivity/Haag-duality failures. They explicitly note
  that assigning algebras to regions is not canonical.
- Bhardwaj and Schäfer-Nameki,
  [*Generalized Charges, Part II*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.17159), classify
  generalized charges through SymTFT defects; the SymTFT encodes both the
  symmetry and the physical theory through boundary conditions.
- Bandyopadhyay, Brassard, Kimmel, and Wootters,
  [*Entanglement Cost of Nonlocal
  Measurements*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.2264), establish entanglement
  production as a lower bound on the LOCC resource cost of exact nonlocal
  measurements.
- Beckman, Gottesman, Kitaev, and Preskill,
  [*Measurability of Wilson loop
  operators*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0110205), remain the physical
  Wilson-loop absorber and causality boundary already banked by `HC-DU-119`.

These are source results. Dynamic Unity may type and compose them but may not
claim their AQFT, symmetry, LOCC, or gauge-theory theorems as novel.

## Frozen operational class

Let \(G\) be a finite Abelian group and let \(n\geq2\) parties each possess a
data register with basis labeled by \(G\). The extended target is total charge

\[
Q(g_1,\ldots,g_n)=g_1+\cdots+g_n\in G.
\]

Freeze the spacelike regional formation class to:

1. local quantum operations at each party;
2. classical communication only after the local interactions;
3. a pre-distributed quantum resource that is charged explicitly;
4. an exact selective nondemolition instrument with projectors \(P_q\) onto
   total-charge sectors; and
5. no quantum carrier crossing between parties during formation.

This is an LOCC-plus-resource contract. A timelike coherent carrier is an
explicitly different operation class, not a loophole.

## Exact theorem target

Use the product input

\[
|+\rangle_G^{\otimes n}
=
|G|^{-n/2}
\sum_{g_1,\ldots,g_n\in G}
|g_1,\ldots,g_n\rangle.
\]

Conditioning the exact charge measurement on outcome \(q\) gives

\[
|\psi_q\rangle
=
|G|^{-(n-1)/2}
\sum_{\sum_i g_i=q}
|g_1,\ldots,g_n\rangle.
\]

Across every nontrivial bipartition \(A|B\),

\[
|\psi_q\rangle
=
|G|^{-1/2}
\sum_{r\in G}
|r\rangle_A\,|q-r\rangle_B,
\]

where the charge-sum states on each side are orthonormal. Each outcome
therefore contains exactly \(\log_2|G|\) ebits across every cut.

The intended necessity result is:

> Any exact LOCC implementation of this nondemolition charge instrument must
> begin with a resource carrying at least \(\log_2|G|\) entanglement across
> every nontrivial party bipartition.

The intended sufficiency result is:

> A \(G\)-GHZ character resource with exactly \(\log_2|G|\) entanglement
> across every cut, local controlled-character phases, and local Fourier
> readout realizes the instrument exactly. Every proper share subset is
> charge-blind; the joined share sum recovers \(q\).

Together these would give an exact necessity/sufficiency law for a natural
finite-Abelian family.

## Exact nonfactorization target

Hold the Abelian charge group and target fixed while comparing:

- local operations and classical communication with no pre-shared
  entanglement; and
- the same class plus the minimal \(G\)-GHZ resource.

If the first class cannot realize the nondemolition instrument and the second
can, the access profile cannot factor through generalized-symmetry data alone.
It requires at least:

```text
theory/net and representation
  + region topology and target sector
  + admitted instrument semantics
  + causal route
  + matter/probe content
  + resource state
  + acquisition and access contract.
```

This does not refute a classification that freezes the complete packet. It
identifies the minimum type of classifier.
