# Physical-arena selection tournament

## Objective

Select the smallest physical arena that can advance the post-`HC-DU-040B`
North-Star dependency rather than merely illustrate it. Compare four arenas
under one unchanged contract:

1. a locally coupled free-field AQFT probe;
2. a materialized gauge-boundary sector recorder;
3. an infrared/asymptotic QED record;
4. a finite laboratory clock/instrument control.

QFT is a bounded hostile comparison inside the tournament, not an open-ended
QFT survey.

## Frozen gate

Each arena must report:

1. the lawful completion class and nonzero law-only target diameter;
2. the local source--probe--pointer--archive formation route;
3. the independently physical symmetry breaker or oriented reference;
4. the future capability envelope, norm/tolerance, and finite horizon;
5. one held-out no-refit target or first-leak witness;
6. every supplied, orbit-selected, dynamically selected, or fitted arrow;
7. the strongest adjacent absorber; and
8. the cheapest local proof, counterexample, or kill.

Selection is lexicographic:

1. fail any of gates 1--5 and the arena cannot lead the next swing;
2. among survivors, minimize supplied arrows;
3. then prefer the cheapest exact local discriminator;
4. use novelty ceiling only after those scientific dependencies.

## Exact finite gate control

For the leading gauge-boundary candidate, use the smallest direct finite
control rather than an executable model:

- two binary interior charge occupations \(q_1,q_2\);
- three-valued boundary flux \(F=q_1+q_2\) fixed by the truncated Gauss law;
- initially blank three-valued external pointer and archive registers;
- a supplied nondemolition write
  \(|q_1,q_2\rangle|0\rangle_P|0\rangle_A
  \mapsto|q_1,q_2\rangle|F\rangle_P|F\rangle_A\);
- boundary-limited actions that depend only on \(F\); and
- one admitted interior action that reads \(q_1\).

The control must separately establish:

- nonzero law-only diameter for total charge;
- exact record-assisted reconstruction of total charge after the write;
- same-record/different-interior-distribution witnesses;
- finality relative to the boundary-limited action envelope; and
- the first leak when interior capability is admitted.

It must also expose rather than hide the remaining burden: gauge law and an
algebraic center do not select the material boundary, coupling, pointer,
archive, decoder, or future action class.

  the fewest supplied arrows and a decisive local next step?

  and laboratory instruments.

  selected record.

  archive without an access map and finite horizon.

  apparatus while the research target remains source-underidentified.
- Do not add an executable fixture when the finite control is transparent by
  inspection.
- Do not infer ontology, new physics, paper novelty, or hardware need from the
  tournament.
