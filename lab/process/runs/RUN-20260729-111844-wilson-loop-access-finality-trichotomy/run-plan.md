## Primary-source boundary

- Beckman, Gottesman, Kitaev, and Preskill,
  [*Measurability of Wilson loop operators*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0110205),
  prove that spacelike non-Abelian Wilson loops cannot be measured
  nondestructively without violating relativistic causality.
- The same paper constructs destructive distributed measurements when
  suitable faithful matter is available.
- For Abelian gauge theories with charged matter, it constructs a
  nondemolition measurement using local couplings to a pre-distributed
  entangled ancilla. Individual local outcomes reveal no field detail; their
  joined product reveals the loop value.
- In a pure Abelian theory on nontrivial topology, a homologically nontrivial
  Wilson loop is outside the local residents' observable algebra: they can
  change its value but cannot measure or apply the loop operator. The
  conjugate nonlocal electric field has the opposite capability profile.
- Friedman, Yin, Hong, and Lucas,
  [*Locality and error correction in quantum dynamics with measurement*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.09929),
  bound the information-propagation advantage supplied by a declared number
  of local measurement regions. Pre-distributed entanglement or measurement
  stations are therefore charged resources, not free nonlocality.

These are source results. Dynamic Unity may type and compose them but may not
claim their gauge-theory or locality theorems as novel.

## Frozen finite controls

Use an `n=3` binary loop surrogate with extended target

\[
W=Z_1Z_2Z_3.
\]

This is a circuit-level control, not continuum or lattice gauge theory.

### A. Abelian regional nondemolition record

Prepare a three-share cat ancilla. Couple each share locally to one data bit
with a controlled phase, then measure every share in the conjugate basis.
Verify exactly:

1. every proper subset of share outcomes is independent of the loop parity;
2. the product/parity of all outcomes reconstructs \(W\);
3. the induced nonselective channel is
   \(P_+\rho P_+ + P_-\rho P_-\);
4. coherence inside one \(W\)-eigenspace is retained; and
5. coherence between different eigenspaces is removed.

This is an exact `(3,3)` threshold record. The target is one bit, while its
spacelike formation uses three local output shares and a pre-shared entangled
resource. Access occurs only after causal aggregation.

### B. Destructive local refinement

Measure all three data bits locally and derive their parity afterward. Verify
that this also reconstructs \(W\), but its nonselective channel removes
coherence between different bitstrings even when they have equal parity.
The destructive transcript is therefore a strictly finer record, not another
implementation of the same nondemolition interface.

### C. Timelike sequential alternative

Pass one coherent ancilla through all three local couplings and read it only
after the route completes. Verify that it implements the same parity
instrument as control A without a pre-distributed cat state, while changing
the temporal, route, coherence, and acquisition contract.

### D. Abelian/non-Abelian algebraic discriminator

Enumerate the smallest non-Abelian control group \(S_3\). Verify that the
product of the transposition conjugacy class is not one conjugacy class,
whereas every conjugacy class of \(\mathbb Z_2\) is a singleton with
well-defined multiplication. This checks only the algebraic hinge named by
the primary source; it is not a proof of the relativistic no-go.
