# Self-tested modular reconstruction gate

## Question

Can intervention-labeled classical Bell records reconstruct the
noncommutative observable algebra and faithful state needed by `HC-DU-077`
well enough to determine nontrivial modular data, and what is lost when the
intervention labels or separation contract are removed?

## Why this is the next unbanked seam

`HC-DU-077` proves that the complete state on a commutative record algebra
does not determine ambient modular dynamics. That result leaves one sharp
positive escape:

```text
formed classical outcomes
  + independently retained intervention labels
  + a bipartite separation/commutation contract
  + complete conditional correlations
    -> self-tested noncommutative operator relations and state
    -> modular spectrum and dimensionless modular flow up to certified gauge.
```

Device-independent self-testing is the strongest existing theorem family for
this route. Dynamic Unity has Bell controls, but no banked result tests whether
self-testing closes the algebra--state step of the modular ladder or merely
moves the supplied interface into settings and separation.

## Scope and controls

- **Owning lane:** Lane 1.
- **Supporting lanes:** Lanes 3, 4, 6, and 7.
- **Channels:** formal proof, exact finite control, primary-source collision,
  and synthesis.
- **Maximum return:** a conditional Grade-3 reconstruction result plus a
  scoped Grade-4 context-erasure necessity result. Tilted-CHSH and
  self-testing mathematics remain absorbed.
- **Strongest absorber:** exact and robust tilted-CHSH self-testing already
  reconstructs partially entangled two-qubit states and measurements up to
  local isometry and auxiliary degrees of freedom.
- **Cheapest kill:** an exact tilted-CHSH-maximal correlation that fails to
  determine the target local state spectrum or operator relations under the
  standard self-testing assumptions.
- **Mandatory positive control:** the \(\theta=\pi/8\) tilted-CHSH strategy
  attains its exact quantum maximum and fixes a faithful nontracial local
  state whose modular eigenvalue ratio is \(3+2\sqrt2\).
- **Mandatory hostile control:** erase the setting labels from the same
  record law and construct a setting-independent classical source with the
  identical pooled output distribution.
- **Mandatory null control:** maximal ordinary CHSH self-tests a
  noncommutative Pauli representation but leaves the local reduced state
  maximally mixed, so its local modular flow is trivial.
- **Local computation:** one exact analytic two-qubit certificate with
  numerical cross-checks. It validates the proof boundary; it does not
  simulate hardware or estimate finite-shot robustness.
- **Hardware:** unavailable, unnecessary, and unauthorized.
- **Cross-repository posture:** Dynamic Unity only.

## Pre-registered targets

1. Reconstruct the exact tilted-CHSH state, observables, score, local reduced
   state, and modular eigenvalue ratio for \(\theta=\pi/8\).
2. State precisely what exact self-testing certifies and its local-isometry,
   auxiliary-system, and complex-conjugation gauge.
3. Prove that the certified spectrum and modular automorphism class survive
   that gauge.
4. Construct the exact context-erasure pair: one tilted quantum process and
   one classical setting-independent process with identical unlabeled output
   records but incompatible labeled conditional correlations.
5. Separate outcome records, setting/intervention provenance, separation,
   quantum-model validity, measurement independence, complete acquisition,
   and physical record formation.
6. Determine whether the result reopens modular reconstruction, selects a
   scientific successor, or only banks a conditional bridge and a new stop.

## Required stops

Do not:

- say that classical records by themselves derive a quantum algebra;
- call a Bell input label a physically selected measurement interface;
- infer spacetime separation from the commuting-operator premise;
- identify dimensionless modular parameter with proper time;
- infer ontology, record formation, provenance, access, or geometry from
  self-testing;
- treat exact maximal violation as an experimentally robust certificate; or
- activate hardware, a paper, sibling work, or a scientific successor unless
  the physical selection and held-out-transfer obligations are independently
  met.
