# Action-indexed physical selection frontier

## Cold-start contract

- **Purpose:** make physical reality intelligible as one coherent,
  evidence-accountable whole.
- **North Star:** determine what target-blind physical antecedents, if any,
  select material, provenance-bearing, observer-indexed record interfaces;
  then establish their reconstruction, operational-duality, and finite-
  remainder boundaries.
- **Honest boundary:** `HC-DU-159` proves that trivial formal instrument
  sections always exist and that unitary channels admit no informative
  refinement. It selects no physical sampler, archive, provenance, observer,
  law, prediction, or ontology.
- **Perspective/ontology:** observer-indexed means indexed to physical access,
  actions, and resources, not subjective. The action class may index an
  equivalence relation; it is not automatically another antecedent in that
  relation's selection poset.
- **Program/action:** Joe's `Go` activates only
  `MPA-02-PHYSICAL-SELECTION-FRONTIER`.

## Exact question

Which minimal target-blind physical antecedent bundles shrink a declared
interface orbit to one operational equivalence class? Is there one universal
minimum, a complete antichain of incomparable minima, or no finite frontier?

## Scope and grade

- **Owning lanes:** Lane 1 and Lane 3.
- **Supporting lanes:** Lanes 2, 4, and 7.
- **Channels:** formal proof, finite counterexample, literature collision, and
  synthesis.
- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 for an exact action-indexed frontier
  theorem and counterfactual minimality witnesses.
- **No Grade 5:** no physical remainder, observed excess, or new physics can
  be earned here.
- **Hardware/local model:** no hardware. Use direct proof and only one exact
  finite fixture if it establishes an antichain or boundary that prose cannot.

## Frozen distinctions

```text
action/access class H
  indexes
operational equivalence ~H

antecedent bundle aS
  may or may not select
one ~H-equivalence class

formal instrument
  != physical sampler
  != actual outcome
  != material archive/provenance
  != observer access
```

Changing \(H\) changes the selection target. Counterfactual removal of \(H\)
inside one fixed-target poset is ill typed.

## Method

1. Define antecedent refinement and selection by kernel containment.
2. Prove monotonicity and characterize minimal selecting bundles.
3. Construct the smallest finite fixture showing multiple incomparable
   minimal bundles and a frontier change under action-class enlargement.
4. Freeze one finite quantum measurement-model class and test whether
   coupling/isometry plus pointer observable is jointly sufficient and
   individually necessary for a formal instrument.
5. Counterfactually remove sampler, archive, provenance, and access from the
   complete packet, reusing earned witnesses rather than rebuilding them.
6. Collide the result with minimal measurement-model, instrument,
   process-tensor, sufficient-statistic, and candidate-key mathematics.
7. Return the smallest scope that survives and stop before Swing 3.

## Assumptions and warrants

- `STANDARD`: finite sets/Hilbert spaces, kernels, CP instruments, and
  measurement-model dilation.
- `PROJECT_NATIVE`: target blindness, complete-packet typing, action-relative
  equivalence, and evidence grades.
- `CONDITIONAL_POSIT`: the bounded candidate antecedent family is declared
  before the finite frontier calculation.
- `DERIVED`: kernel/frontier theorems and finite counterexamples.
- `CONSTRUCTIVELY_REALIZED`: the qubit dilation/pointer witnesses.

## Cheapest kills

- If the intersection of all selecting bundles selects, the claimed
  unavoidable antichain collapses to one minimum.
- If an alleged minimum contains the interface or held-out target itself, it
  is circular and removed.
- If changing the action class leaves the same response equivalence, it does
  not demonstrate an indexed frontier.
- If measurement-model deletion witnesses change the system, outcome
  alphabet, or frozen equivalence contract, they do not establish necessity.
- Known sufficient-statistic, candidate-key, and minimal-dilation results kill
  novelty at their exact scope.

## Stop

Stop without Swing 3 if:

- the five `HC-DU-159` coordinates cannot be typed as antecedents versus
  predicates versus indices;
- only the complete packet selects itself;
- no source-pinned material coordinate survives;
- the result is fully absorbed; or
- a new model would add no learning beyond direct proof and literature.
