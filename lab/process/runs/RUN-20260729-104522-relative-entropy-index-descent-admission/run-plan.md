# Relative-entropy and GU index/direct-action descent admission gate

## Cold-start contract

Dynamic Unity asks whether independently selected, observer-indexed certified
causal records reconstruct all observer-accessible time, geometry, fields, and
capability, or expose a finite physical remainder. `HC-DU-115` and
`HC-DU-116` established that exact mediator elimination preserves the complete
source action and its total on-shell background response when the complete
translation is retained; pointwise matching and local mediator partitions are
strictly weaker.

An external council proposed two possible next objects:

1. Araki relative entropy as a type-III-safe replacement for trace entropy in
   a capacity or Landauer-style statement; and
2. a field/direct-action descent question for the GU generation or chirality
   invariant.

Neither enters the portfolio by recommendation. This run first asks whether
either object is both novel enough and typed enough to change Dynamic Unity's
scientific successor state.

## Construction forks

### Relative entropy

- **Standard operator-algebra object:** Araki relative entropy of normal
  states on a von Neumann algebra, with monotonicity under a declared normal
  channel or algebra restriction.
- **Dynamic Unity object under test:** relative-entropy loss across a
  physically selected record channel, with a frozen state family, reference
  state, target, observer access, action class, and resource contract.

The first is established mathematics. It must not be renamed a capacity,
physical remainder, record, or capability without the second contract.

### GU generation/chirality

Per GU's own construction-fork rule, two candidates must remain distinct:

- a standard integer/Fredholm index; and
- GU's program-native proposed 3-primary torsion carrier.

GU currently marks the generation count `OPEN / located-not-forced`, the
source action unbuilt, and the bridge from the torsion carrier to integer
three unproved. This run may audit whether a descent theorem is well posed; it
does not adjudicate or edit GU.

## Frozen finite controls

The relative-entropy arm uses exact rational classical controls:

1. a sufficient record with parameter-independent conditional hidden law,
   for which full and record-level Kullback--Leibler divergence agree;
2. an insufficient constant record with positive full divergence and zero
   record divergence;
3. two targets on the same lossy record, one reconstructable and one not, to
   show that positive information loss is not itself a target-relative
   remainder; and
4. relabeling invariance as a positive naturality control.

The descent arm uses a finite determinant-sign control only to distinguish
two contracts:

1. a positive source kernel such as \(D^\dagger D\), which can erase an
   orientation sign; and
2. the complete determinant/effective-action datum, which can retain it.

This is not a GU generation-index model. Its sole purpose is to prove why a
descent question must name the complete translated invariant and cannot be
posed on a source-magnitude quotient alone.

## Exact questions

1. Is the proposed relative-entropy object new, or is its core absorbed by
   Araki monotonicity and Petz sufficiency?
2. Does equality of relative entropy characterize preservation of the full
   admitted state family under a fixed record channel?
3. Is full statistical sufficiency necessary for one held-out target?
4. Can relative-entropy loss alone be called capacity, physical remainder,
   record formation, or observer capability?
5. Does GU currently provide one established generation/chirality invariant
   whose descent through field elimination can be tested?
6. What data are minimally required for a well-posed field/direct-action
   descent theorem?
7. Does a source-only positive kernel preserve determinant orientation, or
   must the complete effective action be retained?
8. Does either arm admit a new Dynamic Unity flagship successor?

## Strongest absorbers

- Araki relative entropy on general von Neumann algebras;
- data processing and Petz sufficient-subalgebra/recovery theory;
- classical sufficient statistics and Blackwell comparison;
- Holevo and channel-capacity theory, which require channel, coding, task,
  and resource contracts beyond a state divergence;
- effective actions, determinant phases, anomalies, and index theory; and
- GU's own `OPEN / located-not-forced` construction fork.

## Cheapest kills

1. Identify a DU-owned theorem that follows from relative entropy but is not
   a form of data processing, sufficiency, recoverability, or an already
   declared target-factorization result.
2. Show that one positive relative-entropy loss determines whether every
   independently declared target reconstructs.
3. Derive a channel capacity without specifying channel uses, code/task
   family, error criterion, and resources.
4. Exhibit a current GU theorem selecting one generation invariant, a
   completed source action, and a field-to-direct-action translation on which
   that invariant can be compared.
5. Show that \(D^\dagger D\) determines the sign or phase of \(\det D\) in the
   finite orientation control.

## Stops

- Do not equate relative-entropy loss with capability, capacity, physical
  remainder, occurrence, record formation, or finality.
- Do not require full-state statistical sufficiency when the frozen target
  factors through a coarser record.
- Do not flatten GU's integer-index and 3-primary torsion constructions.
- Do not claim GU's generation count is an established bundle index.
- Do not use a finite determinant-sign control as a model of GU.
- Do not turn a missing source action or translation map into evidence for
  either field or direct-action ontology.
- Do not select a successor merely because a question has high ceiling.
