# Instrument no-section admission

## Exact question

In the smallest nontrivial multi-time process arena, can a complete
instrument–sampler–archive–provenance–access packet be selected by a natural,
target-blind assignment respecting physical symmetries, operational
equivalence, mixtures, and composition; or is there an exact no-section
obstruction?

## Scope

- **Owning repository:** Dynamic Unity.
- **Primary lanes:** Lanes 1 and 3.
- **Supporting lanes:** Lanes 2, 4, 6, and 7.
- **Channels:** formal proof, literature collision, and synthesis.
- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 for an exact naturality theorem or
  obstruction; no Grade-5 physical remainder in this action.
- **Hardware:** unavailable, unnecessary, and unauthorized.


## Method

1. Reconstruct the exact boundary already earned by `HC-DU-063`,
   `HC-DU-137`, `HC-DU-142`, and `HC-DU-156`.
2. Define the smallest process category/groupoid and the fibre of admissible
   complete interfaces over each process.
3. Attempt one natural constructive section, including deterministic and
   stochastic-valued possibilities.
4. Seek the smallest exact automorphism or operational-equivalence obstruction
   that survives quotient typing.
5. Collide the resulting statement with primary literature on instruments,
   channel dilations, no-programming, contextuality, sufficient statistics,
   process tensors/combs, and naturality.
6. Return only the smallest scope that survives.

## Cheapest kills

- A universal deterministic section is killed by a process automorphism whose
  interface fibre has no fixed point.
- A universal stochastic section is killed only if the same fibre admits no
  invariant normalized probability; deterministic nonselection alone is
  insufficient.
- A claimed no-section theorem is killed by a natural trivial or canonical
  section inside its stated contract.
- Novelty is killed by a known theorem that covers the exact typed statement.
