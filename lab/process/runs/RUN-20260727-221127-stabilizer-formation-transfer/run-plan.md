# Stabilizer-syndrome formation transfer

## Question

Does the resource-decorated record-formation framework of `HC-DU-065`
transfer unchanged to a second serious physical arena with a held-out
capability consequence, or does stabilizer quantum error correction require
a different formal object?

## Scope and controls

- **Owning lanes:** Lanes 3 and 5, with Lanes 1, 4, and 7 supporting.
- **Channels:** formal proof, synthesis, and literature collision.
- **Maximum return:** scoped Grade-4 exact transfer or non-transfer result in
  the ideal three-qubit repetition-code arena.
- **Strongest absorber:** stabilizer QEC, Knill--Laflamme correctability,
  perfect state/subspace discrimination, QND syndrome measurement, and
  ordinary dimension/cardinality bounds.
- **Cheapest kill:** the proposed result merely restates `HC-DU-040E`, or the
  same semantic \(M_3\) lattice fails to support the unchanged
  orthogonal-output formation criterion.
- **Local-computation disposition:** no model. Finite-dimensional support,
  syndrome, and recovery proofs decide the question exactly.
- **Hardware disposition:** unavailable, unnecessary, and irrelevant.

The code, error class, stabilizers, pointer couplings, archive, decoder, and
observer access are frozen antecedents. One error occurrence is not silently
equated with one black-box channel use. Encoded-carrier redundancy,
pre-existing reference entanglement, pointer capacity, readout rounds,
intermediate export, interaction locality, and archive support remain
separate charged resources.

## Required outputs

1. the exact stabilizer-syndrome \(M_3\) control;
2. an unchanged perfect-formation criterion in terms of orthogonal
   conditional output supports and archive capacity;
3. exact parallel, sequential, and joint-pointer syndrome routes with their
   resource differences;
4. a lower bound excluding one binary terminal pointer;
5. a held-out record-only recovery consequence;
6. a Pauli-channel/stabilizer comparison proving native resource
   non-unification;
7. novelty collision, stops, and explicit non-promotions; and
8. live-state transition, validation, commit, push, and session completion.

## Stop

Stop when the transfer and the first exact non-unification are proved. Do not
build a circuit, run hardware, claim a new QEC theorem, treat code redundancy
as free, or infer endogenous selection from a supplied syndrome instrument.
