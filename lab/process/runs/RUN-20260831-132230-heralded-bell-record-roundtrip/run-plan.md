# Frozen question

Can one unchanged, source-grounded quantum apparatus physically form a
provenance-bearing, observer-accessible record whose pre-registered
action-relative quotient is
both necessary and sufficient for a held-out action—and what selection,
actualization, archive, and finite-resource obligations remain after that
positive control?

# Specimen and source freeze

The primary specimen is Arenskötter et al., “Full Bell-basis measurement of an
atom-photon 2-qubit state and its application for quantum networks,” *Physical
Review Research* **6**, 023061 (2024), DOI
[`10.1103/PhysRevResearch.6.023061`](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.6.023061).

The admitted source claims and apparatus objects are frozen before the local
formal check:

- one trapped `40Ca+` memory ion;
- one incoming 854-nm photonic qubit and its SPDC partner;
- 393-nm Raman-herald polarization;
- first/second absorption passage, distinguished by arrival time;
- final atomic-qubit projection;
- the eight raw outcomes in the paper's Eqs. (10)--(13);
- their four Bell-state classes and corresponding Pauli corrections;
- the remotely retained partner photon as the held-out action target;
- the reported uncorrected and corrected process fidelities and finite success
  probabilities; and
- the paper's actual initialization, control, readout, conditioning, and reset
  operations.

No raw-data availability, implementation-complete attempt census, ontological
collapse, external hardware access, or apparatus derivation from bare quantum
laws is presumed.

# Typed contract

- **Ontic status:** the ion, photons, interactions, detector transitions, and
  materially formed detector outputs are treated as physical. The analysis is
  neutral among empirically equivalent detailed ontologies.
- **Epistemic layer:** state/process tomography, background correction, and
  inferred fidelities are scientific estimates from records.
- **Scope:** one local apparatus and its linked remote photon; no global record
  ontology or public finality.
- **Formation:** ordinary disclosed quantum dynamics under the frozen
  preparation and apparatus. No issuance claim.
- **Observer/access role:** the controller that receives the herald timing,
  herald polarization, and atomic projection and may condition a Pauli action
  on the partner photon.
- **Raw record:** `r=(p,h,a)` in `Z_2^3`, with `p` the absorption-passage bit,
  `h` the Raman-herald polarization bit, and `a` the atomic-projection bit.
- **Action target:** select the Pauli correction that recovers an arbitrary
  input qubit on the remote photon in the ideal protocol.
- **Resources:** per-attempt success probability, finite retries, confidence,
  time, source rate, detector efficiency, background, binning, and retained
  attempt lineage remain charged.
- **Representation equivalence:** raw outcomes may be quotiented only when the
  induced correction is unchanged for every admitted input state.

# Frozen branches

1. **Positive reconstruction:** a pre-registered action-relative quotient of
   the physically formed raw record is sufficient for the held-out correction.
2. **Minimality:** every exact deterministic correction-sufficient quotient
   has at least four classes; dropping either actionable bit causes a finite
   loss for an unknown input.
3. **Audit remainder:** raw distinctions discarded by the action quotient may
   remain useful for provenance, calibration, or fault diagnosis without being
   required by the ideal correction.
4. **Resource correction:** conditional fidelity and finite-horizon capability
   separate once failed/rejected attempts are charged.
5. **Ontology boundary:** downstream records cannot distinguish theories that
   induce the same frozen instrument and record-conditioned target channel.

# Grade, absorber, kill, and stop

- **Lane:** 3, supported by 1, 4, and 7.
- **Channels:** `CH-SYN`, `CH-FORMAL`, `CH-COLLIDE`, `CH-EMPIRICAL`, and
  `CH-MODEL` only for exact finite checks.
- **Maximum grade:** Grade 4 for a scoped necessity/minimality theorem grounded
  in the physical specimen. Grade 5 is unavailable without a nonabsorbed
  finite discriminator.
- **Strongest absorber:** standard teleportation, heralded trapped-ion
  measurement, quantum-instrument theory, data processing, and ordinary
  resource-accounted retry analysis.
- **Cheapest kill:** the raw outcomes fail to factor through a four-class
  correction quotient, a smaller quotient remains exact for every unknown
  qubit, or the reported apparatus does not physically retain/access the
  variables used by the claimed quotient.
- **Stop:** do not infer apparatus selection from laws, single-world
  actualization from a click, unconditional capability from heralded fidelity,
  or ontology from operational equivalence. Stop after the exact quotient,
  minimality, resource, and nonidentification boundaries are decided.
- **Hardware:** ineligible. All first learning is exact and local.
