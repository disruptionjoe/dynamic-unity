## Objective

Determine whether a single scalar "objectivity threshold" is scientifically
defensible. The primary target is instead a typed separation theorem for:

1. **reconstruction** — enough joint evidence to distinguish a declared
   history within an error contract;
2. **finality** — enough authenticated, locked, provenance-bearing support to
   exclude incompatible certificates within a fault contract; and
3. **capability** — enough certified evidence, after cost and reversal risk,
   to make a declared action admissible.

The swing must establish exactly when these thresholds coincide, when they are
independent, and when local scalar hardening before aggregation destroys
action-relevant evidence retained by provenance-preserving joint
finalization.

The deliverable is a finite, executable theorem-and-counterexample package.
It is not a claim that physical reality literally runs a consensus protocol,
that every finite crossover is a phase transition, or that information is
ontologically fundamental.

## Frozen Typed Contract

### Classical evidence channel

- A history bit \(H\in\{-1,+1\}\) is drawn from a frozen prior.
- Each authenticated fragment has a declared provenance identity and a
  declared conditional channel.
- A raw coalition channel preserves every supplied fragment value and its
  reliability or provenance type.
- A local-hardening channel applies a frozen deterministic scalar map to each
  fragment before aggregation.
- Reconstruction is evaluated by Bayes error under the frozen channel and
  decision rule. An IID concentration bound is reported only for the IID
  control; a common-shock model is required as a correlated null.

### Finality channel

- There are \(N\) authenticated validators, at most \(f\) Byzantine.
- A certificate contains signatures from a size-\(q\) subset and identifies
  one of two incompatible histories.
- An honest validator locks and cannot support both histories in the same
  declared finality instance.
- Incompatible certificates are impossible exactly when every two
  size-\(q\) quorums have more than \(f\) validators in common:

  \[
  2q>N+f.
  \]

- When \(2q\le N+f\), the probe must construct an exact split view whose
  overlap can be entirely Byzantine.
- This is a known quorum-intersection control, not the novelty claim.

### Capability channel

- A finite action table, loss function, verification cost, resource budget,
  and maximum admitted failure/reversal probability are frozen before
  outcomes.
- A capability is present only when some action newly passes that independent
  risk-and-cost contract after certification.
- Agreement, confidence, or reconstruction alone does not imply capability.

### Quantum access control

- Use an explicit finite quantum channel or state family with declared
  coalition measurements.
- The minimum control is the GHZ phase pair
  \((|000\rangle\pm|111\rangle)/\sqrt2\): every proper-subset reduced state is
  identical across the pair, while the full coalition distinguishes them.
- This establishes a collective reconstruction/access threshold only. It
  does not establish public finality, broadcast objectivity, collapse,
  irreversible classicality, or a physical ontology.

## Target Statements

2. **Hardening data-processing theorem.** A deterministic local-hardening

3. **Correlation no-go.** Equal fragment count and equal one-fragment accuracy

5. **Quantum access control.** Verify the proper-subset/full-coalition GHZ

   public finality.
6. **Cross-platform invariant candidate.** Decide whether the only surviving
   shared statement is a typed access-to-stabilization architecture, or
   whether a nontrivial new theorem joins the classical and quantum cases.

## Success And Stop Conditions

Success requires:

- every threshold is typed, independently frozen, and executable;
- exact theorems and finite converse fixtures pass deterministic checks;
- correlations, provenance, locking, rollback, and action cost cannot be
  silently replaced by fragment count;
- classical and quantum claims retain their distinct channel semantics;
- the source audit grades each proposed claim as `KNOWN`, `SPECIALIZATION`,
  `POSSIBLE CONJUNCTION`, or `SEARCH-INCOMPLETE`; and
- the result changes a concrete next research decision.

Stop or narrow if:

- the shared law is only the word "threshold";
- reconstruction, consensus, and capability use different primitives but are
  plotted on one untyped axis;
- an IID law of large numbers is generalized to correlated or adversarial
  fragments;
- metastable confidence is renamed finality without an escape or rollback
  bound;
- a GHZ or secret-sharing access structure is renamed classical objectivity;
- a standard quorum, data-processing, Chernoff, coding, or access-structure
  result is promoted as novel; or
- the capability threshold is manufactured by refitting the utility contract.
