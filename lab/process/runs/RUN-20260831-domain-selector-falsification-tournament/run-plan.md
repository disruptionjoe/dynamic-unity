# Exact question

Can any established target-blind physical principle select, or jointly
generate, the candidate domain on which it acts rather than merely filter a
domain supplied in advance?

The tournament tests five mechanism families without assuming that absolute
selection from nothing is either possible or physically required:

1. anomaly, index, and cobordism constraints;
2. unitarity, positivity, analyticity, and bootstrap consistency;
3. locality, causality, and factorization;
4. renormalization-group stability, fixed points, and ultraviolet completion;
5. variational/action selection and dynamical stability.

# Common anti-smuggling contract

For every mechanism, freeze and report:

- the typed domain on which the rule is defined;
- every spacetime, symmetry, field, operator, state, boundary, and regularity
  assumption required to state it;
- what objects the rule eliminates or selects;
- whether the rule selects the domain, generates it from a smaller antecedent,
  or only filters it;
- uniqueness only modulo declared physical equivalence;
- one locked observable or response image, if the rule sharpens one;
- the physical ruler needed to compare that response; and
- every record, detector, access, provenance, archive, and consumer interface
  that remains supplied.

No family may borrow an interface or capability contract from another DU
branch. No target may be used to choose the domain, assumptions, or selector.

# Attempt order

1. Apply definition-level kills before detailed reconstruction.
2. Reconstruct only survivors from primary sources.
3. Distinguish four outcomes: relative filtering, generated-domain selection,
   consistency-intersection sharpening, and minimal-antecedent stability.
4. Test premise ablation and benign domain enlargement wherever the literature
   supports an exact comparison.
5. Decide whether `HC-DU-213`'s candidate-domain reopener is physically
   meaningful as written or demands the impossible target of a source-free
   source.
6. Bank one survivor, one exact boundary, or a clean no-ready result.

# Evidence, grade, absorbers, and kills

Consumes `HC-DU-211--213`. Maximum grade is scoped Grade 4 for an exact typed
necessity/nonimplication result and source-grounded disposition. It cannot earn
a new law of physics, a selected complete handoff, or a physical prediction.

Strongest absorbers:

- anomaly and cobordism constraints;
- S-matrix positivity and conformal bootstrap;
- soft theorems and local/factorizing QFT;
- Wilsonian RG, the `a`-theorem, and asymptotic-safety programs;
- ordinary variational calculus, causal variational principles, and inverse
  problems; and
- type theory, model theory, and specification/implementation separation.

Cheapest kill: the rule is defined only after a candidate field, state,
observable, history, or geometry category is supplied, and its output is only
a subfamily of that category.

Stop if:

- a literature constraint is promoted into a DU novelty;
- a conjectural swampland or UV-completion criterion is called proved;
- an intersection of filters is called absolute selection;
- a union of hand-picked domains is called domain generation;
- a ruler, detector, access boundary, or record consumer is hidden;
- the regress of antecedents is turned into an unfalsifiable demand for
  selection from nothing;
- a small formal example is called a physical model; or
- any repository other than Dynamic Unity is written.
