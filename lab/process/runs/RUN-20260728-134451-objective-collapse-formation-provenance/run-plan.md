# Objective-collapse occurrence-to-archive formation and provenance gate

## Question

Conditional on each complete theory

\[
\Theta_f=(L_{\rm GRW},PO_f)
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
\Theta_m=(L_{\rm GRW},PO_m),
\]

does the primitive process itself turn a blank bounded carrier into a retained,
future-readable record of its causal formation, or does a supplied apparatus
merely create an ontology-specific terminal outcome pattern?

## Frozen bounded apparatus contract

The two arms share:

- a bounded apparatus worldtube \(W_A\);
- a ready carrier \(A=b\) at \(t_0\);
- a two-branch source \(S\) and fixed source--apparatus coupling during
  \([t_0,t_1]\);
- a nonzero retention interval \([t_1,\tau]\);
- one bounded readout coupling \(X_A\) at \(\tau\);
- one target-independent binary outcome quotient \(Z\);
- a separate downstream pointer \(B\), coupled only after \(\tau\), whose
  binary response in a fixed later window is escrowed as \(T\); and
- the same worldtube, horizon, action budget, response regions, and rival
  histories.

The ontology-native candidates differ only where their types require:

\[
Q_f=F\cap W_A\cap[\tau-\delta,\tau],
\]

a finite late-flash pattern, and

\[
Q_m=\operatorname{CG}_{\ell}\!\left(m|_{W_A,\tau}\right),
\]

a finite-resolution matter-density pattern. Supplied calibration maps
\(\zeta_f,\zeta_m\) may send these different native candidates to the same
operational outcome \(Z\). Equality of \(Z\) is operational quotient agreement,
not identity of the primitive ontologies.

Two provenance predicates remain distinct:

- \(P_W=1\): the declared blank-to-written source coupling occurred; and
- \(P_C=1\): at least one specified spontaneous collapse/flash occurred in the
  declared source interval.

The outcome \(Z\), neither provenance predicate, and the future response \(T\)
may be used to define one another.

## Hostile histories

The fixed rival set contains:

1. a genuine source-coupled write from the ready carrier;
2. a preloaded terminal pattern with no source-coupled write;
3. erase and rewrite to the same terminal accessible pattern;
4. relocation of provenance into support outside the observer boundary while
   preserving the bounded apparatus state; and
5. reset of the accessible carrier after a genuine occurrence.

A trusted ready-state certificate or append-only trace may defeat some rivals,
but it must then be charged as additional physical archive structure rather
than attributed to the primitive ontology alone.

## Decision contract

Test separately:

1. **outcome formation:** does the supplied apparatus dynamics make \(Z\)
   source-conditioned and persistent for the declared horizon?
2. **native retention:** is the retained carrier selected by the primitive
   ontology rather than by \((A,\zeta,\delta,\ell)\)?
3. **bounded access:** can \(X_A\) retrieve the candidate under the matched
   finite contract?
4. **write provenance:** is \(P_W\) constant on fibres of the complete bounded
   candidate record?
5. **collapse provenance:** is \(P_C\) constant on those fibres?

The scaffold's labels are not assumed mutually exclusive. Return a typed
tuple if conditional outcome formation coexists with provenance
non-identification:

```text
ENDOGENOUS_FORMED_PROVENANCE_ARCHIVE
PHYSICAL_OCCURRENCE_WITHOUT_RETAINED_ARCHIVE
TERMINAL_PATTERN_WITHOUT_OCCURRENCE_IDENTITY
ONTOLOGY_CONDITIONAL_FORMATION_ONLY
```

## Scope and controls

- **Primary lanes:** 1 and 3.
- **Supporting lanes:** 2, 4, and 7.
- **Channels:** formal proof, literature collision, and synthesis.
- **Completed dependency:** `HC-DU-080`.
- **Maximum return:** scoped Grade-4 provenance non-identification plus a
  conditional Grade-2 formation representation; no North-Star reconstruction
  or new prediction.
- **Strongest absorber:** the GRW outcome formalism already derives experiment
  statistics from supplied apparatus, calibration, and primitive-ontology
  histories; standard measurement, memory, and statistical-decision theory
  absorb the component mathematics.
- **Cheapest kill of ontology-native archive selection:** show that the
  experiment specification supplies the calibration/window/carrier, and that
  two lawful bounded histories can share the future-readable candidate while
  differing in \(P_W\) or \(P_C\).
- **Hardware:** unavailable and irrelevant.
- **Local model:** prohibited unless source and exact analysis leave one
  decision-changing finite question.
