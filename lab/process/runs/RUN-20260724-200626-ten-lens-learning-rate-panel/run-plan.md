# Ten-lens learning-rate panel

## Objective

Attach an efficient research approach to every hypothesis in the current
36-hypothesis technical portfolio. The panel does **not** decide which
hypotheses are worthwhile, eliminate hypotheses, revise the quadratic vote,
or authorize one hypothesis over another.

For each hypothesis, record:

1. the smallest informative object;
2. the highest-leverage proof, search, statistical, or experimental method;
3. the shared method kit or fixture to reuse;
4. the first decisive calculation or contrast;
5. the condition for escalating to a more expensive realization;
6. the useful artifact the work should emit; and
7. the characteristic brute-force or semantic-refit trap to avoid.

## Ten lenses

The five problem-solving specialists are:

1. category theorist;
2. counterfactual statistician;
3. formal counterexample engineer;
4. experimental metrology architect; and
5. research-portfolio and operations scientist.

They are joined by the five standing Science Council lenses:

6. orthodox professor;
7. heterodox professor;
8. commercial scientist;
9. wild-frontier scientist; and
10. philosopher of science.

The lenses are methodological advisers, not voters and not evidence.
