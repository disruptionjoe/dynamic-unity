## Factory contract applied

A Factory seed is a cheap publication-opportunity pointer, not a scientific
promotion or draft-ready packet. The audit therefore separates:

1. Factory seed readiness;
2. scientific/source hardening;
3. manuscript distance; and
4. merge, transfer, or non-standalone disposition.

A complete potential seed records:

- stable ID and working title;
- owning source and revision;
- durable evidence pointers;
- possible contribution and intended audience;
- current grade and open conditions;
- obvious overlap or merge candidates; and
- an unselected/no-hardening-request posture unless the Factory acts.
