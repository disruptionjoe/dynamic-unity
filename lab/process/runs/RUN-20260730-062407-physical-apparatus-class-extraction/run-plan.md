# PDSI-01 physical apparatus class extraction

## Cold-start contract

- **Purpose/North Star:** make physical reality intelligible by determining
  whether independently selected certified causal records reconstruct
  observer-accessible physics or leave a finite physical remainder.
- **Perspective/ontology:** methodological physical realism; observer indexing
  is physical access, not subjectivity; this audit chooses no record, field,
  direct-action, or apparatus ontology.
- **Active program/action:** `CCR-PHYSICAL-RECORD-INTERFACE-SELECTION` /
  `PDSI-01-PHYSICAL-APPARATUS-CLASS-EXTRACTION`.
- **Consumed evidence:** `HC-DU-150` requires conserved apparatus completion;
  `HC-DU-151` makes response orthogonality over the physically admitted
  completion class the exact descent condition.
- **Remaining obligation:** determine whether one primary-source GIE model
  selects that class or merely supplies a nominal apparatus.
- **Lanes/channels:** Lane 1 primary; Lanes 2, 3, 4, 6, and 7 supporting;
  `CH-COLLIDE`, `CH-FORMAL`, and `CH-SYN`.
- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 selection/nonselection. No Grade-5 result
  without a complete no-refit relational discriminator.

## Exact question

Does Christodoulou et al.'s linearized-gravity GIE model select a closed
controller-completion class and response domain sufficient to make
`HC-DU-151`'s orthogonality target well posed?

## Frozen ledger

Audit, without changing definitions:

1. matter paths and branch/source stress;
2. magnetic control and apparatus/support stress;
3. boundary conditions, gauge, propagator, and approximation order;
4. conservation completion and admitted apparatus variations;
5. heavy/homogeneous limits and their error or topology;
6. observer, preparation, readout, calibration, resources, and target; and
7. selected, assumed, calibrated, neglected, or out-of-scope status.

## Preregistered returns

```text
SELECTED_PHYSICAL_CLASS

```

- `SELECTED_PHYSICAL_CLASS` requires dynamics and boundary data to define a
  closed variation class with a norm/topology and response domain.
- `PARTIAL_PHYSICAL_TYPING` requires a nontrivial physically selected
  restriction whose remaining supplied coordinates are exactly named.
- `UNSELECTED_INTERFACE` fires if the source gives nominal trajectories or
  qualitative limits but no law-defined variation class.

## Controls

- **Strongest absorber:** the source may already include apparatus stress and
  a sufficient heavy/homogeneous approximation.
- **Cheapest kill:** the apparatus is introduced only existentially, while its
  stress, dynamics, variations, or approximation error remain unspecified.
- **Positive control:** distinguish a selected matter trajectory/branch class
  from the potentially unselected apparatus completion.
- **Stop:** do not simulate, construct apparatus twins, estimate a correction,
  activate Swing 2, or infer a published-phase problem when the class is not
  selected.
- **Local/hardware boundary:** primary-source reconstruction only. No local
  model or external hardware is warranted.

strongest absorber, maximum grade, and live-authority transition to Swing 2,
Swing 6, or `NO_READY_SUCCESSOR`.
