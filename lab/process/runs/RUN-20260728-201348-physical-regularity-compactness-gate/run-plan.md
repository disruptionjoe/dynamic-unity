# Physical regularity compactness gate

## Purpose and North-Star relation

`HC-DU-095` proves that a compact physical completion class turns a
target-separating continuum capability surface into a finite robust
certificate at every fixed target resolution.

This run tests whether ordinary physical regularity or energy estimates
supply that compactness premise:

> Does a physically warranted bounded solution class become compact in a
> topology strong enough for the held-out geometric target, and does the
> dynamics select that class or only transport antecedently bounded data?

## Frozen typed objects

Fix:

- a compact observer region or finite causal slab \(D\) on a declared
  \(d\)-dimensional manifold;
- a gauge-fixed metric/matter completion class
  \(\Theta\subset H^s(D)\);
- a weaker comparison topology \(H^{s'}\) or \(C^k\);
- a held-out target \(T\) declared continuous in that comparison topology;
- a finite time interval on which the physical evolution map exists;
- uniform Lorentzian signature/cone, volume-radius or noncollapse, boundary,
  source, and constraint controls; and
- the measurement-family and acquisition requirements already frozen by
  `HC-DU-095`.

Keep distinct:

1. boundedness in the controlling energy norm;
2. precompactness in a weaker topology;
3. compactness after gauge quotient;
4. compactness of each causal diamond;
5. compactness of a class of spacetimes;
6. local well-posedness and continuous dependence;
7. propagation of a compact initial-data set; and
8. physical selection of the initial-data class, gauge, topology, and target.

## Exact questions

1. Which regularity gap makes a bounded completion class precompact?
2. How many derivatives must remain for causal cones, connections,
   geodesics, and curvature targets?
3. What exact bounded same-topology counterexample blocks the naive energy
   argument?
4. Can metric and causal structure converge while curvature fails?
5. Does hyperbolic evolution create compactness or only preserve/transport
   compactness already present in initial data?
6. What noncollapse, gauge, topology, boundary, and finite-time premises
   remain supplied?
7. Does any audited physical arena select the full package required by
   `HC-DU-095`?

- `STANDARD`: Rellich compactness, Sobolev embedding, Fourier controls,
  continuous images of compact sets, and hyperbolic PDE well-posedness.
- `IMPORTED`: bounded-\(L^2\)-curvature Einstein existence results,
  Lorentzian/null-distance convergence frameworks, and Lorentzian
  Cheeger--Gromov-type compactness.
- `PROJECT_NATIVE`: target-relative completion fibres, physical interface
  selection, joint finite measurement packets, complete acquisition, and
  evidence grades.
- `CONDITIONAL_POSIT`: fixed compact domain/topology/gauge, uniform higher
  regularity, Lorentzian nondegeneracy, noncollapse, finite evolution time,
  target continuity in the compactness topology, and physical target
  separation.

Observer indexing remains a physical access/action relation. Compactness is a
property of the admitted physical class and topology, not evidence that
records are ontologically fundamental.

## Strongest absorbers

- Rellich--Kondrachov compactness and Sobolev embedding;
- Cheeger--Gromov and Lorentzian compactness/convergence theories;
- low-regularity Einstein well-posedness and energy estimates;
- inverse-problem compact model classes; and
- standard dynamical-systems facts about continuous evolution of compact
  initial sets.

## Cheapest positive and kills

Cheapest positive:

- prove that a uniformly \(H^s\)-bounded, closed metric/matter class on a
  fixed compact domain is precompact in \(H^{s'}\) for \(s'<s\), and in
  \(C^k\) when \(s>k+d/2\); compose this with `HC-DU-095` when the target is
  continuous in that weaker topology.

Cheapest kill of boundedness-as-compactness:

- use a normalized orthogonal Fourier sequence in the same \(H^s\) norm.

Cheapest geometric target failure:

- use \(\phi_n=n^{-2}\sin(nx)\): bounded in \(H^2\), convergent to zero in
  \(C^1\), but with nonconvergent second derivatives, so metric/cone data can
  settle while curvature-like targets do not.

## Stops

- do not infer compactness from bounded energy in the same topology;
- do not use weak or lower-topology compactness for a discontinuous target;
- do not claim coordinate compactness before fixing gauge or quotienting
  diffeomorphisms;
- do not infer a class-of-spacetimes compactness theorem from compact causal
  diamonds inside one spacetime;
- do not extend finite-time estimates through singularity or loss of
  hyperbolicity;
- do not credit evolution with selecting the initial-data, topology, source,
  boundary, or regularity class;
- do not build a local numerical model unless it changes the theorem
  boundary; and
- do not activate a successor without one arena that independently supplies
  the complete premise packet.
