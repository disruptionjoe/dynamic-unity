## Success, kill, and maximum grade

- **Positive:** one source-pinned apparatus physically selects formation,
  finite retention, or reset without the digital readout doing the selecting.
- **Cheapest kill of a complete packet:** distinct upstream source histories
  have overlapping retained-record laws, or the access/archive remains
  externally supplied.
- **Advance gate:** a later campaign wave activates only if the candidate also
  supplies an invariant, unabsorbed response-order question with a finite
  held-out consequence.
- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 for an exact target-factorization or
  selection boundary; Grade 2 for source reconstruction; no new-physics
  credit without empirical excess.
- **Strongest absorbers:** nucleation, metastability, detector response,
  signal detection, experimental acquisition, and measurement theory.
- **Local-model boundary:** no simulation. Primary sources and exact
  factorization decide the gate.
