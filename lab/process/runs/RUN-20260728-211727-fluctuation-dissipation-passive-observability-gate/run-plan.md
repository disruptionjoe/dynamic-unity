## Frozen typed objects

- a supplied observable algebra and dynamics \((\mathcal A,\alpha_t)\);
- an inverse temperature \(\beta\) and a \(\beta\)-KMS state \(\omega\);
- supplied readout \(A\) and conjugate perturbation \(B\);
- the passive two-time record
  \(C_{AB}(t)=\omega(\alpha_t(A)B)\);
- the retarded linear-response target
  \(\chi_{AB}(t)=(i/\hbar)\mathbf 1_{t\geq0}
  \omega([\alpha_t(A),B])\); and
- a finite observer worldtube, duration, bandwidth, detector, calibration,
  attempt lineage, and retained statistic.

### Exact finite control

Use the stationary overdamped equilibrium process

\[
dX_t=-\mu k X_t\,dt+\mu f(t)\,dt+
\sqrt{2\mu/\beta}\,dW_t,
\qquad
\beta,\mu,k>0.
\]

The passive record is the unforced stationary path law. The held-out target is
the impulse-response kernel to the independently typed force \(f\).

- with calibrated \(\beta\), show that two passive covariance values in the
  exact Langevin control reconstruct \(k,\mu\), and the complete held-out
  linear response.

Cheapest nonidentifiability kill:

  \((\beta,k,\mu)\mapsto(a\beta,k/a,a\mu)\); prove that the complete unforced
