# State-only record-functional gate

## Question

Can record-ness be a nontrivial representation-independent property of an
instantaneous physical state alone, or must every viable record ontology add
physically selected factorization, history, and access structure?

## Scope and controls

- **Owning lane:** Lane 1, with Lanes 2, 3, 4, and 7 supporting.
- **Channels:** formal, synthesis, and adversarial collision.
- **Maximum return:** a scoped necessity/no-go theorem and exact positive
  reopener. No new physics or ontology can be selected by this gate.
- **Strongest absorber:** orbit invariance, the spectral theorem,
  observable-induced tensor-product structure, SBS/strong Quantum Darwinism,
  consistent histories, and DU's existing provenance/access obstructions.
- **Cheapest kill:** one same-orbit record/no-record pair.
- **Local-computation disposition:** no model. A two-bit analytic witness
  decides the gate exactly.
- **Hardware disposition:** unavailable, unnecessary, and irrelevant.

Keep global representation invariance distinct from local physical symmetry
after a subsystem/algebra has been selected. Do not infer that records are
unreal or that a global quantum state is incomplete.

## Required outputs

1. one inline whole-repository opportunity comparison;
2. one general orbit-separation criterion;
3. one exact quantum same-spectrum record/no-record witness;
4. one unchanged classical control;
5. one minimum typed record-data ladder and exact reopener;
6. one live-state transition; and
7. validation, commit, push, and repository-session completion.

## Stop

Stop after the state-only claim is either separated on the orbit quotient or
refuted by one mixed record/nonrecord orbit. Do not build another record host.
