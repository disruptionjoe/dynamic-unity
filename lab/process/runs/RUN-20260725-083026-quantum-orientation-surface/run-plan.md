# Quantum-foundations orientation and cheap-test routing

## Objective

Create a thin navigation surface that lets a research lead determine:

1. what kind of scientific object is being compared;
2. which assumptions and epistemic statuses must remain distinct;
3. which known theorem or framework sharpens the discrepancy;
4. the cheapest informative test already available in Dynamic Unity; and
5. which canonical ledger receives any resulting hypothesis, claim, concept,
   prediction, or lane change.

The surface must preserve, rather than collapse, the distinctions among
ontological-model taxonomies, operational theories, no-go theorems,
interpretations, causal frameworks, emergence mechanisms, and physical
extensions.

## Work packages

### A. Spekkens and ontological-model passport

- Record the Harrigan--Spekkens `psi`-ontic / `psi`-epistemic distinction in
  its distribution-over-ontic-state form.
- Keep `psi`-complete / `psi`-supplemented on its separate axis.
- Mark approaches without the required observer-independent ontic state
  variable as outside, rather than falsely placed inside, this taxonomy.
- Attach PBR and generalized contextuality as conditional no-go tools with
  their assumptions exposed.

### B. Non-flattened discrepancy map

- Separate operational expressiveness, ontology, causal explanation,
  contextual identity, temporal process, objectivity/finality, relativistic
  measurement, and geometry/gravity interfaces.
- For each discrepancy family, state what is established, what remains open,
  the cheapest first test, and what result would justify escalation.
- Preserve both restrictive and permissive foils.

### C. Dynamic Unity tool router

- Point to current contracts, exact probes, benchmark suites, concept and
  prediction registers, paper portfolio, and lane state.
- State what each tool can and cannot establish.
- Reuse the ten-lens efficient-approach kits rather than create a rival method
  registry.

### D. Prioritization integration

- Add one constitutional pointer requiring the orientation surface to be read
  before central quantum/foundations reprioritization.
- Make clear that the surface routes work but has no claim, lane, or ranking
  authority.
- Add a discoverability pointer without making the surface an authoritative
  state ledger.

## Stop conditions

- Do not claim that PBR excludes every epistemic interpretation or that
  `psi`-ontology classifies every interpretation.
- Do not use a no-go theorem outside its frozen assumptions.
- Do not equate operational equivalence, ontological identity, public
  finality, and physical fundamentality.
- Do not turn the orientation surface into a scalar ranking, replacement
  ledger, or exhaustive encyclopedia.
- Promote no hypothesis, claim, prediction, paper, ontology, or lane priority
  merely because it appears on the surface.
