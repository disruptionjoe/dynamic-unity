# Operational duality and no-priority transfer

## Question

When a physical presentation and a certified-record presentation carry
exactly the same frozen action/decision content, can any experiment internal
to that contract decide whether physics is ontologically prior to records or
records are ontologically prior to physics?

The swing will state the answer once and transfer it unchanged across:

1. a stabilizer/QEC specimen;
2. an authenticated distributed-record specimen; and
3. a deliberately source-unbound same-alphabet negative control.

## Why this is live

Dynamic Unity's North Star admits record-first reconstruction, operational
duality, and a physics-first remainder. The repository has earned scoped
instances of all three, but has not banked the cross-platform no-priority
theorem named in approach family `AF-14`.

This is now locally decidable because the repository has:

- exact target/action-indexed factorization and deficiency;
- formed quantum and material record specimens;
- authenticated distributed provenance and handoff controls;
- complete capability-record closure and formation profiles; and
- explicit first leaks under enlarged actions.

## Frozen theorem schema

Let \(H\) be a finite history class. Let:

- \(P:H\to X\) be a physical operational presentation;
- \(R:H\to Q\) be a certified-record presentation;
- \(A\) be a frozen family of actions/decisions; and
- \(B_A:H\to Y_A\) be their complete response vector.

The deterministic duality condition is

\[
\ker P=\ker R=\ker B_A.
\]

The stochastic version replaces equality of kernels by mutual
target-independent Blackwell garbling between the physical and record
experiments.

## Pre-registered targets

### T1 — deterministic operational-duality theorem

Prove that equal kernels make the images of \(P\) and \(R\) canonically
isomorphic quotients of \(H\). Every target factors through one presentation
if and only if it factors through the other.

### T2 — stochastic no-priority theorem

Prove that mutually Blackwell-sufficient presentations have identical
achievable risk sets for every downstream bounded decision problem in the
frozen contract. Therefore no such decision can identify ontological
priority.

### T3 — first-leak theorem

For an enlarged action family \(A'\), duality survives exactly when the new
response vector still factors through the old record. Return the smallest
same-record/different-response witness when it fails.

### T4 — unchanged cross-platform transfer

Use the same history/presentation/record/action schema for:

- correctable stabilizer errors modulo the declared code symmetry;
- authenticated public execution with hidden physical formation mode; and
- a same-alphabet preloaded record unbound from the target.

Do not rename formation, authentication, reconstruction, or selection into
one another to force transfer.

### T5 — North-Star and portfolio disposition

Decide whether the result:

- earns the operational-duality branch as a cross-platform theorem;
- answers the record-first versus physics-first ontological question;
- changes the missing physical premise; or
- activates a scientific successor.
