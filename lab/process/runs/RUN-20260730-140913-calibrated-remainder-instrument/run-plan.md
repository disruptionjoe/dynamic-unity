## Frozen question

> In a completely known finite world, is the reconstruction remainder exactly
> the causally downstream-only structure, or is it the structure left
> nonconstant on the fibres of the frozen action--observation signature?

The finite world contains five independently variable components:

1. a visible render bit;
2. a near collision bit;
3. a remote, action-modifiable collision bit;
4. an NPC navigation-policy bit; and
5. an authority/reconciliation bit.

The action envelopes are nested. They begin with a near collision query and
add visual inspection, NPC observation, remote interaction, and authority
conflict in that order.

## Pre-registered propositions

1. **Exact calibration theorem.** For finite world class \(W\), frozen
   experiment family \(A\), signature \(S_A\), and target \(T\), exact
   reconstruction is equivalent to
   \(\ker S_A\subseteq\ker T\). Because source truth is known, every fibre and
   every target-changing twin can be enumerated rather than inferred.
2. **Monotonicity.** If \(A\subseteq B\), then the \(B\)-partition refines the
   \(A\)-partition. Action or observation enlargement can shrink the
   structural remainder; repeating an unchanged deterministic query cannot.
3. **Downstream-only kill.** Causal direction is neither necessary nor
   sufficient for remainder membership:
   - a downstream-only render bit is reconstructible when visual observation
     belongs to the certified record; and
   - an action-modifiable remote collision bit remains unreconstructible while
     the remote interaction lies outside the admitted action envelope.
4. **Dual-mesh control.** Authenticating the render bit cannot identify an
   independently variable collision bit.
5. **Transfer boundary.** The calibration arena may validate DU's remainder
   measurement method. It may not transfer its ontology, component labels,
   action set, or causal architecture to physics.

## Strongest absorber, cheapest kill, and stop

- **Strongest absorber:** finite sufficient statistics, controlled-system
  observability, automata minimization/Myhill--Nerode equivalence,
  probabilistic bisimulation, POMDP belief-state identification, and active
  system identification.
- **Cheapest kill of the downstream-only conjecture:** one downstream-only
  coordinate that is constant on every current record fibre, or one
  action-coupled coordinate that varies inside a current record fibre.
- **Positive control:** the full five-action envelope reconstructs all 32
  worlds exactly.
- **Negative control:** repeating the near-collision query preserves the
  original 16-element fibres.
- **Stop:** after the theorem, exact controls, calibration curve, absorber
  placement, and physical-transfer boundary. Do not infer that nature has a
  render mesh, collision mesh, server, preferred frame, or hidden
  implementation.
