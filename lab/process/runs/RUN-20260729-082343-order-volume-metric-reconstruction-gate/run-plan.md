## Cheapest constructive positive

For \(g=\Omega^2\bar g\) in four dimensions,

\[
\frac{d\mu}{d\mathrm{vol}_{\bar g}}
=
\Omega^4.
\]

Therefore

\[
\Omega
=
\left(
\frac{d\mu}{d\mathrm{vol}_{\bar g}}
\right)^{1/4}
\]

is unique and positive. In the finite piecewise-constant arena,

\[
V_j=\Omega_j^4\bar V_j,
\qquad
\Omega_j=\left(\frac{V_j}{\bar V_j}\right)^{1/4}.
\]
