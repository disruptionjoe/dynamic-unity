# Must a dormant type leave a detectable physical footprint?

## Question

`HC-DU-088` leaves two local source-sensitive routes before the full
completion theorem:

1. a preactivation intervention that distinguishes dormant presence from
   absence; or
2. a preactivation physical footprint in storage, energy, stress, latency, or
   another conserved/resource variable.

Does either route supply a general issuance discriminator, or only a
model-relative test after an action, carrier, compression, region, time,
reliability, and source boundary are fixed?

## Frozen contract

- **Primary Lane:** 1, supported by 3, 4, 6, and 7.
- **Channels:** formal, literature collision, synthesis, and empirical
  discriminator design.
- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 operational-preexistence and resource-
  premise necessity; conditional physical bounds retain their original
  warrant. No new thermodynamic law, universal per-type cost, source issuance,
  or new physics.
- **Target:** whether type \(\tau\) lies in the preactivation fixed physical
  source/generator closure.
- **Complete preactivation signature:** every admitted intervention response
  plus declared storage, energy/stress, spatial-support, reliability, latency,
  and access measurement before activation.
- **Strongest absorbers:** `HC-DU-056` response-equivalence, compact generators,
  algorithmic compression, degenerate memory states, reversible computation,
  completed history, hidden/external source relocation, and unrestricted
  activation time.
- **Cheapest kill:** exhibit dormant-fixed and absent/issuance models with the
  same complete admitted preactivation response/resource signature.
- **Positive control:** a model requiring \(N\) independently addressable
  reliable preactivation states in a fixed region and deadline, so ordinary
  capacity or quantum-speed bounds can exclude part of its parameter space.
- **Local-model boundary:** exact factorization, one degenerate-memory control,
  and primary-source bounds decide the gate. No simulation or hardware.

## Method

1. Specialize `HC-DU-056` to the preactivation action family and state exactly
   what a separating intervention proves.
2. Separate static storage, logical erasure, state capacity, and processing
   speed.
3. Test the proposed per-type Landauer cost against reversible and degenerate
   controls.
4. Apply finite-region entropy/capacity and finite-deadline speed bounds only
   to reservoir models satisfying their antecedents.
5. Construct the compact-generator and matched-footprint absorbers.
6. State the minimum premises under which a measured footprint can kill a
   declared dormant-reservoir model.
7. Return the corrected issuance reopener and stop.

## Stop

Stop when the complete preactivation signature either separates the source
classes or one matched pair proves nonidentifiability. Do not infer a universal
energy cost from type count, apply Landauer to passive storage, treat a
finite-capacity bound as a selector of coding, or let a supplied action family
define the target after the result.
