Define `E_sel` as the least equivalence relation containing

```text

```

declared symmetry orbits and unchanged by every permitted post-write

observer `i`. Freeze an authorized coalition family `Q`.

  on `join_{S in Q} meet(S)`;

  `X / join_{S in Q} meet(S)`.

These are finite set/partition results. They do not by themselves select the
physical `G`, `K`, access family, readout cut, scalar-label convention, or
