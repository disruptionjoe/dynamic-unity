# Result

`HC-DU-230` answers the explicit escape left by `HC-DU-229`. Causal nonlinear
quantum theories are a nonempty class: Kaplan--Rajendran provide a
field-theoretic, full-state/history-dependent nonlinear law with an additive
multi-system prescription. This is a real change of theory, not the rejected
state-only cutoff.

The class exit does not solve the North Star. Kaplan--Rajendran define a
measurement only after an observable, stable apparatus state, engineered
interaction, and desired pointer family are chosen, and their nonlinear
apparatus is generally noisy. Fiorentino--Weigert keep ordinary states,
unitaries, composition, observables, and Born probabilities fixed and show that
multiple generalized update rules survive their six core consistency and
no-signalling conditions. Lüders becomes unique only after extra coherence and
composition-compatibility assumptions.

Thus complete evolution, no-signalling update, selected measurement interface,
realized outcome, retained material archive, provenance, and observer access
remain different achievements. Neither framework derives the low-amplitude
offset, its `c`, or a complete all-attempt detector law.
