# Purpose and North Star

Dynamic Unity seeks the target-blind physical antecedent, if any, that selects
a material, provenance-bearing, observer-indexed record interface, and then
asks what that record reconstructs or fails to reconstruct. This run tests the
strongest explicit escape left by `HC-DU-229`: a complete causal nonlinear
quantum theory rather than a nonlinear state-only detector response.

# Evidence boundary

- **Current state:** revision 179 is explicit `no_ready` quiescence. This is a
  bounded reopener audit, not a preselected successor.
- **Consumed evidence:** `HC-DU-229` proves that the simple cutoff cannot be a
  fixed density-state response compatible with ordinary mixing or a
  branchwise response compatible with Bell steering.
- **Question:** do existing causal nonlinear or generalized-update frameworks
  provide the missing process/composition/locality law, and do they also
  select the measurement and record interface needed by the North Star?
- **Perspective/status:** state, law, measurement observable, apparatus,
  outcome, archive, provenance, and observer access remain separately typed.
- **Formation:** a mathematically complete update, a causal evolution, a
  measurement interaction, and a formed accessible record are not synonyms.
- **Lane/channel:** Lane 1, supported by Lanes 3 and 7; `CH-FORMAL` and
  `CH-COLLIDE`.
- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 for a necessity/nonimplication boundary.
- **Strongest absorbers:** Kaplan--Rajendran causal nonlinear QFT,
  Fiorentino--Weigert generalized update rules, Gisin--Polchinski signalling,
  quantum instruments, and relativistic covariance/integrability analysis.
- **Cheapest kill:** the candidate framework leaves the nonlinear coupling,
  full-history boundary data, observable, apparatus interaction, pointer
  states, or update rule supplied, and does not derive the cutoff.
- **Dependencies:** internal prior results are complete; primary literature is
  nonblocking. No sibling theorem is imported.
- **Local-model disposition:** `DESK_RESEARCH_FIRST`. The source papers and a
  direct typed audit answer the question at the available grade; a simulation
  would only replay supplied equations.
- **Hardware:** unavailable, unnecessary, and not approached.

# Tests

1. Reconstruct the nonlinear state, history/boundary, composition, locality,
   and measurement commitments in Kaplan--Rajendran.
2. Reconstruct which axioms Fiorentino--Weigert hold fixed and which update
   structures remain nonunique.
3. Separate causality, no-signalling, relativistic covariance, foliation
   independence, and measurement consistency.
4. Mark every record-interface component as selected, derived, supplied, or
   absent.
5. Test whether either framework derives the low-amplitude offset, its value,
   its apparatus, or its complete all-attempt response.
6. Decide whether `PRED-DU-006`, a new law, paper, successor, model, or hardware
   action is earned.
