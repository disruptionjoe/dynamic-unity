# Evolutionary selection-level typing

## Question

Can physical evolutionary dynamics select predictive record/interface
architectures even though predictive value alone cannot select a carrier, and
does that support the stronger claim that physical laws evolved toward
forecasting capacity or source issuance?

## Frozen contract

- **Lane:** 1, supported by 2, 3, 4, and 7.
- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 attribution/necessity theorem; no new
  evolutionary law or physics.
- **Strongest absorbers:** evolutionary value of information, replicator and
  multitype branching dynamics, Blackwell comparison of experiments,
  `HC-DU-033F`, and `HC-DU-083`.
- **Cheapest kill:** predictive information has selection value only through
  a supplied physical growth or persistence consequence; law selection is
  undefined without an outer population and inheritance dynamics.
- **Local-model boundary:** exact ratio dynamics and counterexamples decide
  the gate; do not build a simulation.
- **Hardware:** unavailable and irrelevant.

## Method

1. Type source-law, organism/interface, population/ecology, and law-ensemble
   levels separately.
2. Prove the minimum multiplicative interface-selection result.
3. Test whether predictive information is necessary or sufficient for that
   selection.
4. Reapply the `HC-DU-083` symmetry obstruction at the evolutionary level.
5. State the extra objects needed to make selection among physical laws
   meaningful.
6. Test whether an outer selection process earns source issuance or merely
   relocates fixed-law disclosure.

## Stop

Stop without simulation when the exact ratio law and typed counterexamples
settle the attribution. Do not turn fitness into a fundamental objective,
population selection into occurrence provenance, or a fixed meta-law into
source issuance.
