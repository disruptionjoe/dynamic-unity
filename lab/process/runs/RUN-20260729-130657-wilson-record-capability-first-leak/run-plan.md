## Frozen physical and record arena

Retain the complete `HC-DU-121` contract:

1. \(G\) is a nontrivial finite Abelian group;
2. \(n\ge2\) dressed links form one oriented cycle;
3. regular-representation matter makes the physical orbit basis
   \(\mathbb C[G]^{\otimes n}\);
4. the already formed record is \(q=Q(y)=\sum_i y_i\);
5. no local link value or QND ancilla transcript is added after seeing the
   held-out target; and
6. a capability is a physically admitted family of charged-probe responses.

For an integer path coefficient vector \(c=(c_1,\ldots,c_n)\), define

\[
L_c(y)=\sum_i c_i y_i\in G.
\]

The operational response family is

\[
\mathcal R_c(y)
=
\{\chi(L_c(y)):\chi\in\widehat G\}.
\]

Characters separate finite-Abelian group elements, so reconstructing
\(\mathcal R_c\) is equivalent to reconstructing \(L_c\).

Physical cases include:

- a loop winding \(k\) times: \(c=(k,\ldots,k)\);
- the once-around Wilson response: \(c=(1,\ldots,1)\); and
- a proper open path with matter-saturated endpoints:
  \(c_i\in\{0,1\}\), with at least one included and one excluded link.

## Exact theorem target

Let \([m]_G:G\to G\) denote multiplication by integer \(m\).

The proposed factorization criterion is:

\[
\mathcal R_c
\text{ factors through }Q
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
[c_1]_G=\cdots=[c_n]_G.
\]

If the endomorphisms agree, \(L_c(y)=[c_1]_G(Q(y))\). Conversely, if
\(L_c=f\circ Q\), evaluating configurations supported on one link forces
\(f=[c_i]_G\) for every \(i\).

Consequences:

1. every closed-loop winding response is exactly reconstructed from \(q\);
2. every proper simple open path fails for every nontrivial \(G\);
3. the smallest witness uses two link changes:

   \[
   y=(0,\ldots,0),\qquad
   y'_i=a,\quad y'_j=-a
   \]

   for nonzero \(a\), with \(i\) inside and \(j\) outside the path;
4. \(Q(y)=Q(y')\), while some character separates the open-path responses;
5. every Wilson-record fibre contains \(|G|^{n-1}\) physical basis
   configurations; and
6. reconstructing the full dressed-link basis from \(q\) needs at least
   \(n-1\) additional \(G\)-valued record coordinates, and \(n-1\) dressed
   links attain the cardinality bound.

The last item is an informational repair theorem. Measuring the added
coordinates may destroy within-Wilson-sector coherence; it is not claimed as
an unchanged nondemolition instrument.

## Cheapest kills

1. Two physical states with the same \(q\) give different closed-loop winding
   character responses.
2. A proper open-path response is constant on every \(Q\)-fibre.
3. The endomorphism criterion fails for a finite product group such as
   \(\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2\).
4. A one-link rather than two-link change preserves \(Q\) and separates a
   response.
5. Fewer than \(n-1\) additional \(G\)-valued coordinates injectively label
   a fibre of size \(|G|^{n-1}\).
6. Matter-saturated open Wilson lines are not gauge-invariant admitted
   operations in the frozen arena.
7. The held-out response or record is changed after seeing the result.
