# Stabilizer syndrome record duality and logical remainder

## Objective

Freeze one complete error-correction process whose record algebra is selected
independently of the held-out target by a supplied stabilizer-code
architecture. Determine whether:

1. the complete formed syndrome archive is operationally dual to the
   correction-relevant physical quotient;
2. a protected logical distinction remains inside each complete syndrome
   fibre;
3. that distinction survives every code-internal classical record refinement
   that preserves the full encoded logical action algebra; and
4. the gauge-boundary and QEC anchors share one exact quotient/kernel
   structure without implying physical identity.

This is not the earlier noisy-record fixture in which an omitted error
syndrome repaired insufficiency. Here the full generator-invariant syndrome,
check provenance, archive, and correction record are present from the start.

## Frozen complete process

Use a finite \([[n,k]]\) stabilizer code with \(k>0\):

1. prepare an arbitrary encoded state, including an external reference;
2. apply one Pauli error from a frozen finite error class;
3. nondestructively measure the complete stabilizer character with ancillas;
4. archive every check identity, round, outcome, route, reset, and decoder
   version;
5. apply one correction depending only on that frozen record; and
6. expose one held-out logical action or observable.

The code, stabilizer group, local check family, error class, physical
system/pointer/archive boundary, measurement coupling, decoder, and future
action class are premises. Individual stabilizer-generator labels are
representation choices; the joint character/eigenspace record is the
generator-invariant object.

## Strongest admissible record class

The candidate class contains every classical record formed by the complete
stabilizer-check process and every target-independent code-internal
nondemolition refinement that:

- preserves arbitrary encoded states and their entanglement with a reference;
- preserves the full logical action algebra;
- retains every selective route, invalid attempt, reset, provenance, and
  decoder field already present in the process; and
- does not add a target-coded logical measurement or an external source log
  absent from the frozen process.

An external noise-source/controller log is a charged boundary enlargement. A
logical measurement that removes a conjugate logical capability is action
contract retyping, not ordinary refinement.

## The theorem package to prove or kill

1. **Syndrome quotient.** Modulo Pauli phases, the syndrome map is a
   homomorphism whose kernel is the Pauli normalizer/centralizer
   \(N(S)\). Hence the complete syndrome is exactly the quotient
   \(\mathcal P_n/N(S)\).
2. **Correctability criterion.** A frozen Pauli error class is exactly
   correctable from syndrome iff every same-syndrome error pair differs by a
   stabilizer, equivalently iff its pair products satisfy the
   Knill--Laflamme condition.
3. **Logical remainder.** For the unrestricted Pauli class, the residual
   same-syndrome ambiguity modulo physically trivial stabilizers is
   \(N(S)/S\), the logical Pauli group, of binary symplectic dimension \(2k\).
4. **Decoder no-go.** No one syndrome-only decoder corrects both \(E\) and
   \(EL\) for a nontrivial logical Pauli \(L\); their residuals differ by
   \(L\).
5. **Refinement boundary.** No classical code-internal refinement can
   distinguish that logical quotient while preserving the full logical
   algebra and arbitrary reference entanglement. Closing it requires a
   source/controller record, a logical measurement, a restricted logical
   task, or another boundary/action/resource change.
6. **Homological lift.** In a surface/toric-code specimen, local syndrome is
   the boundary of an error chain and the protected remainder is its homology
   class. A noncontractible closed loop has zero local syndrome and nontrivial
   logical action.
7. **Two-anchor exact sequence.** State, without novelty inflation, the common
   DU form: a physically supplied record homomorphism reconstructs its
   quotient, while \(\ker r/G\) is the exact action-relative remainder after
   declared gauge/trivial transformations \(G\) are removed.
