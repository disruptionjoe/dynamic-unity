## Required clarification

- Observer indexing denotes a physical relation or operational role, not a
  synonym for subjectivity or belief.
- First-person/local and third-person/public/global are scope terms; ontic and
  epistemic are status terms. They do not determine one another.
- A public final record is an action-qualified cross-perspective fact, not by
  itself the global ontology.
- Beginning scientific reconstruction from local records is an epistemic
  method, not proof of their ontological priority.
- Dynamic Unity adopts methodological physical realism while leaving the
  completeness and ontological status of the quantum state, global valuation,
  locality/separability, foliation, and genuine issuance open.
- “Records create reality” is prohibited shorthand unless a candidate names
  the physical carrier, formation map, rival disclosure model, and finite
  excess consequence.
