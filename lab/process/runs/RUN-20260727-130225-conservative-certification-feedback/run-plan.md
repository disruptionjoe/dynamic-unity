# Conservative certification and physical feedback formal spine

## Objective

Determine whether a certificate has any additional physical effect after the
complete physical boundary process by which it controls a response system is
fixed, and separately determine when a declared action label is a sufficient
quotient of that complete boundary.

The result must distinguish:

1. a semantic certificate verdict;
2. the physical carrier, verifier, controller, policy, and complete boundary
   input through which the verdict changes action;
3. a declared control label that may erase physical implementation detail;
4. the response process and every retained memory, route, timing, reset, and
   occurrence field; and
5. capability change caused by ordinary physical feedback from a purported
   certificate-only force.

## Frozen typed object

Let:

- \(X\) be the complete admitted pre-response state/history of the response
  system and environment;
- \(R\) be the physically formed source record;
- \(K\) be the complete certificate carrier and verifier/controller state;
- \(C=\gamma(R,K)\) be the certificate verdict or semantic status;
- \(P\) be the policy mapping verdicts to physical action families;
- \(B\) be the complete physical boundary input from the certificate/control
  apparatus into the response system, including every selected operation and
  side channel;
- \(U=q(B)\) be the declared control label, possibly a lossy quotient;
- \(Y\) be the later response; and
- \(L\) be the complete resource/reset ledger.

The conservative candidate is

\[
Y\perp C\mid X,B.
\]

The stronger declared-control candidate is

\[
Y\perp B\mid X,U.
\]

The second is not assumed. A same-\(U\) witness may hide controller memory,
carrier phase, route, timing, hysteresis, reset state, or another physical
boundary variable.

## The theorem package to prove or kill

1. **Deterministic semantic inertness.** Once \(X\) and complete physical
   boundary \(B\) are fixed, a semantic relabeling of the same physical
   realization cannot change \(Y\) without adding \(C\) as a new physical
   input, changing the dynamics, or mismatching occurrences.
2. **Deterministic quotient criterion.** For response
   \(F:X\times B\to Y\), a decoder
   \(\bar F:X\times U\to Y\) with
   \(F=\bar F\circ(\mathrm{id}_X,q)\) exists exactly when
   \(\ker(\mathrm{id}_X,q)\subseteq\ker F\).
3. **Stochastic postprocessing criterion.** For a physical response kernel
   \(M(y\mid x,b)\) and declared-control channel \(Q(u\mid b)\), declared
   control is sufficient exactly when a stochastic decoder
   \(T(y\mid x,u)\) exists with
   \(M_x=QT_x\). A signed linear solution is not a physical decoder;
   positivity and normalization are load-bearing.
4. **Quantum process criterion.** For a frozen process tensor or quantum
   comb \(\mathcal W\), semantic labels attached to one identical complete
   inserted instrument have identical outputs. A declared control class is
   sufficient exactly when every within-class instrument difference lies in
   the kernel of the process link map.
5. **Capability without source change.** A certificate may strictly change
   safe action capability through policy and physical control while leaving
   the source law unchanged. This does not imply a new force.
6. **Matched ordering control.** Every conclusion must remain unchanged when
   the preferred-leaf representation is replaced by a causal-partial-order
   representation preserving the complete process and forgetting privileged
   simultaneity.
