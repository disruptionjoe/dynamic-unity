# QRF outcome-broadcast and layered-finality gate

## Cold-start contract

- **Purpose/North Star:** determine whether independently selected,
  observer-indexed certified causal records reconstruct observer-accessible
  physics or leave a finite physical remainder.
- **Perspective/ontology:** methodological physical realism. Outcome
  registers, spatial position, communication carriers, and leaked
  which-branch information are physical degrees of freedom; observer
  indexing is not subjective belief.
- **Current authority:** `CURRENT-RESEARCH.yaml` revision 106 is quiescent
  after `HC-DU-153`; no scientific successor is selected.
- **Trigger:** Vanrietvelde's July 2026 preprint gives a concrete protocol in
  which a position-superposed lab broadcasts a definite internal outcome to
  a well-localized receiver without decohering the lab's position.
- **Lanes/channels:** Lane 1 primary; Lanes 3, 4, 5, and 7 supporting;
  `CH-COLLIDE`, `CH-FORMAL`, `CH-MODEL`, and `CH-SYN`.
- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 necessity/nonimplication and operational
  record-layer boundary. No Grade-5 remainder, prediction, or new physics
  without an independently selected measurement/interface packet and a
  no-refit discriminator beyond standard quantum theory.

## Exact question

Does outcome broadcast from a position-superposed laboratory imply that:

1. the laboratory's position must decohere;
2. one scalar finality status applies to the whole laboratory;
3. the physical measurement and record interface has been selected; or
4. only information retained about the reference branch—not outcome
   information itself—controls the reference coherence?

## Controls

- **Strongest absorber:** ordinary unitary quantum mechanics,
  which-path/visibility complementarity, quantum erasure, decoherence, and
  QRF operational semantics.
- **Cheapest kill of global classicalization:** one exact unitary protocol
  copies a readable outcome while returning the external message to a product
  state with the reference position.
- **Positive decoherence control:** retain a copy of the temporary branch tag
  in an inaccessible register and require position coherence not to return.
- **Partial-leak control:** use message overlap \(3/5\) and require pure
  two-path visibility \(3/5\), distinguishability \(4/5\), and
  \(V^2+D^2=1\).
- **Anti-conflation:** a definite internal outcome is assumed after a
  Heisenberg cut. The protocol does not derive actualization or solve
  Wigner's-friend scenarios.
- **Stop:** do not infer global finality, new collapse dynamics, selected
  observers, regional BFT consensus, or empirical excess from an
  outcome-only broadcast.
