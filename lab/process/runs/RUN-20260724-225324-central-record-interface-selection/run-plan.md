# Central record-interface selection swing

## Objective

Attack the first open dependency in Certified Causal Reality directly:
classify when independently frozen finite dynamics selects a nontrivial
classical record interface, rather than merely allowing a basis or archive
chosen by the modeler.

The primary class is a finite abelian random-unitary channel with an explicit
Stinespring branch environment. The run separates two questions that prior
fixtures often conflated:

1. **stability:** which observables are fixed by the channel; and
2. **formation/access:** which stable classical sector labels are actually
   distinguishable under the declared complementary-environment operation
   algebra.

## Frozen theorem target

For a random-unitary channel

\[
\Phi^*(A)=\sum_g w_g U_g^\dagger A U_g
\]

whose positive-weight support generates a finite abelian representation:

- the fixed algebra is the commutant of the represented unitaries;
- its canonical classical part is the center, with one projector per
  represented character sector;
- for the uniform full-group twirl, character orthogonality makes the
  complementary environment states of distinct sectors orthogonal;
- multiplicity degrees of freedom inside one sector remain quantum and no
  finer basis is selected by the channel alone.

The theorem is accepted only as a scoped specialization. A broad novelty
claim fails if established fixed-algebra, noiseless-subsystem, pointer,
decoherence, or no-broadcasting results absorb its components.

## Hostile controls

- identity channel: a large fixed algebra does not imply a classical record;
- deterministic unitary: a nontrivial fixed abelian algebra can exist while
  the environment forms no distinguishable record;
- biased twirl: the same exact fixed center can coexist with only partial
  environment distinguishability;
- degenerate character sector: the environment records only the central
  sector, while internal bases remain unselected;
- rational coordinate conjugation: the selected algebra must transform
  naturally rather than privilege the computational basis;
- arbitrarily small noncommuting branch: exact central selection may collapse
  discontinuously, exposing the need for an approximate robust successor;
- matched noncommuting Pauli channels: hold exact fixed-algebra type and
  finite-time \(Z\)-signal constant while varying complementary-environment
  record quality;
- environment-basis gauge: record distinguishability must depend on the
  complementary-channel Gram matrix, not a chosen branch labeling.
- access-algebra switch: coherent Walsh decoding must recover the uniform
  sector code, while branch-basis dephasing before decoding must erase it.

## Falsifiers

The scoped theorem fails if any positive-weight finite abelian fixture has a
fixed-space dimension different from its representation commutant, if the
uniform full-group environment fails character orthogonality, if a block
rotation changes a central projector, or if the classifications change under
declared coordinate/environment gauge transformations.

The larger physical program remains open even if the theorem passes. The
channel, group action, and system/environment split are independently frozen
inputs, not derived laws of nature.
