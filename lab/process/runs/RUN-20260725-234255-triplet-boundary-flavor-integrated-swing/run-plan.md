# Triplet--boundary--flavor integrated swing

## Purpose

triplet carrier to:

1. a physically meaningful chiral or low-energy selection;
2. a distinction between fundamental-three and effective-three models; and
3. predictive flavor differentiation rather than an unrestricted fit.

Dynamic Unity owns the typed integration, completion controls, finite proxy,
and operational classification. `gu-formalization` retains authority over the
actual GU carrier, source action, operator/domain, index, chirality, and
generation verdict.

## Starting facts and corrections

- GU conditionally supplies a canonical triplet carrier but currently has
  vectorlike `3E_+ + 3E_-` structure.
- The physical source action and lawful integer spectral pairing remain
  undefined.
- Prior DU work already killed spectral flow as a mode-mint: a fixed family
  can disclose an index without creating new degrees of freedom.

  defective. It can explain a count when independently derived; the relevant
  failure is target import, not mere stage-zero fixedness.
- Domain-wall and anomaly-inflow mechanisms already establish that chiral
  boundary modes can arise from vectorlike or higher-dimensional structures.
  That mechanism alone is not a DU novelty claim.

## Counterfactual tournament

The swing must keep four rivals distinct:

1. `FUNDAMENTAL_THREE`: three is a protected global chiral index;
2. `EFFECTIVE_THREE`: a vectorlike or larger completion has three modes under
   the declared low-energy/access boundary;
3. `TARGET_CODED_THREE`: a flux, defect, rectangularity, projector, rank, or
   threshold was chosen to return three;
4. `CARRIER_ONLY`: the triplet exists, but chirality and flavor are
   unselected.
