# Certified Causal Reconstruction formal spine

## Objective

Prove, absorb, or refute the smallest integrated theorem that can serve as
the shared spine for the next Dynamic Unity campaign swings. Freeze a minimal
kernel and instantiate it without semantic refitting on:

1. one finite multi-time quantum-instrument anchor; and
2. one authenticated adversarial distributed-process anchor.

The swing must distinguish a common theorem from a vocabulary bridge. It must
also freeze the exact prerequisites that later physical formation, regional
finality, capability, and geometry work must inherit.

## Minimal kernel under test

Freeze:

- admitted histories or completions \(H\);
- intervention slots and actions \(A\);
- every acquisition/selection stratum \(S\);
- response or complete selective-continuation target \(Y\);
- independently declared candidate record kernel \(R\);
- representation/equivalence group \(G\);
- target-independent admissible refinement class \(\mathcal C\);
- resource partial order;
- reset/causal-break scope; and
- held-out target family \(T\).

The candidate normal form asks whether complete stratum-and-response behavior
factors through \(R\), whether \(\ker R\) is stable under every admitted
selective continuation, and whether a failed base record has a
resource-Pareto-minimal admissible repair or a bounded separating
intervention.

## Parallel arms

1. **Formal constructive arm:** state the finite necessity-and-sufficiency
   theorem, its converse, witness bound, and exact assumptions.
2. **Hostile arm:** find the smallest endpoint, selection-stratum,
   continuation, reset-memory, representation, and empty-fibre
   counterexamples.
3. **Collision arm:** translate the result into process-tensor,
   quantum-instrument, Blackwell/statistical-experiment, probabilistic
   bisimulation/lumpability, and system-identification language; locate the
   strongest absorber.
4. **Assurance arm:** derive the minimum locally decidable discriminator,
   acquisition fields, reset receipt, uncertainty target, and pre-hardware
   stop.
