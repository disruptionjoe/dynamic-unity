# Frozen question

Does one physically formed raw record determine a unique operational record
quotient independently of future tasks, or is every nontrivial compression
selected only relative to a declared family of physically admissible
continuations?

# Consumed evidence

`HC-DU-201` supplies the physical positive and finite carrier:

```text
r=(passage, herald, atom) in Z_2^3,
q_correction=(passage, herald XOR atom) in Z_2^2.
```

The two-bit quotient is minimal for the teleportation-correction task. The
third raw bit remains potentially audit-live. This run decides whether that
task-relative remainder is accidental, canonical, or forced by the choice of
future action family.

# Typed contract

- **Physical carrier:** the acquired outcome set `R=Z_2^3` under the frozen
  apparatus. No claim is made that `R` is the complete quantum state.
- **Task:** a deterministic response target `T:R->Y` or a finite family of
  such targets. The task is declared before quotient construction.
- **Sufficient quotient:** `q:R->Q` such that every declared target factors as
  `T=d_T o q`.
- **Minimal quotient:** the coarsest sufficient partition, unique only up to
  relabeling of quotient values.
- **Continuation family:** every future intervention/readout admitted to the
  physical controller/access contract. Two records are continuation-equivalent
  when every admitted continuation has the same response distribution.
- **Target-blind selection:** may use the independently fixed continuation
  family, physical resources, and symmetries, but not one held-out target after
  observing its result.
- **Scope/status/formation:** local apparatus; ontic physical carrier plus
  operational quotients and epistemic estimates; disclosure only.

# Frozen candidates and controls

1. The correction quotient is canonical for every possible future task.
2. It is minimal only for the correction family; adding one independent audit
   query restores the full raw carrier.
3. A unique target-blind quotient exists as continuation equivalence once the
   physically admissible continuation algebra is independently fixed.
4. Preserving every conceivable decision problem forces the full carrier up
   to Blackwell-equivalent relabeling/garbling and therefore supplies no
   nontrivial compression.
5. The apparatus itself selects a continuation family without controller,
   archive, or task assumptions.

Positive controls retain the full raw identity task. Negative controls erase
all tasks. Hostile controls enumerate every subset of the frozen Boolean task
family and every partition of the eight-element carrier.

# Lane, grade, absorber, kill, and stop

- **Lane:** 1, supported by 3, 4, and 7.
- **Channels:** `CH-FORMAL`, `CH-MODEL`, `CH-COLLIDE`, and `CH-SYN`.
- **Maximum grade:** scoped Grade 4 for an exact necessity/nonselection result;
  Grade 5 is unavailable without a nonabsorbed finite physical prediction.
- **Strongest absorber:** minimal sufficient statistics, Blackwell--Sherman--
  Stein comparison of experiments, quantum statistical comparison, automata/
  bisimulation minimization, and ordinary quantum-instrument theory.
- **Cheapest kill:** one nontrivial quotient derived from the physical carrier
  alone that remains coarsest sufficient for every admitted task family.
- **Stop:** do not rename a task statistic as a fundamental record, infer a
  physically selected action algebra from mathematical quantification over all
  actions, or claim new physics from a finite quotient lattice.
- **Local model:** eligible only as exhaustive exact proof control. No
  simulation, provider, or hardware.
