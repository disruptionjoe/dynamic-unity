# Bounded-KMS local-response tail and field-limit gate

## Cold-start contract

Dynamic Unity's purpose is to make physical reality intelligible as one
coherent, evidence-accountable whole. Its North Star is to determine whether
independently selected, observer-indexed certified causal records reconstruct
all observer-accessible time, geometry, fields, and capability, or expose a
finite physical remainder.

The repository begins quiescent at revision 51. `HC-DU-100` proves that no
finite response jet identifies a general analytic nonlinear response map, and
that a common quantitative tail would turn a finite hierarchy into a
conditional finite-resolution bridge. It leaves the tail, higher-response
instrument, field limit, acquisition, and geometric transfer physically
supplied.

This swing tests the strongest constructive arena before another generic
no-go:

> Does finite-time bounded quantum dynamics supply an explicit all-order
> nonlinear-response tail, does that control survive a local many-body limit,
> and does a KMS state turn the hierarchy into a passive formed record?

The honest evidence boundary is exact operator-norm proof, an exact scaling
counterexample, and primary literature collision. A deterministic regression
may preserve the proof controls after the result; it cannot supply new
physical evidence. External hardware is unavailable and unnecessary.

Keep independent:

- **scope:** a fixed finite-time quantum dynamical system, a local lattice
  limit, an extensive-source family, and a continuum-field handoff;
- **status:** a KMS state, algebraic multi-time correlator, controlled response
  coefficient, sequential instrument outcome, and retained observer record
  are different objects; and
- **formation:** the response is disclosure under a frozen Hamiltonian and
  source coupling, not source issuance.

  finite-dimensional case as the smallest arena;
- \(\omega_\beta\) be a fixed KMS state at inverse temperature \(\beta\);
- \(A\in\mathcal A\) be a bounded readout observable;
- \(B(t)=\tau_t(B)\), for bounded self-adjoint \(B\in\mathcal A\), be the
  interaction-picture perturbation;

- \(T_{\mathrm{geom}}\) be a held-out geometric target.

\[
x=2|\lambda|\,\lVert B\rVert\,\lVert f\rVert_1,
\]

the ordered simplex and
\(\lVert[B,X]\rVert\leq2\lVert B\rVert\lVert X\rVert\)
should give

\[
|F(\lambda)-F_N(\lambda)|
\leq
\lVert A\rVert
\sum_{n=N+1}^{\infty}\frac{x^n}{n!}
\leq
\lVert A\rVert e^x\frac{x^{N+1}}{(N+1)!}.

This would close `HC-DU-100`'s abstract analytic-tail dependency for bounded
finite-time controls. The proof must identify whether thermal equilibrium is
actually load-bearing.

Taylor order, its truncation cannot approximate the family uniformly on a
fixed nonzero source interval as \(m\to\infty\). This separates:
