## What was learned

The previous gate treated broad representational frameworks as if they
predicted every law they can encode. That is invalid. A process tensor,
effective action, or response hierarchy can house target-distinct laws
without selecting which one nature realizes.

The exact criterion is target constancy on the incumbent completion fibre.
Two polynomial laws share the training packet `(0,1)` and give held-out
values `2` and `4`. A universal polynomial response family represents both
but predicts neither held-out value until additional structure selects one.
Choosing the matching completion after reveal is refitting.

The first nonzero response difference remains a useful typed target. Its
order is preserved by nonsingular local source-coordinate changes and can
move under singular changes. What was removed is only the impossible demand
that a new physical law require a new representational language.

## Campaign effect

Wave 3 now requires a preregistered response coefficient, scaling,
symmetry relation, or finite statistic that varies across the matched
incumbent fibre, is fixed by the challenger without refit, and is acquired
through a physically selected interface.

No candidate currently satisfies that gate, so the portfolio remains
no-ready. The correction makes the search meaningful rather than
self-disqualifying.
