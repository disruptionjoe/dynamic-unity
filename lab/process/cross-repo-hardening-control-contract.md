# Cross-repo hardening controls

## Purpose

This contract incorporates six useful controls from Temporal Issuance, Time as
Finality, and Possibility to Capability without importing sibling verdicts as
Dynamic Unity facts or turning a narrow model into a universal criterion.

The corrected discriminator is:

> For an independently frozen, nonempty class of causally available,
> future-independent, physically realizable, and resource-bounded completions
> of the source, environment, controller, decoder, access boundary, and record
> interface: do record-isomorphic processes support the same admitted
> predictions and capabilities? If not, what finite intervention identifies
> the minimal omitted structure?

“Every physically legitimate completion” is not an executable quantifier
until the class is declared. “Fixed at stage zero” does not establish
future-independence. Computational non-reducibility may be one
class-relative diagnostic, but it is not the definition of physical novelty,
freshness, or issuance.

## Control suite

### `XH-01` — fixed pre-correlated completion

**Use for:** source, generativity, issuance, open-endedness, and allegedly new
record production.

Give the disclosure rival one source fixed at stage zero that contains:

- the option set;
- the realized path;
- and, when interventions are claimed, a complete counterfactual response
  tree over the declared finite intervention histories.

Stage-fixedness alone does not exclude this rival. Exclusion requires an
independently justified condition such as causal-source separation,
future-independence, limited access/degree, physical realizability, or a
resource bound. Absolute computational non-reducibility is neither empirically
certifiable from a finite trace nor required for physically generated
computable dynamics.

**Source grade retained:** Temporal Issuance supplies a mathematical
countermodel and a narrowed conditional theorem. It asserts no physical
realizability or cross-repo verdict.

### `XH-02` — departed environmental support

**Use for:** local record sufficiency, boundary relocation, observer-relative
capability, and physical remainder claims.

Construct two global processes that are identical under every admitted
statistic and intervention supported in region or access class `R`, while a
predeclared boundary-crossing menu can access departed support and separate an
action capability.

The result is indexed to `R`. It may diagnose omitted environmental support,
not exotic new physics. The boundary, transport, and price of enlarged reach
must be part of the model rather than inferred from the separation.

**Source grade retained:** Time as Finality's T411 is a finite quantum fixture
whose hostile review is queued; its repeated-interaction boundary is adopted,
not unconditionally derived. Dynamic Unity locally rechecks only the finite
control shape unless it independently reproduces that fixture.

### `XH-03` — same-SBS-data quantum absorber

**Use for:** quantum public facts, quantum objectivity, environmental record
redundancy, and broadcastable-finality claims.

Before claiming a quantum distinction, freeze:

- pointer observable and objectivity verdict;
- full declared system-fragment operational data;
- fragment distinguishability or support overlap;
- conditional or strong independence;
- observer-accessible fragment subset;
- provenance partition and how it is physically obtained; and
- audited independent support.

A difference that disappears when those data are shared is SBS/strong-QD
bookkeeping. A surviving difference must change a predeclared action or
finality observable at fixed data through a physical dimension the absorber
cannot import without changing the frozen process.

This is a quantum-arm null. It is not a universal absorber for BFT,
databases, or every form of public finality.

**Source grade retained:** Time as Finality's T162 establishes factorization
for its current finite Q1A family. It is not a theorem about every quantum
record family.

### `XH-04` — benign-refinement naturality

**Use for:** informational distance, topology, temporal scale, causal
geometry, curvature, and meta-record reconstruction.

Declare the morphisms that count as representation-only refinements before
computing a candidate invariant. At minimum test:

- observer relabeling;
- benign edge or relay subdivision;
- cover refinement where applicable; and
- composition of the refinement maps.

Reject a physical reading of path length, relay count, component size, phase,
or dimension when it changes under a declared benign refinement. Do not call
a subdivision benign when the inserted carrier has physical latency, energy,
noise, storage, authentication, or control consequences; those additions
change the model rather than its representation.

**Source grade retained:** Time as Finality's T484/T485/T487 are finite review
gates for one declared trust-edge refinement class. They do not establish
that reachability is the only physical topology invariant.

### `XH-05` — matched task/resource/control capability

**Use for:** every capability enlargement, finality-to-capability, metrology,
precision, access, and error-correction claim.

Translate performance into an executable task before comparing systems.
Freeze, as applicable:

- task threshold and loss;
- system or atom count;
- interrogation or execution time;
- preparation, entangling, recombination, and control operations;
- prior information;
- readout and decoder access;
- failure, rollback, and verification risk; and
- complete error and resource budgets.

Raw precision, entanglement, frequency shift, wider apparent range, or a new
controller is not by itself capability. If the complete instrument changes,
report that construction fork rather than forcing a normalized verdict.

**Source grade retained:** Possibility to Capability's quantum-clock specimen
is an argument with deterministic classification, not a new physical
measurement or universal capability theorem.

### `XH-06` — completion-constructor baseline, never an exhaustive class

**Use for:** every physical refinement or completion challenge.

Begin with the reusable constructor inventory:

```text
seed
boundary
resource
access
hidden state
history
provenance
relabeling / gauge
whole-family containment
```

Then add the DU-relevant rivals rather than declaring closure:

```text
fixed future-correlated oracle or response tree
departed or inaccessible environment
routed implementation and omitted control port
adaptive controller or scheduler
alternative physically formed record instrument
decoder / observer-boundary change
```

Each completion must preserve the independently declared frame, be
outcome-independent rather than fitted to the desired verdict, and carry its
resources. A target-derived behavioral quotient remains a diagnostic
refinement, not a formed physical record.

**Source grade retained:** CompletionClass-P2C v0.1 is an exploratory theorem
of a coarse finite model. Its inventory is a useful baseline battery, not the
set of all physically legitimate completions.

## Lead packet

Every in-scope swing records:

```yaml
cross_repo_controls:
  applicable: [XH-..]
  source_grade_preserved: true
  local_recheck: <proof, construction, executable control, or pending>
  completion_class_frozen: <yes/no/not-applicable>
  representation_refinement_class: <declared/not-applicable>
  matched_task_resource_frame: <declared/not-applicable>
  result_scope: <model/class/physical platform>
```

An inapplicable control is marked `not-applicable` with one sentence. It is
not forced onto unrelated work.

## Nonclaims

This contract does not establish:

- genuine source issuance;
- absolute computational irreducibility;
- the physicality of a particular observer boundary;
- a universal SBS theory of public finality;
- reachability as the only physical topology;
- a universal capability scalar;
- an exhaustive completion class; or
- record-first ontology.
