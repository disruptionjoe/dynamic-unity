# Certified Causal Reality

## Charter

Dynamic Unity determines whether, and under what minimal covariant and
non-circular conditions, an interventionally complete process of causal record
formation and certification is sufficient to reconstruct the effective time,
causal geometry, public facts, and bounded-risk capabilities available to an
observer. When it is not sufficient, the program seeks a finite operational
witness of the irreducible physical structure required.

In plain English:

> **Can everything that becomes physically real and actionable for an observer
> be reconstructed from the causal histories it can certify? If not, what
> irreducible physical remainder is required?**

The program is not premised on information or records being fundamental.
Certified Causal Reality is an adjudication program with three coequal
outcomes:

1. **Record-first reconstruction.** Independently selected record dynamics
   reconstructs the effective physics and capabilities available to observers.
2. **Operational duality.** Minimal physical and certified-record
   descriptions translate into one another without empirical remainder.
   Equivalence does not by itself establish ontological priority.
3. **Physics-first remainder.** Two realizations share the candidate
   certified-record structure yet differ under a finite admitted intervention.
   The separating witness identifies the additional physical structure.

Underidentification is work still open, not a fourth verdict. A negative
record-first result is a successful program result if it sharply locates the
remainder.

## Central conjecture and decisive discriminator

### Certified Causal Sufficiency Conjecture

For a declared local or covariant model class, observer/access class,
adversary class, intervention family, and resource bound, suppose that the
record dynamics and certification rule are selected independently of the
target physical observables. Then the resulting certified-history quotient is
sufficient for every observer-accessible prediction and bounded-risk
capability, and its meta-record accessibility relations reconstruct effective
causal geometry up to gauge.

If `Q_O(M)` is the certified history quotient available to observer `O`, the
target implication is

```text
Q_O(M1) isomorphic to Q_O(M2)
    =>
observer-accessible predictions and bounded-risk capabilities agree.
```

The equivalence on the left must be defined before and independently of the
target experiments on the right. Defining records as “everything that makes a
physical difference” and then proving that they capture every physical
difference is forbidden.

The word **causal** is typed at two levels. The primitive kernel may contain
only declared operational precedence, intervention slots, compositional
dependence, or influence reachability. It does not thereby contain a clock,
metric, light cone, foliation, or spacetime causal order. A physical benchmark
may supply those as explicitly labeled antecedents. `HC-DU-038` asks whether
effective spacetime causal and metric structure beyond the primitive
operational relation can be reconstructed; it never claims to derive the
primitive relation from itself.

### Factorization-or-witness discriminator

Given two models whose independently defined record-generation,
certification, provenance, access, and intervention structures are
isomorphic:

- either every admitted physical prediction and capability factors through
  that common quotient;
- or construct a bounded-complexity intervention that distinguishes the
  models.

The first outcome supports record sufficiency or operational duality. The
second identifies a physics-first remainder. Merely fitting a finite observed
trace establishes neither.

  reconstructed.

  independent reduction in failure/reversal risk after verification and
  resource cost.
- **Analogy-only unity:** quantum, consensus, database, and causal-structure
  vocabularies correspond rhetorically but no common object or theorem

  novel-consequence argument.
- **Toy-to-universe inflation:** a finite exact fixture is generalized to
  continuum physics, gravity, cosmology, or ontology without typed bridges.
