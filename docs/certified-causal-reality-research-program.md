# Certified Causal Reality

This document defines Dynamic Unity’s durable scientific problem and contracts.
It contains no current priority or executable work. Live state is owned only
by [`CURRENT-RESEARCH.yaml`](../CURRENT-RESEARCH.yaml); stable responsibility
and method live in [`LANES.yaml`](../LANES.yaml).

## Charter

**Purpose**

> Make physical reality intelligible as one coherent, evidence-accountable
> whole.

**Vision**

> Build a living, ontology-neutral architecture in which quantum dynamics,
> records, observers, capabilities, spacetime, fields, particles,
> thermodynamics, and cosmology are related at their honestly earned
> evidentiary grades—and use the unresolved seams to locate, classify,
> dissolve, or decisively test the tensions in present physics.

**Mission**

> Construct, formalize, stress-test, and prepare for publication faithful
> representations, reconstruction theorems, countermodels, no-go results, and
> empirical discriminators that connect—or sharply separate—those layers.

**North Star**

> Determine whether independently selected, observer-indexed certified causal
> records reconstruct all observer-accessible time, geometry, fields, and
> capability—and identify a finite physical remainder wherever they do not.

**Operating principle**

> Whole-picture purpose. Seam-by-seam mission. Theorem-grade North Star.

## Central adjudication

Can independently formed physical records reconstruct every response,
observable, and capability available within a declared observer/access
contract? If not, what additional physical structure is required?

Separately, do record-forming interactions merely disclose distinctions fixed
by an independently warranted prior completion, or can any physically issue a
new distinction? Reconstruction and formation are different adjudications.

The program keeps three outcomes open:

1. **Record-first reconstruction.** Independently selected record dynamics
   reconstructs effective observer-accessible physics and capability.
2. **Operational duality.** Minimal physical and certified-record descriptions
   translate without empirical remainder, while ontological priority remains
   undecided.
3. **Physics-first remainder.** A finite admitted intervention distinguishes
   physical processes that share the candidate certified-record description.

Underidentification is incomplete adjudication. It is not an ontological
outcome.

Operational duality is different from generic underidentification. It is
earned only when physical-operational and record presentations mutually
simulate one another by target-independent maps for the frozen action
contract. Then no decision internal to that contract can identify
ontological priority. A capability enlargement or independently selected
formation arrow may still break the equivalence.

Breaking operational duality and orienting a formation arrow are themselves
different tasks. Against a live common-source rival, one one-sided
intervention may separate one direct-arrow model while leaving the other
confounded. A causal-priority claim must freeze the rival class and use enough
independently realizable arrow-breaking interventions to identify the process.
Even then, a local causal arrow does not select the variables, interfaces, or
ontological substrate.

## Typed kernel

For an admitted physical completion class \(M\), define:

- a physical process/history object \(m\in M\);
- an independently specified record map \(r:M\to Q\);
- observer/access index \(a\);
- admitted interventions/actions \(\mathcal I_a\);
- resource boundary \(B_a\);
- held-out target \(t_a:M\to T_a\);
- physical equivalence or gauge \(G\).

For a realized record \(q\), the completion fibre is

\[
F_q=\{m\in M:r(m)=q\}.
\]

The record reconstructs target \(t_a\) exactly on \(M\) iff:

1. the relevant fibre is nonempty; and
2. \(t_a\) is constant on that fibre modulo the declared equivalence.

Equivalently, in finite deterministic settings,

\[
\ker r\subseteq\ker t_a.
\]

A same-record/different-target pair is a candidate-record insufficiency
witness. It becomes a physical remainder only after every independently
admissible completion, environment, source, boundary, provenance, controller,
decoder, resource, and intervention refinement has been frozen or exhausted
without target coding.

## Forward and inverse programs

### Forward representation

```text
accepted physical dynamics
  -> distinctions and interactions
  -> formed and retained traces
  -> accessible records and certificates
  -> observer capability
  -> layered regional finality
  -> effective classical, temporal, geometric and field structure
```

This direction can earn compatibility or representation without asserting
that records create physical reality.

### Inverse adjudication

```text
complete independently specified certified causal record network
  -> reconstruct observer-accessible targets up to declared equivalence
     OR
  -> return an exact non-unification or finite separating intervention
```

This direction must distinguish:

- law/source narrowing from record refinement;
- state/history sufficiency from interface selection;
- access from information existence;
- certification semantics from the physical feedback path;
- local, regional, public, and global finality;
- target-relative operational closure from source derivation.

## Record and finality type ladder

```text
distinction or event
  != history-conditioned trace
  != retained record
  != independently accessible record
  != rival-excluding certificate
  != public final record
  != action-enabling fact
```

A public-finality claim requires:

- a declared incompatibility relation;
- a rival/fault/adversary class;
- provenance and access semantics;
- a finalization rule;
- an error or risk bound;
- a resource and rollback horizon.

Redundancy, copying, confidence, consensus, threshold crossing, decoherence,
or a cryptographic signature does not alone supply all of these.

## Perspective and ontology contract

Dynamic Unity uses **methodological physical realism**. Within an admitted
candidate theory, systems, interactions, occurrences, and physically formed
records are not constituted by an observer's beliefs about them.
“Ontology-neutral” means neutral among detailed physical ontologies and
priority claims; it does not identify reality with knowledge or language.

Three coordinates must remain independent:

| Coordinate | Typed alternatives | Forbidden inference |
|---|---|---|
| Perspective or scope | local/system-indexed; regional/public; global/completion | local is not epistemic; public is not globally complete |
| Status | ontic physical occurrence/structure; epistemic belief, probability, forecast, or scientific representation | an epistemic description can concern ontic reality; an experience can be a physical event while its represented content remains epistemic |
| Formation | disclosure from the frozen prior completion; candidate issuance beyond it; undecided | record formation or internal underivability does not by itself prove issuance |

Use “observer-indexed” for a physical subsystem's records, access,
interventions, actions, targets, and resources. It does not by itself mean
subjective, consciousness-dependent, ontologically relative, or incompatible
with a global state or preferred foliation. Use “public” for a fact certified
and action-available to a declared participant class under a fault, resource,
and rollback contract. Public or composition-global finality is not the
observer-independent global ontology.

The act of constructing a scientific ontology is epistemic; the proposed
referent may be ontic. The quantum state must therefore be typed per candidate
as ontic, epistemic, relational, operational, or mixed. Dynamic Unity takes
the EPR completeness question seriously without treating the EPR conclusion,
classical context-independent values, locality, separability, or wavefunction
incompleteness as established.

**Disclosure** means the target distinction is already fixed or generated
inside the independently frozen source/completion class. **Source issuance**
means a physical source forms a distinction not fixed by that class after
completed-history, generator, controller, and fixed-oracle rivals are
exhausted. External computability does not by itself prove disclosure;
first-person or internal underivability does not by itself prove issuance.

Beginning reconstruction from local records is an epistemic method forced by
the evidence available to investigators. It is not evidence that records are
fundamental, create reality, or exhaust the global ontology. Any stronger
perspectival or record-first ontology must declare its contextuality,
locality, friend-style, foliation, global-completion, and measurement
assumptions and earn a theorem or finite consequence. Hidden structure
inaccessible to every admitted observer is operationally unavailable, not
thereby nonexistent.

## Regional and recursive composition

Regional records may differ in formation, accessibility, durability, action
safety, fault model, and hardening threshold. Distributed-system tools can
clarify this architecture, but their theorems transfer only under exact
assumption matching.

Keep distinct:

- causal precedence and total order;
- individual, distributed, and common knowledge;
- authentication, provenance, physical source formation, and truth;
- metastable confidence, quorum safety, liveness, accountability, and
  irreversible physical closure;
- a local certificate sufficient for one action and a globally reconstructive
  history.

Composition succeeds only relative to a frozen action/capability contract. A
capability enlargement may split a previously sufficient quotient.

## Evidence grades

| Grade | Earned statement |
|---|---|
| `0 — vocabulary` | A useful analogy or terminology. |
| `1 — compatibility` | No contradiction within a declared scope. |
| `2 — representation` | A faithful typed image of known physics. |
| `3 — reconstruction` | Declared targets recovered up to equivalence. |
| `4 — selection / necessity` | Dynamics selects the structure, or a theorem requires it. |
| `5 — remainder / prediction` | A finite discriminator survives the admitted physical completion class. |

Warrant type and evidence grade are separate. A conditional construction may
be exact without being physically selected. An abductive explanation may be
valuable without being a theorem.

## Core controls

- **Fixed-oracle control:** novelty or growth may still disclose a
  pre-correlated completion.
- **Departed-support control:** identical local record states may have different
  capability because relevant information left the accessible boundary.
- **Objectivity absorber:** provenance, redundancy, support and access may
  collapse into a complete spectrum-broadcast/strong-QD description.
- **Refinement invariance:** conclusions must survive benign subdivision,
  relabeling and equivalent presentation.
- **Matched-resource control:** compare actions only under fixed support,
  controls, time, prior information, readout and error budget.
- **Completion-absorber battery:** test omitted state, source, environment,
  route, controller, decoder, task, resource and intervention structure.
- **Implementation-completeness control:** returned or accepted observations do
  not identify unobserved attempts or controller state.
- **Multi-time control:** base-time transitions and endpoint states are not an
  interventionally complete process.
- **Operational-duality control:** mutual sufficiency earns equal decision
  content and a no-priority verdict only inside the frozen action envelope.
  It does not earn native identity, physical formation, interface selection,
  or an ontology.
- **Causal-priority control:** terminal correlation, authentication, or one
  one-sided first leak need not exclude a common source. Freeze source and
  record ports, test both independently addressed pre-formation directions,
  retain the common-cause rival, and keep process direction distinct from
  substrate priority.

## Physical and computational boundaries

Local computation is warranted only when it:

- finds an exact counterexample;
- validates a proof boundary;
- distinguishes candidate contracts before hardware;
- or preserves a separately earned result as a deterministic regression.

Apply [`LMLG-01`](../lab/process/local-model-learning-gate.md). If the first
decision-changing learning requires external hardware, do not build the local
path. If hardware becomes irreducible after local alternatives are exhausted,
record the minimum external dependency once and use the local fallback or park
the branch.

## Success and stop conditions

Successful returns include:

- a scoped reconstruction or selection theorem;
- an exact non-unification;
- a minimal lawful counterexample;
- a clean no-excess or absorption result;
- a finite prediction with a complete implementation contract;
- a corrected narrower conjecture with a real reopener.

Stop when:

- the claim factors through known mathematics with no DU-specific excess;
- a required interface or coefficient is simply supplied;
- a witness disappears under a legitimate completion;
- the next step merely adds another host, fitted archive, simulator, provider
  adapter, or output observable;
- the external dependency is already known and no local decision can change.
