# Physical Admissible Record Envelope selector contract

## Objective

Determine what a lawful physical process must supply for its Admissible
Record Envelope to be selected rather than merely enumerated, fitted, or
target-coded by an analyst.

The run will:

1. translate the banked ARE, action-center, symmetry, joinability, and AQFT
   boundaries into one typed selector passport;
2. establish necessary equivariance, naturality, formation, access, resource,
   and action-envelope conditions;
3. classify the smallest positive, orbit-selected, plural, and impossible
   controls already present in Dynamic Unity;
4. collide the resulting claim with the closest primary-source mathematics;
   and
5. return a selector theorem, scoped no-go/classification, or the minimum
   additional physical inputs required by the next two arenas.

## Frozen contract

- Dynamic Unity is the sole writable repository.
- The selector is fixed independently of every held-out reconstruction
  target.
- A selected interface must be physically formed and accessible; mathematical
  definability, a formal product, or a chosen measurement does not suffice.
- Lawful process, observer class, candidate interface, formation map,
  provenance, access, decoder, action envelope, resource budget, and
  representation group remain separately typed.
- The result may select one interface, a symmetry orbit, a plural maximal
  family, or none. Uniqueness is not assumed.
- Finite controls establish scoped statements only. They do not establish a
  universal record ontology, new law, or new physics.
- Existing claims, concepts, papers, predictions, Drafting Factory, hardware,
  provider, submission, publication, and sibling-repository states remain
  unchanged unless an independently earned result requires a minimal
  repository-local register update.

## Strongest absorbers

- equivariant-decision and invariant-statistic fixed-point restrictions;
- ordinary quantum instruments and resource theories of measurement;
- reference-frame and spontaneous/supplied symmetry breaking;
- Blackwell comparison and sufficient-statistic constructions;
- AQFT measurement schemes and induced observables; and

1. target independence;

## Controls

- **positive:** a materially oriented gauge-boundary apparatus whose dynamics
  selects a controlled-add/subtract formation orbit;
- **orbit-selected:** the same apparatus before an external orientation
  chooses one convention;
- **plural:** the one-clock Einstein--matter ARE with two incomparable
  symmetry-related maximal interfaces;
- **impossible:** a transitive symmetry action on candidate sharp interfaces
  with no fixed interface; and
- **joinability:** the one-clock/two-clock resource switch, which distinguishes
  a formal tuple from a physically admitted joined interface.

Stop and return the obstruction if:

- the selector depends on the held-out target;
- it chooses an interface outside a symmetry-fixed set without a physical

- the selected tuple is not physically jointly realizable;
- formation, access, decoder, or action semantics remain supplied but are
  reported as selected; or
- the result is already exactly a known theorem with no DU-specific typed
  consequence.
