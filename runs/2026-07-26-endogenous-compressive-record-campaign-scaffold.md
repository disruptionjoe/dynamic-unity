prepare the next five large Dynamic Unity research swings after `HC-DU-041`.
The campaign must attack `H-CCR-17`, the endogenous compressive record gap,
rather than repeat reconstruction through supplied detectors, codes,
record-complete lookups, or injective tomography.

The output must be one cumulative, branch-safe campaign. Each prepared swing
must state:

- the exact uncertainty it reduces;
- the typed object, strongest rival, and attribution test;
- what it inherits from the preceding receipt;
- the cheapest exact proof, counterexample, or primary-source collision;
- positive controls, absorbers, falsifier, and stop/reopen rules;
- the useful output under positive, negative, or incomplete results;
- its maximum evidence grade; and
- its local-computation and hardware disposition.
