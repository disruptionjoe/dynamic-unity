# ECR-N5-S1 endogenous record mechanism tournament

## Objective

Execute the first swing of the prepared endogenous-compressive-record
campaign. Compare five mechanism chains under the unchanged `H-CCR-17`
passport and return exactly one of:

- one selected next arena with a locally decidable theorem contract;
- a near tie with one exact separating question; or
- `NO_READY_ARENA` with a common minimum missing premise and the next
  obstruction target.

## Frozen passport

For one lawful realization class, distinguish:

1. target-independent interface selection;
2. an actual provenance-bearing write from a declared blank memory;
3. strict noninjective compression modulo physical gauge;
4. nonzero lawful target diameter before record conditioning;
5. no-refit reconstruction of an independently held-out target;
6. complete behavior sufficiency under a frozen capability/resource envelope
   \(H\); and
7. one finite verdict under a preregistered enlargement \(H\subset H'\).

## Candidates

1. matter-flow to metastable archive;
2. environment-selected pointer to redundant public record;
3. Hamiltonian-selected topological or logical sector;
4. causal-action or local-QFT source to instrument chain; and
5. conservation-law or phase-boundary recorder.

The material gauge and stabilizer-QEC specimens are supplied-architecture
controls, not candidates that can win by repeating their existing receipts.

## Required method

- Freeze one supplied/orbit-selected/dynamically-selected/fitted ledger for
  every candidate.
- Use primary sources for the strongest established mechanism and absorber.
- Give every candidate the smallest exact local theorem or counterexample
  that changes its tournament disposition.
- Compare lexicographically by the seven obligations, then supplied-arrow
  count and cheapest exact kill.
- Preserve selected algebras/orbits, actual token selection, physical write,
  persistence, observer access, and target factorization as distinct.

## Local-model and hardware disposition

`DIRECT_RESEARCH_AND_FORMAL_ANALYSIS`.

No simulation, trained model, provider adapter, or hardware action is
authorized. A local computation is allowed only if it returns an exact
separator or validates a proof boundary unavailable at equal grade from
direct analysis.

## Planned outputs

- one dated tournament result with five mechanism cards, exact controls,
  primary-source collision, ranking, verdict, and successor contract;
- updates to the campaign progress and cold-start/Lane routing;
- a closing receipt and memory note.

## Validation

- every candidate is assessed under the same seven obligations;
- every source claim is scoped to what its primary source establishes;
- component results are not promoted as a new DU law;
- the verdict determines the sole next executable object;
- local links and Lane routing validate;
- existing exact regressions and the cold-start orientation probe pass;
- `git diff --check` is clean; and
- no paper, prediction, model, hardware, provider, Factory, publication,
  submission, or sibling-repository state changes.

## Stop conditions

Stop or downgrade rather than silently repair if:

- a record interface, basis, boundary, code, blank memory, decoder, observer,
  or target is supplied and then credited as selected;
- a stable variable is only passive transport or an unformed correlation;
- the proposed repair is injective tomography;
- the law already closes the held-out target;
- the first meaningful learning requires hardware; or
- no exact local separator can be stated for a candidate.
