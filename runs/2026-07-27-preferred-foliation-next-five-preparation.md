# Preferred-foliation next-five preparation

## Objective

Prepare one branch-safe five-swing Dynamic Unity sequence through the standard
inline Science Council, divergent-persona, and triple-diamond process while
holding one assumption fixed:

> A preferred foliation exists. It supplies a privileged simultaneity
> structure, and relativistic effects are the internally observed result of
> finite-speed information propagation in that structure.

The assumption must not select the research path. The first position therefore
re-ranks the complete current Dynamic Unity portfolio before any downstream
position is activated.

## Frozen assumption version

The series holds the **exactly compensated** version:

- a continuous preferred foliation and global update parameter exist;
- every admitted observer-accessible prediction remains exactly
  Lorentz/SR-equivalent;
- no lattice, discrete minimum tick, Lorentz-violation coefficient, preferred
  spatial axis, or leakage signature is assumed;
- the assumption supplies time orientation for the admitted region but does
  not supply a detector orientation, observer, record, archive, access route,
  certificate, or finality rule; and
- the assumption is a conditional search premise only. It receives no
  evidentiary grade and enters no claim or prediction ledger.

The version will not switch inside this series. A result requiring observable
foliation leakage returns `LEAKING_BRANCH_REQUIRED` and is parked for a
separate explicitly re-frozen series.

- a global update parameter is called a globally accessible record;
