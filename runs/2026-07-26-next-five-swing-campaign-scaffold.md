# Next five-swing Certified Causal Reality campaign scaffold

## Objective

Use ten divergent research lenses in a compact triple-diamond process to
choose and prepare the next five large Dynamic Unity research swings after
`HC-DU-033E/038E`.

The output must be a dependency-aware campaign, not five active runs or five
unrelated hypotheses. Each prepared swing must state:

- the exact uncertainty it reduces;
- its typed hypothesis and strongest rival;
- the result inherited from prior swings;
- the most efficient method;
- absorbers, positive controls, falsifier, stop/go and branch rules;
- maximum evidentiary grade;
- local-model and hardware disposition; and
- a durable output valuable under positive, negative, or null results.

## Frozen contract

- Dynamic Unity is the sole writable repository.
- Persona outputs are method lenses, not scientific evidence or votes.
- This run prepares but does not execute the five scientific swings.
- Existing paper, prediction, claim, concept, Drafting Factory, provider, and
  hardware states remain unchanged.
- The campaign must advance the North Star and consume the Admissible Record
  Envelope without repeating supplied detectors, code variants, fitted sensor
  matrices, synthetic quotient variants, or provider plumbing.
- Formal joins count only when physically jointly realizable under one
  observer, action, provenance, and resource contract.
- Every conditional or abductive branch follows the existing warrant and
  concept-family rules; a failed formalization does not kill its concept.

## Triple-diamond jobs

1. **Purpose and opportunity:** diverge across the repository, identify the
   highest-value uncertainties, and converge on one campaign objective.
2. **Architecture and sequence:** compare fan-out, one-track concentration,
   and shared-spine alternatives; converge on five composable swings.
3. **Assurance and adaptation:** attack every swing's assumptions, establish
   branch rules, and ensure adverse findings preserve momentum without
   manufacturing success.

## Evidence ceiling

This preparation run earns method/scoping grade only. It promotes no
scientific result. Individual future swings may target scoped grade `3`
reconstruction, grade `4` selection/necessity, or grade `5`
completion-class-relative remainder only when their own contracts and
evidence warrant it.

## Local-model and hardware gate

Disposition: `DIRECT_RESEARCH_AND_FORMAL_PREPARATION`.

No simulation, solver, trained model, provider adapter, or hardware action is
admitted in this preparation run. Each future swing must independently pass
`LMLG-01`; the default is direct proof, counterexample, and primary-source
collision. External hardware remains unavailable by default.

## Stop conditions

Stop rather than scaffold if:

- the sequence is a disguised paper queue or broad hypothesis fan-out;
- later swings require earlier positive findings and have no adverse branch;
- a target behavior selects the record interface intended to reconstruct it;
- the same supplied detector or finite code is merely relabeled across
  swings;
- a simulation's first meaningful learning would require hardware; or
- another writer or overlapping uncommitted change appears.
