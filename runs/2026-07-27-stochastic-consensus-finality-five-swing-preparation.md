# Run: stochastic, consensus, complexity, cryptography, and finality five-swing preparation

## Decision

Interpose a five-swing stochastic/distributed-systems learning sequence before
the previously queued `N5-RS-P2` portfolio pivot.

The preparation must:

1. use eighteen divergent inline lenses consisting of the five-person Science
   Council plus Hashgraph/DAG, Avalanche/metastability,
   Byzantine consensus, epidemic propagation, and MMO networking expertise;
2. add five complexity fields: weak/strong emergence, stigmergic
   self-organization, scaling/universality, cascades/percolation, and
   adaptive/co-evolving systems;
3. add three cryptographic fields: zero-knowledge/commitments,
   homomorphic/MPC computation, and threshold cryptography/DKG;
4. inspect primary theorem terrain rather than reason from protocol slogans;
5. preserve existing `HC-DU-035A/035C` controls;
6. distinguish statistical amplification from information creation;
7. distinguish weak emergence and finite-size threshold behavior from strong
   emergence or a universal phase transition;
8. distinguish verification without disclosure, computation without access,
   and distributed authority from physical record formation;
9. create one sequential, branch-safe five-swing scaffold; and
10. keep every first decision-changing result locally attainable.

## Preparation receipt

The eighteen lenses converged on three boundaries:

```text
large N can amplify independently present target-sensitive evidence
initial wording: large N cannot recover a distinction absent from every admitted input channel

collective order can be weakly emergent from a complete microprocess
collective surprise or a finite threshold does not establish strong emergence
or a universality class

cryptography can certify, conceal, compute, or distribute opening authority
cryptography does not prove the physical truth or completeness of its inputs
```
