## Inclusion test

Register a finding only when an inspected artifact supports at least one of:

1. a tempting implication is false or reversed;
2. a shared word hides a type mismatch;
3. a result depends on a construction fork agents may silently default;
4. admission, representation, compatibility, or location is easy to misread
   as selection, derivation, or physical necessity;
5. a no-go or positive result is easy to broaden beyond its class;
6. a repair is actually record refinement, completion enlargement, resource
   change, gauge change, or occurrence/contract retyping; or
7. a negative or null result preserves a specific useful residual.

Speculative wish-list items, unexecuted proposals, and mere unusual vocabulary
do not qualify.
