# Repository-wide one-swing paper candidates

## Question

If Dynamic Unity could take one large, coherent swing intended to move as
close as possible to a defensible publishable paper, what are the best three
candidates across the **entire repository**?

The assessment distinguishes:

- current exact substance;
- novelty still at risk;
- one-swing deliverable;
- dependence on new physics or unavailable apparatus;
- relevance to the Certified Causal Reality purpose; and
- whether a null result would still leave a paper-shaped theorem or no-go.

No candidate is seeded or authorized here.

## 1. Covariant records are not free

### Candidate paper

**A Covariant Quantum Cellular Automaton Recorder: Construction,
Nonselection, and the Cost of Physical Records**

### Why it ranks first for closeness

This is the only branch already possessing most of a conventional technical
paper spine:

- a complete finite unitary recorder construction;
- exact inheritance of the source Clock-QCA covariance square;
- the stronger `q`-transparent lifting lemma;
- the distinction between algebraic quotient and physical forgetting;
- a passive informative-recorder obstruction;
- exact finite record-capacity bounds;
- an explicit archive/environment extension;
- visibility/distinguishability complementarity;
- evidence-conserving transfer, copy, loss, reunion, and boundary controls;
- executable artifacts and adversarial audits.

The missing work is concentrated rather than foundational.

### One big swing

1. Perform a broad primary-literature collision review across reversible and
   history QCA, quantum clocks and stopwatches, repeated-interaction
   recorders, information-disturbance, autonomous clocks, and covariant
   quantum channels.
2. Generalize the exact obstruction from perfect all-state source-channel
   preservation to an approximate/noisy bound relating record
   distinguishability, source-channel distance, finite support, and archive
   cost.
3. State the minimal construction-obstruction theorem independent of DU
   language, with the Arrighi-Facchini-Forets Clock QCA as the nontrivial
   covariant specimen.
4. Independently audit every proof, test tight examples and counterexamples,
   and prepare the source package for the Drafting Factory.

### Paper-worthy success even under a null

If the combination is not novel, the swing can still identify the exact
known theorem that absorbs each component and prevent a weak submission.
If the approximate tradeoff is nontrivial and tight, it supplies the clearest
new theorem in the branch.

### Main risk

The component mathematics is mature; novelty may collapse to a specialized
application. The paper must contribute a sharp approximate tradeoff or a
previously unstated covariant construction-obstruction conjunction, not just
repackage no-information-without-disturbance.

## 2. Interventional record sufficiency under admissible completion

### Candidate paper

**Interventional Record Sufficiency: A Proof-Carrying
Factorization–Refinement–Remainder Trichotomy**

### Why it ranks second

This is the strongest flagship candidate and the closest to DU's central
purpose. Existing work already supplies:

- the exact finite deterministic behavioral closure;
- a tight complexity-relative separating-word bound;
- a finite-linear reachable-rank analogue;
- absence of a size-free finite certification horizon;
- the correction from candidate-record failure to an admissible-refinement
  challenge;
- a signed coherent-history assay; and
- a concrete cross-repo completion/adversary battery.

What is missing is the genuinely publishable conjunction.

### One big swing

1. Freeze a finite or finitely generated target-independent completion class
   using `XH-01..06`.
2. Build a proof-carrying compiler returning:
   - factorization;
   - minimal sufficient physical refinement;
   - a uniform or refinement-indexed finite remainder witness; or
   - an incomplete comparison contract.
3. Prove the finite intervention-basis or compactness condition that makes the
   class-wide result valid.
4. Carry the exact same typed object through:
   - one authenticated adversarial DAG/database process; and
   - one routed quantum/process-tensor or Clock-QCA history.
5. Preserve negative results as theorem boundaries rather than forcing
   record-first ontology.

### Paper-worthy success even under a null

A counterexample to uniform certification, a lower bound on the necessary
intervention basis, or a theorem proving that no target-independent finite
completion class can support the proposed trichotomy would itself be a
publishable no-go.

### Main risk

The theorem can become either tautological or a bundle of known automata,
Blackwell, system-identification, and process-comparison results. The physical
refinement class and unchanged cross-platform semantics must do real work.

## 3. Higher-order finality as loop-syndrome correction

### Candidate paper

**Higher-Order Public Finality: Loop Obstructions, Provenance Capacity, and
Correctable Action Algebras**

### Why it ranks third

The branch already has an exact finite reversible baseline:

- path-independent facts as a holonomy orbit quotient/fixed algebra;
- reconciliation-frustration monotonicity;
- a tight project-or-lift provenance alphabet;
- cycle-lift classification;
- implementation-provenance absorption of the apparent coherent-curvature
  witness; and
- a strong distributed-systems/QEC/hypergraph hypothesis portfolio.

It is farther from a paper than the Clock-QCA package but may yield a more
distinctive cross-domain result.

### One big swing

1. Replace reversible edge maps with finite noninvertible/noisy instruments.
2. Compute the maximal safe equalizer or approximate fixed algebra and its
   convergence/error bound.
3. Represent higher certificates as relations or syndrome-filling cells and
   prove exactly when they enlarge the safe action algebra rather than merely
   add evidence.
4. Derive the minimum authenticated provenance or syndrome information
   required to preserve a noninvariant task.
5. Instantiate the same theorem in:
   - a metastable-plus-hardening BFT/DAG fixture; and
   - a small operator-algebra QEC or routed quantum fixture.

### Paper-worthy success even under a null

Failure of the higher-certificate correspondence can yield a sharp
non-equivalence theorem locating where consensus finality, database
serializability, and QEC syndrome correction diverge.

### Main risk

Group actions, cohomology, equalizers, fixed algebras, QEC, and consensus
certificates are mature terrain. The work must prove one unchanged
necessary-and-sufficient statement, not offer an attractive analogy.

## Why the other major branches do not make the top three

| meta-record geometry / perspectival curvature | highest foundational ceiling, but record formation, refinement class, and benign-refinement uniqueness are still missing |

| Bianconi influence and `Lambda~1/sqrt(N)` | selector, physical identity, units, and nonzero scale remain absent; the bounded reopener is consumed |
| GU / Standard Model / cosmology recovery | current dependencies make a one-swing result likely conditional or fitted rather than defensible |

## Recommendation

If the objective is **closest to a paper**, choose Candidate 1.

If the objective is **highest flagship upside while remaining plausibly
paper-shaped**, choose Candidate 2.

If the objective is **most distinctive cross-domain theorem**, choose
Candidate 3.

The root recommendation for one next swing is Candidate 1: finish and
challenge the Clock-QCA construction-obstruction package, with an approximate
record-disturbance-resource theorem as the load-bearing novelty target. It is
the only option where most of the formal spine, computational evidence, and
paper narrative already exist before the swing begins.
