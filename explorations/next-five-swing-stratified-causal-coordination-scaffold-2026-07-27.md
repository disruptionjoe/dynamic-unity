# Next five-swing scaffold: stratified causal coordination to physical excess

## Executive decision

The game-engine comparison has finished its most useful job. It exposed
render/action separation, authoritative reconciliation, pair-relative access,
and the difference between local prediction and harder finality. It should
not remain the program's frame.

The larger candidate picture is:

```text
physical dynamics forms local events and records
    -> causal channels make some facts available to some systems
    -> monotone facts can propagate and merge without global coordination
    -> nonmonotone claims, exclusivity, absence, and global invariants need
       additional timing, failure, threshold, trust, or coordination premises
    -> regional certificates close capability-relative actions
    -> harder cross-region reconciliation can form stronger but costlier
       finality
    -> observer-accessible effective physics is reconstructed from the
       resulting stratified causal coordination structure
       OR a finite physical remainder survives
```

Call this the **stratified causal coordination picture**. It is not the claim
that “the universe is a distributed database.” It is a typed research
architecture for asking:

> Which physical distinctions form locally, which can be propagated or
> merged, which require coordination, which become action-safe for which
> observers, and which—if any—change physical dynamics rather than merely
> knowledge and capability?

This picture could unify Dynamic Unity's record, access, regional-finality,
and reconstruction work at representation grade. It earns new physics only
through an operational delta after the complete standard process is fixed.

The smartest next five-position sequence is:

```text
P1  execute the existing whole-portfolio minimum-premise and excess compiler
P2  derive a typed coordination possibility/impossibility spine
P3  prove or break conservative certification under physical feedback
P4  derive regional-to-global descent or its smallest topological obstruction
P5  collide the unchanged structure with multiple physical arenas and lock
    one prediction, theorem, or clean non-unification
```

Position 1 retains the already executable identity `N5-RS-P2`. This
preparation creates no competing active campaign. Positions 2–5 receive no
run identity until Position 1 returns its portfolio verdict.

## 1. The larger picture, stated without the game metaphor

### The base physical layer

Quantum dynamics, QFT, GR, or another independently admitted law determines
which processes and interactions are physically possible. This layer forms
the source events and records. A signature, hash, quorum, proof, or consensus
protocol cannot substitute for it.

### The causal-access layer

Finite propagation, subsystem boundaries, instruments, and observer
capabilities determine which records can reach which systems. Different
observers can therefore have different complete action-sufficient views
without there being one privileged “real person.”

### The coordination layer

Facts differ by logical and operational type:

- positive, monotone evidence can remain true as records grow;
- absence, uniqueness, revocation, exclusivity, and global invariants are
  nonmonotone and can require coordination;
- distributed knowledge, individual knowledge, common knowledge, total
  order, conflict safety, liveness, and physical truth are different
  receipts.

### The regional-finality layer

Hash ancestry, epidemic propagation, metastable preference, BFT locking,
threshold access, zero-knowledge verification, and database consistency can
support different actions under different timing, fault, trust, and access
contracts. There need not be one scalar finality or one global rule.

### The descent layer

A region can act as a higher-level node only for targets that factor through
its exported record and declared context. Regions can therefore form a
dynamic network of networks. Whether these local quotients glue into one
global object is a theorem question, not an assumption.

### The physical-excess gate

All of the above can be a faithful representation of standard physics.
Physical excess begins only if:

1. a coordination or certification variable changes an outcome after the
   complete standard state/process is matched;
2. a universal no-refit relation among physical observables survives across
   arenas; or
3. a finite record-relative remainder survives every admitted standard
   completion.

## 4. Triple Diamond 1 — what the panel thinks was previously missing

### Divergence

The panel produced twelve candidate hypotheses.

#### H-SCC-1 — Monotonicity boundary

Facts preserved under record extension may admit coordination-free regional
merging; absence, uniqueness, revocation, exclusivity, and global conservation
claims may require added coordination or a stronger physical invariant.

This is inspired by CALM but is not yet its physical theorem.

#### H-SCC-2 — Proposition-indexed finality

Finality is not a property of a node, record, or region alone. It is a
relation

\[
F(R,p,A,\mathcal E,\mathcal F,\tau)
\]

among region \(R\), proposition \(p\), action/capability class \(A\),
evidence/access envelope \(\mathcal E\), fault model \(\mathcal F\), and
horizon \(\tau\).

#### H-SCC-3 — Conservative certification

If every certificate is a post-processing of formed physical records and all
feedback enters through admitted physical controls, the certified extension
cannot change the underlying physical outcome law beyond those controls.

#### H-SCC-4 — Certification hysteresis implies hidden physical state

If crossing a certificate threshold changes later physics after every
standard record and process memory is erased, then the standard physical state
description is incomplete or the dynamics is modified. “The certificate
still mattered” requires a carrier.

#### H-SCC-5 — Regional sufficiency without global biography

Different incomplete regional quotients can each be complete for their frozen
local actions while no single global history is reconstructed. Global binding
is required only for propositions or actions that cross those quotients.

#### H-SCC-6 — Gluing obstruction

Pairwise compatible regional records need not admit a jointly realizable
global section. The smallest obstruction may be topological, categorical,
resource-based, adversarial, or simply an empty physical compatibility
complex.

#### H-SCC-7 — Common-knowledge/public-fact split

Redundant accessibility, distributed knowledge, mutual knowledge, and literal
common knowledge are distinct. A physical public fact may require only a
capability-indexed fixed point weaker than formal common knowledge.

#### H-SCC-8 — Foliation-branch-invariant observer indexing

Observer-indexed records and actions remain necessary operational types whether
or not a hidden preferred foliation/global completion exists. Only the
stronger ontic conclusion depends on rejecting the foliation branch.

#### H-SCC-9 — Complexity leakage needs representation invariance

Logical depth or circuit complexity cannot source a physical residual until
the measure is invariant or covariant under admissible compilers, gate sets,
coarse-grainings, and descriptions.

#### H-SCC-10 — Arena bounds do not select architecture

Entropy, energy, and causal-capacity bounds constrain admissible physical
states. They do not identify what architecture, if any, implements the arena
without an independent architecture-sensitive observable.

#### H-SCC-11 — Hidden-shard equivalence

“Matter or information realized in another inaccessible region” is
operationally an ordinary hidden sector unless its coupling depends on
certification or network topology in a way not representable by covariantly
conserved stress-energy and standard fields.

#### H-SCC-12 — Empirical-equivalence trilemma

A hidden coordination/substrate variable either changes an operational
likelihood, is representation/gauge-equivalent to the baseline, or is
inaccessible. Only the first carries empirical excess.

### Convergence

The twelve reduce to four research fronts:

1. **Coordination spine:** H-SCC-1/2/5/7/8.
2. **Conservative-extension theorem:** H-SCC-3/4/10/12.
3. **Regional descent/obstruction:** H-SCC-5/6 plus topological and
   resource compatibility.
4. **Physical residuals:** H-SCC-9/11 and the council's perspectival-curvature
   and layered-certificate branches.

The cognitive-relocation hypotheses remain controls, not active physical
fronts.

## 8. Position 3 — conservative certification and physical feedback

### Decision

Can layered gossip, provenance, cryptography, consensus, and finality change
physical outcomes without adding a physical state, interaction, control, or
completion?

### Candidate theorem

Let:

- \(X\) be the complete admitted physical process state/history;
- \(r(X)\) be formed physical records;
- \(c=f(r(X))\) be any randomized or deterministic downstream certificate;
- \(u=\pi(c)\) be an admitted physical control selected from the certificate;
  and
- \(Y=T(X,u)\) be the standard physical response.

Then every certificate-dependent change in \(Y\) factors through the admitted
control \(u\). If two cases have the same \(X\) and \(u\), a remaining
certificate-dependent difference requires an extra physical state or modified
dynamics.

### Best approach

1. Prove the finite measurable/Markov form.
2. Generalize to stochastic kernels and complete process tensors.
3. Add adaptive, threshold, zero-knowledge, FHE/MPC, and Byzantine
   certificates as post-processing controls.
4. Add physical feedback explicitly; separate knowledge/capability change
   from source-law change.
5. Attack with certification hysteresis, erased provenance, hidden memory,
   and metalevel-editor countermodels.
6. State exactly when the theorem is data processing, control factorization,
   or a genuinely new composition result.

### Positive and adverse returns

```text
CONSERVATIVE_CERTIFICATION_THEOREM
PHYSICAL_FEEDBACK_FACTORIZATION
EXTRA_STATE_REQUIRED
MODIFIED_DYNAMICS_REQUIRED
COUNTEREXAMPLE_TO_CONSERVATIVITY
```

### Kill

A complete standard physical model with identical \(X\) and \(u\), but a
reproducible response difference determined only by certificate status, kills
the conservative theorem and becomes a Grade-5 candidate residual.

### Stop

Do not hide physical controller memory, certificate carriers, setup state,
energy, or environment behind a semantic “metalevel.”

## Bottom line

The most important new possibility is not that reality is a game engine.
It is that observer-accessible reality may have a **stratified coordination
architecture** in which different facts, regions, observers, and actions
close under different physical and epistemic conditions.

Distributed systems theory supplies a mature map of what becomes possible
only after timing, failure, monotonicity, trust, and coordination assumptions
are paid. Quantum theory and gravity supply different native constraints.
Dynamic Unity's opportunity is to type the intersection precisely and ask
whether the resulting architecture is:

1. a faithful representation of known physics;
2. a reconstruction theorem;
3. a clean impossibility/non-unification result; or
4. a source of one finite physical prediction.

The first swing is the existing portfolio compiler because it is the
highest-information way to decide which of those four can be earned next.
