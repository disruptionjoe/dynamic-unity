# Entangled layered finality: absorption boundary and candidate registry

## Plain-English result

Layered finality can fit entanglement, but that fact alone does not extend
quantum theory.

The cleanest interpretation is that an entangled state constrains the joint
records that separated observers can later form. It is not a set of hidden
local votes waiting for a regional consensus rule. A local record-forming
interaction can erase recoverable joint coherence without changing the
remote marginal, and coherent reversal can restore the state when every
record carrier is retained. This gives a standard-quantum account of
hyper-local, regional, and public records as different physical access and
recovery structures.

The important boundary is:

> A finite finality label that is physically retained and selects ordinary
> quantum channels is itself an ordinary quantum control register.

This absorbs finite thresholds, hysteresis, provenance labels, route labels,
and metastable state machines. Merely renaming those registers “finality”
does not create a measurable delta from quantum mechanics. The sharp
extension question is whether a history-dependent residue remains after a
causal break re-prepares the system and every admitted quantum and classical
memory, while no-signalling and the entangled joint statistics remain
correct.

That residual is not established here. The swing installs the control that a
future claim must beat.

## Exact finite absorption proposition

Let \(H\) be a finite classical history register and let
\(\{\Phi_h\}_{h\in H}\) be a family of linear completely positive
trace-preserving maps on system \(S\). For Kraus representations

\[
\Phi_h(\rho)=\sum_\alpha K_{h\alpha}\rho K_{h\alpha}^{\dagger},
\qquad
\sum_\alpha K_{h\alpha}^{\dagger}K_{h\alpha}=I,
\]

define the controlled map on a classical-quantum state by

\[
\Psi\!\left(\sum_h p_h |h\rangle\!\langle h|\otimes\rho_h\right)
=
\sum_{h,\alpha}p_h |h\rangle\!\langle h|
\otimes K_{h\alpha}\rho_hK_{h\alpha}^{\dagger}.
\]

The operators \(|h\rangle\!\langle h|\otimes K_{h\alpha}\) satisfy Kraus
completeness on the classical-quantum domain. Therefore \(\Psi\) is an
ordinary CPTP process on the enlarged state.

A finite threshold is a classical function written into \(H\). Finite
hysteresis is a finite-state transducer whose memory is included in \(H\).
Finite route and provenance dependence are the same construction. Thus:

> No finite history-labelled family of linear CPTP dynamics is beyond
> standard quantum mechanics merely because the label is called a finality
> state.

This is known controlled-channel, dilation, and quantum-process-memory
terrain. Its value is as a Dynamic Unity program boundary, not as a novelty
claim.

## Executed entanglement gate

`tests/du_entangled_finality_absorption_probe.py` supplies a deterministic
finite numerical fixture for the analytic controls. It verifies:

- a Bell pair has maximally mixed local states, perfect \(ZZ\) and \(XX\)
  correlations, and CHSH value \(2\sqrt 2\);
- local trace-preserving dephasing changes the joint coherence without
  changing the remote marginal;
- orthogonal pointer labels can fan out into multiple records, while one
  fixed unitary cannot perfectly broadcast arbitrary nonorthogonal states;
- exact uncomputation restores the pre-record state for one, two, and three
  record carriers;
- finite threshold and hysteresis rules embed as a classical-quantum
  controlled channel;
- a route-conditioned phase is predicted by the retained route register and
  disappears under route uncomputation;
- a system-only causal break leaves an apparent history residual when a
  memory bit is omitted;
- resetting that memory removes the residual; and
- a naive ensemble-dependent nonlinear escape makes two decompositions of
  the same density operator distinguishable and therefore fails the
  no-signalling gate.

The artifact reports `18/18` checks. It establishes neither an extra
finality variable nor a departure from quantum theory.

## Shared best-approach contract

Every candidate below should use the same sequence:

1. define the smallest process that can express the proposed delta;
2. write the complete standard quantum or physical absorber first;
3. identify the physical register, coupling, graph invariant, resource, or
   tensor that carries the claimed effect;
4. hold terminal states, admitted instruments, routes, environments,
   resources, and entanglement marginals fixed as required;
5. derive one contrast that the completed null predicts to be exactly zero or
   tightly bounded;
6. test the cheap kill before scaling;
7. increase size or apparatus complexity only when the residual survives
   completion and has a preregistered scaling law; and
8. promote only a gauge-, refinement-, and representation-invariant result.

This is deliberately not a ranking rule. It prevents every lead from paying
again for the same omitted-memory and semantic-refit failures.

## Candidate cards

### 1. Finality-memory residual

- **Route:** `H-CCR-15`, `PRED-DU-003`, and `CONCEPT-DU-005`.
- **Conditional conjecture:** there exists a physically complete causal-break
  instrument after which two pre-break certified-history classes have the
  same re-prepared quantum/classical state but different later joint
  statistics.
- **Standard absorber:** retained environment, controller, clock, route,
  reference frame, detector state, or classical history represented in the
  multi-time process.
- **Smallest useful object:** a Bell pair, one history register, a replacement
  channel, and one later \(XX\) correlation test.
- **Best method:** process-tensor causal-break analysis followed by
  all-port tomography and randomized history assignment. Expand the memory
  boundary adversarially before fitting an extra law.
- **First decisive contrast:** the later joint correlation differs between
  histories while every declared post-break state and each remote marginal
  agrees.
- **Cheap kill:** the difference vanishes when one omitted memory or route
  register is reset, or a complete ordinary process predicts it.
- **Scale only if:** a repeatable residual survives nested memory completion,
  leakage bounds, no-signalling, and a frozen effect-size estimator.
- **Current grade:** highest-priority conditional extension; no residual
  observed.

### 2. Graph-threshold actualization

- **Route:** `PRED-DU-001/002` and `CONCEPT-DU-009`.
- **Conditional conjecture:** an invariant of the certified record graph adds
  a non-smooth or universal recoverability term beyond the calibrated
  graph-dependent quantum process, finite classical control, ordinary
  decoherence, error correction, and known critical families.
- **Standard absorber:** finite threshold logic, retained hysteresis memory,
  environment connectivity, QEC/percolation thresholds, and smooth
  architecture-specific reliability.
- **Smallest useful object:** an entangled system with independently
  addressable record fragments and two graph families having matched local
  channels but different global connectivity.
- **Best method:** construct process-equivalent graph pairs where possible,
  then add matched up/down sweeps, exact coherent uncomputation, finite-size
  scaling, held-out sizes, and explicit graph-dependent
  smooth-versus-critical quantum rivals. Use rewiring rather than raw
  record-count changes to separate topology from coupling strength.
- **First decisive contrast:** an invariant graph term predicts a residual
  beyond the calibrated routed quantum process, ideally between two
  process-equivalent implementations with different graph presentations.
- **Cheap kill:** every curve is explained by retained labels, ordinary
  critical dynamics, decoherence, or representation-dependent node count.
- **Scale only if:** a stable order parameter and exponent or hysteresis area
  predict held-out graph sizes without platform-specific redefinition.
- **Current grade:** conditional model family; the universal scalar threshold
  is already rejected.

### 3. Reconciliation curvature

- **Route:** `H-CCR-13/14` and `CONCEPT-DU-006`.
- **Conditional conjecture:** a closed reconciliation loop has an operational
  residue after ordinary dynamical/geometric phase, route registers,
  reference frames, process memory, provenance, and benign graph
  subdivisions are included.
- **Standard absorber:** noncommuting operation order, routed implementation,
  Berry/Pancharatnam or holonomic phase, clock mismatch, and incomplete
  process tomography.
- **Smallest useful object:** three coherent recorders joined in a triangle,
  with route-controlled traversal and an independently calibrated reference
  arm.
- **Best method:** formulate the loop as a quantum comb or groupoid, quotient
  vertex gauge, perform routed process tomography, and run clone,
  relabeling, orientation-reversal, and edge-subdivision controls.
- **First decisive contrast:** a gauge-invariant closed-loop observable or
  resource cost remains when all open paths and pairwise endpoint channels
  are matched.
- **Cheap kill:** the route register, ordinary gate sequence, or a known
  geometric phase predicts the result.
- **Scale only if:** the residue composes under loop concatenation and remains
  invariant under benign refinement.
- **Current grade:** mathematically strong public-fact baseline; new physical
  curvature unobserved.

### 4. Actualization cost

- **Route:** `H-CCR-07`, `HC-DU-035B`, and `CONCEPT-DU-008`.
- **Conditional conjecture:** establishing the same certified distinctions
  and action capability has a nonzero minimum work, heat, or optionality cost
  beyond a complete ordinary resource ledger.
- **Standard absorber:** Landauer erasure, measurement work, memory
  initialization, blank archives, authentication, communication, latency,
  error correction, and coherence loss.
- **Smallest useful object:** two reversible reconciliation circuits that
  end with identical certified records and capabilities but resolve
  differently structured completion classes.
- **Best method:** solve the resource-constrained protocol optimization before
  calorimetry. Use reversible constructions to drive ordinary dissipation
  toward its bound and report a residual confidence interval, not semantic
  “history count.”
- **First decisive contrast:** a strict lower bound depends on an operational
  distinction measure after all known resources and error targets are held
  fixed.
- **Cheap kill:** reversible implementation and full accounting eliminate the
  proposed residual.
- **Scale only if:** the lower bound is encoding-invariant and predicts a
  cross-platform ratio without refitting.
- **Current grade:** known record-coherence backreaction; residual cost open.

### 5. Capability backreaction

- **Route:** `H-CCR-05/06` and `CONCEPT-DU-005`.
- **Conditional conjecture:** physically available but unused recovery
  capability changes outcome statistics even when its interaction channel is
  experimentally null.
- **Standard absorber:** stray controller fields, detector loading, changed
  boundary, feedback, leakage, selection effects, and a changed intervention
  set rather than changed dynamics.
- **Smallest useful object:** randomized presence of isolated recovery
  hardware beside an otherwise identical entangled-record experiment.
- **Best method:** begin with a causal-model and Hamiltonian no-coupling
  theorem. Specify a scalar capability functional and the additional coupling
  law it would source before building hardware.
- **First decisive contrast:** outcome statistics covary with isolated
  capability while every physical interaction and tester remains matched.
- **Cheap kill:** any measured coupling, leakage path, changed controller
  state, or observer-boundary redefinition explains the effect.
- **Scale only if:** a nonzero coupling law is mathematically explicit,
  no-signalling-safe, and not a disguised apparatus interaction.
- **Current grade:** highly speculative and low priority until a law exists.

### 6. Record-sourced geometry or gravity

- **Route:** `HC-DU-038/040` and `CONCEPT-DU-008`.
- **Conditional conjecture:** a covariantly defined record-formation quantity
  contributes to causal or gravitational response beyond standard
  stress-energy and open-system dynamics.
- **Standard absorber:** stress-energy redistribution, entropy flow, control
  fields, ordinary decoherence, environmental mass, and
  representation-dependent record counting.
- **Smallest useful object:** not an apparatus. It is a local covariantly
  conserved candidate source tensor with a well-defined unit map.
- **Best method:** impose diffeomorphism covariance, conservation, locality,
  refinement invariance, and known weak-field bounds symbolically. Only a
  surviving tensor earns a matched-stress-energy mesoscopic design.
- **First decisive contrast:** equal standard stress-energy histories but
  different invariant record-source histories imply a bounded differential
  gravitational observable.
- **Cheap kill:** the source is nonconserved, basis/observer dependent,
  subdivision sensitive, or equivalent to ordinary stress-energy.
- **Scale only if:** the theory closes consistently and supplies a coefficient
  range not already excluded.
- **Current grade:** frontier conjecture below active experimental grade.

## Priority after the swing

The most productive next sequence is:

1. **finality-memory residual**, because the probe now supplies its exact
   omitted-memory counterexample and causal-break target;
2. **graph-threshold actualization**, because it can reuse existing record
   fan-out and threshold fixtures while forcing a genuine scaling result; and
3. **reconciliation curvature**, because the exact holonomy baseline already
   tells us which route effects do not count.

Actualization cost should develop in parallel only at the theorem/resource
ledger level. Capability backreaction and record-sourced geometry should not
consume experimental effort until they have an explicit interaction or
source law.

is not claiming that standard quantum theory lacks history, memory,
thresholds, or layered records. It is asking whether any carefully completed
record/finality construction leaves a residual that those standard
frameworks cannot absorb.
