# HC-DU-035A layered threshold objectivity — novelty audit

## Verdict

The wave does not produce a novel threshold theorem.

Every static mathematical component has a close mature neighbor:

- threshold access and secret sharing;
- Byzantine quorum intersection and probabilistic quorum systems;
- Blackwell/data-processing order and decentralized detection;
- IID concentration and correlated-failure countermodels;
- metastable or probabilistic consensus;
- quantum secret sharing, quantum error correction, quantum Byzantine
  agreement, and verifiable quantum multiparty computation;
- no-broadcasting and spectrum-broadcast objectivity; and
- sharp thresholds in random structures.

Even the tempting cross-domain statement

```text
authorized quantum access
    corresponds to
adversarial quorum intersection
```

is established terrain. Quantum secret sharing constrains authorized and
forbidden sets through no-cloning; Byzantine quorum systems constrain quorum
and fault families through honest intersection.

The defensible value of `HC-DU-035A` is a **typed synthesis and exact control
suite**. It prevents reconstruction, consistency, finality, objectivity,
criticality, and capability from being collapsed into one count.

The residual novelty zone is much narrower:

> Begin with a physically selected and dynamically formed record instrument,
> then derive a nontrivial lower bound or necessary-and-sufficient condition
> on the resources required to make its distinction simultaneously
> fork-safe, independently accessible, broadcastable or approximately
> commutative, and bounded-risk actionable.

No located source establishes that full formation/finality/action/resource
statement. This bounded audit therefore grades it
`POSSIBLE CONJUNCTION / SEARCH-INCOMPLETE`, not novel.

## Classification rule

- **KNOWN** — a standard theorem or direct result in the primary literature.
- **SPECIALIZATION** — the DU statement follows by instantiating known
  mathematics.
- **POSSIBLE CONJUNCTION** — ingredients are known, but a precisely stated
  coupled theorem may add something.
- **SEARCH-INCOMPLETE** — no novelty language is licensed.
- **UNSUPPORTED** — the proposed inference is contradicted by the typed
  contract or neighboring literature.

## Primary collision map

### Threshold access, consistency, and adversarial quorums

- Adi Shamir,
  [“How to Share a Secret”](https://doi.org/10.1145/359168.359176),
  establishes polynomial threshold secret sharing. Reconstruction threshold is
  not by itself dealer consistency, agreement, or finality.
- Dahlia Malkhi and Michael Reiter,
  [“Byzantine Quorum Systems”](https://doi.org/10.1007/s004460050050),
  establish the adversarial quorum-system setting in which consistency
  requires intersections that cannot be wholly faulty.
- Dahlia Malkhi, Michael Reiter, Avishai Wool, and Rebecca Wright,
  [“Probabilistic Byzantine Quorum
  Systems”](https://doi.org/10.1145/277697.277781),
  already combine quorum structure with probabilistic masking.
- Maofan Yin and colleagues,
  [“HotStuff: BFT Consensus in the Lens of
  Blockchain”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.05069),
  show why locking, phases, view change, timing, responsiveness, and quorum
  certificates are protocol structure beyond static intersection.

These sources make the locked-quorum theorem, converse, and stochastic-fault
tail controls rather than novelty claims.

### Statistical experiments, hard decisions, and correlated evidence

- David Blackwell,
  [“Equivalent Comparisons of
  Experiments”](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177729032),
  supplies the decision-theoretic information order behind the hardening
  inequality.
- Z. Chair and P. Varshney,
  [“Optimal Data Fusion in Multiple Sensor Detection
  Systems”](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAES.1986.310699),
  is direct primary terrain for local decisions and fusion.
- Wassily Hoeffding,
  [“Probability Inequalities for Sums of Bounded Random
  Variables”](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1963.10500830),
  supplies the IID concentration control.
- Svante Janson,
  [“Large Deviations for Sums of Partly Dependent Random
  Variables”](https://doi.org/10.1002/rsa.20008),
  illustrates why dependence assumptions must be explicit.

The DU two-fragment strict fixture and common-shock no-go are exact and
useful, but they are standard decision/reliability specializations.

### Metastability, thresholds, and common knowledge

- Team Rocket and colleagues,
  [“Scalable and Probabilistic Leaderless BFT Consensus through

  make metastability a declared probabilistic consensus mechanism rather than
  hard finality by vocabulary.
- Ehud Friedgut and Gil Kalai,
  [“Every Monotone Graph Property Has a Sharp
  Threshold”](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-96-03732-X),

  [“Scaling Theory for Finite-Size Effects in the Critical

- Joseph Halpern and Yoram Moses,
  [“Knowledge and Common Knowledge in a Distributed
  Environment”](https://doi.org/10.1145/79147.79161),
  separate local, distributed, and common knowledge.

## Residual contribution test

```text
physically selected record formation
    + causal provenance
    + action-sufficient statistical channel
    + adversarial incompatibility and locking
    + independently accessible public algebra
    + disturbance / memory / redundancy / latency / entropy ledger
```

1. a tight coupled lower bound;
2. a necessary-and-sufficient formation-to-finality condition;

   fixtures.
