## Executive judgment

I did not treat `HC-DU-035A` or thresholds as the assignment. I surveyed the
new Certified Causal Reality program, its exact recorder/archive work, causal

The most worthy bounded attempt from an orthodox scientific perspective is
the first exact specialization of the program's decisive ontology
discriminator:

> Given an independently frozen certified-record quotient, does every
> admitted intervention response factor through it, or does a finite
> intervention expose a remainder?

For finite deterministic controlled processes, the answer is exact:

1. trace-response sufficiency is equivalent to behavioral equivalence inside
   every record fiber;
2. if it fails, a separating intervention word exists after at most
   `n-2` action inputs for an `n`-state comparison system, counting the
   initial readout as free;
3. the bound is tight;
4. the behavioral quotient is the unique coarsest transition-stable
   refinement preserving the frozen record and target readout;
5. a stronger autonomous Markov quotient requires a separate local
   transition-congruence condition; and
6. there is no size-free finite certification horizon.

This is a useful baseline for `HC-DU-036`, but it is not a new theorem.
Moore's 1956 sequential-machine results already contain the state-
distinguishability and direct-sum machinery, with a one-step difference in
length convention because Moore counts the initial output as an experimental
stage. The result should be banked, if at all, only as an exact known
specialization and a program stop rule.

The important scientific consequence is not “records are sufficient.” It is:

> A finite failure to find a remainder supports record sufficiency only
> relative to an independently justified bound on behavioral complexity.
> Without such a bound, a rival can always agree through the tested horizon
> and diverge later.

That sharply narrows the next publishable target. Dynamic Unity now needs a
physical or operational replacement for raw hidden-state count—such as
reachable process rank, memory dimension, or certified-channel codimension—
and a bound that survives a quantum history fixture and an adversarial
distributed fixture without redefining the objects.

## 2. Frozen finite contract

This swing deliberately chooses the smallest class in which the central
discriminator has a sharp answer.

Let

\[
\mathcal M=(S,A,\{\delta_a\}_{a\in A},r,y,S_0)
\]

where:

- `S` is a finite physical or hidden state set, `|S|=n>=2`;
- `A` is a finite, frozen intervention alphabet;
- each `delta_a:S->S` is a deterministic transition;
- `r:S->Q` is an independently specified certified-record label;
- `y:S->Y` is a separately specified observer-accessible target readout;
- `S_0 subseteq S` is the declared comparison surface of admissible current
  realizations; and
- state, action, record, and output relabelings have no physical significance.

The visible symbol is

\[
v(s)=(r(s),y(s)).
\]

For an intervention word \(w=a_1\cdots a_k\), write
\(\delta_w=\delta_{a_k}\circ\cdots\circ\delta_{a_1}\) and define the complete
response function

\[
B_s(w)=v(\delta_w(s)).
\]

Because every prefix is itself an admitted word, equality of \(B_s\) and
\(B_t\) is equivalent to equality of their complete visible intervention
traces.

### What is supplied rather than derived

This class supplies:

- exact finite hidden-state closure and the number `n`;
- the complete intervention alphabet;
- exact deterministic transitions;
- exact readout with no noise or disturbance;
- the record label `r`, including any provenance or certificate semantics it
  is claimed to have;
- unit intervention cost and a free initial readout; and
- no Byzantine behavior, stochastic dependence, quantum coherence, resource
  export, or interface-selection problem.

Consequently, the theorem does **not** form a record, certify its provenance,
derive finality, select an observer, or establish physical ontology. It asks
only whether a supplied record label is sufficient for a supplied finite
response contract.

### Rival and falsifier

The rivals are two states, or two states in the disjoint union of two models,
that have the same current record label. A falsifier of record sufficiency is
a frozen intervention word whose visible response differs.

A falsifier of the theorem would be:

- a distinguishable pair in an `n`-state deterministic process with no
  distinguishing word of at most `n-2` action inputs;
- a stable visible congruence not refining the computed behavioral quotient;
  or
- a quotient passing the local Markov test but failing some finite visible
  trace.

## 3. Exact theorem package

### Definition — finite behavioral equivalence

Define

\[
s\equiv_\infty t
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
B_s(w)=B_t(w)\quad\text{for every }w\in A^*.
\]

Equivalently, begin with

\[
E_0=\ker(v)
\]

and recursively define

\[
E_{k+1}
=
E_k\cap
\bigcap_{a\in A}
(\delta_a\times\delta_a)^{-1}(E_k).
\]

Then \(sE_kt\) exactly when no word of at most `k` action inputs separates
their terminal visible symbols.

### Theorem 1 — finite trace factorization or bounded witness

On the declared comparison surface \(S_0\), the following are equivalent:

1. there exists a response map
   \[
   \bar B:r(S_0)\longrightarrow V^{A^*}
   \]
   such that \(B_s=\bar B(r(s))\) for every \(s\in S_0\);
2. for all \(s,t\in S_0\),
   \[
   r(s)=r(t)\Longrightarrow s\equiv_\infty t;
   \]
3. every observer-accessible visible intervention prediction in this
   contract factors through the current record label on \(S_0\).

If these conditions fail, some \(s,t\in S_0\) with the same record label have
a separating intervention word \(w\) satisfying

\[
\boxed{|w|\le n-2}.
\]

The factor \(\bar B\) is unique on the realized record image \(r(S_0)\).

#### Proof

Conditions 1 and 2 are the elementary quotient-factorization criterion:
\(\bar B(q)\) is well-defined exactly when every realized state with record
label `q` has the same response function. Condition 3 is the operational
reading of the same equality because \(B_s\) contains every admitted
intervention response.

For the length bound, \(E_0\) is the visible-output partition. If every state
has the same visible output, no transition can ever lead to a different
visible output, because all states remain inside the same constant-output
set. Thus any distinguishable process has at least two \(E_0\) blocks.

Every nonstationary refinement \(E_k\to E_{k+1}\) adds at least one block.
There are at most `n` blocks. Therefore the refinement reaches
\(E_\infty\) after at most `n-2` strict steps. If two states are not
behaviorally equivalent, the refinement step that first separates them
constructs a word of that length. \(\square\)

### Theorem 2 — the minimal stable remainder refinement

The quotient

\[
S/E_\infty
\]

is the coarsest partition of `S` that:

1. refines equality of the supplied record and target readout; and
2. is stable under every admitted intervention transition.

More explicitly, if \(P\) is any equivalence relation such that

\[
P\subseteq\ker(v)
\]

and

\[
sPt\Longrightarrow\delta_a(s)P\delta_a(t)
\quad\text{for every }a\in A,
\]

then

\[
P\subseteq E_\infty.
\]

#### Proof

Induct on word length. The first condition gives visible equality at the
empty word. Transition stability carries a \(P\)-related pair through every
word, after which visible equality still holds. Thus every \(P\)-related pair
has identical response functions and lies in \(E_\infty\). The recursive
definition of \(E_\infty\) itself makes it visible-respecting and
transition-stable. \(\square\)

This is the minimal **deterministic behavioral** remainder label inside the
supplied model. It is not automatically an ontic variable or physically
accessible record.

### Theorem 3 — response factorization is weaker than an autonomous record dynamics

The supplied record quotient `Q` itself supports a well-defined deterministic
quotient process with target readout,

\[
\bar\delta_a(r(s))=r(\delta_a(s)),
\qquad
\bar y(r(s))=y(s),
\]

if and only if, for every pair \(s,t\in S\) in the same record fiber,

\[
y(s)=y(t)
\]

and

\[
r(\delta_a(s))=r(\delta_a(t))
\quad\text{for every }a\in A.
\]

These are exactly target factorization and transition congruence. When they
hold, induction gives every future record and target response from `Q`.
Conversely, a well-defined quotient process forces both equalities.

This distinction matters:

- response factorization on a restricted comparison surface says a current
  record label fixes the full declared response there;
- an autonomous Markov record state says the same quotient has a closed
  one-step law on the complete declared state space.

The first must not be silently promoted to the second.

### Theorem 4 — the bound is tight

For every `n>=2`, take states `0,...,n-1`, one intervention `a`,

\[
\delta_a(i)=\min(i+1,n-1),
\]

a constant record label, and target output

\[
y(i)=
\begin{cases}
1,&i=n-1,\\
0,&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]

On comparison surface \(S_0=\{0,1\}\), the first target difference appears
after exactly `n-2` applications of `a`:

\[
0\mapsto n-2,\qquad 1\mapsto n-1.
\]

Thus no smaller uniform action-input bound is possible under the present
convention.

### Corollary 4a — two-model physical-remainder bound

For two deterministic models with `n_1` and `n_2` states, common intervention
and output alphabets, and aligned current certified records, form their
disjoint union. If the nominated current states differ operationally, some
word of length at most

\[
\boxed{n_1+n_2-2}
\]

separates them.

Agreement through that horizon proves operational equivalence only inside
this complete deterministic model class. It does not prove that either
state-space description is physically fundamental.

### Corollary 4b — no size-free finite certification horizon

For every proposed finite horizon `H`, choose the tight chain with
`n=H+3`. Its two nominated same-record starts agree through every intervention
word of length at most `H` and first differ at `H+1`.

Therefore:

> No fixed finite amount of agreement certifies unrestricted record
> sufficiency when hidden behavioral complexity is unbounded.

This is the most important stop supplied by the swing.

### Corollary 4c — bounded-risk capability within the trace contract

Suppose an action policy, its payoff or loss, verification cost, and reversal
risk are all functions only of the declared visible intervention trace. If
two realizations are behaviorally equivalent, they have the same feasible
trace-based policies and the same outcomes under those policies.

Hence, in this deterministic class:

- trace-response factorization entails factorization of every
  trace-measurable capability; and
- a claimed capability difference either contains a separating intervention
  trace or depends on hidden structure absent from the declared target
  readout/resource contract.

This is a scoped bridge to `HC-DU-037`, not a general finality-capability law.

## 5. Primary-source collision check

The exact deterministic result is occupied terrain.

- E. F. Moore's
  [*Gedanken-Experiments on Sequential Machines*](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~cdm/resources/Moore1956-gedanken-experiments.pdf)
  develops state distinguishability by recursively refining output
  partitions. Its Theorem 6 gives `n-1` experimental stages and its Theorem 7
  applies the construction to a direct sum of two machines. Moore counts the
  initial output as the first stage; this memo counts only subsequent action
  inputs, giving `n-2` and `n_1+n_2-2` respectively.
- Nerode's
  [*Linear Automaton Transformations*](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1958-0135681-9)
  is foundational prior art for behavioral quotients and minimal finite
  automata.
- Tzeng's
  [probabilistic-automata equivalence algorithm](https://doi.org/10.1137/0221017)
  shows that moving from deterministic to probabilistic response equivalence
  does not by itself create a novel research zone.
- Finite quantum automata also have bounded equivalence tests; for example,
  Li and Qiu give dimension-dependent horizons for several
  [one-way quantum finite-automaton classes](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0703087).
- For actual multi-time quantum physics, process tensors already provide an
  operational framework for arbitrary interventions and memory:
  [Pollock et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00589).

Therefore the honest novelty ledger is:

| item | grade |
|---|---|
| deterministic response factorization iff behavioral equivalence | known / direct specialization |
| partition-refinement witness construction | known |
| `n-2` action-input convention and tight chain | known result under shifted counting convention / direct fixture |
| disjoint-union two-model reduction | known |
| no size-free horizon | direct corollary |
| response-versus-autonomous-Markov typing inside CCR | useful program synthesis, not shown novel |
| capability corollary for trace-measurable utilities | elementary specialization |
| physical certified-process rank bound across quantum and distributed fixtures | open / claim-specific search incomplete |

The deterministic result should not be drafted as a standalone novelty paper.
Its value is that it fixes the baseline, exposes the missing physical
quantity, and prevents finite-fit inflation.

## 6. What this changes in the whole program

The central conjecture becomes more precise.

The phrase “bounded-complexity intervention” is incomplete until the
comparison class supplies a complexity measure. In the finite deterministic
baseline, that measure is hidden-state count. But raw hidden-state count is
not a good foundational invariant:

- equivalent machines can contain redundant states;
- physical models may be continuous or quantum;
- a quantum state space has continuum many pure states even at finite Hilbert
  dimension;
- adversarial systems include scheduler, fault, and provenance structure;
- a record quotient may be sufficient on a restricted experiment surface
  without being an autonomous state variable; and
- an unknown complexity bound turns every finite pass into
  underidentification.

The likely physically meaningful replacement is a **minimal reachable
behavioral dimension relative to the frozen intervention and readout
contract**, not substrate state count. Candidate realizations include:

- the number of classes in a minimal deterministic behavioral quotient;
- Hankel or predictive-state rank for stochastic/linear systems;
- reachable effect-space dimension for a process tensor;
- memory or bond dimension under a frozen quantum instrument family;
- adversarial state/provenance complexity after scheduler and replay
  quotients; and
- a resource-bounded covering number in approximate/noisy classes.

This is a stronger direction than merely “apply bisimulation.” It asks
whether one operational complexity coordinate controls:

1. how long one must intervene before a hidden physical remainder appears;
2. how much additional record structure is needed to restore sufficiency; and
3. whether the same bound retains its meaning in a quantum and a distributed
   history.

It may still collide with weighted-automata, realization, process-discrimination,
or model-checking results. The publishable burden is the certified-history,
provenance, adversary, resource, and cross-platform conjunction.

## 9. Newly visible hypotheses, theorems, and conjectures

Ranked by scientific leverage rather than confidence:

### 1. Certified behavioral-rank witness theorem

**Conjecture.** For a finite-dimensional stochastic or quantum multi-time
process, a frozen certified-record supermap, admitted tester space, and
resource boundary determine a minimal reachable behavioral rank `d_B`. Either
every admitted response factors through the record image, or a separating
tester sequence exists with complexity bounded by `d_B` rather than an
arbitrary substrate-state count.

**Why it matters.** This is the natural physical lift of the exact baseline
and directly sharpens `HC-DU-036`.

**Collision risk.** High: weighted automata, realization theory, quantum
finite automata, and process-tensor tomography already provide rank and
dimension bounds. Novelty would require the independent certification,
provenance/adversary, cost, and cross-platform conjunction.

### 2. Minimal remainder-dimension correspondence

**Conjecture.** Relative to a fixed record channel and intervention family,
the smallest additional accessible statistic that restores response
sufficiency has size or rank equal to the behavioral distinctions lying
inside record fibers. In approximate theories, the corresponding quantity is
a resource-bounded covering or robust rank.

**Finite antecedent.** Exact here: the minimal refinement is
`S/E_infinity`.

**Risk.** The general statement may collapse to minimal realization or
sufficient-statistic theory unless record formation and access cost enter
the theorem.

### 3. Certified coherent-history remainder witness

**Hypothesis.** Two processes can share every supplied classical certified
record and terminal statistic while a fixed coherent-recombination tester
separates them. The minimal witness cost is controlled by the component of
the process tensor annihilated by the record channel but visible to the
admitted tester space.

**Repository antecedent.** `HC-DU-031C` already supplies an
interface-relative reunion witness. The next step is to express it as a
frozen process-quotient kernel statement rather than another pointer
calculation.

### 4. Trace-capability sufficiency theorem with costs

**Hypothesis.** Under a frozen stochastic loss, verification, latency, and
rollback contract, certified-response equivalence entails equality of
bounded-risk capability sets. A strict capability difference is dual to a
cost-admissible separating tester.

**Finite antecedent.** Exact here for deterministic trace-measurable
utilities.

**Risk.** Without public-finality and resource structure, this is likely a
Blackwell/decision-theory specialization.

### 5. Cross-platform behavioral-complexity invariance

**Conjecture.** At least one normalized quantity—perhaps minimal separating
tester cost divided by reachable behavioral rank—retains the same typed
meaning in a coherent quantum-history fixture and an adversarial
database/consensus fixture.

**Failure condition.** If the “rank,” tester, certificate, or cost must be
redefined per platform, the result remains analogy.

### 6. Complexity-bounded sufficiency is the strongest empirical verdict

**Philosophical-scientific conjecture.** No finite experiment establishes
unrestricted record sufficiency. The strongest empirically defensible
positive verdict is sufficiency relative to an independently warranted
model-complexity and intervention-resource class, plus survived attempts to
expand that class.

The deterministic no-horizon family supports this framing but does not prove
it for every physical theory.

## 12. Plain-English interpretation

The repository's biggest question is now a little less mystical.

Imagine two possible underlying realities that have produced exactly the
same certified records so far. In a genuinely bounded finite system, there is
a deadline: either every experiment will keep giving the same answer, or
some experiment no longer than a calculable number of steps will expose the
difference. We proved and exhaustively checked that clean finite version.

But the deadline comes from knowing how complicated the hidden system is. If
we do not know that, there is always a larger rival that imitates all the
tests we have run and breaks on the next one. So “we have not found a
difference” is not yet “records contain all of reality.”

That tells Dynamic Unity what the next profound object probably is: not
another threshold and not another metaphor about information, but a
physically justified measure of how much distinct future behavior a system
can have. If certified records capture all of that behavioral capacity, they
are sufficient for the declared observer. If they do not, the cheapest
experiment that reaches the missing behavior is the concrete physical
remainder.
