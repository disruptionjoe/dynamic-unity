# Philosopher of science: successor wave

## Executive judgment

The enlarged corpus changes one major research judgment.

The first wave treated cosmological renormalization at causal posts
(`WF-D1`) as a conjectural long-term route. It is not conjectural at that
level. In classical sequential growth (CSG), conditioning on a post with
`p=|past(omega)|` induces the exact projective coupling map

```text
tilde t_n^(p) = sum_(k=0)^p binom(p,k) t_(n+k),
M(t)_n        = t_n + t_(n+1).
```

For the bare family

```text
t_n = t^n/n!,
```

the declared large-`p` regime approaches transitive percolation with

```text
t_eff ~ sqrt(t/p).
```

This is a covariant history-to-hierarchy mechanism already present in the
causal-set literature. It is mathematically distinct from the additive/CLT
route to a half-power. It is also not yet `Lambda`.

The most accurate ontological grade is:

> **conditional historical large-number transduction.**

The post-past cardinality is an invariant property of the order, not a birth
label. Changing matched invariant prehistory changes the conditional future
measure. The effective coupling therefore is not empty gauge bookkeeping.
But the fundamental CSG coupling sequence has not changed; the new sequence
is the conditional law seen after excluding the pre-post region. Within one
post era it can be frozen with no loss. That is memory or hysteresis at the
effective level, not demonstrated online feedback or an active return arrow.

The half-power is also family-selected. For the admissible nonnegative family

```text
t_n = t^n/(n!)^alpha,
```

a saddle estimate gives

```text
t_eff ~ t^(1/(alpha+1)) p^(-alpha/(alpha+1)).
```

The probe finds slopes `0`, `-0.4962`, and `-0.6397` for
`alpha=0,1,2`, approaching the predicted `0`, `-1/2`, and `-2/3`.
Post conditioning does not force `-1/2`; the factorial family does.

## 4. Bounded evidence-producing swing

### 4.1 Exact post-conditioning object

For CSG couplings `t_n`, a post `omega` with

```text
p = |past(omega)|
```

changes the effective after-post weights by:

```text
tilde t_n^(p) = sum_(k=0)^p binom(p,k)t_(n+k).
```

Equivalently:

```text
M(t)_n = t_n + t_(n+1)
tilde t^(p) = M^p(t).
```

Only ratios of the effective `t_n` matter. The probe multiplies every bare
coupling by `37` and recovers the same projective law to
`1.14e-13` maximum error.

This resolves one ontological question:

- `p` is unchanged by relabeling the same causal order;
- the common scale of `tilde t` is gauge;
- the projective ratios are not gauge, because they alter future transition
  probabilities.

### 4.2 The half-power and its family dependence

For

```text
t_n=t^n/n!,
```

and large `p`, the terms relevant to fixed `n` permit:

```text
binom(p,k) ~ p^k/k!

tilde t_n^(p)
  ~ t^n sum_k (pt)^k/[k!(n+k)!]
  = (t/p)^(n/2) I_n(2 sqrt(pt)).
```

Thus:

```text
tilde t_(n+1)/tilde t_n
  ~ sqrt(t/p) I_(n+1)(2sqrt(pt))/I_n(2sqrt(pt))
  -> sqrt(t/p).
```

That is the known factorial-family result.

To test whether the exponent belongs to posts or to the family, I generalized
only the nonnegative coupling sequence:

```text
t_n=t^n/(n!)^alpha.
```

The ratio of neighboring summands has saddle:

```text
A_(k+1)/A_k
  ~ pt/k^(alpha+1)

k_* ~ (pt)^(1/(alpha+1)).
```

Consequently:

```text
tilde t_(n+1)/tilde t_n
  ~ t/k_*^alpha
  ~ t^(1/(alpha+1)) p^(-alpha/(alpha+1)).
```

The finite exact convolution gives:

| `alpha` | Predicted exponent | Measured tail slope |
|---:|---:|---:|
| `0` | `0` | `0.000000` |
| `1` | `-1/2` | `-0.496220` |
| `2` | `-2/3` | `-0.639661` |

The `alpha=2` finite range has not reached its asymptote as closely as
`alpha=1`; this is finite numerical support for the saddle estimate, not a
general theorem. It is enough to defeat universality of the half-power under
post conditioning: `alpha=0` is an exact no-running counterfamily.

### 4.3 The two-model identifiability counterexample

Define:

```text
HISTORICAL MODEL H
  bare family B is fixed
  invariant post past p is part of the history
  Q_H(p)=projectivize[M^p(B)]
  future Y~CSG(Q_H(p))

FROZEN-LAW MODEL F
  choose the full infinite projective sequence
  Q_F=(tilde t_n^(p0)/tilde t_1^(p0))_(n>=1)
  as the fundamental post-era law
  omit the edge p->Q
  future Y~CSG(Q_F)
```

At the observed history `p=p0`:

```text
Q_H(p0) = Q_F
```

exactly. Every post-era transition distribution is therefore the same. A
fixed-history replay using the complete effective law cannot tell whether
that law was inherited or fundamental. This exact equivalence is definitional
at the level of the full infinite sequence and the same originary transition
rule. The executable artifact represents only `n=1..6`; its zero difference
checks that finite represented sector, not the entire infinite law.

At a matched different history `p=p1`:

```text
Q_H(p1) != Q_H(p0)
Q_F      = Q_H(p0).
```

The models separate. In the probe's factorial fixture:

```text
p0 = 4096
p1 = 16384
q_eff(p1)/q_eff(p0) ~ 0.5009
half-power prediction          = 0.5000.
```

A one-parameter percolation replay using the first effective ratio is already
close but not identical: its maximum projective error through `n=6` is
`0.08284`. A replay using the complete effective vector is exactly identical
within that era. Resetting to the bare factorial law is strongly
distinguishable.

### 4.4 What replay identifies

| Replay/intervention | What it tests | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Freeze the complete conditioned law within `p0` era | Are online coupling updates required inside the era? | No |

```text

```

### 4.5 The global-post caveat

The cardinality of the past of a specified post is invariant. But being a
post means being comparable with every event, including all future events.
An element born above all currently existing elements is not thereby
certified to remain below every future element.

Therefore:

- the exact result is a conditional law given a post;
- using “post detected” as an online feedback trigger can import future
  information unless replaced by a finite, presently observable order event;
- post selection is not a birth-label problem, but it can be a filtration or
  retrospective-selection problem.

This distinction is absent from a bare “order-defined landmark” label.

### 4.6 Historical transfer versus generated scale

The mechanism begins with order-one `t` and converts a large realized
historical number `p` into a small effective coupling. That is meaningful
naturalness progress:

```text

```

But it does not yet explain:

- why the factorial family is selected;
- why repeated posts occur for that family;
- the distribution or typicality of large `p`;
- why our era should be conditioned on the required history;
- a microscopic-to-physical unit map; or
- an identity between `t_eff` and `Lambda`.

Calling this “merely transferred” would understate a real endogenous
historical mechanism. Calling it “generated from nothing” would hide the
contingent large-number input. `HISTORICAL-LARGE-NUMBER-TRANSDUCTION` preserves
both facts.
