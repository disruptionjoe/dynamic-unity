# Objective-collapse event process to certified-record adjudication

## Plain-English recommendation

The next series should ask whether an objective-collapse theory supplies the
physical ingredient that Dynamic Unity has repeatedly found missing:

> Does a law that selects real spacetime occurrences also select a retained,
> provenance-bearing record accessible to an observer, or does the record
> interface remain an additional physical construction?

This is now the highest-value prepared route because it changes the
antecedent rather than changing the representation of the same antecedent.
The completed `HC-DU-075..079` chain showed that output fields, subsystem
factors, modular data, self-tested correlations, and indivisible-stochastic
lifts do not by themselves select the physical carrier, interface, gauge,
complete process, or archive. Objective-collapse models are different in one
important respect: some versions explicitly posit a stochastic physical event
process or a matter-density ontology, and they make deviations from ordinary
unitary quantum mechanics in principle measurable.

That does **not** mean flashes, collapses, or matter density are already
certified records. It means they are the best current hostile case against
the claim that the physical-formation premise is simply unavailable.

The five positions below form one conditional funnel:

```text
collapse law and primitive ontology
  -> physically selected occurrence or matter process
  -> retained provenance-bearing archive, or an exact failure
  -> bounded observer access and held-out target sufficiency
  -> unchanged relativistic and cross-ontology transfer
  -> finite excess, no-go, or North-Star disposition
```

Only Swing 1 is prepared for a later explicit authorization. Swings 2–5 are
conditional cards, not pre-authorized work.

## 1. Why this wins the whole-repository priority tournament

The tournament was run against the repository's current evidence, not against
paper readiness or personal appeal.

| Develop another consensus or cryptographic analogy | high conceptual | low physical readiness | Physical adapter and occurrence identity remain unselected | Use later as a composition method, not as the trunk |

Objective collapse wins for five reasons:

1. **It changes a load-bearing premise.** A flash ontology supplies random
   spacetime points as local beables; a mass-density ontology supplies a
   spacetime matter field. This is not another Hilbert representation of an
   unchanged reduced channel.
2. **It directly probes the central type wall.** A selected occurrence is
   still not automatically a retained record. The campaign can identify the
   exact first missing arrow.
3. **It has empirical content.** Collapse models make deviations from ordinary
   quantum mechanics and are experimentally bounded. A clean null therefore
   cannot be rescued merely by calling the same mathematics a new ontology.
4. **It is locally decidable before hardware.** Primary-source reconstruction,
   exact finite stochastic models, and small counterexamples can decide the
   formation and sufficiency boundaries. No provider or quantum computer is
   needed.
5. **Every branch is valuable.** A positive would reopen physical record
   formation. A negative could show that even explicit objective occurrences
   do not select archives and access. A split would separate physical
   finalization from observer certification more sharply than the current
   literature-facing map.

## 2. New input and source discipline

The immediate reopener comes from the newest Time as Finality conclusion, but
that sibling result is an input, not Dynamic Unity evidence. Its scoped
finding is that objective-collapse **rates** are genuinely discriminable
while a preferred slicing is not required. Dynamic Unity must reconstruct the
underlying primary sources and apply its own typed contract.

The initial source set is deliberately small:

- Roderich Tumulka,
  [“A Relativistic Version of the Ghirardi--Rimini--Weber Model”](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0406094):
  a Lorentz-covariant stochastic set of spacetime flashes, without a preferred
  slicing, for the paper's stated model class.
- Roderich Tumulka,
  [“A Relativistic GRW Flash Process With Interaction”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.00482):
  an interacting extension built from a supplied interaction-local
  Tomonaga--Schwinger evolution; the paper explicitly notes compatibility
  with both flash and matter-density ontologies and limits concerning
  indistinguishable or variable particle number.
- Daniel Bedingham et al.,
  [“Matter Density and Relativistic Models of Wave Function Collapse”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.1425):
  a relativistic matter-density proposal based on the wave function on the
  past light cone.
- Valia Allori, Sheldon Goldstein, Roderich Tumulka, and Nino Zanghì,
  [“On the Common Structure of Bohmian Mechanics and the Ghirardi--Rimini--Weber Theory”](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0603027):
  the primitive-ontology distinction among particles, fields, and discrete
  spacetime points.
- Roderich Tumulka,
  [“Paradoxes and Primitive Ontology in Collapse Theories of Quantum Mechanics”](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.5767):
  an explicit statement that real collapse dynamics and primitive ontology
  are distinct theoretical ingredients.
- Sandro Donadi et al.,
  [“Underground test of gravity-related wave function collapse”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.13490):
  a finite empirical bound that rules out the natural parameter-free
  Diósi--Penrose version tested there.

The source set may expand only when a missing definition or claimed theorem
requires it. A broad review does not substitute for exact model primitives.

## 3. The type wall this campaign must preserve

For a collapse theory, declare:

```text
L       stochastic dynamical law and parameters
PO      primitive ontology: flashes, matter density, or another local beable
H       complete physical event/matter history
W       blank-to-written formation map
P       occurrence and source provenance
R_tau   retained carrier over a finite horizon tau
X_A     observer-A bounded access map
U_A     target-independent intervention/action menu
C_A     rival-relative certificate/finality rule
M_L     lawful completion class under L
G       physical gauge/equivalence
q_A     certified observer record
T       independently escrowed future target
```

Keep the following distinctions explicit:

```text
real collapse
  != primitive ontology
  != occurrence event
  != event history
  != retained physical record
  != observer-accessible record
  != rival-excluding certificate
  != public finality
  != target reconstruction.
```

Three selection questions must not be collapsed:

1. Does the stochastic law \(L\) select a probability law over histories?
2. Does the complete theory independently specify the primitive ontology
   \(PO\)?
3. Does the resulting physical process select \(W,P,R_\tau,X_A,C_A\), or are
   those supplied apparatus and observer structures?

The campaign may earn a conditional result relative to a fully stated
\((L,PO)\). It may not report that the collapse dynamics *derives* the
primitive ontology if multiple ontologies are compatible with the same
dynamics.

### Held-out target

Before testing any record candidate, escrow:

> the distribution of one finite downstream macroscopic response under a
> fixed post-formation intervention and matched resource budget.

The target may be instantiated as a later pointer response, detector
response, or bounded action/capability outcome. It must not define the flash
set, matter-density field, archive, access map, or collapse parameters.

### Standard absorbers

Every swing must test:

- ordinary unitary/open-system dynamics plus decoherence;
- the collapse law without primitive-ontology selection;
- empirical equivalence among primitive ontologies where applicable;
- target-coded detector, pointer, or archive choice;
- complete-environment or process-tensor repair;
- archive relocation and inaccessible-support twins;
- law-only target determination;
- resource-expanded tomography; and
- contract retyping after a failure.

## 6. Swing 2 — occurrence-to-archive formation and provenance

### Activation

names an independently escrowed target.

### Exact question

Does the admitted collapse ontology physically turn a blank finite carrier
into a retained record bound to an occurrence, or does it supply only an
event history or terminal macroscopic pattern?

### Smallest arena

Use one two-branch microscopic source, one finite amplification chain, one
blank macroscopic carrier, and one finite retention horizon. Compare:

- a genuine collapse/flash-produced history;
- a preloaded terminal pattern;
- a no-event trajectory with the same terminal accessible macrostate where
  admitted;
- an archive-relocation twin; and

formation witness or counterexample not already visible analytically.

```text
ENDOGENOUS_FORMED_PROVENANCE_ARCHIVE
```

candidate record and future-readable state but different claimed occurrence
provenance, or move the history information outside the observer boundary
without changing the source law.

that survives the matched controls.

## 7. Swing 3 — bounded access, lawful fibre, and first leak

### Activation

Swing 2 supplies a formed finite-horizon record or an explicitly
ontology-conditional record construction.

### Exact question

Does the bounded accessible collapse record restrict a nonempty lawful
completion fibre enough to determine the escrowed downstream target beyond
what the collapse law alone already fixes?

### Formal decision

Let:

\[
\mathcal M_L
\]

be the lawful collapse histories modulo gauge and physically irrelevant
ontology equivalence, and define:

\[
\mathcal F_{\rm law}(a)
=
\{m\in\mathcal M_L:A(m)=a\},
\]

\[
\mathcal F_{\rm rec}(a,q_A)
=
\{m\in\mathcal F_{\rm law}(a):R_A(m)=q_A\}.
\]

Compare:

\[
\operatorname{diam}T(\mathcal F_{\rm law})
\quad\text{and}\quad
\operatorname{diam}T(\mathcal F_{\rm rec}).
\]

The useful outcomes are:

- law-only closure;
- strict record-assisted contraction;
- exact target constancy after recording;
- a same-record/different-target witness; or
- an empty/invalid fibre.

### Required return

```text
RECORD_ASSISTED_TARGET_RECONSTRUCTION
STRICT_FINITE_REMAINDER_CONTRACTION
LAW_ONLY_ABSORPTION
SAME_RECORD_DIFFERENT_TARGET
SUPPLIED_ACCESS_OR_INVALID_FIBRE
```

### Cheapest kill

One nonempty lawful pair with the same complete bounded record and different
held-out target.

### Stop

Do not repair a failure by adding the complete flash history, wave function,
environment, detector internals, or target-specific observable unless that
enlargement is explicitly charged as a new access/resource contract.

## 8. Swing 4 — relativistic and cross-ontology no-refit transfer

### Activation

Swing 3 banks a nonvacuous reconstruction, remainder, or exact obstruction.

### Exact question

Does the same typed result transfer without refitting across:

1. flash and matter-density primitive ontologies; and
2. nonrelativistic and relativistic collapse constructions within their
   honest common domain?

### Assurance

- Keep the record, target, observer, action, horizon, and resource definitions
  unchanged.
- Treat a changed primitive ontology as a changed physical theory, not a gauge
  move, unless an access-preserving equivalence is proved.
- Keep the relativistic model's given background, interaction locality, and
  particle-content limitations explicit.
- Do not infer Standard Model, QFT, gravity, or cosmological completion from a
  fixed-particle background-spacetime model.

### Required return

```text
ONTOLOGY_INVARIANT_OPERATIONAL_RECORD_THEOREM
ONTOLOGY_RELATIVE_RECORD_THEOREM
EXACT_FLASH_VERSUS_DENSITY_NON_UNIFICATION
RELATIVISTIC_OR_QFT_SCOPE_BLOCK
```

### Cheapest kill

One unchanged observer-access contract under which empirically matched flash
and matter-density models disagree on whether the proposed record exists or
on the first target leak.

### Value

A transfer earns a reusable typed theorem. A failure shows that “objective
collapse creates records” was ontology-relative rather than a consequence of
collapse dynamics.
