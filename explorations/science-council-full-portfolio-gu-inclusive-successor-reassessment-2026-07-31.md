
This is a work-ordering result, not a scientific claim. The council does not
find a hidden unworked winner in the current portfolio.

The highest-ceiling route is now GU-inclusive:

> A source-owned GU action selects a stable physical background and operative
> interface; that interface forms a material record; and the unchanged DU
> reconstruction contract either recovers an observer-accessible target or
> exposes the first finite physical remainder.

That route is not executable today. GU RB7 produces the first
source-action-shaped Cartan support, but it is an unstable saddle. Its relevant
operators commute, its polar quantity vanishes, its complement is ungapped,
and the support does not select the required base-incidence locus. It therefore
supplies neither a stable selected flag nor a phase-space lift, positive
physical sector, record instrument, or observer-access packet.

The best GU-independent answer is currently a **reopener class**, not a named
ready program:

> Admit the first source-pinned physical antecedent that dynamically marks a
> formation stage or selects an informative output algebra, sampler, material
> carrier, provenance path, or access-preserving interface family.

No existing GU-independent branch supplies such a new antecedent beyond what
the repository has already tested. More variants would repeat a known
boundary rather than gain new knowledge.

## Why this is not an accidental null

The portfolio has already supplied strong positive controls:

- finite Abelian gauge theory supports a formed regional Wilson record and an
  exact ancillary-entanglement resource law;
- source-action response operators form finite complete records for frozen
  source capabilities and descend through mediator elimination;
- nonlinear wave interactions physically create propagating events and can
  conditionally reconstruct geometry;
- AQFT supplies covariant measurement schemes, realizability filters,
  generalized-symmetry algebra inclusions, and relative-entropy diagnostics;
- collapse theories and real detectors supply physical occurrences, material
  traces, timing, polarity, and acquired packets under scoped contracts; and
- a calibrated one-mode radiative receiver can conditionally reconstruct an
  incident Gaussian state.

Those are not failures. They show that formation, response, reconstruction,
and even regional access are individually real. The repeated missing joint is
narrower:

```text
lawful dynamics or admitted process
  -> one physically selected stage/interface family
  -> formed material output with lineage
  -> access-preserving record quotient
  -> held-out target or finite remainder
```

The completed independent branches supply one or more arrows but do not
select the whole joint. Their named stops are now portfolio evidence against
another unmotivated variant, not evidence that the North Star is impossible.

## The five council lenses

### Orthodox professor

**Steelman.** Remain inside established physics until a concrete theory
changes a missing interface duty. AQFT, generalized symmetries, nonlinear
inverse problems, collapse models, and detector physics are serious positive
controls, but DU has already tested their present selection limits.

**Recommendation.** Do not reopen them generically. The next independent
candidate must arrive with an explicit stage-bearing physical mechanism or a
theorem that a target descends across the complete unselected interface
family.

**GU verdict.** GU is admissible as a conditional physical theory, not as a
current input. RB7 is a saddle and therefore has not selected a stable
physical structure.

### Heterodox professor

**Steelman.** The most profound possibility is that one action selects both
the geometry and the interface through which that geometry becomes
operational. GU is the only live portfolio candidate with that full ceiling.
Direct action remains a serious ontology rival, but it does not solve record
formation merely by eliminating fields.

**Recommendation.** Protect the GU-inclusive round trip as the moonshot, but
do not spend DU work pretending the upstream selector exists. Preserve the
second route from `HC-DU-181`: unique interface selection is unnecessary if
the observer target provably descends across every admitted interface.

### Commercial scientist

**Steelman.** Expected information per swing is now poor on all familiar
physical branches. Each has a precise external or antecedent reopener. More
local simulations, detector variants, response hierarchies, or apparatus
controls would mostly reproduce already banked conclusions.

**Recommendation.** Keep one scientific WIP slot empty. Admit a new candidate
only when its passport shows exactly which current dependency it changes and
what can be learned locally before hardware. Quiescence is cheaper and more
credible than manufacturing a successor.

### Wild-frontier scientist

**Steelman.** The blockbuster remains available: the same source action may
select a stable four-dimensional sector, quantum physical structure, and a
record-bearing interaction whose DU reconstruction closes the loop. RB7's
Cartan support is the first suggestive source-shaped object, but instability
and nonselection are decisive, not cosmetic.

**Recommendation.** Reopen only when GU produces a stable noncommuting,
base-incident selected orbit with a phase-space/positive-sector lift. If a
non-GU theory reaches the same gate first, use it instead. The North Star is
substrate-neutral.

### Philosopher of science

**Steelman.** The repeated boundary is itself a substantive learning: a law
can select lawful responses without selecting the interface by which one
occurrence becomes a provenance-bearing fact for an observer. This is not a
proof of universal nonselection, but it is enough to stop relabeling response,
decoherence, correlation, topology, or realizability as record selection.

**Recommendation.** Treat `NO_READY_SUCCESSOR` as a disciplined result. Do
not turn GU custody into evidence, and do not interpret the absence of a ready
mechanism as closure of the North Star.

## Why the apparent near-term alternatives do not win

### Relative entropy

The absorber check is already paid. Araki/Petz sufficiency, AQFT relative
entropy, and type-III Landauer work establish it as the correct diagnostic
after an algebra and channel are fixed. It does not select the algebra,
channel, target, or observer operation. A standalone relative-entropy campaign
would repeat `HC-DU-117/118` and the AQFT compression results.

### Generalized-symmetry or Wilson access

This is already a strong positive, not an unworked opening. `HC-DU-121`
transfers an exact finite-Abelian Wilson-QND instrument and resource law into
a matter-completed lattice gauge theory. The matter, probe, station, ancilla,
and access contract remain supplied. Repeating the finite gauge construction
does not select them.

### Nonlinear interaction and hyperbolic geometry

`HC-DU-105--114` already move from joint-source provenance through finite
event localization, calibration, conformal transfer, scale anchors, and
multi-standard attribution. The open packet requires a physically selected
source/readout/archive rather than another inverse-problem or geometry
calculation.

### More quantum-gravity receiver controls

`HC-DU-189--199` progressively separate mean response, higher cumulants,
ordered response, detector process, mediator access, lawful interventions,
and radiative tomography. Detector-only refinements cannot identify the
mediator, and the radiative packet still lacks a source-selected stable state,
formed calibration, gravitational lineage, and a target separating the direct
quantum twin.

## Exact GU reopener

The GU-inclusive round trip becomes eligible only after a source-owned GU
construction supplies all of:

1. a stable stationary physical branch rather than an unbounded or unstable
   saddle;
2. target-blind selection of the operative four-dimensional section and
   complex--Cartan/quantization structure, or a proof of target descent across
   the full admitted family;
3. a nondegenerate physical Hessian or other positive-sector construction;
4. a lawful hyperbolic/BV evolution and declared state/sector;
5. a material interaction, instrument, or output algebra with retained
   occurrence provenance and observer access; and
6. one no-refit DU target whose reconstruction or finite remainder can be
   adjudicated from that selected packet.

The first four are predominantly GU work. The fifth and sixth are the DU
handoff. GU need not be “finished”; it need only satisfy this exact packet.

## Disposition

No scientific successor should be selected from the present portfolio.
Dynamic Unity should remain quiescent and preserve two conditional triggers:

1. **GU trigger:** the exact six-part GU handoff above; and
2. **independent trigger:** any new source-pinned stage-bearing physical
   antecedent satisfying the candidate passport.

The next worthy DU swing begins when one trigger fires. Until then, additional
local physical variants are more likely to circle the shared boundary than
advance the North Star.
