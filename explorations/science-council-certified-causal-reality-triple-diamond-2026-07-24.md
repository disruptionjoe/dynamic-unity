# Science Council triple-diamond: Certified Causal Reality

## Verdict

The council recommends and the repository implements a genuine recharter:

> **Dynamic Unity determines whether, and under what minimal covariant and
> non-circular conditions, causal record formation and certification are
> sufficient to reconstruct the effective time, causal geometry, public facts,
> and bounded-risk capabilities available to an observer. If they are not, it
> seeks a finite operational witness of the irreducible physical structure
> required.**

Plain English:

> **Can everything that becomes physically real and actionable for an observer
> be reconstructed from the causal histories it can certify? If not, what
> irreducible physical remainder is required?**

The charter keeps three coequal outcomes live:

1. record-first reconstruction;
2. operational duality;
3. physics-first remainder.

This is a program-design verdict, not evidence that any outcome is true. The
scientific authority is computation, experiment, proof, and survived
comparison—not persona agreement.

Six umbrella purposes were compared:

1. record-first reconstruction;
2. Certified Causal Reality adjudication;
3. adversarial public objectivity;
4. meta-record geometry;

### Convergence

Certified Causal Reality won because it contains the profound record-first
possibility without assuming it, makes negative outcomes valuable, and turns
the physical-versus-informational substrate question into an interventionally
decidable research program.

Adversarial Objectivity, Meta-Record Geometry, and
Finality-Capability-Optionality become theorem families under the umbrella.
GU-plus-dynamics becomes a recovery target and comparator. Record-first
reconstruction becomes a possible positive verdict.

The purpose-level discriminator is:

> Given two models whose independently defined record-generation,
> certification, provenance, access, and intervention structures are
> isomorphic, do all observer-accessible physical predictions and capabilities
> agree, or can a bounded intervention distinguish them?

“Independently defined” prevents the sufficiency result from becoming a
tautology.

- multi-time certification witness;

The council found that no one ingredient is sufficiently novel. Process

conjunction, and it must transfer between physical and distributed fixtures

This is a collision-class inventory, not a claim-specific source-pinned
novelty audit. Novelty for every proposed theorem remains
**OPEN / SEARCH-INCOMPLETE** until Lane 4.1 compares an exact statement against
primary literature.

## Diamond 3 — migration and assurance

### Divergence

The assurance lenses identified the principal ways a polished recharter could
fail:

- changing vocabulary while leaving the old priorities underneath;
- defining “complete records” so richly that sufficiency is automatic;
- smuggling in observer, basis, clock, geometry, archive, irreversibility, or
  capability;
- calling dissemination, decoherence, confidence, or copying finality;
- moving resource costs beyond an uncharged boundary;
- choosing an adversary class weak enough to manufacture certification;
- confusing state agreement with provenance;
- treating dephasing or visibility loss as nonclassical history;
- converting operational equivalence into metaphysical priority;
- deriving dimensional time or geometry from bare order/count;
- relabeling known theorems as new laws;
- rewriting historical grades or routing as if the new charter existed
  earlier.

### Convergence

The implementation rules are:

1. preserve every dated exploration, probe, artifact, claim grade,
   `HC-DU-001..032`, prediction, and `P01-A..P50-B` item;
2. add one governing agenda and explicit migration table;
3. replace only active authority and routing;
4. preserve the complete revision-17 lane tree as a labeled legacy snapshot;
5. make GU, cosmology, clocks, causal growth, archive work, and recursive
   viability typed antecedents or applications rather than deleting them;
6. add non-circularity, prior-art collision, resource, covariance, and
   toy-to-universe stops;
7. keep exactly one active North Star: Certified Causal Sufficiency;
8. validate authority consistency, historical discoverability, YAML
   structure, paths, stale language, exact probe regressions, and Git hygiene.

The recharter itself earns only a coherent-program and typed-program grade. It
does not bank a theorem, law, prediction, physical reconstruction, or ontology.

## First meaningful research sequence

1. **Record selection:** enumerate the smallest nontrivial equivariant record
   instruments and find the minimal extra structure selecting a nonconstant
   algebra. Return a theorem or interface-necessity no-go.
2. **Quorum Objectivity:** define fragments, provenance, incompatible
   certificates, access sets, faults, and action semantics; derive a tight
   threshold and converse split-view attack; then test a nonorthogonal quantum
   fixture.
3. **History certification:** build an equal-endpoint multi-time process in
   which classical-mixture and coherent-history rivals can be separated.
4. **Factorization or witness:** mechanically find the smallest admitted
   intervention separating realizations, or prove factorization in the
   declared class.
5. **Transfer before manifesto:** require the same typed statements on one
   physical quantum and one adversarial distributed fixture before advancing
   geometry or universal-law language.

## Success ladder

- **L0 — coherent recharter:** authority surfaces agree and history is intact;
- **L1 — typed research program:** objects, hypotheses, rivals, falsifiers, and
  dependencies are explicit;
- **L2 — scoped theorem:** one nontrivial certification, objectivity, or
  sufficiency result survives countermodels and prior-art comparison;
- **L3 — cross-platform invariant:** the same definitions work without refit
  on quantum and distributed fixtures;
- **L4 — physical reconstruction:** time, geometry, state, or capability is
  recovered with calibration and known-physics limits;
- **L5 — ontology discriminator:** a finite witness selects record-first,
  duality, or physical remainder;
- **L6 — law/theory:** a quantitative invariant or bound survives several
  physical systems and produces a novel empirical consequence.

This implementation reaches L0–L1 only.
