# Science Council premise-free foundational-advance wave contract

## Purpose

Run a fifth independent five-persona Science Council wave from the complete
current Dynamic Unity research state without supplying a new physical or
methodological premise.

Each persona chooses the path it judges capable of producing the greatest
foundational advance now. It should then do as much of that work as the bounded
wave permits:

- resolve low-hanging definitional or computational obligations;
- build the smallest useful object;
- reproduce the relevant positive antecedent;
- expose the first decisive question;
- run cheap kills and hard nulls;
- correct a contested or overstated finding;
- or establish a theorem, counterexample, quotient, or route decision that
  changes the dependency state.

This is not an invitation to produce five longer roadmaps. Each persona must
take a scientific swing before recommending future work.

The five lenses are:

1. orthodox professor;
2. heterodox professor;
3. commercial scientist;
4. wild-frontier scientist; and
5. philosopher of science.

The personas work independently. Each freezes its chosen target before reading
any same-wave peer output. Persona agreement is not evidence. Root will inspect
and reproduce every result used in shared state.

## No new common premise

This wave supplies no new assumption beyond the repository's current typed
state.

Personas may introduce a local modeling assumption when a concrete
construction requires it, but must:

1. name it before using it;
2. state which result depends on it;
3. retain the corresponding no-assumption or rival-assumption control;
4. avoid carrying it into the shared synthesis as truth; and
5. distinguish a conditional construction from a selected physical law.

The wave should not assume:

- that influence concentration selects a source;
- that causal posts recur or have acceptable geometry;
- that a universal post half-power exists;
- that local or global time is fundamental;
- that Clock-QCA circuit covariance supplies observer records;
- that recursive viable systems exist physically;
- that the universe has one integer recursion depth;
- that system boundaries are trees;
- that units emerge from dimensionless ratios;
- that GU must or must not be the substrate; or
- that the current portfolio ranking is correct.

Every current result and priority may be defended, narrowed, overturned, or
replaced through evidence.

## What counts as a foundational advance

A swing is foundational when it does at least one of the following:

- identifies or falsifies a program-native object;
- changes the admissible candidate class;
- turns a verbal identity into a typed map or commuting diagram;
- proves a reusable theorem or counterexample;
- identifies an invariant quotient under a known nonidentification;
- reproduces a positive antecedent and tests the missing DU bridge;
- kills a live route with a correctly scoped control;
- supplies a held-out discriminator among live architectures;
- reveals a generated scale, unit, boundary, record, or response without
  fitting it; or
- produces a credible stage decision that prevents substantial misallocation.

The following do not count by themselves:

- another literature list;
- another unexecuted roadmap;
- a renamed prior idea;
- persona agreement;
- a larger wish list;
- repeating an already passed exact toy check;
- moving a burden into a newly named latent object;
- selecting a favored history, partition, epoch, scheduler, or controller;
- adding free parameters until a target is matched; or
- choosing a finishable byproduct over the North Star merely because the
  larger question is difficult.

## Required target selection

Before executing, each persona must freeze:

1. **Chosen target.**
2. **Why it is the highest-value foundational move from the current state.**
3. **What current dependency it would change.**
4. **Why the move is not circular repetition of a prior wave.**
5. **The smallest decisive deliverable possible in this wave.**
6. **The closest rival use of the same effort.**
7. **A stop rule.**

The persona may choose the current Clock-QCA observer-record arena, the
causal-post generativity/running/geometry arena, the recursive-viability
overlay, a bridge among them, a different program-native object, or a
well-supported route stop.

## Required swing shape

Each persona must complete the following in the order best suited to its
target:

### A. Reconstruct the evidence state

State what the four waves collectively established, narrowed, killed, and left
open. Distinguish executable results, literature antecedents, conditional
constructions, abductive preferences, and structural analogies.

### B. Freeze and justify the target

Record the seven target-selection fields above before inspecting peer output.

### C. Specify the object

Define:

- state or sample space;
- dynamics or inferential map;
- observables and units;
- symmetries, covariance, or representation quotient;
- inputs and free choices;
- intervention or comparison family;
- success criterion; and
- failure scope.

### D. Retain a serious rival set

Include every target-relevant member of:

- status quo / no new object;
- the closest standard or literature explanation;
- the simplest flat, replay, fixed-history, or coarse-graining control;
- a different current DU path;
- representation/refinement/scheduler/hidden-fibre alternatives;
- held-out or out-of-family failure; and
- a complexity or free-access rival.

### E. Take the swing

Prefer an executable finite probe, exact derivation, theorem,
counterconstruction, source reproduction, or bounded empirical calculation.
If executable work is impossible, provide the strongest checkable analytic
result and explain why code would add no information.

### F. Run the cheap kills

At minimum test:

- whether the object exists independently of the readout;
- whether the result survives relabeling or the declared quotient;
- whether a simpler rival matches it;
- whether one selected history/partition/resolution creates it;
- whether it generalizes to held-out cases;
- whether a dimensional result merely imports a unit;
- whether a final-state fit hides provenance; and
- whether disabling the purported mechanism changes the outcome.

### G. Reconcile by warrant

State separately:

- what is exactly established;
- what is conditionally constructed;
- what is abductively favored;
- what is overturned;
- what remains nonidentified;
- what remains merely analogous; and
- what should change in the portfolio.

### H. Produce the next action sequence

Give a dependency-ordered roadmap with:

- first object or computation;
- expected decision;
- stop condition;
- reopener;
- downstream consequence; and
- explicit work that should not be done yet.

### I. Maintain the ledgers

Include:

- hypotheses;
- conjectures;
- possible future claims;
- current claims, if any;
- stop/retirement conditions; and
- unresolved disagreements.

No persona may bank a claim or seed a prediction.

### J. Re-read the complete five-wave path

Explain in plain English:

- what this persona uniquely learned across all five waves;
- what the fifth swing changes;
- what prior outcome was being misread;
- what is newly possible;
- what is no longer worth time; and
- which dependency should control the sixth action, whether or not that action
  is another council wave.

### K. Final unconstrained divergent pass

After the bounded work is complete, preserve every additional path genuinely
noticed while the full context is loaded.

For each item record:

- stable persona-prefixed ID;
- idea;
- why it may matter;
- first object or calculation;
- collision with known stops;
- relationship to earlier IDs; and
- `UNRANKED / UNCOMMITTED / NOT_A_CLAIM`.

Do not pad the list and do not let it alter the roadmap.

## Shared minimum rival principles

These principles apply regardless of the chosen target:

1. **No assumption laundering.** A locally introduced premise stays
   conditional.
2. **No endpoint-only ontology.** Final fit does not identify mechanism,
   history, scheduler, boundary, or control.
3. **No free latent structure.** Hidden fibres, relays, gates, partitions, and
   global access pay an explicit explanatory price.
4. **No unit creation by naming.** Ratios and counts remain dimensionless until
   a physical unit map is supplied.
5. **No claim from execution count.** Passing checks establishes
   reproducibility, not physical truth.
6. **No forced synthesis.** If the strongest result is that two live programs
   remain separate, preserve that.
7. **No endless wave.** The result must change the dependency state or
   recommend stopping this mode of inquiry.
