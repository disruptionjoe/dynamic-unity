# Conditional GU Source-Action Toy: Dynamic Unity Acceptance Contract

## DU's question

The GU construction program asks whether a source action can dynamically
produce a protected chiral boundary sector from a globally vectorlike triplet
carrier. Dynamic Unity asks the distinct question:

> If such a sector is constructed, which part of its apparent three-family
> reality is a global physical invariant, which part is regional
> accessibility, which part is a certified record, and which part changes an
> observer's capabilities under a complete matched-resource process?

Record, finality, and observer language cannot supply a missing source
operator. They become relevant only after the physical construction declares
what exists globally and how an observer can interact with it.

## Frozen rival models

| rival | global completion | accessible sector | what would distinguish it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `FUNDAMENTAL_INDEX_THREE` | net protected index three | three chiral modes | deformation-stable global index and anomaly certificate |
| `UNIT_INDEX_TIMES_TRIPLET` | source boundary has unit index; carrier supplies triplet | three chiral modes | independent provenance for both factors |
| `VECTORLIKE_ACCESS_THREE` | global index zero with mirrors | three modes below a declared boundary or energy threshold | partner thresholds and interventions that enlarge access |
| `TARGET_CODED_THREE` | arbitrary | chosen projector, flux, rank, or decoder returns three | dependence on target-selected input |

All four may present the same low-energy endpoint. Endpoint agreement is
therefore not the discriminator.

## Complete-process passport

Every toy entering DU must state:

1. bulk and boundary fields;
2. source field, action, equations, and initial/topological sector;
3. operator, domain, grading, real/Krein structure, and regulator;
4. global index, total zero modes, mirror content, and anomaly inflow;
5. observer boundary and every admitted intervention;
6. energy, coherence, memory, support, latency, controller, and decoder costs;
7. record instrument and complete multitime provenance;
8. the physical rule that makes any region or mode accessible;
9. benign relabeling, subdivision, and inert-stabilization class; and
10. one held-out consequence fixed before fitting.

An omitted mirror, environment, controller, or heavy-sector access channel is
an absorber, not an irreducible remainder.

## Smallest DU assay

Run the same source construction under two access classes:

- `A_local`: interventions confined to the localized low-energy boundary
  algebra;
- `A_extended`: interventions that can raise the energy, cross the wall,
  recouple the mirror sector, or read the bulk/inflow record.

For each class, record:

- accessible algebra and rank;
- distinguishable task set;
- minimum error at a frozen resource budget;
- partner/mirror detection threshold;
- record provenance and finality rule; and
- invariance under benign boundary refinement.

This separates four possible findings:

```text
GLOBAL_INDEX_ONLY
EFFECTIVE_ACCESS_ONLY
ACCESS_CHANGES_CAPABILITY
RECORD_STRUCTURE_ADDS_NO_PHYSICAL_INCREMENT
```

`ACCESS_CHANGES_CAPABILITY` is not automatically new physics. It may be an
ordinary consequence of changing the admitted algebra or resources.

## Promotion boundary

The toy advances `HC-DU-040A` only if it supplies an independently frozen
physical operator/domain and the DU assay distinguishes the rival completions
without fitting the observer interface after seeing the result.

The maximum pre-physical grade is:

> conditional representation of how one global vectorlike process can induce
> different observer-accessible sectors and capabilities.

No ontology, generation count, record-first substrate, or new-physics claim
follows from that representation.

## Best sequence

1. Consume the finite matrix toy as an identifiability control.
2. Apply the passport to the imposed-wall positive control.
3. Compare the dynamical wall under `A_local` and `A_extended`.
4. Repeat on the overlap/Ginsparg--Wilson regulator.
5. Admit a GU-native interpretation only after the program-native transport
   packet closes.

The useful result under every outcome is a typed answer to whether “three”
means global index, independently factored index, regional access rank, or a
target-coded interface.
