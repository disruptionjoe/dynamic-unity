# Graded Observer-Geometry 4+6+4 Work Program

## Verdict boundary

This is a conditional work object, not an ontology or claim that reality has
four local, six relational, and four access dimensions. It asks whether a
graded exterior representation can make a difference to Dynamic Unity's
North-Star reconstruction test.

The companion GU audit proves an important type boundary:

- \(\Lambda^1\oplus\Lambda^2\oplus\Lambda^3\) has rank \(4+6+4=14\);
- bundled over \(X^4\), its total space is 18-dimensional;
- a total-14 exterior comparator over \(X^4\) instead has rank-10 fibre
  \(\Lambda^2T^*X\oplus\Lambda^3T^*X\); and
- GU's total-14 metric bundle has a different rank-10 fibre
  \(S^2T^*X\), with no natural \(GL(4)\)-equivariant isomorphism between
  them.

## 1. Conditional semantic hypotheses

For a four-dimensional carrier \(V\), the tempting operational reading is:

| grade | dimension | conditional interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| \(\Lambda^1V^*\) | 4 | local address, event, gradient, or observer-readable covector data |
| \(\Lambda^2V^*\) | 6 | pairwise relation, flux, interaction, oriented area, or transport data |
| \(\Lambda^3V^*\) | 4 | hypersurface, cut, archive boundary, access, or certification data |

These are **candidate meanings**, not consequences of exterior degree. A
physical instrument must establish which distinction each component records,
who can access it, how it persists, and which intervention it changes.

The conservative mathematical meaning of a 3-form is codimension-one,
orientation-sensitive data. Calling it capability or access requires a
separate operational map.

## 2. Two branches that must not be merged

### Branch A — geometry admitted

If a metric and orientation are already admitted, Hodge duality gives

\[
*:\Lambda^3V^*\longrightarrow\Lambda^1V^*.
\]

Then grade 3 may be a dual representation of grade 1 rather than four
independent physical dimensions. The scientific question becomes:

> After matching the admitted metric, orientation, resources, and observer
> access, does grade 3 change any held-out intervention or capability beyond
> grade 1?

If not, the proposed access sector is Hodge-absorbed.

### Branch B — geometry not admitted

Without a metric, grade 3 retains only the canonical determinant-twisted
relation

\[
\Lambda^3V^*\cong V\otimes\Lambda^4V^*.
\]

The question is then harder and more valuable:

> Can a physical source, instrument, or certified-record process select the
> required orientation/density and make a 3-form access boundary operational
> without importing the geometry targeted for reconstruction?

A successful no-metric construction could supply genuinely independent
structure. An assigned Hodge map cannot.

## 3. Typed North-Star assay

Let:

- \(\mathcal P_{\mathrm{phys}}\) be a frozen physical completion class;
- \(A\) be a declared observer with instrument, boundary, decoder, and action
  set;
- \(r_A:\mathcal P_{\mathrm{phys}}\to Q_A\) be an independently selected
  certified-record map;
- \(Q_A\) retain separately typed grade-1, grade-2, and grade-3 components;
- \(t_A:\mathcal P_{\mathrm{phys}}\to T_A\) be a held-out target such as
  elapsed time, metric relation, field response, or action capability.

For an observed record \(q\), first require

\[
F_q=\{p\in\mathcal P_{\mathrm{phys}}:r_A(p)=q\}\ne\varnothing.
\]

Then reconstruction holds exactly when \(t_A\) is constant on \(F_q\).
Locally, with gauge already quotiented or complemented, the useful criterion
is

\[
\ker Dr_A\cap T\mathcal P_{\mathrm{phys}}
\subseteq
\ker Dt_A.
\]

The graded proposal helps only if its independently formed record components
shrink a nonempty fibre or alter the held-out target. A dimension match,
coordinate rewrite, or target-coded decoder does not count.

## 4. Four hostile comparisons

### D1 — Hodge absorber

Hold the physical process, metric, orientation, grade-1 record, resources,
and observer fixed. Add the grade-3 record.

- If it is computable from the grade-1 record by the admitted Hodge map, it
  is representational redundancy.
- If it changes a held-out result, identify the extra provenance, boundary,
  or instrument access that did the work.

### D2 — same lower grades, different cut

Construct two lawful completions with the same grade-1 and grade-2 records
but different grade-3 cut/archive data. Ask whether an admitted intervention
distinguishes them.

This is a candidate access witness, but it must survive the strongest
absorber: ordinary omitted boundary, environment, route, provenance, or
decoder information.

### D3 — representation invariance

Apply benign basis change, relabeling, graph subdivision, dual description,
and admitted gauge transformations. A physical reconstruction or capability
delta must be invariant. Component counts and diagram lengths are not.

### D4 — completion and resource matching

Compare the exterior representation with the GU metric bundle and standard
physics only after matching:

- completion class;
- observer and instrument;
- source and boundary conditions;
- controller and decoder;
- metric/orientation assumptions;
- intervention set; and
- time, energy, memory, error, and risk budgets.

An exterior model wins only if the same physical contract yields a sharper
factorization, a smaller certified fibre, or a finite new witness.

## 5. Work sequence

1. **Type the intended object.** Decide whether the scientific candidate is
   the rank-14 grading, the rank-10 exterior fibre over \(X^4\), or a field
   valued in one of them.
2. **Name the instrument.** Define how a physical process writes each grade
   without using the held-out target.
3. **Freeze the completion class.** Include admitted metric, orientation,
   source, environment, controller, and boundary freedoms.
4. **Run Hodge and provenance absorbers.**
5. **Search for the smallest witness.** Prefer one exact pair of same-record,
   different-target completions over a larger simulation.
6. **Only then build locally.** A model is warranted only if it discovers the
   witness or validates a proof boundary before any external hardware.
7. **Report one of four outcomes:** redundant representation, useful
   refinement, class-relative reconstruction, or physical remainder
   candidate.

## 6. Acceptance, kill, and reopening criteria

The graded proposal earns a useful DU result if it produces at least one of:

- an exact sufficiency or non-sufficiency theorem for a natural physical
  completion class;
- a minimal instrument condition under which grade-3 data becomes
  interventionally indispensable;
- a representation-invariant held-out prediction;
- or a proof that the entire access reading is absorbed by ordinary Hodge,
  boundary, provenance, or resource structure.

The physical-access interpretation is killed if its only support is the
number \(4\), a chosen naming convention, or a Hodge map that presupposes the
geometry being derived.

Reopen construction when a concrete source/instrument supplies:

1. grade-typed records,
2. a nonempty physical completion fibre,
3. a held-out observer target, and
4. a result that can differ under the hostile comparisons above.

No new Python fixture is justified now. The current learning is exact and
comes from type, representation, and closure analysis.
