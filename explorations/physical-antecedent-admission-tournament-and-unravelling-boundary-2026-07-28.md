```text

    !=
```

good physical realization of a chosen detector, not a law selecting the

## 2. Strongest new source: the gravitational filtering construction

John Gough and Dylon Rees,
[“The Universe as a Detector: A Quantum Filtering Formulation of the
Diósi--Penrose Model”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17384),
arXiv:2601.17384, revised 22 March 2026, starts from the
Diósi--Penrose Lindbladian and constructs a Hudson--Parthasarathy dilation.

For continuum collapse operators \(\widehat L(k)\), the unitary
input--output dynamics gives

\[
d\widetilde B_{\mathrm{out}}(k,t)
=
d\widetilde B_{\mathrm{in}}(k,t)
+j_t\!\left(\widehat L(k)\right)dt.
\]

The paper then **chooses** to monitor the output quadrature

\[
Y_{\mathrm{hom}}(k,t)
=
\widetilde B_{\mathrm{out}}(k,t)
+\widetilde B_{\mathrm{out}}(k,t)^\ast
\]

and derives the corresponding quantum Kushner--Stratonovich filter. This
recovers the Gaussian noise covariance used by Diósi.

That is stronger than merely postulating a classical noise field: the noise
can be represented as the innovations process of a continuously monitored
quantum output.

The source also marks the exact boundary:

- it says that after constructing the dilation and input--output relation,
  one must “fix an appropriate measurement scheme” for the output;
- Section III.2 gives number counting as an alternative strategy;
- the homodyne and counting filters have different innovations processes and
  conditional paths; and
- the conclusion explicitly says the paper provides no specific mechanism
  for how the Universe functions as a detector and does not explain how a
  measurement readout tells spacetime how to curve.

The construction therefore supplies a candidate output family, not a
selected physical record.

\[

or equivalently its unconditional Lindblad generator \(\mathcal L\) together
with the displayed output-field dilation.

Two interfaces are admitted by the source:

\[
\begin{aligned}
I_{\mathrm{hom}} &: \text{continuously monitor }
  B_{\mathrm{out}}+B_{\mathrm{out}}^\ast,\\
I_{\mathrm{count}} &: \text{monitor output number increments}.
\end{aligned}
\]

They are not lossless relabelings:

- \(I_{\mathrm{hom}}\) produces a continuous Gaussian record and a diffusive
  conditional state;
- \(I_{\mathrm{count}}\) produces a counting record and a jump conditional
  state; and
- their generated commutative output algebras and individual conditional
  histories are operationally different.

After averaging over either record, both reproduce the same unconditional
generator \(\mathcal L\). Hence one antecedent fibre contains at least two
operationally inequivalent interfaces:

\[
\left|\Sigma_A^{I}(A)\right|\geq2.
\]

By `HC-DU-033F`, \(A\) does not point-select an interface.

Adding “the Universe homodynes this quadrature” to \(A\) makes the conditional
filter well defined, but that is contract retyping: the monitoring interface
has been included in the antecedent. It is not a derivation of that interface
from the prior dynamics.

This is standard quantum-trajectory/unravelling freedom, already anticipated
by `HC-DU-061`. The new contribution here is a current, physically motivated,
source-pinned admission control. It is not a new theorem about open quantum
systems.

## 4. Autonomous detector: real formation machinery, supplied interface

Emanuel Schwarzhans et al.,
[“Quantum detectors as autonomous machines: assessing the nonequilibrium
thermodynamics of information acquisition”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16375),
arXiv:2508.16375, models a detector as a quantum thermal machine driving a
three-level gain medium into a metastable nonequilibrium steady state.

This earns useful physics:

- a fully specified Hamiltonian and Lindblad evolution;
- an amplified detection current;
- detector efficiency, jitter, dead time, and dark-count metrics;
- a reset time controlled by the smallest nonzero Liouvillian eigenvalue;
  and
- quantitative entropy/performance tradeoffs.

It does not pass the selector gate:

- the detector energy levels and resonant couplings are chosen to implement
  the task;
- the Lindbladian includes a designated detection dissipator
  \(\mathcal D_D\);
- the incoming particle is inserted by replacing a subsystem state and
  conditioning on particle presence;
- energy released through the designated channel is assumed to be
  macroscopically detectable or to trigger a von Neumann chain; and
- consecutive events are assumed independent after perfect return to the
  steady state, so no retained between-run history is modeled.

The source supports an important positive statement:

> Once a detector architecture is physically supplied, autonomous
> nonequilibrium dynamics can amplify a microscopic event into a bounded
> current and exposes the thermodynamic price of detector quality.

It does not establish:

> The underlying physics uniquely selects that detector, its output channel,
> its record semantics, its archive, or its observer access.

This is inside the mechanism class already closed by `HC-DU-061`, so it does
not constitute a new reopener.

## 6. North-Star consequence

cannot be merely:

- a new state representation;
- a dilation of a known channel;
- one selected unravelling among several;
- a designed autonomous detector;
- a realizability theorem for supplied instruments;
- a robust memory sector without occurrence provenance; or
- a canonical record merge without physical sourcing.

The missing move is narrower and more physical:

```text
antecedent dynamics
  must select the output/interface class itself,
  not only evolve a supplied apparatus or condition a supplied monitoring.
```

physically indexed plurality. But its residual index must be selected or
proved irrelevant before the campaign can ask whether the interface forms,
retains, certifies, and reconstructs a record.

The exact candidate class retained for future intake is:

```text

  + retained provenance archive

```
