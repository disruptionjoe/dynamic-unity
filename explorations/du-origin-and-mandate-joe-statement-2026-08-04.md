# Why Dynamic Unity exists — Joe's statement of the founding mandate

## Joe's statement (2026-08-04, close to verbatim; his wording preserved)

> When we created DU, it was because the formalization was split — time as
> finality, possibility to capability, and temporal issuance. All were
> studying different angles of the problem, and we kept finding that the
> communication between what each of the repos was doing wasn't putting
> together a coherent source. They were learning things independently that
> were useful for each other.
>
> But once we got to the point where we could put together a conditional
> construction that potentially could handle all of the physics except for
> the source action that still needs to be built — then we thought, what if
> we could learn from including the dynamics and potential of what we've
> learned about records and this idea of issuance, all combined in, like,
> initial conditions, and then possibility, capability, all these things.
> What if those told the dynamic story of what happens to the shape of GU
> over time?
>
> And so maybe we should start a new project where it wasn't off limits to
> just go for the more coherent, colder truth, rather than having each repo
> be responsible with its own part. The other repos could continue doing
> things within their charter and scope, but the point of Dynamic Unity was
> to have something where what the other repos were doing wasn't exactly out
> of bounds for it to pull into the picture.

## What this says DU is (reading, not addition)

1. **A response to a composition failure, not to an open physics question.**
   The sibling repos were each correct within scope and were not composing.
   DU exists because that gap was real and nobody owned it.
2. **A permission structure.** DU's distinguishing feature is what is IN
   bounds: the other repos' work is available to be pulled into one picture.
   The siblings keep their charters; DU is where cross-repo composition is
   licensed rather than fenced.
3. **A named target.** The dynamic story of what happens to the shape of GU
   over time, assembled from records, issuance, initial conditions, and
   possibility/capability.
4. **A named precondition.** The conditional construction that could
   potentially handle the physics except the source action, which is still
   to be built.
5. **A named posture.** Go for the more coherent, colder truth — the license
   to prefer coherence over per-repo tidiness.
