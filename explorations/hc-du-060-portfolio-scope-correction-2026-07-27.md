non-unification between physical-interface selection and held-out target

admitted by that sequence fired its declared reopener.

pass that tournament did not establish that the whole repository had no

set of continuations downstream of `HC-DU-058/059`; it was not an exhaustive
compiler over every open hypothesis, mechanism family, or newly authorized
abductive route in Dynamic Unity.
