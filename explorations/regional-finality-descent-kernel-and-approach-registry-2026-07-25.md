# Regional Finality Descent Kernel

## Executive result

The “global versus local” framing is too coarse for the Dynamic Unity
question.

The useful object is a changing network whose nodes may themselves be causal
record networks. Each region can possess a different record, access,
validation, adversary, disclosure, rollback, and action-finality structure.
If its boundary certificate preserves every upper-layer action that matters,
the whole region may be represented as one node at the next layer. Several
such nodes can then form another DAG, hypergraph, or higher-order cover with
its own finality rules.

This does **not** imply that the universe runs a blockchain, that entanglement
is voting, or that consensus creates truth. It creates a precise architecture
for asking:

> Under what conditions do recursively certified regional causal networks
> compose into a higher-level action-safe reality, and when they fail to
> compose, is the obstruction missing provenance, insufficient access,
> adversarial inconsistency, inadequate resources, or additional physics?

The finite swing establishes known component controls and prepares ten
successors. It does not establish a new physical theory.

## 1. Charter fit and roadmap correction

Certified Causal Reality remains the governing program. Regional finality is
the intermediate composition object that the charter previously left
implicit:

```text
physical record formation
    -> regional record/access/certification
    -> descent or typed obstruction across overlapping regions
    -> safe recursive promotion of a region to a higher-layer node
    -> capability/resource consequences
    -> geometry or finite physical remainder
```

This preserves the three charter outcomes:

1. **record-first reconstruction:** the regional stack reconstructs the
   observer-accessible physics;
2. **operational duality:** the physical and regional-certificate
   descriptions translate without a remainder; or
3. **physics-first remainder:** an implementation-complete pair shares the
   certified regional structure but differs under a finite intervention.

The immediate new work package is `HC-DU-035C`. It sits after node-level
record formation and before general interventional sufficiency. It does not
remove the current `HC-DU-033` physical-interface dependency. The exact
regional kernel uses supplied regions and validators; selecting them
physically remains open.

## 2. Regional certified causal network

A finite regional object \(R\) carries the following typed fields:

\[
R =
(G_R,\mathcal A_R,P_R,D_R,V_R,\mathfrak B_R,F_R,C_R,U_R,\Phi_R).
\]

Their intended meanings are:

| Field | Meaning | Must not be silently replaced by |
|---|---|---|
| \(G_R\) | internal event/process DAG or primitive composition structure | a spacetime metric, global clock, or inferred causal order |
| \(\mathcal A_R\) | record distinctions physically retained in the region | every microscopic state variable |
| \(P_R\) | provenance and formation history | an endpoint value |
| \(D_R\) | access, decoder, or disclosure structure | existence of a value |
| \(V_R\) | validator/support structure | raw copy or signature count |
| \(\mathfrak B_R\) | declared fault, corruption, loss, or rival class | generic “uncertainty” |
| \(F_R\) | typed certification/finality rule | confidence or redundancy alone |
| \(C_R\) | boundary certificate exported upward | the complete internal biography |
| \(U_R\) | higher-layer interventions and safe actions | precision or information alone |
| \(\Phi_R\) | forward feedback from certification to future controls | retroactive record rewriting |

Regions relate through inclusions, overlaps, restrictions, translations,
authenticated channels, or reconciliation maps. A collection of regions is
a **cover** only relative to a declared upper task and access class. A
higher-layer node is earned by factorization through \(C_R\), not declared
because a box was drawn around the region.

### Six relations that remain distinct

1. **Causal influence:** which intervention may physically change which
   response.
2. **Entanglement constraint:** nonfactorizable joint statistics without a
   signalling edge.
3. **Record formation:** which distinction becomes physically retained.
4. **Access/disclosure:** which coalition can read, decode, reconstruct, or
   open it.
5. **Validation/finality:** which incompatible alternatives are excluded
   under which adversary and error bound.
6. **Control/capability feedback:** which future actions become safe or
   available after certification.

A cross-platform theorem fails if it changes the meaning of one of these
relations between its quantum and distributed specimens.

## 3. Finality is a typed vector

The kernel does not impose one scalar “amount of finality.” At minimum,
record the following separately:

```text
formed record
locally readable record
coalition reconstructibility
public certificate verification
witness disclosure/opening
fork safety under declared adversary
liveness under declared withholding/network model
rollback profile
bounded-risk action enablement
physical irreversibility or optionality loss
```

These properties can be nonmonotone or incomparable. For example, a
zero-knowledge proof can strengthen public verification without disclosing
the witness. A larger quorum can improve safety while destroying liveness.
A rejecting hardening layer can prevent unsafe composition without producing
a higher-level fact.

## 4. Exact finite controls executed

`tests/du_regional_finality_descent_probe.py` supplies a deterministic

### 4.1 Affine regional descent

Let a connected regional graph carry binary translations

\[
x_v=x_u\oplus \delta_{uv}.
\]

Choose a root and propagate a potential along a spanning tree. A consistent
assignment exists exactly when every cycle has zero parity syndrome. When
consistent, one root bit determines the connected graph, so there are exactly
two global sections. When a loop has syndrome one, there is no global
section even though every proper two-edge subsystem of the triangle is
satisfiable.

The probe exhausts all connected simple graphs through four vertices and
every binary edge labeling: `647` cases, with no disagreement between
flatness and the exact solution count.

This is standard graph-cohomology/constraint-satisfaction mathematics. Its
program value is the typed distinction:

```text
all local constraints individually admissible
    !=
one higher-level compatible fact.
```

For the nontrivial \(\mathbb Z_2\) holonomy orbit, the path-independent
state quotient has one orbit and therefore zero state-capacity bits.
Preserving the noninvariant identity action instead requires a provenance
alphabet of size two. This recovers the existing project-or-lift control.

### 4.2 Quorum safety, liveness, and independent support

For \(N\) validators, fault bound \(f\), and equal-size quorum \(q\), two
incompatible certificates are excluded exactly when

\[
2q>N+f.
\]

The probe exhausts `204` \((N,f,q)\) cases through \(N=8\). At
\(N=4,f=1\):

- \(q=2\) is live under one withholding validator but unsafe;
- \(q=3\) is safe and live;
- \(q=4\) is safe but not live under one withholding validator.

Three apparent validators controlled by one origin also pass a raw
three-signature count while supplying only one independent support source.
Validator count and independent provenance rank are separate receipts.

### 4.3 Certification versus threshold disclosure

An abstract two-of-three monotone access structure permits a public
certificate to be visible while no individual holder can reconstruct the
private witness. The finite access calculation is exact.

It is **not** a cryptographic construction or security proof. A real route
must freeze identity, key generation, setup, corruption timing, trust,
computational assumptions, proof system, share refresh, and resource cost.
Computational secrecy cannot silently become physical inaccessibility.

### 4.4 Recursive node-promotion theorem

Let internal states \(s\in S\) export boundary summary \(C(s)\), and let
\(\{Y_a(s)\}_{a\in U}\) be the frozen upper-layer responses. Replacing the
region by one higher-level node labeled by \(C\) preserves the upper process
exactly when

\[
C(s)=C(t)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
Y_a(s)=Y_a(t)
\quad\text{for every }a\in U.
\]

Equivalently, every upper response factors through the boundary summary.
The smallest sufficient boundary alphabet has one label per distinct
response vector.

The four-state fixture has a two-label coarse boundary that preserves a
local read but loses an action-relevant syndrome. A four-label lift restores
all three frozen upper responses. This is a standard sufficient-statistic,
bisimulation, or behavioral-congruence result—not a new theorem—but it
precisely states when “a network becomes a node.”

The boundary summary must be independently formed. Defining it from all
target responses would recreate the sufficiency tautology prohibited by the
charter.

### 4.5 Benign refinement

Subdividing one edge with a causally inert relay changes hop count from one
to two. It preserves both the zero/nonzero cycle syndrome and the exact
global-section count in flat and frustrated controls.

Therefore hop count, raw depth, relay count, and node count are not candidate
physical invariants without additional carrier, latency, energy, noise,
access, or control consequences.

### 4.6 Forward capability feedback

The finite feedback specimen keeps the prior accessible record fixed while a
later regional certificate enlarges the future safe action set from
`{observe, wait}` to `{observe, wait, commit}`.

This is ordinary controller-state semantics. It establishes only the
directional typing:

```text
certificate at t
    -> changed safe actions at t and later
    !=
rewritten record before t.
```

A physical finality-capability law remains open and must beat the complete
ordinary-controller absorber.

### 4.7 Distributed and quantum twins

The distributed fixture exercises all four return types:

- `DESCENDS`;
- `REQUIRES_PROVENANCE_OR_LOGICAL_LIFT`;
- `REJECTS_FRUSTRATED_OR_UNSAFE_COMPOSITION`; and
- `INCOMPLETE_CONTRACT`.

The quantum fixture uses the known GHZ phase pair. Every proper-subset
reduced state is identical for \(|GHZ_+\rangle\) and
\(|GHZ_-\rangle\), while the global \(X\otimes X\otimes X\) response is
\(+1\) versus \(-1\). A boundary summary containing only proper-subset
records is insufficient for a phase-sensitive upper action; exporting the
logical parity restores factorization.

This does not make qubits validators. It shows that the same
**boundary-sufficiency question** can be asked without changing its meaning:
does the exported boundary object retain the distinction needed by the
declared higher-layer action?

## 7. Literature collision map

The regional kernel is a conjunction, not an empty literature category.

- Standard causal-set dynamics uses a locally finite partial order and
  covariant sequential growth, not the present validation/finality
  architecture:
  [Rideout and Sorkin](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9904062).

  [Hawkins, Markopoulou, and Sahlmann](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0302111).
- Causal sites make regions and their causal/inclusion relations primary:
  [Christensen and Crane](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0410104).
- Sheaf conditions on nets of local algebras already formalize regional
  compatibility and independence:
  [Wolters and Halvorson](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.5639).
- Quantum Darwinism and spectrum-broadcast structures treat redundantly
  accessible environmental records:
  [Zurek](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0505031) and
  [Korbicz, Horodecki, and Horodecki](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.6588).
- DAG dissemination and BFT ordering are explicitly separated in
  [Narwhal and Tusk](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.11827), while the Snow family
  supplies metastable leaderless consensus:
  [Team Rocket et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.08936).
- Higher-order multiplex complexes already carry dynamics on nodes, links,
  simplices, and interacting layers:
  [Krishnagopal and Bianconi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.14189).
- Process tensors already represent multi-time quantum memory and
  intervention structure:
  [Pollock et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00589).

The finite theorems executed here are absorbed by graph cohomology, sheaf
descent, quorum intersection, monotone access structures, sufficient
statistics/bisimulation, and stabilizer logical access. The potentially
distinct research contribution would require one unchanged typed theorem
that:

1. covers noisy/noninvertible regional maps;
2. includes authenticated adversarial safety and liveness;
3. includes physical quantum record formation and logical access;
4. is natural under refinement and recursive promotion;
5. produces a resource or capability consequence; and
6. does not change semantics between platforms.

That conjunction is search-incomplete and unproved.

## 8. What remains missing

1. **Endogenous regionalization:** what physically selects a region, overlap,
   scale, split, or merge?
2. **Validator individuation:** what makes supports independent and prevents
   correlated/Sybil inflation?
3. **Noninvertible/noisy descent:** how do approximate records, losses, and
   irreversible maps compose?
4. **Safety and liveness across layers:** when can a safe architecture
   actually finalize?
5. **Security typing:** computational, information-theoretic, quantum, and
   physical access guarantees require different assumptions.
6. **Feedback consistency:** how do higher certificates constrain lower
   futures without circularity or retroactive rewriting?
7. **Resource conservation:** where do memory, entropy, coherence, trust,
   latency, and displaced information move?
8. **Scale and continuum control:** which invariants survive benign
   refinement and recursive coarse-graining?
