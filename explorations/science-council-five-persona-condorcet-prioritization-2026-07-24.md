# Science Council five-persona Condorcet prioritization

## Epistemic status

One facilitator agent represented five declared evaluative lenses:

1. orthodox professor;
2. heterodox professor;
3. commercial scientist;
4. wild-frontier scientist; and
5. philosopher of science.

The facilitator made twenty complete strict rankings: five persona ballots for
each of four elections. Every ballot used all fifty-six source IDs exactly
once. It then computed every pairwise contest.

This is a transparent prioritization exercise, not scientific evidence. The
five lenses are simulated by one agent and are not statistically independent
experts. A Condorcet winner records robustness to these declared preference
lenses; it does not establish truth, feasibility, novelty, or physical
relevance.

The source index and persona memos remain authoritative for the ideas. This
receipt may guide or provoke successor work but cannot promote, merge, delete,
bank, or seed an item.

## Rubric

Each candidate received category-specific `0..4` ratings before conversion to
a strict ranking.

- **Short term:** information gain, present buildability, dependency-gate fit,
  cheap falsifiability, downstream leverage, minus known-stop risk.
- **Long term:** eventual DU-purpose reach, endogenous mechanism,
  geometry/scale/return-arrow unification, covariance robustness, path to
  physical identification, minus answer-import risk.
- **Dormant/worst:** live-stop collision, answer insertion, proxy or ornamental
  proliferation, misleading-interpretation risk, opportunity cost, minus
  unique diagnostic salvage.
- **Intriguing/unorthodox:** surprise, coherence, generativity, cross-domain
  bridge power, concrete foothold, minus known-stop gravity.

Persona modifiers emphasized:

- orthodox: identifiability, derivation, null discrimination, robustness;
- heterodox: counterexample power, native dynamics, open-system mechanism,
  response;
- commercial: smallest decision product, kill clarity, reuse, dependency
  cost;
- wild frontier: new invariants, conceptual bridges, formal compression,
  mechanism novelty; and
- philosopher: provenance, intervention semantics, grade discipline,
  category-error prevention.

Ties were resolved by the category's primary criterion, then smaller
dependency cost or first object, then canonical source ID. Borda totals below
are secondary summaries only and did not replace the pairwise rule. With 56
candidates, the maximum Borda total is 275.

## Use in the successor wave

Successor personas must inspect the complete source list, not only the four
winners. They may:

- attack a winner;
- choose a runner-up;
- reopen a dormant item only by satisfying its stated reopener;
- combine distinct source items while retaining every source ID; or
- choose any lower-ranked item when they can state why the vote's rubric
  undervalued it.

They may not cite the election as evidence that a mechanism works or that an
idea is scientifically preferable.
