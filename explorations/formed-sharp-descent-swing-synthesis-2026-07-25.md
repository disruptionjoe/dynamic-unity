# Formed-Sharp Descent Swing

## Plain-English result

The swing found a useful physical-selection boundary, but the proposed
abstract Formed-Sharp Descent Theorem does not survive as a new theorem.

An explicit system–pointer–archive circuit can earn a sharp record inside a
frozen binary QND family. It does so only after a source algebra and a
candidate coupling have already been specified. Exact repeatability then
coincides with a sharp aligned PVM and perfect disclosure of the supplied
sectors. This is known sharpness and measurement-instrument mathematics.

The important negative result is that the usual record conditions do not
finish the selection:

- if nondisturbance is defined relative to each candidate's own basis, every
  unitarily rotated basis passes;
- freezing an independently selected source algebra rejects a named
  misaligned basis, but does not generate the pointer coupling;
- the same sharp effects, archive, repeatability, and central
  nondisturbance can admit different selective post-measurement
  continuations;
- a noisy pointer-to-archive channel can make the final observer-accessible
  measurement unsharp even when the underlying pointer measurement is sharp;
  and
- a process-class certificate remains different from a record that discloses
  which history occurred.

The descent branch then closes the abstract gluing route. For projective
measurements on one common quantum system, pairwise joint measurability
already implies global joint measurability by standard commutation and
spectral theory. A cyclic family of locally sharp-looking archives with no
global extension therefore shows that common physical occurrence identity
was never earned; it is not a counterexample to quantum sharp-measurement
descent. If sharpness is weakened, three noisy Pauli measurements at
\(\eta=2/3\) are pairwise jointly measurable but not jointly measurable as a
triple.

The hostile gate exposes the final dilemma:

```text
contextwise sharp dilation
    is universal and too weak;

one common commuting full-cover dilation
    is equivalent to global joint measurability;

therefore
    the missing result is an independent physical selector
    for one cross-context instrument and archive identity.
```

The concentrated opportunity has moved upstream. Dynamic Unity should not
look for another abstract theorem saying sharp things glue. It should try to
derive why one physical source–pointer–archive interface, rather than a
conjugate, context-refitted, or continuation-twisted rival, is selected and
remains selected under held-out perturbations and matched resources.

## Result A — what physical formation earns

The executed family has one system qubit, one fresh pointer qubit, one fresh
archive qubit, an explicit system–pointer unitary, pointer-to-archive CNOT,
archive readout, provenance, and fresh support for repeatability.

For pointer crossover \(\epsilon\in[0,1/2]\), the effects have eigenvalues
\(\{1-\epsilon,\epsilon\}\). Within the frozen binary family:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{projection defect} &= \epsilon(1-\epsilon),\\
\text{two-pass mismatch} &= 2\epsilon(1-\epsilon),\\
\text{archive disclosure} &= 1-2\epsilon.
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence sharp effects, exact immediate repeatability, and perfect archive
disclosure of the two supplied sectors coincide exactly at
\(\epsilon=0\). The relation is a scoped calibration inside occupied
repeatability/sharpness and quantum-instrument theory.

The physical increment is the dependency ordering:

```text
source-selected algebra
    + explicit pointer/archive formation
    + source-relative nondisturbance
    + exact repeatability
    -> aligned sharp PVM in the frozen binary family

but not
    -> generation of the coupling
    -> unique basis without source structure
    -> unique selective continuation
    -> end-to-end sharpness through a noisy archive
    -> public finality.
```

The exact finite receipt passes `34/34`.

## Result B — what descent does and does not add

For finite PVMs on one common Hilbert space, pairwise joint measurability
implies pairwise commutation. The product of the commuting projectors gives
the global joint PVM. That result is established measurement theory.

At the finite commutative probability layer, a family of context tables has
a global distribution exactly when it can be represented as
coarse-grainings of one diagonal sharp master archive. This is a
representation equivalence, not evidence that the physical system formed
that archive.

The controls establish:

1. matching separator marginals glue on a join-tree cover;
2. the cyclic constraints
   \(A=B\), \(B=C\), and \(C\ne A\) have matching one-site marginals but no
   global extension;
3. the cyclic obstruction survives outcome relabeling, an inert relay, and
   redundant singleton marginals;
4. at \(\eta=2/3\), noisy \(X,Y,Z\) qubit POVMs have explicit pairwise
   joints but violate the exact necessary global bound
   \(3\eta\leq\sqrt3\);
5. equal sharp effects can hide different selective maps, total channels,
   and held-out continuations; and
6. even a valid global archive can be insufficient for an upper action after
   a lossy certificate is exported.

The descent receipt is therefore still a vector:

\[
\left(
\text{formed typed identity},
\ \text{full-cover compatibility},
\ \text{upper-action factorization},
\ \text{provenance/access/resources}
\right).
\]

No one coordinate can replace the others. The exact finite descent receipt
passes `36/36`.

## Result C — why this does not yet select quantum theory

The previous probability-level frontier recovered known Local
Orthogonality/Consistent Exclusivity and did not exclude almost-quantum
correlations. Moving to sharp measurements reaches a stronger known result:
Specker's principle excludes almost quantum. That exclusion is due to Gonda
et al.; it is not a new Dynamic Unity result and does not uniquely reconstruct
all of quantum theory.

The current physical branch does not derive a theory-independent Specker
premise. Contextwise pointer/archive formation is too weak because every
finite POVM has a sharp Naimark dilation on an enlarged system. Requiring a
single common commuting dilation across all contexts is already equivalent
to asking for the global joint measurement.

## The integrated selection-gap result

The strongest defensible repository-level statement is:

> **Formed-Sharp Selection Gap.** Record formation, repeatability,
> nondisturbance, provenance, access, and matched finite resources can
> classify and test an instrument relative to an independently frozen source
> and interface, but they do not by themselves select the physical coupling,
> a context-independent occurrence identity, a unique selective
> continuation, or a full-cover joint instrument. Contextwise dilation is too
> permissive, while full-cover common dilation restates joint measurability.

The finite witnesses and adjacent absorbers make this a precise program
correction. A novelty audit is still required before treating the conjunction
as a publishable no-go theorem, so no theorem ID is added.

This kills one tempting shortcut:

```text

    + locally look sharp
    + pairwise compatible
    therefore
one new global physical fact law.
```

The conclusion does not follow. Common occurrence identity and the physical
interface must be earned before the known descent mathematics applies.

## Consequence for the two larger stories

### Operational duality

The operational-duality story remains credible but is not yet established.
A complete certificate network cannot be called an equivalent description of
observer-accessible physics if its record algebra, measurement interface, or
decoder was selected after inspecting the physics it is meant to replace.
This swing identifies independent instrument selection as an upstream
requirement for a noncircular duality theorem.

### Generative issuance

The issuance-first story remains the higher-risk moonshot. The current
results do not show that record formation creates a new joint fact rather
than implements ordinary quantum measurement and known contextual gluing.
Before claiming generative issuance, one common physical interface must be
selected independently and then produce an operational difference that
survives fixed-oracle completion, boundary enlargement, provenance
refinement, and matched resources.

The negative result is still informative: if the selector cannot be derived,
the irreducible physics-first remainder has been localized to source
dynamics, the system–pointer interaction, the physical boundary, the
continuation rule, or the access channel.
