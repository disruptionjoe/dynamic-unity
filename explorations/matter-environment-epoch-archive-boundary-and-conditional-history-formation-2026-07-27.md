# Matter-environment epoch-archive boundary and conditional history formation

## Executive verdict

The matter--environment lift returns a four-part result:

```text
MINIMAL BINARY EPOCH TRANSDUCER UNIQUE
CLOSED BOUNDED REVERSIBLE LATCH OBSTRUCTED
FRESH-OUTPUT HISTORY FORMATION CONDITIONAL
METASTABLE HOST DOES NOT SELECT THE EPOCH ARCHIVE
```

The disposition for the inherited metastable host is:

```text
SUPPLIED_EPOCH_ARCHIVE
```

This is not simply a negative result.

The minimum logical object is now exact. For the bounded proposition

> “Has at least one selected write occurred since a certified epoch
> boundary?”

the two states “not yet” and “yes” are necessary and sufficient, and their
set/retain/reset transition table is unique once the event semantics are
frozen.

But that one-bit **logical statistic** is not a free one-bit **physical
implementation**. Its write and reset operations are many-to-one. Closed
reversible dynamics on the carrier cannot implement them for every admitted
input. Exact repeated operation must instead:

- export the overwritten distinction to other degrees of freedom;
- consume fresh blank support;
- restrict the admitted inputs or horizon; or
- weaken exactness, retention, or occurrence semantics.

A fresh-output Markov dilation gives the clean conditional positive. Each
resolved matter branch writes to a fresh orthogonal environment cell. The
resulting output tape is a physically formed history record in that
specified completion, and the binary epoch flag is a strict autonomous
quotient for the narrow occurrence capability.

The same reduced metastable generator does not select:

- that dilation;
- the fresh-cell tensor factorization;
- the physical output fragment;
- the retained archive basis;
- the observer access route; or
- the epoch-reset event.

Moreover, using the host's own slow turnover edge as the reset does not
repair provenance. The resulting flag is exactly the ready/retained class
already encoded in the terminal matter state.

The decisive distinction is therefore:

\[
\text{history physically formed in a specified completion}
\quad\neq\quad
\text{history archive selected by the matter host}.
\]

`H-CCR-17` remains open. `ECR-N5-S4` should now test what, if anything,
transfers across physically admissible environment completions rather than
assuming that the minimal epoch flag has been endogenously selected.

\[

The inherited question was therefore no longer “does a stable value form?”
It was:

> Does the same physical process form the missing history distinction, and
> is its minimum compression a physical record rather than an analyst's
> path function?

## 3. The minimum epoch transducer

Use the event alphabet

\[
\Sigma=\{I,C,W,E\},
\]

where:

- \(I\) is idle/no relevant event;
- \(C\) is the host's internal turnover edge;
- \(W\) is a selected fast write edge; and
- \(E\) is a certified external epoch reset.

For the proposition

\[
h(\omega)
=
\mathbf 1
\{\text{at least one }W\text{ occurred after the latest }E\},
\]

the exact two-state update is:

| event | \(b=0\) | \(b=1\) |
|---|---:|---:|
| \(I\) | \(0\) | \(1\) |
| \(C\) | \(0\) | \(1\) |
| \(W\) | \(1\) | \(1\) |
| \(E\) | \(0\) | \(0\) |

Thus:

\[
\delta_I(b)=b,
\qquad
\delta_C(b)=b,
\qquad
\delta_W(b)=1,
\qquad
\delta_E(b)=0.
\]

### Proposition 1 — binary minimum

The language “at least one \(W\) since the latest \(E\)” has exactly two
Myhill--Nerode classes:

1. no write has yet occurred in the current epoch; and
2. at least one write has occurred.

The empty continuation distinguishes them: it must answer \(0\) for the
first and \(1\) for the second. Therefore no one-state deterministic
transducer suffices.

### Proposition 2 — transition uniqueness

Once the two logical meanings and the event meanings are frozen, the four
maps above are forced. The exhaustive certificate checks all

\[
4^4=256
\]

deterministic assignments of maps

\[
\{0,1\}\to\{0,1\}
\]

to \(I,C,W,E\). Exactly one satisfies the contract.

Without the semantic labels, the same object is unique up to relabeling the
two states together with their meanings. No absolute physical \(0/1\)
orientation is inferred.

## 4. The reversible epoch-archive obstruction

The minimum logical state count does not determine the implementation
resource.

Let \(Y\) be a closed physical carrier and let

\[
b:Y\to\{0,1\}
\]

be a surjective archive readout. Suppose an exact write operation is a
closed reversible map

\[
U_W:Y\to Y.
\]

Exact idempotent write semantics require

\[
b(U_Wy)=1
\qquad
\text{for every }y\in Y.
\]

Hence:

\[
U_W(Y)\subseteq b^{-1}(1).
\]

But reversibility makes \(U_W\) surjective, so:

\[
U_W(Y)=Y.
\]

Because \(b^{-1}(0)\neq\varnothing\), this is a contradiction.

The same proof applies to an exact reset \(U_E\) satisfying

\[
b(U_Ey)=0
\qquad
\text{for every }y\in Y.
\]

### Theorem — reversible epoch-archive boundary

No closed reversible carrier with both logical sectors can implement the
exact repeated set/retain/reset epoch transducer on every admitted carrier
state.

For a quantum implementation, the same conclusion follows from inner
product preservation. Two orthogonal logical inputs cannot be sent to one
identical physical output. If their logical outputs agree, the distinguishing
information must remain in another orthogonal degree of freedom.

Therefore an exact physical implementation must use at least one of:

1. **restricted inputs** — for example, a certified blank state and no
   repeated write before the next reset;
2. **exported information** — an ancillary or bath degree retains what the
   logical map overwrote;
3. **fresh support** — later updates use new blank cells or output modes;
4. **bounded semantics** — only a declared finite horizon or event count is
   required; or
5. **relaxed semantics** — aliasing, error, decay, or approximate reset is
   admitted.

This is a necessity result, not a universal numerical energy law.

### Why a reversible toggle is not enough

On one bit, the only nontrivial reversible update is a swap:

\[
0\leftrightarrow1.
\]

If each \(W\) toggles the bit, it records write parity, not write
occurrence. The inherited four-jump return contains two writes:

\[
CWCW.
\]

Starting from \(0\), a toggle returns \(0\), while the required occurrence
answer is \(1\).

The exact probe checks all

\[
2^4=16
\]

assignments of the two bit permutations to the four event symbols. None
implements the frozen epoch contract.

### Collision with reversible computation

[Bennett's reversible-computation construction](https://doi.org/10.1147/rd.176.0525)
shows the standard positive route: an irreversible logical function can be
embedded in reversible dynamics by retaining enough input or intermediate
history, copying the desired output, and uncomputing what can be restored.

The present theorem is that familiar mechanism applied to a record claim:
the physical implementation of a one-bit idempotent archive may require a
larger history/resource ledger than the one-bit statistic suggests.

No \(kT\ln2\) cost, cosmological cost, or universal finality price is claimed
here.

## 5. Why the host's internal reset does not solve it

There is an obvious attempted repair:

```text
turnover edge C: b := 0
write edge W:    b := 1
```

Initialize \(b=1\) in \(x_1\). Along the selected cycle:

\[
x_1\xrightarrow{C}x_2\xrightarrow{W}x_3
\xrightarrow{C}x_0\xrightarrow{W}x_1,
\]

the bit evolves:

\[
1\to0\to1\to0\to1.
\]

But this is exactly the terminal ready/retained classification:

\[
b_t
=
\mathbf 1\{X_t\in\{x_1,x_3\}\}.
\]

The equality follows by induction over every allowed jump and is preserved
by no-jump intervals.

### Proposition — internal-reset collapse

A flag set by \(W\) and reset by the host's own \(C\) edge carries no
history distinction beyond the already selected terminal matter state.

It therefore cannot separate:

- the stale path that remains in \(x_1\); and
- the rewritten path \(CWCW\) that returns to \(x_1\).

To separate those paths, the latch must instead be initialized at an epoch
boundary independent of the internal turnover cycle and retain \(1\) after
every later \(C\). That physical epoch boundary is not selected by the
stationary host.

This is the smallest exact location of the “smuggled clock” risk:

> The required extra object is not necessarily a metric clock. It is a
> physically certified occurrence boundary whose reset is not already just
> the matter state.

## 6. Conditional positive: a formed output history

The obstruction does not say physical histories cannot form. It says the
required information must go somewhere.

For one resolved collision step, let \(n(x)\) be the next state on the
selected cycle and choose a branch probability \(p_x\in[0,1]\). Matching the
continuous-time generator over a small interval \(\Delta\) uses
\(p_x=\lambda_x\Delta+O(\Delta^2)\). A fresh environment cell initially in
\(\lvert0\rangle\) supports the exact step isometry:

\[
V\lvert x\rangle\lvert0\rangle
=
\sqrt{1-p_x}\,
\lvert x\rangle\lvert N_x\rangle
+
\sqrt{p_x}\,
\lvert n(x)\rangle\lvert J_{x\to n(x)}\rangle
.
\]

Take the output states

\[
\{\lvert N_x\rangle,\lvert J_{x\to n(x)}\rangle\}_{x\in X}
\]

to be mutually orthogonal. Then the input columns remain orthonormal, so
the isometry extends to a reversible permutation/unitary on a larger
space.

Iterating against fresh cells produces:

\[
\mathcal E_{\mathrm{out}}
=
\mathcal E_1\otimes\cdots\otimes\mathcal E_N
\]

and an output word that retains the resolved no-jump/jump history. The
write-edge subsequence is then a physical environment record, not merely an
unobserved path variable.

### What is positively formed

Relative to the specified fresh-output completion:

### Exact archive-access completion twin

The selection failure has a finite witness. For every resolved branch, write

\[
(x,s)\longmapsto(y,t),
\]

where \(s\) is the branch input, \(y\) the next matter state, and \(t\) a
source-labelled token. Compare:

\[
V_{\mathrm{visible}}:
(x,s)\longmapsto(y,t_A,\mathrm{blank}_H)
\]

with:

\[
V_{\mathrm{hidden}}:
(x,s)\longmapsto(y,\mathrm{blank}_A,t_H).
\]

Both maps are injective and therefore admit reversible extensions. Their
reduced matter branch map is identical. In the first completion the admitted
archive fragment \(A\) has eight distinguishable one-step branch tokens. In
the second it has one blank value; the distinguishing token is retained in a
reservoir \(H\) outside the declared access boundary.

Once the physical wiring that makes \(A\) accessible and \(H\) inaccessible
is frozen, the two arms are not a coordinate relabeling. But that wiring is
absent from `A_matter`. Therefore the reduced host cannot select whether the
history enters the admitted archive.

The twin does **not** show that reversible dynamics destroys the
distinguishing information. It shows exactly the opposite: the information
must survive somewhere, while the matter law does not determine whether that
somewhere is an observer-accessible archive.

## 7. Why an unravelling is not automatically the archive

[Wiseman and Diósi](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0012016) parameterize
multiple diffusive unravellings of one Markovian master equation and give
their interpretations as different environment-monitoring arrangements.

That result enforces two distinctions:

1. a reduced open-system law does not select one observed trajectory; and
2. once a complete physical environment coupling and access route are
   frozen, merely rewriting the same record in a different coordinate basis
   is representation, not a new completion.

DU must therefore neither:

- promote a convenient stochastic trajectory into a record; nor
- dismiss a real output field as conventional merely because an isometric
  representation is possible.

The invariant question is whether the complete physical process contains a
retained, accessible history distinction over the declared action envelope.

## 8. Collision with redundant objective histories

[Riedel, Zurek, and Zwolak](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.0331) show that
redundant environment records can select a small family of consistent
histories and make an objective past locally accessible to many observers.

This is the strongest absorber and the correct positive comparison.

The present result does not deny that environment histories can become
objective. It localizes the missing step:

\[
\text{reduced matter law}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{selected retained history archive}.
\]

When the complete environment interaction actually distributes the relevant
history distinction into stable fragments, the history record is formed.
When only the terminal value is broadcast, occurrence provenance is still
absent. When the environment contains the distinction only in an inaccessible
or later-uncomputed degree of freedom, public or action-bearing finality has
not followed.

## Bottom line

The key finding is a separation among three statements that are often
collapsed:

1. **The write occurred in the physical path.**
2. **Some environment degree retained enough information to distinguish
   that history.**
3. **The matter law selected a stable, accessible epoch archive carrying the
   distinction.**

The fresh-output completion makes statement 2 true. The inherited
metastable host does not make statement 3 true.

And the one-bit summary conceals a real implementation requirement:

> An exact bounded occurrence claim needs only one logical bit, but repeated
> reversible formation of that bit needs somewhere for the discarded
> history to go.

That is the new research boundary passed to the next swing.
