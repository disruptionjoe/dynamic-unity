# Science Council five-persona recursive viable-systems wave synthesis

## Executive result

The fourth wave admitted Joe's premise in its strongest testable form:

> There may be an unknown number of recursively repeated viable-system-like
> organizations that must be discovered across scales.

The premise produced a useful new research object, but the literal
"one unknown number of levels from bottom to top" version did not survive.

The exact result is:

1. **Multilevel active regulation can be real.** Several finite constructions
   contain lower units, upward summaries, and downward actions. Additional
   levels repair held-out disturbances that shallower candidates cannot.
2. **A raw integer depth is generally not identifiable.** An identity relay
   can subdivide an internal edge and change `m=2` into `m=3` without changing
   any external behavior. Resolution choices can also change the selected
   depth.
3. **The recoverable object is richer than a ladder.** Internal lesions and
   held-out joint interventions can identify a minimal, possibly overlapping
   cover of causal roles. It may be a tree, a poset, a hypergraph, or a broad
   scale spectrum rather than one chain.
4. **Survival alone is far too weak.** Passive replay, arbitrary partitions,
   ordinary coarse-graining, redundant wrappers, duplicate encodings, and
   flat global controllers can all imitate parts of the result.
5. **Locality is now a load-bearing physical question.** A flat regulator
   ties the recursive regulator when global access is free. Recursion becomes
   explanatory only if the underlying law itself prices or forbids global
   fan-in, communication, latency, state, or energy.
6. **Beer functions, recursion levels, and physical strata are different
   axes.** Systems 1–5 are roles within one viable-system description. They
   are not five recursion levels and do not identify finality, causal depth,
   observer depth, or physical scale.
7. **System 4 has a narrower scientific role than previously stated.** The
   exact fixed controllers remain viable on their declared disturbance
   families without an environment model. An S4-like function becomes a live
   requirement for out-of-family adaptation, controller generation, or
   self-revision—not for every instance of fixed-family viability.
8. **Levels do not manufacture units.** The constructions generate
   dimensionless support-size and response-time ratios. They do not derive a
   dimensional clock, length, mass, microscopic unit, `Lambda`, or cosmology.

The portfolio decision is therefore:

> Add a **recursive-viability passport** to `HC-DU-031A` and `HC-DU-011A`.
> Do not create a third broad RVS simulation program.

No physics claim is banked or seeded.

## What is positively established inside the finite models

### Active multilevel control is constructible

The positive fixtures do more than draw nested boxes. They specify:

- a system boundary and essential variables;
- a perturbation family;
- upward state or load summaries;
- downward repair or resource allocation;
- controller-on/controller-off contrasts;
- held-out disturbances;
- durable repair provenance; and
- a cost for added structure.

In those fixtures, deeper active organization can make a measurable
difference. That is a legitimate conditional and constructive result even
though it is not derived from DU's present fundamental law.

### A minimal role cover is identifiable in cases where depth is not

The philosopher's construction sharpens the shared result. A raw level count
can be changed by inserting an identity relay. But a held-out double lesion can
reveal that:

```text
one degree of freedom depends on regulator A,
one depends on regulator B,
one depends on the joint availability of A and B.
```

That intervention signature survives relabeling and the declared resolution
quotient. It identifies organization by causal role, not by a privileged
drawing. This is the strongest new object produced by the wave.

### Viability and ordinary emergence are separate dimensions

Repeated coarse-graining, a Markov blanket, high effective information, a
long-lived macrostate, or a correct root summary can all exist without active
downward repair. Conversely, an actively regulated system can have no unique
best RG level. Renormalization, causal emergence, conditional independence,
compression, and viability may correlate, but none currently substitutes for
the others.

## What the wave rules out or narrows

The following are no longer adequate evidence for recursive physical
organization:

- choosing a particle-to-universe ladder by name;
- counting Beer's S1–S5 roles as five recursion levels;
- treating every coarse-graining level as a viable system;
- treating every stable state as actively regulated;
- selecting depth from endpoint survival alone;
- adding levels until a fit succeeds;
- treating a hidden or duplicated state fibre as a new level;
- treating every update opportunity as a durable record;
- inferring a dimensional unit from multiple dimensionless ratios;
- claiming that an absent S4 makes every fixed-family system nonviable; or
- calling an unobservable refinement gauge without showing complete
  intervention equivalence.

The wave does not rule out multiscale organization in nature. It replaces a
loose hierarchy claim with a much harder empirical admission test.

## Corrections to the inherited Dynamic Unity vision

Three axes must remain separate:

```text
Beer role inside one system
    × recursion or organizational relation
    × physical finality / causal / scale stratum.
```

A correspondence between those axes may later be discovered. It may not be
assumed. In particular:

- a causal post is a historical landmark, not a higher viable system unless
  it has an interventionally active downward consequence;
- an observer record is a candidate system variable, not a viable observer
  merely because it persists;
- a Clock-QCA layer is a computation level, not a recursion level unless
  perturbations expose repeated operational closure;
- a fixed-family regulator establishes robustness, not anticipation;
- adaptation under a changed family is the clean S4-like test; and
- generating a genuinely new controller or rule remains a still stronger
  self-authoring test.

The `stratified-finality-levels` and blockbuster-ceiling documents now carry
this correction explicitly.

## Relationship to the first three waves

The four waves now form one scientific progression:

```text
Run 1: a mathematical signature is not yet a mechanism
    ↓ ask whether invariant history changes the law
Run 2: a post can carry exact conditional history, but not unique physics
    ↓ ask what records that history and how clocks compare
Run 3: record/clock architectures are conditionally buildable but finitely
       underidentified
    ↓ ask which persistent systems own the records and interactions
Run 4: active multilevel systems are conditionally discoverable, but the
       scientific object is an intervention cover rather than a raw depth.
```

The recurring lesson is not that only derivations matter. Each wave admitted
an abductive or conditional construction. What repeatedly did the scientific
work was a rival-sensitive intervention: natural-label path weights,
cross-history replay, reunion, scheduler reslicing, controller lesions,
held-out disturbances, or family shift.

## The RVS overlay passport

Any future `HC-DU-031A` or `HC-DU-011A` survivor should answer:

1. What are the essential variables, viable region, duration, and disturbance
   family?
2. Which boundaries are inferred rather than named?
3. What changes when each putative controller is disabled?
4. What information moves upward, and what action or constraint moves
   downward?
5. Does an added level improve held-out intervention outcomes?
6. Does the advantage survive passive replay, arbitrary partitions, redundant
   refinements, duplicate fibres, ordinary RG, and resolution changes?
7. Can a flat controller match it, and if so, what native locality, access,
   communication, latency, state, or energy cost separates them?
8. Does an overlapping cover outperform or better identify the dynamics than
   a tree?
9. Which events are opportunities, actions, copied signals, and durable
   records?
10. Does the candidate survive an out-of-family change, or merely its
    training family?
11. Are any scale ratios dimensionless, and where does the microscopic unit
    enter?
12. What is invariant after quotienting causally redundant refinements?

The first application should be bounded:

- on `HC-DU-031A`, ask whether exact Clock-QCA dynamics contains persistent,
  perturbation-stable record subsystems with multilevel repair and
  representation-independent reunion records;
- on `HC-DU-011A`, ask whether a recurrent causal post acts as an active
  macrolevel constraint on later transitions rather than merely indexing
  inherited conditional history.

Stop the overlay if the candidate has no controller-ablation effect, no
held-out advantage, only analyst-chosen boundaries, or a free flat global
equivalent.

## Primary-source scope retained

The source audit preserves four important boundaries:

- Beer describes recursive VSM embedding while also allowing arbitrary
  boundary choices and membership in multiple recursive chains;
- Ashby's requisite-variety relation constrains regulation but is not a
  sufficient identity criterion for viable systems;
- Wilsonian coarse-graining concerns scale-dependent effective description,
  not active regulation; and
- causal emergence and hierarchical Markov-blanket results provide candidate
  macrolevel and boundary tools only under their declared intervention or
  generative-model assumptions.

Those are sources for the passport and rival set, not evidence that DU's
current dynamics realizes a VSM.
