# Finster baryogenesis, GU Krein branches, and DU attribution

## Plain-English result

The new GU calculation says that Finster's Dirac-sea rate can be mathematically
defined on the stable Hermitian branch but not through the complex-spectrum
core of the pathological branch.

For Dynamic Unity, the important lesson is about **credit**:

> A physical law excluding a branch is not a record selecting that branch.

Even if a future causal action really chose the stable branch, that would be
a law-level result. Records or finality would add something only if the law
still left multiple physically possible completions and a separately formed
record reduced that remaining ambiguity.

The present collision therefore gives DU a clean attribution assay, not a
baryogenesis mechanism and not evidence that records create reality.

## 1. Typed objects

Keep four maps distinct:

- \(D\): the domain on which an imported physical functional is well typed;
- \(L\): the physical law or selector applied to admissible completions;
- \(r\): the independently formed and selected certified-record interface;
- \(t\): the held-out physical target.

For a supplied completion class \(M\), define

\[
M_D=\{m\in M:D(m)\},
\qquad
M_L=\{m\in M_D:L(m)\}.
\]

For a formed record value \(q\), the lawful record fibre is

\[
F^{L}_{q}=\{m\in M_L:r(m)=q\}.
\]

The two relevant target diameters are

\[
\Delta_L=\operatorname{diam}t(M_L),
\qquad
\Delta_{L,q}=\operatorname{diam}t(F^L_q).
\]

They yield four cases:

1. \(\Delta_L=0\): **law-only closure**. Records receive no marginal
   selection credit.
2. \(\Delta_L>0\) and \(0\leq\Delta_{L,q}<\Delta_L\):
   **record-assisted reconstruction**.
3. \(\Delta_{L,q}=\Delta_L>0\): the record does not close the target.
4. \(M_L=\varnothing\) or \(F^L_q=\varnothing\): incompatibility, never
   reconstruction.

This is an application of DU's existing law/interface/reconstruction
separation, not a new mathematical theorem.

## 2. The exact current collision

The paired GU receipt establishes:

- the W216 good block is Hermitian with real spectrum;
- the W216 pathological core is Krein-self-adjoint but has nonreal spectrum;
- no positive metric can make that core self-adjoint; and
- no common branch-to-CFS-universal-measure map has been supplied.

That supports a conditional domain statement:

\[
D(\text{good})=\text{true},
\qquad
D(\text{bad core})=\text{false},
\]

if the GU branch generator is used as the Finster spectral regularization
operator.

It does **not** support a selector \(L\). Domain membership says where a
formula can be evaluated; it does not say the physical action chose the only
remaining input. The actual disposition is therefore:

> **DOMAIN ADMISSIBILITY ONLY / COMMON CFS ACTION NOT DEFINED / RECORD
> INTERFACE NOT SELECTED.**

## 3. Why records and layered finality do not yet enter

Finster's spectral projector is not automatically:

- an observer;
- a measurement instrument;
- a pointer record;
- a durable archive;
- an access rule;
- a regional certificate; or
- a layered finalizer.

It describes which spectral subspace participates in the regularized
Dirac-sea dynamics. Additional physics must still form and stabilize an
observer-facing record.

Likewise, GU's source-side Krein sign cannot be renamed as “finality” to give
DU credit. That would replace an unresolved source selector with an
unselected record label.

Layered regional finality could matter later in either of two precise ways:

1. it selects a real physical interface and the resulting record reduces a
   still-open lawful target fibre; or
2. record formation backreacts on the regularization dynamics through a
   separately derived coupling, changing a held-out rate under matched
   physical resources.

Neither arrow is present here.

## 4. Executable attribution control

`tests/du_krein_sea_flux_attribution_probe.py` passes `13/13` checks and
writes
`tests/artifacts/du_krein_sea_flux_attribution_result.json`.

The probe independently rechecks the minimum real/complex spectral fork and
then exercises:

- law-only closure with a constant record;
- a law-open case closed by a separating record;
- an uninformative record that leaves the target open;
- an empty lawful fibre;
- an unselected record interface; and
- the actual GU/Finster incomplete-selector case.

The exact rule is:

> Records receive marginal reconstructive credit only when a separately
> selected and formed record reduces a nonempty, still-open lawful target
> fibre.

The test is set-theoretic attribution and known finite spectral algebra. It
does not establish a physical law, novelty, baryogenesis, record ontology, or
new physics.

## 5. What this contributes to the North Star

Dynamic Unity's North Star asks whether a certified causal-record network
reconstructs everything an observer can experience and do, or exposes a
physical remainder.

This swing advances that by preventing a false positive. If a supplied
physical law already eliminates the alternative, a later record reconstruction
has learned nothing about the branch. Conversely, if a law leaves both
branches, the record must be independently formed and must close a held-out
target before it receives any explanatory credit.

That is useful across more than this case:

- field equations versus geometric records;
- decoherence versus public finality;
- source selection versus observer access;
- causal-action minimizers versus record interfaces; and
- quantum spectral sectors versus certified capabilities.

## 6. Stop and reopen rule

No further DU simulation is justified now. The local checkpoint has produced
its decision before any external-hardware boundary.

Reopen only after GU or CFS supplies a common, frozen physical selector for
both branches. Then ask, in order:

1. Does the law alone close the branch target?
2. If not, which physical dynamics selects and forms the record interface?
3. Does record conditioning reduce the remaining target diameter?
4. Does layered regional finality change a held-out consequence after the
   complete standard process, environment, controller, and resource budget
   are included?

Until then, fitting a cosmological rate or inventing record feedback would
encode the hoped-for mechanism rather than discover it.
