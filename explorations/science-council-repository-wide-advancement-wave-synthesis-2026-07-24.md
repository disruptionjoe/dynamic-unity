## 2. Unified finite interventional-closure theorem

### 2.1 Frozen contract

Let:

- \(S\) be a finite state or complete-history realization, \(|S|=n\);
- \(A\) be a finite frozen intervention alphabet;
- \(T_a:S\to S\) be the transition induced by \(a\);
- \(R:S\to Q\) be an independently supplied record or certificate map; and
- \(Y:S\to O\) be a separately declared accessible physical readout.

Define the visible diagnostic

\[
Z(s)=(R(s),Y(s))
\]

and, for every intervention word \(w\in A^*\),

\[
B_s(w)=Z(T_ws).
\]

The target readout is used to **test** the already frozen record. It may not
be used afterward to declare a physically selected replacement record.

Let

\[
E_0=\ker R\cap\ker Y
\]

and iteratively define

\[
E_{k+1}
=
E_k\cap\bigcap_{a\in A}T_a^{-1}(E_k).
\]

The descending sequence stabilizes at \(E_*\).

### 2.2 Exact result

\(E_*\) is the greatest intervention-stable equivalence relation contained
in \(E_0\). It is exactly behavioral equivalence for the declared
intervention/readout contract.

Therefore:

```text
E* = ker R
    -> every admitted response factors through R

E* strictly refines ker R
    -> some same-record pair has a finite separating intervention

model / intervention / readout / resource class unfrozen
    -> no comparison verdict
```

When factorization holds, distinct internal realizations may still differ.
The result is operational equivalence inside the declared class, not
internal-state identity or ontological priority.

When it fails, \(S/E_*\) is the minimal deterministic predictive refinement
inside the supplied model. Because it was computed using target responses, it
is a **remainder diagnostic**, not an independently formed physical record.

### 2.3 Tight deterministic witness bound

Let \(b_0\) be the number of classes in \(E_0\). If the record is
insufficient, a separating word exists with

\[
\boxed{|w|\le n-b_0}.
\]

Every strict refinement adds at least one partition class, so the bound
follows from finite stabilization. The chain fixture with constant record,
binary terminal readout, and

\[
T(i)=\min(i+1,n-1)
\]

achieves \(b_0=2\) and first separates its nominated pair after

\[
\boxed{n-2}
\]

interventions. The bound is tight under the convention that the initial
readout costs no intervention.

For two models, disjoint-union comparison gives an additive
state-count bound under the same state-output convention. Synchronized
product search gives a more general but looser
\(n_0n_1\) bound for the heterodox action-output convention. These statements
are compatible; they do not establish a universal physical horizon.

### 2.4 No size-free horizon

For every proposed finite horizon \(H\), a larger finite process can agree
through \(H\) and first diverge at \(H+1\). The heterodox family strengthens
the control:

- every intervention transcript agrees through the frozen horizon;
- the first separating sequence returns both candidates to the same
  endpoint; and
- delaying the response for \(h\) steps requires \(h+1\) pairwise distinct
  residual behaviors in that exact deterministic fixture.

Thus:

> Failure to find a remainder in finitely many experiments supports
> sufficiency only relative to an independently warranted behavioral
> complexity or memory bound.

Longer passive histories do not repair an unbounded rival class.

### 2.5 Finite linear extension

Let a finite linear realization carry an initial difference \(v\), admitted
maps \(M_a\), and readout functional \(\ell\), so

\[
f(w)=\ell(M_wv).
\]

Let

\[
V_\infty(v)=\operatorname{span}\{M_wv:w\in A^*\},
\qquad
r(v)=\dim V_\infty(v).
\]

If any intervention word detects the difference, one of length at most

\[
\boxed{r(v)-1}
\]

does. Before the reachable subspace stabilizes, every depth must add at least
one dimension; after stabilization, no later word reaches a new direction.

Raw realization rank can be inflated by invisible modes. The stronger
successor object is a minimal behavioral or Hankel rank under the frozen
intervention/readout contract. No stochastic, quantum, adversarial,
continuous, or relativistic theorem is earned merely by writing the finite
linear statement.

## 3. The ontology-license correction

### 3.1 Exact witness-absorption theorem

Let \(\equiv_A\) be equality of all admitted future responses. If a candidate
record \(r\) is insufficient, the abstract refinement

\[
r^+(s)=\bigl(r(s),[s]_{\equiv_A}\bigr)
\]

is sufficient and retains \(r\) as a factor.

Therefore a separating intervention proves:

```text
the candidate record omitted an action-relevant distinction
```

It does not prove:

```text
no physical record could contain that distinction.
```

Calling \(r^+\) a physical record would be circular because it is built from
the complete target-response relation.

### 3.2 Admissible-refinement challenge

Freeze a nonempty class

\[
\mathrm{Ref}_{\Sigma,G,L}(r)
\]

of refinements that:

1. preserve the old record;
2. arise from a formation family \(\Sigma\) fixed before the target witness;
3. respect the declared representation group \(G\);
4. have explicit access, provenance, and certification semantics;
5. fit the complete resource ledger \(L\); and
6. do not refit the interface or decoder after the outcome.

Then three dispositions are honest:

1. **Interface incompleteness.** Some admissible physical refinement absorbs
   every admitted response difference.
2. **Formation-relative physical remainder.** The class is nonempty and no
   admissible refinement is sufficient. The remainder is relative to the
   declared formation, symmetry, access, intervention, and resource class.
3. **Incomplete adjudication.** The refinement class is absent, empty for an
   accidental reason, target-defined, or left open after the result.

For a finite refinement class, one may enumerate it and return a witness for
each failed member. A **single uniform witness** against an infinite class
requires additional compactness, finite-basis, minimax, or closure
assumptions. This wave does not claim such a uniform theorem.

The result changes the ontology fork:

```text
factorization through independently formed records
    -> record sufficiency or operational duality within the class

candidate-record witness + admissible refinement succeeds
    -> incomplete interface

candidate-record witness + every admissible refinement fails
    -> class-relative physical remainder

unfrozen refinement class
    -> incomplete comparison, not an ontology result.
```

## 4. Signed all-port coherent-history assay

### 4.1 Frozen process

For history label \(B\), clock or target \(C\), state \(\rho\), declared
history operations \(V_0,V_1\), and real visibility \(v\), use

\[
\rho_v={1\over2}\left(
|0\rangle\langle0|\otimes V_0\rho V_0^\dagger+
|1\rangle\langle1|\otimes V_1\rho V_1^\dagger+
v|0\rangle\langle1|\otimes V_0\rho V_1^\dagger+
v|1\rangle\langle0|\otimes V_1\rho V_0^\dagger
\right).
\]

Measure \(X_B\), recording port \(s\in\{-1,+1\}\), and a bounded binary clock
observable \(W\), recording \(x\in\{-1,+1\}\). Retain both values and report

\[
Y=sx.
\]

### 4.2 Exact null and coherent signal

\[
\mathbb E_{\rho_v}[Y]
=
\operatorname{Re}\!\left[
v\,\operatorname{Tr}(W V_0\rho V_1^\dagger)
\right].
\]

Every arbitrary-weight incoherent mixture of the same frozen histories,

\[
\sigma_{\mathrm{cl}}
=
\sum_i p_i|i\rangle\langle i|\otimes V_i\rho V_i^\dagger,
\]

has

\[
\boxed{\mathbb E_{\sigma_{\mathrm{cl}}}[Y]=0},
\]

because \(X_B\) has zero diagonal in the history basis.

For the exact control

\[
V_0=I,\quad V_1=X,\quad
\rho=|0\rangle\langle0|,\quad W=X,
\]

\[
P(s,x)={1+s x v\over4},\qquad \mathbb E[Y]=v.
\]

The reduced clock endpoint is \(I/2\) both for the coherent \(v=1\) Bell
state and the dephased \(v=0\) mixture. The signed joint record detects a
distinction the endpoint loses.

In the symmetric two-port fixture, signing makes both ports equivalent
evidence. Keeping both produces one usable observation per preparation;
postselecting one port requires two preparations on average. The exact factor
of two is fixture-relative, not a universal quantum advantage.

### 4.3 Conservative shot gate

If calibration upper-bounds the admitted classical-null mean by \(b\), the
alternative predicts \(\mu\), and \(g=\mu-b>0\), Hoeffding's inequality for
\(Y\in[-1,1]\) yields a preregistrable sufficient condition

\[
n\ge
{2\left(\sqrt{\ln(1/\alpha)}
+
\sqrt{\ln(1/\beta)}\right)^2\over g^2}
\]

for false-positive probability at most \(\alpha\) and power at least
\(1-\beta\).

This is an assay gate, not a proof that the ideal null covers SPAM, leakage,
drift, phase errors, or arbitrary classical replacement protocols. Those
must be frozen as an instrument-level convex null before physical
certification.

memory dimension, predictive rank, thermodynamic support, coherence time, or
authenticated protocol state.

### 4. Multi-history character pooling

For more than two histories, Fourier- or character-weighted use of every
recombination port may recover the off-diagonal history-coherence matrix
without postselection. Determine the optimal accessible observables and
sample advantage.

### 5. Cross-platform remainder-rank invariance

One definition of record, intervention, difference series, behavioral rank,
gap, and bounded-risk capability change survives a quantum multi-time fixture
and an adversarial DAG/database fixture without semantic refitting.

### 6. Public-finality nonrepair theorem

Finalizing an insufficient quotient cannot restore future action
distinctions it already erased. It may stabilize the incomplete fact but
cannot create the missing response information.

## 11. Plain-English meaning

Suppose two possible worlds have left exactly the same records.

If we know how complicated the hidden process can be and what experiments are
allowed, then there is a deadline. Either every allowed experiment will keep
giving the same answers, or some bounded sequence of experiments will expose
what the records left out. We now have the exact finite version of that
statement and a way to compute the missing distinction.

If we do **not** know the process-complexity bound, there is no universal
deadline. A more complicated rival can imitate every finite test and reveal
its difference one step later. So a long winning streak for record
sufficiency is still conditional evidence, not proof that records contain all
of physics.

There is one more important caution. When an experiment gets past the
records, it proves those records were incomplete. Maybe a better physical
record could have captured the distinction. Only after we define which better
records can actually form, what they cost, and whether any of them succeeds
can we call the leftover difference a physical remainder.

The proper-time assay makes this concrete. A normal clock readout can erase
which coherent history mattered. Keeping both recombination ports and signing
the clock result by the port can recover exactly the cross-history
correlation that every specified incoherent mixture lacks. That gives the
program a plausible physical place to test the new logic.

The deepest combined lesson is:

> Reality for an observer is not merely the record it currently has. It is
> the future-response structure that the record can faithfully carry under
> the interventions the observer can actually perform—and the physical cost
> of forming a better record when it cannot.
