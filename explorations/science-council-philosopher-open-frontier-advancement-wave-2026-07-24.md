## Passive Refinement Flatness

Let \(\Omega\) be a fixed nonempty completion space. A certified fact \(c\)
determines \(C_c\subseteq\Omega\), and passive record acquisition updates

\[
J_c(X)=X\cap C_c.
\]

Then

\[
J_cJ_d(X)=X\cap C_d\cap C_c=J_dJ_c(X),
\qquad
J_c^2=J_c.
\]

Therefore:

1. passive completion-class refinement is associative, commutative and
   idempotent;
2. every ordering of the same certified records gives the same completion
   class;
3. a refinement loop
   \(X_0\supseteq X_1\supseteq\cdots\supseteq X_n=X_0\) is trivial; and
4. a proposition constant on \(X\) remains constant with the same value on
   every nonempty \(Y\subseteq X\).

Pure learning of fixed facts cannot generate perspectival holonomy. A route
effect needs active disturbance, loss/coarse-graining, context-dependent
translation, noncommuting instruments, a changed completion class, omitted
provenance or resource-limited approximate gluing.

An empty intersection is inconsistency, not universal actuality.

### Global obstruction is not automatically holonomy

On \(\Omega=\{0,1\}^3\), impose

\[
a=b,\qquad b=c,\qquad c\neq a.
\]

Every pair is satisfiable and all three are inconsistent. Every acquisition
order nevertheless reaches the same empty intersection. This is a
global-section obstruction without an order-dependent refinement loop.

## Holonomy–Public Fact Theorem

Let record perspectives form a connected groupoid. Perspective \(A\) carries
an action-relevant algebra \(\mathcal A_A\), and reversible path
\(p:A\to B\) induces isomorphism \(T_p\). Let \(H_A\) be the closed-loop
transformations at \(A\).

The maximal path-independent algebra is

\[
\boxed{\mathcal P_A=\mathcal A_A^{H_A}}.
\]

Indeed, \(a\) has equal transports along every two paths \(p,q:A\to B\)
exactly when

\[
T_q^{-1}T_p(a)=a
\]

for every loop \(q^{-1}p\).

For finite \(H_A\),

\[
E_H(a)=\frac1{|H_A|}\sum_{h\in H_A}h(a)
\]

projects onto \(\mathcal P_A\).

Nontrivial holonomy need not destroy public reality. It restricts
provenance-free public facts to the fixed algebra. Retaining a noninvariant
distinction requires provenance as part of the record. For noninvertible
instruments the corresponding object is an equalizer of parallel maps and
need not itself be an algebra.

## Provenance absorption

Suppose \(q\) behaviorally distinct paths lie inside one coarse endpoint
record fiber. Any exact sufficient refinement needs at least \(q\) accessible
labels within that fiber, hence at least

\[
\boxed{\lceil\log_2q\rceil}
\]

classical bits. A perfectly accessible quantum record needs at least \(q\)
orthogonal code states. The error-tolerant equiprobable version obeys the
standard Fano lower bound.

The target-derived refinement

\[

\]

always absorbs the effect abstractly. It is a diagnostic, not an
independently formed physical record.
