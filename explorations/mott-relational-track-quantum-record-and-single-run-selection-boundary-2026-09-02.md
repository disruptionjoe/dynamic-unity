# Executive result

Mott's cloud-chamber analysis supplies a particularly clean standard-physics
positive control for Dynamic Unity:

> Symmetric quantum dynamics can select the **relational geometry of the
> admissible record family** without selecting one member of that family as
> the unique material outcome.

An alpha particle emitted as a spherical wave can jointly excite chamber atoms
with appreciable amplitude only when the source and the atoms are approximately
collinear. The interaction therefore explains why the allowed multi-atom
responses are track-shaped rather than arbitrary constellations. It does not
choose one absolute direction from the rotationally symmetric family, produce a
law of single-run actualization, or certify the upstream source from one visible
track.

This resolves an important vocabulary issue. A physical quantum carrier may
already contain orthogonal, direction-conditioned record sectors before a
classical observer reads a track. In the unconditioned global state, however,
those sectors may remain in coherent superposition. They are alternative
quantum records, not several jointly accessible classical biographies. Later
decoherence or material amplification can make the ordinary track basis stable,
but the resulting track statistics do not identify whether the antecedent was a
coherent unitary state, a dephased mixture, an objective collapse, a Bohmian
configuration, or an Everettian branch structure.

The exact finite control proves four boundaries:

1. matched two-event correlations can be selected while every local marginal

2. no deterministic equivariant map selects one direction from a fully

# 1. The exact physical question

The apparent puzzle is familiar. A radioactive source is rotationally
symmetric and the emitted alpha particle is described by an outgoing spherical
wave. A cloud chamber nevertheless displays one approximately straight track.
There are at least five different questions hidden in that sentence:

1. Why are multiple excitations aligned rather than scattered arbitrarily?
2. Which direction-conditioned alternatives are encoded in the quantum state?
3. Why do those alternatives become dynamically noninterfering for ordinary
   access?
4. Why does one experiment present one material track?
5. What can that track certify about the upstream decay and its ontology?

Mott answers the first question. The answer constrains the second and helps
with the third. It does not by itself answer the fourth or fifth.

This run treats the source, chamber atoms, ionizations, droplets, and visible
track as physical. It remains neutral among standard operational quantum
theory, Everett, Bohmian mechanics, and objective-collapse completions. The
neutrality is not indecision about the calculation; it is the result that
ordinary track statistics do not discriminate those ontologies.

# 2. What Mott actually established

Mott's original paper is [“The Wave Mechanics of Alpha-Ray
Tracks”](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.1929.0205). He treated the alpha particle
and ionizable atoms quantum mechanically and evaluated the joint excitation
amplitude perturbatively. The later reconstruction by Figari and Teta makes the
central result explicit: the double-excitation probability is approximately
zero unless the source and the two atoms lie approximately on one line, with
the farther excitation downstream of the nearer one
([Figari--Teta 2012](https://arxiv.org/abs/1209.2665)).

Dell'Antonio, Figari, and Teta proved the corresponding separation in a
one-dimensional time-dependent model. With two wave packets moving in opposite
directions and two harmonic-oscillator detectors, the probability of exciting
detectors on opposite sides is negligible relative to exciting two detectors
on the same side, within the stated perturbative and scale assumptions. Their
calculation uses the Schrödinger equation for the composite system and no
collapse postulate
([Dell'Antonio--Figari--Teta 2007](https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.2230)).

The earned result is therefore:

```text
spherical source state
+ source--atom interaction Hamiltonian
+ high-momentum / weak-coupling / perturbative regime
  -> strongly concentrated joint-excitation support on track-like relations.
```

It is not:

```text
rotationally symmetric antecedent
  -> one dynamically selected absolute ray
  -> one observer-independent classical outcome.
```

The original calculation also treats atomic excitations as the precursors of
the amplified observation; it does not derive the full thermodynamic droplet,
retention, camera, observer-access, or reset chain. Those duties are physically
realizable, but DU already audited them in `HC-DU-178--184` and does not need a
second detector survey.

# 3. Relation selection is not member selection

Let a symmetry group `G` act on a symmetric antecedent `a` and on candidate
track directions `D`. Suppose `a` is fixed by every group element and a proposed
deterministic selector `s` is equivariant:

\[
s(g a)=g s(a).
\]

Then `s(a)` must be fixed by every `g`. If `D` has no global fixed point, no
such selector exists. For the rotational action on the sphere of directions,
there is no rotation-invariant direction.

This elementary no-section statement does not prevent the dynamics from
selecting the invariant relation

\[
\mathcal R=\{(d,d):d\in D\},
\]

meaning successive excitations share one direction. The set is invariant even
though none of its direction labels is invariant. In plain English:

> The law can select “the marks line up” while remaining unable to select
> “the track points this way.”

Nor does symmetry forbid a stochastic equivariant law. The uniform kernel on
directions is rotationally invariant. But such a kernel selects probabilities,
not one ontically actual sample unless the theory also supplies a physical
stochastic actualization rule. Objective-collapse models take that route;
ordinary Mott unitary dynamics does not.

This is the physical positive control that the recent common-view work needed.
`HC-DU-222` says a source action can select an admissible response family
without selecting one detector. Mott shows the same distinction inside standard
quantum dynamics: the action selects a relationally matched response family
without selecting one realized member of the orbit.

A chamber imperfection, external field, anisotropic source, thermal
microconfiguration, hidden variable, stochastic collapse, or actual apparatus
configuration can break the symmetry or select a member. Each is a legitimate
physical enlargement of the antecedent. It must be named and tested; it cannot
be credited to the symmetric state and Hamiltonian that omit it.

# 4. Where a quantum record begins

Dynamic Unity does not require a record to be classical at its formation
instant. A candidate quantum record must still have a physical carrier,
formation channel, retention horizon, accessible algebra, provenance relation,
and action consequence, as established by `HC-DU-075`.

The smallest idealization of the Mott structure is

\[
|\Psi\rangle
=\frac{1}{\sqrt{|D|}}\sum_{d\in D}
  |d\rangle_{\alpha}|R_d\rangle_{\rm chamber},
\tag{1}
\]

where `R_d` is a direction-conditioned excitation pattern. If the pattern
states have orthogonal support under the admitted chamber access, then each
conditional `R_d` is a perfect quantum record of `d`. Equation (1) nevertheless
remains one coherent global state. It contains a family of correlated record
sectors; it is not one selected classical track value.

Tracing out the alpha particle or uncontrolled environment gives the same
track-basis density operator as dephasing the direction sectors:

\[
\rho_{\rm ptr}=\frac{1}{|D|}\sum_d |R_d\rangle\langle R_d|.
\tag{2}

Therefore the ordinary pointer measurement has the same direction statistics
for the coherent state and the corresponding incoherent mixture. A sufficiently
global coherent recombination can distinguish them in the finite control, so
the two states are not physically identical. The classical track shadow has
simply discarded that distinction.

This answers the earlier question “can superpositions both be records?” with a
typed yes:

- multiple conditional sectors can each carry a record relation;
- the unconditioned state can coherently contain all those alternatives;
- that does not make them multiple simultaneously readable classical outcomes;
- a later observer's access may expose only the stable classical shadow; and
- the shadow does not prove that coherence was fundamentally destroyed.

Hartle's records-first decoherent-histories formulation formalizes a nearby
idea: records are correlations with histories inside a declared coarse graining,
and strong records imply decoherence of that history set
([Hartle 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.04145)). It does not select the one
universal coarse graining or settle the interpretation of individual outcomes.

# 5. Material formation and single-run actuality

The cloud chamber adds real physics beyond the joint amplitude:

```text
atomic excitation / ionization
  -> condensation nucleus
  -> amplified droplet sequence
  -> visible finite-lived track
  -> optical archive and observer access.
```

Once one track exists in a declared one-run ontology or operational outcome
contract, it is a genuine material record of the chamber-level droplet/track
occurrence. This is not merely an observable or an expectation value. The
carrier has changed and persists long enough to affect later systems.

But standard unitary Mott dynamics plus decoherence does not, without an
interpretive or dynamical addition, derive one uniquely actual member of (1).
The options remain distinct:

- operational quantum theory conditions predictions on the registered outcome;
- Everett treats decohered branches as the relevant relative outcomes;
- Bohmian mechanics adds an actual configuration;
- objective-collapse theories add stochastic nonlinear localization; and
- ensemble-only apparatus models predict frequencies without a new single-run
  selection law.

`HC-DU-142` already established this formation/actualization/archive split.
Mott makes the split unusually concrete: alignment is explained by the
Hamiltonian before the one-track ontology is decided.

Recent proposals about the first droplet should not be used to erase this
boundary. Schonfeld proposes a barely subcritical droplet and Coulombic
ionization mechanism and reports an exploratory track-origin analysis
([first-droplet proposal](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.05178),
[track-distribution study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.13249)). These are useful
candidate mechanisms and possible experimental directions, not an established
resolution of the measurement problem or Born rule.

# 6. What one realized track reconstructs

Apply `HC-DU-226` only after a classical or otherwise discriminable realized
record is declared.

In the ideal direction-sector model, each direction `d` produces a disjoint
track support `S_d`. The realized track then admits an exact direction decoder.
This is a valid positive control:

\[
S_d\cap S_{d'}=\varnothing \quad(d\ne d').
\]

Three stronger claims fail.

## 6.1 Shape is not direction

If the retained record says only “a straight track formed,” all directions map
to one value. The relation selected by Mott is then retained while its orbit
member is erased.

## 6.2 Direction is not source identity

Two upstream source types or formation routes can induce the same direction
and track-morphology law. Their record supports overlap, so no track-only
decoder supplies zero-error source provenance. Energy loss, curvature, timing,
particle-identification channels, source localization, and coincidence records
can improve the inference by refining the physical packet. They do not make
provenance intrinsic to the original track geometry.

## 6.3 Track statistics are not actualization ontology

The coherent state and dephased mixture in (1)--(2) give identical pointer
statistics. An observed track therefore does not certify which ontology or
collapse mechanism produced the one-run account. Distinguishing coherence
requires a different global intervention, not more confident inspection of the
same classical image.

This exposes two different nonselection problems:

1. **orbit-member selection:** which direction/outcome is actual relative to
   the declared ontology; and
2. **upstream provenance:** which source history generated the same material
   pattern.

Solving one does not solve the other.
