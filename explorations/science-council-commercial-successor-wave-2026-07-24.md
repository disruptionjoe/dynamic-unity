# Commercial scientist: successor wave

## Executive verdict

The enlarged corpus changes my top commercial recommendation.

The first wave treated cosmological renormalization at causal posts
(`WF-D1`) as an intriguing long-term conjecture and exact unlabeled-causet
enumeration (`COM-D1`) as the immediate decision product. The relevant
causal-set literature makes that division obsolete:

- the causal-post renormalization map is already an exact result inside
  classical sequential growth (CSG), not a mechanism DU must discover from
  generic enumeration;
- for the factorial family `t_n=t^n/n!`, a large post past conditionally
  produces an effective percolation parameter
  `q_eff≈sqrt(t/N_0)` and a dimensionless hierarchy
  `1/q_eff≈sqrt(N_0/t)`;
- the same map is exactly replayable, conditional on the post past, as a fixed
  effective CSG law, so it is genuine historical memory but not an active
  post-era return arrow; and
- the transitive-percolation regime to which this route flows already has
  serious adverse geometry evidence from interval abundance and asymptotic
  order structure.

I independently recomputed the first three points in a bounded executable
assay. It passes `8/8` checks. The maximum error between the post-conditioned
law and its frozen replay is exactly `0.0` on three finite causal fixtures;
different post histories remain distinguishable with diamond-law total
variation `0.0560324`; and the finite factorial-law transition distribution
converges monotonically toward the percolation law.

The smallest decision-changing product is therefore not a broad
`HC-DU-011A` platform. It is:

> **A causal-post route-disposition packet: exact map and assumption ledger,
> fixed-history intervention control, and a held-out geometry kill.**

Generic small-causet enumeration remains useful shared infrastructure when a
novel law actually needs it. It is ornamental if used to rediscover the known
CSG map before confronting the route's geometry.

My exact disposition is:

> **`WF-D1` promoted from conjectural mechanism to exact conditional
> history-to-law and hierarchy control; narrowed as a full DU route because
> active feedback is absent and the known attractor geometry is adverse.
> `COM-D1` retained but narrowed from first product to demand-driven library.
> `COM-D3` and `COM-D9` promoted to the immediate decision pair.**

No cosmological claim, continuum claim, physical scale, `Lambda` value, bank,
or seed follows.

Reusable canonicalization, extension multiplicities, and path-weight

The vote simultaneously overstates and understates `WF-D1`.

  exact fixed-history replay.

I retain `WF-D1` as a high-value control and a clean example of history
becoming law. I would not currently fund it as the leading route to recovered

## 4. Bounded evidence-producing swing

### 4.1 Exact post map

The executable assay checks with exact rational arithmetic that iterating

```text
(R t)_k = t_k + t_(k+1)
```

four times equals

```text
(R^4 t)_k = sum_[j=0]^4 binom(4,j) t_(k+j)
```

for an arbitrary rational sequence. It also verifies exactly that

```text
R^N(q^k) = (1+q)^N q^k.
```

These are independent algebraic recomputations of the literature result, not
novel theorem claims.

### 4.3 Fixed-history replay

The assay uses the standard CSG transition weight

```text
lambda(varpi,m)
  = sum_[k=m]^varpi binom(varpi-m,k-m) t_k
```

on the nonempty post-containing down-sets of originary antichain, diamond, and
chain fixtures.

For each `N_0`, it compares:

1. recomputing the post transform from the bare factorial law and fixed past;
   and
2. replaying the resulting effective coupling vector as a fixed CSG law.

The maximum transition-probability difference is exactly `0.0`.

This is not the claim that history is irrelevant. The effective diamond laws

> Once the post past is held fixed, the known construction contains no

It is long memory, not yet an active return arrow.

## 9. Newly visible or previously misused outcomes

### 9.1 Historical count is not automatically a birth-label clock

The first-wave path-covariance kill correctly defeats direct live `p(N)`.
It should not be generalized into “any cardinality dependence is
noncovariant.” The number of elements in the past of an order-defined post is
an invariant historical record. The distinction is:

```text
live stage label N                 -> forbidden in the killed formalization
invariant past size of a post N_0 -> admissible conditional state/history
```

Whether a post is selected after seeing the desired result remains a separate
inference problem.

### 9.2 Fixed-history replay is a type test, not a universal absorber

`COM-D9` is most valuable when it names what survives:

- if replay succeeds, the candidate may still contain real long memory;
- what it lacks is intervention-sensitive adaptive feedback;
- if replay fails under a controlled intervention, the resource and
  covariance ledgers must explain why.

This is more informative than classifying every fixed replay as “nothing
happened.”

### 9.3 A generated hierarchy can be a transfer, not creation from nothing

The factorial route converts accumulated causal history into a small effective
coupling. It avoids a tiny bare `t`, but it does not explain why the history
has the realized `N_0`, why a post occurs, or why the regime recurs. The right
question is provenance of the large historical number, not whether the
hierarchy is algebraically real.

### 9.4 Universality can strengthen a kill

A broad basin is valuable only when its fixed family has the required
physics. Flow toward a nonmanifoldlike percolation class makes the adverse
geometry more reusable across bare couplings. This is the opposite of treating
universality as automatic robustness evidence.

### 9.5 The disagreement between maximum and mean interval fits is a method lesson

A visually persuasive maximum-interval de Sitter curve and a poor mean or
interval-abundance holdout can coexist. Statistic choice is part of the claim,
not presentation detail. The right product is a frozen multi-statistic
decision surface, not a better fit to the favorable summary.

- exact enumeration: covariance infrastructure;

- interval abundance: geometry kill;
