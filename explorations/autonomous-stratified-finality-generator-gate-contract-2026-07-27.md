# Autonomous stratified-finality generator gate

## Exact question

Across three independently motivated autonomous physical mechanism classes,
does any mechanism select—without target coding—a robust nested family of
long-lived distinguishable states, physical carrier/write structure, and
future action-equivalence classes sufficient to reopen a Dynamic Unity
physical-record or layered-finality program?

The alternatives are equally valuable:

1. one mechanism selects a genuinely missing object and is admitted to one
   exact construction;
2. all mechanisms select only state sectors or timescales while leaving the
   formed accessible interface supplied; or
3. the whole proposed filtration is representation-, threshold-, or
   target-dependent.

## Candidate mechanism classes

Use exactly these three in the first gate:

1. **Liouvillian spectral metastability and invariant algebras.**
2. **Thermodynamic symmetry breaking, nucleation, and durable memory.**
3. **Autonomous dissipative error correction or stabilization.**

Do not add a fourth mechanism unless the first three expose a typed gap that
none of them can adjudicate.

## Frozen object/query architecture

For each mechanism freeze before assessment:

```text
antecedent:
  state space + autonomous generator + boundary conditions
  + control parameters + representation/gauge + resource regime

candidate physical object:
  selected sectors/manifold + carrier support + blank/written transition
  + retention/escape dynamics + provenance + readout/access ports
  + admitted future actions and resources

external queries:
  horizon and error contract + target action family
  + rival/fault model + reconstruction/finality question

derived verdict:
  selected filtration / supplied filtration / partial selection
  / operational sufficiency / physical-interface gap
```

No target, desired record basis, observer, threshold, or finality verdict may
be inserted into the antecedent.

## Unchanged mechanism passport

For every candidate answer:

1. What exact primary-source theorem or construction exists?
2. Which state space and generator are supplied?
3. Which invariant or slow structure is independently selected?
4. Is the partition exact, asymptotic, metastable, tolerance-relative, or
   fitted?
5. Is a physical carrier support selected?
6. Is there an actual blank-to-written transition with occurrence
   provenance?
7. What retention/escape error is earned at what horizon?
8. Which readout/access ports are selected rather than supplied?
9. Which future action family is fixed independently?
10. Does capability enlargement refine or destroy the proposed quotient?
11. Which resources and displaced information are charged?
12. What is the strongest standard-physics absorber?
13. What exact North-Star dependency changes?
14. What is the cheapest counterexample or kill?

## Formal target

For fixed generator \(\mathcal L\), horizons \(\tau\), and independently
declared action families \(\mathcal A_\tau\), define:

\[
x\sim_\tau x'
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\sup_{a\in\mathcal A_\tau}
d\!\left(P_a^\tau(\cdot\mid x),P_a^\tau(\cdot\mid x')\right)
\leq\epsilon_\tau.
\]

The gate asks whether the physical mechanism independently selects enough of

\[
\left(\mathcal A_\tau,\epsilon_\tau,\sim_\tau,
\text{carrier},\text{write},\text{access}\right)
\]

to make a nested quotient family a physical result rather than a fitted
analysis.

Exact nesting, approximate nesting, hysteretic reopening, and non-nesting are
all admissible outcomes. Do not force the word “filtration” if the maps do not
compose.

## Three bounded arms

1. **Source auditor.** Reconstruct the strongest primary-source result for
   each mechanism and distinguish selection from supplied modeling choices.
2. **Formal compiler.** Apply the unchanged passport and write the exact
   selection/factorization statements at the smallest honest scope.
3. **Adversarial killer.** Search first for alternative decompositions,
   threshold dependence, hidden observer/instrument choices, capability
   reopening, and ordinary enlarged-state absorption.

The chairman reconciles the first failed typed arrow, not persona preference.

## Admission rule

A mechanism may proceed to construction only if it:

1. independently selects at least one object still missing from the current
   North-Star stack;
2. yields a locally decidable proposition not already banked;
3. has a complete standard-physics null or absorber;
4. does not require hardware for its first decision-changing result; and
5. states the maximum honest scientific grade.

## Strongest absorber

Standard open-system, metastability, memory, and control theory may already
provide every selected state/timescale result, while the record carrier,
write occurrence, readout boundary, access, and action semantics remain
supplied. In that case the mechanism gives a useful physical representation
of layered finality but does not reopen interface selection or new dynamics.

## Cheapest kill

Show that every candidate quotient changes under an equally admissible
threshold, decomposition, unraveling, action family, or observer boundary
while the frozen autonomous physics remains unchanged.

## Stop

Stop after one mechanism passes admission or all three return the common
minimum missing premise. Do not:

- construct all three;
- add another supplied archive host;
- infer new physics from standard metastability;
- reopen preferred foliation;
- build a provider or hardware path;
- promote a paper; or
- execute the admitted successor inside this gate.

## Durable output

One source-audited three-mechanism comparison, one exact selection/failure
map, one admitted successor or clean `NO_READY_MECHANISM`, and one live
portfolio transition.
