# Thermodynamic quantum-record packet: selector audit and unravelling nonselection

## Result in one paragraph

The strongest bounded quantum-measurement sources nearly fill Dynamic Unity's
physical-record packet, but they do so in different and non-equivalent ways.
Explicit metastable apparatus models dynamically form robust macroscopic
pointers and reproduce Born frequencies, yet their solution is expressly
ensemble-level rather than a law selecting the outcome of one run. A 2026
autonomous detector model physically supplies amplification, nonequilibrium
thermodynamics, efficiency, jitter, dark counts, and reset, but evaluates an
average output current and assumes that its emitted excitation becomes a
macroscopic click or starts a further von Neumann chain. Quantum-trajectory
thermodynamics supplies single conditioned paths only after a detection scheme
and complete measurement record are chosen. This dependence is exact, not
verbal: the same dephasing Lindbladian admits a photon-counting record with
zero information about a binary \(Z\)-eigenstate source and a homodyne record
with strictly positive information. Bohmian and objective-collapse theories
show that single-run actuality is a legitimate physical-theory option, but
they purchase it through primitive ontology or modified stochastic dynamics;
they do not thereby select a thermodynamic archive, access contract, or
no-refit capacity law. The missing object is therefore not one undifferentiated
"record." It is the joint selection of **formation, single-run actualization,
and retained accessible provenance**.

## 1. Question and bounded scope

`HC-DU-141` showed that a frozen binary hybrid apparatus can induce an exact
finite-time channel and capacity while leaving its reversible thermodynamics
and actual retained outcome unselected. The present audit asked whether one
published physical model closes that packet by jointly selecting:

1. the measured coupling or observable;
2. thermodynamically typed pointer dynamics;
3. one actual outcome in an individual run;
4. retention, reset, and provenance;
5. observer access and a source-to-record channel; and
6. a no-refit capacity or held-out reconstruction statement.

The search was deliberately bounded to the strongest adjacent source classes:

- continuously monitored stochastic thermodynamics;
- metastable and phase-transition apparatuses;
- autonomous quantum detectors; and
- theories with an explicit single-run primitive ontology or objective
  stochastic dynamics.

This is a selector audit over those source classes, not a universal theorem
that no physical theory can complete the packet.

## 2. The packet has three independent physical joints

The literature makes a three-way distinction that the prior shorthand
"actual retained record" hid.

### 2.1 Formation

A microscopic difference is amplified into a stable or macroscopically
detectable degree of freedom. Examples include a ferromagnetic order parameter,
an avalanche, or an output excitation current.

### 2.2 Single-run actualization

One outcome, rather than an ensemble mixture or unconditioned density
operator, is physically actual in an individual process. A conditioned
stochastic trajectory is not automatically such an ontology: in ordinary
continuous-measurement theory it is indexed to an actually implemented
monitoring scheme and its record.

### 2.3 Archive, provenance, and access

The actualized result persists in a physical carrier, remains linked to the
occurrence that formed it, and lies in a declared observer's later action
envelope. An emitted excitation or current is not yet such an archive. A stable
pointer can be one, but only after its individual value and accessible
lineage—not merely its ensemble distribution—are physically typed.

No implication among these three joints is automatic:

\[
\text{formation}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{single-run actualization}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{retained accessible provenance}.
\]

The reverse implications also fail. A primitive-ontology theory may give an
actual configuration without deriving a thermodynamic detector or a task-
relative archive.

## 3. Strongest near-complete physical apparatuses

### 3.1 Curie--Weiss measurement apparatus

Allahverdyan, Balian, and Nieuwenhuizen model a tested spin, a macroscopic Ising
magnet, and a phonon bath:
[“The Quantum Measurement Process: Lessons from an Exactly Solvable Model”](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0702135).

The supplied coupling measures \(s_z\). The magnet begins in a metastable
paramagnetic state and relaxes to one of two stable ferromagnetic states. Its
magnetization is a macroscopic pointer:

\[
\hat m=\frac1N\sum_{n=1}^{N}\hat\sigma_z^{(n)}.
\]

This model earns several important pieces:

- the system--apparatus--bath dynamics is explicit;
- amplification and registration are physical relaxation processes;
- the pointer states are stable and macroscopically distinguishable;
- the pointer can be read later without restarting the measurement;
- the final pointer frequencies reproduce the initial Born weights; and
- the thermodynamic apparatus, rather than an abstract projective map alone,
  carries the record.

But the authors also state the exact scope boundary: statistical mechanics
describes an ensemble of measurement processes, not an individual process.
The final density operator and the two pointer peaks determine frequencies and
permit post-hoc subensemble sorting. They do not add a law selecting which
pointer value is actual in one run.

The model also supplies the measured observable, coupling schedule, initial
metastable preparation, and apparatus architecture. It is a very strong
formation-and-retention model, not a source-independent selector of every
record interface.

### 3.2 Autonomous nonequilibrium detector

Schwarzhans et al. construct a minimal autonomous particle detector as a
quantum thermal machine:
[“Quantum Detectors as Autonomous Machines”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16375).

A two-qubit heat engine maintains a three-level gain medium out of equilibrium.
An incoming particle excites the gain medium, which releases a higher-energy
excitation through a declared detection channel. The model supplies:

- autonomous operation without externally timed switching;
- a nonequilibrium ready state;
- signal gain;
- detection efficiency;
- timing jitter;
- dark-count rate;
- dead time and autonomous reset; and
- explicit transient and steady-state entropy production.

This is the strongest thermodynamic detector packet in the bounded audit. It
also corrects a common idealization: a useful detector is not merely a memory
that is prepared and erased. It is an open machine whose readiness itself
requires ongoing dissipation.

The exact boundary is equally clear. The paper evaluates

\[
J_{\rm D}(t)=\operatorname{Tr}\!\left[\mathcal J_{\rm D}\rho(t)\right],
\]

the **average** detection current, and obtains efficiency by integrating its
excess over the steady dark current. Consecutive events are assumed
independent and memoryless after reset. The emitted excitation is assumed to
be macroscopically detectable or sufficiently large to trigger a further von
Neumann chain. The downstream single-shot readout and durable memory are not
part of the autonomous model.

Thus the detector physically selects a signal-producing channel and its
thermodynamic performance, but not the final archive. Reset is present;
retention is intentionally absent.

### 3.3 Avalanche and phase-transition models

Merlin's many-body avalanche model has one thermodynamically metastable ready
state and macroscopically distinct pointer vacua:
[“An Exactly Solvable Model of an Avalanche-Type Measuring Device”](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0507250).

It is a useful formation control, but its conclusion leaves open whether a
generic bath coupling produces true single-run collapse or only an improper
mixture. This is the same joint exposed more explicitly by the Curie--Weiss
model.

Matos, Kantorovich, and Ford derive stochastic entropy production and a
detailed fluctuation theorem for time-dependent open-system protocols intended
to represent measurement:
[“Stochastic entropy production for continuous measurements of an open quantum system”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.07288).
Their stochastic environmental disturbance is added to mimic a measuring
device, and the connect/disconnect protocol is supplied. This gives genuine
thermodynamic trajectory structure but not a selected macroscopic archive.

## 4. Exact unravelling nonselection witness

Elouard et al. state the operational dependency precisely:
[“The role of quantum measurement in stochastic thermodynamics”](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41534-017-0008-4).
A Lindblad master equation is the average over trajectories. A chosen detection
scheme fixes its Kraus operators and unravelling; the initial state plus the
complete measurement record then reconstructs the conditioned trajectory.

The following two-monitoring witness proves that this dependence changes the
record's information content.

### 4.1 One unconditional dynamics

Let

\[
\mathcal L_Z(\rho)=\gamma(Z\rho Z-\rho)
\]

be a qubit dephasing generator, and let a uniform source
\(X\in\{-1,+1\}\) be encoded in the \(Z\)-eigenstate satisfying
\(Z|X\rangle=X|X\rangle\).

### 4.2 Direct-counting unravelling

Take the jump operator

\[
L=\sqrt\gamma\,Z.
\]

The infinitesimal jump probability is

\[
\Pr(dN_t=1\mid X)
=
\langle X|L^\dagger L|X\rangle dt
=
\gamma\,dt,
\]

independent of \(X\). Consequently

\[
N_t\mid X\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\gamma t)
\]

for both source values, so

\[
I(X;N_{[0,t]})=0.
\]

The conditioned state receives known phase flips, but the count transcript
carries no information about the encoded eigenvalue.

### 4.3 Homodyne unravelling

Monitor the informative output quadrature instead. With unit efficiency, the
integrated record for a \(Z\)-eigenstate can be normalized as

\[
dY_t=2\sqrt\gamma\,X\,dt+dW_t,
\]

so

\[
Y_t\mid X
\sim
\mathcal N(2\sqrt\gamma\,Xt,t).
\]

For every \(t>0\), these two conditional laws differ. Even the coarse sign
record \(\widehat X=\operatorname{sgn}Y_t\) is a binary symmetric channel with

\[
q(t)=\Phi(-2\sqrt{\gamma t})<\frac12
\]

and hence

\[
I(X;\widehat X)=1-h_2(q(t))>0.
\]

### 4.4 Consequence

Both records unravel the same \(\mathcal L_Z\), but one has zero source
capacity and the other has positive source capacity. They are not
information-equivalent. Therefore:

> **Unravelling nonselection theorem, scoped.** An unconditional Lindblad
> generator and system state do not determine a unique trajectory record or
> its observer-accessible capacity, even up to information equivalence.

Any record assignment must additionally type a physically implemented
monitoring boundary, detector, and transcript. This is standard
quantum-trajectory mathematics applied as a Dynamic Unity selector boundary,
not a new result in open-system theory.

## 5. The two honest single-run exits

The audit must not mistake the standard-unitary boundary for a theorem against
all objective outcomes.

### 5.1 Bohmian configuration

In de Broglie--Bohm theory, the actual configuration supplies an individual
trajectory. Tastevin and Laloë explicitly model a measured system together
with many pointer particles and show that the apparatus configuration can
determine the result of each run:
[“The outcomes of measurements in the de Broglie-Bohm theory”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.02519).

This closes the ensemble-to-individual joint by adding a primitive
configuration ontology. It also demonstrates that the outcome is contextual:
it may be fixed primarily by the apparatus's actual configuration rather than
reveal a pre-existing value of the measured system.

The model does not thereby supply a thermodynamic metastable memory, reset,
provenance certificate, observer action envelope, or no-refit capacity law.
Those must be modeled in the apparatus.

### 5.2 Objective collapse

GRW/CSL models replace the Schrödinger equation with a nonlinear stochastic
law whose random localization events are objective and whose amplification
grows for macroscopic systems:
[Bassi, Dorato, and Ulbricht, “Collapse Models”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.14969).

This is another genuine single-run exit. It also has empirical excess over
standard quantum mechanics. But the collapse noise, rate, localization scale,
and primitive ontology are additional physical postulates. The collapse
review explicitly notes that the physical origin of the random localization
process remains open.

An objective collapse is not yet a thermodynamic record packet. The theory
must still show how a declared apparatus retains the localized result, what
its reset and entropy production are, which observer can access it, and what
conditional channel supports a held-out task. Those questions are not answered
merely because one branch is objectively selected.

## 7. What is selected and what remains supplied

The bounded audit supports a typed ladder:

\[
\begin{aligned}
L_0&:\text{unconditional source/system dynamics},\\
L_1&:\text{apparatus coupling and candidate pointer},\\
L_2&:\text{monitoring or unravelling and transcript law},\\
L_3&:\text{one actual outcome},\\
L_4&:\text{retained archive and provenance},\\
L_5&:\text{observer action/access envelope},\\
L_6&:\text{source--record channel, capacity, and held-out target}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The sources give real arrows between adjacent levels, but no source gives a
canonical shortcut from \(L_0\) to \(L_6\).

Three particularly important corrections follow.

1. **A current is not a retained record.** It becomes one only through a
   physical detector/archive that retains a trial-indexed result.
2. **A stable pointer distribution is not an individual outcome.** It gives
   Born frequencies and objective macroscopic alternatives; a single-run
   value requires either an operationally conditioned record or an explicit
   ontology/dynamics.
3. **An actual outcome is not yet an accessible capability.** Retention,
   provenance, action, readout, and task semantics remain additional physical
   interfaces.

## 8. Disposition

### Earned

- A scoped Grade-4 exact unravelling nonselection theorem.
- A bounded source audit showing that formation, single-run actualization,
  and archive/access are separately closable joints.
- A stronger positive assessment of metastable apparatus and autonomous
  detector models than the prior shorthand allowed.
- Two honest objective-outcome exits—Bohmian configuration and collapse
  dynamics—kept distinct from ordinary conditioned-trajectory language.

### Not earned

- No universal measurement no-go.
- No conclusion that standard quantum mechanics cannot support operational
  records.
- No proof that Bohmian or collapse ontology is correct.
- No source-selected complete record packet.
- No thermodynamic finality-cost law.
- No observer-independent record ontology.
- No empirical excess attributable to Dynamic Unity.
- No hardware need or paper promotion.

### Exact reopener

Reopen only for one **unchanged physical apparatus packet** that:

1. fixes the system, apparatus, bath, source ensemble, and admissible
   interventions;
2. supplies or derives one individual outcome under a stated ontology;
3. physically retains a provenance-bearing pointer through the read horizon;
4. includes reset and entropy bookkeeping without replacing reliability by
   an unrelated scalar;
5. fixes the observer's access and task; and
6. yields a no-refit source-to-record channel and held-out reconstruction
   result.

A useful future comparison may instantiate the same apparatus under standard
unitary statistical mechanics, Bohmian mechanics, and one objective-collapse
law. It is not ready until the apparatus and retained record can be held fixed
across all three descriptions. Until then, another generic measurement-model
survey or local simulation would repeat the present boundary.

## 9. Plain-English meaning

Physics already contains credible machinery for making a microscopic event
large, stable, noisy, costly, and readable. It also contains coherent theories
in which exactly one result is physically real. What has not been supplied in
one package is the whole chain from the underlying dynamics to **this actual
event, stored here, with this provenance, available to these future actions**.

That is narrower than “solve the measurement problem,” and more useful. It
identifies the exact interface Dynamic Unity needs rather than treating every
pointer, trajectory, click, or collapse as if it were already the same thing.
