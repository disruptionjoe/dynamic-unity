## 2. What “selection” means here

The campaign does **not** ask a universal law to choose a laboratory from the
vacuum. It works relative to a complete physical antecedent that is fixed
without record terminology or target fitting.

Let a physical packet be

\[
\mathsf P
=
(\mathcal H_{\mathsf P},D_{\mathsf P},\mu_{\mathsf P},
\mathcal B_{\mathsf P},o,\mathcal A_o,G_{\mathsf P}),
\]

where:

- \(\mathcal H_{\mathsf P}\) is the admitted physical-history class;
- \(D_{\mathsf P}\) is the dynamics or action;
- \(\mu_{\mathsf P}\) contains independently fixed initial/source/boundary
  data;
- \(\mathcal B_{\mathsf P}\) is the material system and coupling boundary;
- \(o\) is a physical observer subsystem, not a privileged knower;
- \(\mathcal A_o\) is the physically available intervention/readout class;
  and
- \(G_{\mathsf P}\) is the declared groupoid of physical equivalences.

None of these inputs may contain:

- a variable already labeled “the record” or “the pointer”;
- the held-out target;
- a decoder fitted after target inspection; or
- an equivalence chosen only to make the proposed selector unique.

A candidate record interface is

\[
\mathsf I=(Q,r,f,\alpha,\kappa),
\]

with:

- \(Q\): record-value space;
- \(r:\mathcal H_{\mathsf P}\to Q\): history-to-record map;
- \(f\): formation/provenance relation identifying blank-to-written
  occurrence paths;
- \(\alpha\): physically realizable observer access/readout map; and
- \(\kappa\): retention, overwrite, reset, and invalid-attempt boundary.

A selector

\[
\mathfrak S(\mathsf P)=[\mathsf I]_{\rm op}
\]

returns an operational equivalence class, not a preferred coordinate system
or arbitrary serialization.

## 3. Selector obligations

Every positive must satisfy all of the following before it can support a
North-Star reconstruction claim.

### `S1` — antecedent and target blindness

\(\mathsf P\) is frozen before the record labels, decoder, and held-out target
are inspected. A target-coded sufficient statistic is not selected.

### `S2` — physical naturality

Every physical equivalence \(g:\mathsf P\to\mathsf P'\) induces an
operational equivalence

\[
\mathfrak S(\mathsf P')
\simeq
g_*\mathfrak S(\mathsf P).
\]

A coordinate, basis, gauge, or factorization choice is not a selector unless
the physical packet breaks that freedom.

### `S3` — formation and provenance

A returned value must distinguish an actual blank-to-written causal history
from a preloaded or merely correlated endpoint whenever the declared future
actions distinguish them. Endpoint correlation alone is insufficient.

### `S4` — access soundness

The observer can acquire the returned quotient through operations in
\(\mathcal A_o\). Mathematical existence outside that action class is not an
accessible record.

### `S5` — response sufficiency and minimality

For the frozen action class, histories identified by the record must have the
same future response signature. The interface must not preserve distinctions
that no admitted action can use unless formation/provenance independently
requires them.

### `S6` — representation descent

When two descriptions are related by a proved elimination, duality, gauge
equivalence, or presentation change, the selected interface must either
descend through that relation or explicitly declare the additional physical
operation that distinguishes the descriptions.

### `S7` — retention and reset closure

Every future-readable controller, environment, retry, invalid-attempt, route,
and archive memory admitted by the action class must be represented or
demonstrably reset. Returned shots alone are not implementation completeness.

### `S8` — nonempty physical fibre

For an observed record \(q\),

\[
\mathcal C_{\mathsf P}(q)
=
\{h\in\mathcal H_{\mathsf P}:r(h)=q\}
\]

must be nonempty after law, source, formation, access, and calibration
constraints. An empty fibre is incompatibility, not a remainder.

### `S9` — held-out reconstruction

Only after `S1--S8` pass may a target \(T\) be evaluated. The assay asks
whether \(T\) is constant, finitely bounded, or variable on
\(\mathcal C_{\mathsf P}(q)\) without changing the interface.

## 6. Nine gated swings

### Swing 1 — selector contract and arena gate — `COMPLETE`

Freeze `S1--S9`, the return classes, the arena ladder, transfer rules, and
stop conditions. No scientific grade is earned.

### Swing 2 — finite selector or nonselection theorem — `EXECUTABLE`

Extend the banked finite reciprocal source-response system with one physical
probe/memory coupling. Before naming any record:

1. freeze the action, source/boundary class, observer subsystem, action class,
   and physical equivalences;
2. compute the automorphism/factorization freedom;
3. derive the response-minimal quotient;
4. test whether formation and access select the same quotient naturally; and
5. return either one operational selector class or the smallest
   no-fixed-selector witness.

**Positive:** one target-blind natural interface class satisfying `S1--S7`.

**Cheapest kill:** a physical automorphism preserves the entire antecedent
and future-response structure while moving every candidate interface outside
its operational equivalence class.

### Swing 3 — dynamic formation and provenance

Only if Swing 2 returns a coherent selector candidate or identifies one
specific formation premise. Add a two-state local-detailed-balance memory
with blank, source-conditioned write, finite interval, success/failure/retry,
controller and entropy ledger, readout, retention, and complete reset.

This consumes `PRRF-02`. Its job is now to decide `S3`, `S4`, and `S7`.
Resource-to-capacity is a receipt, not the selection criterion.

**Stop:** endpoint correlation, fitted step labels, omitted retry/controller
memory, or a supplied archive.

### Swing 4 — field/direct-action descent

Apply the unchanged selected interface to an explicit mediator and its exact
source-only reduction.

**Positive:** the formed accessible record descends through mediator
elimination for the frozen actions.

**Alternative positive:** one independently realizable mediator-facing
intervention proves the interface is genuinely branch-relative.

**Stop:** representation labels are inserted into the record.

### Swing 5 — constructive local-QFT transfer

Translate the unchanged contract into one explicit local-QFT
system--probe coupling. Use a constructive realizability class rather than a
general realizability decider.

**Positive:** the physical antecedent selects an operational interface class
up to natural equivalence.

**Negative theorem:** all realizable schemes still form a nontrivial orbit
with no physically selected member or quotient.

### Swing 6 — finite-time infrared-memory transfer

Test whether soft dynamics, asymptotic symmetry, finite observation, and a
declared observer worldtube select enough of the interface to clear
`S1--S7`.

**Stop:** QED selects only the carrier while detector resolution, time window,
archive, or access remains freely supplied.

### Swing 7 — held-out reconstruction and first leak

Freeze one temporal, hard-process, extended-algebra, or capability target
only after the interface is fixed. Compare the law-only fibre with the
law-plus-record fibre and enlarge the action class until the first target
