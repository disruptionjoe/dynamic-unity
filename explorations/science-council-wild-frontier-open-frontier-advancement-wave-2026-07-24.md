## Finite record descent

Let a connected graph carry finite record alphabets \(X_v\) and invertible
reconciliation maps

\[
\phi_e:X_{s(e)}\rightarrow X_{t(e)}.
\]

Choose root \(r\). Closed paths generate a holonomy group
\(H_r\leq\operatorname{Sym}(X_r)\).

### Finite Record-Descent Theorem

A path-independent public fact is a family \(q_v:X_v\to F\) satisfying

\[
q_{t(e)}\circ\phi_e=q_{s(e)}
\]

on every edge. Then:

1. such families correspond exactly to \(H_r\)-invariant root facts;
2. the universal finest public fact is the orbit quotient
   \(\pi_r:X_r\to X_r/H_r\);
3. every other path-independent public fact factors uniquely through
   \(\pi_r\);
4. lossless common identification of every local distinction exists iff the
   holonomy action is trivial; and
5. a global section for one particular root value exists whenever that value
   is fixed by all holonomies, even if the complete action is nontrivial.

**Proof.** Transport a root fact along chosen paths. Independence from the
path choice is exactly invariance under every closed-loop holonomy. Conversely
an invariant root fact extends consistently. The orbit projection is the
universal invariant map. QED.

The correction is important:

> Nontrivial holonomy does not imply that no public fact exists. It implies
> that only holonomy-invariant facts can be public without retained route
> provenance.

## Action-relative finality

For an independently declared action-relevant readout \(y:X_r\to Y\), a
provenance-free path-independent final record sufficient for \(y\) exists
exactly when

\[
y(hx)=y(x)
\qquad
\text{for every }h\in H_r,\ x\in X_r.
\]

Otherwise a loop and initial record form a finite separating witness. A
spanning tree gives a finite assay: it is enough to test the fundamental
cycles associated with non-tree edges.

### Exact zero-error provenance bound

Suppose the orbit quotient \(\pi_r(x)\) remains available as endpoint side
information and an auxiliary provenance symbol must restore \(y(x)\). Define

\[
M_y
=
\max_{O\in X_r/H_r}
|\{y(x):x\in O\}|.
\]

The minimum zero-error provenance alphabet has exactly \(M_y\) symbols.

- Necessity follows because distinct \(y\)-values within one available
  endpoint fiber require distinct auxiliary messages.
- Sufficiency follows by indexing the distinct \(y\)-values separately
  inside each orbit.

At least

\[
\boxed{\lceil\log_2M_y\rceil}
\]

authenticated bits of support are therefore required under the frozen
zero-error and no-hidden-side-information contract.

This yields a **project-or-lift dichotomy**:

> Public finality must project away non-invariant distinctions or lift the
> public record with enough provenance to retain them.

### Minimal counterexample

Take a triangle with binary alphabets and

\[
\phi_{AB}=I,\qquad
\phi_{BC}=I,\qquad
\phi_{CA}=X.
\]

The loop holonomy is the bit flip:

- the maximal path-independent fact is constant;
- identity-valued action cannot be final without provenance;
- one provenance bit repairs it; but
- a constant action requires zero extra provenance.

Holonomy therefore cannot by itself define a universal scalar
“reconciliation charge.” The charge is action- and access-relative.

## Quantum fixed-algebra extension

For finite-dimensional record algebras and edge *-isomorphisms, the public
observable algebra is

\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{pub}}
=
\mathcal A_r^{H_r}
=
\{A:\alpha_h(A)=A\ \forall h\in H_r\}.
\]

For a finite unitary holonomy group,

\[
E(A)=\frac1{|H_r|}
\sum_{h\in H_r}U_h^\dagger A U_h
\]

is the idempotent projection onto that algebra. For
\(\mathcal A_r=M_d\),

\[
\mathcal A_{\mathrm{pub}}=\{U_h\}'.
\]

Full public algebra survives only when holonomy acts trivially on the
admitted algebra. Irreducible action leaves only scalars. Protected public
facts can nevertheless live in invariant, multiplicity or noiseless
subsystem sectors.

For binary \(U=X\), the fixed algebra is
\(\operatorname{span}\{I,X\}\); \(Y\)- and \(Z\)-facts do not descend without
provenance.

A central phase is invisible to the target-only adjoint channel but can
become visible under coherent path control. That enlarges the admitted
process/tester contract; it does not contradict the fixed-algebra result.

## Plain-English meaning

When records can be reconciled along more than one route, a loop may change
some distinctions. The route-free shared reality is exactly the part the loop
does not change.

If a participant needs a route-sensitive capability, the network must either
discard that distinction or remember enough provenance to say how it got
there. That is a rigorous connection among perspectival curvature, logical
coding and layered consensus. It is not yet a new curvature of nature.
