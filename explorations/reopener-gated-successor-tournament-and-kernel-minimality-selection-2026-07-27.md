### Minimality

A field is independently necessary in a declared model class when there are
two admissible models equal on every other field but whose North-Star verdict
differs when that field changes.

### Redundancy

A field is redundant in that class when it is a natural function of the
remaining fields and removing it never changes the verdict.

### Missing field

The basis is incomplete when two models agree on the whole declared bundle
but differ on an admitted observer-accessible result because another physical
or operational field was omitted.

These definitions keep “minimal” indexed by model class and target contract.
No universal ontology follows from a finite independence table.
