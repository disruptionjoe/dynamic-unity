# Verdict

Forty inline lenses—twenty computational and twenty mathematical or
physical—each supplied one strongest hypothesis for the law that might select
Dynamic Unity's matched family of record, capability, composability,
regional-finality, and ruler views.

The panel then cast two separate ballots:

1. an ordinary linear allocation of 100 credits; and
2. a quadratic allocation of 100 credits, where (v) vote units cost (v^2).

Self-votes were forbidden. A third analysis measured support across a declared
nine-community disciplinary graph.

The three leading signals are:

```text
LINEAR WINNER       P35  COVARIANT ACTION-RESPONSE CLOSURE LAW
QUADRATIC WINNER    P30  INTERVENTIONAL CAUSAL-STATE SELECTOR
SOCIAL-BRIDGE WINNER P30 INTERVENTIONAL CAUSAL-STATE SELECTOR
```

The A/B rankings are unusually stable: Spearman rank correlation is `0.9779`,
and eight of the top ten candidates are shared. Quadratic weighting therefore
does not expose two incompatible camps. It reverses the top two and increases
the priority of fixed-point, naturality, and instrument-selection formulations.

The useful synthesis is not that P30 defeats P35:

> A covariant physical action must first select the admissible intervention and
> response family; the interventional causal-state quotient can then select the
> minimal matched state/view family inside that physically admitted envelope.

P30 alone still supplies its action family. P35 alone does not prove that its
action selects an observer, consumer, archive, or ruler. Their composition is
the highest-priority theorem target. P40, the Regional Plurality Obstruction,
is the registered negative rival: the global selector may not exist even if
regional selectors and translations do.

# Vote contract

## Shared rubric

Each proposal declared one sentence, one decisive test, one strongest
absorber, and five bounded qualities: selector power, integration, testability,
novelty, and tractability. Each voter also declared two criterion priorities,
a method vocabulary, a family, and a disciplinary community.

The deterministic utility surface uses:

- the five declared proposal qualities;
- the voter's two criterion priorities;
- bounded method affinity;
- bounded disciplinary proximity; and
- a small cross-family bonus.

This makes the simulated judgments reproducible. It does not make the rubric
objective.

## Linear arm

Each voter allocates exactly 100 ordinary credits over its twelve highest-
utility non-owned hypotheses using Hamilton apportionment.

## Quadratic arm

Each voter allocates exactly 100 credits over non-owned hypotheses. An exact
dynamic program maximizes the same utility surface subject to quadratic cost.
Every ballot explicitly records zero for unselected proposals and `null` for
the owned proposal.

## Social distance

Nine communities form a declared adjacency graph: consensus/networks,
data/composition, security/provenance, computation/learning,
information/control, quantum measurement, fields/spacetime, formal
mathematics, and foundations/causal structure.

For each proposal the receipt reports:

- quadratic supporter count;
- supporting community count;
- computational-versus-physics vote balance;
- mean and median owner-to-supporter graph distance;
- share of vote units arriving from distance two or more; and
- a bridge score combining support, distance, and breadth.

Bridge score is descriptive. It is not an automatic winner rule.

# What the vote changes

## 1. One integrated positive target

The best positive target is now:

### Action-Selected Interventional Closure

> A complete covariant physical antecedent selects an admissible
> action/response algebra; the coarsest invariant causal-state quotient of
> that algebra uniquely selects the matched record, capability,
> composability, and finality family; the same action's propagation structure
> plus a selected calibrated response closes the ruler/metric surface.

This is a composition of `P35 -> P30 -> P33`, with `C12`, `P23`, `C14`, and
`P26` as absorber and exactness controls. It is not a new Grade-0 bank entry;
it is the now-preferred test decomposition of `CVC-A`.

## 2. One exact negative rival

`P40` and `P31` jointly define the negative branch:

> One physical antecedent may select only regional closures. If their descent
> has no global section, the result is not failed engineering; the obstruction
> is the theorem and any invariant holonomy is the candidate finite remainder.

## 3. The main circularity is now explicit

P30 wins the concentrated vote but begins with “one physical action family.”
Dynamic Unity has repeatedly shown that an action, observer, consumer, and
resource boundary may be supplied. P30 becomes a physical selection law only
if P35 or another antecedent selects that family target-blindly. Otherwise it
is an exact reconstruction theorem inside a supplied contract.

## 4. Distributed mechanisms remain downstream until typed

Hashgraph, Avalanche, quorum, CRDT, sharding, and cryptographic hypotheses
remain useful implementations and adversarial controls. Their low aggregate
rank means the council did not treat any one protocol metaphor as the
fundamental physical selector. `C16` survives best because regional gluing is
an architecture-level obligation rather than a transferred consensus slogan.

# CTS-A2 approach selected by the assessment

The vote does not complete `CTS-A2`. It selects this attempt order:

1. Freeze one complete source-pinned physical action and its admitted boundary
   data without supplying a view, consumer, or ruler.
2. Test `P35`: compute the stationary/Hessian/constraint/response structure and
   every residual physical automorphism.
3. Test `P30`: ask whether that same antecedent selects its realizable action
   family and a unique coarsest intervention-invariant causal quotient.
4. Test `P33`: ask whether propagation plus an action-selected calibration
   closes metric scale without target fitting.
5. Use `C12`, `P23`, `C14`, and `P26` as standard-theory absorbers and
   nonuniqueness controls.
6. If any global uniqueness step fails, test `P40/P31` for an exact regional
   no-section or gluing obstruction rather than repairing the selector by hand.

The cheapest kill remains two inequivalent matched families or ruler
responses under one frozen physical antecedent. No external hardware or large
simulation is warranted.
