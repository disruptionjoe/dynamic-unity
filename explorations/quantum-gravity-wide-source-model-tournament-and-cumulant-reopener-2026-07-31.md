# Wide-source model tournament and the cumulant reopener

## Executive return

`HC-DU-190` showed that a wide stationary wavefunction is not automatically
a broad deterministic local energy-density eigenprofile. This swing tests the
smallest plausible repairs rather than repeating that objection.

The result is a restricted but exact **profile-sharpness/novelty trilemma**:

1. a quantum-coherent or collectively delocalized source occupies multiple
   local-density sectors and therefore does not have one exact local profile;
2. a fragmented occupation-number state can have an exact broad profile, but
   a density--density gravitational phase on that state is exactly the
   classical Newton functional of the same extended density; and
3. a macroscopic Hartree or coherent-field source makes relative profile
   fluctuations small, but only through ordinary law-of-large-numbers or
   mean-field concentration. At fixed accumulated mean phase, the correction
   discarded by the mean profile vanishes as \(1/N\).

The source-model reopener therefore does not close with a novel mean phase.
It does reveal a better target:

> Prepare sources with the same mean stress profile but different controlled
> higher stress cumulants, retain complementary coherence access, and test
> whether gravity transfers those cumulants in a way not absorbed by
> quantized Newton interaction, stochastic gravity, semiclassical gravity,
> direct action, or matter-sector cross-talk.

That target is physically meaningful but not yet distinctive. Stress
fluctuations, noise kernels, BEC non-Gaussianity, and gravity-mediated
entanglement already occupy much of the component mathematics. The next work
must compare predictions, not merely rename variance as a Dynamic Unity
record.

## 1. The source claim and corrected question

Chen and Giacomini's published construction expands a general static source in
a common local-energy basis,

\[
\widehat T_{00}(x)|E\rangle=E(x)|E\rangle,
\]

and assigns two such profiles the phase

\[
\Theta_{AB}[E_A,E_B]
=-
\frac{\kappa t}{4\pi\hbar}
\int d^3x\,d^3y\,
\frac{E_A(x)E_B(y)}{|x-y|}.
\tag{1}
\]

The paper proposes changing the source potential to vary the functional form
of \(E(x)\). It treats the static stress commutator as negligible in its
admitted cases. `HC-DU-190` established that this does not by itself provide a
normalizable state or preparation map with a broad joint-sharp profile.

The corrected question is not merely whether a wide source exists. It is:

> Which physical source classes make a broad local stress profile exact or
> operationally sharp, and does the response that survives that preparation
> carry predictive content beyond the classical extended-density functional
> and standard stress-fluctuation theories?

## 2. Exact occupation-density theorem

Let \(N_r\) be commuting local occupation observables for cells
\(r=1,\ldots,d\), with joint number basis \(|\mathbf n\rangle\). Let two
sources interact through the frozen density kernel

\[
V_K
=-
Gm_Am_B
\sum_{r,s}K_{rs}N_r^A N_s^B.
\tag{2}
\]

This is the smallest controlled arena in which broad profiles, source
coherence, classical extended densities, and profile-dependent phases can all
be compared without changing definitions.

### Theorem 1: exact profile versus profile coherence

For

\[
|\psi\rangle=\sum_{\mathbf n}c_{\mathbf n}|\mathbf n\rangle,
\]

the local profile is exact if and only if every occupied basis vector has the
same \(\mathbf n\). If two occupied sectors differ in any cell, that cell has
positive variance unless a degenerate quotient has deliberately erased the
difference.

Thus coherence across distinct density profiles and one exact density profile
are incompatible descriptions of the same state. This is a joint-spectral
fact, not a quantum-gravity result.

### Theorem 2: exact-profile phase is the classical extended-density functional

For joint profile eigenstates
\(|\mathbf n^A\rangle|\mathbf n^B\rangle\), Eq. (2) gives the scalar phase

\[
\Theta_K(\mathbf n^A,\mathbf n^B)
=
-\frac{Gtm_Am_B}{\hbar}
\sum_{r,s}K_{rs}n_r^A n_s^B.
\tag{3}
\]

This is identically the phase generated by the discretized classical Newton
interaction energy of the two extended densities. A fragmented Fock state
may be broad and deeply many-body, but its profile-conditioned phase supplies
no excess over that
classical density functional.

This conclusion is scoped to the profile-dependent phase. It does not say
that the full many-body state, its preparation, or every other observable is
classical.

### Theorem 3: uncertain profiles produce cumulants, not the phase of the mean

For profile law \(p_{\mathbf n}=|c_{\mathbf n}|^2\) and a profile-dependent
phase \(\phi_{\mathbf n}\), the diagonal phase assay returns

\[
\Gamma
=
\sum_{\mathbf n}p_{\mathbf n}e^{i\phi_{\mathbf n}},
\tag{4}
\]

the characteristic function of the phase distribution. In general it is not

\[
\exp\!\left(i\sum_{\mathbf n}p_{\mathbf n}\phi_{\mathbf n}\right).
\tag{5}
\]

The logarithm of Eq. (4) contains the mean phase followed by its variance and
higher cumulants. An expectation-density closure retains only the first term.

| Source candidate | Local-profile result | Response result | Disposition |

| Delocalized one particle | Multiple local sectors; nonzero local variance, and transport can make local-energy cells noncommuting | Exact localized sectors reproduce the Newton kernel | Fails exact broad-profile gate |
| \(N\)-boson Hartree condensate | Binomial cell occupations with relative noise \(O(N^{-1/2})\) | Mean phase becomes accurate as \(N\to\infty\), while finite-\(N\) cumulants change visibility | Approximate repair, mean-field absorbed |
| Fragmented local-number Fock state | Exact broad occupation profile is possible | Phase equals classical extended-density Newton functional | Exact preparation positive, novelty negative |

### 3.1 Hartree condensate: the concentration-only escape

For \(N\) bosons in one split mode,

\[
|\Psi_N(p)\rangle
=
\frac{(\sqrt p\,a_L^\dagger+\sqrt{1-p}\,a_R^\dagger)^N}
{\sqrt{N!}}|0\rangle,
\tag{6}
\]

the local occupation is binomial:

\[
P(N_L=k)=\binom Nk p^k(1-p)^{N-k},
\quad
\mathbb E[N_L]=Np,
\quad
\operatorname{Var}(N_L)=Np(1-p).
\tag{7}
\]

The relative width

\[
\frac{\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(N_L)}}{\mathbb E[N_L]}
=
\sqrt{\frac{1-p}{Np}}
\tag{8}
\]

shrinks as \(N^{-1/2}\), but the profile is never exact for finite
\(N\) and \(0<p<1\).

If a potential difference contributes phase \(\chi\) per left-cell particle,
the exact coherence factor is

\[
\Gamma_N(\chi)
=
\left[(1-p)+pe^{-i\chi}\right]^N,
\tag{9}
\]

whereas replacing the profile with its mean gives
\(e^{-iNp\chi}\), whose visibility is always one.

At fixed accumulated mean phase \(\Theta=Np\chi\),

\[
|\Gamma_N|^2
=
\left[
1-4p(1-p)\sin^2\!\left(\frac{\Theta}{2Np}\right)
\right]^N,
\tag{10}
\]

so

\[
1-|\Gamma_N|
=
\frac{(1-p)\Theta^2}{2Np}+O(N^{-2}).
\tag{11}
\]

The macroscopic source therefore repairs operational sharpness by approaching
the ordinary mean-field limit. It does not construct a new exact local-energy
eigenprofile.

### 3.2 Fragmented state: the exact but absorbed escape

A state \(|n_L,n_R\rangle\) with both occupations nonzero is spatially broad
and exactly sharp for the commuting local number algebra. This is a real
positive missed by the one-particle model.

It does not rescue the proposed novelty by itself. On that state, Eq. (2) is
already a scalar classical extended-density interaction. The fragmentation
literature also warns that under generic dynamics fixed finite fragmentation
need not remain closed, so preparation is not the same as persistence.

### 3.3 Soliton: localization imports the missing selector

A semiclassical soliton with a fixed center supplies an extended profile only
relative to a trap, boundary, reference, or symmetry-breaking sector. A
translation-invariant quantum treatment promotes the center to a collective
coordinate. Delocalizing that coordinate produces a superposition of
translated profiles, restoring local-density variance. Pinning it restores an
approximately classical extended profile while supplying the location
selector externally.

This is not a complete quantum-soliton theorem. It is the exact structural
fork the next explicit soliton construction must defeat.

### 3.4 Free field: the continuum route remains open but does not restore the mean

A free-field coherent state is an eigenstate of annihilation operators, not of
the local stress tensor. Its mean stress approaches a classical field, while
local stress fluctuations and vacuum terms remain. A global energy Fock state
likewise does not imply a sharp family of smeared \(T_{00}(f_r)\).

Kuo and Ford already formulate semiclassical validity in terms of stress
fluctuations. Calzetta--Hu and Phillips--Hu promote the stress two-point
function to a noise kernel driving stochastic metric fluctuations. Fewster
and Ford study full smeared stress probability distributions. These are
strong absorbers of the statement that “higher stress moments matter.”

The continuum opening is narrower: derive a *different locked response* to
the same prepared cumulants across matched quantum-gravity, semiclassical,
stochastic, direct-action, and matter-field models.

## 4. Same mean profile, different cumulants

The strongest finite positive is a clean comparison class. A Hartree state
with \(p=1/2\) and a fragmented state with \(N_L=N_R=N/2\) have the same mean
local density. The fragmented state has zero occupation variance; the Hartree
state has variance \(N/4\). Under Eq. (9), they have different visibility at
the same mean phase.

That does not yet certify quantum coherence. An incoherent classical mixture
with the same occupation distribution produces the same diagonal
characteristic function. A complementary observable such as

\[
\langle a_L^\dagger a_R\rangle
\tag{12}
\]

separates the pure Hartree state from the corresponding number-diagonal
mixture. The eventual assay must therefore combine:

1. a same-mean/different-cumulant source pair;
2. a gravity-conditioned phase or visibility target;
3. a complementary recombination/coherence witness;
4. retained preparation and acquisition lineage; and
5. a no-refit comparison across the full rival family.

This mirrors the proper-time-history lesson already imported into DU:
dephasing alone can have a classical-mixture explanation; coherent
recombination is required to exclude it.

cumulant-response separation from the strongest rivals.

## 6. Corrected reopener

The mean-profile route reopens only if a source defeats all three tournament
horns. The more promising cumulant route reopens with one packet supplying:

1. two normalizable, finite-control source states with the same renormalized
   mean smeared stress profile and different locked higher cumulants;
2. a source-pinned preparation and persistence/error contract;
3. a complementary coherence or recombination witness that excludes the
   number-diagonal mixture;
4. a complete operator-valued response through the proposed gravitational
   theory;
5. explicit predictions from quantized Newton, semiclassical, stochastic,
   direct-action, collapse, and matter-cross-talk rivals;
6. one finite statistic on which those predictions differ without refitting;
7. a relational joint readout with attempted-run lineage; and
8. no hardware work until the prediction table already separates at least one
   live rival locally.

The cheapest next swing is therefore not another source search. It is a
**same-mean/different-cumulant prediction tournament** using the Hartree versus
fragmented pair as the frozen source input. Compute which rival theories
predict only the mean, which propagate the noise kernel, which create
entanglement or non-Gaussianity, and whether any finite observable remains
unabsorbed after complementary coherence access is included.
