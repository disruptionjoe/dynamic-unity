# Science council: what is Dynamic Unity's core?

## 0. Grounding, and what this pass is not

The council was convened on the question "what is DU's core claim." Joe
corrected the question mid-pass. His correction is filed in his own words at
`explorations/du-origin-and-mandate-joe-statement-2026-08-04.md` and governs
everything below.

The correction is not a detail. It says DU was created to answer a
**composition failure** across the sibling formalization repos, not to answer
an open physics question — and that the "core claim" framing may itself be an
accretion. The council accepts this and reframes: *what is DU's mandate, and
what claim-shaped objects does that mandate license?*

**The failure mode this pass is under orders to avoid.** An assistant took one
idea from a 2026-07-27 capture (regional consensus), registered it as a
question, cast it in a synthesis as DU's thesis, and built a campaign around
it. Joe corrected it 2026-08-04. The one-level-up version of that error would
be for this council to invent a core claim for DU. **This council states no
core claim.** Where it offers claim-shaped sentences, they are offered as
*things Joe could assert if he wants to*, each labelled with its earned grade
and its falsifier, and every one of them is derived from an artifact that
already exists in the corpus. Nothing below is minted.

Three coordinates, per contract: scope regional/repository-governance; status
epistemic (this is a reading of existing artifacts, not physics); formation
disclosure only.

## 2. The mandate reading (Q3-new) — five seats

**Philosopher of science.** The reframe is correct and it changes the grading
regime, not just the vocabulary. An integration repo's earned output is
composition results, cross-repo typings, requirements documents, and
non-composition theorems — *exact statements of what does not compose are as
valuable as compositions*. Graded as a first-order physics program, DU looks
like six weeks of negative results; graded as an integration seat, the same
corpus looks like a systematically built joint type discipline. **The corpus
did not fail. It was mis-priced.** Recommended move: adopt the integration
grading regime explicitly, and add "exact non-composition" to the success
conditions at `docs/certified-causal-reality-research-program.md:346`, where
"exact non-unification" already sits.

**Orthodox professor.** A mandate is not a licence to skip the object. If DU is
the composition seat, then its deliverable must still be a stated object: *what
composes with what, under which morphisms, preserving which invariants.* The
danger of the mandate reading is that "pull things into the picture" is
unfalsifiable. I want the mandate cashed as a theorem-shaped target before I
grant it standing. Recommended move: state the composition target as a
diagram with named arrows and named obstruction, or do not call it a mandate.

**Heterodox professor.** The mandate reading rescues something the CCR recharter
threw away. Joe's founding hypothesis — dynamics on a GU-like shape class tells
a better story than the static geometry — was demoted by a council in a week
where it had just been elevated by Joe. That hypothesis is still the only
DU-native object anyone has proposed that would be *new physics if true*. The
mandate names it again ("the dynamic story of what happens to the shape of GU
over time"). I read 2026-08-04 as a restoration, not a novelty. Recommended
move: treat CCR as what it actually is — a superb method stack built while the
program's own target was parked — and re-hang it on the restored target.

**Commercial scientist.** Then measure it. The mandate is a claim about DU's
throughput, and the throughput is measurable: 38/380 explorations touch a
sibling; ~30% of those compose; the one actual cross-repo assembly lives in
another repo, unratified, and DU fenced it. **DU is currently delivering its
mandate at roughly 3% of its output.** That is the number that should
embarrass, and it is the only number in this note that a governance change can
move next week. Recommended move: instrument it — a standing count of
composition artifacts per wave, in `LANES.yaml`, so the mandate is visible in
state rather than in prose.

**Wild frontier scientist.** Everyone is being careful and missing the shape of
the thing. Joe described a *permission structure*: DU is where the fences come
down. What actually happened is that DU built the most elaborate fence system
in the workspace — 242 counter-assumptive rows, seven stop rules, a nine-field
candidate packet — and then used its own fences to refuse the one composition
artifact its mandate called for. The stop rule that fenced the q0076 synthesis
(`COVARIANT-FINALITY-DYNAMICS`, "do not spend work on another knee, consensus
analogy…") fired *correctly on its face* and *wrongly in effect*. Recommended
move: give DU one explicitly fence-free artifact class — a POSIT with a debts
table, exactly the shape the wiki synthesis already uses — that can be written
inside DU without satisfying a reopen rule, because it asserts nothing.

**Convergence.** All five accept the mandate reading. All five say the mandate
is currently under-delivered. **Divergence:** orthodox wants the target
formalized before it counts; wild-frontier wants a fence-free artifact class
first; those two are in real tension and the aggregation must resolve it.

## 4. The D1/D2 contribution (Q4), and whether the campaign was aimed at DU's target

### 4.1 What the two results are

Both stand as physics regardless of framing, and both were independently
verified in Wave 3 (D2's arithmetic recomputed by a disjoint method; the
entanglement certificate derived in closed form; probe re-run 16/16).

- **D1.** Two of three preregistered mechanism signatures — finite-size
  critical scaling, and discrete participation jump (quorum) — are **EXCLUDED
  at measured scales on ≥2 independent platforms each**. The third
  (order-independent monotone convergence) is **SILENT on the wrong
  observable**; its theory-side burden routes to D7, its empirical leg parks at
  the hardware boundary with the one permitted awareness note already written.
- **D2.** Inside the null's own declared model class, states exist on which the
  quantum-Darwinism plateau identity I(S:F) = H(S) holds **exactly** while
  three audit quantities fail by finite, separately measurable margins, at
  fragment counts and capabilities published experiments already possess. The
  null's step 4 — auditing is classical post-processing with no empirically
  distinguishable content — is **dead in its frozen form**.

### 4.2 What they license

Exactly one thing: **the type-ladder non-degeneracy claim of §3**, at Grade 4,
on one rung. Before D2, the live charge against DU's ladder was vacuity. That
charge is refuted on the null's home turf. This is a *removed defeater, not
added content* — and it is mandate-relevant for a reason nobody has stated:
**the ladder is DU's composition vocabulary.** If the rungs were bookkeeping,
every cross-repo typing DU has issued would be bookkeeping too. D2 says at
least one rung is not. That is the single line of the campaign that serves the
mandate.

### 4.3 What they now FORBID

1. **Any consensus, quorum, threshold, or critical-scaling mechanism in the
   classicality transition at measured scales.** D1 excluded two of three
   directly; HC-DU-057 had already returned `NO_CURRENT_DU_SPECIFIC_EXCESS` on
   the same architecture's three most tempting physics claims; D7 closes the
   third either way. **Joe's framing correction is now backed by data, not only
   by memory.** The accretion is not merely unauthorized — it is empirically
   dead in existing evidence.
2. **Saying "the audit layer."** The campaign's own definition has three
   components (audited access + fragment independence + provenance). Access was
   witnessed. Independence was **struck from the verdict basis** (W2 is outside
   the frozen model class). **Provenance was never witnessed at all** — and
   provenance is where HC-DU-043's idle-versus-write fibre lives, arguably the
   rung DU cares about most. Any citation must say "one of three declared audit
   components."
3. **Claiming the physics.** The witnessed conditions are published SBS results
   (Horodecki–Korbicz–Horodecki 2015; Le–Olaya-Castro 2018/2019; Korbicz 2021).
   DU's share is a coincidence claim on one rung.
4. **Claiming it about completed records.** The audit margin tracks residual
   system coherence C and vanishes as C/ln 2. The content exists *proportional
   to the incompleteness of decoherence* — which is precisely where DU's own
   ladder does not yet license the word "record." This is the sharpest thing in
   the whole campaign and it cuts against DU.
5. **Generalizing beyond the branching family on DU's own work.** That step
   rests on a cited random-matrix result DU did not re-derive — flagged by the
   adjudication as "the single largest unearned step in D2's reasoning."

### 4.4 Was the campaign aimed at DU's actual target? Five seats, and they agree

**No.** Under the mandate reading, DU's target runs through the composition of
records / issuance / initial conditions / possibility-capability into a
GU-dynamics story, whose critical path is Z_U, the B5 packet, TI's named
physical exclusion, and DU's own absent covariant generator. **The Q-0063
campaign touches none of them.** It confronted an accreted claim (the
consensus-mechanism casting) inside an accreted identity (the CCR classicality
arena), using a layer decomposition its own source document said must be
rebuilt or abandoned.

The seats differ only on the verdict's temperature. Orthodox: "the work was
excellent and correctly executed; the target selection was the error, and
target selection is upstream of everything I can check." Commercial: "it
consumed the single scientific-flagship slot for the period in which the
mandate's own first deliverable was never attempted — that is the cost, and it
is the only cost that recurs." Wild frontier: "it is the purest case in the
corpus of the machinery running beautifully on a target the machinery
generated." Heterodox and philosopher both enter the mitigation: **the campaign
killed the accretion, which is exactly what a healthy program's immune system
looks like, and it produced the one ladder result that serves the mandate.** It
was not wasted. It was not aimed.
