# Certified Causal Response Theory — five-wave conditional campaign

## Charter position

Dynamic Unity's ratified North Star is unchanged:

> Determine what target-blind physical antecedents, if any, select material,
> provenance-bearing, observer-indexed record interfaces; then establish
> their observer-accessible reconstruction, operational duality, and
> smallest finite-remainder boundaries.

**Certified Causal Response Theory (`SEED-DU-MPA-21`) is a candidate theory
under that North Star.** It is not a replacement charter, scientific result,
or premise.

The motivating conditional picture is:

```text
one covariant multi-history response object
  -> mean geometric / source / gauge projections
  -> causal linear response and propagation
  -> higher fluctuations and nonlinear response
  -> selected stage-bearing material interface, or exact obstruction
  -> certified record network
  -> observer-accessible reconstruction or finite remainder
```

Every arrow is independently tested.

## Shared typed contract

Every wave keeps separate:

1. action or generating functional;
2. state and admissible history class;
3. equations of motion and lawful solutions;
4. gauge and representation quotient;
5. linearized response operator and Green support;
6. higher response kernels or correlations;
7. formation stage or process slot;
8. material carrier and persistence horizon;
9. provenance, observer access, and action class;
10. certified record quotient;
11. held-out target and finite first leak.

The campaign does not infer:

```text
common action = physical ontology
response hierarchy = formed record
hyperbolicity = stage selection
causal propagation = archive persistence
mean equation = complete quantum source
effective equivalence = ontological priority
```

## Wave 1 — stage, resource, and persistence

**Status:** complete as `HC-DU-176`.

**Result.** The three differently scoped DU results do not license a universal
linear implication. For a scoped retained provenance record of a nontrivial
relational character:

\[
\ker a_H\subseteq\ker s\cap\ker c
\]

is the exact joint-target criterion, and horizon persistence is
target-relative rather than global injectivity. The three target conditions
are logically independent in the abstract fixture; independent physical
realizability was not proved.

**Handoff.** A response formalism may supply the character/response and
propagation coordinates without selecting the stage or archive. Wave 2 must
test that boundary against the strongest established formalisms.

## Wave 2 — common-response-object absorber and typing audit

**Question.** Is there a single established response formalism that
physically selects more of the typed ladder than Dynamic Unity currently
credits?

**Mandatory candidates.**

1. classical action, Peierls response, and covariant phase space;
2. in-out 1PI quantum effective action;
3. closed-time-path/in-in effective action and influence functional;
4. Schwinger--Dyson and \(n\)PI response hierarchies;
5. stochastic gravity and stress-tensor noise kernels;
6. locally covariant QFT and relative Cauchy evolution;
7. process tensors/quantum combs;
8. direct-action and field-eliminated descriptions; and
9. one action-selection rival such as causal fermion systems.

**Optimized method.**

1. Freeze a typed adapter before comparison: object category, source,
   state, contour, boundary, gauge, renormalization, query, output, and
   admitted equivalence.
2. Freeze one duty matrix: supplied inputs, selected outputs, response
   semantics, stage, carrier, persistence, provenance, access, certified
   quotient, and empirical excess.
3. Reconstruct exact definitions from primary sources.
4. Separate formal representation, lawful selection, and physical
   interface selection.
5. Treat response order as meaningful only for a frozen physical
   source--observable pairing. Locally invertible source reparameterizations
   preserve the first nonzero order but mix higher tensors by the chain
   rule; auxiliary-field, integration-out, and representation changes
   require an explicit adapter.
6. Test gauge and field-parametrization dependence, including the
   Vilkovisky--DeWitt issue where applicable.
7. Find the smallest pair of candidates that agree on low-order equations
   but differ on a higher response or interface duty.
8. Stop if every apparent delta is already standard effective-action,
   Schwinger--Dyson, AQFT, stochastic-gravity, or process-tensor structure.

## Wave 3 — smallest hyperbolic causal-response tower

**Activation.** Wave 2 identifies one physically serious host that both
selects at least one previously missing physical-interface duty and exposes
an invariant, unabsorbed response-order question.

**Question.** In the smallest honest arena, how do the first, second, and
higher variations compose into causal response, and where does the first
physical distinction beyond the low-order projections appear?

**Frozen obligations.**

1. globally hyperbolic background or explicitly declared alternative;
2. field, gauge, state, boundary, and renormalization class;
3. gauge-fixed principal operator and constraint propagation;
4. retarded/advanced Green support;
5. first three nontrivial response orders;
6. one low-order matched hostile pair;
7. one held-out higher-order target; and
8. no record or observer credit unless physically formed.

**Positive.** A theorem identifies the least response order that separates
the matched pair and proves its causal support.

**Cheapest kill.** Ill-posedness, gauge dependence, state/refit dependence,
or complete absorption by standard response theory.

**Local-model rule.** Symbolic or exact finite-mode checks may validate a
proof boundary. Do not numerically simulate a full field theory when formal
analysis supplies the same result.

## Wave 4 — target-blind record-selector challenge

**Activation.** Wave 3 supplies a lawful response tower and a nontrivial
physical higher-order distinction.

**Question.** Does the host select a stage-bearing material carrier whose
horizon archive retains that distinction with provenance?

**Adversarial tests.**

1. alternative Cauchy cuts or process factorizations;
2. gauge- or symmetry-related stage choices;
3. equivalent dilations with relocated environment support;
4. same response hierarchy with different accessible archives;
5. persistent preloaded versus freshly formed values;
6. archive erasure or coherent uncomputation; and
7. observer/action enlargement revealing a hidden remainder.

**Positive.** A target-blind selector fixes the stage, carrier, persistence
class, and access semantics up to declared gauge.

**Negative.** A no-section, automorphism, or completion-twin theorem proves
which additional antecedent remains supplied.

**Maximum grade:** Grade 4 unless a finite physical discriminator survives
the complete admitted class.

## Campaign stop rules

Stop on:

- a mathematical generator renamed as physical ontology;
- a higher functional derivative confused with a higher spacetime
  derivative;
- an unproved or ill-posed hyperbolic reduction;
- a state, detector, boundary, archive, or observer chosen after seeing the
  target;
- a low-order matched pair repaired by refitting the nuisance class;

  theorem claimed as new;
- hardware required before decision-changing local learning; or
- an empirical claim without a finite mechanism-specific discriminator.
