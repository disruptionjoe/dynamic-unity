# Objective-collapse law, ontology, and access

## Executive result

Swing 1 returns a conjunction, not one exclusive label:

```text
law_level:
  PO_PLURALITY

complete_theory_level:
  BOTH_CONDITIONALLY_ADMITTED

operational_record_level:
  RECORD_ARCHITECTURE_SUPPLIED
```

In plain terms:

> A GRW collapse law does not by itself choose whether physical reality is a
> discrete set of flashes or a continuous matter-density field. But a complete
> theory that explicitly adds either primitive ontology does supply a real
> physical occurrence or matter process. Neither move yet supplies a bounded
> observer's retained record, access map, or certificate.

The two positive statements are compatible:

1. **Law-only non-identification.** The interacting relativistic GRW
   construction explicitly says that the same collapse dynamics can be
   formulated with flash or matter-density ontology. The standard GRWf and
   GRWm theories are reported to be empirically equivalent while assigning
   radically different primitive histories.
2. **Complete-theory admission.** Once `PO = flashes` is included in the
   theory, the law gives a joint probability distribution over actual
   spacetime points. Once `PO = matter density` is included, a covariant
   matter field is derived from the collapse-state history on past light
   cones. These are serious physical antecedents, not mere representations of
   one reduced channel.

The third statement is the Dynamic Unity boundary:

> Physical definiteness is not bounded accessibility. The collapse literature
> itself explicitly warns that what is real and what is accessible must not be
> blurred, and gives the number of collapses in a time interval as a
> well-defined quantity that cannot be measured reliably.

A flash is an occurrence, not automatically a trace retained elsewhere. A
pattern of flashes can constitute a macroscopic object over a time interval,
but the sources do not select one blank-to-written archive, retention horizon,
bounded observer access, rival class, or certification rule. A matter-density
field defines local matter facts, but its current value is not automatically a
provenance-bearing memory of which collapse history produced it.

The result therefore changes one current dependency without closing the North
Star:

```text
physical occurrence or matter-process antecedent:
  conditionally available from a fully specified (L, PO)

law-only primitive-ontology selector:
  absent

formed certified observer record:
  still open
```

This earns a scoped Grade-4 law-only non-identification/source-classification
result and a conditional Grade-2 physical-antecedent admission. It does not
show that objective collapse is true, select one primitive ontology, derive a
record, or make a new empirical prediction.

## 1. Three levels that must remain separate

Let:

- \(L\) denote the stochastic wave-function/collapse law together with its
  declared parameterized model class;
- \(PO\) denote a primitive ontology;
- \(H_{PO}\) denote the realized primitive history;
- \(\mathcal A\) denote a supplied apparatus and observer-access contract; and
- \(Q_A\) denote a bounded observer-\(A\) record.

The relevant ladder is:

\[
L
\;\not\Rightarrow\;
PO
\;\not\Rightarrow\;
Q_A,
\]

while:

\[
(L,PO,\text{initial data})
\longrightarrow
\mathsf P(H_{PO})
\]

is a legitimate conditional physical theory.

The distinction is not semantic. Each level has a different domain:

| level | native object | what it can earn |
|---|---|---|
| \(L\) | stochastic evolution law for the wave function | empirical deviations and a law over collapse-state histories |
| \((L,PO_f)\) | discrete flash history \(F\subset M\) | actual random spacetime occurrences |
| \((L,PO_m)\) | continuous matter field \(m(x)\) | actual matter distribution in spacetime |
| \((L,PO,\mathcal A)\) | physical coupling, carrier, readout, and access | a candidate formed and accessible record |

An observer may be made from the primitive ontology without every primitive
fact becoming accessible to that observer.

## 2. Native objects and supplied-versus-selected ledger

### Flash theory

In relativistic GRWf, the primitive ontology is a discrete set of spacetime
points at which collapses are centered. Given the model law, initial
wavefunction, background, particle content, parameters, and seed/boundary
data, the theory specifies the joint distribution of the flashes. A realized
history is then stochastically selected.

The noninteracting construction:

- uses a supplied Minkowski or suitable curved background;
- uses \(N\) distinguishable particles and a multi-time Dirac evolution;
- supplies collapse rate \(1/\tau\) and localization width \(a\);
- does not require a preferred slicing;
- provides a covariant joint distribution of flashes; and
- does not cover particle creation and annihilation.

The interacting construction:

- supplies an interaction-local Tomonaga--Schwinger unitary evolution;
- inserts discrete collapses;
- handles \(N\) interacting distinguishable particles;
- does not provide a corresponding model for indistinguishable or
  variable-particle-number systems; and
- explicitly remains compatible with both flash and matter-density
  ontologies.

### Matter-density theory

In GRWm, matter density is an additional physical variable. In the
nonrelativistic theory it is a mass-weighted marginal of \(|\psi_t|^2\). In
the relativistic proposal, the density at a spacetime point is defined from
the wave function on that point's past light cone.

Thus:

- the **choice** to use matter density is a primitive-ontology postulate;
- the **field value**, once that choice and the collapse-state history are
  fixed, is derived within the theory; and
- the matter-density proposal leaves regularization and other technical
  subtleties outside its scoped construction.

## 3. Scoped law-only non-identification theorem

Let \(L_{\rm GRW}\) be the shared GRW collapse-state law in the common domain
of GRWf and GRWm. Let:

\[
\mathcal H_f
\]

be the space of discrete flash histories and:

\[
\mathcal H_m
\]

the space of continuous matter-density histories.

The primary-source constructions supply two completions:

\[
(L_{\rm GRW},PO_f)
\quad\text{and}\quad
(L_{\rm GRW},PO_m),
\]

with different primitive physical objects. In the standard shared domain,
their outcome distributions for every experiment are reported to agree.

### Proposition 1 — law-only primitive-ontology non-identification

The shared collapse-state law and its operational outcome distributions do
not determine a unique primitive ontology.

**Reason.** The same law is completed by a discrete point-process ontology
and by a continuous field ontology. Their primitive history spaces and local
beables differ, while their admitted experimental outcome distributions
agree. Therefore a map selecting one of those primitive objects is not a
function of the shared law and operational distributions alone. It requires
an additional ontology postulate or a new selection principle. \(\square\)

This is a scoped logical/source-classification result. It does not prove that
no deeper future theory can select one ontology.

### Corollary 1 — no law-only record domain

A law-only record functional:

\[
Q(L_{\rm GRW})
\]

is ill-typed until the physical domain on which it acts is fixed. A record
defined over flash histories and one defined over matter-density histories
may have a common operational quotient, but that quotient must be proved; it
cannot be inferred from the shared collapse law.

### Corollary 2 — empirical excess and ontology excess separate

Collapse dynamics can have nonzero empirical content relative to ordinary
unitary quantum mechanics while the flash-versus-density choice has zero
operational excess inside the shared GRW domain.

This separates:

```text
Does the collapse law differ from ordinary QM?
  yes, in principle and in bounded model-specific ways

Does the law select flashes rather than matter density?
  no
```

## 4. Conditional physical-antecedent admission

Law-only non-identification does not make the primitive ontologies empty
relabels.

### Proposition 2 — complete-theory occurrence admission

Conditional on the full theory \((L_{\rm GRW},PO_f)\), the realized flash set
is a physical spacetime occurrence process with a law fixed by the model
antecedents.

Conditional on \((L_{\rm GRW},PO_m)\), the realized matter-density field is a
physical spacetime matter process derived from the collapse-state history.

These are stronger antecedents than:

- a nonunique Stinespring environment;
- a chosen classical unravelling;
- a bare indivisible transition matrix;
- a terminal reduced state; or
- a detector-resolution quotient supplied after the fact.

They therefore reopen a **conditional** physical-formation test.

The admission is Grade 2 rather than Grade 4 because the primitive ontology
is part of the theory's postulated physical vocabulary. The law does not
independently select it.

## 5. Why an occurrence is not yet a record

The sources make three boundaries unusually clear.

### 5.1 One flash is an event, not a retained trace

In GRWf, macroscopic matter consists of patterns or “galaxies” of flashes.
A single flash is a primitive occurrence. It is not by that fact a distinct
carrier that:

- was blank before the event;
- retains the event afterward;
- can be read by a bounded observer;
- survives a declared disturbance class; or
- excludes a rival history.

A spacetime history can contain an event without the event being encoded in a
later accessible subsystem.

### 5.2 A macroscopic flash fact is interval-relative

The primitive-ontology analysis says that a flash ontology often must be
considered over a time interval rather than at one instant. This makes a
flash-pattern statistic a credible record candidate, but it also means the
window, spatial coarse graining, object identity, and access route must be
typed. They are not selected merely by naming the complete flash set.

### 5.3 Reality and accessibility are independent

The collapse literature explicitly distinguishes what is real from what
inhabitants can measure. It identifies the number of collapses in an interval
as well-defined but not reliably measurable.

That supplies a physical counterexample to:

```text
primitive fact
  -> observer-accessible fact.
```

For Dynamic Unity, this is the strongest part of Swing 1. The event/access
wall is not an artificial standard imposed on collapse theory; it is already
visible inside the theory's own ontology analysis.

## 7. Relativistic and empirical scope

The relativistic flash results matter, but their scope must remain exact:

- the original model handles noninteracting distinguishable particles;
- the interacting extension assumes a supplied interaction-local
  Tomonaga--Schwinger evolution;
- the interacting model does not cover indistinguishable or
  variable-particle-number systems;
- the flash model requires no preferred foliation in its stated domain; and
- this does not establish a complete relativistic collapse QFT, quantum
  gravity theory, or Standard Model completion.

Collapse theories have genuine empirical exposure. Their parameters and
forms are constrained rather than protected by interpretation alone. Donadi
et al. rule out the natural parameter-free Diósi--Penrose formulation tested
in their underground radiation experiment. That result does not rule out
GRWf, GRWm, CSL, or objective collapse as a whole.

The campaign therefore cannot use “objective collapse” as one unbounded
family. Every later prediction must freeze the exact model and parameter
region.

## 9. Best next swing

The next swing should be ontology-conditional and analytic before numerical.

### Flash arm

Freeze:

- one bounded apparatus worldtube;
- one source interaction;
- one target-independent flash-pattern statistic;
- one finite retention horizon;
- one bounded observer coupling; and
- the escrowed later pointer response.

Ask whether the statistic is a sufficient history variable for the later
response and whether two histories with the same statistic can disagree on
occurrence provenance or the target.

### Matter-density arm

Use the same worldtube, horizon, source, and held-out operational response.
Replace the flash statistic with a matter-density functional selected without
looking at the target. Test current-state twins, preloaded patterns,
erase/rewrite histories, and archive relocation.

### Cross-ontology discipline

Do not demand literal equality between a point set and a continuous field.
Compare only an access-preserving operational quotient:

\[
\Pi_f(H_f)
\quad\text{and}\quad
\Pi_m(H_m),
\]

with the same bounded action and response contract. A common quotient would
be an operational duality result, not proof that the ontologies are identical.

The cheapest useful negative is one pair of lawful histories with the same
complete bounded candidate record and different provenance or held-out
response. The cheapest useful positive is one finite statistic proved
sufficient relative to the frozen theory, action class, horizon, and target.
