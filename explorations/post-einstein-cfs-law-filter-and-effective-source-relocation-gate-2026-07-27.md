## 2. Effective-source relocation invariance

### 2.1 Typed setup

Let:

\[
M
\]

be a fixed physical configuration class,

\[
E:M\rightarrow W
\]

the baseline field-equation operator, and

\[
C:M\rightarrow W
\]

a proposed correction. Let \(J\in W\) be an otherwise unrestricted effective
source. Define the corrected and baseline lawful graphs by

\[
\mathcal L_C
=
\{(m,J)\in M\times W:E(m)+C(m)=J\},
\]

\[
\mathcal L_0
=
\{(m,J_0)\in M\times W:E(m)=J_0\}.
\]

The record and target are fixed maps of the physical configuration:

\[
r:M\rightarrow Q,
\qquad
t:M\rightarrow Y.
\]

This is the scope in which a source is an unobserved explanatory variable,
not itself part of the certified record.

### 2.2 The theorem

**Theorem (`HC-DU-062`, effective-source relocation invariance).** The map

\[
\Phi_C:\mathcal L_C\rightarrow\mathcal L_0,
\qquad
\Phi_C(m,J)=(m,J-C(m)),
\]

is a bijection with inverse

\[
\Phi_C^{-1}(m,J_0)=(m,J_0+C(m)).
\]

It preserves \(m\). Therefore it preserves every record, target, gauge orbit,
physical completion fibre, record-conditioned target set, and target diameter
that factors through \(m\).

**Proof.** If \(E(m)+C(m)=J\), then

\[
E(m)=J-C(m),
\]

so \(\Phi_C(m,J)\in\mathcal L_0\). Conversely, if \(E(m)=J_0\), then

\[
E(m)+C(m)=J_0+C(m),
\]

so \(\Phi_C^{-1}(m,J_0)\in\mathcal L_C\). The maps are mutual inverses and do
not change \(m\). Composing either lawful graph with \(r\) or \(t\) therefore
gives the same physical values. \(\square\)

### 2.3 Consequence for restricted sources

The theorem does **not** say every correction is empty. Let
\(\mathcal S(m)\subseteq W\) be an independently fixed admissible source class.
Then the corrected lawful physical class is

\[
M_C^{\mathcal S}
=
\{m\in M:E(m)+C(m)\in\mathcal S(m)\}.
\]

The correction can change reconstruction exactly when the frozen source class
is not closed under the translation induced by \(C\). This yields the minimum
admission contract:

1. state the explicit operator \(C\);
2. freeze the configuration, gauge, boundary, and source classes before the
   calculation;
3. fix or independently select its coefficients and regulator;
4. show that the source class does not absorb \(C\);
5. prove a changed rank, nullspace, fibre, or witness on the same \(3+1\)
   packet; and
6. state the regulator-robust or selected-regulator empirical meaning.

Without those items, writing an equation in Einstein form and moving the
remainder to \(T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{eff}}\) is attribution bookkeeping, not a
new physical reconstruction result.

## 3. Collision with the current CFS results

### 3.1 Fischer--Finster: leading continuum law

Fischer--Finster construct the curved-spacetime causal-fermion-system setting
from supplied globally hyperbolic geometry and algebraic quantum-field data.
Their continuum-limit equations recover the trace-free Einstein condition;
with specified on-shell fermionic matter they recover the corresponding
Einstein--Dirac source equation.

For Dynamic Unity this is a serious compatibility and lawful-completion
input. It is not yet a post-Einstein discriminator:

- in \(1+1\) dimensions the trace-free Ricci condition is identically
  vacuous, as already banked;
- in the completed \(3+1\) control its leading vacuum content is the ordinary
  Einstein absorber; and
- the paper does not provide one explicit finite-regularization
  \(\Delta E_\theta\) with a frozen coefficient/source contract for the DU
  packet.

### 3.2 Finster--Krpoun: Einstein form and correction program

Finster--Krpoun derive Lorentzian geometry and an Einstein-form equation from
the causal variational structure under declared smoothness, short-range, and
osculation assumptions. Their effective stress-energy encodes osculation
errors and small-scale effects, with a stated \(O(\delta^2)\) scale in the
construction.

The source also names several places where further corrections can arise:

- higher orders in the microscopic length \(\delta\);
- osculation, operator, connection, and torsion effects;
- the regularizing vector field; and
- modified-measure effects.

Those are legitimate open calculations. They are not yet a single typed
operator that DU can apply unchanged. In particular, the paper says the
regularizing-vector effects on the Einstein equations remain to be analyzed,
and the systematic higher-order calculation remains future work.

If the resulting terms are left inside an unconstrained
\(T_{\mu\nu}^{\mathrm{eff}}\), `HC-DU-062` applies: the physical lawful class is
unchanged after source relocation. If CFS independently restricts that source
or fixes a correction that cannot be absorbed, the reopener fires.

linear baseline operator \(L\), record \(R\), target \(T\), and admitted source
difference space \(D_J\), local reconstruction requires

\[

A correction changes this test only after its effect on the **frozen**
admissible source space is specified. If every new term can be reclassified as

The research boundary is now sharper:

