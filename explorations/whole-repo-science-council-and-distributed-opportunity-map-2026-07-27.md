# Whole-repository Science Council and distributed-systems opportunity map

## Executive synthesis

The panel does not agree that Dynamic Unity has reached a scientific wall.
It agrees that one *method family* has reached a wall:

> Supplying another recorder, archive, protocol, quotient, or field list will
> not explain why that physical interface forms or why it has the claimed
> access and action semantics.

The strongest convergence is toward a different question:

> Can independently motivated autonomous physics select a nested family of
> long-lived, distinguishable, action-stable record classes—something that
> genuinely behaves like layered finality—without fitting the record basis,
> threshold, observer, or target afterward?

This is the recommended primary. The first move is a mechanism gate, not a
new simulation:

1. Liouvillian/open-system spectral metastability;
2. thermodynamic symmetry breaking, nucleation, and durable memory; and
3. autonomous dissipative error correction or stabilization.

The gate asks what each mechanism independently selects: state sectors,
timescales, carrier support, write/retention dynamics, accessible readout
ports, and future action-equivalence classes. A mechanism that selects only a
slow manifold but not a formed accessible interface is a useful partial
result, not a positive `H-CCR-17` specimen.

The reserve is a typed physical-to-coordination adapter theorem. It should
begin only after one physical mechanism supplies real objects to adapt.

The maximum-ceiling frontier remains a complete-process
three-perspective reconciliation-holonomy test. It stays frontier because no
physical perspective connection or invariant loop observable is selected yet.

## How to read the panel

These are twenty-one analytical lenses reasoning inline over the same
repository state:

- five Science Council lenses;
- eleven distributed, networking, database, and cryptographic specialists;
- five additional lenses chosen for physical mechanism, causal modeling,
  formal transfer, experimental discrimination, and representation
  discovery.

Each supplies three proposals and an approach. Suggestions are not votes,
evidence, claims, or priority authorization. The synthesis is judged against
the repository's exact reopeners and absorbers afterward.

# Part I — five-person Science Council

## 1. Orthodox professor

1. **Derive a spectral finality filtration from standard autonomous
   dynamics.** Start from a fixed finite Lindbladian or other complete
   open-system generator. Use its invariant algebra, slow eigenspaces,
   metastable manifolds, and robustness under perturbation to determine
   whether nested action-stable quotients are selected without a fitted
   pointer basis or threshold.
2. **Prove a physical-to-coordination adapter theorem.** Define the source
   and target categories before importing FLP, CALM, quorum, or knowledge
   results. Require preservation of event identity, access, fault model,
   interventions, and resources. Bank the first failed assumption if no
   faithful adapter exists.
3. **Close the holonomy frontier at its smallest honest scope.** Freeze one
   complete three-party quantum process and every ordinary phase, operation
   order, route-memory, and gauge variable. Prove either that every
   reconciliation loop factors through that process or exhibit the smallest
   invariant residual.

## 2. Heterodox professor

1. **Model finality as a co-evolving network of physical memory domains.**
   Allow carrier topology, access, and stable sectors to change each other.
   Begin with a tiny adaptive hypergraph whose update law is fixed before
   records are classified; attack any apparent hierarchy with a static
   enlarged-state absorber.
2. **Search for a metastable-to-binding transition rather than a universal
   threshold.** Ask whether a hierarchy of escape times and barrier classes
   selects local, regional, and effectively irreversible action regimes.
   Preserve hysteresis and rare-event reopening instead of idealizing them
   away.
3. **Treat capability expansion as an ontic stress test.** Re-adjudicate every
   proposed final record when new interventions become available. A hierarchy
   that survives systematic action enlargement is more interesting than one
   defined by passive observation.

## 3. Commercial scientist

1. **Build one reusable autonomous-finality passport.** For any candidate
   mechanism, require generator, selected sectors, physical carrier, write,
   retention, access, actions, resources, error versus horizon, absorber, and
   falsifier. Apply it unchanged to only three high-quality mechanisms.
2. **Produce one benchmark with ground truth and one physical specimen.**
   First calibrate the distinction among state stability, record formation,
   access, and action safety in a fully specified model; then transfer the
   unchanged measures to the winning physical mechanism.
3. **Extract a publishable negative if selection fails.** A scoped theorem
   that autonomous metastability selects timescales and state quotients but
   not an accessible record interface would be useful if positioned against
   open-system, measurement, and distributed-finality literatures.

## 4. Wild-frontier scientist

1. **Take the reconciliation-holonomy moonshot.** Hold local reduced states
   and all pairwise terminal record overlaps fixed while varying only the
   physical route through which three perspectives reconcile. Look for a
   gauge-invariant loop difference outside the complete ordinary process.
2. **Test record backreaction only through matched physical resources.**
   Construct two protocols with equal energy, entropy export, memory,
   communication, and stress-energy but different formed-record structure.
   Any surviving response is interesting; disappearance kills semantic-force
   stories cleanly.
3. **Ask whether a finality generator can self-modify its own admissible action
   algebra.** Begin with an autonomous controller whose physical outputs alter
   later available interventions. Compare it with a fixed enlarged-state
   machine before calling the feedback genuinely generative.

## 5. Philosopher of science

1. **Construct a comparative theory rather than prematurely choose an
   ontology.** State record-first, operational-duality, and physics-first
   models over one common intervention language. Identify exactly which
   empirical or explanatory virtues distinguish them.
2. **Separate truth, certification, disclosure, publicness, and capability in
   one physical example.** Use one record that can be retained, hidden,
   proven, jointly opened, and made action-enabling under different physical
   channels. This prevents each transition from being called
   “actualization.”
3. **Harden the non-unification into a research compass.** Every new program
   should declare whether it targets interface formation, target
   reconstruction, public coordination, or new dynamics. Reject projects
   whose apparent profundity comes from moving silently among those jobs.

# Part II — distributed, networking, database, and cryptographic specialists

## 6. Hashgraph and gossip-about-gossip specialist

1. **Test second-order provenance as a physical sufficient statistic.**
   Compare endpoint records with records that also retain who could have
   learned what from whom. Use held-out interventions sensitive to route,
   equivocation, and delayed disclosure; do not infer physical origin from a
   signed ancestry graph.
2. **Separate ancestry, knowledge, virtual voting, and public order.** Build a
   typed ladder from formed physical events to authenticated DAGs and then to
   protocol verdicts. Locate which arrow, if any, has a physical analogue
   selected rather than supplied.
3. **Use route loops as the clean holonomy control.** Calculate every
   order-dependent result already implied by the complete routed process.
   Only a residual invariant under DAG refactoring should reach the
   perspectival-curvature program.

## 7. Avalanche and metastable-consensus specialist

1. **Derive thresholds from physical escape dynamics rather than choose
   them.** Translate spectral gaps, nucleation barriers, or first-passage
   distributions into bounded-risk commitment times. A threshold counts only
   if it is fixed by the physical model and robust over a declared class.
2. **Run a real finite-size scaling test.** Vary system size and topology,
   report error and escape-time distributions, and distinguish a smooth
   confidence knee from a selected critical regime or universality class.
3. **Attack independence with correlated physical noise.** Replace ideal
   random subsampling by spatially or causally correlated failures. Determine
   whether apparent regional hardening survives effective-support loss,
   adversarial bias, and rare coherent shocks.

## 8. Byzantine consensus and reliable-broadcast specialist

1. **Define proposition-indexed physical finality under an explicit adversary
   class.** Separate safety, liveness, source truth, availability,
   accountability, and action safety. Do not allow a quorum certificate to
   stand for all six.
2. **Make membership and epoch identity physical.** Ask what selects the
   relevant participants, refresh boundary, and event identity in a physical
   record network. Dynamic membership is the shortest route to exposing
   hidden reference frames.
3. **Construct the smallest source-truth/quorum-safety split.** Hold the
   downstream certificate valid while changing whether the certified value
   was physically formed from the claimed source. Use it as the standing
   falsifier for “consensus creates the event.”

## 9. Epidemic and network-propagation specialist

1. **Study objectivity as topology-dependent record percolation.** Replace
   independent environment fragments with an explicit contact graph and
   calculate when accessible redundant evidence forms a giant component.
   Compare with ordinary Quantum Darwinism and correlated-noise absorbers.
2. **Replace copy count with effective independent support.** Measure
   correlation length, common-shock susceptibility, delivery probability,
   and accessible cut structure. Raw redundancy receives no finality credit.
3. **Make finality horizon-relative.** Calculate which distinctions can reach
   which observers before decay, intervention, or topology change. This can
   produce capability-relative regions without claiming a fundamental
   network ontology.

## 10. MMO networking and selective-replication specialist

1. **Formalize capability-relative views and handoff.** Give two agents
   different interest sets that are each sufficient for their current
   actions, then expand one action set and identify the minimum state transfer
   required for safe handoff.
2. **Use a game world as a calibrated remainder instrument.** Because source,
   collision, render, replication, and authority state are known, calculate
   exactly what each certified interaction history reconstructs and what
   remains. Transfer only the measurement method, not the ontology.
3. **Separate prediction, rollback, and binding commitment.** Model tentative
   local action, authoritative correction, and cross-shard irreversible
   effects as distinct finality levels. Then ask which distinctions survive
   when the central server is removed.

## 11. Distributed-database, provenance, serializability, and CRDT specialist

1. **Test global history as serializability.** Represent formed record
   transactions and ask whether all local histories admit a common
   serialization. Compare non-serializability with contextuality only after a
   faithful physical adapter is proved.
2. **Test a CALM-style monotonicity boundary.** Identify physical queries
   preserved under record extension and ask whether they compose without
   coordination. Absence, uniqueness, revocation, and exclusivity provide
   hostile nonmonotone controls.
3. **Make provenance query-specific.** Determine the minimal provenance
   semiring or causal annotation needed for each held-out intervention. This
   may yield a useful hierarchy without claiming one universal record schema.

## 12. DAG, hypergraph, and higher-process specialist

1. **Build physically formed overlap covers.** Let regional interfaces arise
   from one physical mechanism, then calculate compatibility on overlaps and
   whether a global section exists. Avoid choosing the cover to manufacture
   cohomology.
2. **Search for a refinement-invariant obstruction.** Require any
   reconciliation charge to survive relabeling, benign subdivision, and
   equivalent hyperedge presentations. Kill it if it depends on the diagram.
3. **Use higher-order interventions where pairwise graphs fail.** Test whether
   multi-party operations require hyperedges or process-level objects that
   cannot be reconstructed from pairwise data. The first exact pairwise
   insufficiency is the useful result.

## 13. Systems-security and adversarial-database specialist

1. **Create an implementation-completeness threat model for every flagship
   assay.** Enumerate hidden controller memory, rejected attempts, stale
   epochs, route state, side channels, rollback, and provenance substitution
   before crediting a residual.
2. **Generalize archive relocation adversarially.** Search for transformations
   that preserve the claimed antecedent and operational training surface
   while moving, forking, delaying, or corrupting the evidence needed by the
   held-out target.
3. **Develop proof-carrying physical records.** Bind every record claim to its
   physical formation, carrier, occurrence identity, access path, and
   resource receipt. Then test which bindings survive composition rather than
   merely verify syntax.

## 14. Zero-knowledge and commitment specialist

1. **Build one physical existence/certification/disclosure separation.** Use a
   formed record whose declared predicate can be verified without exposing
   the underlying history. Test action capability, not metaphysical
   actualization.
2. **Investigate physical commitments.** Ask whether quantum or relativistic
   constraints supply binding-without-current-disclosure structures, and
   state the exact no-go or conditional assumptions. Do not assume every
   remote region is an unopened classical value.
3. **Test whether public facts require witness disclosure.** Compare complete
   reconstruction, predicate certification, and safe action under identical
   resource accounting. A separation would sharpen DU's access and
   capability layers using known physics.

## 15. Homomorphic encryption and secure-computation specialist

1. **Formalize capability without plaintext access.** A coalition can compute
   a declared function of concealed inputs while no member can reconstruct
   them. Map the exact action gain and resource cost without calling it new
   physical information.
2. **Harden the joint-input no-minting theorem.** Prove the strongest scoped
   statement that post-processing cannot recover a target distinction absent
   from the complete joint admitted input, while preserving synergistic
   correlations as the positive control.
3. **Treat observer coalitions as changing decoders.** Determine when several
   partial observers jointly gain a capability unavailable to each
   individually, and whether that change is record refinement, resource
   enlargement, or a genuinely new physical interaction.

## 16. Threshold cryptography and distributed-key-generation specialist

1. **Model layered access thresholds independently from agreement
   thresholds.** Separate enough shares to decrypt, enough validators to act,
   enough evidence to audit, and enough observers to reach public knowledge.
2. **Use resharing and revocation as finality stress tests.** A fact may be
   actionable in one epoch and inaccessible in another without changing its
   underlying physical carrier. Track epoch binding and displaced
   information.
3. **Measure provenance loss under certificate compression.** Compare full
   signer/share histories with one threshold signature. Identify capabilities
   preserved by the compressed certificate and audits that require the
   discarded formation history.

# Part III — five additional high-value lenses

## 17. Open-quantum-systems and nonequilibrium-metastability theorist

1. **Derive an autonomous spectral finality filtration.** From a fixed
   generator, calculate invariant algebras, metastable manifolds, decay
   modes, escape rates, and perturbation stability. Ask whether these define
   nested future-response equivalence classes over expanding action sets.
2. **Test autonomous physical record formation.** Require distinguishable
   carrier support, a blank-to-written transition, retention over a declared
   horizon, and an accessible readout channel. Determine which features
   follow from dynamics and which remain initial-condition or partition
   choices.
3. **Separate dissipative criticality from mere timescale separation.** Use
   multiple sizes or a controlled thermodynamic limit, a selected order
   parameter, finite-size scaling, and standard phase-transition absorbers
   before claiming a universal finality transition.

## 18. Quantum causal-model and process-tensor theorist

1. **Represent the entire record-to-action loop as one multi-time process.**
   Include interventions, memory, routes, selection, and causal breaks.
   Terminal marginals or base-time transition families are inadequate.
2. **Locate interface formation as a higher-order map.** Ask whether the
   dynamics selects an instrument or supermap, merely permits a family, or
   requires an external tester. This directly targets the missing arrow.
3. **Construct the minimum reconciliation-loop assay.** Match complete process
   tensors across routes and then quotient ordinary operation order,
   geometric phase, and retained memory. Only the unmatched kernel reaches
   the holonomy hypothesis.

## 19. Category, type, and naturality theorist

1. **Prove a typed adapter or non-adapter theorem.** Define a functor from
   physical processes and formed records to a native distributed task
   category. Require it to preserve composition, access, adversary semantics,
   and resources.
2. **State the finality filtration by a universal property.** Avoid another
   coordinate checklist. Define the coarsest quotient sufficient for a
   declared action family at each horizon and study natural maps as actions
   or evidence expand.
3. **Use automorphisms to test selection.** If a physical antecedent is fixed
   while candidate interfaces move under a symmetry, no natural selector
   exists without added symmetry-breaking structure. Characterize the minimum
   admissible breaker.

## 20. Counterfactual statistician and metrologist

1. **Design the minimum dependency-changing intervention first.** For every
   mechanism, specify two completions equal on the proposed record and
   different on one held-out target, then identify the cheapest intervention
   that separates them.
2. **Require no-refit transfer.** Fit definitions, thresholds, resources, and
   equivalence in one arena; apply them unchanged to another. Treat failure
   to transport as information about the missing premise.
3. **Maintain acquisition visibility as a separate grade boundary.** Report
   which attempts, rejected strata, controller memories, and side channels
   are observed. More samples cannot repair a missing stratum.

## 21. Neural-network, representation-learning, and causal-state researcher

1. **Discover operational quotients from interventions, not correlations.**
   Train a model to compress histories while preserving held-out response
   distributions across an action family. Compare its latent partition with
   the exact causal-state or sufficient-statistic quotient.
2. **Search for hierarchical action-indexed representations.** Expand the
   available actions and test whether learned state partitions refine in a
   stable nested way. Use exact small models so the learned hierarchy can be
   audited rather than interpreted impressionistically.
3. **Use adversarial out-of-distribution histories to find omitted state.**
   Generate route, reset, delayed-access, and capability-expansion cases not
   seen in training. A failure identifies missing physical memory or a
   target-coded representation; success is a tool result, not an ontology
   result.

# Part IV — synthesis

## Convergence without pretending it was a vote

The recommendations cluster into five families:

1. **Autonomous physical selection and stratified finality.** The strongest
   convergence. Physics must select the stable sectors, timescales, and
   ideally the carrier/write/access/action structure.
2. **Typed physical-to-coordination translation.** Very strong convergence,
   but it needs a real physical source object before it can do more than police
   analogies.
3. **Complete-process reconciliation holonomy.** The largest blockbuster
   ceiling and the lowest present readiness.
4. **Provenance, implementation completeness, and adversarial relocation.**
   Essential controls across every path, but not a standalone North-Star
   successor.
5. **Capability without disclosure or individual access.** A useful,
   underdeveloped bridge among physical records, observer coalitions, and
   cryptographic proof structures; likely standard-physics/representation
   work before any new physics.

## Primary — autonomous stratified finality generator

### Exact candidate

For a fixed autonomous physical generator \(\mathcal L\), horizon family
\(\tau\), and increasing admitted action families \(\mathcal A_\tau\), define
the future-response relation

\[
x\sim_{\tau}x'
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{every }a\in\mathcal A_\tau
\text{ gives responses equal within the declared error at horizon }\tau.
\]

The live question is whether independently selected spectral, thermodynamic,
or dissipative structure determines a robust nested family

\[
X/{\sim_{\tau_1}}
\longleftarrow
X/{\sim_{\tau_2}}
\longleftarrow\cdots
\]

together with a physically formed and accessible carrier, without fitting the
partition, tolerance, observer, or target after seeing the result.

### Why it is new work rather than another host

- It begins from generator-selected slow/invariant structure, not a hand-made
  record variable.
- It tests a whole mechanism class before constructing a favored example.
- It makes future action equivalence and perturbation robustness
  load-bearing.
- It distinguishes selection of state sectors and timescales from selection
  of carrier, write, provenance, access, and action rights.
- It can return a useful impossibility or partial-selection theorem without
  claiming new physics.

### First bounded action

Run an unchanged mechanism passport over:

1. Liouvillian spectral metastability and invariant algebras;
2. thermodynamic symmetry breaking, nucleation, and durable memory; and
3. autonomous dissipative error correction or stabilization.

Admit a mechanism to construction only if primary sources and direct
mathematics show that it independently selects at least one previously
missing North-Star object. Otherwise return the common minimum missing
premise.

## Reserve — physical-to-coordination adapter theorem

Once the primary supplies a physical record/finality object, define one typed
adapter into native distributed tasks. Test:

- causality and event identity;
- access and knowledge;
- failure and adversary classes;
- monotonicity and revocation;
- membership and epoch;
- action safety and liveness; and
- resource and horizon accounting.

The desired result is either a faithful theorem-transfer boundary or the
first exact non-adapter. Do not begin from a protocol metaphor alone.

## Frontier — complete-process reconciliation holonomy

Preserve the moonshot, but do not activate it yet. Its reopener remains:

- an independently formed three-perspective connection;
- a complete process-tensor match;
- a gauge-invariant loop observable; and
- survival after operation order, standard quantum phase, route memory,
  recoupling, and representation changes are removed.

## Stopped

- another supplied record host;
- generic consensus or cryptographic analogy;
- another coordinate schema or minimal-field count;
- exactly compensated preferred-foliation work in DU;
- provider or hardware work before a local discriminator;
- proxy proliferation for \(\Lambda\), information distance, or record cost;
- paper promotion as a substitute for scientific selection.
