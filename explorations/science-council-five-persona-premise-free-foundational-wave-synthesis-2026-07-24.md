# Science Council five-persona premise-free foundational wave synthesis

## Executive decision

The fifth wave introduced no common physical premise. Each persona chose the
foundational move it judged most valuable after reconstructing all four prior
waves. The result changes the dependency state in two live programs.

First, the published Clock QW/QCA construction is an exact covariance
antecedent, but its clock variables are propagation/control phases rather than
an accumulated observer memory. A durable record is not waiting in the source
to be renamed. It requires an explicit recorder, readout, intervention
interface, and resource boundary.

Second, the post-conditioned CSG law has an exact representation-independent
predictive state: the projective transformed coupling sequence
`[T_1,T_2,...]=[M^P t]`. That object is sufficient for all future transition
probabilities inside the declared standard CSG model. It is not yet an
accessible observer record, an active return channel, a physical scale, or a
source reconstruction.

The shared correction is:

```text
update opportunity
    != record-forming event
    != retained operational capacity
    != stored accessible observer memory
    != inherited predictive law-state
    != interventionally owned observer/system record.
```

The immediate build is therefore no longer “find the record already inside
Clock QCA.” It is:

> Construct the smallest explicit source-compatible recorder extension, declare
> its physical interface and resource boundary, and test one covariance square
> under held-out encoding, reslicing, recorder-off, alias, archive, and reunion
> controls.

This is a bounded, buildable reopener. It does not derive proper time, a
dimensional speed, gravity, cosmology, `Lambda`, or Dynamic Unity. The causal
post recurrence/running/geometry program remains independently live. A bridge
between the two is forbidden until both produce compatible typed objects.

No claim is banked or prediction seeded. Another premise-setting council wave
is not the next action; the dependency state now supports direct construction.

## What is now exactly established

### 1. Clock-QCA covariance and observer memory are separate constructions

The source construction of Arrighi, Facchini, and Forets supplies exact
`1+1` discrete Lorentz covariance for its declared Clock QW/QCA. Its internal
countdown is decremented during idle propagation and reset when the effective
coin acts. In the QCA presentation, crossing control signals replace the
countdown. Neither published map supplies an accumulated, durable,
observer-accessible history register.

The encoding maps a source point into a patch. Raw microstep and patch counts
therefore change with representation. A macro circuit interaction trace can
descend through the declared encoding, but a stored record of that trace must
be appended and tested. This narrows the antecedent without rejecting the
globally stepped construction class.

### 2. Exact record capacity is experiment- and boundary-relative

For histories `H_t` and a declared family of physical experiments `U`, define

```text
h ~_U h'  iff every admitted future intervention/readout has the same response
C_t = log2 |H_t / ~_U|.
```

Then a finite deterministic classical state space of size `M` can expose no
more than `log2 M` retained bits. If every one of `T` independent binary
events must remain exactly recallable, at least `2^T` distinguishable states
are required. If only the elapsed count `0..T` is required, at least `T+1`
states suffice.

This is not a claim that every physical observer is finite and classical. It
is a reusable exact lower bound for that class. Continuous, quantum, noisy,
approximate, lossy, and thermodynamic models remain open and must declare
their own distinguishability criterion.

An unbounded lattice can evade a fixed-local-state bound through growing
support. That is a live escape, not a free one: the physical boundary,
propagation, archive ownership, accessible dimension, and reunion readout
must be charged.

### 3. Durability and copyability do not identify a record

Identity dynamics makes every predicate durable. For any chosen binary
predicate, a predicate-specific reversible classical copy map can also be
written. Yet the automorphism group of bare identity dynamics is the full
symmetric group, whose only invariant scalar predicates are constants.
Coordinate and parity predicates are conjugate.

A declared readout interface reduces the admissible group to its stabilizer;
the finite controls then recover exactly the Boolean algebra generated by
that readout. Alternatively, a record channel may transform equivariantly
with its codomain. Thus physical representation-independence means a declared
natural/equivariant relation under a justified group action. It does not mean
a preferred coordinate predicate fixed under every conceivable bijection.

### 4. A post carries predictive state without thereby storing observer memory

In standard originary CSG, a post of invariant past size `P` transforms the
couplings by

```text
T_n(P) = (M^P t)_n = sum_k binom(P,k) t_(n+k),    n >= 1.
```

The complete future nonempty-precursor transition algebra determines, and is
determined by, the projective sequence `[T_1,T_2,...]`. Singleton-origin
probabilities recursively recover every exposed ratio `T_n/T_1`.

The result also has two sharp limits:

- the geometric/transitive-percolation family stays on one projective fixed
  line across post depths, so raw `P` is not the predictive state; and
- for every finite horizon `r`, strictly positive couplings can make `P` and
  `P+1` agree on every transition through parent size `r` and first diverge at
  `r+1`.

So the quotient is exact only relative to the complete declared experiment
algebra. A bounded physical agent has, at most, a resource-bounded quotient or
filtration. Predictive sufficiency does not establish physical access, causal
use, recurrence, geometry, observer memory, units, or source identity.
