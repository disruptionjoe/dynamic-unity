# Science Council orthodox-professor successor wave

## Executive result

The enlarged corpus changes one major status assignment and exposes one
important identifiability error.

The first wave correctly killed direct birth-cardinality running
`p(N)`/`g(N)`. It did not thereby leave covariant history-to-coupling
transduction wholly unbuilt. Classical sequential growth (CSG) already has an
exact conditioning transformation at a post or break:

```text
(M^N t)_n = sum_{k=0}^N binom(N,k) t_(n+k),   n >= 1.
```

The map is projective, composes as a binomial semigroup, and leaves
transitive-percolation couplings `t_n=t^n` fixed up to common scale. For the
selected bare family `t_n=t^n/n!`, it has the known asymptotic
`t_eff=sqrt(t/N)` under explicit small-precursor conditions. The new probe
reproduces these identities and the finite asymptotic behavior with `13/13`
checks.

This **overturns “the post-RG antecedent is merely conjectural or unbuilt.”**
It does not derive CSG from Dynamic Unity, generate posts, select the bare
family, recover geometry, create units, or identify `Lambda`. The scientifically
live problem moves one level downstream: whether an admitted DU law
endogenously generates the post/break history and robust basin needed to use
the standard transformation.

The same probe supplies the required orthodox null for the intriguing
information-bottleneck route. On the four-event diamond, appending hidden
Bernoulli fibres leaves every physical down-set probability and
`F_downset=0.5793500751` unchanged, while the retained ratio
`F_downset/F_raw` falls from `0.67712725` to `0.0067712725`. The ratio is
coordinate invariant for a fixed raw model but is not identified by the
physical order-transition law. `WF-D12` therefore needs `PHIL-D2`'s
quotient-and-fibre work before it deserves a geometry test.

No claim is banked or seeded.

### 4.1 Exact post-map block

```text

M^a M^b              = M^(a+b)
M^N(t^n)             = (1+t)^N t^n
```

### 4.3 Fisher hidden-fibre null

Let `X` be the raw direct-link history and `Y` its physical causal down-set.
For the logit parameter:

```text
F_X = F_Y + E_Y[F_(X|Y)].
```

Append `k` independent Bernoulli variables controlled by the same `p`, but
forget them under the map to `Y`. Then:

```text
F_Y(k)   = F_Y(0)
F_X(k)   = (n+k) p(1-p)
F_Y/F_X  = (F_Y/F_X at k=0) * n/(n+k).
```

Enumeration through `k=4` leaves every physical outcome probability unchanged
to `2.78e-16` and physical Fisher unchanged to `9.99e-16`.

A coordinate change `h=sinh(g)` leaves the ratio invariant for each fixed raw
