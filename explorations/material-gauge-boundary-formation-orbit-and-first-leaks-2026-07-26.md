## 1. Exact finite `Z3` gauge region

Let

\[
\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}.
\]

There are two hard-core matter sites with number operators

\[
n_i=|1\rangle\langle1|,
\qquad
\Omega_i=\omega^{n_i}.
\]

The oriented graph is

```text
v1  -- internal link l -->  v2  -- boundary link b -->  outside.
```

Each link is a qutrit with electric basis \(\{|e\rangle:e\in\mathbb Z_3\}\).
Define the electric clock and holonomy shift by

\[
X|e\rangle=\omega^e|e\rangle,
\qquad
Z|e\rangle=|e+1\rangle.
\]

They obey

\[
XZX^\dagger=\omega Z.
\]

Use the local Gauss generators

\[
G_1=\Omega_1X_\ell,
\qquad
G_2=\Omega_2X_bX_\ell^\dagger.
\]

The physical subspace is

\[
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{phys}}
=
\{|\psi\rangle:G_1|\psi\rangle=G_2|\psi\rangle=|\psi\rangle\}.
\]

Multiplying the two constraints gives

\[
\Omega_1\Omega_2X_b=I.
\]

Define the outward flux observable

\[
\Phi_b=X_b^\dagger.
\]

On the physical subspace its eigenvalue is

\[
\Phi_b=\omega^{n_1+n_2}.
\]

Because \(n_1,n_2\in\{0,1\}\), the total

\[
F=n_1+n_2\in\{0,1,2\}
\]

has no modular alias in this arena. Gauss law therefore identifies the
boundary sector with total enclosed charge while leaving all three sector
values physically possible.

### Gauge-invariant regional Hamiltonian

Take

\[
\begin{aligned}
H_{\mathrm{reg}}
={}&m_1n_1+m_2n_2\\
&-J\left(\sigma_1^-Z_\ell\sigma_2^+
        +\sigma_1^+Z_\ell^\dagger\sigma_2^-\right)\\
&+h_\ell(X_\ell+X_\ell^\dagger)
 +h_b(X_b+X_b^\dagger).
\end{aligned}
\]

The internal hopping term is gauge invariant:

\[
\Omega_1\sigma_1^-\Omega_1^\dagger=\omega^{-1}\sigma_1^-,
\qquad
X_\ell Z_\ell X_\ell^\dagger=\omega Z_\ell,
\]

and at the second vertex,

\[
\Omega_2\sigma_2^+\Omega_2^\dagger=\omega\sigma_2^+,
\qquad
X_\ell^\dagger Z_\ell X_\ell=\omega^{-1}Z_\ell.
\]

Consequently,

\[
[H_{\mathrm{reg}},G_1]
=[H_{\mathrm{reg}},G_2]
=[H_{\mathrm{reg}},\Phi_b]=0.
\]

The Hamiltonian can redistribute one unit of charge between the two interior
sites, but it cannot change the total charge or the outward flux while the
boundary remains closed.

This is an exact target-independent physical host for the prior four-state
control. The flux record is not inserted as a function of the desired
interior target; it is fixed by the Gauss constraint and the chosen material
region.

   \(r(f+g)=r(f)+r(g)\).

\[
V\bigl(|\psi_f\rangle|0\rangle_P\bigr)
=
|\psi_f\rangle|r(f)\rangle_P.
\]

Perfect distinguishability makes \(r\) injective. Additivity makes it an
injective endomorphism of \(\mathbb Z_3\). Therefore

\[
r(f)=af,
\qquad
a\in\mathbb Z_3^\times=\{+1,-1\}.

The two representatives are

\[
W_+
=
\sum_f\Pi_f\otimes S_P^f,
\qquad

=
\sum_f\Pi_f\otimes S_P^{-f}.

## 4. Exact endpoint-to-Hamiltonian nonuniqueness

Even one exactly fixed final write does not determine how the detector
physically evolved during the write interval.

Let \(W\) denote one chosen oriented write and let \(H_0\) be a spectral
self-adjoint logarithm, scalar on each eigenspace of \(W\), satisfying

\[
e^{-i\tau H_0}=W.
\]

The controlled write has degenerate eigenspaces. Let \(Q\) be a nontrivial
projector inside one such eigenspace, chosen to be local to the boundary
link--pointer system and to commute with the Gauss generators. Then

\[
H_Q=H_0+\frac{2\pi}{\tau}Q
\]

also satisfies

\[
e^{-i\tau H_Q}=W.
\]

Within a degenerate eigenspace, equal-rank projectors \(Q\) can vary
continuously. The corresponding \(H_Q\) have the same final write, and
equal-rank choices have the same eigenvalue multiset, while their
intermediate-time evolutions differ.

An intermediate-time pointer operation that does not commute with \(Q\)
distinguishes two such detector histories. No observation confined to the
final flux instrument and archive can do so.

There are additional standard freedoms:

- different integer branches of the Hamiltonian logarithm;
- different unitaries away from the blank-pointer subspace;
- different inaccessible ancillary dilations; and
- different time-dependent control paths with the same endpoint.

A principal logarithm, shortest-control path, restricted coupling ansatz, or
minimum-micromotion rule could choose one representative. Each would be a new
dynamical premise. The final record does not derive it.

This establishes an exact history-versus-endpoint boundary:

> A selected final instrument need not select the microscopic formation
> history that realized it.

## 5. Robustness is horizon-indexed

Let \(U_0(\tau)\) be the ideal write and let the actual Hamiltonian be

\[
H(t)=H_0(t)+V(t).
\]

Set

\[
\delta
=
\int_0^\tau\|V(t)\|\,dt.
\]

The Duhamel bound gives

\[
\|U(\tau)-U_0(\tau)\|\le\delta.
\]

For a flux-\(f\) input and blank pointer, the ideal final state lies entirely
in the correct record subspace. Hence

\[
P(\text{wrong pointer record}\mid f)
\le
\min(1,\delta^2).
\]

This is a finite-interval stability statement, not permanent finality.

### Archive storage

Let \(R_f\) be the archive record projectors. Split a storage perturbation
into:

\[
V=V_\parallel+V_\perp,
\]

where \(V_\parallel\) is block preserving,

\[
[V_\parallel,R_f]=0,
\]

and \(V_\perp\) contains the cross-record blocks. If

\[
\delta_A(T)
=
\int_0^T\|V_\perp(t)\|\,dt,
\]

then the probability of leaving the initially correct archive block obeys

\[
P_{\mathrm{archive\ error}}(T)
\le
\min(1,\delta_A(T)^2).
\]

When \(V_\perp=0\), the archive value is exact for the whole declared
projector-preserving envelope, even if phases and within-record dynamics
continue. When \(\delta_A(T)>0\), only the declared horizon \(T\) is earned.

### Resource-charged redundancy

For odd \(N\) independently corrupted archive cells, each with error
probability \(p<1/2\), majority decoding gives the Hoeffding bound

\[
P_{\mathrm{majority\ failure}}
\le
\exp\!\left[-2N\left(\frac12-p\right)^2\right].
\]

This requires:

- \(N\) blank cells;
- \(N\) controlled copies of an orthogonal record label;
- an independently implemented decoder;
- an error model making the cell failures independent; and
- a frozen finite horizon.

Correlated or coherent adversarial errors do not inherit the exponential
bound. Redundancy can improve reliability under a declared model; it does
not convert a finite archive into an unconditional final fact.

\[

=
