# Science Council repository-wide advancement wave

## Direction

Run one independent bounded research swing for each of:

1. orthodox professor;
2. heterodox professor;
3. commercial scientist;
4. wild-frontier scientist; and
5. philosopher of science.

This is a repository-wide wave. `HC-DU-035A` and the ten threshold lenses are
new evidence available to the council, not a mandated topic. Each persona must
inspect Dynamic Unity's complete current framing and choose the attempt it
judges most worthy.

The common objective is:

> Find the most profound, useful, novel, publishable, and defensibly
> advanceable opportunity visible anywhere in the current Dynamic Unity
> program, then do the largest honest piece of that work that fits in one
> bounded swing.

Persona agreement is not evidence. Divergence is preserved before synthesis.

## Common repository evidence

Every persona begins from:

- `AGENTS.md`;
- `README.md`;
- `LANES.yaml`;
- `docs/certified-causal-reality-research-program.md`;
- `explorations/concept-register.md`;
- `explorations/prediction-register.md`;
- the major prior Science Council syntheses and wish lists;
- the exact record, recorder, archive, causal-growth, clock, recursive,
  influence, and conditional-physics explorations and probes relevant to its
  candidate;
- `HC-DU-033A` as the finite classical record/access quotient baseline; and
- `HC-DU-035A` as the layered reconstruction/finality/capability control.

The current Certified Causal Reality theorem spine is a map, not a mandatory
sequence:

- physical record formation and interface selection (`HC-DU-033`);
- multi-event or multi-time certification (`HC-DU-034`);
- formation-to-finality cost (`HC-DU-035B`);
- factorization or finite separating witness (`HC-DU-036`);
- finality, capability, and coherent optionality (`HC-DU-037`);
- meta-record geometry (`HC-DU-038`);
- cross-platform invariance (`HC-DU-039`); and
- physical recovery, GU, Standard Model, gravity, and cosmology
  (`HC-DU-040`).

Lane 2 conditional constructions, surviving causal-growth and record-clock
branches, physics-first remainders, and negative/no-go results remain eligible.
No candidate is excluded merely because it is not next in the current
dependency order. Its assumptions and dependency consequences must instead be
made explicit.

## Independent selection rule

Before choosing, each persona must briefly compare at least five materially
different candidate attempts drawn from the repository as a whole. Comparison
uses five non-ordinal considerations:

1. potential profundity;
2. scientific usefulness;
3. novelty potential after collision;
4. publishable result shape; and
5. probability of a defensible one-swing advance.

No scalar score is required. Each persona states why its winner dominates for
that lens and which attractive alternative it deliberately leaves alone.

## Execution rule

The persona must do research, not only propose it. A valid swing produces at
least one of:

- a scoped theorem and proof;
- an exact counterexample or no-go;
- an executable finite construction or discriminating witness;
- a source-grounded refutation or narrowing;
- a conditional model with a held-out discriminator; or
- a claim-specific primary-source collision result that materially changes
  the candidate.

Every result declares:

- the frozen object, process, intervention, and access contract;
- imported assumptions and free choices;
- rival or adversary class;
- resource boundary;
- result and warrant type;
- finite falsifier or kill condition;
- novelty status; and
- exact failure scope.

The repository's record ladder and Certified Causal Reality stops apply. A
persona may reject the current framing or find a physics-first remainder, but
it may not promote analogy, finite fit, or council agreement into ontology.

## Required persona output

Each memo contains:

1. repository-wide candidate comparison;
2. chosen attempt and selection rationale;
3. concrete advancement;
4. exact result and honest grade;
5. failed, killed, or low-value paths;
6. ranked new hypotheses, theorems, or conjectures;
7. next decisive experiment, proof obligation, or collision search;
8. a short unconstrained wish list while the context is live; and
9. a plain-English interpretation.

Dedicated outputs:

- `explorations/science-council-orthodox-repository-wide-advancement-wave-2026-07-24.md`;
- `explorations/science-council-heterodox-repository-wide-advancement-wave-2026-07-24.md`;
- `explorations/science-council-commercial-repository-wide-advancement-wave-2026-07-24.md`;
- `explorations/science-council-wild-frontier-repository-wide-advancement-wave-2026-07-24.md`; and
- `explorations/science-council-philosopher-repository-wide-advancement-wave-2026-07-24.md`.

Optional small probes and artifacts use persona-specific
`tests/du_council_<persona>_*` and
`tests/artifacts/du_council_<persona>_*` prefixes.

## Root synthesis rule

The root pass must:

1. reproduce every exact or executable result;
2. separate complementary results from contradictory ones;
3. reconcile claims by warrant rather than vote;
4. check whether any candidate merely restates known mathematics;
5. identify what changes the theorem spine, live hypotheses, and next action;
6. preserve the divergent wish lists;
7. update canonical state only where the evidence warrants it; and
8. explain the collective meaning in plain English.

No claim is banked, seeded, submitted, or published merely because this wave
completes.
