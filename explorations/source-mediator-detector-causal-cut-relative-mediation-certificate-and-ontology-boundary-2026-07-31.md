# Source–mediator–detector causal-cut boundary

## Result

`HC-DU-196` proved that complete detector-only tomography identifies some
hidden-resource invariants but not the physical mediator. This swing asks the
exact successor:

> What is the smallest expanded interface that can establish mediation, and
> what does it still fail to establish?

The answer separates three levels that the phrase “mediator-facing record” had
left too close together.

1. **One named midpoint or one reset arm is not a mediation certificate.** A
   direct source-to-detector dependence can agree with the serially mediated
   model both under natural operation and under one complete replacement of
   the candidate mediator.
2. **An interventionally complete port can certify a causal bottleneck within
   a declared model class.** In the finite binary arena, natural operation plus
   both fixed mediator replacements reconstructs the full response
   (f(S,M)). It decides whether detector response depends directly on the
   source at fixed mediator value.
3. **A causal-bottleneck certificate does not identify physical ontology.** A
   gravitationally named port and a nongravitational quantum ancilla can have
   the same complete intervention table or quantum process. The port’s
   physical name still requires independently formed lineage, a selected
   coupling, or a theory-level result making that property constant on the
   complete operational fibre.

The strongest earned statement is:

> An informationally complete intervention surface can reconstruct the
> operational causal role of a port. It cannot, by itself, reconstruct a
> physical property that varies among operationally equivalent realizations
> of that port.

This is a scoped Grade-4 causal-identification and factorization boundary. Its
causal mathematics is absorbed by interventions, process tensors, and quantum
causal models. Dynamic Unity’s increment is the explicit ladder from an added
port to a physical provenance-bearing record. No selected gravitational
interface, Grade-5 remainder, observation, hardware action, new causal
theorem, or active successor is earned.

## The smallest exact counterexample

Let (S,M,Din{0,1}) denote source, candidate mediator port, and detector.
Natural evolution writes

\[
M=S.
\]

The serially mediated candidate is

\[
D=M.
\tag{1}
\]

Compare the direct-edge rival

\[
D=S\wedge M.
\tag{2}
\]

The rival genuinely depends on (S) at fixed (M=1). Yet the candidate and
rival agree in both of these arms:

| arm | candidate (D) for (S=0,1) | rival (D) for (S=0,1) |
|---|---|---|
| natural, (M=S) | (0,1) | (0,1) |
| replace (M\leftarrow0) | (0,0) | (0,0) |

They separate only when the port is also prepared at the other value:

| arm | candidate (D) for (S=0,1) | rival (D) for (S=0,1) |
|---|---|---|
| replace (M\leftarrow1) | (1,1) | (0,1) |

Therefore a source-deletion or reset-to-blank arm is a valuable control but
not a sufficient mediation certificate. A direct path can be dormant on that
one slice.

This counterexample does not exploit intervention leakage or a conspiratorial
controller. The same fixed response function is used in every arm.

## Exhaustive finite identification theorem

There are exactly 16 deterministic Boolean responses

\[
f:\{0,1\}_S\times\{0,1\}_M\to\{0,1\}_D.
\]

Against the reference (f(S,M)=M), exhaustive enumeration returns:

| acquired surface | compatible responses |
|---|---:|
| natural (M=S) only | 4 |
| natural plus replace (M\leftarrow0) | 2 |
| natural plus replace (M\leftarrow1) | 2 |
| natural plus both replacements | 1 |

### Theorem

In this frozen deterministic split-node class, source-conditioned detector
responses under both values of a surgical mediator replacement reconstruct
the complete truth table (f(S,M)). They are therefore necessary and
sufficient to decide whether

\[
\exists m:\ f(0,m)\neq f(1,m),
\tag{3}
\]

which is the declared direct (S\to D) functional-dependence target.

### Proof

The two source settings and two mediator replacements evaluate all four
entries of the truth table. Equality of all four entries is equality of the
response function. Condition (3) is then decidable directly. Necessity in the
frozen arm family follows from the enumeration: omitting either replacement
leaves two functions, one with the reference bottleneck behavior and one with
a direct source dependence, that agree on every retained arm. \(\square\)

This theorem certifies only the declared functional target under surgical
interventions. It assumes:

- the replacement sets the complete typed port, not one visible coordinate;
- the source setting and replacement choice are independently randomized and
  joined to the detector output;
- the intervention command does not leak through another route;
- the response mechanism is held fixed across arms;
- the candidate causal graph and system boundaries are declared before the
  results; and
- selection, loss, and accepted-attempt rules do not change by arm.

Without these conditions, “the response vanished when we reset it” need not
mean the reset port was the complete mediator.

## Quantum lift

For a quantum port, two state replacements do not generally span the allowed
operation space. Replace the Boolean truth table with a process tensor or
quantum comb (Upsilon), and replace the two bit assignments by an
informationally complete family of completely positive interventions

\[
\{\mathcal A_j\}_j
\]

spanning the admitted port-operation space. Each observed probability is a
linear contraction of (Upsilon) with the chosen instruments. Equality on a
spanning intervention family fixes the operational process within that typed
domain; a quantum Markov or causal-model factorization can then test whether
the port is a complete operational bottleneck.

This is standard terrain:

- Pollock et al. construct experimentally accessible multi-time process
  tensors and show how arbitrary non-Markovian processes are reconstructed:
  [arXiv:1512.00589](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00589).
- Their operational Markov condition gives a necessary-and-sufficient process
  criterion rather than relying on one reset arm:
  [arXiv:1801.09811](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.09811).
- Barrett, Lorenz, and Oreshkov connect unitary circuits, DAGs, quantum Markov
  conditions, and intervention rules:
  [arXiv:1906.10726](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.10726).
- Allen et al. show how a complete quantum common-cause claim imposes a
  specific channel factorization:
  [arXiv:1609.09487](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.09487).

Dynamic Unity should therefore not claim a new causal theorem here. The
useful result is the precise acquisition and attribution boundary applied to
the gravitational-detector branch.

## Why complete causal mediation still does not name gravity

Let the expanded operational record be

\[
R_{SMD}(c)
=
\text{the complete joined source/port/detector process in completion }c.
\]

Let

\[
G(c)\in\{0,1\}
\]

mean that the port is specifically a gravitational field degree rather than
an operationally identical quantum ancilla or direct relational realization.
Gravitational attribution exists exactly when

\[
\ker R_{SMD}\subseteq\ker G.
\tag{4}

\]

Complete causal tomography does not guarantee (4). Two candidates can expose
the same input, intervention, and output ports and implement the same process
while assigning different physical realizations to the internal carrier.
Renaming the port is not the scientific issue; realization-equivalent
Hamiltonians, dilations, or effective source actions are.

The probe retains an exact pair:

```text
same complete natural-and-replacement record
+ same binary port dimension
+ same causal-bottleneck role
+ different physical implementation label
```

This does not make the causal result empty. It fixes the strongest justified
claim:

> The selected operational port is a complete causal bottleneck relative to
> the frozen intervention class.

It blocks the stronger cast:

> The port is therefore the gravitational field as a uniquely identified
> ontology.

## The corrected interface ladder

The gravitational detector branch should now use this ladder:

```text
detector endpoint
  -> complete detector process
  -> added named port
  -> one causal-break arm
  -> interventionally complete port process
  -> relative causal-bottleneck certificate
  -> independently selected physical port and lineage
  -> named-component attribution over a frozen rival class
```

Each arrow adds information or assumptions. None can be skipped by changing
terminology.

In particular:

- a second detector can reveal shared memory while leaving the carrier
  unidentified;
- a midpoint value can distinguish one direct implementation while leaving
  other port-equivalent realizations;
- a single reset can miss a dormant direct path;
- complete port interventions can identify an operational causal graph;
- only independent physical selection/provenance can attach the graph node to
  a named field or material carrier; and
- even a selected port proves only the claim indexed to the declared rival
  class and acquisition contract.
