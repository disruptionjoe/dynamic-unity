# Science Council: ten alternative formulations of the strengthened substrate hypothesis

## Chairman's decision

The naive game-engine hypothesis remains dead as a physical proposal. The
strengthened family is not dead, but it divides sharply:

- the identity claim, “physics is a computationally closed process with no
  separate implementer,” is coherent and is not refuted by the absence of
  shortcuts;
- that identity claim has no empirical excess by itself;
- eight descendants below state a difference from the relevant standard
  quantum-and-gravitational baseline and therefore have conditional excess;
- none of those eight currently supplies both a frozen quantitative law and
  an earned residual that survives the complete standard physical
  completion class;
- two descendants relocate the architecture into cognition or embodied
  control and have no substrate-level excess.

The clean current result is therefore:

> **H\* is not an earned new theory. It is a branching hypothesis family.
> Its safe core is empirically empty; its contentful descendants become
> ordinary, strongly constrained new-physics conjectures.**

This is not a vote against exploring them. It is the Lakatos boundary needed
to stop null results from being absorbed by an indefinitely smarter
substrate.

No theorem, hypothesis, prediction, ontology, paper, or lane priority is
promoted by this council.

## Scope and corrections to the supplied brief

The brief's three “theorems against the data model” are useful pressures but
are too broad as written.

| Supplied wording | Correct scope |
|---|---|
| Kochen–Specker proves “no world-state object.” | Kochen–Specker excludes a global **noncontextual** assignment of values to all quantum observables that preserves the required functional relations. It does not exclude a universal wavefunction, a Bohmian configuration, or every contextual global ontology. |
| Bell proves “no spatial data layout.” | Bell excludes local-causal hidden-variable factorizations under the theorem's assumptions. It does not show that quantum field theory lacks spatial localization, local algebras, causal propagation, or every possible spatial data structure. It does show that no ordinary local lookup table reproduces all Bell correlations. |
| The sign problem proves “no classical bounded substrate approximates quantum many-body dynamics.” | Troyer and Wiese prove NP-hardness of a **generic solution to the fermionic quantum Monte Carlo sign problem**. This is a worst-case/generic shortcut obstruction, not a theorem against every classical approximation, tensor-network method, special Hamiltonian, spatial decomposition, or substrate. |

These corrections weaken the claimed universal refutation without restoring
the engine. A contextual, nonlocal, exponentially costly or quantum
substrate has surrendered the architectural advantages that made the engine
analogy explanatory.

The preferred-foliation correction also stands. A hidden foliation can be
empirically equivalent to a Lorentz-covariant presentation. Discreteness and
preferred foliation are separate axes; causal-set Poisson sprinkling is the
standing counterexample to “discrete implies preferred frame.”

Primary anchors:

## The Lakatos contract

Let \(B\) be the relevant, fully specified standard operational baseline:
ordinary quantum theory or QFT in its admitted regime, GR or the declared
semiclassical limit, the complete apparatus and environment model, and all
known noise, memory, selection, and calibration channels.

A formulation has non-zero excess only if it does at least one of the
following:

1. predicts
   \[
   P_H(o\mid I)\neq P_B(o\mid I)
   \]
   for a predeclared intervention \(I\);
2. forbids a process admitted by \(B\); or
3. imposes a cross-arena relation among observables that \(B\) leaves
   independent, and transfers that relation without refitting.

A hidden object that never changes the operational likelihood is
unidentifiable. A representation of \(B\) is Grade 2 at most when the map is
faithful, not Grade 5. A precise but untested conjecture is not upgraded
merely because its sentence is mathematical.

Each formulation below pre-registers its kill and retreat before the
council's assessment.

## F1 — Dependency-invalidation dynamics

1. **Formulation.**

   > On each preferred leaf, physical evolution reuses unchanged dependency
   > sectors and recomputes only sectors reached by an observer-independent
   > invalidation relation, producing an outcome residual that depends on the
   > size or topology of the invalidated dependency frontier.

2. **Lens and missing object.** The Engine Architect. H\* retained a budget
   and a foliation but removed the engine architect's most diagnostic object:
   the rule that decides what must be recomputed after a change. This
   formulation restores cache invalidation without restoring a spatial
   broadphase, noncontextual values, or observer-triggered rendering. It
   relaxes H\*(i), because reuse is a shortcut.

3. **Excess content.** **NON-ZERO, CONDITIONALLY.** After the complete
   standard process has predicted an outcome distribution, a residual
   dependence on a separately defined invalidation-frontier statistic is not
   predicted by QM + GR.

4. **Novel risky prediction.** Choose intervention networks with the same
   frozen standard outcome law, Hamiltonian, energy, geometry, bath, and
   acquisition contract but different dependency-frontier sizes \(D\).
   Measure whether the calibrated residual
   \[
   \epsilon(D)=d\!\left(P_{\rm observed},P_B\right)
   \]
   has a reproducible nonzero onset, hysteresis, or scaling with \(D\).
   This must be an outcome/noise statistic, not the wall-clock latency of the
   laboratory controller.

5. **Kill condition.** Kill this formulation if every apparent
   \(D\)-dependence factors through the complete standard process, omitted
   environment memory, control latency, heating, acquisition selection, or
   ordinary causal propagation across all predeclared hostile networks.

6. **Retreat cost.** The formulation must surrender physical cache
   invalidation and return to uniform irreducible evolution. “The substrate
   invalidates perfectly” is not a defense; it is H\* with no excess.

**Council assessment.** Open but uninstantiated. No invariant physical
definition of \(D\) or coefficient is supplied. Current evidentiary status:
`UNRANKED / UNCOMMITTED / NOT_A_CLAIM`; maximum possible grade 5.

## F2 — Relational reconciliation curvature

1. **Formulation.**

   > Certified transformations among observer-relative record networks form
   > a physical connection whose closed reconciliation loops can have a
   > gauge-invariant holonomy that changes joint outcome probabilities beyond
   > ordinary quantum-channel composition, reference-frame gauge, and
   > geometric phase.

2. **Lens and missing object.** The Relational Quantum Foundationalist.
   Pairwise facts alone do not say whether pairwise transformations compose
   globally. H\* misses the curvature of that composition. This formulation
   is compatible with no preferred global state, but it can also be stated
   operationally in a preferred-foliation completion.

3. **Excess content.** **NON-ZERO.** Rovelli-style relational indexing by
   itself is an interpretation of quantum theory. A residual loop holonomy
   after the total standard process is fixed would be new.

4. **Novel risky prediction.** In a coherent three-agent network, construct
   two echo-compensated reconciliation loops with the same local reduced
   processes, final pairwise record overlaps, complete standard process
   tensor, energy, and known phases. Measure a nonzero difference in a final
   joint interference or channel observable that changes with loop
   orientation.

5. **Kill condition.** Kill this formalization if every loop effect is
   absorbed by noncommuting operation order, omitted route state, ordinary
   unitary or geometric phase, decoherence, communication, or the first
   noncentral fibre recoupling.

6. **Retreat cost.** Drop physical curvature. The remaining statement is
   relational quantum mechanics, quantum reference frames, or ordinary
   path-dependent channel composition—a representation, not DU excess.

**Council assessment.** This is the highest-novelty descendant, but
`NI-DU-45` already warns that ordinary public-kernel order or omitted
provenance is the first absorber. Current status:
`UNRANKED / UNCOMMITTED / NOT_A_CLAIM`; maximum possible grade 5.

## F3 — Scheme-independent cross-scale descent

1. **Formulation.**

   > There exists one dynamically selected, scheme-independent descent law
   > from microscopic quantum processes to scale-indexed record,
   > capability, and regional-finality algebras, and its critical scaling
   > transfers across physically different arenas without refitting.

2. **Lens and missing object.** The Emergence Physicist. H\* prohibits
   authored level-of-detail but does not explain why mutually consistent
   effective levels exist. Renormalization already supplies derived
   coarse-graining; the possible excess is a unique descent law tying record
   formation and capability to it.

3. **Excess content.** **NON-ZERO ONLY THROUGH NO-REFIT TRANSFER.** “EFT is
   level-of-detail” is a metaphorical relabel. A single selected scaling
   function or exponent family that standard EFT leaves platform-dependent
   would have excess.

4. **Novel risky prediction.** Freeze a finality order parameter, scaling
   variable, exponent, and finite-size correction on one platform. Without
   changing them, predict the onset and scaling curve of independently
   recoverable actionable records on at least two different quantum-network
   or many-body platforms.

5. **Kill condition.** Kill the universal descent if the transfer requires a
   new coarse-graining map, order parameter, exponent, record definition, or
   fitted coefficient in each arena, or if standard RG derives the same
   relation.

6. **Retreat cost.** Demote to platform-specific EFT, decoherence, or
   open-system phase-transition work. The general idea of emergence
   survives, but the substrate formulation loses its excess.

**Council assessment.** A legitimate cross-arena conjecture, not a current
prediction. The selection of the descent law is the load-bearing missing
piece. Status: `UNRANKED / UNCOMMITTED / NOT_A_CLAIM`; maximum possible
grade 5.

## F4 — Logical-depth execution leakage

1. **Formulation.**

   > At fixed standard physical resources and complete standard dynamics,
   > the minimum logical depth of a realized process contributes an
   > additional, nonzero error or deformation term to its observed outcome
   > law.

2. **Lens and missing object.** The Glitch-Hunter. H\* says the process is
   resource-bounded and irreducible but gives no failure surface. This
   formulation turns the budget into an adversarial signature: the deepest
   allowed processes are the out-of-bounds cases.

3. **Excess content.** **NON-ZERO.** Ordinary quantum theory does not make
   Born-rule accuracy degrade because a state or process is hard for another
   computer to produce or simulate. Standard hardware noise can correlate
   with circuit depth, so it is the strongest absorber.

4. **Novel risky prediction.** Use predeclared circuit/process families with
   lower-bounded depth under a frozen gate set. After predicting and
   measuring ordinary control error, decoherence, leakage, energy, spacetime
   volume, and acquisition selection, test for a provider- and
   platform-independent residual that turns on with certified logical depth.
   The hostile order is:
   (a) coherent deep circuits with exact controls;
   (b) strongly correlated many-body processes;
   (c) high-multiplicity scattering;
   (d) only then, inaccessible Planck or black-hole regimes.

5. **Kill condition.** Kill the declared scaling law if the residual remains
   zero within the predeclared envelope or all depth dependence is
   hardware-, noise-, state-, or EFT-accounted.

6. **Retreat cost.** Either remove measurable budget leakage, permit an
   equally powerful or hypercomputational substrate, or allow arbitrary
   external time. Each retreat returns to an empirically empty identity
   claim.

**Council assessment.** This is a real anomaly-search program only after a
representation-invariant complexity measure and numerical scaling law are
frozen. Lorentz-violation and lattice searches constrain some possible leak
channels but do not test this whole family. Status:
`UNRANKED / UNCOMMITTED / NOT_A_CLAIM`; maximum possible grade 5.

## F5 — Physical Church–Turing–Deutsch closure

1. **Formulation.**

   > Every finitely realizable physical process is simulable to arbitrary
   > operational accuracy by a universal quantum computer operating by
   > finite means, and no physically realizable process computes a
   > nonrecursive function.

2. **Lens and missing object.** The Complexity Theorist. “The substrate is
   physics” need not be a reductio; it becomes contentful when it is a
   closure law rather than a metaphor. This separates computational identity
   from a discounted external implementation.

3. **Excess content.** **NON-ZERO AS A PROHIBITION.** QM + GR do not by
   themselves settle every physical Church–Turing thesis. Idealized
   Malament–Hogarth spacetime constructions are a known GR-side challenge.

4. **Novel risky prediction.** No finite physical protocol—including
   relativistic, gravitational, analog, nonlinear, or continuum protocols—
   yields a reproducible operational solution to a nonrecursive problem or
   exposes an exact oracle with unbounded information in a finite
   realizable region.

5. **Kill condition.** One independently reproducible physical
   hypercomputation, exact continuum oracle, or other finite experimental
   process computing a nonrecursive function kills the strong closure law.

6. **Retreat cost.** “Physics can be described computationally” remains, but
   that statement is a relabel shared by any computably presented theory.
   Allowing unspecified hypercomputation makes the resource-budget argument
   non-falsifiable.

**Council assessment.** The claim has logical excess but poor positive
confirmability: every finite successful test is itself compatible with an
ordinary computation. It can be falsified more cleanly than confirmed.
Deutsch's principle and the Malament–Hogarth literature are the immediate
collision set. Status: `UNRANKED / UNCOMMITTED / NOT_A_CLAIM`; maximum grade
5, presently no earned DU grade.

## F6 — Total certified-record refinement

1. **Formulation.**

   > For every frozen observer, admitted intervention, and capability
   > envelope, the observer-accessible operational process factors naturally
   > through an independently formed certified-record quotient, and those
   > factorizations commute under benign record refinement and regional
   > composition.

2. **Lens and missing object.** The Type Theorist. The render/collision
   question is not whether two representations resemble one another; it is
   whether a total, coherent abstraction/refinement map exists. H\* says
   capability is pairwise but does not require all accessible physics to
   descend through records.

3. **Excess content.** **NON-ZERO.** Standard physics predicts outcomes from
   its physical state/process. It does not generally prove that an
   independently formed strict record compression is sufficient for every
   observer action. This is Dynamic Unity's North-Star positive branch.

4. **Novel risky prediction.** In an implementation-complete experiment with
   all attempts, selection strata, admitted memories, resets, records, and
   held-out interventions observed, no two complete histories with the same
   certified record yield different observer-accessible action
   distributions.

5. **Kill condition.** One nonempty, implementation-complete
   same-record/different-action fibre under the frozen completion and action
   class kills the universal formulation.

6. **Retreat cost.** Every repair must be charged. Narrowing actions or
   completions makes the result class-relative; adding the separating record
   enlarges the interface; injective full-history tomography gives up
   compression. Redefining “record” as all target experiments makes the
   claim tautological.

**Council assessment.** This is the most directly useful formulation for
Dynamic Unity. Existing finite theorems establish scoped positive and
negative controls, not the universal physical statement. Status:
`UNRANKED / UNCOMMITTED / NOT_A_CLAIM`; maximum possible grade 5.

## F7 — Enactive capability closure

1. **Formulation.**

   > Every capability of an embodied observer is an invariant of its closed
   system–environment sensorimotor dynamics, with no independently
   efficacious access representation or metalevel variable outside that
   ordinary physical loop.

2. **Lens and missing object.** The Enactivist. H\* separates systems and
   capability relations before explaining the system–world loop that
   constitutes the agent. This formulation removes the navmesh as a
   representation and treats affordance as closed-loop dynamics.

3. **Excess content.** **NOTHING AT THE SUBSTRATE LEVEL.** When “closed loop”

   interpretation of QM + GR, not an alternative physical substrate.

4. **Novel risky prediction.** In its proper cognitive domain, hold the

5. **Kill condition.** Kill the strong enactive claim if an independently

6. **Retreat cost.** Admit causally efficacious internal models or mediating
   states. The weaker embodied claim may remain useful, but it supplies no
   substrate physics.

**Council assessment.** Relocation, not excess. Grade 0 for the substrate
question; a separate cognitive program could grade it on its own evidence.

## F9 — Layered regional certificate-gated dynamics

1. **Formulation.**

   > Physical transition permissions are indexed by a hierarchy
   > \(C_\ell(A,B,R)\) of pairwise, region-, scale-, observer-, and
   > capability-relative certificates, and crossing a higher-layer
   > certification threshold can change the lower-layer permitted transition
   > algebra even after the complete standard quantum state, spacetime
   > fields, controls, and process memory have been matched.

2. **Lens and missing object.** The Capability & Access Theorist. H\*(iii)
   has pairwise relations but flattens their levels. This formulation admits
   fast provenance-based local action, metastable regional confidence,
   harder Byzantine-style binding, threshold or zero-knowledge access, and
   a possible higher layer that edits lower affordances without lower-system
   participation. Different regions need not share one consensus or finality
   rule.

3. **Excess content.** **NON-ZERO.** Cryptographic verification, gossip,
   quorum safety, and metastable consensus normally change knowledge,
   authority, or safe action while remaining ordinary physical processes.
   Excess begins only when certificate status itself changes a physical
   transition probability after the complete standard process is fixed.

4. **Novel risky prediction.** Prepare two histories that standard dynamics
   returns to the same complete operational state and process memory. Let one
   history cross a predeclared regional certification threshold and the
   other not. The formulation predicts a later difference in an interaction
   cross-section, interference channel, force, or other permitted
   transition, possibly with scale-specific hysteresis as regional
   certificates harden.

   A cosmological hidden-shard version has content only if it predicts
   discrete or topology/history-dependent gravitational or lensing effects
   that cannot be represented by a local covariantly conserved
   stress-energy source. “Nonluminous matter exists elsewhere” alone is
   already a hidden-sector or dark-matter relabel.

5. **Kill condition.** Kill the extra physical gate if every certificate
   effect factors through ordinary stored records, fields, mediators,
   Hamiltonians, environments, conserved stress-energy, access control, or
   process memory. Kill the cosmological branch if it supplies no frozen
   lensing/dynamics relation beyond an ordinary dark sector.

6. **Retreat cost.** Certificates return to epistemic, cryptographic, and
   action-safety objects. Layered finality remains useful as a representation
   of observer access, but it is not a new physical force or source. A
   higher-level editor without an observable effect is an inaccessible
   metalevel and has no empirical content.

**Council assessment.** This is the strongest formulation of the user's
regional/sharded possibility. It also bears the heaviest type burden.
`HC-DU-047` through `HC-DU-051` already show that pooling, provenance,
hardening, selective views, and physical amplification do not mint source
truth or distinctions absent from upstream physical records. The new-physics
remainder is still open but wholly unearned. Status:
`UNRANKED / UNCOMMITTED / NOT_A_CLAIM`; maximum possible grade 5.

## Productive collisions

### F2 versus F3 — fundamental relation or emergent descent?

F2 makes relational composition fundamental and asks for curvature. F3
treats layered relational structure as a derived cross-scale description.
They separate only if a loop residue survives after standard coarse-graining
and process composition are fixed. Without that residue, F2 is interpretation
and F3 is ordinary RG.

### F7 versus F8 — direct affordance or internal model?

This is a cognitive-science dispute. It can explain why the engine metaphor
feels natural, but neither side supplies excess substrate physics. Dynamic
Unity should use it to type observer access, not repair a physical leg with
capability language.

### F4 versus the Historian's F10 — fuzzing or motivated anomaly hunting?

F4 has a physical scaling variable only after logical depth is frozen
independently. F10 has one only after certified-record load is frozen
physically rather than semantically. Without those preregistrations, both
amount to searching for unexplained residuals and renaming whatever appears.

### F6 versus F5 — total refinement or computational closure?

F5 constrains what physical processes can compute. F6 constrains what an
observer's independently formed records suffice to predict. Neither implies
the other. A universe can be computationally closed while leaving a
record-relative physical remainder, or record-sufficient while remaining
silent about hypercomputation.

## Chairman synthesis

### Does the family have non-zero excess?

Yes, **conditionally**. F1–F6, F9, and F10 state observable differences or
prohibitions not contained in bare QM + GR. But none is currently a completed
theory:

- most lack a selected physical variable or coefficient;
- several define their signature only as an unexplained residual;
- all must survive complete state/process, environment, selection,
  acquisition, and resource accounting;
- none has an earned positive residual.

The base H\* has less content than its descendants. Clause (ii) has content
only when a preferred-foliation leakage law is stated. Clause (iv) has
content only when a metalevel produces an effect not representable by an
ordinary physical mediator or state variable. Clauses (i) and (iii) alone are
structural interpretations.

### Is squeeze-to-vacuity the real shape?

Mostly yes, but not for the overbroad reasons in the supplied brief.

A fourth route reaches the same terminus through operational
identifiability:

1. if a hidden substrate variable changes an operational likelihood, it is
   an ordinary empirical variable and must face a finite discriminator;
2. if it never changes any likelihood, it is unidentifiable by observation;
3. if it maps bijectively or gauge-equivalently to the standard theory, it is
   a representation unless it adds a separately testable cross-arena law.

That trilemma is independent of game-engine architecture, resource budgets,
and artifact hunting. It suggests the squeeze is the epistemic shape of the
question, not merely an artifact of the metaphor.

It is not yet an exhaustive no-go theorem. To publish a no-go by attrition,
Dynamic Unity would need a frozen class of allowed substrate extensions and a
proof that every member lands in leakage, empirical equivalence, or
inaccessibility. The present council supplies the candidate partition, not
the proof.

## Adjudication of the two standing queries

### Query 1 — “the substrate is physics” can be a result

Accepted with a grade correction.

- “Physics is describable computationally” has no excess.
- “Physics is computationally closed and no physical hypercomputer exists”
  is F5 and has nonzero prohibitive content.
- “Every physical transformation is resource-accounted” becomes scientific

## Observer indexing and the preferred-foliation branch

The charter should treat observer indexing as a **methodological and
operational index**, not as an unconditional ontological theorem.

This distinction leaves both branches open:

1. **No preferred foliation/global completion admitted.** Contextuality,
   Bell-type constraints, and friend-style no-go assumptions place stronger
   pressure on observer-relative facts and records.
2. **Preferred-foliation or global-completion branch admitted.** A global
   hidden configuration may exist, but observer access, certified records,
   interventions, and capability remain indexed because observers do not
   automatically possess the global completion.

Thus the North Star does not need to reject a preferred foliation. It does
need to stop claiming that observer-relative ontology follows
unconditionally from Kochen–Specker, Bell, or Wigner-friend results. A
stronger ontic claim must state which theorem assumptions and foliation
