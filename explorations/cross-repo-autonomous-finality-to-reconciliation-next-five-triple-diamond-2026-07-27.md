sibling updates do not replace it. They reveal what must happen immediately
after it:

```text
autonomous physical selection
  -> smallest selector witness or non-selection theorem
  -> record-reconciliation naturality and source/disclosure controls
```

   operation-order, and complete-process explanations have been removed.

# Diamond 1 — purpose

## Divergence: six plausible controlling questions

1. **Physical selector:** Does autonomous dynamics independently select
   stable record/finality structure?
2. **Source fork:** Is any apparent novelty genuine source extension or
   bounded disclosure?
3. **Reconciliation:** Do independently formed local records merge naturally,
   or is there irreducible path/context dependence?
4. **Coordination translation:** Which distributed-system theorems transfer
   to physically formed records?
5. **Geometry recovery:** Can the selected structure recover GU-like boundary
   content or observer-accessible geometry?
6. **Holonomy:** Does a complete three-perspective reconciliation loop carry
   a residual outside standard quantum process structure?

## Counterfactual framing

For every proposal compare at least these worlds:

| world | frozen difference |
|---|---|
| `W_fixed_access` | one fixed complete source; only observer aperture or decoder changes |
| `W_selected_write` | autonomous dynamics selects carrier sectors and a blank-to-written transition |

| `W_regional_merge` | local records are independently retained and later reconciled |
| `W_hidden_memory` | route, controller, environment, or rejected-attempt memory remains outside the candidate record |
| `W_action_expand` | the future intervention/capability family is enlarged without changing past records |

Any claimed creation, finality, or remainder must distinguish the relevant
worlds under a complete physical contract.

```text
SEAM 1 — formation/selection:
```

## Value-of-information ordering

Sequence `A` wins because the first two questions control all later branches:

1. If no autonomous mechanism selects more than timescales or a state
   partition, naturality, adapters, and holonomy would concern supplied
   interfaces.
2. If a mechanism selects carrier and write but not access, the next question
   becomes interface selection—not consensus.
3. If carrier, write, and access are selected, reconciliation naturality can
   distinguish ordinary gluing from a path-sensitive frontier.
4. Only then can distributed theorems be transferred without category error.

The sibling updates therefore do not change Position 1. They elevate
reconciliation naturality to Position 3 and add source/disclosure and
threshold-synergy controls to Positions 1–3.
