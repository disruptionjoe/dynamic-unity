# Evolution can select a predictive interface without selecting physics

## Executive result

The proposed story contains one real physical arrow and two unsupported
extensions:

```text
physical population + ecology + heredity + differential growth
  -> predictive interface types can be selected

predictive interface selection
  -/-> physical laws were selected for forecastability

fixed law or fixed meta-law
  -/-> source issuance.
```

Natural selection is not merely an analyst's score. Once a population,
heritable interface variants, environment, action coupling, resource costs,
and reproduction dynamics are part of the physical antecedent, differential
growth physically changes which interface types persist. A predictive
interface can therefore be independently selected at an emergent biological
or ecological level even though the underlying microscopic law does not
select a unique record carrier by itself.

This corrects an overly narrow reading of physical selection. Dynamic Unity
should not require every observer interface to be selected by bare fundamental
law alone. It should require the claimed selector to be physical,
antecedent-fixed, level-declared, and independent of the later reconstruction
target.

The correction does not support a universal forecasting objective.
Evolution selects reproductive or persistence consequences, not information
as such. A perfectly predictive but irrelevant or costly sensor can lose to a
less informative one. Symmetry-equivalent carriers remain unselected as
points. And saying that the laws themselves evolved requires a population of
law-bearing worlds, an inheritance/mutation kernel, a reproduction weight, and
a sampling rule. Those objects are a new meta-theory, not consequences of
predictive usefulness.

The result is therefore:

```text
EMERGENT_PHYSICAL_INTERFACE_TYPE_SELECTION_IS_REAL
UNIVERSAL_FORECASTING_OBJECTIVE_NOT_DERIVED
LAW_SELECTION_REQUIRES_EXPLICIT_META_DYNAMICS
SOURCE_ISSUANCE_NOT_ESTABLISHED
```

## 1. The levels that must not be collapsed

| level | physical object | what can be selected | what remains supplied |
|---|---|---|---|
| source law | state space \(X_L\) with law \(L\) | trajectories or stationary structures admitted by \(L\) | \(L\) and its domain |
| individual interface | sensor/record channel \(R_i\), policy \(\pi_i\), formation and access mechanism | behavior conditional on one formed individual | boundary, architecture, and available actions |
| population/ecology | heritable types \(i\), environment process \(E\), reproduction/growth kernel \(W_i\), mutation \(M\) | interface type or orbit by differential persistence | ecology, heredity, resource ledger, initial support |
| law ensemble | law types \(\lambda\), world reproduction \(B_\lambda\), inheritance kernel \(K\), sampling measure \(\mu\) | law parameter distribution | the entire meta-dynamics and measure |

Selection at one row does not automatically lift upward or downward.
Population selection can explain why a type of observer interface is common.
It does not identify the provenance of one particular record occurrence, and
it does not show that the microscopic law was selected for producing such
observers.

## 2. `HC-DU-084A` — exact population selector

Let \(n_i(k)>0\) be the abundance of heritable interface type \(i\) after
generation \(k\). Freeze a physical ecology and let \(g_i\) be its asymptotic
resource-accounted log growth rate, including sensing, memory, action, delay,
and maintenance costs. In the no-mutation control,

\[
n_i(k)=n_i(0)e^{k g_i}.
\]

For any two types,

\[
\frac{n_i(k)}{n_j(k)}
=
\frac{n_i(0)}{n_j(0)}
e^{k(g_i-g_j)}.
\]

### Theorem — antecedent-relative interface-type selection

If \(g_i>g_j\), type \(i\) exponentially displaces type \(j\) whenever both
are initially present. If one type uniquely maximizes \(g\), its population
frequency tends to one in this frozen control.

This is elementary multiplicative selection mathematics, not a new
evolutionary theorem. Its Dynamic Unity consequence is nevertheless
load-bearing:

> The complete physical antecedent \(L+E+W+M\) can select an interface type
> even when \(L\) alone selects only an admitted family or symmetry orbit.

With mutation, stochastic environments, finite populations, or frequency
dependence, the stationary object can instead be a distribution,
quasispecies, metastable family, or polymorphism. The claim is selection of
the object actually fixed by the declared dynamics, not automatic
point-selection.

## 3. Prediction is neither the selected quantity nor a universal monotone

Let a record channel \(R_i\) carry information about a future environmental
variable and let the organism act through \(\pi_i\). Its selection value
enters only through the resulting physical growth rate:

\[
g_i
=
\sup_{\pi_i}
\mathbb E[\log W(\pi_i(R_i),E_{\mathrm{future}})]
-c_i,
\]

where \(c_i\) includes the resource cost of forming, retaining, and using the
interface.

### Counterexample 1 — perfect but irrelevant prediction

Let \(R_1\) perfectly predict a fair future bit \(Z\), while growth depends
only on an independent environmental variable \(E\). Let \(R_0\) be constant.
Then \(R_1\) has one bit more predictive information, but no policy can turn
that bit into growth. If \(c_1>c_0\), the more predictive interface is
selected against.

### Counterexample 2 — useful action without an informative record

In a constant environment, a fixed phenotype can have maximal growth with a
constant record. A costly sensor that accurately represents the unchanged
environment adds information-processing structure without improving action
or growth.

### Blackwell boundary

If channel \(R_0\) is a garbling of \(R_1\), \(R_1\) has weakly greater
decision value for every supplied decision problem when the extra information
can be ignored at no cost. Strict physical selection still requires an
ecology in which the distinction matters and a resource ledger in which the
advantage exceeds its cost.

This agrees with the biological literature:

- Taylor, Tishby, and Bialek show that achieving a specified mean growth rate
  can require a minimum information level and that competition can favor
  information-efficient regulation
  ([primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.4382)).
- Bergstrom and Lachmann distinguish Shannon information from its
  decision-theoretic fitness value; equality occurs only in special cases
  ([primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/q-bio/0510007)).
- Moffett et al. show that delay and robustness can produce a nonzero critical
  sensor-error level below which additional accuracy has no growth advantage
  ([primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.00058)).

The correct physical statement is therefore not “evolution maximizes
forecasting.” It is:

> Under a frozen ecology, predictive structure may be selected when its
> causal contribution to matched-resource growth or persistence is positive.

## 4. Evolution does not evade the carrier-symmetry theorem

Suppose two heritable carriers \(i_0,i_1\) implement the same record channel,
policy, cost, and growth rate, while a symmetry of the complete population
antecedent swaps them. Their abundance ratio has no deterministic selection
term:

\[
g_{i_0}=g_{i_1}.
\]

Finite-population drift, mutation, or an asymmetric initial condition may
pick a realized member, but those are additional physical antecedents. The
fitness functional itself selects only the orbit or neutral class. This is
the evolutionary specialization of `HC-DU-083`, not an escape from it.

Evolution can therefore select:

- a predictive interface architecture rather than a symmetry-equivalent
  microscopic carrier;
- a cost/accuracy tradeoff rather than maximal information;
- a polymorphism or metastable family rather than a unique point; and
- a relational fit between interface and ecology rather than an intrinsic
  “forecasting capacity” of an object.

## 5. `HC-DU-084B` — why selection of laws needs a meta-law

For a fixed physical law \(L_\lambda\), the state trajectory lives in
\(X_\lambda\):

\[
x_{t+1}=F_\lambda(x_t).
\]

The parameter \(\lambda\) is not selected by this trajectory merely because
some states support better predictors. A selection statement over laws needs,
at minimum:

1. a space \(\Lambda\) of law-bearing world types;
2. more than one realized or measure-bearing member of \(\Lambda\);
3. a reproduction or weighting functional \(B_\lambda\);
4. a transmission, mutation, or transition kernel
   \(K(\lambda'\mid\lambda)\);
5. a resource and competition interpretation; and
6. a sampling measure connecting the ensemble to observations.

Without those objects, “laws evolved toward forecastability” is not false; it
is an untyped selection claim.

With them, it becomes a genuine conditional cosmological model. Smolin's
cosmological natural selection is a useful comparator because it explicitly
supplies world reproduction through black holes, parameter variation, and
black-hole number as the proposed fitness proxy
([primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9404011);
[later status](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0612185)). It does not select
forecastability unless forecastability is separately shown to increase the
declared reproduction weight.

### Meta-selection relocation theorem

Any law-selection account represented by a fixed
\((\Lambda,B,K,\mu)\) attributes selection to that meta-dynamics. It does not
derive the meta-dynamics from the selected laws. A complete fixed meta-law can
encode the entire law lineage, so the construction remains compatible with
third-person disclosure.

Thus adding a universe ensemble can make law selection meaningful, but it
does not by itself earn source issuance. Issuance would require a further
argument that the admissible meta-process cannot be represented as a fixed
completed history or oracle. That is exactly the Temporal
Issuance/fixed-completion boundary and is not discharged here.

## 6. What this changes for Dynamic Unity

### Correction

Replace the overly strong demand

```text
bare fundamental law must uniquely select every record interface
```

with

```text
an independently fixed physical antecedent must select the claimed
interface object at the declared explanatory level.
```

An evolved observer interface can meet that corrected condition at the
population level. The receipt must include the ecological boundary, heritable
variation, action coupling, growth/persistence consequence, costs, and
symmetry quotient.

### What remains open

Evolutionary selection does not yet provide:

- the first origin or issuance of interface variants;
- provenance of one particular blank-to-written record;
- a universal observer boundary or action alphabet;
- a fundamental law optimized for prediction;
- a reason the ecology or fitness proxy has its declared form;
- a source-internal escape from fixed completion; or
- a cross-physics record reconstruction theorem.

The next high-value reopener is not another evolutionary toy. It is a

3. the selection transfers across environments without refitting;

   and
5. the claimed gain exceeds ordinary fitness, stability, and process
   prediction if a stronger Dynamic Unity claim is sought.
