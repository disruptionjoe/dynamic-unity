# Heterodox professor: recursive viable-systems wave

## Executive verdict

The recursive-viability premise is scientifically useful only after three
objects that the direct precursors conflate are pulled apart:

1. **Beer functions** `S1..S5` are simultaneous functions inside one proposed
   viable-system model.
2. **Recursive levels** are adjacent scales at which a complete system
   passport repeats and has typed upward and downward maps.
3. **Finality strata** are degrees or regimes of record settlement,
   reversal cost, or causal depth. They can crosscut several systems and
   several recursion levels.

Neither a deeper record nor an `S4` label creates another viable-system level.
Neither a Markov blanket nor a coarse grain establishes active regulation.

The bounded swing constructs a real but deliberately narrow positive case.
Nine binary record carriers are arranged into a recursively composed
three-way repetition regulator, but the leaf labels are scrambled and the
partition is not supplied to the discovery procedure. Under a declared local
fan-in-three resource class:

```text
m=0: no regulator
      -> held-out survival 0

m=1: three local Rep3 regulators
      -> held-out survival 7/9

m=2: the local regulators participate as the three children of one Rep3 root
      -> held-out survival 9/9
      -> held-out repair-record prediction 9/9

m=3: one silent wrapper
      -> no new survival or prediction
      -> rejected by minimum description cost
```

There are `280` partitions of nine leaves into three unlabeled triples.
Endpoint survival alone leaves **220** partitions with a perfect score.
Causal support and provenance of the repair records reduce that set to
exactly one on training interventions, and the same one survives the held-out
interventions. The constructed nested Markov-blanket factorization has zero
violations, and the macro record commutes on all `2^9=512` microstates.

That positive result is paired with decisive negative controls:

- a flat all-to-all regulator has the same endpoint viability if nonlocal
  fan-in and fan-out are free;
- a passive environmental replay has `100%` survival with the nominal
  controller both on and off, so stability alone falsely earns control;
- ordinary majority coarse-graining retains the correct root bit while the
  microstate remains outside the viable region;
- duplicate encodings manufacture a fake third level until records are
  quotiented by causal provenance; and
- a third silent wrapper fits perfectly but adds nothing.

The exact disposition is:

> **Recursive operational viability is conditionally constructible and
> discoverable from interventions and cross-level records. Endpoint
> persistence, blankets, causal compression, or hierarchy depth alone do not
> identify it.**

This also overturns one strong reading of the precursor:

> **“No System 4 implies nonviability” is not supplied as a transferable
> physics theorem.**

The finite regulator remains viable against its declared perturbation family
with no environment model, adaptation channel, or rule-authoring capacity.
That does not show that adaptation is unnecessary in an expanding or
distribution-shifting environment. It shows that **operational viability,
adaptation, and self-authorship are different claims**.

The recursive premise therefore adds a conditional physical construction and
a useful discovery passport. It also narrows the self-authoring-ledger vision:
the constructed hierarchy maintains an inherited code; it does not mint a
new code, law, scale, objective, or possibility. No claim is banked or seeded.

## 2. Audit the direct VSM/finality precursors

`stratified-finality-levels-2026-07-21.md` contains several generative

onto a shallow-to-deep finality tower. This is not Beer recursion. In Beer's

Finality depth is yet another coordinate. One local group can produce both a

settlement regime can span multiple systems. No equality among these axes has
been shown.

  invariant burden; and

## 3. Strongest recursive-viability premise

### 3.1 Definitions

**System.** A system is a set of state variables plus a declared boundary,
environment, transition kernel, inputs, outputs, allowed interventions, and
persistence criterion. A named scale is not a system.

**Boundary.** A boundary is an intervention-stable conditional-independence
or channel-support relation. In the fixture it is the exact set of three
children read and written by one syndrome/repair channel.

**Environment.** For a level-1 group, the environment is the other groups
plus the disturbance channel and the downward root command. For the root it
is the declared perturbation family.

**Viability.** At an episode's stabilization boundary, all descendant leaves
must equal the inherited root identity bit. The criterion is tested under a
declared perturbation family and a declared finite episode horizon.

**Active regulation.** The controller earns causal credit only when disabling
it changes held-out survival. Passive return or an external replay is not
regulation.

**Recursion.** The same `Rep3` passport acts once on leaf records and once on
the three resulting group records:

\[
M_3(x_1,x_2,x_3)
   =\mathbf 1[x_1+x_2+x_3\ge 2],
\]

\[
M_9^{\mathcal P}(x)
   =M_3\!\left(
        M_3(x_{\mathcal P_1}),
        M_3(x_{\mathcal P_2}),
        M_3(x_{\mathcal P_3})
      \right).
\]

Recursion is not visual self-similarity. It is the repeated state/control
passport plus a commuting upward map and a downward repair map.

**Scale.** A scale is an admitted resolution and response cadence. Two levels
give dimensionless depth and cadence ratios only. The fixture imports its
micro episode cadence and generates no dimensional unit.

**Clock.** Each level is event-driven: local checks occur before the root
check. This ordering is a causal denominator for the construction, not
physical time. Disjoint local checks commute.

**Record.** A record is a provenance-bearing state or action that can
condition a later repair:

- bit flip/update opportunity;
- local syndrome and targeted repair;
- copied upward majority bit;
- downward root repair command; and
- durable root episode result.

A copied majority payload is not another elapsed-time record merely because
it appears in another storage cell.

**Depth.** `m` is the minimum number of interventionally useful macrolevel
passports required to predict held-out viability and repair provenance within
the declared resource class. A silent wrapper does not count.

## 13. Four-run learning arc in plain English

### After Run 1

I learned that “the activity dies” was too broad. A changing environment can
keep the geometric degrees of freedom active indefinitely. But the toy
environment supplied the rhythm, so we had not found nature's own driver or
scale.

Collectively, the council learned that mathematically live influence patterns
were real but easy to overread. Their shape did not choose a physical
response, unit, or cosmological meaning. That pushed us toward a covariant
causal process capable of supplying its own interventions.

### After Run 2

I learned that causal history already has one exact way to change an effective
law at a physical landmark: conditioning across a post. In one factorial
family it produces a square-root hierarchy without changing the law at every
birth.

Collectively, the council learned that the square root is not universal. It
belongs to a tail class, and even that family has not been shown to generate
the posts, geometry, response, or units needed for physics. That shifted the
question from “can history matter?” to “what physical events and records
carry that history?”

### After Run 3

I learned that a global clock is not disqualified merely by being global. The
literal one-record-per-tick model fails reunited-clock comparisons, but a
global opportunity clock with real local record dynamics remains possible.
Local causal order also recovers more than expected when bulk event density
is added, although local memory count is not bulk volume.

Collectively, the council learned that finite traces cannot identify local
versus arbitrarily gated global succession. We need fixed dynamics, durable
record semantics, and interventions that expose scheduler leakage. That made
“which records belong to which system and level?” the natural next question.

### After Run 4

I learned that multiple viable levels can be made physically and
non-anthropomorphically precise. In the finite witness, the second level earns
its place because it handles a new perturbation class and sends repairs back
down. The levels are discovered by intervention-support records, not by their
names.

I also learned that hierarchy is much easier to hallucinate than to discover.
Most partitions have perfect endpoints, passive replay looks alive,
coarse-graining looks organized, record copies add fake depth, and a flat
controller can do the same job when locality is free.

The new premise therefore changes the program in one useful way: it supplies
a passport for searching for levels inside the live post and clock programs.
It does **not** show that reality is a VSM hierarchy or that System 4 is the
missing law of becoming. It makes those statements harder, clearer, and more
testable.

## 14. Newly visible or previously misused outcomes

1. **Record provenance is a boundary observable.** Run 3 treated provenance
   mainly as a way to distinguish opportunities, records, and copies. The new
   probe shows that the causal support of a record can also identify a system
   boundary when endpoints cannot.
2. **Fixed-history replay generalizes to viability.** Run 2 used replay to
   distinguish inherited memory from online feedback. Here, passive replay
   distinguishes apparent recovery from a causally efficacious regulator.
3. **Posts and stabilization boundaries must remain typed.** Both segment
   histories, but only a CSG post has the required causal-order relation.
4. **A Markov blanket is a candidate boundary, not a controller.** The blanket
   can be exact while the active viability advantage is zero.
5. **Causal emergence and requisite variety answer different questions.**
   Effective information tests causal effectiveness under interventions;
   requisite variety bounds control; neither alone certifies persistence.
6. **The `G-gated` problem is already multilevel.** A global opportunity,
   local microtransition, observer memory, and copied message can occupy
   different levels. This is a reason to add the passport to `HC-DU-031A`,
   not evidence that the architecture works.
7. **The post route may contain level changes without a recursive system.**
   A changed effective law after a post is a scale/history transformation.
   Recursive viability additionally requires a boundary, essential variables,
   and downward intervention.
8. **The self-authoring vision loses a false shortcut.** A system can be
   viable, recursive, and record-bearing while merely preserving an inherited
   identity. A source that mints new admissibility remains a separate burden.
9. **Beer points toward heterarchy as much as hierarchy.** Nonunique chains
   and arbitrary boundaries make overlapping-system rivals mandatory.

regimes.
