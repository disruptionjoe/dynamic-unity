# 1. Mathematical correction: what the four degrees and metric are

A four-dimensional carrier is written conditionally as a fibre (V_x) over a
base point or context (x). The construction does **not** identify its four
degrees with:

- spacetime coordinates;
- fundamental physical dimensions;
- Hilbert-space dimension;
- latent neural dimensions;
- GU carrier coordinates; or
- four already selected observables.

Those are different claims.

A metric is not itself “the metric bundle.” Pointwise, a metric is a section

\[
g\in\Gamma(\operatorname{Sym}^2 T^*M),
\]

or the corresponding structure on another selected carrier. A connection
supplies transport between fibres. Projectors select components, subspaces, or
quotients. An action functional constrains lawful dynamics. None is generally
identical to another, and none is selected merely because all can be placed in
one diagram.

For a symmetric metric on a four-dimensional tangent space, there are ten
independent components before gauge, field equations, symmetry, or special
coordinates reduce them:

\[
\frac{4(4+1)}2=10.
\]

Four axial ruler responses (g(e_i,e_i)) determine only the four diagonal
components in the chosen frame. The six pair responses

\[
g(e_i+e_j,e_i+e_j),\qquad i<j,
\]

then determine the six cross terms. The exact probe verifies rank (4) for
the axial family and rank (10) for axes plus pair sums.

This does not mean ten experimental rulers are universally necessary. Field
equations, symmetries, gauge quotients, or other observables may reduce the
unknown class. It means that “four degrees plus four rulers” does not identify
a general metric without those additional premises.

# 3. Perspective relationship atlas

A record view is an operational quotient

\[

archive, access route, or downstream consumer.

A capability view is a physically admitted family of interventions or
response queries under a declared resource and horizon contract. It is not an

## 3.3 Classical composability

compared, transported, merged, and used consistently under composition. It

It is not identical to material record formation. A commutative copy/delete
structure need not select a sampler, archive, provenance, access path, or
consumer. [`HC-DU-159`](instrument-no-section-admission-and-smallest-natural-selection-boundary-2026-07-30.md)

not be a chain. Finality adds the physical claim that a particular closed

# 4. Joint-view factorization theorem

Let (X) be the admitted completion/state class after a declared physical
gauge quotient, and let

\[
v_i:X\to Y_i
\]

be target-independent perspective maps. Write

\[
v=(v_1,\ldots,v_n):X\to\prod_iY_i.
\]

For a held-out target (t:X\to Z):

> (t) factors through the joint perspective (v) exactly when
> \(\ker v\subseteq\ker t\), equivalently
> \(\bigcap_i\ker v_i\subseteq\ker t\).

The full admitted state is reconstructed up to physical gauge (E_G) exactly
when

\[
\bigcap_i\ker v_i=E_G.
\]

The proof is the standard quotient-factorization criterion: (t) is
well-defined on a joint-view fibre precisely when it is constant on that
fibre. This is not new mathematics. Its use here is to stop three mistakes:

1. treating one lossy perspective as the common object;

   common kernel; and
3. adding the held-out metric as a “ruler view” chosen after seeing which
   geometry must be reconstructed.
