# Autonomous finality: three-mechanism selection gate

## Executive verdict

The three mechanisms select three different and genuinely physical parts of
the proposed layered-finality picture:

| mechanism | strongest independently selected object |
|---|---|
| Liouvillian spectral metastability | invariant/slow operator structure and its relaxation timescales |
| thermodynamic symmetry breaking and durable memory | material phase sectors, carrier support, barriers, and finite retention scaling |
| autonomous dissipative stabilization | an attractive code/steady manifold and correction dynamics relative to engineered jump operators |

None of the three, in the strongest standard construction audited here,
independently selects the complete object Dynamic Unity still lacks:

```text
source-bound blank-to-written occurrence
  + retained provenance
  + physical archive route
  + readout/access ports
  + independently fixed future action envelope.
```

The return is therefore:

```text
DYNAMICS_SELECTS_STATE_FILTRATION_ONLY
PARTIAL_SELECTION: THERMODYNAMICS SELECTS CARRIER AND RETENTION
APPROXIMATE_OR_HYSTERETIC_NON_FILTRATION
THRESHOLD_OR_PARTITION_SUPPLIED
OBSERVER_ACCESS_PORT_SUPPLIED
NO_READY_MECHANISM
```

This does **not** say autonomous dynamics can never form a record. It says the
three standard mechanism results do not yet provide an antecedent-invariant
formed accessible record interface. The result closes this frozen
three-mechanism reopener, not Dynamic Unity, the North Star, or every possible
physical mechanism.

## The important new learning

The program should stop treating “stronger stability” as the likely missing
step. Stability, record formation, and access are different axes:

\[
\text{retention strength}
\;\not\Rightarrow\;
\text{write provenance}
\;\not\Rightarrow\;
\text{accessible action sufficiency}.
\]

The mechanisms order differently on those axes:

- Liouvillian theory is best at deriving a multiscale state hierarchy from a
  generator.
- thermodynamic memory is best at deriving a natural material carrier and
  finite retention law;
- autonomous error correction is best at maintaining a chosen information
  subspace without measurement-based feedback.

Their intersection is not a complete record. It is a **stable response
structure conditional on an interface**.

That is scientifically useful because it localizes the frontier. A new
reopener must select or physically force the interface-changing event—not
merely produce a slower eigenmode, a higher barrier, a more redundant endpoint,
or a better protected code.

## 1. Scope and claim boundary

The frozen mechanism classes are:

1. Liouvillian spectral metastability and invariant algebras;
2. thermodynamic symmetry breaking, nucleation, and durable memory; and
3. autonomous dissipative error correction or stabilization.

The audit asks what follows from their standard antecedents:

\[
a=
(\text{state space},\text{autonomous generator},
\text{boundary},\text{controls},\text{representation},
\text{resource regime}).
\]

It does not demand that a fundamental law uniquely select a laboratory
apparatus from no matter or boundary data. It does demand that a claimed
record interface not be copied into \(a\) under names such as “code,”
“pointer,” “decoder,” “monitor,” “bit,” or “logical state.”

The result is scoped to the cited theorem/construction classes and the frozen
passport. It is not an exhaustive theorem over all nonlinear, non-Markovian,
gravitational, cosmological, biological, or unknown dynamics.

## 2. Primary-source reconstruction

### 2.1 Liouvillian spectral metastability and invariant algebras

[Macieszczak et al. (2016)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.05801) show that a
spectral separation in a Markovian open-system generator yields a
low-dimensional metastable manifold built from low-lying eigenmatrices.
[Macieszczak et al. (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.01227) sharpen the
classical case: approximately disjoint metastable phases support effective
classical stochastic dynamics, while time-coarse-grained continuous
measurement records can resemble noisy classical trajectories.

The exact asymptotic counterpart is also mature.
[Blume-Kohout et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.4282) characterize preserved
information structures through fixed points and matrix algebras.

What the source selects:

- the generator spectrum;
- exact invariant/fixed structures;
- a slow subspace and metastable manifold when timescales separate;
- effective transition rates among classical metastable phases; and
- a retention/relaxation hierarchy.

What it does not select:

- one environmental monitoring;
- one trajectory decomposition;
- one blank carrier or source-to-carrier write;
- one retained occurrence history;
- one observer-access port; or
- one future action family.

This is not a philosophical objection. A single master equation admits
multiple trajectory realizations. [Wiseman and
Diósi](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0012016) parameterize diffusive
unravelings and give their distinct environment-monitoring interpretations.
Thus the reduced Liouvillian can select slow structure while failing to
select which, if any, trajectory is a physical record.

**Return:** `DYNAMICS_SELECTS_STATE_FILTRATION_ONLY`, with exact invariant
structure and approximate horizon-indexed metastable structure.

### 2.2 Thermodynamic symmetry breaking, nucleation, and durable memory

For finite quantum systems,
[Wallace](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.09547) describes spontaneous symmetry
breaking through approximately dynamically isolated, symmetry-related
sectors rather than pretending finite systems possess exact
thermodynamic-limit degeneracy.

The memory literature supplies both positive structures and sharp boundaries:

- [Dennis, Kitaev, Landahl, and
  Preskill](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0110143) connect protected
  information, thresholds, and phase structure, while their robust local
  measurement-free recovery requires four or more spatial dimensions.
- [Bravyi and Terhal](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.1983) prove that local
  one- and two-dimensional stabilizer Hamiltonians have no macroscopic
  energy barrier, while explicitly contrasting the two-dimensional
  classical ferromagnet.
- [Alicki, Fannes, and
  Horodecki](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.4584) show, in their Markovian weak
  coupling model, that the relaxation time of the two-dimensional Kitaev
  model is bounded independently of system size.
- [Bravyi, Hastings, and
  Michalakis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.0344) prove scoped zero-temperature
  stability of topological order under weak local perturbations.

What thermodynamic dynamics can select:

- a material order-parameter support once the Hamiltonian and physical
  system are fixed;
- a symmetry orbit of phases or logical sectors;
- energetic/free-energy barriers;
- nucleation and escape dynamics;
- finite-horizon robustness; and
- in suitable models, lifetime scaling with system size.

What it does not select by itself:

- which symmetry-related branch is the record value;
- whether a terminal phase was newly written, inherited, or spontaneously
  nucleated;
- the epoch to which the putative write belongs;
- a source-binding relation between an event and the phase;
- the observer/readout boundary; or
- the action family under which the phase quotient is final.

The law can select the orbit while a fluctuation, initial condition, boundary
field, quench, or write pulse selects a realized branch. That is physical
branch formation, but terminal branch value alone is not retained occurrence
provenance. Over longer horizons a finite metastable system also escapes, and
under stronger actions its domain walls and microscopic state reopen.

**Return:** `PARTIAL_SELECTION: CARRIER_AND_RETENTION_SELECTED; WRITE,
PROVENANCE, ACCESS, AND ACTION ENVELOPE NOT SELECTED`, plus
`APPROXIMATE_OR_HYSTERETIC_NON_FILTRATION`.

### 2.3 Autonomous dissipative error correction or stabilization

[Pastawski, Clemente, and
Cirac](https://arxiv.org/abs/1010.2901) construct quantum memories protected
by engineered dissipation. [Lebreuilly et
al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.05007) give revised Knill--Laflamme
conditions for the existence of an engineered Lindbladian protecting
against a bounded number of consecutive natural errors and characterize the
resulting logical dynamics.

A current strong example makes the selection boundary unusually explicit.
[Kumar and Weimer
(2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.19288) encode a cellular-automaton toric-code
decoder into Lindblad dynamics and report a self-correction phase transition.
The paper states that the jump operators implement the decoder and have the
toric-code ground states as dark states. The result is important, but it is
also an exact example of the record/code target being built into the
engineered dynamics.

What the full engineered generator can select:

- a dark, steady, or code manifold;
- an attraction/correction basin;
- a dissipative gap or correction-rate hierarchy;
- suppression of declared error classes; and
- conditional logical capability inside the chosen code.

What it does not independently select:

- why this code, logical algebra, syndrome family, or decoder is the physical
  record ontology;
- a blank-to-written occurrence and its provenance;
- an external readout/decoder port;
- the action family used to call the logical quotient sufficient; or
- the reservoir engineering itself from target-blind underlying physics.

“Autonomous” removes measurement-based feedback from the online correction
loop. It does not remove design from the Hamiltonian, jump operators, ancilla,
bath, code, or logical target. Error syndromes are commonly exported as
entropy rather than retained as occurrence records.

**Return:** `ENGINEERED_DYNAMICS_SELECTS_CHOSEN_STEADY_OR_CODE_MANIFOLD`,
not `DYNAMICS_SELECTS_ACCESSIBLE_INTERFACE`.

## 4. Exact selection and failure map

Let \(M\) be the class of complete physical implementations, let

\[
a:M\rightarrow A
\]

retain the mechanism-defining antecedent, and let

\[
\xi:M\rightarrow\Xi
\]

return the complete accessible formed interface:

\[
\xi=
(\text{carrier},\text{blank/write},\text{provenance},
\text{archive route},\text{access},\text{actions},\text{resources}).
\]

The mechanism selects the interface exactly when

\[
\ker a\subseteq\ker\xi.
\]

This is the existing `HC-DU-033F/054` implementation-fibre criterion.

The three classes fail at different first arrows:

```text
Liouvillian:
  generator -> invariant/slow structure
            -/-> physical unraveling/write/access

Thermodynamic:
  Hamiltonian + material -> carrier + phase orbit + retention
                         -/-> occurrence provenance/access/action envelope

Autonomous dissipative:
  engineered generator -> chosen code/steady manifold + correction
                       -/-> target-blind selection of that generator
                       -/-> occurrence provenance/external access.
```

Equivalently, each standard class contains one of two failure modes:

1. the same mechanism-level antecedent admits different complete interface
   realizations; or
2. the desired interface has already been encoded into the supplied
   generator.

Neither failure denies the physical reality of the selected sectors. It
denies one particular attribution: that the autonomous mechanism has derived
the whole record interface.

## 5. Action-indexed hierarchy result

For fixed physical dynamics, independently declared actions
\(\mathcal A_\tau\), horizon \(\tau\), and tolerance \(\epsilon_\tau\), define

\[
x\sim_\tau x'
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\sup_{b\in\mathcal A_\tau}
d\!\left(P_b^\tau(\cdot\mid x),P_b^\tau(\cdot\mid x')\right)
\leq\epsilon_\tau.
\]

All three mechanisms can make this quotient useful:

- spectral relaxation supplies response scales;
- thermodynamic barriers supply error/lifetime scales; and
- dissipative correction supplies logical response/error scales.

But the mechanism generally does not select
\(\mathcal A_\tau\) or \(\epsilon_\tau\). Exact nesting is therefore not
automatic.

- New actions refine equivalence.
- Longer horizons can destroy metastable equivalence.
- Hysteresis can make the apparent quotient path dependent.
- A different monitoring can change the trajectory partition while
  preserving the reduced generator.
- A differently engineered decoder changes the stabilized code.

The canonical object available without choosing one threshold is a
**parameterized response family**, not necessarily a filtration of formed
records. Calling every such family “layered finality” would be a relabel.

## 6. Hostile controls

### Threshold and horizon

The exact invariant algebra is canonical for a fixed process. A metastable
manifold is selected only relative to a spectral separation and an error/time
window. Recording the entire persistence curve avoids a fitted threshold but
does not create a write or access port.

### Decomposition and unraveling

Different environment monitorings can realize the same reduced Liouvillian
with different trajectory records. A physical dilation can remove this
ambiguity only by entering the antecedent; it must then also select the
carrier, channel, provenance, and access structure being claimed.

### Symmetry and nucleation

A symmetric law can select a phase orbit without selecting its orientation.
An initial fluctuation can select a realized branch without making the
terminal value a timestamped or source-bound occurrence record.

### Capability enlargement

Microscopic, coherent, logical, long-horizon, or environment access reopens
distinctions suppressed by the restricted quotient. This is expected and is
not a defect; it means finality remains action/resource relative.

### Fixed-source disclosure

A protected logical state, threshold-opened secret, or jointly reconstructed
value can have been fixed in the initial state or engineered generator. Better
access and better retention do not establish source-side creation.

## 7. Common minimum missing premise

The missing premise is an **autonomous formed-interface selector**:

> A target-blind physical antecedent must make the complete
> source--carrier--write--provenance--archive--access--action interface
> invariant across its admissible implementation fibre, or return the exact
> physical orbit/plural family that remains.

At minimum, a future candidate must supply a locally decidable answer to:

1. what was blank;
2. what physical event changed it;
3. what retained distinction proves that change occurred in the declared
   epoch;
4. how that distinction is causally bound to its source;
5. which physical systems can access it;
6. which future actions it is sufficient for; and
7. which energy, entropy, memory, coherence, latency, and discarded support
   paid for the formation.

This premise is stronger than “there is a stable phase” and weaker than “one
fundamental law uniquely chooses every apparatus.” It allows realized matter,
boundary, and environment dynamics to do the selecting. It forbids merely
renaming a supplied code or order parameter as a record.
