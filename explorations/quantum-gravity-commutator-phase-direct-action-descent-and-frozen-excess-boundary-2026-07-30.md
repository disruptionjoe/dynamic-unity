Chen and Giacomini have identified a genuinely better experimental target
than ordinary Newtonian gravitational entanglement. Their published
linearized-quantum-gravity calculation predicts:

1. an interaction phase for sufficiently delocalized quantum sources whose
   functional form differs from the Newton potential and the classical or
   semiclassical models they test; and
2. additional probe phases sourced by canonical gravitational-field
   commutators, with a time dependence different from the leading phase.

Those are nonzero conditional predictions. Dynamic Unity should not flatten
them into “just another representation.”

But the predictions support two different claims:

- **Model-class claim:** a measured phase surface can exclude the Newton
  potential and specified classical or semiclassical dynamics.
- **Ontology claim:** the gravitational field, as a named independently real
  component, must have mediated the interaction.

The first claim remains live. The second does not follow from source--probe
records alone.

The source paper itself makes the distinction visible for the wide-source
phase. It writes an explicit nonlocal operator acting only on the matter
sources which reproduces the phase. The authors call that interaction ad hoc
because no established gravity theory is known to select it. That is exactly
the right status:

```text
matter-only representation exists
≠ a physical rival law selects it
≠ the field derivation is uniquely identified
```

The exact Dynamic Unity control strengthens the same point for a
commutator-generated phase. Controlled noncommuting operations on a mediator
can return the mediator exactly while inducing a direct phase on the source
and probe. Every complete endpoint state is then reproduced by a direct
source--probe gate. Mid-process access to the mediator separates the two
implementations immediately.

The correct scientific result is therefore asymmetric:

> The proposed functional shapes can be strong no-refit discriminators among
> frozen physical theories. The fact that one derivation contains a
> gravitational-field commutator does not, by itself, make field mediation
> identifiable against every matched direct-action or influence-functional
> representation.

No observed packet, Dynamic Unity prediction, hardware authorization, or
ready successor follows.

## 1. The primary-source object

Chen and Giacomini's
[published Physical Review X result](https://doi.org/10.1103/hl1c-t8z9)
studies linearized quantum gravity in the Schrödinger field
representation. The corresponding
[full preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10288) gives the derivation and
assumptions.

The paper is already careful about the ordinary gravity-entanglement
inference:

- Newtonian gravitational entanglement does not by itself establish which
  degrees of freedom mediated the interaction;
- other descriptions can reproduce the same operational result;
- theory-specific arguments are useful but do not become
  theory-independent ontology proofs.

Its contribution is to move beyond the ordinary Newton-potential observable,
not to erase those inference conditions.

### 1.1 Wide delocalized sources

The standard Newtonian treatment is recovered after restricting the source
to a semiclassically localized packet whose position and momentum
delocalization are negligible at the experiment's resolution. For a general
static quantum source, Chen and Giacomini instead diagonalize the
stress-energy density and solve the linearized gravitational constraints.

For two delocalized sources, the resulting interaction phase depends on the
source energy-density eigenfunctions in a form which is not the usual
position-space Newton potential. The paper compares it against:

- the Newton potential;
- positive classical--quantum hybrid dynamics; and
- the Schrödinger--Newton equation.

Those declared alternatives give different behavior. Most importantly for
this audit, the paper also supplies its own exact representation absorber: an
ad hoc nonlocal coupling acting directly on the two matter sources can
reproduce the phase without gravitational field degrees of freedom.

That does not make the prediction empty. It changes its evidential type:

| Observation | Earned inference |
|---|---|
| Phase differs from Newton potential over a frozen source family | Newtonian interaction is insufficient in that arena |
| Phase differs from the declared CQ and Schrödinger--Newton models | those model classes are excluded under the frozen controls |
| Phase matches the linearized-QG source-shape law without refitting | support for that law over the tested rivals |
| Same phase is representable by a source-only nonlocal operator | field ontology is not selected by the phase alone |
| No independently motivated source-only theory predicts the law | linearized QG may retain a substantial explanatory/selection advantage |

The last two rows coexist. Representation is not selection.

### 1.2 Canonical commutator phase

The second construction uses:

- one general static quantum source;
- one moving probe;
- externally controlled source/probe potentials;
- a weak linearized gravitational field;
- negligible probe back-reaction on that field;
- no gravitational radiation, so the field follows the source adiabatically;
  and
- a joint source--probe measurement preserving the relevant coherence.

The free field Hamiltonian and probe--field interaction fail to commute on
the physical state. The Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff expansion then produces
additional phase terms containing one and two gravitational-field
commutators. In the published calculation the first commutator correction is
proportional to \(t^3\), rather than sharing the leading phase's time
dependence. All retained terms are first order in the gravitational coupling;
the effect does not rely on graviton emission or graviton loops.

This supplies a real conditional discriminator:

```text
same gravitational order
+ different time scaling
+ different probe stress-energy dependence
+ joint coherent readout
```

It is still conditional on the temporal-gauge description, back-reaction
approximation, neglected matter commutators, source/probe preparation,
adiabatic/no-radiation regime, and the declared rival classes. Those are
parts of the physical packet, not minor annotations.

## 2. The exact central-commutator descent theorem

The mediator attribution issue can be decided without simulating gravity.

Let \(U,V\) be unitary operations on a mediator \(M\) satisfying a central
Weyl relation

\[
UV=\omega VU,
\qquad |\omega|=1.
\tag{1}
\]

Let source label \(s\) and probe label \(p\) control the corresponding
mediator actions. The group commutator is

\[
U^sV^pU^{-s}V^{-p}
=\omega^{sp}I_M.
\tag{2}
\]

Therefore the complete controlled process is

\[
\sum_{s,p}
|sp\rangle\!\langle sp|
\otimes
U^sV^pU^{-s}V^{-p}
=
\left(
\sum_{s,p}
\omega^{sp}|sp\rangle\!\langle sp|
\right)
\otimes I_M.
\tag{3}
\]

The left side is explicitly mediated. The right side is a direct
source--probe phase tensored with the identity on the mediator. The equality
holds for every joint input, including an arbitrary initial mediator state.

This is not a perturbative approximation. It is an operator identity.

### 2.1 Smallest qubit control

Take

\[
U=X_M,\qquad V=Z_M,\qquad XZ=-ZX.
\tag{4}
\]

The mediated circuit is

```text
C_X(S,M)
→ C_Z(P,M)
→ C_X(S,M)
→ C_Z(P,M).
```

Its exact action is

\[
CZ_{SP}\otimes I_M.
\tag{5}
\]

The executable regression verifies:

- all eight computational-basis inputs;
- 64 informationally spanning product inputs drawn from
  \(\{|0\rangle,|1\rangle,|+\rangle,|+i\rangle\}^{\otimes3}\);
- zero state-vector difference from the direct phase;
- exact restoration of the mediator for every tested input; and
- the \(-1\) phase only on the \(S=P=1\) sector.

This is stronger than an endpoint coincidence. It proves equality of the
complete three-system endpoint operator in the finite arena.

### 2.2 Positive control

The theorem does not say a mediator can never be identified. It says the
source--probe endpoint cannot identify a target erased by the descent.

For input

\[
|+\rangle_S|0\rangle_P|0\rangle_M,
\tag{6}
\]

access the mediator after the first controlled \(X\):

- in the mediated implementation, \(P(M=1)=1/2\);
- in the direct implementation, \(P(M=1)=0\).

The first leak is therefore exact and small:

> mediator-facing mid-process access, a retained interaction receipt, or a
> physical theorem excluding the direct realization.

Adding that access changes the admitted action/interface class. It is not
“more source--probe tomography.”

## 3. The continuum absorber: influence functionals

The finite identity is a theorem for central commutators, not a derivation of
linearized gravity. The continuum reason the issue persists is standard
open-system mathematics.

Feynman--Vernon elimination treats the matter variables as the system and the
field as the environment. For a Gaussian mediator linearly coupled to matter,
integrating out the mediator produces an exact matter-history influence
functional. Its kernels retain the mediator's response/commutator and
fluctuation/noise information. Aurell, Kawai, and Goyal's
[operator derivation](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.02671) makes this explicit
and shows how higher environmental cumulants enter higher-order influence
terms outside the Gaussian case.

The typing is important:

| Object | What it preserves | What it does not select |
|---|---|---|
| Local explicit field description | field variables, local couplings, and full declared dynamics | whether the field representation is ontologically privileged |
| Matter influence functional | reduced matter process after the declared field treatment | local field history or independently accessible field observables |
| Direct matter action | source/probe behavior in its frozen query class | a physical law that chooses that action |
| Observed source--probe phase | an operational functional shape | its unique microscopic realization |

An influence functional can be temporally nonlocal, complex, state
dependent, or difficult to interpret as a closed matter-only Hamiltonian.
Therefore:

```text
field-free effective representation exists
≠ a simple local classical theory exists
≠ a fundamental direct-action theory is selected
```

But it is enough to block the inference:

```text
field commutator appears in one derivation
⇒ the endpoint uniquely proves field mediation.
```

This is the same boundary `HC-DU-115/116/126` established for exact
source-action elimination, now applied to the strongest conditional
field-commutator reopener preserved by `HC-DU-147`.

## 4. The frozen no-refit empirical contract

The useful part of the proposal is not a single anomalous phase. It is a
multi-configuration phase surface.

Before looking at outcomes, freeze:

1. source energy-density family and preparation procedure;
2. source delocalization/width and external source potential;
3. probe momentum or energy superposition and its stress-energy values;
4. interaction times;
5. temporal gauge/laboratory-time conversion;
6. back-reaction and no-radiation regime;
7. all retained and neglected commutator terms;
8. Newtonian, CQ, Schrödinger--Newton, direct-action/influence-functional,
   and nuisance rival classes;
9. which parameters are shared across configurations;
10. source--probe joint instrument, calibration, lineage, selection, and
    acquisition contract.

Then fit no model anew per configuration. Test at least two independent axes:

- **source-shape transfer:** one parameter packet must predict how the phase
  changes as the source energy-density functional is changed; and
- **time/stress-energy transfer:** the same packet must predict the leading
  and commutator-dependent time powers across probe preparations.

The possible returns are:

| Return | Grade |
|---|---|
| Newtonian/classical rival survives no-refit transfer | proposed excess killed in that arena |
| Linearized QG and a matched source-only action both survive | effective law supported; field ontology unresolved |
| Linearized QG survives every independently admitted physical direct rival | component-specific empirical support within that frozen class |
| Mediator-facing/provenance measurement also separates | field-attribution evidence within the expanded interface |
| Unmodeled selection, drift, calibration, or refitting explains the shape | acquisition/nuisance absorption |

This is a legitimate future test architecture. It is not yet Dynamic Unity's
