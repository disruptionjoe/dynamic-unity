# Predictive selection to forecasting issuance

## Executive result

The new idea contains a potentially important research direction, but most of
its first layer is already known:

> A record can summarize the past in exactly the way needed to predict a
> declared class of futures.

Computational mechanics supplies minimal predictive causal states.
Information-bottleneck work relates them to relevant sufficient statistics.
Nonequilibrium thermodynamics connects nonpredictive memory with dissipation.
Semantic-information and evolutionary work ask when information causally
contributes to persistence or fitness. Dynamic Unity already has exact finite
behavioral quotients, intervention-stable refinements, bounded witnesses, and
the warning that a target-derived quotient is a diagnostic rather than a
physically formed record.

The live possibility begins one level later:

> Does physical dynamics independently form and stabilize a
> target-independent forecasting interface because that interface changes
> what the system can subsequently do and record—and can the resulting
> recursive expansion be issuance rather than fixed-law disclosure?

That question is worthy of one bounded first swing. It is not yet a new
scientific successor. The repository should remain quiescent until the
residual survives the known absorbers and a naturality audit.

## 1. Four types that must stay separate

Let \(H_{\le t}\) be a past history, \(A\) a frozen admissible action class,
\(\rho\) a resource budget, and \(H_{>t}\) a future history.

### Prediction

A predictor estimates one supplied target

\[
Y=f(H_{>t})
\]

under a fixed law, instrument, horizon, and action.

### Forecasting

A forecasting state \(S_t\) preserves a declared family of interventional
future kernels:

\[
P(H_{>t}\mid H_{\le t},do(a))
=
P(H_{>t}\mid S_t,do(a))
\quad
\text{for every }a\in A.
\]

It is stronger than one-target prediction, but still relative to \(A\),
future horizon, process class, and equivalence tolerance.

### Capability

A capability is an attainable task, action, discrimination, or control
outcome under the same access and resource contract. Information present in a
state is not a capability unless an admitted system can use it.

### Issuance

Operational issuance would require a strict expansion

\[
\mathcal F_{t+1}(A,\rho)
\supsetneq
\mathcal F_t(A,\rho)
\]

of the realizable forecasting/action frontier without quietly enlarging the
external resource envelope or reading a prewritten oracle. Frontier growth is
not by itself third-person ontological novelty; its maximum initial claim is
an operational expansion under a frozen contract.

## 3. The smallest potentially unabsorbed conjunction

The following five clauses must all survive:

1. **Physical selection.** Dynamics selects a formed and retained interface;
   an analyst does not compute it afterward from a supplied process.
2. **Naturality.** The selection commutes with physical relabelings,
   refinements, gauge/equivalence maps, and allowed changes of
   representation.
3. **Target generality.** The interface is fixed before the target, held-out
   action, and scoring rule and retains a nontrivial future response family.
4. **Capability consequence.** Possessing and accessing it changes a
   matched-resource action or discrimination frontier.
5. **Recursive non-disclosure.** Using the new capability changes future
   record-formability in a way whose claimed grade survives the fixed-oracle,
   completed-history, and controller-completion attacks.

Any four without the fifth are already plausible within known science:
physical learning systems can form predictive memory and act on it. The
research question is whether the conjunction yields a theorem, law, or exact
boundary not already captured by process dynamics, control, thermodynamics,
or evolution.

## 5. The conditional five-swing sequence

Only Swing 1 is the next candidate for explicit authorization. Swings 2–5 are
conditional cards.

### Swing 1 — residual and naturality audit

**Question.** After the strongest known predictive-state, thermodynamic,
semantic, evolutionary, control, and Dynamic Unity results are stated at full
strength, what exact proposition remains?

**Work.**

1. Freeze definitions of prediction, forecasting, capability, and issuance.
2. Build a supplied-versus-selected table for process, system boundary,
   future horizon, action set, target, score, resource budget, record carrier,
   access map, and formation law.
3. Test candidate formulations for naturality under relabeling, refinement,
   representation changes, and matched-resource action expansion.
4. Return one exact residual statement or a clean absorption theorem.

**Cheapest kill.** Every candidate functional depends on a supplied target,
horizon, action class, observer boundary, or retrospective process model.

**Allowed returns.**

```text
NO_EXCESS_BEYOND_PREDICTIVE_STATE_THEORY
TARGET_OR_ACTION_RELATIVE_ONLY
PHYSICAL_SELECTION_RESIDUAL_SURVIVES
RECURSIVE_ISSUANCE_RESIDUAL_SURVIVES
```

Only the last two can activate Swing 2.

### Swing 2 — observer-neutral action-indexed forecasting quotient

**Question.** Does the residual admit a unique or canonical quotient of
histories that preserves a frozen family of interventional futures?

**Work.**

- Define equivalence by the full frozen future-response kernel, not one
  selected target.
- Prove existence, minimality, refinement behavior, and relation to
  `HC-DU-036H`.
- Determine whether the quotient is merely computed by an analyst or is a
  candidate physically formed object.

**Cheapest kill.** The quotient is exactly a known causal state,
probabilistic bisimulation, sufficient statistic, or target-derived
behavioral quotient with no formation claim.

### Swing 3 — physical selection versus retrospective usefulness

**Question.** In the smallest source-pinned physical arena, does dynamics
select between resource-matched candidate interfaces because of forecasting
value rather than ordinary stability, dissipation, or supplied fitness?

**Counterfactual design.**

1. equal stability and dissipation, different held-out forecasting value;
2. equal forecasting value, different stability;
3. interface fixed before target and action reveal; and
4. no-refit transfer to a second process family.

**Cheapest kill.** The selection disappears when ordinary thermodynamic or
evolutionary variables are matched, or the interface must be supplied.

### Swing 4 — recursive capability frontier and issuance/disclosure fork

**Question.** When the formed forecast state changes the actions available to
the system, does the reachable forecast/action frontier strictly expand under
a frozen external resource envelope?

**Work.**

- define and measure the frontier before and after the handoff;
- separate learning a latent parameter from creating a new admissible action;
- run the fixed-controller, fixed-oracle, completed-history, and source
  intervention controls; and
- grade first-person operational expansion separately from third-person
  underivability.

**Cheapest kill.** A fixed complete controller or pre-correlated source
reproduces the full trajectory and frontier under the same contract.

## Plain-English assessment

The promising idea is not “the universe predicts.” It is that a physical
system may build compressed records of its past, use them to forecast several
possible futures, gain new actions from those forecasts, and thereby change
what can be recorded next.

Science already explains many versions of that story. The open Dynamic Unity
question is whether nature itself selects the relevant forecasting interface
without an analyst choosing the target and whether the recursive loop creates
a genuinely larger operational frontier rather than revealing the next step
of a fixed program.

That is an ambitious, worthy question. The smartest next move is a cheap
residual audit because it can tell us whether there is a real new theorem to
pursue before we build another model around a familiar predictive-state idea.
