# Metastable write architecture and occurrence-tokenization obstruction

## Executive verdict

The minimal metastable host returns a split result:

```text
WRITE ARCHITECTURE SELECTED UP TO Z2
ENDPOINT OCCURRENCE PROVENANCE OBSTRUCTED
```

A target-independent weighted generator can select, within a frozen typed
source--memory class:

- a fast ready/transition class;
- two long-lived record phases;
- a write-edge orbit;
- a turnover/reset-edge orbit;
- a finite write-success bound;
- a finite retention bound; and
- the record-value alternatives up to an unavoidable global swap.

That is real progress beyond `HC-DU-042`: the same generator does more than
select a slow simplex. It selects the **architecture of writing and
retention**.

But the terminal metastable value does not certify **which occurrence wrote
it**, whether it was newly written during the declared interval, or whether
it simply persisted. Two lawful paths can have the same generator, initial
state, terminal state, stable value, and arbitrarily many redundant copies of
that value while differing on whether any write occurred in between.

Therefore:

\[
\text{stable value}
\;\not\Rightarrow\;
\text{occurrence token}.
\]

The minimum exact repair for the bounded binary question “was this value
written during this epoch?” is one physically retained binary distinction
plus a certified epoch/reset boundary. For exact unbounded occurrence
identity, finite fixed memory eventually aliases; a growing token, bounded
horizon, or weakened semantics is required.

Quantum Darwinism and Spectrum Broadcast Structure absorb value
objectivity. They do not absorb this endpoint-only provenance obstruction.
The stronger redundant-consistent-history program shows the correct positive
route: the environment must redundantly carry a **history distinction**, not
only copies of a terminal value.

`H-CCR-17` remains open. `ECR-N5-S3` is retyped from a generic formation
test to one concentrated **provenance-lift and formation-descent** test.

## 2. Exact finite metastable normal form

Let

\[
X=\{x_0,x_1,x_2,x_3\}
\]

and use the row generator

\[
Q_{\alpha,\kappa}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
-\alpha & \alpha & 0 & 0\\
0 & -\kappa & \kappa & 0\\
0 & 0 & -\alpha & \alpha\\
\kappa & 0 & 0 & -\kappa
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\alpha>\kappa>0.
\]

Thus the only jumps are

\[
x_0\xrightarrow{\alpha}x_1
\xrightarrow{\kappa}x_2
\xrightarrow{\alpha}x_3
\xrightarrow{\kappa}x_0.
\]

The physical reading is:

| state | source phase | carrier status |
|---|---:|---|
| \(x_0\) | \(0\) | ready/blank |
| \(x_1\) | \(0\) | retained value \(0\) |
| \(x_2\) | \(1\) | ready/blank |
| \(x_3\) | \(1\) | retained value \(1\) |

This is an exact metastable normal form, not a microscopic Hamiltonian or a
claim that all material records have four states.

The fast edges model source-conditioned settling into a retained phase. The
slow edges model source turnover together with return to a ready carrier.

## 3. What the generator selects

### 3.1 Ready and retained classes

The total exit rates are:

\[
q(x_0)=q(x_2)=\alpha,
\qquad
q(x_1)=q(x_3)=\kappa.
\]

Because \(\alpha\neq\kappa\), the weighted graph itself distinguishes:

\[
B=\{x_0,x_2\},
\qquad
R=\{x_1,x_3\}.
\]

Relative to `A_typed`, \(B\) is the ready class and \(R\) is the retained
record class. The fast edge orbit

\[
W=\{x_0\to x_1,\;x_2\to x_3\}
\]

is the write architecture. The slow edge orbit

\[
C=\{x_1\to x_2,\;x_3\to x_0\}
\]

is the turnover/reset architecture.

No held-out target enters this classification.

### 3.2 Exact metastable spectrum

The characteristic polynomial is

\[
\det(\lambda I-Q)
=
\big((\lambda+\alpha)(\lambda+\kappa)-\alpha\kappa\big)
\big((\lambda+\alpha)(\lambda+\kappa)+\alpha\kappa\big).
\]

Hence:

\[
\lambda_0=0,
\qquad
\lambda_1=-(\alpha+\kappa),
\]

and

\[
\lambda_{\pm}
=
\frac{-(\alpha+\kappa)
\pm\sqrt{(\alpha+\kappa)^2-8\alpha\kappa}}{2}.
\]

For

\[
\frac{\alpha}{\kappa}>3+2\sqrt2,
\]

the nonzero eigenvalues are real. As
\(\alpha/\kappa\to\infty\),

\[
\lambda_+\sim-2\kappa,
\qquad
\lambda_-\sim-\alpha,
\]

so the stationary mode plus one slow mode form a two-phase metastable
manifold separated from two fast modes.

The stationary weights are:

\[
\pi(x_0)=\pi(x_2)
=\frac{\kappa}{2(\alpha+\kappa)},
\qquad
\pi(x_1)=\pi(x_3)
=\frac{\alpha}{2(\alpha+\kappa)}.
\]

Thus the process spends a fraction

\[
\frac{\alpha}{\alpha+\kappa}
\]

of stationary time in the retained phases.

### 3.3 Selection is orbit-valued, not label-valued

The nontrivial weighted directed-graph automorphism is

\[
\sigma:
x_0\leftrightarrow x_2,
\qquad
x_1\leftrightarrow x_3.
\]

For \(\alpha\neq\kappa\), the full automorphism group is

\[
\operatorname{Aut}(Q_{\alpha,\kappa})\cong\mathbb Z/2.
\]

It preserves \(B\), \(R\), \(W\), and \(C\), while swapping the two source
and record values. Therefore the generator selects:

- the ready class;
- the retained class;
- the write and reset edge sets; and
- the two-value record orbit;

but not an absolute convention saying which member is called \(0\).

That global swap is harmless only for targets and actions that transform
equivariantly with it. A later oriented task must charge its symmetry-breaking
reference.

### 3.4 Finite write and retention contracts

Starting in \(x_{2i}\), the probability that the fast write has occurred by
time \(w\) is

\[
P_{\mathrm{write}}(w)=1-e^{-\alpha w}.
\]

Starting in the corresponding retained state, the probability of no
turnover/reset for a further horizon \(h\) is

\[
P_{\mathrm{retain}}(h)=e^{-\kappa h}.
\]

Thus an ideal one-epoch write-and-retain contract has the exact lower bound

\[
P_{\mathrm{success}}(w,h)
\ge
(1-e^{-\alpha w})e^{-\kappa h}.
\]

This is a finite horizon and error contract, not permanent finality.

### 3.5 Perturbation stability

Let the same directed cycle carry fast rates
\(\alpha_0,\alpha_1\) and slow rates \(\kappa_0,\kappa_1\), with

\[
\min_i\alpha_i>\max_j\kappa_j.
\]

The rate gap continues to select the same fast/ready and slow/retained
classes and the same write/reset edge types. Exact \(\mathbb Z/2\) symmetry
may be broken by unequal rates, but the interface architecture persists.

Uniform finite bounds become:

\[
P_{\mathrm{write}}(w)
\ge
1-e^{-\alpha_{\min}w},
\qquad
P_{\mathrm{retain}}(h)
\ge
e^{-\kappa_{\max}h}.
\]

The selector is therefore stable over a declared open rate-ordering class,
not just at one fitted parameter point.

## 4. Exact occurrence-provenance obstruction

Let \(\Omega_T\) be the allowed paths on \([0,T]\). Define:

- \(e_T(\omega)=\omega(T)\), the endpoint;
- \(r_T(\omega)\), the endpoint record value when
  \(e_T(\omega)\in R\); and
- \(p_T(\omega)=1\) iff at least one fast write edge in \(W\) occurred
  during \([0,T]\), otherwise \(0\).

Consider two paths with the same initial and terminal state \(x_1\).

### Stale path

\[
\omega_{\mathrm{stay}}:
x_1\quad\text{with no jump on }[0,T].
\]

It has positive probability:

\[
P(\omega_{\mathrm{stay}}\text{ cylinder})=e^{-\kappa T}>0.
\]

Its provenance bit is:

\[
p_T(\omega_{\mathrm{stay}})=0.
\]

### Rewritten path

\[
\omega_{\mathrm{cycle}}:
x_1\to x_2\to x_3\to x_0\to x_1
\]

with all four jumps before \(T\) and no later jump.

Every required exponential holding-time interval has positive density, so
this path cylinder has positive probability for every \(T>0\). It returns to
the same endpoint but contains two fast writes:

\[
p_T(\omega_{\mathrm{cycle}})=1.
\]

Therefore:

\[
(X_0,e_T,r_T)(\omega_{\mathrm{stay}})
=
(X_0,e_T,r_T)(\omega_{\mathrm{cycle}})
\]

while

\[
p_T(\omega_{\mathrm{stay}})
\neq
p_T(\omega_{\mathrm{cycle}}).
\]

Equivalently:

\[
\ker(X_0,e_T,r_T)
\not\subseteq
\ker p_T.
\]

### Theorem — endpoint value does not certify occurrence

For this complete generator, no decoder of the initial state, terminal
metastable state, and terminal record value can determine whether a write
occurred during the interval.

This is not an inference failure caused by too few samples. It is an exact
factorization failure.

The complete path determines the answer. The endpoint record does not retain
it. A history existing in the physical realization is not yet a present
provenance-bearing record.

## 5. Redundancy does not repair missing provenance

Let

\[
c_n(v)=(v,\ldots,v)
\]

be \(n\) perfect copies of the terminal value. Because \(c_n\) is injective
on the value alphabet,

\[
\ker(c_n\circ r_T)=\ker r_T.
\]

Hence, for every finite \(n\),

\[
\ker(X_0,e_T,c_n\circ r_T)
\not\subseteq
\ker p_T.
\]

Arbitrarily redundant terminal-value copies can make the value public,
robust, and independently accessible while leaving the occurrence question
exactly unresolved.

This yields the scoped result:

> **Redundancy can harden a value only at the granularity actually
> broadcast. It cannot certify an omitted history distinction.**

The claim does not say Quantum Darwinism cannot encode history. It says
state-value redundancy is not automatically history redundancy.

## 6. Primary-source collision

### 6.1 Open-system metastability

[Macieszczak et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.01227) establish that a
timescale-separated open quantum system can have a metastable manifold
approximately represented by mixtures of finitely many metastable phases,
with effective classical dynamics among them. They also show that
time-coarse-grained continuous-measurement records can resemble noisy
classical trajectories.

[Brown, Macieszczak, and Jack](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.14107) analyze
unravelled metastable quantum trajectories, including a three-state reset
model with fast relaxation into phases, slow transitions, and computable
committors.

**Collision.** These results support the slow-phase, committor, and
trajectory ingredients. They do not make a terminal phase label a complete
certificate of which transition occurrence produced it.

### 6.2 Reduced generator versus monitoring route

[Wiseman and Diósi](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0012016) parameterize
multiple diffusive unravellings of one Markovian master equation and give
their environment-monitoring interpretations.

**Collision.** A reduced Lindblad generator selects average dynamics, not
one physical monitoring record. Distinct physical environment couplings or
access routes must remain distinct completions. Mathematical
reparameterizations of one fixed monitoring must remain gauge. This is why
the present positive selector is claimed only for the complete typed
generator, not for a reduced system master equation.

### 6.3 Spectrum Broadcast Structure

[Korbicz, Horodecki, and Horodecki](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.1712) derive
spectrum broadcast states in a photonic environment: independently
accessible fragments redundantly encode the same pointer value. Their model
also makes explicit that the interaction supplies the privileged observable
whose information is broadcast.

**Collision.** SBS is the strongest absorber for formed, redundant value
records. Perfect SBS copies of only \(r_T\) still identify the same two paths
above. If the fragments also encode a write edge or epoch, the interface has
been enlarged to a history record and must be credited accordingly.

### 6.4 Redundant histories

[Riedel, Zurek, and Zwolak](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.0331) go beyond
state-value redundancy: redundant records of consistent histories can make
an objective past locally accessible to many observers.

**Collision.** This does not kill the obstruction; it identifies the exact
positive repair. Provenance becomes recoverable when the environment
physically retains and redundantly exposes the relevant history distinction.
The remaining DU question is whether the same target-independent
matter--environment dynamics selects and forms that epoch/history record,
rather than an analyst adding it after the terminal-value obstruction is
seen.

## 7. Minimum physical repair

Inside one declared finite epoch, the terminal fibre above contains two
provenance classes:

\[
p_T=0
\quad\text{and}\quad
p_T=1.
\]

Any exact finite repair must therefore retain at least two distinguishable
states. This is a one-binary-distinction lower bound:

\[
\left\lceil\log_2 2\right\rceil=1.
\]

It is a distinguishability count, not a Landauer-energy claim.

A sufficient bounded repair is a physical flag \(b\in\{0,1\}\) with:

```text
certified epoch/reset boundary: b := 0
fast write edge in W:          b := 1
all other admitted motion:     retain b
```

Then \((r_T,b_T)\) determines whether a write occurred in that epoch.

But the repair earns provenance only if:

1. the epoch/reset boundary is itself physically certified;
2. no other admitted transition can set the flag;
3. the flag survives the declared horizon and perturbation class;
4. its environment/access route belongs to the complete process; and
5. it was selected independently of the held-out target.

For unbounded exact occurrence identity, a single flag is insufficient.
Repeated occurrences alias. The process must use a growing identifier,
retain a bounded history, export records to growing support, or weaken the
semantics. This is the same finite-support boundary already tracked by
`H-CCR-10`.

The new minimum premise is therefore:

> **a causally retained epoch witness, not more copies of the value.**
