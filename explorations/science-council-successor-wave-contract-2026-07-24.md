# Science Council five-persona successor-wave contract

## Purpose

Run a second independent work wave from the complete epistemic state produced
by the first five-persona wave and its subsequent Condorcet prioritization.

The successor wave is not a rerun, a vote ratification, or a requirement that
all personas work on `COM-D1`, `WF-D1`, or `WF-D12`. The enlarged starting
surface should let each persona notice a higher-leverage relationship,
counterexample, construction, or route that was unavailable before the first
wave's outputs were visible together.

The five lenses remain:

1. orthodox professor;
2. heterodox professor;
3. commercial scientist;
4. wild-frontier scientist; and
5. philosopher of science.

This is parallel work, not deliberation. Persona convergence is not evidence.
Root will personally recheck every result used in shared state.

## Required order of work

Each persona works in this order:

1. **Reconstruct the combined learning.** State what the first wave actually
   established, what it merely made plausible, and what it killed locally.
   Identify one non-obvious relationship among at least two prior outputs or
   wish-list items.
2. **Audit the prioritization.** Say which vote outcome the persona most
   agrees with, which it most doubts, and whether a lower-ranked source item is
   being systematically undervalued. The vote is an object to examine, not an
   instruction.
3. **Select one contested successor finding.** State the exact proposition,
   why the enlarged corpus makes it newly testable or newly questionable, and
   the evidence that could change it.
4. **Take one bounded evidence-producing swing.** Prefer an exact enumeration,
   analytic derivation, independent recomputation, counterexample, finite toy
   construction, discriminating comparison, or executable probe. A memo-only
   result is allowed only when the output is an exact dependency,
   dimensional, logical, or intervention analysis. Do not manufacture a
   result when the minimum object is absent.
5. **Reconcile the result.** Mark the proposition upheld, narrowed,
   overturned, or still not evaluable. Separate a concept-level consequence
   from a formalization-local result and from a prioritization change.
6. **Write the next-actions roadmap.** Order actions by dependency and
   information gain. Every action requires a concrete output, falsifier or
   stop, and why it belongs now.
7. **Write hypothesis and stop ledgers.** Include hypotheses, conjectures,
   possible claims, current non-ordinal warrant and grade, decisive tests,
   forbidden overclaims, stopped work, and exact reopeners.
8. **Name newly visible or misused outcomes.** Identify what becomes useful
   only after combining multiple first-wave results.
9. **Take a final unconstrained divergent pass.** Record every additional path
   genuinely worth preserving while the complete context remains active. Do
   not pad the list, rank it as a current priority, or make it pass the
   roadmap's admission bar.

For every divergent item, capture:

- a stable successor-wave ID;
- the idea or question;
- why it might matter;
- the first object, observation, or calculation;
- its collision with a known stop or failed class;
- its relationship to any first-wave source IDs; and
- status `UNRANKED / UNCOMMITTED / NOT_A_CLAIM`.

## Shared scientific constraints

- The first-wave central result remains the starting state:
  live formalization components exist, but no selector, physical observable
  identity, unit-bearing nonzero scale, or `Lambda` value is identified.
- The ordinary quadratic null makes the terminal residue and replication
  signature generic controls, not concept discriminators.
- Open Bianconi driving constructs persistent activity only with imported
  cadence and anisotropy.
- Direct `p(N)` or `g(N)` cardinality running is a killed formalization because
  it fails full natural-labeling path covariance.
- Dimensionless order may generate a hierarchy only relative to a declared
  microscopic unit; it cannot create physical units ex nihilo.
- Statewise relabeling, full path covariance, and physical update-record
  distinctions must remain typed separately.
- Expected transition Fisher information and empirical record score energy are
  distinct objects.
- Response `R`, physical embedding `E`, and unit map `U` may be built
  sequentially; `R and E` is needed for a selector result and `R and E and U`
  for candidate physical identification.
- A failed successor construction closes that formalization, not every concept
  sharing its vocabulary.
- Check counts, persona agreement, Borda totals, and Condorcet victories are
  never scientific warrant.

## Selection freedom and anti-circling rule

Every one of the fifty-six source items is available. A persona may combine
items when the typed relationship is explicit and every source ID is
preserved.

The following do not count as successor progress:

- repeating the same first-wave fixture without a new discriminator;
- implementing a vote winner merely because it won;
- generating another normalized influence proxy;
- prescribing an external schedule and calling the resulting activity native;
- fitting a post, cutoff, dimension, scale, or epoch after observing the
  desired answer;
- reporting small-causet statewise symmetry as full covariance;
- treating finite enumeration as continuum recovery;
- relabeling a stopped route without changing its load-bearing object; or
- expanding the divergent list instead of taking the bounded swing.

## Persona-specific search prompts

These prompts focus attention without restricting selection.

### Orthodox professor

Stress-test whether the proposed exact-enumeration and information-bottleneck
programs have the correct nulls, asymptotic target, and identifiability. Look
for a finite result that can be derived or exhaustively checked and that
distinguishes a real structural property from normalization or estimator
choice.

Owned output:
`explorations/science-council-orthodox-successor-wave-2026-07-24.md`.

### Heterodox professor

Seek a native open-system or feedback construction that does not prescribe its
clock. Challenge whether causal posts and coupling records exhaust the live
routes, whether a dormant item has a legitimate native reopener, or whether a
lower-ranked item reveals a different mechanism class.

Owned output:
`explorations/science-council-heterodox-successor-wave-2026-07-24.md`.

### Commercial scientist

Turn the enlarged corpus into the smallest decision-changing product and take
the cheapest useful piece of it. Challenge scope creep in `HC-DU-011A`, expose
dependency waste, and distinguish reusable infrastructure from research that
cannot alter the next decision.

Owned output:
`explorations/science-council-commercial-successor-wave-2026-07-24.md`.

### Wild-frontier scientist

Attempt the boldest coherent bridge among causal covariance, information
retention, emergent dimension, physical coupling records, covariant landmarks,
and a generated hierarchy. It must expose inserted choices and contain a cheap
kill capable of defeating the construction.

Owned output:
`explorations/science-council-wild-frontier-successor-wave-2026-07-24.md`.

### Philosopher of science

Stress-test the ontology and inference of paths, posts, records, interventions,
geometry, and scale. Ask when two birth histories are gauge descriptions of
one state and when they are physically distinct, and whether the successor
program's grades and vote-driven attention preserve that distinction.

Owned output:
`explorations/science-council-philosopher-successor-wave-2026-07-24.md`.

## File and coordination boundary

Each persona owns only its named memo plus uniquely prefixed optional probe and
artifact files. Use the prefix `du_successor_<persona>_...` for executable
work. Personas may read and rerun every repository receipt but must not edit:

- `LANES.yaml`;
- shared concept, claim, prediction, hardening, process, synthesis, contract,
  or vote files;
- another persona's output;
- durable memory; or
- Git history.

Do not commit or push. Root owns integration, shared-state changes, validation,
staging, commit, and publication.

Every memo must report:

- complete source audit and selected cross-output relationship;
- vote audit;
- files created;
- commands, calculations, or derivations used;
- contested proposition and result;
- roadmap;
- hypothesis and stop ledgers;
- newly visible or misused outcomes;
- unconstrained non-padded divergent wish list; and
- unresolved disagreement with the first-wave synthesis or vote.
