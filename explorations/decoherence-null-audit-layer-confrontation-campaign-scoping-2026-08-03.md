## 1. The null, stated at its strongest

Steelman, stated so that a proponent would sign it:

1. **Dynamics.** Unitary quantum mechanics plus environmental coupling
   produces einselection of pointer states and exponential suppression of
   interference on characteristic decoherence timescales. This is
   quantitatively confirmed wherever the transition (not just the endpoint)
   has been watched: cavity-QED mesoscopic superposition decay (Brune et al.
   1996; Deléglise et al. 2008 — full Wigner tomography of the decay),
   collisional and thermal decoherence of macromolecule interference
   (Hornberger et al. 2003; Hackermüller et al. 2004 — visibility curves
   matching decoherence theory with no free mechanism dial), engineered
   reservoirs in ion traps (Myatt et al. 2000). [All pins at execution.]
2. **Redundancy.** Quantum Darwinism predicts redundant proliferation of
   pointer information into environment fragments (the mutual-information
   plateau), observed in photonic and NV-centre experiments (Ciampini et al.
   2018; Unden et al. 2019; Chen et al. 2019). [Pins at execution.]
3. **Genericity — the null's heavy weapon.** The Brandão–Piani–Horodecki
   theorems (Nat. Commun. 6:7908, 2015; strengthened by Qi–Ranard 2021)
   prove that objectivity structure is *generic*: for any dynamics
   distributing information into many fragments, most fragments carry
   information only about one fixed POVM (measure-and-prepare structure),
   with no assumption on the dynamics. Consensus-like structure is therefore
   a *theorem of quantum channel structure*, not an added mechanism. There
   is no dial for a consensus mechanism to turn.
4. **The audit layer is bookkeeping.** Auditing access and independence is
   classical post-processing over the same physical state. It changes what
   an observer may *claim*, never what the system *does*: no new parameter,
   no dynamical term, no deviation in any functional form. TaF's own
   executable witness (environment R_delta = 3 with outside-observer
   D1 = (0,0,0,0)) is a fact about a formal model's bookkeeping, not an
   empirical discriminator. DU's own audit (HC-DU-057) already returned
   `NO_CURRENT_DU_SPECIFIC_EXCESS` on the three most tempting physics
   claims of the consensus architecture.
5. **The mergeable-sector spine is free.** The Wave 1a absorption note
   already records the null's newest weapon: the states-side Kadison pair
   is bare commutative-algebra mathematics, so *any* dynamics that
   einselects a commuting pointer sector inherits the entire
   merge-availability structure at zero cost. Consensus-flavored theorem
   structure therefore accrues to plain decoherence automatically; a
   DU-distinctive claim must locate a residual strictly above it.
6. **Prediction.** The null predicts *zero new phenomena*: every
   classicality transition shows the decoherence functional form at the
   system-specific timescale; every redundancy measurement shows the QD
   plateau; every proposed audit-layer discriminator returns "absorbed."
   Quantum Darwinism + decoherence timescales explain everything DU
   explains in this arena, with the audit layer as bookkeeping over it.
