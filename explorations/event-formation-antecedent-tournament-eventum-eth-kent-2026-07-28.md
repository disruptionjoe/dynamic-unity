# Event-formation antecedent tournament

## Executive verdict

Three approaches that take physical events seriously do three importantly
different jobs:

| Candidate | What it constructs | Tournament disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Belavkin Eventum Mechanics | a commutative, nondemolition output history and a quantum state conditioned on that history inside a quantum stochastic input--output model | `SUPPLIED_EVENT_INTERFACE` |
| Fröhlich--Pizzo ETH approach | a candidate event algebra selected, conditional on a supplied future-algebra co-filtration and state, by the center of the state's centralizer; an added collapse axiom stochastically selects the realized event | `PARTIAL_EVENT_SELECTION` |
| Kent Lorentzian quantum reality | a one-world beable history conditioned on a sampled asymptotic or final-boundary outcome | `GLOBAL_COMPLETION_ONLY` |

The tournament therefore returns `PARTIAL_EVENT_SELECTION`, not
`ANTECEDENT_FOUND`.

ETH contains the only new object that warrants another bounded gate. It does
not begin by choosing an apparatus pointer observable. Given a future event
algebra \(\mathcal E_{\ge t}\) and a state \(\omega_t\), it defines the
candidate event algebra as

\[
Z_{\omega_t}(\mathcal E_{\ge t}),
\]

the center of the centralizer of the state. This is a genuine conditional
selector of an actuality algebra inside the supplied algebraic contract. A
generating event partition can still be nonunique unless atomicity and a
minimal-projection condition fix it. ETH is not yet a physical selector of:

- the system boundary;
- the future-algebra co-filtration;
- the time direction or localization rule;
- the collapse law itself;
- retained provenance;
- observer access;
- public certification; or
- regional finality.

No audited candidate presently has demonstrated excess empirical content over
its matched standard-quantum account. None establishes source issuance. None
closes Dynamic Unity's North Star.

The earned next question is narrower and better:

> Is the ETH center-of-centralizer actuality algebra natural and invariant
> across every physically admissible representation, future-algebra
> co-filtration, restriction, and time coarse-graining of one frozen
> process—and are its generating event families unique up to the declared
> physical equivalence?

That is the exact reopener for a possible Swing 2. It is not activated by this
result.

## What was frozen

The candidates were assessed using one unchanged passport:

1. the physical state or process and the role of the quantum state;
2. temporal, causal, algebraic, subsystem, and boundary antecedents;
3. the event criterion and stochastic occurrence law;
4. whether event identity is selected rather than merely represented;
5. retained record, provenance, access, action, and public-composition status;
6. local/system-indexed, regional/public, and global/completion scope;
7. disclosure, candidate issuance, or undecided formation status;
8. representation, gauge, and interface freedom;
9. empirical content beyond ordinary quantum theory;
10. strongest absorber, cheapest kill, and exact reopener.

An event did not receive credit as a record. A commutative algebra did not
receive credit as an archive. A single-world completion did not receive credit
as local formation. Stochastic occurrence did not receive credit as source
issuance.

## Candidate 1 — Belavkin Eventum Mechanics

### Exact positive construction

In the quantum stochastic model, an extended Hilbert space carries a unitary
input--system--output dynamics. The observable output process
\(Y_{[0,t]}\) is commutative and satisfies the nondemolition conditions

\[
[Y(r),Y(s)]=0,\qquad
[X(s),Y(r)]=0\quad (r\le s),
\]

for the future system quantities \(X(s)\) admitted by the model. Therefore
the realized output history can be treated as a classical stochastic record,
and the posterior state is obtained by quantum conditioning or filtering on
that history.

This is stronger than merely writing down a POVM at a terminal time. It gives:

- a consistent time-indexed classical output history;
- a quantum future algebra conditioned on that history;
- an error--disturbance relation tied to the observation channel;
- prediction and feedback laws inside the declared channel; and
- an irreversible observable semigroup induced from an enlarged unitary
  dynamics.

### Selected versus supplied

| Object | Status in the construction |
|---|---|
| extended unitary dynamics | supplied by the Hamiltonian, coupling, noise, and boundary model |
| system/environment split | supplied |
| input and output channels | supplied |
| observed output quadrature or counting process | supplied |
| classical past algebra | induced once that output is chosen and the nondemolition condition holds |
| realized output history | stochastic within the chosen model |
| posterior quantum state | derived by conditioning on the output history |
| retained provenance beyond the output values | not selected |
| access and action interface | supplied by the observation/control contract |
| public or regional finality | not derived |

The critical distinction is between selecting a consistent past algebra and
selecting which physical degrees of freedom constitute the past record.
Eventum proves the former conditional on the chosen observation channel. It
does not derive the latter from a bare closed-system Hamiltonian.

The same reduced open-system dynamics can generally admit different
unravellings and different monitored outputs. If those choices induce
different trajectories while the unconditional reduced dynamics remains
fixed, the trajectory cannot be credited as an interface selected by that
reduced dynamics alone.

### Scope and formation status

- **Perspective scope:** local/system-indexed output, with a global unitary
  dilation in the background.
- **Status:** output events are ontic within Eventum's postulated beable
  algebra; the filtered state is predictive and record-conditioned.
- **Formation:** conditional physical output formation within a supplied
  input--output architecture.
- **Issuance:** not established. A unitary dilation plus supplied boundary and
  output algebra is compatible with completed-history disclosure.
- **Empirical excess:** none isolated here beyond standard quantum
  input--output and filtering predictions.

### Disposition

`SUPPLIED_EVENT_INTERFACE`.

The positive result is useful: a classical past and quantum future can coexist
without making a conscious observer primitive. But the result is already
absorbed, for Dynamic Unity's purposes, by quantum stochastic dilation,
continuous measurement, and quantum filtering once the output channel is
supplied.

**Cheapest kill of a stronger claim:** exhibit two admissible monitored
outputs or unravellings of the same unconditional dynamics with incompatible
trajectory records.

**Exact reopener:** a physical theorem that selects one output algebra or
unravelling from an independently fixed source, boundary, and interaction
contract.

## Candidate 2 — the ETH approach

### Exact positive construction

The ETH approach starts from a co-filtration of von Neumann algebras

\[
\mathcal E_{\ge t'}\subseteq\mathcal E_{\ge t}
\quad\text{for }t'>t,
\]

interpreted as the potential events still available at and after time \(t\).
Its Principle of Diminishing Potentialities makes this inclusion physically
load-bearing.

For a state \(\omega_t\) on \(\mathcal E_{\ge t}\), define the centralizer

\[
C_{\omega_t}(\mathcal E_{\ge t})
=
\{Y\in\mathcal E_{\ge t}:
\omega_t([Y,X])=0\ \text{for every }X\in\mathcal E_{\ge t}\},
\]

and its center

\[
Z_{\omega_t}(\mathcal E_{\ge t})
=Z\!\left(C_{\omega_t}(\mathcal E_{\ge t})\right).
\]

An actual-event family is a partition of unity whose projections generate a
nontrivial \(Z_{\omega_t}(\mathcal E_{\ge t})\) and have positive Born
weights. ETH's Collapse Postulate then selects one projection
\(\pi_{\xi_*}\) with probability \(\omega_t(\pi_{\xi_*})\) and updates the
state by conditioning:

\[
\omega_{t,\xi_*}(\cdot)
=
\frac{\omega_t(\pi_{\xi_*}(\cdot)\pi_{\xi_*})}
{\omega_t(\pi_{\xi_*})}.
\]

This matters because the actuality algebra is not inserted as an arbitrary
measurement basis after \(\mathcal E_{\ge t}\) and \(\omega_t\) have been
fixed. It is mathematically determined by their center-of-centralizer
structure. In a finite atomic case its minimal projections provide a
distinguished family up to labels. A general Abelian algebra can admit
multiple generating families, so ETH does not automatically select one
partition in every case.

### What is genuinely gained

Conditional on the co-filtration and state, ETH provides:

- a basis-independent algebraic criterion for whether a nontrivial event is
  available;
- a selected Abelian actuality algebra and, in an atomic case, its minimal
  event projections up to labels;
- a stochastic law for which event becomes actual;
- a history/tree structure from repeated event occurrence; and
- a clean distinction between ontic events and the predictive role of a
  quantum state.

That last distinction agrees with Dynamic Unity's perspective/ontology
contract: a system-indexed event can be physically ontic while a probability
assignment about its future remains epistemic. This agreement is conceptual
compatibility, not evidence for ETH.

### What remains supplied

| Object | Status in the construction |
|---|---|
| isolated/open system and Hilbert representation | supplied |
| observable/event algebra | supplied |
| future-algebra co-filtration \(\mathcal E_{\ge t}\) | supplied or model-derived from additional locality/dynamics assumptions |
| time direction and localization | supplied; relativistic derivations remain conditional |
| state \(\omega_t\) before the event | supplied by prior history and law |
| actuality-algebra rule \(Z_{\omega_t}(\mathcal E_{\ge t})\) | selected by the ETH definition within that contract |
| realized event | selected stochastically by the additional Collapse Postulate |
| Collapse Postulate | postulated as a new law |
| durable carrier, occurrence identity, and provenance | not generally selected |
| observer access, interventions, and action capability | not generally selected |
| public certification and regional finality | not derived |

The precise partial success is therefore:

```text
supplied system + future-algebra co-filtration + prior state
    -> algebraically selected actuality algebra
    -> generating event family [unique only under added conditions]
    -> postulated stochastic actualization
    -/-> complete certified record interface.
```

### The live ambiguity

The actuality-algebra selector is natural only if the physical theory selects
the co-filtration, state restriction, and admissible equivalence class tightly
enough that all legitimate descriptions yield the same actuality-algebra
quotient and equivalent generating families.

That has not been established by this tournament. In particular:

- different admissible subalgebras can have different centralizers and
  centers;
- different system boundaries or state restrictions can change the event
  partition;
- even one Abelian actuality algebra can admit multiple generating
  partitions unless atomicity and a minimal-projection rule apply;
- degeneracy and coarse-graining can change which projections are minimal;
- alternative future-algebra nets can encode different losses of access; and
- the same observed process may be representable by ordinary instruments
  without empirical excess from treating the event as an added ontology.

The cited models demonstrate the law in idealized discrete-time
atom--radiation systems. The authors explicitly note model limitations,
including the status of continuous-time extensions and spectral conditions.
The models do not prove that a unique event co-filtration is selected in
realistic interacting four-dimensional QFT.

### Scope and formation status

- **Perspective scope:** system-indexed event occurrence; histories can
  compose at the level of the supplied algebraic system, but no public
  finality theorem follows.
- **Status:** actual events are intended as ontic; the state plays a
  predictive role on future potentialities.
- **Formation:** candidate physical actualization under an added law.
- **Issuance:** undecided. Stochastic nonunitary state evolution does not by
  itself defeat a richer completed-history or fixed-noise-source rival.
- **Empirical excess:** no quantitative delta from a matched ordinary quantum
  instrument was identified in the audited sources.

### Disposition

`PARTIAL_EVENT_SELECTION`.

ETH earns the only exact continuation because it puts a nontrivial selector
between state/future-algebra data and an actuality algebra. The continuation
must attack that selector's naturality, the generating-family ambiguity, and
physical uniqueness; it must not merely simulate the published example.

**Cheapest kill of the continuation:** two equally admissible
future-algebra co-filtrations of one frozen physical process yield
incompatible centers-of-centralizers and neither is independently selected.

**Positive threshold:** prove that the actuality-algebra quotient is invariant
under the admissible representation, restriction, automorphism, and
time-coarse-graining class fixed before calculation, and classify any
remaining generating-family freedom.

**Higher threshold:** derive rather than postulate the physically selected
future co-filtration and show a held-out operational consequence not already
fixed by the matched standard quantum process.

## Candidate 3 — Kent's Lorentzian quantum reality

### Exact positive construction

Kent's proposal starts from a closed quantum system with an initial state and
unitary dynamics. In the finite model, commuting mass-density observables are
mathematically measured at a late final time. One final outcome is sampled by
the Born rule. Intermediate beables are conditional expectations given
complementary final data, using a pre- and post-selected rule. The Lorentzian
version replaces the final-time slice with asymptotic data on suitable
spacelike hypersurfaces and defines local beables from data outside the
relevant future light cone.

The final measurement is explicitly a mathematical operation, not an
interaction with an external observer. A quasiclassical event is definite
when it leaves an effective record in the chosen asymptotic observable.

This gives a precise, single-world realist completion that can preserve
standard quantum dynamics and Lorentz covariance.

### Selected versus supplied

| Object | Status in the construction |
|---|---|
| initial state and unitary law | supplied |
| asymptotic/final boundary and limiting behavior | assumed |
| division into field or particle classes | supplied; several alternatives are acknowledged |
| final commuting observable | supplied; mass, stress-energy, charge, or photodetection variants exist |
| sampled final outcome | stochastic by the Born rule |
| intermediate beables | derived conditionally from the final outcome |
| quasiclassical event identity | inferred when final data retain an effective record |
| local retained record formation | not independently selected |
| access, certification, and public finality | not derived |

Kent explicitly notes nonuniqueness in the choice of beables, class
decompositions, and post-selection rules. The proposal selects one global
history after these choices. It does not select the choices from ordinary
unitary QFT.

### Scope and formation status

- **Perspective scope:** global/completion first; local beables are conditioned
  on the global asymptotic outcome.
- **Status:** beables are intended as ontic.
- **Formation:** disclosure from a sampled global completion.
- **Issuance:** not established. The framework is deliberately a one-world
  completion consistent with standard unitary dynamics.
- **Empirical excess:** the base proposal is designed to preserve standard
  quantum predictions. Kent discusses generalized beable-guided theories,
  but those are separate modifications.

### Disposition

`GLOBAL_COMPLETION_ONLY`.

Kent is a valuable hostile control because it shows that definite events and
a realist one-world history do not imply locally issued facts. The same
record-like appearance can be read from a globally sampled boundary.

**Cheapest kill of a local-formation claim:** change the admissible final
observable or field-class split while preserving the initial state and
unitary dynamics; the beable history changes.

**Exact reopener:** a naturality or uniqueness theorem selecting the final
observable, class partition, and boundary construction—or an empirical delta
from a specific beable-guided generalization.

## Cross-candidate collision

| Question | Eventum | ETH | Kent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Where is the classical/actual object? | commutative past output history | center-of-centralizer actuality algebra and a generating event family | one sampled beable completion |
| What selects its values? | stochastic output of chosen channel | added Collapse Postulate | Born-sampled final outcome |
| What selects its type/interface? | chosen output channel and nondemolition algebra | center-of-centralizer, conditional on chosen future algebra and state | chosen final observable, class split, and asymptotic rule |
| Local or global? | local output with global dilation | system-indexed event within supplied co-filtration | global completion with derived local beables |
| Is the quantum state ontic? | future dynamical/predictive state conditioned on past output | predictive state over future potentialities | generates probability over ontic beables |
| Complete record interface? | no | no | no |
| Public/regional finality? | no | no | no |
| Source issuance? | no | no | no |
| Excess prediction over matched QM? | none isolated | none isolated | none in base theory |

The approaches therefore expose a three-way distinction that Dynamic Unity
must retain:

```text
formed output history
    != algebraically selected actuality algebra
    != global one-world completion.
```

All three can be called “events.” They answer different questions.

## `HC-DU-090` — earned result

> **Conditional event-algebra selection and complete-interface
> nonidentification.** Among Eventum Mechanics, the ETH approach, and Kent's
> Lorentzian quantum reality construction, only ETH supplies a nontrivial
> algebra-native selector after the system, state, and future-algebra
> co-filtration are fixed: the center of the state's centralizer determines
> the candidate actuality algebra; a generating partition is unique only
> under additional conditions, and a collapse axiom selects a realized
> member. Eventum instead derives a consistent classical output
> history conditional on a supplied monitoring channel, while Kent derives
> local beables conditional on a supplied sampled final boundary. None of the
> three independently selects the complete record, provenance, access,
> action, certification, and regional-finality interface; none identifies
> source issuance; and none presently supplies a quantitative empirical delta
> over its matched standard-quantum account.

This is a scoped Grade-4 selection/nonselection boundary. The mathematics used
by ETH and the quantum filtering/conditioning mathematics are known. Dynamic
Unity's contribution is the typed comparison and exact placement of the
remaining burden. The result is not a novel event theorem and does not
validate any candidate ontology.

## Exact successor gate

Only one continuation has positive value of information:

### ETH actuality-algebra naturality and selection gate

Freeze one process and:

1. enumerate its physically admissible future-algebra co-filtrations,
   representations, restrictions, automorphisms, and time coarse-grainings;
2. compute \(Z_{\omega_t}(\mathcal E_{\ge t})\) unchanged in every arm;
3. quotient actuality algebras by the declared physical equivalence;
4. test whether one actuality-algebra quotient is invariant and nontrivial;
5. classify generating-event-family ambiguity before applying the stochastic
   collapse law;
6. compare the resulting multi-time process with a matched standard quantum
   instrument without refitting; and
7. stop before provenance, access, or public finality unless the lower gate
   passes.

Return one of:

```text
NATURAL_EVENT_QUOTIENT
ALGEBRA_RELATIVE_EVENT_AMBIGUITY
STANDARD_INSTRUMENT_ABSORPTION
NO_PHYSICAL_COFILTRATION_SELECTOR
```

The cheapest exact finite arena is a small matrix algebra with two admissible
nested future subalgebras and one frozen state/dynamics. Local symbolic
calculation can decide the algebraic gate. No external hardware is relevant.

## What not to do next

- Do not build a generic collapse simulation.
- Do not repeat the existing selector-orbit, Galois-closure, or
  completion-fibre theorem.
- Do not infer a durable archive from an Abelian event algebra.
- Do not infer observer access from a system-indexed event.
- Do not infer regional consensus from a sequence of local collapses.
- Do not treat the ETH state/event distinction as evidence that records are
  fundamental.
- Do not use stochasticity as evidence of source issuance.
- Do not compare predictions until the matched standard instrument and all
  supplied interface choices are frozen.

## North-Star implication

The tournament improves the upstream side of the North Star without changing
its overall priority:

```text
physical dynamics
  -> candidate actuality algebra     [ETH partially reaches this]
  -> realized event                  [ETH postulates a stochastic law]
  -> retained provenance-bearing record
  -> access and action interface
  -> regional/public certification
  -> reconstruction or finite remainder.
```

The important learning is not that “ETH is the answer.” It is that a serious
event theory can select an event algebra while still leaving record
architecture and observer access unselected. That narrows the missing bridge
from “derive events somehow” to “test whether the physical algebraic
antecedent is itself unique, then type the passage from event to record.”
