# Ten-lens efficient-approach registry

## Executive result

This panel does **not** select, eliminate, defer, or rerank the 36 technical
hypotheses. It answers a different question:

> If a lead chooses any one of these hypotheses, what is the smartest first
> attack, what work can be reused, and what method avoids brute force while
> maximizing useful learning?

The ten lenses converge on one operational answer:

1. freeze the meaning of the claim before calculating;
2. construct the smallest object on which it can succeed or fail;
3. quotient symmetries and exploit exact structure before sweeping parameters;
4. ask one counterfactual contrast at a time;
5. emit a reusable proof object, countermodel, calibrated fixture, or resource
   bound regardless of the result; and
6. increase physical scale or experimental cost only after the predicted
   distinction is calculable on the frozen interface.

The result is a one-to-one method attachment for every hypothesis ID. All 36
remain live at their existing grades. The earlier quadratic vote remains
unchanged and is not used here as an execution order.

## 1. The ten lenses

| ID | Lens | Method instruction it contributes |
|---|---|---|
| `CAT` | Category theorist | Find the typed object, universal property, invariant, and composition law; solve equivalent hypotheses through one construction and test naturality under representation changes. |
| `CFS` | Counterfactual statistician | State the estimand and rival interventions first; use matched contrasts, sequential value of information, negative controls, and held-out predictions instead of undirected sweeps. |
| `FCE` | Formal counterexample engineer | Quotient symmetries, synthesize the smallest witness with SAT/SMT/model checking or exhaustive canonical enumeration, and preserve a proof certificate. |
| `EMA` | Experimental metrology architect | Calculate identifiability and effect-to-error margin before building; freeze calibration, all ports, provenance, and a complete outcome record. |
| `RPO` | Research-portfolio and operations scientist | Reuse one contract and a small fixture library, batch compatible tests, cache intermediate artifacts, and make every run produce a transferable output. |
| `ORT` | Orthodox professor | Put the strongest standard model and known theorem in executable form first so novelty and residuals are measured against a serious absorber. |
| `HET` | Heterodox professor | Change one usually hidden assumption at a time, search for counterfamilies and dual formalisms, and distinguish failure of a formalization from failure of the concept. |
| `COM` | Commercial scientist | Define the decision-enabling output, build the thinnest vertical slice, and keep interfaces stable enough that another lead can reuse the result immediately. |
| `WILD` | Wild-frontier scientist | Preserve the profound conjunction, but make its first incarnation a tiny discriminating toy model with an invariance audit and a route to a risky prediction. |
| `PHIL` | Philosopher of science | Separate operational, mathematical, physical, and ontological conclusions; expose underdetermination and prohibit changing the meaning of “record,” “public,” or “actual” after the result. |

These lenses are advisers, not voters. Their agreement is not evidence.

## 2. Shared method kits

The registry avoids 36 bespoke workflows. A lead composes a small number of
reusable kits while retaining the hypothesis-specific contrast below.

| Kit | Reusable method | Standard output |
|---|---|---|
| `K0` | **Semantic and estimand lock.** Freeze the typed process, record instrument, provenance, access/adversary class, action, representation group, rival, and exact claim grade. | machine-readable contract digest and claim-to-observable map |
| `K1` | **Symmetry-reduced finite search.** Canonicalize labels, quotient automorphisms, use SAT/SMT/model checking or exact enumeration, and minimize the first witness. | smallest countermodel or finite exhaustive certificate |
| `K2` | **Proof-carrying algebra/compiler.** Prefer exact symbolic maps, fixed points, equalizers, rewriting, game fixpoints, and independently verifiable proof objects. | theorem conditions, algorithm, certificate, and tight example |
| `K3` | **Counterfactual sequential design.** Compare matched interventions, specify negative/positive controls, and choose the next observation by expected discrimination when predictive models permit it. | causal contrast, uncertainty update, and next-intervention rule |
| `K4` | **Authenticated distributed twin.** Reuse one replayable DAG/database/BFT fixture with provenance, adversarial schedules, safe actions, resource accounting, and complete logs. | protocol trace, attack witness, and capability delta |
| `K5` | **Routed quantum twin.** Reuse one process-tensor/channel fixture with every control port, route, memory, decoder, history record, standard-quantum null, and coherent/dephased controls. | reconstructed process, null residual, and logical/action algebra |
| `K6` | **Metrology preflight and sequential assay.** Compute the predicted separation margin, calibration/error budget, optimal control, positive control, and sample rule before physical execution. | preregistered assay passport and calibrated outcome record |
| `K7` | **Scaling and universality toolkit.** Use analytic bifurcation or asymptotics first, then targeted continuation, up/down sweeps, held-out sizes, and rival scaling laws. | phase/scaling discriminator with uncertainty and finite-size controls |
| `K8` | **Resource and provenance accounting.** Compare matched protocols, charge support/memory/coherence/trust/latency, and pair lower bounds with constructions where possible. | complete ledger, lower/upper bound, and resource-achieving specimen |
| `K9` | **Reconstruction and uniqueness ladder.** Generate nonisomorphic countermodels first, add assumptions one at a time, require uniqueness up to declared gauge, and reserve held-out predictions. | assumption-minimal reconstruction or explicit irreducible remainder |

`K0` accompanies every hypothesis. The other kits are tools, not priority
stages. A lead chooses the smallest composition that answers its hypothesis.
The cross-repo hardening contract
`../lab/process/cross-repo-hardening-control-contract.md` accompanies `K0` as
a scoped adversary overlay: apply only the relevant `XH-01..06` controls,
retain their source grades, and mark the rest `not-applicable`.

## 3. Standard lead packet

Before starting work, the lead should be able to fill this one-page packet:

```text
hypothesis ID and exact grade
frozen typed claim and counterfactual estimand
smallest informative object
selected method kits
strongest absorber/null and one positive control
applicable XH-01..06 controls and frozen completion/refinement/resource frame
first decisive calculation or intervention
artifact emitted even if the focal effect is absent
condition for increasing model or experimental scale
brute-force / semantic-refit trap to avoid
```

If the first decisive contrast cannot yet be written, the efficient work is
to repair the contract—not to enlarge the computation.

## 5. Cross-hypothesis efficiencies

Several methods can be computed once and reused without forcing the
hypotheses into one project:

- The `K0` contract digest should use one schema across all 36 IDs.
- `XH-01..06` should be attached by scope rather than copied wholesale:
  fixed-oracle and completion controls for generativity/sufficiency,
  departed-support and exact matched-frame controls for capability,
  same-SBS-data only for quantum public facts, and benign-refinement naturality
  for geometry/topology. The constructor inventory is a baseline, never a
  declaration that all physical completions have been enumerated.
- `HG-B`, `DC-A`, `DC-B`, `DB-A`, `QI-B`, and `FM-A` can share loop,
  equalizer, and fixed-algebra primitives while retaining different theorem
  statements.
- `AD-A`, `AD-B`, `DB-B`, `MC-A`, `MC-B`, `NW-A`, and `NW-B` can share the
  authenticated distributed twin and saved adversarial schedules.
- `QI-A`, `QI-B`, `QE-A`, `QE-B`, `EM-A`, `EM-B`, and `CG-B` can share the
  routed quantum process schema and implementation-complete null.
- `IT-A`, `CT-A`, `CT-B`, and `CP-A` can share a finite
  intervention/action quotient library.
- `MC-A`, `SP-A`, and `SP-B` can share continuation and finite-size tooling;
  `TR-A` and `TR-B` should always share the same resource ledger and matched
  protocol pair.
- `CG-A` and `CG-B` should consume frozen outputs from other fixtures when
  available, but their reconstruction methods remain usable whenever a lead
  chooses them; the registry does not defer them.

These are infrastructure economies, not a scientific claim that the mapped
hypotheses are equivalent.

## 6. What “higher learning rate” means here

No universal scalar score is imposed. When predictive probabilities are
available, a lead may use expected information gain to choose the next
intervention. Otherwise report this non-ordinal vector:

```text
branch discrimination:
    which rival outcomes this move separates
semantic stability:
    whether the same frozen contract survives every comparison
reusability:
    which other hypotheses can consume the artifact
decisiveness:
    proof/counterexample/bound/calibrated contrast versus suggestive fit
cost and reversibility:
    compute, apparatus, time, and irreversible commitments
```

The best approach is the one that produces the sharpest reusable distinction
for the least irreversible work—not necessarily the approach with the
smallest runtime.

## 7. Method selector for upcoming swings

An upcoming swing not yet present in the 36-item portfolio can use the same
system:

| Shape of the question | Default efficient composition |
|---|---|
| definition, invariance, composition, or exact correspondence | `K0+K1+K2` |
| finite impossibility, insufficiency, or adversarial claim | `K0+K1+K2`, with the smallest countermodel and an independently checked certificate |
| distributed protocol or public-finality claim | `K0+K3+K4`, adding `K1/K2` for a bound or theorem and `K8` for resource or provenance claims |
| coherent-history, channel, or logical-algebra claim | `K0+K3+K5`, adding `K1/K2` for exact finite structure |
| physical metrology proposal | `K0+K3+K5+K6`; the process twin computes the separation margin before apparatus work |
| phase, threshold, or universality claim | `K0+K3+K7`, using analytic bifurcation/asymptotics before simulation |
| capability-cost or actualization-cost claim | `K0+K3+K8`, with the positive law and ordinary-accounting null on the same matched protocol |
| geometry or ontology reconstruction | `K0+K1+K2+K9`, beginning with nonunique countermodels rather than a favored reconstruction |

For a compound swing, do not merge claims into one vague success condition.
Give every leg its own decisive contrast, reuse one frozen contract and
fixture where possible, and let the outputs compose. That preserves the
economy of one run without making the conclusions rise or fall together.

## 8. Custody and limits

The machine-readable one-to-one attachment is:

`tests/artifacts/du_hypothesis_efficient_approach_registry_result.json`

It is validated by:

`tests/du_hypothesis_efficient_approach_registry_probe.py`

This registry:

- changes no hypothesis statement, owner, grade, vote, or rank;
- declares no hypothesis unworthy or closed;
- does not authorize an experiment or external action;
- supplies no scientific or novelty evidence; and
- may be revised whenever a lead discovers a demonstrably better method.
