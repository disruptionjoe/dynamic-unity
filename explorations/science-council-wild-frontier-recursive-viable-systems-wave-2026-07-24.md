### 1.2 Primary antecedent audit

The VSM sources do not license the precursor's strongest language.

- [Beer](https://www.kybernetik.ch/dwn/Viable_System_Model.pdf) presents a
  cybernetic model of independently viable organizations, with complete viable
  systems recurring inside System One units. The same treatment allows
  arbitrary boundaries, nonunique recursion, and participation in multiple
  recursive chains. It does not supply one objectively privileged physical
  hierarchy.
- Beer has a named **Recursive System Theorem**, management axioms, and a law
  of cohesion. I did not locate a formal theorem with specified physical state
  spaces and assumptions whose conclusion is “a system without System 4
  cannot be viable.” The 1984 paper describes its own task as a methodological
  synopsis rather than a new demonstration of validity. The precursor's phrase
  **“Beer viability theorem”** is therefore a stronger interpretation, not an
  imported physical theorem.
- [Ashby's law of requisite
  variety](https://ashby.info/Ashby-Introduction-to-Cybernetics.pdf) supplies a
  quantitative regulation bound within its disturbance/regulator setting. It
  is not sufficient for boundary reality, active adaptation, or five-function
  organization.
- [Wilson's RG](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.47.773) supplies
  scale-dependent effective descriptions and flows. Repeated coarse-graining
  is not active viability unless a downward intervention and survival
  advantage are separately shown.
- [Hoel, Albantakis, and
  Tononi](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1314922110) show that effective
  information can peak at a macro description for some finite transition
  systems. That is a causal-abstraction criterion, not a regulator or unique
  hierarchy.
- [Friston](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2013.0475) offers a heuristic result
  under ergodic random-dynamical and Markov-blanket assumptions. A conditional
  independence boundary is not by itself a controller, observer, or durable
  record.
- [Palacios et
  al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2019.110089) construct two-level
  hierarchical Markov-blanket self-organization when microscopic elements
  have the relevant priors and attractor dynamics. They explicitly do not
  claim that every coupled system becomes hierarchical.
- [Jansma and Hoel](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02649) provide a modern
  multiscale causal-contribution framework. Its useful opportunity is
  discovering causally important scales, not equating causal contribution with
  operational viability.
