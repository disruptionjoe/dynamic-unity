## Executive decision

Five independent successor-persona agents began from the complete first-wave
corpus: all five prior memos, all three executable probes, the fifty-six-item
divergent index, the integrated synthesis, and the four Condorcet receipts.

The wave found a real opportunity that the first integration had understated:

> **Classical sequential growth already contains an exact covariant
> history-to-effective-law transformation at a causal post. For the selected
> factorial coupling family it conditionally produces a square-root hierarchy.**

That is not yet the Dynamic Unity result we want. The wave also found the
precise limits:

- the post map is established causal-set antecedent, not a DU derivation;
- the square-root exponent is selected by the factorial bare-coupling tail,
  not by post conditioning alone;
- the factorial construction assumes the posts needed for its cosmological
  cycle rather than establishing their recurrence;
- a post is a global history condition, so an online trigger needs a separate
  finite-stem or filtration-safe formulation;
- the effective coupling is a dimensionless conditional law, not curvature,
  vacuum stress, a physical unit, or `Lambda`;
- a fixed-history replay defeats online updates within one era, but cannot
  erase inherited causal memory or identify whether the frozen law was
  fundamental;
- the existing de-Sitter-like evidence is finite and estimator-sensitive; and
- raw-to-physical Fisher retention is not order-identifiable without a
  justified microhistory fibre.

The wave therefore changes the immediate research sequence without promoting
a physics claim:

1. **Build a lean, literature-aware post passport and measure post
   generativity/recurrence across the factorial and nearby tail families.**
2. **Run a preregistered held-out geometry tournament only on laws that
   jointly produce posts and nontrivial running.**
3. **Only for a survivor, test cross-history interventions or physical
   coupling records, then response, units, and cosmology.**

Broad generic enumeration is now demand-driven infrastructure, not the
research product. No claim is banked or seeded.

## What the mathematics now says

### 1. A lawful history transform exists

For a fixed CSG coupling sequence `t=(t_0,t_1,...)`, conditioning on a post
with invariant past size `P` gives, in the physical post sector,

```text
T_n(P) = sum_(k=0)^P binom(P,k) t_(n+k),  n>=1.
```

The maps compose as a binomial semigroup:

```text
M^A M^B = M^(A+B).
```

Geometric couplings `t_n=t^n` are projective fixed points. This is
categorically different from changing the fundamental coupling directly as a
function of birth stage. The first-wave natural-labeling counterexample still
kills the latter; it does not kill conditional post memory.

### 2. The square root is real but family-conditional

For:

```text
t_n = u^n/(n!)^alpha,
```

a fixed-`n`, large-`P` saddle estimate gives:

```text
T_(n+1)/T_n
    ~ u^(1/(alpha+1)) P^(-alpha/(alpha+1)).
```

The corrected physical `T_2/T_1` finite transform returns:

| `alpha` | Predicted exponent | Fitted exponent |
|---:|---:|---:|
| `0` | `0` | `0.000000` |
| `1/2` | `-1/3` | `-0.337891` |
| `1` | `-1/2` | `-0.498091` |
| `2` | `-2/3` | `-0.649039` |

Therefore post conditioning alone does not explain why the North-Star
exponent is `1/2`. It turns that exponent into a discriminator for the bare
coupling-tail class. A rigorous arbitrary-tail universality theorem remains
open.

This mechanism is distinct from the additive/summable-correlation CLT
half-power already carried in Lane 1.3. The two mechanisms share an exponent,
not yet a physical observable.

### 3. Historical generation is neither a forbidden clock nor creation from nothing

Three uses of a large count must be separated:

```text
externally scheduled or fitted N0
    answer insertion / stopped

invariant P generated by a specified causal history
    admissible historical input

P selected retrospectively because it gives the desired epoch
    selection failure / stopped
```

The post map can transform an order-one bare coupling and a genuinely realized
large history into a small effective coupling. That is real historical
large-number transduction. It still leaves the distribution and typicality of
`P`, the occurrence of the post, and the microscopic-to-physical map open.

### 4. Memory is not the same as active feedback

At one post past `P_0`, two models can have the same complete projective
post-era law:

```text
historical model:
    Q_H(P_0) = (T_n(P_0)/T_1(P_0))_(n>=1)

frozen model:
    Q_F = Q_H(P_0).
```

No post-era observation can distinguish their origin while the complete law
is identical. A fixed-history replay therefore tests whether online coupling
updates are required inside that era. It does not test away the dependency of
`Q_H` on prehistory. Comparing matched histories with different `P`, bare law
held fixed, does distinguish the models.

The current standard post map is consequently:

```text
invariant causal memory / conditional hysteresis: supported
online within-era feedback: not supported
punctuated post-to-post feedback: open
source-owned active return arrow: open.
```

### 5. The information-bottleneck winner loses its current selector

The orthodox hidden-fibre construction holds the physical down-set
probability law and its Fisher information fixed while refining an unobserved
raw microhistory fibre. The ratio:

```text
F_downset/F_raw
```

can then be made arbitrarily smaller without changing the physical order law.
It is coordinate invariant for one chosen raw model but is not identified by
the order alone.

This does not kill physical transition Fisher information. It kills use of a
raw-retention fraction as an order-only dimension selector before the raw
ontology or quotient/fibre experiment is justified. `WF-D12` is therefore
downstream of `PHIL-D2`/`ORTH-SD4`, not the immediate intriguing build.
