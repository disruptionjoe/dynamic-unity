# Stage, relational resource, persistence, and the causal-response host gate

## Executive result

The proposed linear slogan

```text
record
  -> marked stage
  -> symmetry-reducing resource
  -> persistent carrier
```

does not follow universally from the three typed Dynamic Unity results.

An ordinary classical occurrence flag can be formed and retained without
carrying any nontrivial symmetry character. A stable relational coupling can
support a bounded action without forming a historical archive. A preloaded
reference can persist without certifying when or whether it was formed.

The surviving theorem is target-scoped:

> A horizon archive exactly certifies both a declared formation/provenance
> target and a declared relational-character target exactly when its
> equivalence classes refine both target partitions. Persistence is
> target-relative: the horizon dynamics may erase arbitrary nuisance detail
> but may not merge archive states carrying different declared target
> values.

The three declared target conditions are logically independent in the
abstract finite fixture:

1. **stage:** which occurrence or composition cut the distinction belongs to;
2. **resource:** which interaction or asymmetric carrier makes the declared
   nontrivial relational distinction physically available; and
3. **persistence:** which physical support retains the target-bearing
   distinction through the declared horizon.

The earlier Dynamic Unity results compose only for a correspondingly scoped
object:

```text
retained provenance record
of a nontrivial relational character
over a declared continuation horizon
```

They do not form one universal record law without a shared physical domain
and one fixed meaning of resource. The fixture does not prove that three
independently realizable physical mechanisms exist.

This gives the proposed common causal-response object a precise gate. A
quantum response functional and its hyperbolic linearization may supply the
relational response and causal propagation. They do not thereby select the
formation stage or the material archive. Certified Causal Response Theory is
therefore preserved as a high-ceiling candidate theory under the ratified
North Star, not installed as a new North Star or physical claim.

## 1. Duplicate and scope gate

Three component results were already banked.

### 1.1 Stage

`HC-DU-172` proves that if an instrument assignment over a bare channel must
preserve the same earlier-stage labels under every downstream channel, every
component is a process-independent coin multiple of the channel. The result
requires the declared downstream-naturality contract. It establishes:

> informative stage-local provenance requires a marked cut, selected
> classical sector, restricted continuation class, selected dilation, or
> another stage-bearing antecedent.

It does not establish that every physical distinction must be stored as an
explicit stage label.

### 1.2 Relational resource

`HC-DU-175` proves that a fully \(U(1)^2\)-covariant process cannot create a
nontrivial relative-character mode from a locally invariant input. A
number-conserving exchange interaction creates that mode by reducing
independent local \(U(1)^2\) to diagonal \(U(1)\), without choosing an
absolute phase.

The theorem concerns records or actions whose target contains that
nontrivial character. It is not a necessity theorem for invariant classical
records.

### 1.3 Persistence and provenance

The epoch/archive results prove two different boundaries:

- a terminal value does not certify whether a write occurred; and
- exact repeated set/retain/reset semantics cannot be implemented on one
  closed reversible carrier over every admitted input.

The missing distinction must be retained in restricted input structure,
exported information, fresh support, bounded semantics, or an admitted
approximation. These are horizon and implementation claims, not stage or
symmetry-selection claims.

Re-crediting the three ingredients as one new universal theorem would
overstate their common domain.

## 2. Scoped joint-target theorem

Let \(X\) be a finite set of complete histories. Freeze:

\[
s:X\to S
\]

as the formation/provenance target,

\[
c:X\to C_\chi
\]

as a nontrivial relational-character target, and

\[
t=(s,c):X\to S\times C_\chi
\]

as their joint target.

Let

\[
a_\tau:X\to A_\tau
\]

be the archive at the declared formation stage and let

\[
d:A_\tau\to A_H,
\qquad
a_H=d\circ a_\tau
\]

be the archive evolution to the declared horizon.

For any map \(f\), write

\[
\ker f
=
\{(x,y)\in X^2:f(x)=f(y)\}.
\]

### Theorem 1 — joint certification

The horizon archive \(a_H\) exactly certifies \(t=(s,c)\) iff

\[
\ker a_H
\subseteq
\ker s\cap\ker c.
\]

Equivalently:

\[
t=\bar t\circ a_H
\]

for a unique map \(\bar t:a_H(X)\to t(X)\).

### Proof

If \(t=\bar t\circ a_H\), equality of archive values implies equality of
joint target values, and therefore equality of both component values.

Conversely, if equality of archive values implies equality of both \(s\) and
\(c\), define

\[
\bar t(a_H(x))=(s(x),c(x)).
\]

The kernel condition makes this definition independent of the chosen
representative. Uniqueness holds on \(a_H(X)\). \(\square\)

This is ordinary factorization/sufficient-statistic mathematics. Its value
here is typing the joint target; it is not a new general theorem.

### Theorem 2 — target-relative persistence

Assume \(a_\tau\) already certifies \(t\). It induces a target map

\[
\bar t_\tau:a_\tau(X)\to t(X).
\]

The horizon archive remains exact iff

\[
\ker\!\left(d|_{a_\tau(X)}\right)
\subseteq
\ker\bar t_\tau.
\]

Thus \(d\) need not be injective on the complete stage archive. It may erase
calibration labels, implementation detail, or any other nuisance coordinate.
It must only remain injective **between different declared target classes**.

This corrects the stronger but false slogan that persistence requires global
microscopic injectivity.

## 3. Exact independence controls

Take four histories

\[
X=\{(s,c):s,c\in\{0,1\}\}.
\]

### 3.1 Stage plus persistence, no relational character

Let

\[
a_H(s,c)=s.
\]

The archive exactly retains the stage target and is blind to \(c\). It is a
stable occurrence flag with no nontrivial character record.

Therefore:

```text
STAGE + PERSISTENCE -/-> RELATIONAL RESOURCE
```

### 3.2 Relational character plus persistence, no stage provenance

Let

\[
a_H(s,c)=c.
\]

The archive retains the relational target and is blind to formation stage.
This is the finite analogue of a persistent preloaded reference: the
relation exists, but its present value does not identify when or whether it
was formed in the admitted interval.

Therefore:

```text
RESOURCE + PERSISTENCE -/-> STAGE PROVENANCE
```

### 3.3 Stage plus character, no horizon persistence

Let

\[
a_\tau(s,c)=(s,c),
\qquad
a_H(s,c)=0.
\]

The complete joint distinction is present at the marked stage and absent
from the declared horizon archive.

Therefore:

```text
STAGE + RESOURCE -/-> PERSISTENCE
```

Physical reversibility does not require that the information vanish from the
global completion; it may be relocated outside the admitted archive. The
control concerns the declared record boundary.

### 3.4 Supplied all-three positive

Let

\[
a_H(s,c)=(s,c).
\]

The supplied architecture retains the complete target. This proves
compatibility and sufficiency inside the fixture, not dynamic selection of
the stage, resource, carrier, or horizon.

### 3.5 Nuisance-erasure positive

Add a nuisance bit \(n\) and take

\[
a_\tau(s,c,n)=(s,c,n),
\qquad
a_H(s,c,n)=(s,c).
\]

The horizon map is noninjective but preserves the complete declared target.
Microscopic loss is not automatically record failure.

### 3.6 Target-merger hostile

Taking

\[
a_H(s,c,n)=s\oplus c
\]

merges distinct target classes and fails exact certification.

### 3.7 Persistent endpoint without occurrence provenance

The stale and freshly written histories

\[
(W_{\rm occurred},v_H)=(0,1)
\quad\text{and}\quad
(1,1)
\]

share the same persistent terminal value. No decoder of that value can
identify whether the write occurred.

These controls prove logical independence of the three typed obligations.
They do not claim that the finite product fixture is a microscopic physical
theory.

## 4. The correct physical conjunction

For a **retained provenance record of a nontrivial relational character**,
the three prior results can be composed without changing their scopes:

1. **Stage-bearing antecedent.** The declared occurrence must survive the
   admitted continuation semantics. A bare end-to-end arrow cannot provide
   that cut for free under arbitrary downstream naturality.
2. **Character-bearing resource.** From a symmetry-invariant input, the
   required nontrivial mode must enter through an asymmetric resource,
   symmetry-reducing interaction, boundary, controller, or retained
   correlation.
3. **Target-bearing persistence.** The physical evolution to the declared
   horizon must not merge the relevant stage/character classes. It may erase
   every nuisance direction.

This is a typed conjunction of three conditional necessity results. It is
not one new universal law and does not establish that records are
fundamental.

\[

or vacuum response; a gauge-fixed Hessian producing a hyperbolic response

It does not follow that it supplies:

- a marked formation stage rather than one chosen factorization;

```text

```

No arrow may be inferred from shared notation.
