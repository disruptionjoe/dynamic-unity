# Record Formation → Certified Composition

## Plain-English result

This wave found a sharper architecture for the project, but it did not find
new physics.

A physical interaction can select a stable distinction between histories
without selecting the measurement that turns that distinction into a record.
A measurement can then create a certificate about the *relationship among
histories* without revealing which history occurred. When certificates from
different regions are composed, three independent questions have to be
answered:

1. do repeated labels refer to the same typed physical operation;
2. do all the local records possess one globally compatible realization; and
3. does the exported certificate preserve the action the next layer needs?

Pairwise agreement answers none of these questions by itself. On tree-like
covers, matching overlap distributions can be glued. On cyclic covers, every
pair can agree while no global history exists. A scalar “distance from
compatibility” can even change when a causally inert relay is inserted, while
the actual obstruction remains unchanged.

The quantum-strength branch then supplied the decisive negative control.
Applying only probability-level incompatibility and independent composition
recovers the known Local Orthogonality/Consistent Exclusivity principle. It
excludes two PR boxes but admits almost-quantum correlations. Probability-level
finality is therefore too coarse to select quantum theory.

The most credible integrated research target is consequently:

> **Derive a physically formed sharp record instrument, then determine when
> pairwise compatible instances descend to one global action-sufficient
> instrument.**

The potentially distinctive part is not the abstract pairwise-to-global rule.
It is deriving the class of instruments to which the rule applies from
formation, repeatability, nondisturbance, provenance, continuation, access,
and resources.

## Executed result B — recursive composition is a vector contract

Starting from independently formed interfaces, the regional branch established
three exact finite components.

### Typed overlap identity

For an independently supplied evidence equivalence \(E\) and predeclared typed
signatures \(\sigma_t\), the unique greatest warranted identity relation is

\[
E^\star=E\cap\bigcap_t\ker\sigma_t.
\]

The required types depend on the upper task. Equality of effects does not
promote itself to equality of selective instruments, multi-time histories,
provenance, or actions.

For total-variation discrepancies, identity errors along a path are
subadditive and common stochastic postprocessing cannot increase them. The
finite fixture saturates the triangle bound.

### Full-cover compatibility

Matching separator marginals glue exactly on a finite join tree. Cycles need a
full-cover primal/dual or equivalent obstruction receipt. The sharp triangle
control has pairwise overlap agreement and valid pair records

```text
A = B
B = C
C != A
```

but no global extension.

### Action sufficiency

If an actual response is within \(\alpha\) of a simulator through the first
boundary summary and that simulator is within \(\beta\) of one through the
second summary, the composite action defect is at most
\(\alpha+\beta\). The fixture attains the bound \(1/4+1/4=1/2\).

The regional contract is therefore not one scalar:

\[
\Delta_{\rm recursive}
=
\left(
\Delta_{\rm typed\ identity},
\ \text{full-cover marginal receipt or obstruction},
\ \Delta_{\rm upper\ action}
\right).
\]

The identity and action coordinates have path bounds. The marginal coordinate
must be recomputed on the full glued cover.

An inert subdivision of a frustrated \(n\)-cycle preserves its
\(\mathbb Z_2\) obstruction and extension verdict but changes the naive
per-edge \(L_\infty\) distance from \(1/3\) to \(1/4\). Thus a scalar
compatibility cost is not refinement-natural unless benign relays are first
collapsed or edges carry independently justified physical weights.

Finally, recursive certificates can preserve a declared parity-class action
while remaining noninjective on the underlying histories. This is exact
functional non-disclosure, not a zero-knowledge protocol or physical secrecy
claim.

These are known kernel-intersection, total-variation, join-tree, marginal,
parity-obstruction, and sufficient-statistic components. The useful program
increment is their unchanged typed recursive contract and the explicit
refinement-naturality counterexample.

## Executed result C — the probability-level quantum selector closes

The most natural probability-level finality principle says:

1. records that assign different outputs to the same local measurement are
   incompatible;
2. a pairwise incompatible event family has probability sum at most one; and
3. independent regions preserve the rule under products.

On Bell events this is Local Orthogonality, or the Bell specialization of
Consistent Exclusivity.

- One PR box is valid and no-signalling, has `CHSH=4`, and passes the
  single-copy rule.
- Two independent PR boxes contain a five-event locally orthogonal family
  with total probability \(5/4\), so product composition excludes them.
- Ordinary quantum theory satisfies the rule.
- The almost-quantum set also satisfies it.

```text

NEW DYNAMIC UNITY PRINCIPLE: NO — IT IS LO/CE
```
