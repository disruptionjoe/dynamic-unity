# Preferred-Leaf Descent and the Branch-Invariant Certified Causal Reality Kernel

## Result in plain English

A hidden global ordering can settle which spacelike event is “first” in a
God's-eye description. That is real additional completion structure under the
held assumption. But it does not, by itself, create or select a physical
record, an observer boundary, an archive, a finality rule, an action, a
resource budget, or a measurable target.

Once those physical and operational objects are matched, the test is exact:
forget the preferred ordering of causally independent events. If every
load-bearing map is unchanged by that forgetting, then the preferred-leaf and
causal-partial-order descriptions give exactly the same reconstruction,
capability, finality, and held-out predictions. If something observable
changes, either the comparison omitted a physical premise or the preferred
foliation leaks into an observer-accessible process.

The exactly compensated branch excludes the second option by definition.
Therefore this series returns:

```text
BRANCH_INVARIANT_CERTIFIED_CAUSAL_REALITY_KERNEL
NO_EXCESS_CONTENT
NORTH_STAR_PORTFOLIO_RESET
```

This does **not** prove that a preferred foliation does not exist. It proves
that an exactly compensated one supplies no missing North-Star arrow after
complete matching.

## 1. Frozen typed contract

Let:

- \(\widetilde X\) be complete histories carrying a preferred-leaf order;
- \(X\) be the corresponding causal-partial-order histories;
- \(\pi:\widetilde X\twoheadrightarrow X\) forget only the ordering and
  simultaneity labels of causally independent events;
- \(\widetilde{\mathcal P}\) be the complete multi-time intervention process;
- \(\widetilde r\) be the formed record together with provenance;
- \(\widetilde a\) be observer access and admissible intervention structure;
- \(\widetilde f\) be regional finality under a declared rival/fault contract;
- \(\widetilde c\) be the action/capability envelope;
- \(\widetilde h\) be resource accounting; and
- \(\widetilde t\) be the held-out target.

The comparison is complete only when each object has a causal-order
counterpart:

\[
\mathcal P,\ r,\ a,\ f,\ c,\ h,\ t
\]

such that:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\widetilde{\mathcal P}&=\mathcal P\circ\pi,\\
\widetilde r&=r\circ\pi,\\
\widetilde a&=a\circ\pi,\\
\widetilde f&=f\circ\pi,\\
\widetilde c&=c\circ\pi,\\
\widetilde h&=h\circ\pi,\\
\widetilde t&=t\circ\pi.
\end{aligned}
\]

For a stochastic or quantum process, the first equality means equality of
the **complete operational kernel** for every admitted multi-time instrument,
not merely equality of terminal probabilities. Set-valued access and action
families must also be constant on \(\pi\)-fibres.

The held preferred foliation is exactly compensated only at this complete
operational level. Calling two branches “empirically equivalent” while
changing an instrument, archive route, observer boundary, reset lineage,
resource horizon, or action family would not satisfy the contract.

For an observer-accessible target already contained in the complete process
signature, descent of \(\widetilde{\mathcal P}\) forces descent of that target.
The explicit target map remains in the ledger to prevent a hidden
preferred-leaf quantity from being substituted for the previously frozen

## 2. Preferred-leaf descent theorem

Define the complete observer-operational bundle:

\[
\widetilde\Omega
=
(\widetilde{\mathcal P},
  \widetilde r,
  \widetilde a,
  \widetilde f,
  \widetilde c,
  \widetilde h,
  \widetilde t).
\]

### Theorem — complete operational descent

For a surjective foliation-forgetting map
\(\pi:\widetilde X\twoheadrightarrow X\), the following are equivalent:

1. there is a bundle \(\Omega\) on \(X\) such that
   \(\widetilde\Omega=\Omega\circ\pi\);
2. every component of \(\widetilde\Omega\) is constant on every
   \(\pi\)-fibre; and
3. histories differing only in the preferred ordering of causally independent
   events are indistinguishable by the complete declared process, records,
   access, finality, actions, resources, and targets.

When these conditions hold, every observer-accessible result factors through
the causal-partial-order history. The preferred leaf narrows hidden
completions but adds no observer-accessible content.

### Proof

If \(\widetilde\Omega=\Omega\circ\pi\), then equal \(\pi\)-images plainly have
equal \(\widetilde\Omega\)-values.

Conversely, if \(\widetilde\Omega\) is constant on every \(\pi\)-fibre, define
\(\Omega(x)=\widetilde\Omega(\widetilde x)\) for any
\(\widetilde x\in\pi^{-1}(x)\). Fibre constancy makes the definition
independent of the representative, and surjectivity makes it total. The third
condition is simply the operational reading of the first two. \(\square\)

This is ordinary quotient factorization. The Dynamic Unity value is the
typed list of maps that must descend before the hidden ordering can be called
operationally inert.

## 3. Reconstruction is branch-invariant

Suppose the record and target descend:

\[
\widetilde r=r\circ\pi,
\qquad
\widetilde t=t\circ\pi.
\]

Then:

\[
\widetilde t=d\circ\widetilde r
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
t=d\circ r.
\]

The forward direction uses surjectivity: choose a preferred representative of
each causal history. The reverse direction follows by composition.
Equivalently:

\[
\ker\widetilde r\subseteq\ker\widetilde t
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\ker r\subseteq\ker t.
\]

The same argument applies to every frozen action-indexed target family. The
range of the target on a preferred-leaf record fibre is exactly the range on
the corresponding causal-order record fibre. Therefore:

- a reconstruction theorem cannot be gained merely by adding an inaccessible
  preferred ordering;
- an existing record-relative remainder cannot be removed merely by choosing
  one hidden linear extension; and
- capability enlargement refines the same response-equivalence classes on
  both branches.

A target such as “preferred-leaf rank” does not descend. But in the exactly
compensated branch it is not observer-accessible, so failure to reconstruct it
is a hidden-completion difference rather than a North-Star physical
remainder.

## 4. The first-failure converse

Suppose two preferred histories satisfy:

\[
\pi(\widetilde x_1)=\pi(\widetilde x_2)
\]

but some matched observer-accessible result differs. Then at least one of the
declared maps is not constant on the \(\pi\)-fibre.

The first failure must be located as one of:

1. **process failure:** dynamics or a complete instrument depends on leaf
   order;
2. **formation failure:** a write, provenance, orientation, or archive route
   differs;
3. **access failure:** the observer boundary or admitted intervention family
   differs;
4. **finality failure:** the rival/fault contract or certification result
   differs;
5. **capability/resource failure:** available actions or their costs differ;
   or
6. **target failure:** the target itself contains preferred-leaf information.

Cases 2–5 are incomplete matching unless independent physics selects the
difference. Case 6 changes the matched target contract or names hidden
completion structure rather than an observer-accessible target. Case 1 is
observer-accessible preferred-foliation dependence—a leaking branch.

Thus there is no third possibility in the frozen contract:

```text
all load-bearing maps descend
    -> no operational excess

some load-bearing map does not descend
    -> unmatched physical premise or observable foliation leak
```

The exactly compensated assumption rules out a creditable leak inside this
series. A leaking theory would require a separately frozen generator,
coupling, observable, standard null, and current-bound collision.
