This is the clearest positive physical specimen yet for Dynamic Unity's
layered-record architecture.

A Wilson loop is one extended gauge-invariant quantity. It does not have one
context-free measurability status. Its record status depends on:

- whether the gauge group is Abelian or non-Abelian;
- whether suitable charged matter is present;
- whether the loop is homologically trivial;
- whether the participants possess a pre-distributed entangled ancilla;
- whether the measurement must preserve loop eigenstates;
- which local variables participants can manipulate or measure; and
- when and where the distributed outputs can be joined.

The resulting physical regimes are sharply different.

| Physical contract | What can be formed or done? | Honest type |
|---|---|---|
| Abelian gauge field plus suitable matter and pre-shared entangled ancilla | local couplings imprint only the Wilson-loop value into a distributed ancilla; later aggregation reveals it without resolving finer local field data | regional nondemolition threshold record |
| Abelian or suitable non-Abelian theory with local matter readout | participants measure local transport variables and multiply them later; the loop is reconstructed, but extra field detail is recorded and loop eigenstates are generally disturbed | destructive finer record |
| relativistic non-Abelian theory under the Beckman--Gottesman--Kitaev--Preskill assumptions | spacelike nondemolition Wilson-loop measurement permits superluminal signalling | inadmissible record interface |
| pure Abelian theory on nontrivial topology | a nontrivial Wilson loop is outside the local observable algebra; local residents can change its value but cannot measure or apply the loop operator | changeable but unmeasurable extended variable |
| conjugate nonlocal electric sector in that pure theory | distributed local electric-field measurements can identify it, but local operations cannot change its sector value | measurable but locally unchangeable sector |

This directly rejects a tempting simplification:

> Existence, measurability, changeability, nondemolition recordability,
> destructive reconstructibility, and observer access are not one nested
> property.

They form a typed capability profile.

The Abelian nondemolition construction is especially important for regional
finality. The target-selective imprint is distributed across a loop on one
spacelike slice. No local share reveals the target. Only the complete joined
share set does. The global value need not be accessible to any participant
until later causal aggregation.

That is a real physical analogue of a threshold-certified regional record.
It is not evidence that gauge theory is a consensus system or that records
are the ontological substrate. It proves that known physics already realizes
the architecture:

```text
extended physical distinction
  -> simultaneous local interactions
  -> individually blind distributed shares
  -> later causal aggregation
  -> one action-enabling regional fact.
```

## 1. The source result

[Beckman, Gottesman, Kitaev, and
Preskill](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0110205) ask which Wilson-loop
measurements can be physically localized in a relativistic gauge theory.
Their answer is not uniform.

### 1.1 Non-Abelian nondemolition measurement

For a spacelike non-Abelian Wilson loop, the nonselective projective
operation would decohere distinct loop eigenspaces while preserving
eigenstates. The paper constructs a signalling protocol involving
spacelike-separated magnetic flux tubes. When two tubes link the measured
loop, the nondemolition operation can transfer correlated non-Abelian
Cheshire charge between them. One party can then learn whether the other
party's tube linked the loop.

Under the paper's unbroken weakly coupled gauge-theory assumptions—and in a
confining theory below the relevant confinement scale—this gives:

\[
\text{non-Abelian spacelike Wilson-loop QND operation}
\Longrightarrow
\text{superluminal signalling}.
\]

Therefore the proposed operation is not physically realizable in a
relativistic theory satisfying the causal contract.

This is stronger than saying that nobody has found an apparatus. It is a
scoped impossibility theorem.

### 1.2 Destructive measurement

The obstruction does not forbid every way to infer a non-Abelian Wilson-loop
value. With suitable faithful matter, parties distributed along the loop can
measure local gauge-invariant transport variables. Their reports can be
multiplied and traced centrally to determine the Wilson loop.

The protocol records more than the loop value. It resolves local transport
data and generally disturbs a loop eigenstate. It is therefore a destructive
measurement, not a repair of the forbidden nondemolition operation.

The distinction is load-bearing for Dynamic Unity:

\[
\text{same derived target value}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{same record quotient or physical instrument}.
\]

### 1.3 Abelian nondemolition measurement

For an Abelian group—or a one-dimensional representation—the conjugacy-class
obstruction disappears. With suitable matter, each party can couple a local
gauge-invariant transport variable to one share of an entangled ancilla
distributed around the loop in advance.

The local outcomes are individually random. Their joined product reveals the
Wilson loop, while no additional local field configuration is learned. The
target-selective interaction occurs on the spacelike slice; the complete
result becomes accessible only after the reports are brought together.

The pre-shared entanglement is not a cost-free mathematical convenience. It
is a prior physical resource extending around the loop. The paper's
continuous-variable ideal uses an unnormalizable state for perfect precision
and notes that normalizable approximations can reach arbitrarily good
precision. The exact finite binary control below isolates the architecture,
not that continuum resource limit.

### 1.4 Pure Abelian topology

Matter content changes the observable algebra.

In a pure Abelian gauge theory on a spatial manifold with nontrivial
homology, a Wilson loop around a nontrivial cycle is not generated by the
local gauge-invariant observable algebra. Local residents cannot measure the
operator, destructively or nondestructively, and cannot apply it.

Yet a local electric-field operation on a link can rotate its value. Thus the
value is locally changeable without being locally measurable.

The conjugate nonlocal electric field has the opposite profile. Distributed
participants can measure its value by combining local electric-field
results, but local operations cannot change the associated superselection
sector.

This is a physical counterexample to treating action and observation as one
access relation.

## 2. Exact finite regional-record construction

The executable control uses three data qubits and the extended parity
operator

\[
W=Z_1Z_2Z_3.
\]

This is the smallest useful finite shadow of the Abelian loop protocol. It is
not lattice gauge theory.

Let the data computational-basis string be
\(x=(x_1,x_2,x_3)\), with parity

\[
p(x)=x_1\oplus x_2\oplus x_3.
\]

Prepare the three-share cat state

\[
|\mathrm{cat}\rangle
=
\frac{|000\rangle+|111\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
\]

Each participant applies a local controlled phase from data qubit \(i\) to
ancilla share \(i\). The ancilla becomes

\[
\frac{|000\rangle+(-1)^{p(x)}|111\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
\]

Each share is then measured in the \(X\) basis. Write the three binary
outcomes as \(s=(s_1,s_2,s_3)\).

### Proposition 1 — exact all-shares threshold record

Conditioned on data parity \(p\),

\[
\Pr(s\mid p)
=
\begin{cases}
2^{-2},&p(s)=p,\\
0,&p(s)\neq p.
\end{cases}
\tag{1}
\]

Therefore:

1. the joined record reconstructs \(p\) exactly;
2. every one- or two-share marginal is uniform and independent of \(p\);
3. the full share distribution depends only on \(p\), not on the finer data
   string; and
4. all three shares are required under the frozen share interface.

### Proof

For an \(X\)-basis result \(s\), the ancilla amplitude is proportional to

\[
1+(-1)^{p(x)+p(s)}.
\]

It vanishes unless the two parities agree. The four allowed share strings
have equal amplitude magnitude and therefore probability \(1/4\).

Projecting any one or two coordinates leaves equally many completions of
either allowed parity, so every proper marginal is uniform. \(\square\)

For an equal-prior binary target, this is a perfect finite `(3,3)` threshold
record:

```text
target information in each proper share subset = 0 bits
target information in all three shares          = 1 bit
random share entropy conditional on target       = 2 bits
```

The threshold is a property of this physical protocol, not a proposed
universal finality threshold.

## 3. The formed instrument

Let

\[
P_\pm=\frac{I\pm W}{2}.
\]

For any allowed share result \(s\), the induced Kraus operator is
proportional to \(P_{p(s)}\). Summing over all share results gives

\[
\mathcal L_W(\rho)
=
P_+\rho P_+
+P_-\rho P_-.
\tag{2}
\]

### Proposition 2 — the distributed protocol is nondemolition

Equation (2):

- preserves every \(W\)-eigenstate;
- preserves coherence between basis states with the same parity; and
- removes coherence between different parity sectors.

The executable checks all \(8^2\) computational matrix units.

For example,

\[
|000\rangle\langle011|
\]

is even-to-even coherence and survives (2), while

\[
|000\rangle\langle001|
\]

is even-to-odd coherence and is removed.

Thus the distributed shares form only the parity distinction. They do not
silently form the complete computational-basis record.

## 4. Destructive reconstruction is a different record

Suppose instead that every participant measures the local data bit and sends
the result to a central processor. The parity is still reconstructible:

\[
p(x)=x_1\oplus x_2\oplus x_3.
\]

But the nonselective channel is now

\[
\mathcal D(\rho)
=
\sum_x |x\rangle\langle x|
\rho
|x\rangle\langle x|.
\tag{3}
\]

### Proposition 3 — destructive access is strict refinement

The local-data transcript \(x\) refines the parity record \(p(x)\), but the
two instruments are not equivalent.

Equation (3) removes \(|000\rangle\langle011|\), even though the two strings
have the same parity and equation (2) preserves that coherence.

Therefore:

\[
\text{destructive local transcript}
\succ
\text{nondemolition parity record}
\]

as information, while

\[
\mathcal D\neq\mathcal L_W
\]

as physical state update.

Calling the destructive procedure a way to "access the same fact" is correct
only at the final target-value level. It is false at the record, disturbance,
provenance, and future-capability levels.

## 5. Same instrument, different physical formation route

There is a second exact way to implement (2). A single coherent ancilla in
\(|+\rangle\) can visit the three data sites sequentially, acquire one
controlled phase at each encounter, and be measured in the \(X\) basis after
the route completes.

The final ancilla phase is

\[
(-1)^{x_1+x_2+x_3},
\]

so its outcome is the same parity and its induced ideal instrument is again
\(\mathcal L_W\).

The two routes are operationally equal at the frozen target instrument and
physically different:

| Route | Spatial resource | Time/route burden | Intermediate vulnerability | Acquisition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| distributed cat ancilla | pre-shared entanglement around the loop plus three local shares | local interactions can occur on one slice; entanglement must have been distributed earlier | loss or corruption of any share | later aggregation of all shares |
| sequential coherent ancilla | one coherent carrier | causal route through every coupling region | accumulated decoherence and route provenance | one terminal readout |

This is another exact non-unification:

```text
same ideal target instrument
    != same formation history
    != same resource receipt
    != same regional architecture
    != same failure surface.
```

It strengthens rather than overturns DU's antecedent-relative selection
result. Once either complete apparatus is physically instantiated, the
instrument is realization-relative and determinate. The universal gauge law
does not select which apparatus exists.

## 6. Formation, accessibility, and finality time

The Abelian distributed protocol separates three moments.

1. **Target-selective physical imprint.** Local couplings correlate the
   extended loop value with the distributed ancilla.
2. **Local share formation.** Each ancilla share is measured or otherwise
   retained locally.
3. **Regional accessibility.** The shares enter one causal future and are
   aggregated.

The loop value is encoded in the joint distributed state after step 1. It is
not available to any proper local subset. A classical regional record exists
after step 2 only as a jointly sufficient family of shares. One observer can
act on the value only after step 3.

So these statements are compatible:

- the physical interaction forming the distinction occurred on the earlier
  slice;
- no local participant knew the distinction then;
- no superluminal signal was available; and
- the region later acquired one certified value.

This is the cleanest physical answer yet to the recurring question of whether
regional finality must mean one globally readable state. It need not. It can
mean that a target is already fixed in a distributed record relation whose
authorized reconstruction lies only in a later common causal future.

The record is not "globally final" in an unlimited sense. Its finality is
relative to:

- the loop target;
- the admitted action family;
- the complete share set;
- the trust/noise/adversary model;
- the aggregation route;
- the archive horizon; and
- the nondemolition or destructive contract.

## 7. The Abelian/non-Abelian hinge

The source paper identifies multiplication of conjugacy classes as the
algebraic hinge.

For an Abelian group, every conjugacy class is a singleton. Multiplying local
transport contributions therefore gives one unambiguous group value.

For the finite non-Abelian control \(S_3\), the three transpositions form one
conjugacy class. Products of two members of that class can be:

- the identity, when the transpositions agree; or
- a three-cycle, when they differ.

Thus class multiplication lands in more than one conjugacy class. The
executable enumerates the six group elements and verifies this split.

That enumeration is not the causality proof. It checks the exact algebraic
feature used by the physical argument and prevents the Abelian protocol from
being transferred to a non-Abelian target by replacing one group label with
another.

## 8. Relation to measurement-assisted locality

[Friedman, Yin, Hong, and
Lucas](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.09929) extend Lieb--Robinson reasoning to
local dynamics with measurements and feedback. Their bounds make the resource
accounting principle explicit: declared local measurement regions can
accelerate information-processing tasks, but the number and placement of
those regions and their outcomes are physical resources.

The Wilson-loop protocol does not evade locality:

- the entangled ancilla was distributed before the spacelike coupling;
- local operations reveal no target value individually;
- the readout becomes available only after causal communication; and
- a sequential alternative pays causal traversal time.

The apparent "instantaneous regional fact" is therefore a resource-shifted
formation architecture, not superluminal information transmission.

## 9. What this changes for Dynamic Unity

### A. The generalized-symmetry reopener is physically real

`HC-DU-118` required an explicit physical operation before treating an
extended algebraic target as observer-accessible. Wilson-loop measurement
proves that such operations exist in important gauge-theory classes.

The reopener is only partially closed:

- the operation is explicit and localizable in the Abelian-with-matter case;
- it forms a target-selective regional record;
- the target does not factor through any proper share subset;
- access and resource requirements are explicit.

But this is not yet a theorem for the particular modern
additive/maximal-net specimen in `HC-DU-118`, and the apparatus remains a
physically supplied realization rather than a universally selected one.

### B. Capability and access are not a chain

The pure-gauge topological example gives both directions:

```text
changeable but unmeasurable
measurable but locally unchangeable.
```

Any Dynamic Unity interface that represents capability by one scalar,
one nested access hierarchy, or one finality level loses this physical
structure. Candidate records need at least separate action and observation
relations.

### C. Regional records can be threshold-secret

An individually blind but jointly complete share family is not merely a
distributed-systems analogy. It is a physical measurement architecture.

Cryptographic vocabulary remains downstream:

- the cat correlations behave like a perfect `(n,n)` secret sharing of the
  target value;
- the quantum state and local couplings, not a cryptographic proof system,
  provide the shares;
- authentication, adversarial robustness, and Byzantine agreement are not
  supplied by the ideal protocol; and
- later share aggregation does not certify that the apparatus was honestly
  built or operated.

### D. A destructive repair can erase the question

When nondemolition access is impossible, a destructive measurement may
return the target by changing the record and disturbance contract. That is
scientifically useful, but it must not be logged as proof that the original
record interface was sufficient.

### E. Physics constrains finality architecture

The difference between Abelian and non-Abelian groups is not a convention
about how observers summarize data. It changes which spacelike record
operations are causally admissible. Matter content and topology further
change the accessible algebra.

This is evidence for the modest physics-first statement:

> Physical structure constrains which distinctions can become which kinds of
> records for which agents.

It is not evidence for the stronger record-first claim that records create or
derive the gauge theory.

The run does not select a new flagship.

```text

  + constructively localizable measurement

  + formed local shares
```

A ready successor now requires one of:

### Reopener A — generalized-symmetry access classification

Prove a new theorem, beyond the 2001 Wilson-loop cases, classifying which
generalized-symmetry operators are:

- locally measurable;
- regionally measurable with pre-shared resources;
- destructively measurable only;
- applicable but unmeasurable;
- measurable but not locally changeable; or
- forbidden by causality.

The classification must use one frozen physical operation class and yield a
new invariant or held-out consequence.

### Reopener B — selected regional formation mechanism

Provide dynamics or a physical boundary/source condition that selects one
regional carrier and aggregation architecture, rather than merely proving a
protocol exists.

### Reopener C — invariant resource law

Derive a target-independent lower bound that compares pre-distributed
entanglement, number of local stations, coherent traversal time, archive
capacity, error, and causal aggregation across a natural physical family.
The law must do more than restate standard secret-sharing or
Lieb--Robinson bounds.

### Reopener D — no-refit North-Star transfer

Use the same formed regional record and access contract, without adding a
new detector or quotient, to reconstruct a held-out time, geometry, field, or
capability target.

Until one fires, more finite parity or stabilizer variants would repeat known
structure.
