## Frozen finite model

\(n=|V|\) and \(m=|E|\). Every reversible link has a unitary implementation

\[

\[
z_e\longmapsto g_{t(e)}z_eg_{s(e)}^{-1}.

### Cycle-Lift Classification Theorem

The unitary-lift families compatible with one fixed connected family of

\[
U(1)^E/\delta U(1)^V
\cong H^1(G;U(1))

\[

for a graph without supplied two-cell relations. QED.

## Coherent-route witness

For two routes \(p,q\) with common endpoints, coherently control the route.
The joint state has the cross term

\[
|0\rangle\langle1|\otimes U_p\rho U_q^\dagger.
\]

For target observable \(W\),

\[
\langle X_C\otimes W\rangle
=
\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}
\left(WU_p\rho U_q^\dagger\right),
\]

while \(Y_C\) obtains the other quadrature. Changing only the unitary lifts
multiplies the cross term by the closed-loop phase \(z_pz_q^*\). Two
implementations with identical ordinary edge channels can therefore have
different coherent-route responses.
