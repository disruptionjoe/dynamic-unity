# Capability-indexed North-Star adjudication and the endogenous-compression gap

## Result in plain English

The five-swing campaign did not produce one global verdict in which records
either reconstruct physics or fail to do so. It found that this question is
not well typed until five things are fixed:

1. which physical possibilities are admitted;
2. which record interface physical dynamics actually selects;
3. which public record that interface forms;
4. which future actions and resources the observer possesses; and
5. which held-out physical target is being reconstructed.

Once those are fixed, all three North-Star outcomes occur in the current
controls:

- a public quotient reconstructs some targets;
- native differences can be perfectly screened from a restricted action
  class, giving operational duality at that boundary; and
- enlarging the action class can expose a same-public-record difference,
  giving a capability-relative remainder.

These are not contradictions. They can occur in one system for different
targets and capability envelopes.

The exact lesson is:

> A record is final only relative to a frozen future capability class. If a
> newly admitted action exposes a hidden distinction, the old record was not
> final for the enlarged class.

The campaign also exposes a stricter gap. Several existing controls reconstruct
targets, but none yet combines all of:

- independent physical selection of the interface;
- an actually formed observer-indexed record;
- strict noninjective compression of the lawful nongauge physical class;
- held-out physical-target reconstruction; and
- source-independent calibration with no refitting.

The gauge and QEC controls compress and reconstruct, but their boundary or code
architectures are supplied. The Einstein two-clock and phase controls
reconstruct by identifying the full finite source class, which is tomography
rather than strict compression. The AQFT public shadow is complete for its
declared public behavior, but the apparatus is supplied and no independent
native physical target has yet been reconstructed from that quotient.

Therefore the current North-Star verdict is:

```text
scoped record reconstruction:        EARNED in supplied/engineered arenas
screened operational duality:        EARNED relative to frozen actions
capability-relative remainder:       EARNED in finite controls
endogenous compressive reconstruction: OPEN
representation-robust remainder:     NOT EARNED
global ontology verdict:             INCOMPLETE_CONTRACT
```

The next bottleneck is not another record-rich detector. It is to find—or
obstruct—a physical process that selects its own useful compressive record
interface.

## 1. Frozen typed contract

Let:

- \(M\) be the lawful physical completion class modulo the declared
  representation/gauge equivalence;
- \(a:M\to A\) be the antecedent whose alleged selector power is being tested;
- \(\xi:M\to\Xi\) be the native interface architecture;
- \(r:M\to Q\) be the independently formed public record;
- \(\beta_H:M\to B_H\) be the complete behavior visible to the observer under
  the frozen capability/action envelope \(H\); and
- \(t:M\to T\) be one held-out physical target.

The three required factorization receipts are:

\[
\ker a\subseteq\ker\xi
\tag{selection}
\]

\[
\ker r\subseteq\ker t
\tag{target reconstruction}
\]

\[
\ker r\subseteq\ker\beta_H.
\tag{\(H\)-finality/action sufficiency}
\]

They are logically independent.

- Successful target reconstruction cannot select the interface that produced
  the record.
- Interface selection cannot prove that one public quotient is sufficient for
  a held-out target.
- Sufficiency for one target cannot establish finality for every future
  action.
- Behavioral finality under \(H\) need not survive a capability expansion.

This separation is the central attribution correction of `CCR-N5-S1` through
`CCR-N5-S5`.

## 2. Fixed-envelope exclusion theorem

### Theorem 1 — finality excludes an accessible remainder at fixed \(H\)

Suppose:

\[
\ker r\subseteq\ker\beta_H
\]

and the target is accessible through the admitted behavior,

\[
t=f\circ\beta_H
\]

for some \(f:B_H\to T\). Then:

\[
\ker r\subseteq\ker t.
\]

### Proof

For \(m_1,m_2\in M\), if \(r(m_1)=r(m_2)\), action sufficiency gives
\(\beta_H(m_1)=\beta_H(m_2)\). Applying \(f\) gives
\(t(m_1)=t(m_2)\). Therefore \(t\) factors through \(r\). \(\square\)

### Corollary 1 — no fixed-envelope double credit

If two lawful completions share \(r\) and differ on a target genuinely
accessible under \(H\), then \(r\) is not final for \(H\).

Therefore a same-record/different-target witness has only two honest readings:

1. the target is screened from \(H\), so the pair is operationally dual at
   that boundary; or
2. an \(H\)-action separates the pair, so the record fails \(H\)-finality.

It cannot simultaneously be an \(H\)-final record and an \(H\)-observable
physical remainder.

This theorem is elementary factorization. Its program value is to stop the
same witness from being counted both as successful public finality and as a
same-envelope remainder.

## 3. Capability filtration theorem

### Theorem 2 — capability enlargement refines physical equivalence

Let \(H\subseteq H'\), with every \(H\)-behavior recoverable from the complete
\(H'\)-behavior. Then:

\[
\ker\beta_{H'}\subseteq\ker\beta_H.
\]

### Proof

If two completions agree under every action in \(H'\), they agree under its
subset \(H\). \(\square\)

The observer's operational physical states therefore form a filtration of
quotients:

\[
M/{\sim_{H'}}
\longrightarrow
M/{\sim_H}.
\]

Increasing capability can split an old public equivalence class. It cannot
make previously distinguishable completions identical.

This is standard observational-equivalence, sufficient-statistic, control,
and process-tensor structure. Dynamic Unity's earned point is the typed
connection:

```text
public record finality
    is indexed by
future action class + horizon + resources + access boundary.
```

It is not one observer-independent scalar property.

## 4. Strict-compression criterion

An injective record \(r:M/G\to Q\) reconstructs every target on the defended
class. That result can be valuable tomography, metrology, or source
identification. It is not yet evidence that a smaller record substrate
reconstructs physical reality.

### Definition — nontrivial record-first reconstruction

Relative to a frozen lawful class and target, require:

1. **law openness:** \(t\) varies on the lawful class before record
   conditioning;
2. **independent selection/formation:** the interface and record are selected
   and formed without inspecting the held-out target;
3. **strict compression:** \(r\) is noninjective on the physical nongauge
   quotient;
4. **target factorization:** \(\ker r\subseteq\ker t\);
5. **held-out transfer:** the same record and decoder work on a frozen
   held-out source/intervention class;
6. **no refitting:** source, calibration, resources, decoder, boundary, and
   completion class do not change after the target is inspected.

### Proposition 3 — injective-repair ceiling

If a failed quotient is repaired only by adding enough independent
sensitivities to make the joined map injective on \(M/G\), the result earns
source tomography or identification. It does not satisfy condition 3 and
therefore cannot, by itself, establish nontrivial record-first
reconstruction.

This is an attribution rule, not a criticism of tomography. It distinguishes:

```text
the records retained exactly the target-relevant quotient
```

from:

```text
the records logged the entire defended source class.
```

## 6. North-Star decision packet

### 6.1 What is reconstructed

Current records reconstruct real, scoped targets:

- gauge boundary charge;
- correction action on a declared correctable code class;
- held-out clock/redshift combinations after sufficient calibrated
  sensitivities are joined;
- complete declared public behavior after an explicit AQFT or classical
  interface is formed; and
- public certificate-dependent actions in the hostile distributed control.

These are grade-3 reconstructions inside supplied or engineered interface
contracts.

### 6.2 What is operationally dual

Native completions that differ only inside a fibre screened from the frozen
action envelope are operationally equivalent for that observer:

- gauge-interior differences under boundary-only actions;
- logical differences under syndrome/recovery-only actions;
- AQFT apparatus/native-lift differences with the same complete public
  shadow; and
- implementation differences below a complete authenticated public process.

This earns operational duality at the frozen boundary, not native identity or
ontological equivalence.

### 6.3 What remains

Finite native distinctions become observable when the action class expands:

- interior gauge readout exposes same-flux configurations;
- logical actions expose same-syndrome code histories;
- new independently calibrated clock/source sensitivities split old source
  fibres; and
- noncentral future couplings expose a previously screened quantum fibre.

These are capability-relative remainders. They demonstrate the need to
re-adjudicate finality when access grows. They do not establish a
representation-robust physical remainder that survives every legitimate,
target-independent refinement.

### 6.4 What remains incomplete

No current specimen proves that physical dynamics itself selects a
nontrivial, compressive, observer-indexed record that reconstructs a held-out
physical target. No current finite completion class is physically exhaustive
enough to support a universal remainder verdict.

The global outcome is therefore `INCOMPLETE_CONTRACT`, with exact scoped
results underneath it. Underidentification remains incomplete work, not a
fourth ontology.
