# The conditional composition audit

## 0. What this pass is, and the one rule that keeps it honest

### 0.1 The question

Joe's mandate statement names four legs and one target: records, issuance,
initial conditions, and possibility/capability, assembled into *the dynamic
story of what happens to the shape of GU over time*, conditional on a source
action still to be built
(`explorations/du-origin-and-mandate-joe-statement-2026-08-04.md:14-35`). The
council's archaeology found that those four legs "**are never assembled into
one GU-dynamics picture inside DU**"
(`explorations/science-council-du-core-mandate-archaeology-and-adjudication-2026-08-04.md:170`).

This pass performs that assembly once, and asks three questions of it:

- **(a)** conditional on the source action landing, what do the four legs
  *jointly determine* about GU's shape over time?
- **(b)** what does the composition *forbid*? A composition that forbids
  nothing is not a composition.
- **(c)** where must two legs agree, with no stated bridge?

Three coordinates, per contract: scope regional/cross-repository; status
epistemic (this is a reading and assembly of filed artifacts, not physics);
formation disclosure only.

### 0.2 The conjunction rule — the discipline that prevents minting

The named failure mode for this pass is the one the council warned about: an
assembly that quietly becomes an assertion
(`science-council-...-2026-08-04.md:597-601`, "the most expensive mistake").
The rule that prevents it here is stated once and applied everywhere:

> **A conjunction of filed results carries the grade of its weakest conjunct,
> and no more. Conjoining does not add evidence; it only adds constraint.**

The four legs' relevant conjuncts sit at, respectively: GU's
conditional/pre-deposit and `BLOCKED_SOURCE_GAP` grades; TaF's definitional
plus finite-witness plus explicitly **review-only** grades, under a corpus
whose own headline is "**no top-line claim is a proven general theorem**"
(`time-as-finality#CLAIM-LEDGER.md:7`); TI's `formalizing` and `speculative`
claim statuses, with **nothing** at `promoted`
(`temporal-issuance#CLAIM-LEDGER.md:31-52`); and P2C's toy-grade,
class-relative, `NEW-FRAMING-ONLY` grades
(`possibility-to-capability#LANE-STATE.yaml:57-59`).

Therefore the composition below is **review-only and claim-free by
construction**. Its content is a type discipline and a prohibition set, not a
result. That is the honest ceiling and it is stated before the content, not
after.

### 0.3 What this pass does not do

It re-derives nothing, re-verifies nothing, and re-runs nothing. Every
sibling-repository fact is recorded **by pointer at that repository's own
filed grade** under the standing custody discipline (`CONNECTIONS.md`;
cross-repository custody is never evidence). Nothing is absorbed as DU truth.
Where two legs appear to collide, the collision is **flagged and left
standing** per DU's stop rules; it is not resolved here.

## 7. The honest finding
