# Executive result

`HC-DU-222` left one exact reopener: find a natural action-derived response
family that is sufficient for a locked target while remaining noninjective on
irrelevant microstate structure. Ordinary Dirac--Maxwell theory supplies the
cleanest positive control.

Once the field content, `U(1)` representation, coupling normalization,
orientation, region, and boundary conditions are fixed, the action gives the
current and Maxwell equation

\[
 j^\mu=q\bar\psi\gamma^\mu\psi,
 \qquad
 \partial_\nu F^{\nu\mu}=j^\mu.
\tag{1}
\]

Current conservation and Gauss's law then give

\[
 Q_\Omega
 =\int_\Omega j^0\,d^3x
 =\int_{\partial\Omega}\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf S
 =\Phi_{\partial\Omega}.
\tag{2}
\]

Therefore the selected current/flux response is exactly sufficient for the
locked target `total enclosed charge`. It is also strictly compressive:
opposite spin states, different internal distributions, different phases, and
different formation histories can share the same charge and boundary flux.

That positive does **not** form a material record. Equation (2) creates a
lawful physical constraint witness. It does not select a finite region,
pointer material, calibration, blank state, amplification, retention,
provenance, access route, reset, or consumer. A calibrated material write can
preserve the charge target; a magnitude-only write destroys charge-sign
sufficiency. Preloaded and transported charges can have the same final flux,
so the witness also fails to certify occurrence or causal lineage.

# 1. Three objects that must stay separate

| object | source | what it earns |

# 2. Action-selected response and locked-target sufficiency

Use the minimally coupled Dirac--Maxwell action schematically as

\[
 S[A,\psi]
 =\int d^4x\left[
 -\frac14F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}
 +\bar\psi(i\gamma^\mu D_\mu-m)\psi
 \right].
\tag{3}
\]

Varying `A` gives (1). The global matter phase symmetry gives the conserved
current, with charge normalization fixed by the representation and coupling.
The response family is therefore not an analyst-chosen tomographic list once
the theory is frozen. It is generated by the physical coupling.

Let the completion class `M` contain lawful matter/field configurations and
histories for one declared region. Define

\[
 R(M)=Q_\Omega,
 \qquad
 G(M)=\Phi_{\partial\Omega},
 \qquad
 T_Q(M)=Q_\Omega.
\tag{4}
\]

On solutions of the Gauss constraint,

\[
 G=R=T_Q.
\tag{5}
\]

Hence `T_Q` factors through both the current response and the boundary flux.
This is the exact positive requested by `HC-DU-222`: a natural response family
is sufficient for a useful target without reconstructing the complete spinor
or field microstate.

The qualification “frozen theory” is load-bearing. Lorentz covariance alone
does not select electromagnetism, the charge representation, coupling, region,
orientation, or asymptotic/boundary conditions. The full action selects the

\[

# 4. The first leaks are typed, not generic

The same quotient fails immediately for other targets.

## 4.1 Microstate leak

Upper spin-up and spin-down Dirac states can have the same vector current, as
`HC-DU-222` showed exactly. The charge/flux response therefore does not
reconstruct spin. Likewise, configurations with the same total charge but
different interior distributions have identical total boundary flux.

These are **upstream response leaks**. No downstream archive can recover the
missing distinction if its only input is the total flux.

## 4.2 Provenance and occurrence leak

A region that contained charge before the declared episode and a region that
received the same charge during the episode can have the same final `Q` and
`Phi`. Equation (2) identifies the endpoint constraint; it does not identify
the causal history that established it.

Thus the flux is not an occurrence token. It can answer “what charge is
enclosed now?” while failing “was this charge written during this run?” and
“which source supplied it?”

## 4.3 Continuation leak

Different crossing sequences can have the same net final charge. A final flux
therefore does not reconstruct the crossing history. If later actions depend
only on final charge, this is harmless compression. If the task asks for
transport count, ordering, or source identity, the process record must include
the corresponding current-through-boundary history or lineage.

Endpoint sufficiency and process sufficiency are different claims.

# 5. The downstream write boundary

Let a material transducer implement a map

\[
 W:\Phi\longmapsto r.
\tag{7}
\]

Then the retained record is `r=W(Phi)`. Even after the action and Gauss law
make `Phi` sufficient, the record preserves charge only if `W` does not merge
charge-distinct flux values.

Two exact controls locate this boundary:

1. **Calibrated identity write:** `W(Phi)=Phi`. Charge factors through the
   record.
2. **Magnitude-only write:** `W(Phi)=|Phi|`. Opposite charges collide, so sign
   does not factor through the record.

The first is a conditional material-handoff positive. The second proves that
response sufficiency does not automatically descend through an arbitrary
physical write. Neither write is selected by the Dirac--Maxwell action.

This yields a practical two-stage rule for future work:

```text
first test:  does the action-selected response preserve the target?
second test: does the physical write preserve the sufficient response?
```

If the first fails, building a better archive cannot help without enlarging
the interaction family. If the second fails, the response law is adequate and
the engineering/formation layer is the first leak.
