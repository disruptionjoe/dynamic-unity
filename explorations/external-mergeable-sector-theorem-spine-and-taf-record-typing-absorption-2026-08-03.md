### The narrow survivor, with its exact hypotheses

What lives instead is a **states-side pair**, recorded here with the exact
assumptions required by the no-cross-domain-theorem-by-metaphor stop:

> For a C*-algebra A, order the self-adjoint part of the dual by dominance
> (rho <= sigma iff sigma - rho is positive), on the **cone of unnormalized
> states**. Then: this order is a lattice iff the state space S(A) is a
> Choquet simplex iff extremal decompositions are unique iff A is abelian.
> (Observables face: Kadison 1951 / Sherman 1951. States face: Choquet
> theory / Bauer; the lattice-implies-commutative direction runs through
> Kakutani's L-space theorem and is held at source at proof-sketch grade
> with the Banach-lattice compatibility care point named.)
>
> Engineering instance, exact at source: the G-counter CRDT state space is
> the positive cone of the predual of the abelian algebra l-infinity(n),
> and its coordinatewise-max merge **is** that cone's lattice join. The SEC
> theorem (Shapiro et al. 2011) is the engineering half.
>
> Physics witness, verified arithmetically on absorption: in M_2,
> p = |0><0| and q = |+><+| have p - q with trace 0 and determinant -1/2,
> hence indefinite, hence the pair is incomparable and by the anti-lattice
> theorem has upper bounds but **no least upper bound** — no canonical
> merged state. Equivalently order-free: I/2 has two distinct extremal
> decompositions (the standing purification ambiguity).

Fences that travel with the pair, from source: total merges on the whole
state carrier only (a dominance chain of pairwise noncommuting states is a
lattice without an abelian carrier, so no "every lattice sub-carrier is

in `HC-DU-057` section 3.4); the engineering side occupies only the
commutative side of the equivalence, so its content there is the location
of the boundary, not a new tool; and the fan-out/no-broadcasting physics is
untouched.

Companion row P1, at source grade: collapse-as-conditioning is one
