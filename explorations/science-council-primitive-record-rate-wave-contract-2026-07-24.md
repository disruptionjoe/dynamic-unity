# Science Council dual primitive-record-clock wave contract

## Purpose

Run a third independent five-persona work wave from the complete accumulated
council corpus while comparing two new conditional premise branches:

> **Branch L — local cadence:** the most primitive temporal object may be a
> universal local rule for the rate or cadence at which observers add
> irreversible records. Observers need not share a global synchronization.
> Cross-observer time, distance, and candidate signal speed are reconstructed
> from causal message send/receive records.
>
> **Branch G — one global record clock:** one actual fundamental clock or
> update cadence may push record addition forward for all observers. Distance
> and the time taken by messages to traverse it may then generate the time
> differences observers assign to one another.

This is a request to construct and test the strongest non-circular version of
each idea and the relationship between them. Branch G explicitly permits a
single fundamental clock as a posit; it must not be rejected merely because
it is a clock. The work must instead expose what the posit buys, whether its
foliation is physical or gauge, and whether distance-induced message latency
recovers relativistic observables or only apparent clock offsets. Neither
branch may insert a metric distance or the speed of light under a new name.
Both branches are conditional and exploratory. Neither is banked or presumed
true.

The five lenses remain:

1. orthodox professor;
2. heterodox professor;
3. commercial scientist;
4. wild-frontier scientist; and
5. philosopher of science.

This is parallel work, not deliberation. Persona agreement is not evidence.
Each persona must freeze its contested proposition before reading any
same-wave peer output. Root will independently recheck every result used in
shared state.

## The admitted premise branches, typed

Every persona must begin by restating the strongest version of both branches
it can make mathematically coherent. The following clauses are the shared
starting specification, not conclusions:

- **RR-S1 — record events.** An admitted observer has an ordered
  sequence of irreversible local record-accretion events. The word
  "observer" must be operationally defined; it cannot silently mean a
  pre-existing inertial worldline with a metric and proper time already
  attached.
- **RR-L1 — local primitive cadence.** In Branch L, successive local record
  births obey one
  common dimensionless transition rule, hazard, or explicitly declared
  microscopic tick convention. A rate with units is incomplete until its
  denominator is named. It may not be defined per unit of an already-assumed
  global time.
- **RR-L2 — no global synchronization.** In Branch L, a common local rule does
  not imply
  simultaneity, a preferred slicing, equal coordinate-time rates, or an
  external clock shared by observers.
- **RR-G1 — one global update clock.** In Branch G, an actual fundamental
  update index `K` or clock cadence is admitted. It pushes record addition
  forward for every observer. The construction must say whether `K` is
  observable, gauge, hidden-but-physical, or operationally nonidentifying.
- **RR-G2 — exact versus gated addition.** Branch G must split two materially
  different models: `G-full`, in which every observer adds exactly one record
  per global tick, and `G-gated`, in which the global tick only opens an
  update opportunity while a local causal/dynamical rule may admit, skip, or
  weight records. Any gating law is an additional assumption and must expose
  its dependence on motion, position, field state, or the global frame.
- **RR-G3 — distance-induced difference.** Branch G proposes that causal
  message transit across distance produces the time differences observers
  assign to one another. The wave must distinguish raw propagation offset,
  clock synchronization, Doppler shift, relativity of simultaneity, and
  differential elapsed proper time after reunion.
- **RR-G4 — foliation status.** A global tick supplies an ordering or
  foliation. A successful low-energy relativity claim must show either that
  this structure is gauge/operationally inaccessible or why its observable
  preferred-frame effects satisfy the declared target.
- **RR-S2 — message comparison.** Observers compare through causal
  message send, receipt, reflection, or acknowledgement records. Any radar
  time, radar distance, latency, rapidity, or candidate signal speed must be
  constructed from those records.
- **RR-S3 — emergence burden.** A claim that Lorentzian time dilation,
  invariant signal speed, or a causal metric emerges must give the
  operational map and test for preferred-frame or synchronization leakage.
  Reproducing a distance-dependent delay alone is not yet relativistic time
  dilation.
- **RR-S4 — units.** One primitive record or global-clock interval may be
  declared as a
  microscopic unit. Physical length or speed needs an explicit calibration or
  independent map. A dimensionless order cannot create dimensional units ex
  nihilo.
- **RR-S5 — disconnected observers.** The same local law or global driver may
  apply to
  observers that never exchange a message. Empirical comparison between them
  nevertheless requires a causal bridge, a common descendant/ancestor, or a
  separately justified equivalence construction.
- **RR-S6 — conditional status.** This wave asks what follows under each
  branch, how they differ, and which observations discriminate them. It does
  not promote either premise merely because a toy realization can be built.

At minimum, each persona must compare at least one Branch-L and one Branch-G
formalization from this surface:

1. Branch L: equal record count per local proper-time tick;
2. Branch L: an equal local Poisson hazard;
3. Branch L: an asynchronous event graph or Lamport-style logical clock;
4. Branch L: a Lorentz-invariant causal-set sprinkling or transition law;
5. Branch G-full: synchronous global updates with one record per observer per
   tick;
6. Branch G-gated: one global update clock plus a local
   motion/field/causal-state-dependent record-admission law;
7. Branch G-hidden: a global scheduler whose foliation is treated as gauge or
   operationally inaccessible;
8. either branch: a light-clock, echo, or radar-message reconstruction; or
9. another explicitly typed construction.

These alternatives are not equivalent. In particular, (1) is compatible with
special relativity once proper time exists but does not derive proper time;
(3) supplies order without a metric by itself; (5) predicts equal reunited
record counts unless some additional mechanism changes what counts as a
record; (6) can fit time dilation only if its gating law earns rather than
imports the Lorentz factor; and defining radar distance from round-trip
message time can make `c=1` a convention rather than an independent
prediction.

## Central research question

Under what added conditions can either (L) a universal local record cadence
without synchronization or (G) one global record-update clock plus causal
message delay generate an operational Lorentzian causal metric, invariant
message cone, and relativistic elapsed-time comparisons? Which empirical or
mathematical discriminator separates the two branches, and which branch
merely redescribes propagation delay or imports the target?

The useful answer may be a construction, a scoped no-go, a factorization of
what is definitional versus derived, or a sharper intermediate object.

### Primary antecedents surfaced during root setup

These sources are prior art and comparison surfaces, not premises or
directives:

- [Knuth and Bahrenyi, *A Derivation of Special Relativity from Causal
  Sets*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.4172): coordinated observer chains,
  valuations, event projections, a Minkowski scalar, Lorentz
  transformations, and an invariant maximum speed. Audit which
  "coordination" and valuation assumptions do the work.
- [Arrighi, Facchini, and Forets, *Discrete Lorentz covariance for Quantum
  Walks and Quantum Cellular Automata*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.4499):
  exact discrete Lorentz covariance for their `Clock` QW/QCA in `1+1`
  dimensions, while the studied Dirac QW is only first-order covariant.
  This is a positive Branch-G antecedent, not evidence that an arbitrary
  global update clock is hidden or physically Lorentz invariant.
- [Bombelli, Henson, and Sorkin, *Discreteness without symmetry breaking: a
  theorem*](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0605006): an intrinsic measurable
  construction from a Poisson sprinkling cannot select a spacetime direction.
- [Saravani and Aslanbeigi, *On the Causal Set-Continuum
  Correspondence*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.6429): causal order fixes only
  conformal information without scale; element number supplies a statistical
  number-volume map; ordinary regular lattices expose boosted-frame
  irregularity, with qualified `1+1` Lorentzian-lattice exceptions.
- [Malament, *The class of continuous timelike curves determines the topology
  of spacetime*](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.523436): under the theorem's
  spacetime conditions, causal structure fixes topological, differential,
  and conformal structure. It does not by itself supply the conformal scale.

Every use must retain the source's dimensionality, exact covariance notion,
continuum/manifold assumptions, and stated scope. The wave must not claim
novelty for a known antecedent.

## Required order of work

Each persona works in this order:

1. **Reconstruct the accumulated learning.** State what both prior waves
   established, what they only constructed conditionally, what they narrowed,

2. **Audit prior prioritization.** Say which earlier vote or successor
   conclusion the new premise most strengthens, which it most weakens, and
   whether a previously low-ranked path is newly live. Votes remain objects to
   examine, not instructions.
3. **Type and stress-test both premise branches.** Define observer, record, record
   equivalence, order, cadence, the cadence denominator, message, and every
   operational map used. State which RR-L, RR-G, and RR-S clauses are
   assumptions and which, if any, the construction attempts to derive.
   Compare the strongest Branch-L and Branch-G versions before choosing the
   contested proposition.
4. **Select one contested proposition.** State it exactly, explain why the
   enlarged corpus plus the new premise makes it newly testable, and name
   evidence that could change it.
5. **Take one bounded evidence-producing swing.** Prefer an exact finite
   calculation, exhaustive event-graph enumeration, analytic derivation,
   counterexample, simulation with a declared ensemble, operational
   reconstruction, discriminating comparison, or executable probe. A
   memo-only result is allowed only for an exact dependency, dimensional,
   logical, identifiability, or intervention analysis.
6. **Run a synchronization/preferred-frame and delay-only null.** Every positive
   construction must be compared against at least one relabeling, reslicing,
   boost, asymmetric-latency, nonuniform-cadence, or regular-lattice control
   able to expose a preferred frame or clock. It must also face a control that
   subtracts or exactly models signal travel time, so that raw latency cannot
   masquerade as differential clock accumulation.
7. **Reconcile the result.** Mark the proposition upheld, narrowed,
   overturned, or still not evaluable. Separate operational definition,
   conditional construction, kinematic recovery, dynamical selection,
   dimensional calibration, and physical prediction.
8. **Write the next-actions roadmap and ledgers.** Order actions by
   dependency and information gain. Every action needs a concrete output and
   falsifier or stop. Record hypotheses, conjectures, possible claims,
   non-ordinal warrant, forbidden overclaims, stopped routes, and exact
   reopeners. Name newly visible or previously misused outcomes.
9. **Take a final unconstrained divergent pass.** Preserve every additional
   path genuinely worth remembering while the full context is active. Do not
   pad, rank it as a current priority, or require it to pass the roadmap's
   admission bar.

## Required distinctions and common fixture

Every memo must keep these objects distinct:

- local record count;
- global update count;
- causal precedence;
- logical-clock order;
- proper time;
- coordinate time;
- simultaneity convention;
- one-way latency;
- round-trip latency;
- radar time and radar distance;
- independently calibrated spatial distance;
- dimensionless limiting slope;
- dimensional invariant speed; and
- an observer's physical memory or detector record.

Every persona must address at least one two-observer special-relativistic
fixture. A recommended minimum is:

- observer A records a message send at local count `n_s`;
- observer B records receipt or reflection at local count `m_b`;
- observer A records return at local count `n_r`;
- candidate radar time is `(n_s+n_r)/2`;
- candidate radar distance in declared record-tick units is
  `(n_r-n_s)/2`.

The persona must say which quantities this defines, which it measures, and
which it cannot derive. In particular, `c=1` in radar units is not by itself a
prediction of a physical speed. A substantive invariant-speed result needs an
independent distance or metric reconstruction, a nontrivial invariance test,
or a dynamics that selects the signal cone.

Every persona must also address a reunion fixture:

- observers A and B share an initial event `E_0`;
- they follow distinct histories and later share `E_1`;
- signal-propagation delay is absent at the two shared endpoints;
- compare their accumulated record counts between `E_0` and `E_1`.

`G-full` predicts equal counts when every observer records every global tick.
If a target relativistic fixture has unequal elapsed proper times, Branch G
needs a declared gating, path, velocity, or field-response law. The relevant
question is then whether that law is generated independently, whether it
selects a preferred frame, and whether it recovers the right boost and
gravitational limits. The reunion fixture is not presumed to kill every
global-clock theory; it prices the minimum extra mechanism beyond
distance-dependent message delay.

The following route is explicitly live but not granted: causal order may
supply conformal structure while record/event number supplies a volume or
scale element. A successful construction must state the manifoldlikeness,
sampling, density, observer, and calibration assumptions and distinguish a
statistically Lorentz-invariant ensemble from one regular lattice that
selects a frame.

## Shared scientific constraints

- All results from the first two waves remain in force unless this wave
  produces evidence that changes their exact scope.
- The physical CSG post sector begins at `(T_1,T_2,...)`, with
  `t_eff=T_2/T_1`; `T_0` is irrelevant to post-era births.
- The exact CSG post map and factorial-tail half-power are known conditional
  antecedents, not DU derivations. Post conditioning does not universally
  force the half-power.
- Fixed-history replay defeats required online within-era updates but does not
  distinguish a fundamental frozen law from a complete law inherited from
  invariant prehistory.
- Hidden-fibre refinements make raw Fisher-retention nonidentifying as an
  order-only physical selector.
- Direct cardinality-running transitive percolation remains a killed
  formalization because it fails full natural-labeling path covariance.
- A universal local stochastic law can be covariant even when a regular
  realization or coordinate description is not. Conversely, one fundamental
  update clock may define a preferred foliation even if ordinary observers
  cannot immediately see it. Statewise invariance,
  ensemble invariance, full history covariance, and continuum Lorentz
  recovery are separate grades.
- Recovering special-relativistic kinematics does not derive gravitational
  dynamics, the value of dimensional `c`, an observer ontology, or the
  `Lambda` identification.
- A failed record-rate formalization closes only that formalization unless a
  predeclared concept invariant itself fails.
- Conditional entailment, constructive realization, comparative abduction,
  structural analogy, and derivation are non-ordinal warrants. Do not reject a
  useful conditional build merely because it is not a derivation.
- Check counts, persona agreement, and attractive analogies are never
  scientific warrant.

## Anti-circularity and anti-circling rules

The following do not count as progress:

- rejecting Branch G solely because it posits a global clock;
- restating Branch G as records per global tick without deriving a new
  cross-observer consequence;
- assigning every observer proper time and then claiming record count derived
  proper time without an independent comparison;
- defining distance only by message round-trip time and presenting `c=1` as
  an independently predicted constant;
- inserting Lorentz transformations into the simulator and reporting their
  recovery;
- using an external update schedule while concealing that it is the explicit
  Branch-G posit or later calling its consequences observer-native;
- using one regular lattice, preferred foliation, or chosen labeling while
  claiming Lorentz invariance without testing whether the foliation is
  observable;
- reproducing separated-clock offsets that disappear after subtracting
  message travel time and calling them reunion time dilation;
- fitting sprinkling density, signal cone, rapidity, post size, or clock skew
  after seeing the target;
- claiming that causal order alone fixes full metric scale;
- rerunning an earlier probe without a new discriminator;
- treating finite kinematics as continuum dynamics or cosmology; or
- expanding the divergent list instead of taking the bounded swing.
