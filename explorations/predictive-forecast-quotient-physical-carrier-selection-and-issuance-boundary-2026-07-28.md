# Predictive forecasting does not select its physical carrier

## Executive result

Swing 1 returns:

```text
PHYSICAL_SELECTION_RESIDUAL_SURVIVES
RECURSIVE_ISSUANCE_RESIDUAL_NOT_ESTABLISHED
```

The first half of the proposed program is already known. Once a controlled
process, future action family, horizon, and tolerance are supplied, histories
have a canonical coarsest quotient that preserves all declared future
response laws. Computational mechanics calls the corresponding controlled
object an epsilon-transducer; finite automata, probabilistic bisimulation,
predictive-state, and Dynamic Unity's `HC-DU-036A/H` machinery express close
variants.

The quotient does not select where it is physically carried. Two
symmetry-related memories can realize the same minimal forecast state with
the same stability, dissipation, resources, and future value. A natural rule
cannot choose one if the physical antecedent is invariant under their swap.
At most it selects their orbit, a symmetric algebra, or an equivalence class.
A separate physical event, asymmetry, boundary, or access relation is needed
to choose a member.

Thermodynamics, evolution, semantic information, empowerment, and active
inference explain important ways predictive information can be useful or
favored. Each result is relative to typed structure such as a
signal--sensor split, system boundary, viability or fitness criterion,
action/sensor channel, Markov blanket, horizon, or task. None presently
derives one universal physical record interface from bare dynamics.

The recursive story also separates cleanly. A record may enlarge a situated
system's usable action and forecasting frontier. That is real operational
capability growth. Under a fixed complete process or controller, however, it
remains compatible with third-person disclosure. Calling it source issuance
requires an independently justified restriction on completed-history and
fixed-oracle rivals, which this audit does not supply.

The original Swing 2 should not run: it would rebuild known forecast-quotient
mathematics. Swings 3--5 remain inactive until a source-pinned physical theory
selects a formed interface or covariant interface family before the target,
horizon, and held-out action are chosen.

## 1. Frozen types

Let:

- \(\mathcal H\) be the admitted history space;
- \(P\) be a complete controlled process law;
- \(\Pi\) be the admitted family of future policies or action words;
- \(K_h^\pi\) be the future response law after history \(h\) under
  \(\pi\in\Pi\);
- \(\rho\) be the resource contract; and
- \(\varepsilon\) be the operational tolerance, with \(\varepsilon=0\) for
  the exact statements below.

The four terms used in the proposal are not synonyms.

| term | frozen meaning | does not yet imply |
|---|---|---|
| prediction | estimate one declared future target | a target-general state |
| forecasting | retain a declared family \(\{K_h^\pi\}_{\pi\in\Pi}\) | physical formation or access |
| capability | attain a declared action, control, or discrimination outcome under \(\rho\) | source issuance |
| issuance | enlarge an admitted frontier under a frozen external envelope in a way that survives the declared completion rivals | third-person ontological novelty |

## 2. The conditional forecast quotient is canonical

Define

\[
h\sim_{P,\Pi}h'
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
K_h^\pi=K_{h'}^\pi
\quad\text{for every }\pi\in\Pi .
\]

Let

\[
q_{P,\Pi}:\mathcal H\longrightarrow
\mathcal Q_{P,\Pi}:=\mathcal H/{\sim_{P,\Pi}}
\]

be the quotient.

### Proposition 1 — coarsest exact forecasting statistic

The quotient \(q_{P,\Pi}\) is sufficient for every declared future-response
law. If another statistic \(s:\mathcal H\to S\) is also sufficient, then

\[
s(h)=s(h')\Longrightarrow h\sim_{P,\Pi}h'.
\]

Thus the partition induced by \(s\) refines the forecast quotient, and
\(q_{P,\Pi}\) is the unique coarsest exact sufficient partition up to
relabeling.

### Proof

The quotient is sufficient by its definition: one class contains exactly
histories with identical declared future kernels. If \(s\) is sufficient and
\(s(h)=s(h')\), every kernel decoded from \(s(h)\) must equal the
corresponding kernel decoded from \(s(h')\); hence
\(K_h^\pi=K_{h'}^\pi\) for every \(\pi\), so \(h\sim_{P,\Pi}h'\).
\(\square\)

### Proposition 2 — action and horizon refinement

If \(\Pi_0\subseteq\Pi_1\), then

\[
\sim_{P,\Pi_1}\ \subseteq\ \sim_{P,\Pi_0}.
\]

The same inclusion holds when a longer horizon contains all tests from a
shorter horizon. Enlarging observer capability can therefore split a former
forecast state.

This is the stochastic controlled-process analogue of the existing
`HC-DU-036A/H`, `HC-DU-064`, and `NI-DU-94` boundaries. It is known
predictive-state / epsilon-transducer terrain, not new Dynamic Unity
mathematics.

### Naturality that is actually earned

An isomorphism transporting histories, \(P\), and \(\Pi\) transports
\(\sim_{P,\Pi}\) and therefore induces an isomorphism of quotient sets. This
makes the abstract quotient natural relative to the complete typed contract.
It does not make one embedding of that quotient into physical degrees of
freedom natural.

unbounded quantifier.

## 4. `HC-DU-083` — quotient/carrier separation

### Theorem — symmetric predictive value cannot select a point carrier

Let \(x\) be a physical antecedent, \(G_x\) its stabilizer, and
\(\mathcal I\) a set of candidate formed interface embeddings. Suppose:

1. two candidates \(i_0,i_1\in\mathcal I\) realize the same forecast quotient;
2. every declared predictive, stability, dissipation, resource, and
   capability score assigns them the same value; and
3. some \(g\in G_x\) swaps \(i_0\) and \(i_1\).

Then no \(G\)-equivariant selector depending only on \(x\) and those scores
can select either point.

### Proof

An equivariant selector \(s\) would satisfy

\[
s(x)=s(gx)=g\,s(x).
\]

Because \(gx=x\), its value must be fixed by \(g\). Neither \(i_0\) nor
\(i_1\) is fixed: \(g i_0=i_1\) and \(g i_1=i_0\). Therefore neither can be
the selected point. \(\square\)

This is a direct specialization of the already banked `HC-DU-033C`
stabilizer obstruction. The novelty claim is deliberately narrow: the audit
shows that making the score “predictive usefulness” does not evade the
existing interface-selection theorem.

### Minimum exact witness

Take one controlled binary process whose minimal forecast state is
\(Q_t\in\{0,1\}\). Let two resource-matched memory cells be formed with

\[
M_t^{(0)}=M_t^{(1)}=Q_t.
\]

Let the complete source dynamics, state, read cost, retention, and future
action law be invariant under the swap

\[
M^{(0)}\leftrightarrow M^{(1)}.
\]

Reading either cell has identical forecasting and capability value. The swap
fixes the antecedent and exchanges the two point interfaces. Predictive
optimality can identify the common value, the pair, or their orbit; it cannot
choose a unique cell.

This witness does **not** prove that physics cannot select records. It proves
the more useful necessity:

> A physical selector must supply symmetry-breaking state, a relational
> access token, a selected algebra/orbit, or another antecedent not contained
> in predictive value alone.

These results make the proposed physical story plausible:

```text
formed memory
  -> predictive usefulness
  -> energetic, viability, fitness, or control consequence.
```

They do not establish the reverse universal arrow:

```text
bare physical law
  -> unique formed forecasting interface.
```

In particular, “efficient systems must be predictive” is not “prediction
selects the system boundary and carrier.” Evolution can select predictive
organisms relative to fitness without making forecasting the objective of
fundamental physics. Semantic information can identify useful correlations
after a boundary and viability function are frozen without deriving either.

## 6. Forecasting versus prediction

The user's distinction remains valuable even though the quotient is known.

- **Prediction** is usually one target under one loss.
- **Forecasting** retains a family of counterfactual futures under multiple
  actions.

Forecasting is therefore a better target-independent pressure on a record
interface than one fitted prediction score. It makes overfitting harder and
connects naturally to capability. But it is not fully neutral: the action
family, process, horizon, tolerance, and resource envelope still define which
histories count as the same.

The correct research object is consequently not a universal scalar
“predictive capacity.” It is an action-indexed frontier or quotient together
with a separate physical formation receipt.

## 7. The issuance fork

Suppose access to a formed forecast state changes the actions available next:

\[
\mathcal F_{t+1}(A,\rho)\supsetneq\mathcal F_t(A,\rho).
\]

Three claims must remain separate.

1. **Situated capability growth.** The system can now do something it could
   not do from its prior accessible state. This can be exact and physically
   meaningful.
2. **Adaptive or evolutionary open-endedness.** The reachable operational
   frontier keeps expanding under a declared environment and resource flow.
   This is a stronger empirical/process claim.
3. **Source issuance.** The new frontier was not already contained in an
   admissible completed-history, controller, or oracle completion. This is
   the strongest claim.

The first is compatible with standard control, learning, evolution, and
fixed-law dynamics. The second requires sustained-frontier and resource
controls. The third fails without a physically justified restriction on
completion rivals: a fixed complete process or pre-correlated oracle may
encode the whole trajectory and every later action handoff.

Swing 1 therefore does not return
`RECURSIVE_ISSUANCE_RESIDUAL_SURVIVES`. It retains recursive operational
capability as a useful downstream question only after physical interface
selection has been earned.
