# Heterodox professor: successor wave

## Executive verdict

The combined corpus exposed a category mistake in the first-wave synthesis.
The proposition recorded as `COUNCIL-H1`—

> A fixed CSG law can have a covariant post-to-post effective flow.

—is not wholly a `CONJECTURE / OPEN`. Its conditional core is established
causal-set work:

\[
\widetilde t_n^{(r)}
  = \sum_{k=0}^{r}\binom{r}{k}t_{n+k}
  = M^r(t)_n,\qquad M(t)_n=t_n+t_{n+1}.
\]

For the fixed bare family \(t_n=u^n/n!\), a large completed post past gives
the local asymptotic

\[
t_{\mathrm{eff}}
  = \widetilde t_2/\widetilde t_1
  \sim \sqrt{u/r}.
\]

The executable swing reconstructs this result, obtains the expected
half-power across three bare parameters, and shows on one finite fixture that
a fixed post-era law avoids the path-covariance failure of direct
birth-stage running. The result is:

> **`POST-RG-ANTECEDENT-RECONSTRUCTED /
> HISTORY-TO-HALF-POWER-CONDITIONALLY-REALIZED /
> DIRECT-CLOCK-KILL-EVADED / LAW-TO-POST-CYCLE-OPEN`.**

This is a real opportunity, but not yet a Dynamic Unity result. The law has
not been shown here to generate a first post, repeated posts, a viable
post-size distribution, manifoldlike geometry, a response/return arrow,
physical units, or `Lambda`.

### 1.2 What the first wave actually established

1. The influence profiles are live mathematical objects with correct
   decomposition and invariance scopes.
2. Exact replication forces the declared half-power exponents for normalized
   \(L_2\)- and entropy-effective counts. A broad iid/summable-correlation
   class also gives the half-power. This is a normalization/null result.
3. An ordinary quadratic relaxation can produce a point-like normalized
   terminal residue while raw activity vanishes.
4. Open anisotropic Bianconi driving can sustain raw activity, but the
   anisotropy and cadence are imported.
5. Response `R`, embedding `E`, and unit map `U` are separable research
   stages, while `R+E+U` remains the physical-identification gate.
6. Direct \(p(N)\) or \(g(N)\) cardinality running is not repaired by
   statewise isomorphism. It failed full natural-labeling path covariance.

### 1.3 What it made plausible, but did not establish

- Exact unlabeled-causet enumeration could serve as a reusable admission
  harness.
- Order-defined landmarks and physical update records are two possible ways
  to obtain effective change without turning a gauge birth label into a
  clock.
- Transition Fisher geometry might eventually constrain a mobility.
- Held-out geometry and response might discriminate otherwise optional
  formalizations.

None of those statements supplies a selector, physical observable identity,
unit-bearing scale, cosmological value, or prediction.

### 1.4 What it killed locally

- normalized concentration or a terminal residue as a concept discriminator;
- direct birth-count coupling schedules;
- persistent activity from a closed fixed-target relaxation;
- persistent activity from an open drive as evidence of native
  self-organization;
- another free influence proxy as a substitute for response; and
- physical-scale language without a declared microscopic unit and unit map.

### 1.5 Non-obvious cross-output relationship

The useful combination is:

\[
\texttt{WF-D1}
+\texttt{HET-D3}
+\texttt{ORTH-D2}
+\texttt{COM-D8}.
\]

- `WF-D1` supplies an order-defined post rather than a birth-stage clock.
- `HET-D3` asked for discrete accretion shocks rather than a prescribed
  periodic drive.
- `ORTH-D2` and the normalization null show that one half-power can arise
  generically from effective-count normalization.
- `COM-D8` requires any generated hierarchy to remain separate from the
  microscopic unit that would dimensionalize it.

Together they reveal a second, mechanism-distinct half-power. A completed
causal past can change the **conditional law** at a physical landmark,
producing \(t_{\mathrm{eff}}\sim r^{-1/2}\). This is not the normalized
effective-count half-power and is not direct \(N\)-running. The shared
exponent is therefore a mechanism-discrimination problem, not independent
consilience for `Lambda`.

The correction to `HET-D3` is important: an arbitrary birth is only a gauge
stage. A post can be a physical discrete shock because its order relations
define the boundary and the same conditional law governs the whole following
era.

## 2. Prioritization audit

### Outcome I most agree with

I agree with `COM-D1` as the best short-term allocation. Exact unlabeled
transition enumeration is the cheapest common gate for post laws, record
laws, and any future feedback law. Its value is admission and counterexample
production, not continuum extrapolation.

### Outcome I most doubt

I most doubt the **framing and apparent confidence** of `WF-D1` as a unitary
`55–0` long-term research candidate. The vote bundled two different things:

1. an exact conditional post transformation that already exists in the
   literature; and
2. an unbuilt DU program requiring generated posts, repeated eras, a useful
   hierarchy distribution, geometry, response, and units.

The first should not consume work as an open derivation. The second may fail
quickly if posts do not recur in the selected law or if attraction to
transitive percolation erases further useful flow. The idea remains
high-leverage, but the vote does not measure this dependency risk.

### Systematically undervalued item

`HET-D3`, discrete accretion shocks, was undervalued because its first-wave
wording treated birth events too loosely. Replacing “birth number” with
“order-defined post/break” supplies the missing native event. It does not yet
supply repeated driving, but it turns the item from a clock-reimport risk into
a concrete conditional mechanism that can be tested.

`COM-D8` is also not glamorous enough to win a mechanism vote, but it is a
mandatory accounting constraint: \(\sqrt{r/u}\) is a dimensionless hierarchy,
not a unit-bearing scale.

## 3. Contested successor finding

### Exact proposition

The selected proposition is `COUNCIL-H1`:

> A fixed CSG law can have a covariant post-to-post effective flow.

### Why the enlarged corpus changes it

The wild-frontier memo proposed posts after its direct count-running fixture
failed, but the synthesis retained the post map itself as conjectural. The
primary-source audit shows that the map is an established antecedent, and the
orthodox null makes its half-power asymptotic especially discriminating: the
same exponent can arise from a different object and mechanism.

### Evidence capable of changing the proposition

The bounded swing had to establish all of the following without fitting a
desired epoch:

1. literal iteration of \(M\) agrees with the binomial post transform;
2. the transitive-percolation family is a projective fixed family;
3. the factorial family approaches one common effective percolation
   parameter with exponent \(-1/2\);
4. the corresponding inverse-\(p\) era proxy grows with exponent \(+1/2\);
5. a single fixed post-era law passes a nontrivial natural-order fixture; and
6. direct stage running fails the identical fixture.

Failure of any item would have narrowed or defeated this formalization.

## 4. Bounded evidence-producing swing

### Construction

The probe uses the fixed bare family

\[
t_n=\frac{u^n}{n!},\qquad u\in\{0.25,1,4\},
\]

and completed post-past sizes

\[
r\in\{10^2,10^3,10^4,10^5,10^6\}.
\]

It computes the binomial transform in log space, projectivizes the physical
after-post sector by \(\widetilde t_1\), and inspects couplings through index
four. The after-post \(t_0\) is physically irrelevant. The probe fits the
asymptotic slopes only after the two smallest fixtures.

### Results

All **8/8** checks pass.

| \(u\) | slope of \(t_{\mathrm{eff}}\) | slope of \(1/p_{\mathrm{eff}}\) | \(t_{\mathrm{eff}}/\sqrt{u/r}\) at \(r=10^6\) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.25 | -0.497517 | 0.496556 | 0.998749 |
| 1 | -0.499483 | 0.497545 | 0.999749 |
| 4 | -0.501196 | 0.497304 | 1.000624 |

The exact-map iteration control has maximum absolute error
\(1.11\times10^{-16}\). The percolation fixed-family control has maximum
absolute error \(5.55\times10^{-17}\). At \(r=10^6\), the maximum relative
error among inspected percolation-like coupling ratios is below `0.007` for
all three bare parameters.

For one originary four-event causet with three natural orders:

| Law | Path probabilities | Spread | Result |
|---|---|---:|---|
| fixed post-era law | `0.165834230240142` for all three up to rounding | \(2.78\times10^{-17}\) | passes this fixture |
| direct stage-running control | `0.1498829`, `0.1498829`, `0.1423888` | `0.007494` | fails; max/min `1.05263` |

The fixed-law check is a finite fixture, not a new proof of general
covariance. Its role is to show why conditioning once at a physical post is
not equivalent to changing the law at each gauge birth stage.

### Controls and admissions

- Transitive percolation is already projectively fixed, so posts do not
  generically create running.
- The factorial bare family is a conditional posit.
- Post occurrence is assumed, not derived.
- No target post size, epoch, geometry, unit, or cosmological value is fit.
- An originary-percolation tree/de-Sitter-like era is only a comparator.
  A finite envelope fit is not manifold, dimension, or continuum recovery.

## 5. Reconciliation

### Proposition disposition

`COUNCIL-H1` is **overturned as a single undifferentiated
`CONJECTURE / OPEN`**, then split:

| Typed proposition | Disposition |
|---|---|
| A fixed CSG law admits an exact conditional post map | `ESTABLISHED ANTECEDENT / RECONSTRUCTED` |
| The factorial family conditionally gives \(t_{\mathrm{eff}}\sim\sqrt{u/r}\) | `SOURCE-ANCHORED / NUMERICALLY RECONSTRUCTED` |
| A fixed post-era law avoids the tested direct-clock covariance failure | `FINITE CONSTRUCTIVE FIXTURE` |
| The factorial law generates one or repeated posts with useful statistics | `CONJECTURE / OPEN` |
| Post eras generate a robust hierarchy and manifoldlike geometry | `CONJECTURE / OPEN` |
| The flow selects a DU response, physical unit, or `Lambda` | `NOT IDENTIFIED` |

### Concept-level consequence

A fixed law can conditionally turn completed causal history into a
dimensionless hierarchy without a direct birth clock. This keeps one
order-first mechanism class live and corrects the claim that all such flow is
merely unbuilt.

### Formalization-local result

The half-power is realized for the factorial coupling family under the
assumption of a sufficiently large post past. It does not establish that the
family produces posts, and transitive percolation supplies a decisive
no-running control.

### Prioritization change

Do not spend the next wave deriving the known post map. After the reusable
exact-enumeration gate, spend it on **post occurrence/repetition and held-out
geometry**. If those fail, the attractive half-power is irrelevant to DU.

## 8. Newly visible or previously misused outcomes

1. **The post route has an exact antecedent.** The open work is no longer
   “invent post RG”; it is “test whether a fixed law generates the
   post-to-post conditions that make the map consequential.”
2. **Half-power is mechanism-diverse.** Normalization and post conditioning
   can produce the same exponent on different objects. Repetition of the
   exponent is not independent evidence.
3. **A completed past is invariant but inherited.** Its cardinality can
   condition a law without becoming a birth clock, yet explaining why it is
   large remains part of the dynamics.
4. **The fixed point is both opportunity and kill.** Attraction toward
   transitive percolation supplies universality, but exact projective
   fixation may extinguish further effective running and leave no
   matter/feedback richness.
5. **`HET-D3` has a lawful version.** Post-induced law change is the discrete
   accretion shock that the first-wave periodic-drive alternative lacked.
6. **The originary-percolation result is a stress fixture, not a recovery
   receipt.** Its tree era and de-Sitter-like finite behavior suggest
   observables to test; its caveats prohibit importing geometry.
7. **One post does not solve source ownership.** The history-to-law map may
   supply a native intervention, but repeated posts and work/resource closure
   are still needed for the return arrow.
8. **The vote confused discovery with dependency.** `WF-D1` was scientifically
   promising, but part of the proposed work was already known and the
   remaining part has a sharper cheap kill.

## Unresolved disagreement with the first-wave synthesis and vote

I agree with the central synthesis that no selector, physical identity,
unit-bearing nonzero scale, `Lambda`, banked claim, or prediction was earned.
I also agree that direct cardinality running is killed and that exact
enumeration should precede geometry.

I disagree with the epistemic granularity of `COUNCIL-H1`. The exact
post-conditioning transformation cannot remain labeled simply
`CONJECTURE / OPEN`; it is an established antecedent and has now been
reconstructed locally. The open proposition begins at endogenous
post/repetition statistics and continues through geometry, response, and
units.

I also disagree with reading the long-term Condorcet win as evidence for one
coherent research package. It mixed a known map with speculative downstream
dependencies. The result should **narrow**, not inflate, `WF-D1`: retain it
only as a post-occurrence → recurrent-flow → held-out-geometry → response
sequence with a kill at every arrow.
