# Science Council: record-relative actuality concept-ledger triage

## Decision

The divergent proposal set contains six distinct concepts that are internally
coherent enough, operationally expressible enough, and falsifiable enough to
protect in the Dynamic Unity concept register:

1. record-relative actuality by completion-class invariance;
2. physical perspective gluing and perspectival holonomy;
3. informational distance and reality bandwidth;
4. reconciliation cost and record-formation backreaction;
5. protected public-fact phases; and
6. observer identity as record colimit.

They are entered as `CONCEPT-DU-005` through `CONCEPT-DU-010`.

This is not a vote that the concepts are true. It prevents one failed
formalization, familiar prior art, or premature ontology preference from
silently deleting a coherent research possibility.

## Inline council

### Orthodox professor

Preserve completion-class actuality because it can be defined without
changing quantum mechanics: fix an observer's certified records, admissible
process class and interventions, then ask which propositions are invariant
over every compatible history. Preserve perspective gluing only if its
observable exceeds ordinary noncommuting operation order, geometric phase and
reference-frame gauge. Treat intervention-indexed facts, predictive causal
states and provenance-sensitive endpoints as formalizations of these
concepts, not independent ontologies.

### Heterodox professor

Preserve merger-order dependence and perspectival curvature as the highest
novelty branch. The strongest coherent departure is not “each observer has a
private world”; it is that a new joint record section may be physically
formed when perspectives interact, and different reconciliation paths might
have a loop obstruction. Preserve binding-without-resolution and sheaf
obstruction as formalizations. Do not require a secret completed biography.

### Commercial scientist

Preserve informational distance and reconciliation cost because both yield
clear products and experiments: measure how many mutually certifiable
distinctions a channel can establish, then price the work, memory, latency,
coherence and authentication required. The decisive tests compare different
spatial layouts at matched channel capacity, and different reconciliation
protocols at matched messages and endpoint memories.

### Wild-frontier scientist

Preserve actualization backreaction, but separate the already exact fact that
informative records alter some coherence from the unearned claim of an
additional gravitational source. Preserve protected public-fact phases and
perspectival holonomy. Hold “record gravity” and causal-actualization noise
as inactive gated forms under the broader backreaction concept—not standalone
concepts—until a covariant, conserved source and a signal distinct from
ordinary stress-energy and decoherence are supplied.

### Philosopher of science

Preserve indexical truth only in the operational completion-class form; the
bare phrase “truth is relative” adds no science. Preserve persons-as-record-
colimits as a scoped observer-identity concept because split/merge cases can
challenge a unique hidden bearer. Do not infer consciousness or moral
personhood. The ledger should contain a small number of invariants with many
formalizations, not fifty renamed metaphysical conclusions.

## Prior-art boundary

The concepts deliberately retain nearby mature terrain as controls:

- Adlam and Rovelli's
  [cross-perspective links](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.13342) require an
  observer's information to be physically stored and accessible to other
  observers. Dynamic Unity's open increment is whether reconciliation forms
  a new certified joint section rather than merely exposing a fixed one.
- Quantum-reference-frame work supplies physical perspective transformations
  and perspective-neutral symmetry structures:
  [Giacomini, Castro-Ruiz and Brukner](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.07207) and
  [Vanrietvelde et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.00556). A DU holonomy must
  survive their gauge structure and ordinary geometric phases.
- Abramsky and Brandenburger's
  [sheaf account](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.0264) already equates
  contextuality with a global-section obstruction. A DU result must connect
  a frozen record-network obstruction to a new physical reconciliation
  witness, capability difference or resource lower bound.
- [Quantum Darwinism](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0505031) already links
  redundant environmental records to objectivity. A DU public-fact phase
  requires typed finality, adversaries and finite-size or asymptotic scaling,
  not redundancy alone.
- [Process tensors](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00589) already provide an
  operational multi-time process state. Completion-class actuality must not
  merely rename a process tensor or conditional state.
- [Quantum-channel distinguishability](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.06306)
  already gives operational costs and rates for channel discrimination.
  Informational distance needs a stable cross-perspective law, not a new name
  for one selected channel divergence.
- [Measurement thermodynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.4098) already
  supplies work bounds for measurement and erasure. Reconciliation cost earns
  novelty only through a residual dependence on certified distinctions after
  complete standard resource accounting.

This is a collision-aware concept triage, not an exhaustive claim-specific
novelty search.

## Not promoted as independent concepts

- **Non-signalling relational collapse:** hold as a possible formalization of
  `CONCEPT-DU-005` only after it differs operationally from ordinary unitary
  quantum mechanics plus record conditioning.
- **Record gravity and causal-actualization noise:** retain only as gated,
  inactive forms under `CONCEPT-DU-008`, not as standalone concepts, until a
  covariant conserved dynamics and an ordinary stress-energy/decoherence null
  exist.
- **Modular distance is the genuine distance:** preserve relative modular
  structure as one `CONCEPT-DU-007` formalization, not as a selected law.
- **Universal objectivity threshold:** rejected by `HC-DU-035A`.
  Platform-specific protected phases remain open in `CONCEPT-DU-009`.
- **Indexical truth as a bare metaphysical slogan:** absorbed into the
  operational invariant of `CONCEPT-DU-005`.
- **Consciousness, memory and personhood conclusions:** retained only as
  downstream applications of `CONCEPT-DU-010`; no consciousness or ethical
  claim follows from record gluing.
- **Equivalence principle for facts:** retain as motivation, not an
  independent concept, until a transformation law and violation observable
  are specified.

## Strongest integrated experiment

The six concepts can first meet in a three-agent coherent record network:

1. hold every agent's local reduced process fixed;
2. hold final pairwise record overlaps and ordinary communication resources
   fixed;
3. vary only the topology and order of certified reconciliation maps;
4. retain complete provenance and both successful and failed ports;
5. measure any loop phase, joint-outcome difference, extra resource cost,
   public logical-algebra change and identity/gluing ambiguity; and
6. subtract ordinary unitary ordering, geometric phase, decoherence,
   communication, memory and authentication effects.

A surviving gauge-invariant loop residue would advance
`CONCEPT-DU-006`. Capacity collapse without a loop residue would still
advance `CONCEPT-DU-007`. A residual reconciliation lower bound would advance
`CONCEPT-DU-008`. A scaling transition in independently recoverable
actionable facts would advance `CONCEPT-DU-009`. Complete absorption by the
standard controls would kill those formalizations without deleting their
concept families.

No lane priority, ontology, claim bank, prediction seed or publication seed
changes in this triage.
