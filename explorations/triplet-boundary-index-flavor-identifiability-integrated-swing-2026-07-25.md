
The first arrow is a standard index-multiplication mechanism. The second

a flux, defect number, boundary condition, rank, or projector because it

Source ownership is provenance-sensitive. Two identical final operators with
different construction histories have identical spectra and indices. No
terminal operator calculation can certify which history occurred; the source
action and construction map must be audited independently.

### Access-three is not index-three

Time as Finality, Possibility to Capability, and Dynamic Unity make a valuable

region, decoder, or intervention boundary may expose three modes from a
globally vectorlike or larger structure. That is an **effective capability
rank**, not automatically a fundamental chiral index.

Standard EFT decoupling or boundary localization absorbs that interpretation
unless the record/access structure contributes a new invariant or prediction.

## Formal kernel

Let \(T\) be a finite-dimensional carrier and let
\(q:H_+\rightarrow H_-\) be Fredholm.

### Lemma 1 — carrier–boundary index multiplication

\[
\operatorname{ind}(q\otimes I_T)
   = \dim(T)\operatorname{ind}(q).
\]

For finite \(T\),

\[
\ker(q\otimes I_T)\cong\ker(q)\otimes T,
\qquad
\operatorname{coker}(q\otimes I_T)
  \cong\operatorname{coker}(q)\otimes T.
\]

Subtracting dimensions proves the result. Thus a target-independent
unit-index boundary operator acting identically on a triplet conditionally
produces index three. The boundary contributes chirality; the carrier
contributes the integer three. The mechanism does not independently derive
three a second time.

The finite executable proxy uses

\[
q=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0\\
0&1&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\operatorname{ind}(q)=1.
\]

For \(T=\mathbb C^3\), \(q\otimes I_3\) has a three-dimensional kernel and
zero cokernel. Sweeping \(\dim T=1,\ldots,5\) returns the carrier dimension
exactly, confirming that the output count is inherited from the carrier.

### Countermodel 1 — target-coded three

A scalar carrier with a map from four dimensions to one dimension also has
index three. Nothing in its terminal index distinguishes it from an
independently derived construction. This is the target-import control:
rectangularity or topological charge chosen to equal three is not an
explanation of three.

### Countermodel 2 — effective three from global index zero

The vectorlike completion

\[
(q\oplus q^\dagger)\otimes I_3
\]

has global index zero, with three kernel and three cokernel modes. Restricting
access to one boundary returns capability rank three; enlarging access returns
six modes. Consequently:

> Observing three accessible low-energy modes does not identify a fundamental
> global index of three.

This is a finite constructive nonidentification result, not a theorem about
the Standard Model's complete physical completion class.

### Lemma 2 — full-equivariance flavor degeneracy

If \(T\) is an irreducible complex triplet representation and an effective
flavor endomorphism \(F:T\rightarrow T\) commutes with the complete triplet
action, Schur's lemma gives

\[
F=\lambda I_T.
\]

It cannot split three masses or select a mixing basis. The executable real
spin-one control independently solves all commutant equations: eight
independent constraints on nine endomorphism coordinates leave only the
one-dimensional scalar commutant.

### Lemma 3 — one common cyclic action cannot generate mixing

Let \(P^3=I\), and suppose two normal effective flavor operators are functions
of the same \(P\):

\[
Y_u=f_u(P),\qquad Y_d=f_d(P).
\]

Then \([Y_u,Y_d]=0\), so they are simultaneously diagonalizable. Their
relative mixing is only phases and permutations. The executable control uses
two nondegenerate Hermitian circulant matrices and obtains zero commutator and
the same Fourier eigenbasis.

Therefore an order-three action alone is not enough to obtain realistic
relative flavor mixing. At least two differently oriented, noncommuting
triplet-breaking structures—or a mathematically equivalent mechanism—must
survive zero-mode projection.

### Positive control — noncommuting breaking can fit

The probe constructs two noncommuting Hermitian operators with three chosen
eigenvalues and a nontrivial chosen relative rotation. It recovers both
exactly. This confirms mathematical sufficiency while demonstrating the
scientific problem: unrestricted operators can insert any hierarchy and
mixing.

The return is `FLAVOR_FIT_ONLY`, not explanation.
