## Visible clusters

The sixty-one paths cluster around:

- extracting persistent viable observers from the exact Clock QCA;
- testing whether causal posts actively regulate later eras;
- identifying minimal intervention covers, overlapping coalitions, and
  refinement quotients;
- comparing viability with RG, causal emergence, Markov blankets, and
  effective information;
- measuring locality, access, message, state, latency, and variety costs;
- distinguishing fixed robustness, adaptation, controller generation, and
  self-authorship;
- tracking opportunity, action, repair, copy, and durable-record provenance;
- testing how inferred organization changes with resolution or disturbance
  family; and
- considering continuum or scale-free organization with no privileged depth.

This clustering is for retrieval only. It does not merge source IDs or imply a
priority.
