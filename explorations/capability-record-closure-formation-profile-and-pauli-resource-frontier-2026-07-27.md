# Capability-record closure, physical formation profile, and the Pauli resource frontier

## Executive verdict

`HC-DU-064` identified a canonical lattice of operational record
requirements. It did not determine whether those requirements can be
physically formed together. The present result closes that exact boundary:

> The semantic joint requirement generated by two individually formable
> record requirements need not be physically formable under the same resource
> contract.

The four Pauli channels provide a minimum exact witness. Under a budget of
one channel use, no ancilla, one preparation/readout route, and a formed
one-bit archive, each of the three atomic requirements \(q_Z,q_X,q_Y\) can be
formed separately. Any two atoms close semantically to the complete
four-channel distinction. Yet no one-use unassisted experiment can write that
four-valued record: four perfectly distinguishable outputs cannot fit in a
two-dimensional output space.

The complete record has two exact, resource-incomparable formation routes:

1. **Repetition route:** two unassisted channel uses, product preparations and
   measurements, and a four-valued archive.
2. **Entanglement route:** one channel use, one maximally entangled reference
   qubit, a joint Bell measurement, and a four-valued archive.

The lower bounds are exact. In the one-use route the reference dimension must
be at least two; the input must be maximally entangled; the measurement must
jointly distinguish the Bell basis; and the durable value archive needs at
least four distinguishable states, or two classical bits. In the unassisted
route at least two uses are required and sufficient.

This produces a second non-chain structure above the `HC-DU-064` lattice:
the same closed record requirement can have several Pareto-minimal physical
formation receipts. There is no scalar “finality cost” until exchange rates
between process uses, entanglement, joint access, archive support, latency,
and other resources are independently supplied.

The mathematics is standard quantum-channel discrimination and dense coding.
The Dynamic Unity result is the typed bridge:

```text
semantic capability-record closure
    != physical joint realizability
    != resource-Pareto formation profile
    != endogenous selection of one formation route.
```

## 2. Frozen typed contract

Let:

- \(X\) be a finite complete process class;
- \(\mathcal Q\) be a frozen family of physical response queries;
- \(L\) be the closed capability--record lattice from `HC-DU-064`;
- \(\mathcal B\) be a preordered set of componentwise resource budgets;
- \(\mathcal I_b\) be the physical record interfaces admissible under budget
  \(b\in\mathcal B\); and
- \(r_i:X\to R_i\) be the formed record map of interface \(i\).

Every interface includes its preparation, process uses, reference systems,
measurement, occurrence token, setting and validity provenance, output
archive, decoder, access route, and retained action horizon. The value
alphabet and provenance metadata remain separate resource entries.

For a closed record relation \(E\in L\), define its exact formation receipts:

\[
\mathsf{Form}_{=}(E)
=
\operatorname{Min}_{\mathcal B}
\left\{
b:
\exists i\in\mathcal I_b,\ \ker r_i=E
\right\}.
\]

Define its sufficient formation receipts:

\[
\mathsf{Form}_{\le}(E)
=
\operatorname{Min}_{\mathcal B}
\left\{
b:
\exists i\in\mathcal I_b,\ \ker r_i\subseteq E
\right\}.
\]

`Min` returns the Pareto-minimal receipts. It need not return one element.
Surplus resources may be ignored, so feasibility is monotone under budget
enlargement.

These profiles do not assign an intrinsic cost to information. They classify
physical implementations relative to a declared resource preorder.

## 3. Closure does not imply formation

### Theorem 1 — semantic join is not a physical join

Let \(E=K(A)\) and \(F=K(B)\) be closed record requirements. The
capability-side join generated by \(A\) and \(B\) has record relation
\(E\cap F=K(A\cup B)\). Even if
\(\mathsf{Form}_{=}(E)\) and \(\mathsf{Form}_{=}(F)\) are nonempty under one
budget \(b\), it does not follow that \(E\cap F\) is formable under \(b\).

### Proof

Individual feasibility means that there is an interface \(i_E\in\mathcal I_b\)
forming \(E\) and, as an alternative, an interface
\(i_F\in\mathcal I_b\) forming \(F\). It does not assert the existence of one
interface that jointly implements both selective processes. Such an
interface exists only when the pair is in the joint-realizability complex of
the frozen physical contract. The Pauli specimen below has three individually
feasible atoms and an infeasible top under the same one-use unassisted
budget, providing an exact counterexample. \(\square\)

### Corollary 1 — semantic closure does not identify resource geometry

The abstract lattice \(L\) cannot determine
\(\mathsf{Form}_{=}\) or \(\mathsf{Form}_{\le}\). Two physical architectures
can implement the same query maps and kernels while differing in which
interfaces coexist, which records are accessible together, and which
resources their formation consumes.

This is a nonidentification result, not an impossibility of physical joins.

## 4. The frozen Pauli specimen

Use the process class

\[
\mathcal P_{a,b}(\rho)
=
X^aZ^b\rho Z^bX^a,
\qquad
(a,b)\in\mathbb F_2^2.
\]

The three atomic testers are:

\[
\begin{aligned}
q_Z &: \lvert0\rangle\ \text{input and \(Z\) readout},&
q_Z(a,b)&=a,\\
q_X &: \lvert+\rangle\ \text{input and \(X\) readout},&
q_X(a,b)&=b,\\
q_Y &: \lvert+i\rangle\ \text{input and \(Y\) readout},&
q_Y(a,b)&=a\oplus b.
\end{aligned}
\]

Each tester uses one instance of the unknown channel, no reference system,
one binary measurement, and one blank output bit. Each therefore physically
forms its atomic one-bit record under the same resource bound, as alternative
interfaces.

The `HC-DU-064` closure theorem gives:

\[
K(\{q_i,q_j\})=\Delta_X
\quad(i\ne j).
\]

Thus any two atoms semantically require the complete label \((a,b)\).

## 5. Exact lower bound without a reference

### Theorem 2 — one unassisted use cannot form the top record

No one-use strategy without an ancillary reference can perfectly identify
all four Pauli channels.

### Proof

For any input density operator \(\rho\) on the qubit, the four possible output
states are

\[
\rho_{a,b}=X^aZ^b\rho Z^bX^a.
\]

Perfect discrimination requires their supports to be pairwise orthogonal.
Every \(\rho_{a,b}\) is a nonzero density operator in a two-dimensional
Hilbert space. At most two nonzero subspaces can be pairwise orthogonal
there. Hence four-way perfect discrimination is impossible. \(\square\)

The argument allows an arbitrary qubit input and arbitrary output POVM. It is
not limited to the three named testers. Classical randomization with a
retained seed cannot help: unit success on average requires unit success on
every positive-probability seed branch, and each branch is excluded by the
same two-dimensional argument.

## 6. Two exact formation routes

### Theorem 3 — two unassisted uses are sufficient

Use the unknown channel twice with the same process identity.

1. Prepare \(\lvert0\rangle\), apply the first use, and measure \(Z\) to
   obtain \(a\).
2. Prepare \(\lvert+\rangle\), apply the second use, and measure \(X\) to
   obtain \(b\).

Writing \((a,b)\) to a four-valued archive gives a formed record with kernel
\(\Delta_X\). Theorem 2 proves that two is the minimum number of uses without
a reference system.

### Theorem 4 — one entanglement-assisted use is sufficient

Prepare

\[
\lvert\Phi^+\rangle
=
\frac{\lvert00\rangle+\lvert11\rangle}{\sqrt2}
\]

on process qubit \(S\) and reference qubit \(R\). Apply the unknown Pauli
channel to \(S\). The four possible states

\[
(X^aZ^b\otimes I)\lvert\Phi^+\rangle
\]

are the four orthogonal Bell states, up to irrelevant phase. A Bell-basis
measurement returns \((a,b)\) in one use. Writing that label to a four-valued
archive forms the top record.

Under Clifford conjugations of the Pauli specimen, channel labels and Bell
outcomes merely permute. The formation kernel is therefore covariant under
the remaining automorphisms of the frozen process family.

## 7. Exact one-use resource necessities

### Theorem 5 — reference dimension and maximal entanglement

Any one-use strategy that perfectly identifies all four Pauli channels needs
a reference space of dimension at least two. With the minimum two-dimensional
reference, the input must be a pure maximally entangled state.

### Proof

Dimension one is the unassisted case excluded by Theorem 2.

For a two-dimensional reference, all four output states live in a
four-dimensional space and have the same rank as the input. Four pairwise
orthogonal nonzero supports fit only if each output has rank one, so the input
is pure. Write it as \(\lvert\psi\rangle_{SR}\) with reduced state
\(\rho_S\).

Orthogonality of every distinct Pauli-transformed pair requires

\[
\langle\psi|
(P_j^\dagger P_k\otimes I)
|\psi\rangle
=
\operatorname{tr}(\rho_S P_j^\dagger P_k)
=0
\quad(j\ne k).
\]

The nonidentity products span \(X,Y,Z\). Hence

\[
\operatorname{tr}(\rho_S X)
=
\operatorname{tr}(\rho_S Y)
=
\operatorname{tr}(\rho_S Z)
=0,
\]

so \(\rho_S=I/2\). A pure bipartite state with maximally mixed qubit
reduction has Schmidt rank two with equal coefficients: it is maximally
entangled. \(\square\)

### Corollary 2 — measurement and archive lower bounds

The one-use outputs are four Bell states. Perfect readout needs a measurement
that distinguishes their four-dimensional joint basis; a Bell measurement
or an operationally equivalent global four-outcome measurement suffices.
LOCC cannot perfectly distinguish even arbitrary triples of Bell states, so
local readout plus classical communication is not an equivalent free
replacement.

Any exact classical value archive needs at least four distinguishable states:

\[
|R|\ge4,
\qquad
\log_2|R|\ge2.
\]

That is only the value lower bound. Setting, trial identity, validity,
preparation, measurement, and access provenance require additional retained
structure.

## 8. The resource-Pareto frontier

For the complete Pauli record, two Pareto-minimal receipts are:

| route | channel uses | quantum reference | measurement | minimum value archive |
|---|---:|---|---|---:|
| repetition | 2 | none | product \(Z\) and \(X\) readouts | 4 states / 2 bits |
| entanglement | 1 | one maximally entangled qubit | joint Bell-basis readout | 4 states / 2 bits |

Theorem 2 proves that the repetition route cannot use fewer unassisted
queries. Theorem 5 proves that the entanglement route cannot remove or weaken
its minimum reference resource. Corollary 2 fixes the output-alphabet floor.

Without an externally justified exchange rate between one additional process
use and one ebit plus joint measurement access, neither route is the unique
least-cost formation. The profile is a Pareto antichain.

This matters for regional finality:

> Non-chain structure occurs both among semantic record requirements and
> among the physical routes that form one fixed requirement.

## 9. Classical architecture control

Return to \(X=\mathbb F_2^2\) and
\(q_1=x_1,q_2=x_2,q_\oplus=x_1\oplus x_2\). The semantic closure lattice is
the same \(M_3\).

Two classical physical architectures realize different formation profiles:

1. A co-located source exposes \((x_1,x_2)\) through one joint read port. One
   read plus a four-valued archive forms the top.
2. Split source ports expose one bit per region with no joint access. Every
   atom is individually formable, but the top is not available to either
   region. Two port reads plus pooling, or a new communication/access route,
   form it.

Nothing in the common abstract \(M_3\) lattice identifies which architecture
is present. The delta between distributed systems and quantum mechanics is
therefore not the kernel lattice itself. It lies in the native physical
formation interfaces, compatibility constraints, and resource geometry.

## 11. What changes for Dynamic Unity

### Earned

- Semantic closure and physical joint formation are exactly separate.
- Individual record feasibility does not imply feasibility of their lattice
  join under the same budget.
- The `HC-DU-064` Pauli top has two exact physical formation routes.
- One-use unassisted formation is impossible.

  entanglement, joint Bell discrimination, and a four-valued archive.

  mechanism;

### Stop

- call a derived query free;
