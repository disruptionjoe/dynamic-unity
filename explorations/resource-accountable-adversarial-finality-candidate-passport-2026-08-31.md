# Resource-accountable adversarial finality

## Candidate in plain English

Treat shared physical reality, conditionally, as compatible local records that
become harder to replace as independent physical systems incorporate them.
“Agreement” then means compatible reconstruction, not identical internal
states. “Finality” means a typed lower bound on the physical resources needed
to replace the shared invariant under an allowed intervention—not confidence,
permanence, observer belief, or a consensus metaphor.

This passport does not assume that computation, observers, records, or
intelligence are fundamental. Participant scale, an outside coordinator, and a
global observer remain open. The candidate begins only after a target-blind
physical process selects a material, provenance-bearing record interface.

only after every assumption in the right column is independently realized.

## Formal core

Let `x` be a complete admitted physical state/history packet, `Q(x)` its
invariant shared-reality class, `A` the frozen class of allowed interventions,
and `c(a)` their complete physical cost. Define the finality margin

\[
F_A(x)=\inf\{c(a):a\in A,\ Q(a\cdot x)\ne Q(x)\}.
\]

The definition is meaningful only if:

1. `Q` is invariant under matched representation, gauge, and decoder changes;
2. record identity and incompatibility are fixed before the target test;
3. `A` distinguishes physical interventions from redescriptions;
4. `c` charges every crossed boundary, including support, memory, latency,
   energy, entropy, coherence, trusted parties, blank archives, and displaced
   information; and
5. provenance identifies which physical commitments were corrupted, expended,
   immobilized, or reversed.

Candidate resources include free or control energy, entropy production,
irreversible memory capacity, causal reach, topological charge, coherence,
phase-space restriction, and action cost. None is selected by this passport.

## Hypothesis family and theorem target

The concept invariant is:

> Compatible regional records admit a representation-invariant finality margin
> whose increase is caused by independently accountable physical incorporation,
> not by copying labels, changing decoders, or subdividing one resource owner.

Materially different formalizations may use energy/action barriers,
thermodynamic or irreversible-memory costs, causal-cut capacity, topological
sector change, fault-tolerant code distance, or a hybrid resource vector.

The first safety/accountability theorem target is conditional:

> Below budget `B`, no two admitted participants finalize incompatible gluing
> classes; if incompatible finality occurs, the provenance records identify at
> least `B` units of corrupted, expended, immobilized, or reversed physical
> resource.

A separate liveness theorem must state when fair propagation permits compatible
records to finalize. Safety without liveness is not a physical protocol.

## Exact tension and strongest absorbers

`HC-DU-207` selects a latent holonomy but no material record. `CTS-A3` asks
whether that invariant selects its sampler, archive, provenance, access, and
consumer. RAAF addresses the next tension only: once such a handoff exists,
what makes regional agreement physically robust and resource-accountable?

The strongest absorbers are Byzantine and fault-tolerant consensus,
capability security, error-correcting codes, metastability and large-deviation
barriers, nonequilibrium thermodynamics, decoherence, Quantum Darwinism, and
redundant physical recording. A successful RAAF result must either reduce
cleanly to one of them or exhibit a scoped invariant, theorem, or finite
remainder they do not already supply.

## Smallest discriminator and aggressive kills

Freeze one material record and provenance chain. Compare:

1. a pure decoder/gauge relabeling;
2. subdivision of one resource-bearing participant into many labels;
3. mere copying to dependent carriers; and
4. independently powered, provenance-bearing downstream incorporation.

The candidate requires `Q` and `F_A` to remain unchanged in cases 1–2,
charges actual added resources in 3, and permits a larger replacement margin
in 4 only when independence and provenance are physically certified.

Kill or absorb the candidate if any of the following occurs:

- no target-blind material record or provenance relation exists;
- `Q` changes under gauge, decoder, cover, or matched-interface choice;
- the proposed resource is freely copied, subdivided, or omitted from the
  complete boundary ledger;
- incompatible finality occurs below the claimed bound;
- propagation changes confidence or redundancy but no invariant reversal
  boundary;
- finality is only ordinary metastability, error correction, decoherence, or
  thermodynamic cost with no surviving typed increment; or
- the theory imports the clock, observer, archive, fault model, or public fact
  it claims to derive.
