# Executive result

`HC-DU-216` established that a physical preparation can legitimately select a
critical regime for a conditional experiment without being generated by the
sample's autonomous dynamics. This result applies the same correction to the
instrument layer and reconciles it with `HC-DU-208`.

```text
PREPARED_INSTRUMENT_IS_A_VALID_PHYSICAL_ANTECEDENT
SOURCE_OBSERVABLE_ALONE_DOES_NOT_SELECT_THE_INSTRUMENT
INDIRECT_MEASUREMENT_PACKET_DETERMINES_ONE_CP_INSTRUMENT
STATISTICAL_INSTRUMENT_DOES_NOT_SELECT_ONE_MICROREALIZATION
INSTRUMENT_DOES_NOT_IMPLY_DURABLE_RECORD_HANDOFF
CONDITIONAL_AND_AUTONOMOUS_INTERFACE_SELECTION_ARE_DISTINCT
TARGET_BLIND_PRECOMMITMENT_AND_NO_REFIT_REMAIN_REQUIRED
COMPLETE_MATERIAL_HANDOFF_STILL_OPEN
NO_READY_SUCCESSOR
```

In standard quantum measurement theory, an indirect measurement model is a
physical packet

\[
 a=(\mathcal K,\sigma,U,M),
\]

consisting of a probe Hilbert space, a fixed prepared probe state, a
system--probe interaction, and a meter observable. It determines the
completely positive instrument

\[
 \mathcal I_a(\Delta)(\rho)
 =\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal K}
 \!\left[(I\otimes E^M(\Delta))
 U(\rho\otimes\sigma)U^\dagger\right].
\tag{1}
\]

Thus a physically instantiated and independently calibrated apparatus can
select an instrument **relative to a declared experiment**. It is not merely a
mathematical interface chosen after the outcome. The prediction is conditional:
given the source system and this prepared apparatus, these outcome and
post-measurement statistics follow.

That does not reverse `HC-DU-208`. A source Hamiltonian and its spectral PVM
still admit distinct instruments with identical Born probabilities and
different conditional continuations. The source alone therefore does not
select the instrument. Nor does equation (1) by itself select a durable
archive, causal provenance, observer access route, consumer, reset protocol,
resource horizon, or finality rule. Different physical implementations may
also realize the same operational instrument.

The corrected claim boundary is:

> Conditional experimental reconstruction may take a target-blind,
> precommitted and calibrated instrument packet as a legitimate physical
> antecedent. A claim that classical records, observers, or interfaces emerge
> autonomously from the source dynamics must instead derive or dynamically
> select that packet and its downstream handoff.

This is a scoped Grade-4 typing and nonimplication result. Davies--Lewis
instruments, Ozawa's indirect-measurement realization, ordinary calibration,
system identification, quantum control, and DU's existing fibre theorem absorb
the mathematics. It supplies no new quantum prediction and does not activate a
scientific successor.

# 1. The ambiguity being corrected

DU had two independently earned results that can look inconsistent if
“selected” is left untyped.

`HC-DU-208` proves:

```text
source Hamiltonian + spectral PVM
  does not determine
the physical instrument or conditional continuation.
```

`HC-DU-216` proves:

```text
an independently fixed physical preparation
  can be a valid antecedent
for a conditional response claim.
```

The apparent conflict comes from forcing every explanation into one of two
categories:

1. autonomously selected by the isolated source law; or
2. arbitrarily supplied in the analyst's description.

Physical experiments occupy a third category. An apparatus can be constructed,
prepared, calibrated, and locked before the trial. Its settings are not caused
by the sample Hamiltonian, but they are physical events in the experimental
antecedent. They can carry predictive content without being credited to the
sample.

# 2. Three interface claims that must remain separate

## 2.1 Mathematical interface

A POVM, channel, instrument, pointer basis, or record map is written into the
analysis with no independently fixed physical realization. It can represent a
possible experiment, but it earns no claim that this interface obtains.

## 2.2 Prepared experimental instrument

The probe state, coupling, meter, timing, calibration, and operating envelope
are physically fixed before the held-out outcome. They select one operational
instrument for the conditional experiment. The apparatus is an action-indexed
antecedent, not a consequence of the sample's autonomous law.

## 2.3 Autonomous natural interface

The admitted source-plus-environment dynamics itself makes one stable interface
or interface orbit obtain without an independently chosen experimental packet.
This is the burden for claims about autonomous classicality, spontaneous
objectivity, naturally privileged records, or a unique observer boundary.

The third claim is strictly stronger than the second. Demonstrating the second
does not answer the third; requiring the third before crediting any laboratory
measurement makes ordinary conditional science impossible.

# 3. Exact standard measurement-theory bridge

Davies and Lewis introduced an instrument as the operational object combining
outcome statistics with the associated state operation, rather than identifying
a measurement only with an observable. Ozawa's measurement-process framework
makes the physical antecedent explicit.

Let:

- `H` be the system Hilbert space;
- `K` be the probe Hilbert space;
- `sigma` be the probe state prepared before interaction;
- `U` be the joint system--probe unitary during the measurement interval; and
- `M` be the probe meter observable with spectral measure `E^M`.

Equation (1) determines both:

\[
 \Pr(\Delta\mid\rho)=\operatorname{Tr}[\mathcal I_a(\Delta)(\rho)]
\]

and, when the event has nonzero probability, the conditional output state

\[
 \rho_\Delta=
 \frac{\mathcal I_a(\Delta)(\rho)}
 {\operatorname{Tr}[\mathcal I_a(\Delta)(\rho)]}.
\]

Ozawa's realization theorem states that every indirect measurement model
induces a CP instrument and every CP instrument has a pure indirect measurement
model. The operational correspondence is therefore strong enough for the
present boundary:

```text
fixed physical measurement model
  -> unique operational instrument.
```

It does **not** say:

```text
system Hamiltonian -> unique measurement model;
CP instrument -> unique apparatus microphysics; or
CP instrument -> complete material record handoff.
```

Indeed, apparatuses are grouped by statistical equivalence when they induce
the same operational distribution. The instrument forgets implementation
detail by design.

# 4. Selection is an antecedent-fibre statement

Let `W` be the admitted physical completions of an experiment. Define:

- `s:W -> S`, the source-only antecedent;
- `p:W -> P`, the complete prepared measurement packet;
- `i:W -> Instr`, the realized operational instrument; and
- `xi:W -> Handoff`, the complete material handoff.

A source-only instrument selector exists exactly when

\[
 \ker s\subseteq\ker i.
\tag{2}
\]

`HC-DU-208` gives an exact counterexample to (2): the same Hamiltonian and PVM
support Lüders and within-eigenspace-twisted instruments with equal outcome
statistics and different conditional states.

A prepared-packet selector exists exactly when

\[
 \ker p\subseteq\ker i.
\tag{3}
\]

For the indirect measurement packet with physical equivalence fixed at the
operational level, equation (1) supplies the factorization in (3). The
instrument is therefore selected **relative to `p`**.

Complete record-handoff selection would require

\[
 \ker p\subseteq\ker\xi.
\tag{4}
\]

Equation (1) does not imply (4). Two completions may agree on the probe,
interaction, meter outcomes, and system continuation while differing in
whether the meter value is amplified, retained, causally attributed, routed to
an observer, used by a controller, reset, or protected against replacement.

This is the exact location of the remaining Dynamic Unity burden. The
instrument interface is no longer an undifferentiated mystery, but it is also
not the whole record.

# 5. Prepared-instrument admission contract

A prepared instrument counts as a physical antecedent for a conditional DU
claim only when all applicable fields are frozen before the held-out outcome:

| field | minimum admission evidence |
|---|---|
| source and input class | independently identified physical system and admissible states |
| probe and blank | physically prepared initial apparatus state |
| interaction | fixed coupling, duration, control sequence, and nuisance envelope |
| meter/readout | declared output variable and calibration relation |
| instrument equivalence | explicit operational quotient and tolerated error |
| timing and trial identity | declared occurrence window and attempt census |
| archive and retention | included only if the claim credits a retained record |
| provenance | included only if the claim identifies an upstream occurrence or source |
| access and consumer | included only if the claim credits observer access or capability |
| resources and reset | fixed whenever reliability, reuse, or finality is claimed |
| target | held out before calibration and analysis |
| transfer | no-refit application to held-out states, controls, or trials |

This is not a demand for microscopic knowledge of every apparatus atom. The
packet may be an experimentally validated equivalence class. But the quotient
must be frozen before the target, and unobserved implementation differences
must be shown irrelevant to the claimed target rather than silently declared
gauge.

# 6. Why this is not target smuggling

An intervention becomes a copied selector key when it encodes the desired
answer or is adjusted after seeing the held-out result. A prepared instrument
does not do that merely by choosing what is measured.

It earns conditional predictive content when:

1. the apparatus packet is fixed independently of the outcome;
2. calibration uses disjoint data or locked standards;
3. its induced instrument predicts outcome and continuation statistics for a
   held-out input family;
4. nuisance parameters are frozen or charged; and
5. failure forces revision of the apparatus model rather than post-hoc retuning.

The attribution then reads:

```text
source law + prepared apparatus + input preparation
  -> conditional instrument statistics.
```

It does not read:

```text
source law alone
  -> apparatus choice;

or

instrument statistics
  -> autonomous emergence of records.
```

# 7. Claim-relative disposition

| proposed claim | prepared instrument status |
|---|---|
| outcome law for a calibrated laboratory measurement | valid physical antecedent |
| conditional post-measurement response | valid if the full instrument, not only the POVM, is calibrated |
| retained detector-level event record | requires material formation and archive fields beyond the instrument |
| unique upstream source-history certificate | requires provenance and rival-exclusion tests |
| observer access or action-enabling capability | requires access and consumer couplings |
| autonomous classicality/objectivity | prepared instrument is insufficient; natural interface selection remains open |
| unique ontology of measurement | not addressed by operational instrument equivalence |

This table prevents two opposite errors. DU must not dismiss a calibrated
measurement because nature did not autonomously build the apparatus. It must
also not use the apparatus to claim that the isolated source law selected its
own observer or record ontology.

# 8. Relation to the North Star

The North Star asks for the smallest target-blind physical antecedent that
selects a material, provenance-bearing, observer-indexed record interface and
then supports reconstruction or exposes a finite remainder.

`HC-DU-217` narrows that search:

```text
source-only instrument selection                    [killed in general]
source + prepared measurement model -> instrument  [standard positive]
instrument -> complete record handoff               [not implied]
complete prepared handoff -> conditional target     [live reopener]
source/environment -> autonomous handoff             [stronger open problem]
```

The highest-information next specimen is no longer “find a Hamiltonian that
chooses a PVM.” It is one complete, precommitted experimental packet that
extends an indirect measurement model through blank-to-written carrier,
archive, provenance, access, consumer, resources, and reset, then compares a
law-and-preparation-only target with the record-conditioned target without
refit.

For autonomous-emergence claims, the separate reopener remains: derive a
stable interface orbit from source-plus-environment dynamics and show that it
survives physical symmetry, apparatus, and access rivals.
