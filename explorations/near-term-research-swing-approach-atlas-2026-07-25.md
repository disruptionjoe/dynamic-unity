# Near-term research-swing approach atlas

## Executive result

Dynamic Unity now has one approach card for every plausible near-term research
family represented by the complete 27-candidate paper portfolio, the active
Lane objectives, and the named scientific dependencies that do not yet have a
standalone paper. The atlas answers:

> If a future lead chooses this problem, what is the cheapest rigorous attack
> that can change a decision, what should it preserve, what artifact remains
> useful under every outcome, and when should it stop or spend more?

It does **not** rank the families, authorize execution, change a claim grade,
reopen a post-ready paper, promote a speculative concept, or alter Drafting
Factory custody. The current two-horizon allocation remains unchanged:
`DU-PAPER-013` is the nearer paper route and `DU-PAPER-007` the higher-ceiling
physical flagship.

The existing
[ten-lens hypothesis registry](ten-lens-hypothesis-efficient-approach-registry-2026-07-24.md)
already attaches efficient methods to all 36 technical hypothesis IDs. This
atlas does not copy it. It supplies the missing layer above it: portfolio
family, active dependency, present gate, first decision-changing move,
cross-family reuse, and explicit no-go conditions for premature physical work.

## The five lenses

| ID | Lens | Question it contributes |
|---|---|---|
| `CFS` | Counterfactual statistician | What two worlds, mechanisms, or interventions differ only in the focal feature, and what estimand distinguishes them? |
| `CAT` | Category theorist | What are the typed objects and maps, what is gauge, what must compose, and what must remain natural under benign refinement? |
| `MET` | Adversarial experimentalist and metrologist | What is the smallest complete specimen, strongest ordinary absorber, calibration envelope, and held-out intervention? |
| `FPE` | Formal proof engineer | What is the weakest theorem kernel, smallest countermodel, proof certificate, and independent verifier? |
| `VOI` | Sequential value-of-information strategist | What cheapest first move can change a decision, what output is reusable under every result, and what gate justifies more cost? |

The lenses are method advisers. Their agreement is not scientific evidence.

## Shared contract

For a declared context

\[
c=(\mathcal I,\mathcal O,\mathcal A,\mathcal C,\mathcal L,\mathcal G),
\]

freeze the interventions, observer/access boundary, action or prediction
task, completion/adversary class, resource and loss ledger, and representation
group before observing the result.

When the swing is a record-sufficiency test, use the class-relative
deficiency

\[
\delta(R,\mathcal C)=
\inf_K\sup_{m\in\mathcal C,\;a\in\mathcal I}
\operatorname{TV}\!\left(
P_m(Y\mid do(a)),R_mK_a
\right).
\]

This is an estimand, not an ontology verdict. A completed swing returns one
of:

1. `FACTORIZATION`;
2. `MINIMAL_REFINEMENT`;
3. `CLASS_RELATIVE_REMAINDER`; or
4. `INCOMPLETE_CONTRACT`.

Only a positive lower bound after a physically justified completion class,
complete boundary, and strongest ordinary absorber could approach a physical
remainder.

At the more general portfolio-decision layer, every swing should also end in
one of four routing states:

- `SURVIVES`: the claim survives the declared absorber and may cross its next
  dependency;
- `ABSORBED`: preserve the absorber, countermodel, or merge module and stop
  the overclaim;
- `INCOMPLETE_CONTRACT`: repair only the missing premise, definition, or
  evidence join; or
- `NOT_IDENTIFIED`: preserve the equivalence class and name the minimum
  intervention that could reopen it.

The preferred first move is the smallest reversible action that can change
one of those branches while producing a useful artifact in all four.

For categorical typing, use a small stack rather than one “category of
everything”:

- \(\mathsf{Phys}_c\): complete physical interfaces and composable
  instruments;
- \(\mathsf{Rec}_c\): formed records, certificates, and provenance;
- \(\mathsf{Beh}_c\): admitted predictions and bounded-risk capabilities;
- \(R_c:\mathsf{Phys}_c\rightarrow\mathsf{Rec}_c\): the independently frozen
  record map; and
- \(B_c:\mathsf{Phys}_c\rightarrow\mathsf{Beh}_c\): the behavior map.

A relabeling or refinement is gauge only when both record and behavior maps
factor naturally through its localization. Observer, task, adversary, or
resource changes are index changes, not harmless morphisms.

## Common high-learning-rate sequence

1. **Check authority and gate.** Confirm source ownership, Factory custody,
   dependency state, and maximum grade. A method card is not permission to
   reopen or activate a candidate.
2. **Freeze one decision.** State one exact claim or decision, its rival, the
   completion/refinement class, and what each possible result changes.
3. **Build the counterfactual pair.** Match everything except the proposed
   cause or structure. If the contrast cannot be stated, repair the semantics
   rather than enlarge the calculation.
4. **Type and quotient.** Declare objects, maps, compositions, gauges,
   resource-bearing changes, and benign-refinement tests. Construct a
   same-record fibre pair before claiming reconstruction.
5. **Use the smallest hostile specimen.** Include one positive control and
   the strongest absorber, not merely an easy null.
6. **Emit proof-carrying evidence.** Prefer exact arithmetic, symbolic
   conditions, primal/dual certificates, minimized countermodels, and a small
   independent verifier. Mutate assumptions and certificates to ensure the
   checker rejects false conclusions.
7. **Spend sequentially.** Increase theorem breadth, simulation size,
   apparatus cost, literature scope, or manuscript work only after the cheap
   gate survives.
8. **Write a decision receipt.** Preserve the contract, result, uncertainty,
   local-versus-concept failure scope, reusable artifact, and exact
   stop/reopen condition.

For physical work, preserve joined trial, calibration, process-convention,
and multitime/reset tables. Aggregates and plotted means cannot reconstruct
missing trials, invalids, provenance, controller versions, selective-map
conventions, or causal-break state.
