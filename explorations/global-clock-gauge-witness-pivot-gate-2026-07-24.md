paper-closing swing in its currently advertised general form.

> a hidden scheduler is gauge exactly when every admitted behavior and

## Frozen contract

### Scheduler-resolved process

Let \(P=(E,\preceq)\) be a finite causal/event partial order and let
\(\operatorname{Lin}(P)\) be its linear extensions. A scheduler-resolved
history contains:

\[
\widetilde h=(h,\lambda,k),
\]

where:

- \(h\) retains the event identities, causal edges, physical state changes,
  messages, records, and provenance;
- \(\lambda\in\operatorname{Lin}(P)\) is a selected total update order; and
- \(k\) contains any scheduler phase, layer, tick, parity, or resource state
  not already present in \(h\).

The target-independent forgetful map

\[
q_K:\widetilde{\mathcal H}\longrightarrow\mathcal H
\]

removes only \((\lambda,k)\). It may not discard a carrier, field, energy
cost, memory cell, phase, record, or controller merely because that object
would reveal the scheduler.

Define

\[
\widetilde h\sim_K\widetilde h'
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
q_K(\widetilde h)=q_K(\widetilde h').
\]

The equivalence is frozen before inspecting the desired clock verdict.

### Complete admitted experiment algebra

Freeze a compositionally closed experiment class \(\mathsf A\) generated by:

1. admissible preparations and boundary conditions;
2. local, message, field, defect, boost, reunion, and closed-loop
   interventions;
3. adaptive controllers and all admitted serial/parallel compositions;
4. the physical record instrument, readout, provenance, and access maps;
5. resource observations for support, update opportunities, stored records,
   latency, energy, coherence, trusted control, and displaced information;
6. declared bounded-risk capability readouts; and
7. the fixed representation maps used for reslicing or discrete boosts.

Every context \(a\in\mathsf A\) induces a complete behavior map

\[
B_a:\widetilde{\mathcal H}\rightarrow\Delta(\mathcal O_a)
\]

in a stochastic model, or the corresponding controlled channel/instrument in
a quantum model. Terminal state alone is not \(B_a\).

### Gauge, hidden physical, and incomplete

- **Gauge relative to the frozen contract:** every \(B_a\), transition,
  record instrument, resource functional, and capability map descends through
  \(q_K\), and the descent is stable under every admitted composition.
- **Hidden but physical:** ordinary transcripts agree, but some admitted
  context distinguishes a \(\sim_K\) pair.
- **Directly observable:** a scheduler tag or resource is already present in
  an admitted readout.
- **Incomplete:** the record instrument, quotient, intervention closure,
  representation family, or resource boundary is not independently fixed.

`Operationally nonidentified by the experiments run so far` is not a gauge
verdict.

## Strongest unified finite theorem target

### Scheduler Gauge-or-Local-Leakage Theorem

Consider a finite scheduler-resolved controlled process satisfying the frozen
contract above. Assume:

1. any two linear extensions of one causal history are connected by adjacent
   exchanges of consecutive incomparable events;
2. the admitted experiment class is generated under composition from a
   finite declared set;
3. record, provenance, resource, and capability readouts are included rather
   than appended after the quotient; and
4. the representation/boost maps and record decoder are fixed before testing
   scheduler dependence.

Then the following are equivalent:

1. **Operational gauge.** Every complete admitted experiment gives identical
   behavior on every \(\sim_K\)-equivalent pair.
2. **Factorization.** For every \(a\in\mathsf A\), there is a unique
   \(\bar B_a\) with

   \[
   B_a=\bar B_a\circ q_K,
   \]

   and \(\sim_K\) is a congruence for all admitted transitions and
   compositions.
3. **Local swap condition.** Every reachable adjacent exchange of
   incomparable events has zero complete instrument defect in every reachable
   admitted context, and the resulting quotient commutes with every declared
   boost/reslicing map.

If these conditions fail, there is:

- a reachable adjacent incomparable-event swap;
- an admitted finite context \(w\);
- a record/resource/capability readout \(Y\); and
- a nonzero operational difference

\[
\delta_K(w,Y)
=
d\!\left(
B_{w,Y}(\widetilde h),
B_{w,Y}(\widetilde h')
\right)>0.
\]

That tuple is a **scheduler-leakage witness**. In the existing finite
deterministic state-output convention, the first separating intervention word
has length at most \(n-b_0\); in a finite linear realization it has length at
most reachable distinction rank minus one. Those bounds are inherited from
`HC-DU-036A`, not proposed as new clock theorems.

### What “smallest” must mean

The paper may not nominate the carried remote record or reunion assay as
universally smallest merely because it is smallest in one hand-built fixture.
For a frozen finite model, compute the Pareto-minimal witness tuple

\[
\left(
|w|,
\text{number of controlled ports},
\text{physical support/resource cost},
\delta_K(w,Y)
\right),
\]

with context length minimized first and operational gap maximized only among
equal-cost witnesses. If no finite basis or complexity bound is frozen, report
`NO UNIFORM MINIMALITY CLAIM`.

### Proof skeleton already available

The abstract proof has three short parts:

1. factorization through \(q_K\) is equivalent to constancy on its fibres;
2. congruence on the declared generators extends that constancy to generated
   contexts; and
3. connectivity of the linear-extension graph localizes any global scheduler
   difference to at least one adjacent incomparable swap, after which
   `HC-DU-036A` supplies the finite distinguishing-context search and bound.

That skeleton is useful infrastructure. By itself it is not a
literature-distinct paper contribution.

### Collision verdict

The general theorem is a conjunction of standard quotient, trace,
refinement, and bisimulation facts. The causal-set literature supplies an
especially close physical analogue: label/birth order is gauge only when the
dynamics and observables are covariant under it.

The plausible unoccupied delta is narrower:

1. a **Clock-QCA-specific, instrument-level** scheduler quotient;
2. a proof that its exact encoded boosts and scheduler quotient commute;
3. a **complete finite basis or tight bound** for Lorentz-sensitive leakage
   experiments; and
4. a result that includes physical records, provenance, and resources rather
   than only solution, path-probability, or visible-trace equivalence.

This scan did not establish that delta as novel.

## Missing proof

The load-bearing missing result is not the elementary factorization
equivalence. It is:

> **Clock-QCA scheduler-defect classification.** For one independently fixed
> finite observer-record extension of the exact Clock QCA, classify all
> admitted scheduler/reslicing fibres and prove either that the complete
> controlled record instrument is natural on those fibres, or that every
> failure is detected by a finite preregistered witness family with a tight
> minimality statement.

Specifically missing:

1. a frozen physical meaning for update opportunity, formed record, copied
   record, rejected update, and scheduler state;
2. an exact scheduler groupoid compatible with the source's encoded boost and
   global-rescaling maps;
3. a proof that the record/archive instrument is a congruence—or the exact
   defect when it is not;
4. a completeness theorem for the witness family, not five successful
   examples;
5. a nontrivial lower bound on witness context, ports, support, or resources;
6. higher-dimensional or sharply scoped `1+1` language that does not present
   construction-specific covariance as generic Lorentz invariance; and
7. a claim-specific novelty review after the theorem is known.

## Hard kill conditions

Kill `DU-PAPER-015` as a standalone paper if any one of these remains true
after the pre-proof:

1. **Definition kill:** the headline theorem is only constancy on quotient
   fibres iff factorization.
2. **Trace kill:** scheduler equivalence is exactly a Mazurkiewicz trace
   quotient with no new physical constraint, converse, or bound.
3. **Bisimulation kill:** “complete intervention algebra” is only contextual
   equivalence or branching/weak bisimulation renamed.
4. **Refinement kill:** the result is ordinary hiding, stuttering invariance,
   or a refinement map after auxiliary scheduler variables are erased.
5. **CSG covariance kill:** the physical content is no stronger than natural
   labeling/path-probability invariance already required by discrete general
   covariance.
6. **Inserted-tag kill:** the smallest witness is a readable scheduler bit
   added expressly to make the scheduler observable.
7. **Fitted-kernel kill:** a `0.25/0.75` or analogous future kernel is chosen
   after the desired distinction rather than selected by the physical model.
8. **Example-minimality kill:** no lower bound proves that carried reflection,
   reunion, or a loop is minimal in the declared class.
9. **Finite-fit kill:** failure to find leakage in the sampled finite traces
   is reported as gauge without a complexity/finiteness bound.
10. **Incomplete-algebra kill:** adaptive controls, resources, provenance,
    decoder/access changes, or relevant boundary crossings are omitted.
11. **Quotient-refit kill:** the scheduler quotient changes across frames,
    implementations, or outcomes.
12. **QCA-analogy kill:** the exact Clock QCA appears only as a positive
    citation and no observer-record instrument is evaluated on its encoded
    boosts.
13. **Lorentz-insertion kill:** time dilation, rod contraction, or the
    preferred-frame response is inserted into the gate law and then
    “recovered.”
14. **Causal-order inflation kill:** a total scheduler, causal order, local
    record density, and spacetime volume are conflated.
15. **Ontology kill:** operational quotient status is presented as proof that
    a global clock does or does not fundamentally exist.

## Final verdict

```text
NARROW
```

`DU-PAPER-015` is a worthwhile bounded pre-proof, but not yet the smartest
full pivot. Its broad theorem is substantially standard. The only credible
standalone route is the physical specialization: an independently fixed
Clock-QCA record instrument, a commuting scheduler/boost quotient, and a
tight minimal Lorentz-sensitive leakage theorem.

If that result cannot be stated without target-fitting before any new
computation, pivot rather than circling the clock question.
