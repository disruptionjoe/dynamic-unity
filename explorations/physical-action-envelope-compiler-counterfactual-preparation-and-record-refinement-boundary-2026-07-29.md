# Physical action-envelope compiler and counterfactual preparation boundary

## Executive result

`HC-DU-137` stopped finite apparatus variants because a fixed-action response
quotient cannot select the actions that define it. This swing tests the exact
reopener: can a physical controller select those actions without inserting a
semantic list by hand?

The answer is conditional and exact:

```text
PHYSICAL_CONTROLLER_PACKET_COMPILES_ACTION_ENVELOPE
+ ACTION_ENVELOPE_COMPILES_RESPONSE_QUOTIENT
+ CONTROLLER_RESOURCE_ENLARGEMENT_REFINES_THE_QUOTIENT
+ SOURCE_OR_DRIFT_ONLY_ACTION_ENVELOPE_NONSELECTION

+ COUNTERFACTUAL_PREPARATION_RESOURCE_CLASS_REMAINS_TYPED
+ MATHEMATICAL_CORE_ABSORBED
+ NO_READY_SUCCESSOR
```

Once the system, physical controller, joint dynamics, reachable controller
preparations, resource limits, readout, and horizon are fixed, the induced
action envelope is no longer an arbitrary semantic list. It is computed from
that physical packet. In finite Hamiltonian control this is the reachable
group generated by the dynamical Lie algebra. In a programmable controller it
is the family of induced channels generated by the physically reachable
program states.

But one actual trajectory, a drift/source law, or a processor alone does not
determine which counterfactual preparations are physically admitted. Two
packets can contain exactly the same actual controller execution while
assigning different reachable preparation sets and therefore different
action envelopes. The hidden premise has been narrowed:

> Dynamics can derive capability relative to a physically typed
> controller/preparation/resource packet. Actual evolution alone does not
> identify that modal packet.

This is a scoped Grade-4 selection/nonselection and typing boundary plus a
conditional Grade-3 response reconstruction. It is not a new quantum-control
theorem, a new programmable-processor theorem, a universal claim that physics
cannot select counterfactual tasks, a selected observer in nature, a record
ontology, a physical remainder, a prediction, a new law, or new physics.

## 2. Typed physical packet

Let a controller-relative action packet be

\[
\Pi
=
(\mathcal H_S,\mathcal H_C,G,\mathsf P,\mathsf R,B,\tau).
\]

Its fields are:

- \(\mathcal H_S\): physical system;
- \(\mathcal H_C\): physical controller/program system;
- \(G\): fixed joint dynamics or processor;
- \(\mathsf P\): physically reachable controller preparations under the
  declared starting and resource conditions;
- \(\mathsf R\): reset and reuse rule;
- \(B\): resource and reliability contract; and
- \(\tau\): action horizon.

For each \(p\in\mathsf P\), let

\[
\Phi_p(\rho)
=
\operatorname{Tr}_C
\!\left[
G(\rho\otimes p)G^\dagger
\right]
\]

with any declared outcome conditioning kept in the type rather than hidden.
The packet-relative action envelope is

\[
\mathcal A_\Pi
=
\{\Phi_p:p\in\mathsf P
\text{ and }p\text{ satisfies }(\mathsf R,B,\tau)\}.
\tag{1}
\]

For a physical history or completion \(m\), its complete admitted response
signature is

\[
\Sigma_\Pi(m)
=
\big(\operatorname{law}(Y\mid m,\Phi)\big)_{\Phi\in\mathcal A_\Pi}.
\tag{2}
\]

The induced response record is the quotient

\[
m\sim_\Pi m'
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\Sigma_\Pi(m)=\Sigma_\Pi(m')
\tag{3}
\]

up to the packet's declared representation equivalences.

Equation (1) is the missing compiler. Equation (3) is the already banked
fixed-action quotient. Keeping them separate prevents the quotient from
quietly selecting its own tests.

## 3. Proposition A — controller-relative action selection

### Statement

For every complete packet \(\Pi\), equation (1) defines a unique action
envelope up to physical equivalence of the packet.

If

\[
(U_S,U_C):\Pi\longrightarrow\Pi'
\]

intertwines the system, controller, processor, reachable preparations,
resources, reset, and horizon, then

\[
\mathcal A_{\Pi'}
=
\{\operatorname{Ad}_{U_S}\circ\Phi\circ
\operatorname{Ad}_{U_S^\dagger}:
\Phi\in\mathcal A_\Pi\}.
\]

Thus the construction is representation-natural relative to the **complete**
packet. It does not select a preferred basis.

### Proof

The map \(p\mapsto\Phi_p\) is determined by the fixed joint dynamics and
partial trace. Restricting its image to the physically reachable and
resource-admissible preparation set gives equation (1). An intertwiner maps
each admitted preparation to its counterpart and conjugates the induced
system channel. Therefore the image action family is preserved up to the
declared physical equivalence. \(\square\)

### Grade

This is a conditional selector, not a source-only selector. The controller
packet contains the physical facts from which the action family is compiled.

## 4. Hamiltonian-control specialization

Consider a closed finite system with

\[
H(t)=H_0+\sum_{j=1}^{k}u_j(t)H_j
\]

and a fixed admissible control-function class. The generated dynamical Lie
algebra is

\[
\mathfrak g_\Pi
=
\operatorname{Lie}
\{-iH_0,-iH_1,\ldots,-iH_k\}.
\]

Subject to the usual finite-dimensional controllability hypotheses and the
declared timing/control bounds, \(\mathfrak g_\Pi\) determines the reachable
unitary group or its bounded-horizon subset. This is the control-theoretic
form of the compiler.

The smallest exact nonselection witness uses one qubit:

\[
H_0=Z.
\]

Packet \(\Pi_Z\) admits no independent control generator, so

\[
\dim\operatorname{Lie}\{-iZ\}=1.
\]

Packet \(\Pi_{ZX}\) has the same drift and admits \(H_1=X\). Since

\[
[Z,X]=2iY,
\]

the generated algebra is \(\mathfrak{su}(2)\), of dimension three.

Therefore the drift/source dynamics alone does not select one action
envelope. If

\[
a_{\rm drift}(\Pi)=H_0,
\]

then:

\[
\ker a_{\rm drift}
\not\subseteq
\ker(\Pi\mapsto\mathcal A_\Pi).
\tag{4}
\]

The extra control coupling and admissibility contract are physical rather
than semantic, but they are extra antecedents.

## 5. Programmable-controller witness

Let both system and program be qubits. Fix one joint processor

\[
G
=
|0\rangle\!\langle0|_C\otimes I_S
+
|1\rangle\!\langle1|_C\otimes H_S.
\tag{5}
\]

Two preparation contracts over the same processor are:

\[
\mathsf P_0=\{|0\rangle\!\langle0|\},
\qquad
\mathsf P_{01}
=
\{|0\rangle\!\langle0|,|1\rangle\!\langle1|\}.
\]

They compile:

\[
\mathcal A_0=\{I\},
\qquad
\mathcal A_{01}=\{I,H\}.
\tag{6}
\]

Now keep a fixed computational-basis readout and compare histories

\[
|+\rangle=\frac{|0\rangle+|1\rangle}{\sqrt2},
\qquad
|-\rangle=\frac{|0\rangle-|1\rangle}{\sqrt2}.
\]

Under \(\mathcal A_0\), both yield

\[
P_Z=(1/2,1/2),
\]

so the response quotient identifies them. Under \(\mathcal A_{01}\),

\[
H|+\rangle=|0\rangle,
\qquad
H|-\rangle=|1\rangle,
\]

and the quotient separates them.

This gives the exact refinement:

\[
\mathcal A_0\subsetneq\mathcal A_{01}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sim_{\Pi_{01}}\ \text{strictly refines}\ \sim_{\Pi_0}.
\tag{7}
\]

It also gives the modal kill. Suppose the actual run uses program
\(|0\rangle\) in both worlds. That actual joint trajectory is identical
whether the admitted counterfactual preparation set was
\(\mathsf P_0\) or \(\mathsf P_{01}\). Yet equations (6)--(7) assign different
capability and record quotients.

Therefore:

\[
\ker(\text{actual processor history})
\not\subseteq
\ker(\text{counterfactual action envelope}).
\tag{8}
\]

No claim about hidden metaphysical possibilities is needed. Equation (8) is
ordinary antecedent nonidentification: a singleton actual execution does not
encode which alternative controller preparations the physical contract
admits.

## 6. Proposition B — resource monotonicity

Let packets \(\Pi\) and \(\Pi'\) agree except that every preparation and
controller protocol admitted by \(\Pi\) is also admitted by \(\Pi'\). Then

\[
\mathcal A_\Pi\subseteq\mathcal A_{\Pi'}.
\]

Consequently:

\[
\Sigma_{\Pi'}(m)=\Sigma_{\Pi'}(m')
\Longrightarrow
\Sigma_\Pi(m)=\Sigma_\Pi(m'),
\]

so:

\[
\sim_{\Pi'}\ \text{refines}\ \sim_\Pi.
\tag{9}
\]

The inclusion may be strict, as equation (7) proves.

This is the precise form of a theme that appeared repeatedly across DU:
observer capability is not an intrinsic label on the observed system.
Changing physical control, access, reset, horizon, or resources can change
which distinctions become actionable without changing the system's actual
past.

## 7. Proposition C — exact finite programming limit

For a fixed deterministic programmable quantum processor that exactly
implements distinct unitaries \(U\) and \(V\), Nielsen and Chuang's
no-programming argument implies that their input program states must be
orthogonal unless \(U\) and \(V\) differ only by a global phase.

The inner-product spine is:

\[
\langle p_U|p_V\rangle\langle\psi|\phi\rangle
=
\langle p_U'|p_V'\rangle
\langle\psi|U^\dagger V|\phi\rangle.
\]

If the input programs have nonzero overlap for all \(\psi,\phi\), then
\(U^\dagger V\) must be proportional to the identity. Hence a
\(d_C\)-dimensional program register can carry at most \(d_C\) mutually
distinct exact deterministic unitary programs.

This does not produce a universal physical resource law. It supplies one
clean model-specific capacity bound and shows why the controller/resource
type cannot be discarded after the action family has been named.
