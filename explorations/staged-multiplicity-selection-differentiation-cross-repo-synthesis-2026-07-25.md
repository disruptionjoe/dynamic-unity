# Staged multiplicity, physical selection, and differentiation

## Decision

The ten-lens generation-mechanism pass reveals one articulation-invariant
concept that is useful to Dynamic Unity:

> Repeated observable physical types may require three separately earned
> explanations: why a mathematical carrier has a given multiplicity, why a
> physical process selects or makes accessible particular sectors of that
> carrier, and why the surviving sectors acquire different effective
> parameters.

This concept is admitted as `CONCEPT-DU-012`. The admission does not endorse
the GU-specific Triplet--Boundary--Flavor Conjecture, derive three matter
generations, or claim that records create matter.

The authoritative full ten-lens exploration and GU scientific status live in
the sibling `gu-formalization` repository:

## Why Dynamic Unity should hold the concept

Dynamic Unity already insists that record formation, observer access,
certification/finality, and capability are different objects. The
generation-mechanism pass exposes an analogous typing error:

```text
representation multiplicity
    != physical/chiral survival
    != low-energy accessibility
    != mass and mixing differentiation
```

That separation can matter well beyond family count. The same architecture
applies whenever a theory has many kinematic sectors but only some become
stable, public, controllable, light, or observer-accessible.

DU's proper role is to help define and test the **physical-selection** stage:
which independently formed records, boundary certificates, interventions, or
capability differences make two sectors physically distinct or make one
sector accessible? It cannot manufacture the source operator, index, anomaly
inflow, or flavor dynamics that the owner theory lacks.

## GU instance

The current GU instance has:

1. a canonical self-dual `SU(2)_+` / `Lambda^2_+` triplet carrier;
2. a vectorlike observer carrier schematically `3E_+ + 3E_- + X`;
3. net chiral count zero under the currently controlled structure;
4. an order-three torsion/spectral lead that still lacks the source-owned
   physical operator and lawful integer pairing; and
5. no derived flavor mechanism.

Therefore the triplet is **located**, while physical selection and
differentiation remain open. Dynamic Unity must not rename “accessible” or
“certified” in a way that hides those missing objects.

The common staged concept appears in several nonidentical forms:

| family | multiplicity stage | selection stage | differentiation stage |
|---|---|---|---|

| symmetry breaking | multiplet | vacuum/order-parameter stabilizer | hierarchical couplings |
| stochastic/metastable dynamics | candidate modes | target-free survival threshold | mode-dependent rates |

| low-energy EFT | full field content | decoupling or accessibility cutoff | running masses and mixing |

These are candidate articulations. Similar words do not establish a
cross-domain identity.

## Controls

Any DU use must retain:

1. a target-blind selection rule fixed before the desired count or sectors are
   inspected;
2. a physical intervention or capability consequence, not label
   multiplication;
3. benign-refinement, relabeling, and completion controls;
4. explicit inaccessible-environment, controller, decoder, resource, and
   heavy-sector absorbers;
5. separation of computational secrecy from physical inaccessibility;
6. separation of consensus/certification from truth or ontic creation; and
7. held-out predictions for the differentiation stage under matched budgets.

## Global kill

The DU-specific concept loses scientific content if every candidate
formalization reduces to:

- an arbitrary copy count followed by a target-selected filter;
- standard decoupling or symmetry breaking with no independently measurable
  record/access/capability structure;
- a representation change with no interventionally accessible consequence;
  or
- a downstream parameter fit with no held-out prediction.

One failed threshold, code, index, or regional model kills that
formalization, not the concept family. A direct no-go showing that physical
selection can never add an invariant beyond ordinary complete-process
dynamics would kill the specifically DU-shaped increment.

## Cheapest useful assay

For any proposed repeated-sector mechanism, require a typed return:

```text
MULTIPLICITY_ONLY
PHYSICAL_SELECTOR_UNDEFINED
SELECTOR_TARGET_IMPORTED
SELECTOR_ABSORBED_BY_COMPLETE_PROCESS
SECTORS_PHYSICALLY_DISTINCT
DIFFERENTIATION_FIT_ONLY
DIFFERENTIATION_HELD_OUT_PREDICTION
```

This assay does not decide the mechanism. It prevents success at one stage
from being reported as success at all three.
