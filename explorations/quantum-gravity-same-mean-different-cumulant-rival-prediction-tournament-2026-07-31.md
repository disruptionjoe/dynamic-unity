# Quantum-gravity same-mean/different-cumulant rival-prediction tournament

## Result in one paragraph

The `HC-DU-191` reopener succeeds only at its narrowest level. Two sources
with the same mean local mass/energy profile and different higher cumulants
can give different phase visibility under a profile-sensitive interaction,
so this is a real finite discriminator against expectation-value-only
semiclassical closure. It is not a unique quantum-gravity witness. A
classical lottery over the same profiles reproduces the entire diagonal
phase characteristic; a Gaussian stochastic model reproduces its mean and
variance terms; an adjustable non-Gaussian stochastic model can reproduce
more; and quantized-field mediation and quantized direct action can implement
the same endpoint matter channel. Complementary coherence and entanglement
exclude narrower rivals, but unique mediator attribution requires access to a
mediator-facing, noncommuting, multi-time response. The novel-mean-profile
branch is therefore closed; the useful survivor is an exact observable and
rival hierarchy.

## Frozen finite arena

Let the source have local occupation observable $K$. Compare:

1. a coherent $N$-boson Hartree preparation with
   $K\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(N,p)$; and
2. a fragmented local-number preparation with sharp $K=Np$, taking $Np$
   integral.

They have the same mean profile,

\[
\mathbb E[K]=Np,
\]

but different variance:

\[
\operatorname{Var}_{\rm H}(K)=Np(1-p),
\qquad
\operatorname{Var}_{\rm F}(K)=0.
\]

Freeze a probe path whose relative interaction phase is $\chi K$. The
off-diagonal probe response after the source is ignored is

\[
\Phi_K(\chi)=\mathbb E[e^{-i\chi K}].
\]

For the Hartree source,

\[
\Phi_{\rm H}(\chi)
=\big[(1-p)+p e^{-i\chi}\big]^N,
\]

whereas the fragmented source gives

\[
\Phi_{\rm F}(\chi)=e^{-i\chi Np}.
\]

The exact probe uses $N=20$, $p=0.35$, and $\chi=0.21$. Both means are
seven, but the Hartree and fragmented visibilities are approximately
$0.904419$ and $1$.

This finite arena is deliberately mechanism-neutral. It tests what a
candidate endpoint statistic can adjudicate before any gravitational label is
attached.

## Exact theorem: endpoint rival quotient

### Proposition 1 — cumulant separation

For the frozen phase channel, expectation-value-only closure sends both
sources to the same unit-modulus response $e^{-i\chi Np}$. The exact
profile-sensitive channel sends them to different visibilities whenever the
Hartree characteristic has modulus below one. Therefore a
same-mean/different-cumulant contrast can reject expectation-value-only
closure in this class.

### Proposition 2 — diagonal classical absorption

Every endpoint response of the form

\[
\Phi_K(\chi)=\sum_k q_k e^{-i\chi k}
\]

is reproduced exactly by a classical lottery that chooses profile $k$ with
probability $q_k$ and applies the corresponding phase. Consequently, no
collection of diagonal phase visibilities— even if it reconstructs all
cumulants—distinguishes a coherent source from a profile mixture without a
complementary coherence measurement.

### Proposition 3 — Gaussian and higher-noise hierarchy

Writing the logarithm of the response as

\[
\log\Phi_K(\chi)
=\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{\kappa_n(-i\chi)^n}{n!},
\]

a Gaussian stochastic rival matches $\kappa_1$ and $\kappa_2$ exactly but
misses the third and higher cumulants. For the chosen binomial source,

\[
\kappa_3=Np(1-p)(1-2p)=1.365.
\]

The probe confirms the leading Gaussian mismatch scales as $\chi^3$.
This makes a third-cumulant target stronger than variance alone, but not
uniquely quantum: a non-Gaussian classical lottery with the full $q_k$
still matches it exactly.

### Proposition 4 — endpoint mediation nonidentification

Let $S$ and $P$ be source and probe qubits. A direct controlled phase

\[
U_{SP}=(-1)^{SP}
\]

has the same endpoint action as a mediator circuit that copies $S$ to
$M$, applies the $M$--$P$ phase, and uncomputes $M$. The mediator
contains source information at the intermediate cut and returns to blank at
the endpoint. Therefore every endpoint matter statistic—including complete
tomography and entanglement—identifies only the effective matter channel, not
whether a field mediator or direct action implemented it.

### Corollary — entanglement is assumption-indexed

The controlled phase sends $|+\rangle|+\rangle$ to a maximally entangled
cluster state. It has

\[
\langle X\otimes Z\rangle+\langle Z\otimes X\rangle=2,
\]

while separable states are bounded by one. This excludes a declared separable
classical random-local-channel model. It does not, without additional
locality, matter-channel, and mediator assumptions, identify a quantized
gravitational field rather than a direct quantum interaction or quantized
matter cross-talk.

## Primary-source collision

The mean-versus-fluctuation split is old and real. Kuo and Ford explicitly
study when stress fluctuations undermine expectation-value semiclassical
gravity, and Phillips and Hu define the stochastic-gravity noise kernel as a
stress-tensor two-point function with a higher-correlation hierarchy
([Kuo--Ford](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9304008),
[Phillips--Hu](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0010019)). Fewster and Ford show
that meaningful stress distributions require finite spacetime smearing and
study their high moments
([primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.02359)). These absorb any claim
that “gravity should notice stress cumulants” is by itself new.

Consistent stochastic semiclassical models also exist. Tilloy and Diósi
construct a continuous-localization-sourced Newtonian model with additional
decoherence, while Kafri, Taylor, and Milburn construct a classical
measurement-channel model whose gravity cannot create entanglement
([Tilloy--Diósi](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.08705),
[Kafri--Taylor--Milburn](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.0946)). These absorb a
simple visibility-versus-mean dichotomy.

The gravitational-entanglement proposals show the next level, and the
literature also shows why its assumptions must remain explicit
([Bose et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06050),
[Marletto--Vedral](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06036)). Howl and collaborators
propose matter non-Gaussianity as a different quantum-gravity witness
([primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.01189)). Aziz and Howl later
argue that a classical gravitational field embedded in a fuller quantized
matter QFT can produce matter entanglement; Marletto and collaborators dispute
the mechanism and attribution
([Aziz--Howl](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.19714),
[Marletto et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07348)). Dynamic Unity need not
settle that dispute here. Its direct implication is that “entanglement” is
not a type-complete prediction until the matter, locality, mediation, and
access classes are frozen.

Finally, Chen and Giacomini's linearized-quantum-gravity paper supplies the
conditional high-ceiling profile-dependent response that motivated this
branch ([published paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/hl1c-t8z9)). `HC-DU-189--191`
show why the formula is not yet a physical finite experiment packet.

## Observable hierarchy

The tournament leaves four levels:

1. **Mean phase.** Too coarse. Mean-field, stochastic, direct, and mediated
   accounts can all match it.
2. **Profile cumulants / visibility.** Excludes expectation-value-only closure,
   but diagonal quantum and classical profile/noise accounts overlap.
3. **Complementary coherence, non-Gaussianity, or entanglement.** Excludes a
   frozen incoherent or separable-channel class. Attribution still depends on
   matter cross-talk, locality, and allowed direct interactions.
4. **Mediator-facing noncommuting multi-time response.** Required to separate
   an endpoint-equivalent quantized field from direct action. Even here the
   instrument and mediator algebra must be physically selected.

Each level is useful. None may inherit the claim of the level above it.

## What changed

- The Chen--Giacomini field-level branch can retain a high ceiling only if it
  supplies a field-specific multi-time response not reducible to an endpoint
  potential.
