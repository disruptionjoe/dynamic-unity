The important finding is not that Dynamic Unity chose the wrong quantum
formalism. It is that some of its strongest record statements are conditional
on a field-algebra presentation that the operational source data may not
select.

Haag's theorem blocks a naive identification of free and interacting
relativistic fields by one interaction-picture unitary under its assumptions.
It does not prove that fields are unreal, and it does not select algebraic QFT
or direct action. Those are two differently typed responses:

- **AQFT retains local observable algebras** and makes inequivalent
  representations part of the mathematical architecture.
- **Direct action removes an independently quantized mediator from the
  interaction description** and connects matter currents through a Green
  kernel.

In the exact quadratic class, integrating out an explicit mediator preserves
the complete on-shell source action and every query that factors through it.
The resulting source kernel does **not** determine the mediator dimension,
factorization, local mediator algebra, or absorber-channel decomposition.
Those become distinguishable only after the action/target class is enlarged
or a reconstruction map is supplied.

This yields a new Dynamic Unity guard:

> A record or reconstruction theorem stated in a local field algebra is
> universal across the Haag fork only if its formed records, interventions,
> targets, and capabilities factor through mediator elimination—or an
> independently selected physical interface distinguishes the branches.

RTI is therefore interesting but not yet the missing selector. It proposes a
more ambitious object than ETH: absorber response as a microscopic,
nonunitary event trigger, with a radiative Born-rule argument. But the trigger
is conditional on a supplied matter/current sector, coupling, direct-action
kernel, absorber decomposition, and actualization interpretation. Its generic
probability reading has a published quantitative objection, and no matched
empirical excess over standard QED was isolated here.

The branch remains open. The portfolio remains quiescent.

## 1. What Haag's theorem does and does not say

Haag's theorem concerns the interaction picture in relativistic QFT. In its
standard form, sufficiently well-behaved free and interacting fields cannot
be related at a fixed time by the kind of unitary transformation assumed by
the naive interaction picture unless the theory is trivial in the relevant
sense.

The theorem's exact assumptions matter. It is not:

- a proof that interacting QFT has no empirical content;
- a proof that renormalized perturbation theory is invalid;
- a proof that no field ontology is possible;
- a proof that direct action is true; or
- a choice among all mathematically viable QFT architectures.

[Halvorson and Mueger's AQFT survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0602036)
describes the algebraic framework, including local observable algebras,
inequivalent representations, sectors, and reconstruction of field/gauge
structure from observable data. This route takes inequivalent
representations seriously rather than requiring the forbidden global
free/interacting identification.

[Kastner's 2015 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.03814) argues for a
different response: in direct-action QED the interacting electromagnetic
mediator need not be represented as an independently quantized Fock-space
field, so the premise generating the Haag conflict is absent. This is a
published proposal about the right physical architecture, not a theorem that
Haag uniquely entails that architecture.

The correction to the mailbox framing is therefore:

> Haag exposes a fork; it does not adjudicate the fork.

Nor are AQFT and direct action the only responses to Haag. Renormalized
perturbative, cutoff, effective, constructive, and other formulations handle
the interaction-picture issue in differently scoped ways. The present result
compares these two because they interact directly with Dynamic Unity's record
typing, not because they exhaust QFT.

## 2. What the direct-action literature establishes

[Wheeler and Feynman's absorber theory](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.17.157)
and their later
[direct-interparticle formulation](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.21.425)
replace an independently acting electromagnetic medium with time-symmetric
interactions among charges plus an absorber response.

[Narlikar's general correspondence](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004100043838)
shows, under stated conditions, that fields of arbitrary spin can have
direct-particle counterparts in curved spacetime. This is a scoped
field/direct-action correspondence, not a theorem that every interacting
quantum field theory, local algebra, regulator, state, or gravitational
coupling is fully equivalent.

[Davies's quantum theory](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004100076751) uses an
S-matrix treatment and reports equivalence with conventional QED to all
orders in its perturbation expansion. His relativistic extension reports
recovery of conventional QED results under stated cosmological/absorber
assumptions
([Part I](https://doi.org/10.1088/0305-4470/4/6/009)).

The earned content is substantial:

- direct current-to-current interaction is a serious physical architecture;
- ordinary radiative and scattering expressions can be recovered in its
  declared domain;
- an explicit mediator can be calculational rather than primitive; and
- Haag's forbidden free/interacting field identification need not be made for
  that mediator.

The limits are equally important:

- S-matrix agreement is query-relative;
- the cited equivalence is not a complete nonperturbative Standard Model or
  quantum-gravity equivalence;
- local off-shell field targets, local stress-energy, gravitational coupling,
  boundary conditions, and instruments require separate maps;
- matter currents and their sectors remain part of the physical antecedent;
  and
- direct interaction does not by itself select an outcome, archive, observer
  boundary, access map, or public-finality rule.

## 3. Exact mediator-elimination theorem

### Frozen object

Let \(j\in\mathbb R^n\) be matter/source coordinates and
\(\phi\in\mathbb R^m\) an explicit mediator. Let \(A=A^\mathsf T\),
\(K=K^\mathsf T\), with \(K\) invertible, and let \(B\) be the coupling
matrix. Define

\[
S(j,\phi)
=
\frac12j^\mathsf TAj
+j^\mathsf TB\phi
+\frac12\phi^\mathsf TK\phi.
\tag{1}
\]

### Theorem 1 — exact source-facing equivalence

The stationary mediator is

\[
\phi_*(j)
=
-K^{-1}B^\mathsf Tj.
\tag{2}
\]

Substituting it into (1) gives

\[
S_{\mathrm{eff}}(j)
=
\frac12j^\mathsf T
\left(A-BK^{-1}B^\mathsf T\right)j.
\tag{3}
\]

The source response also agrees:

\[
\left.
\frac{\partial S}{\partial j}
\right|_{\phi=\phi_*(j)}
=
\frac{\partial S_{\mathrm{eff}}}{\partial j}.
\tag{4}
\]

Therefore every on-shell value, derivative, and finite source query that
factors through \(S_{\mathrm{eff}}\) is unchanged by eliminating the
mediator.

### Proof

Stationarity gives

\[
\frac{\partial S}{\partial\phi}
=
B^\mathsf Tj+K\phi=0,
\]

which yields (2). Substitution completes the square and yields (3).
Differentiating (1) with respect to \(j\) and using (2) gives

\[
Aj+BK^{-1}(-B^\mathsf Tj)
=
\left(A-BK^{-1}B^\mathsf T\right)j,
\]

which is (4). \(\square\)

This is standard Schur-complement mathematics. In a Gaussian path integral,
the same operation produces the source effective action plus a
source-independent determinant under the frozen assumptions. It is the
finite control for the direct-action move, not a new QFT theorem.

## 4. The source kernel does not select the mediator or event partition

The effective source interaction depends on

\[
C=BK^{-1}B^\mathsf T,
\tag{5}
\]

not on \(B\), \(K\), or mediator dimension separately.

### Theorem 2 — factorization non-identifiability

The source kernel \(C\) does not, in general, determine:

- mediator dimension;
- mediator coordinates;
- channel or absorber decomposition;
- a local mediator observable algebra;
- mediator provenance; or
- a candidate event partition indexed by mediator channels.

### Smallest exact witness

Take one scalar source and \(A=0\).

The one-channel completion uses

\[
B_1=(1),
\qquad
K_1=(1).
\]

The two-channel completion uses

\[
B_2=
\begin{pmatrix}
3/5&4/5
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
K_2=I_2.
\]

Both give

\[
B_1K_1^{-1}B_1^\mathsf T
=
B_2K_2^{-1}B_2^\mathsf T
=1.
\tag{6}
\]

They therefore have the same complete source-only quadratic action.
Nevertheless, if the squared coupling components are interpreted as
normalized candidate channel weights, they give

\[
(1)
\quad\text{versus}\quad
\left(\frac9{25},\frac{16}{25}\right).
\tag{7}
\]

The effective source kernel does not select which event partition exists.

The two-channel completion also contains the mediator direction

\[
v=
\left(-\frac45,\frac35\right),
\qquad
B_2v=0.
\tag{8}
\]

It is invisible to every original source coupling but visible to a
mediator-facing probe such as the second-coordinate readout. Adding that
probe enlarges the action/target class and distinguishes the completion.

This establishes the exact type boundary:

```text
same source-only process
    !=
same mediator completion
    !=
same mediator-local action space
    !=
same event or record interface.
```

It does **not** establish that nature contains the extra channel. It shows
that source equivalence cannot decide the question.

## 5. Consequence for Dynamic Unity's AQFT record results

`HC-DU-040B` proves:

> A nontrivial finite sharp record internal to a von Neumann action algebra
> and nondisturbing for every action must lie in the algebra's center.

For a factor, that exact internal center is trivial. This remains correct.
What changes is its scope.

The theorem speaks about a declared field/algebra/action object. Under a
direct-action description:

- the independently quantized mediator algebra may not be primitive;
- matter-current interactions can remain;
- all physical recorders may themselves be matter systems connected by the
  direct interaction; and
- field observables may be derived source functionals rather than primitive
  local degrees of freedom.

Therefore the factor-center no-go is not automatically a no-go for
direct-action records. Conversely, removing the mediator does not create a
record: it relocates the interface question into the current network and its
matter outputs.

The corrected branch contract is:

| Question | Required result |
|---|---|
| source-only scattering or response | may factor through the effective current kernel |
| local mediator field value | requires a reconstruction map and boundary prescription |
| field-local intervention | must be translated into an added physical current/probe or marks a branch difference |
| local stress-energy/gravity target | requires a branch-specific energy and gravitational-coupling map |
| formed record | requires an actual physical matter/output process in either branch |
| universal DU theorem | must be invariant under the declared mediator-elimination equivalence or explicitly branch-relative |

This is not a reason to abandon AQFT. AQFT remains the stronger host for
locality, sectors, and relativistic observable structure. It is a reason to
stop calling an AQFT-internal record boundary universal until it survives the
direct-action translation.

## 6. RTI passport

### What RTI proposes

[Kastner and Cramer](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.04501) take Davies-style
direct action as the physical substrate. Their stated objects are:

- a time-symmetric direct interaction among charged currents;
- an emitter's offer;
- potential absorbers' confirmations;
- a set of incipient transactions with Born weights; and
- one actualized emitter--absorber transaction, interpreted as objective
  nonunitary event formation.

They identify ordinary bound systems capable of excitation—atoms or
molecules—as absorbers. They argue that paired emission and absorption
amplitudes naturally produce the Born-rule square for radiative processes.
Their later
[QDAT overview](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.00712) argues that a quantum
absorber condition need not be a special cosmological “light-tight box.”

This is genuinely more ambitious than a supplied output channel. It proposes
a microscopic occurrence criterion and treats the quantum state as
possibility structure rather than the spacetime event itself.

### What remains supplied or contested

| Object | Status in this audit |
|---|---|
| matter/current sector with bound transitions | supplied physical antecedent |
| coupling amplitudes and interaction Hamiltonian | supplied by the admitted theory |
| direct-action Green kernel and boundary prescription | supplied theory structure |
| emitter/absorber channel factorization | fixed only after the matter completion is specified; not selected by the source kernel alone |
| confirmation generation as physical nonunitarity | RTI posit/interpretation, contested |
| radiative Born weights | derived within the stated paired-amplitude construction, but mathematically the same weights as standard QED |
| one realized transaction | added stochastic actualization claim |
| retained archive and provenance | conditional on the matter transition; not a general archive theorem |
| observer access, action class, certification, and regional finality | not selected |
| empirical excess | none isolated against matched QED |

### Live objection

[Marchildon](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.03060) accepts that RTI aims to define
microscopic absorber response but challenges the generic quantitative step.
If a coupling is itself interpreted as a process amplitude, sufficiently
large charges or strong couplings can make the naive squared value exceed
one. Standard QFT does not assign a standalone physical probability to one
vertex; only the complete gauge-consistent amplitude has that status.

That objection does not prove every transactional model impossible. It
blocks crediting “coupling constant equals event probability” as a general
normalized selection law without a complete process construction.

Kastner and Cramer's later radiative calculation uses the complete paired
emission/absorption amplitude and avoids reducing the Born square to a lone
word. But the remaining actualization step—why one incipient transaction
becomes the event—is described as stochastic actualization or an analogue of
spontaneous symmetry breaking. The audited sources do not derive a unique
random law from a more primitive direct-action source, nor do they isolate a
different empirical distribution from standard QED.

### Disposition

`CONTESTED_CONDITIONAL_EVENT_TRIGGER`.

This is a **conditional Grade-2 theory characterization**, not a Grade-4
physical selector:

```text
supplied matter/current sector
  + supplied direct-action kernel and coupling
  + proposed confirmation/nonunitarity rule
    -> candidate absorber-channel event partition
    -> proposed stochastic actualization
    -> ordinary matter transition that may support a record
    -/-> independently selected complete certified record interface.
```

RTI belongs in any future reopening of the `HC-DU-090` tournament, but its
ambition should not be mistaken for earned selection.

## 7. North-Star implication

The new program-level test is **mediator-elimination invariance**.

For a candidate record map \(r_{\rm field}\), action class
\(\mathcal A_{\rm field}\), target \(T_{\rm field}\), and capability relation
\(\mathsf{Cap}_{\rm field}\), a universal result across this fork needs a
translation

\[
F:
\text{field branch}
\longrightarrow
\text{direct-action branch}
\]

such that, on the admitted physical class:

\[
r_{\rm DA}\circ F=r_{\rm field},
\qquad
T_{\rm DA}\circ F=T_{\rm field},
\]

and the admitted interventions and capability judgments are preserved up to
the declared equivalence.

If only source scattering factors, the result is source-query equivalence.
If formed records and held-out targets also factor, the result can support
operational duality. If a physically realizable finite intervention
distinguishes the branches after every legitimate map and completion is
admitted, it is a candidate physical remainder.

Nothing in this swing supplies that Grade-5 discriminator. It supplies the
test that future field-based work must pass.

1. Haag's theorem is an architectural pressure, not an ontology selector.
2. Direct action is not merely a verbal interpretation; it is a serious

- that fields are unreal;
- that direct action is fundamental;
- that AQFT and direct action are fully equivalent;
- that RTI solves the measurement problem;
- that absorber response selects records, access, or finality;
- that transactions generate spacetime;
- that a new physical law or prediction exists; or
- that Dynamic Unity has a ready successor.
