# Constructor task repertoire and finite observer action envelope

## Executive result

`HC-DU-138` proved that a complete physical controller packet compiles an
observer-relative action envelope, while one source law, processor, or actual
trajectory does not select the counterfactual preparation/resource boundary.
Constructor theory is the strongest direct collision because it makes
possible and impossible transformations primitive physical facts rather than
analyst-supplied modal language.

The collision returns:

```text
COUNTERFACTUAL_CAPABILITY_IS_A_LEGITIMATE_PHYSICAL_OBJECT
+ GLOBAL_ASYMPTOTIC_TASK_REPERTOIRE_IS_NOT_A_FINITE_OBSERVER_ACTION_ENVELOPE
+ SUBSIDIARY_THEORY_AND_ACCESS_RESOURCE_ADAPTER_REMAIN_REQUIRED
+ CURRENT_CONSTRUCTOR_PRINCIPLES_CONSTRAIN_BUT_DO_NOT_COMPLETE_THE_REPERTOIRE
+ GENERAL_WITNESS_THEOREM_IMPORTS_A_SCOPED_MEDIATOR_REMAINDER
+ NO_FROZEN_CONSTRUCTOR_SPECIFIC_DU_EMPIRICAL_EXCESS
+ NO_READY_SUCCESSOR
```

Constructor theory therefore changes the interpretation of the open premise
without closing it:

> The missing action envelope is not necessarily a semantic fiction. It may
> be part of fundamental physics. But a universe-wide partition of tasks into
> possible and impossible still does not say which of those transformations
> one finite observer can prepare, afford, complete, repeat, read, or certify.

That distinction is exact. Constructor theory asks whether a transformation
can be performed by constructors to arbitrarily high accuracy while retaining
the ability to perform it again. Dynamic Unity asks what channels or
interventions a specified observer/controller packet can actually access
under a finite resource, horizon, reliability, and readout contract. Neither
object determines the other without additional physical data.

One theorem family is worth importing. Under its declared locality,
interoperability, determinism, mediation, and no-direct-interaction
assumptions, the general witness theorem says that a mediator capable of
entangling two quantum probes cannot possess only one jointly sharp classical
information variable. This is a real physical-class remainder theorem. It is
not a record-interface selector, an observed anomaly, or evidence that records
constitute reality.

This is a scoped Grade-4 type/selection/remainder-admission result. The
constructor-theory mathematics and witness are prior literature. Dynamic
Unity earns the adapter boundary and portfolio consequence, not a new
constructor-theory theorem, empirical result, law, or new physics.

## 1. Frozen primary-source audit

The collision used the following primary sources:

- Deutsch, [*Constructor Theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.7439), which
  proposes laws stated in terms of possible and impossible tasks rather than
  only trajectories from initial conditions.
- Deutsch and Marletto,
  [*Constructor Theory of Information*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.5563),
  which defines information media through possible copying and permutation
  tasks.
- Deutsch and Marletto,
  [*Constructor theory of time*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08692), which
  deliberately removes time from fundamental constructor-theoretic law and
  seeks to recover duration and dynamics.
- Marletto, Deutsch, and Vedral,
  [*Tests of constructor theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07352), which
  gives the current definitions, reviews proposed and performed tests, and
  explicitly labels the ultimate repertoire-selection principle as work in
  progress.
- Marletto and Vedral,
  [*Witnessing non-classicality beyond quantum
  theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.07974), which supplies the
  mediator-witness theorem used below.

Four source facts decide this swing.

First, a task is a finite set of input/output attribute pairs. A constructor
must perform it while retaining the ability to perform it again, and the task
is possible when physical law imposes no limiting accuracy. Possibility is
therefore asymptotic and repeatability-sensitive.

Second, constructor-theoretic principles are meta-laws. A complete
explanation also requires a compatible subsidiary theory. The subsidiary
theory supplies states, topology, dynamics, and concrete realization data.

Third, the current proposed ultimate selector is not complete. The 2026 review
explicitly leaves open the distance between tasks, the distinction between a
possible task and an allowed process, and the general criterion that makes a
task impossible.

Fourth, the reviewed experimental landscape is mixed:

- mediator witnesses give scoped necessity results under explicit premises;
- the homogenizer experiment demonstrates compatibility of
  constructor-based irreversibility with ordinary unitary quantum theory;
- a constructor-theoretic Born-rule deformation is an outlined research
  route, not a frozen alternative prediction; and
- universal-constructor thresholds and an ultimate task law remain proposed
  future work.

## 2. The two objects have different types

Let a constructor-theoretic task on substrate \(S\) be

\[
\mathcal T
=
\bigcup_i\{\mathbf x_i\longrightarrow\mathbf y_i\}.
\]

Let the global constructor-theoretic repertoire be

\[
\mathfrak R_{\mathrm{CT}}
=
\{\mathcal T:\mathcal T^\checkmark\},
\]

where \(\mathcal T^\checkmark\) means that no law of physics imposes a
positive lower bound on construction error and a constructor can retain the
ability to repeat the task.

By contrast, the `HC-DU-138` packet is

\[
\Pi_O
=
(S,C,G,\mathsf P_O,\mathsf R_O,B_O,\tau_O,\epsilon_O,\mathsf{Read}_O),
\]

where the subscript \(O\) makes the physical observer/controller boundary
explicit. Its finite action envelope is

\[
\mathcal A_{\Pi_O}^{B,\tau,\epsilon}
=
\left\{
\Phi_p\ \middle|\
p\in\mathsf P_O,\quad
\operatorname{cost}(p)\le B_O,\quad
\operatorname{time}(p)\le\tau_O,\quad
\operatorname{err}(p)\le\epsilon_O
\right\}.
\]

The exact mismatch is:

| Coordinate | Constructor repertoire | DU finite action envelope |
|---|---|---|
| Basic item | attribute transformation task | physically induced channel/intervention |
| Quantifier | possible somewhere in the admitted world/laws | accessible to this packet |
| Accuracy | arbitrarily high in principle | declared finite error |
| Repetition | constructor retains ability to repeat | may be repeatable, consumptive, destructive, or one-shot |
| Resources | law-level possibility | explicit finite budget and preparation |
| Time | absent from fundamental task law | explicit horizon and latency |
| Access | not fixed by global task possibility | observer/controller/readout indexed |
| Output use | task completion | response, record write, archive, certification, or action |

The objects may be connected. They are not identical.

## 3. Proposition A — the global repertoire does not select the observer envelope

### Statement

There is no map

\[
F:\mathfrak R_{\mathrm{CT}}\longrightarrow\mathcal A_{\Pi_O}^{B,\tau,\epsilon}
\]

that determines every observer-relative finite action envelope from the global
task repertoire alone.

### Smallest counterexample

Hold one physical world and its global possible/impossible-task partition
fixed. Put a working constructor, its fuel, and its readout behind an
observer-excluding physical boundary. Observer \(O_1\) controls and reads it;
observer \(O_2\) does not.

Then

\[
\mathfrak R_{\mathrm{CT}}(O_1)
=
\mathfrak R_{\mathrm{CT}}(O_2)
\]

because the world and its law-level task repertoire are unchanged, while

\[
\mathcal A_{\Pi_{O_1}}^{B,\tau,\epsilon}
\neq
\mathcal A_{\Pi_{O_2}}^{B,\tau,\epsilon}.
\]

The missing fields are not epistemic labels. They are the physical access
path, controller possession, preparation, resource, horizon, and readout.

### Why task localization does not remove the witness

One may refine the constructor-theoretic task to include where and under what
attributes it is performed. That creates a richer task description. It still
does not infer which physical observer owns the needed constructor or can
cross the access boundary. Encoding those facts in the task is a valid richer
antecedent, not a derivation from the old repertoire.

## 4. Proposition B — the observer envelope does not select the constructor repertoire

### Statement

There is no map

\[
G:\mathcal A_{\Pi_O}^{B,\tau,\epsilon}\longrightarrow\mathfrak R_{\mathrm{CT}}
\]

that recovers the global possible/impossible partition from one finite
observer action envelope.

### Two exact reasons

1. A physically available one-shot device may induce a channel in
   \(\mathcal A_{\Pi_O}^{B,\tau,\epsilon}\) while failing to be a constructor:
   it is consumed and does not retain the ability to perform the
   transformation again. The abstract task might be globally possible through
   some other repeatable constructor, or it might not. The one-shot action
   does not decide which.
2. A task may be possible in the constructor-theoretic asymptotic sense while
   being absent from every finite-\(B,\tau,\epsilon\) envelope currently
   available to \(O\).

The mismatch is therefore not merely global versus local. It is also
asymptotic-repeatable versus finite-operational.

## 5. Proposition C — the honest adapter

The two formalisms compose only after a typed physical bridge is supplied.
Let

\[
\Theta
=
(\mathsf{SubsidiaryTheory},
\mathsf{SubstrateAttributes},
\mathsf{ObserverBoundary},
\mathsf{Controller},
\mathsf{Preparation},
\mathsf{Resources},
\mathsf{Horizon},
\mathsf{Error},
\mathsf{Reset},
\mathsf{Readout}).
\]

Then a conditional compiler can:

1. interpret task attributes in the subsidiary theory;
2. identify concrete controllers and preparation paths;
3. restrict to actions accessible within \(B,\tau,\epsilon\);
4. retain one-shot operations as such rather than misclassifying them as
   constructors;
5. compile induced channels and outcome instruments; and
6. derive the response quotient and any formed record only after the write,
   archive, and access maps are also present.

Schematically:

```text
constructor principles + subsidiary theory
                    |
                    v
       global possible/impossible tasks
                    |
       + observer/controller/access packet
                    v
         finite executable action envelope
                    |
        + write/archive/certification process
                    v
         observer-relative response quotient
```

The adapter is representation-natural relative to the complete packet in the
same sense as `HC-DU-138`. It does not select \(\Theta\). In particular,
constructor theory's removal of time from fundamental law does not remove the
finite horizon from an observer's action envelope; duration must re-enter
through the subsidiary-theory realization.

This is the exact correction to both overstatements:

- “actions are merely analyst supplied” is too weak; and
- “constructor theory selects the actions an observer can perform” is too
  strong.

## 6. The general witness theorem is a useful scoped import

### Frozen physical class

Let quantum probes \(Q_A,Q_B\) have no direct interaction and interact only
locally through mediator \(M\). Under the constructor-information assumptions
used by Marletto and Vedral—locality, interoperability of information,
determinism, and the declared mediation structure—suppose the interaction
produces entanglement between \(Q_A\) and \(Q_B\).

### Necessity result

Then \(M\) cannot be represented as possessing only one classical information
variable whose sharp states are all mutually distinguishable. It must support
at least two observables that are not jointly sharp in the relevant
constructor-information sense.

In Dynamic Unity's language, the admitted single-variable mediator class
\(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cl},1}\) has been excluded:

\[
\operatorname{Entangle}(Q_A,Q_B)
\land
\operatorname{LocalMediation}(M)
\Longrightarrow
M\notin\mathcal M_{\mathrm{cl},1}.
\]

That is a genuine physical-class remainder: the probe output forces more
mediator structure than the one-variable completion class contains.

### What it does not establish

It does not:

- select one microscopic mediator state or ontology;
- select a record-value space, formation map, archive, or observer access;
- show that the additional variables are durable records;
- distinguish all quantum, post-quantum, or field/direct-action
  realizations;
- establish that an experiment has observed the antecedent; or
- supply constructor-theory-specific excess over every standard quantum
  account.

The witness should therefore be kept as a **scoped physical-class necessity
import**, not promoted into a universal record theorem.

The temporal variant is weaker for this purpose. It replaces the
two-probe/no-direct-interaction structure with a single probe plus an assumed
additive conservation law and, in the current presentation, additional
quantum formalism. That is a useful comparator, not a better DU reopener.

No row supplies the preregistered empirical return:

The correct result is not that constructor theory has no empirical program.
It is that its current published program does not yet close this specific DU
selection gap or supply a ready successor at the required grade.

## 8. Inline council adjudication

- **Orthodox professor:** the type mismatch is decisive. A global,
  asymptotic, repeatable task predicate cannot silently replace a finite
  observer/controller process.
- **Heterodox professor:** constructor theory improves DU's ontology of
  capability. The modal object may be fundamental even though its concrete
  repertoire and access realization remain incomplete.
- **Commercial scientist:** require every future “possible task” import to
  include the adapter packet \(\Theta\). This prevents expensive builds around
  a missing observer, constructor, or resource boundary.
- **Wild-frontier scientist:** the general witness theorem is the high-ceiling
  import because it can force hidden mediator structure without assuming the
  mediator's full dynamics. Its assumptions, not its rhetoric, must define the
  completion class.
- **Philosopher of science:** moving counterfactuals from derived language to
  fundamental law is a substantive theoretical proposal, but it is a
  primitive relocation until a selector or novel discriminator earns more.
- **Counterfactual lens:** the two same-input/different-output witnesses above
  prove that neither repertoire nor finite envelope is a sufficient statistic
  for the other.
- **Category/type lens:** the proper relation is a typed, packet-relative
  adapter. Treating it as an identity confuses existential law-level
  possibility with indexed operational accessibility.

## 9. Portfolio disposition

The collision improves `CONCEPT-DU-017` and leaves the program quiescent.

### Bank

- counterfactual capability can be a fundamental physical object;
- the constructor repertoire and observer action envelope are nonidentical;
- a subsidiary theory plus an observer/controller/access/resource adapter is
  required;
- the current ultimate-repertoire selector remains open; and
- the general witness theorem is an admissible scoped mediator-remainder
  import.

### Stop

- no constructor-theory toy model;
- no renaming the `HC-DU-138` controller envelope a constructor repertoire;
- no hardware path from the homogenizer compatibility result;
- no BMV promotion without a frozen observed acquisition packet and its
  locality/mediation assumptions; and
- no claim that a law-level possible task forms, stores, or certifies a
  record.

### Exact reopeners

Reopen only if at least one of the following arrives:

1. a completed constructor principle or subsidiary physical theory that
   uniquely determines the task repertoire **and** the observer adapter packet
   up to declared physical equivalence;
2. a theorem showing that a physically selected global task structure
   determines finite observer envelopes under explicit resource and access
   axioms;
3. a stronger mediator witness whose admitted class and observed acquisition
   packet yield a new DU held-out target or physical remainder; or
4. a frozen constructor-theory-specific quantitative prediction that survives
   ordinary quantum, thermodynamic, and interface-refit absorbers.

Until then, `NO_READY_SUCCESSOR` is the high-information result.
