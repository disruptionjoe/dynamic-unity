## 1. Minimum-premise theorem reused by the compiler

Let:

- \(M\) be a nonempty class of complete physical realizations modulo the
  declared representation/gauge relation;
- \(a:M\to A\) be a claimed physical antecedent;
- \(\xi:M\to X\) be the complete candidate interface architecture, including
  source binding, blank carrier, write, provenance, retention/reset, archive
  support/routing, access, action envelope, and resource horizon.

An \(a\)-only deterministic selector for \(\xi\) exists exactly when

\[
\ker a\subseteq\ker\xi.
\]

Equivalently, \(\xi\) must factor through \(a\). If a transformation or
paired completion preserves \(a\) while moving \(\xi\), no \(a\)-only
selector exists.

Archive relocation is one such hostile transformation. It can leave the
source law and reduced matter behavior fixed while moving the historical
support between an observer-accessible archive and an inaccessible
reservoir. Signatures, hashes, quorums, proofs, replication, and handoff all
occur downstream and therefore cannot break this antecedent fibre.

Adding a premise \(b\) helps exactly when

\[
\ker(a,b)\subseteq\ker\xi.
\]

But \(b\) earns physical-selection credit only if it is target-independent,
physically formed or law-selected, and not simply the desired detector,
archive, observer, access route, or target written into the contract.

## 5. Why conservative certification is the reserve

The reserve theorem asks whether a certificate can change physics without a
physical carrier or control.

Let:

- \(X\) be the complete admitted physical state/history;
- \(R=r(X)\) be the formed record;
- \(C\sim K_C(\cdot\mid R)\) be a downstream certificate;
- \(U\sim K_U(\cdot\mid C,X)\) be the admitted physical control; and
- \(Y\sim K_Y(\cdot\mid X,U)\) be the standard physical response.

Then every certificate-dependent response factors through \(X\) and \(U\).
If two cases match on the complete \(X,U\) law and still differ by
certificate status, at least one of the following is true:

1. the physical state or controller boundary is incomplete;
2. certificate status is itself an additional physical state;
3. the response dynamics is modified; or
4. the claimed residual is not reproducible.

This is highly reusable and locally provable, but its core is close to
standard data-processing and causal-control factorization. It therefore
ranks behind the \(3+1\) seam, which can expose an actual physical target
remainder rather than only police its interpretation.
