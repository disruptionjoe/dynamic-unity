# Pre-saddle quantum-detector channel boundary

## Result

The exact quantum-detector reopener from `HC-DU-194` is real, but its strongest
honest conclusion is narrower than graviton attribution.

Sen, Gangopadhyay, and Bhattacharyya already implement the missing first step:
they replace the free detector arm with a harmonic trap, quantize both the trap
and one linearized gravitational mode, and derive the selection rule
$\Delta n_{\rm detector}=\pm2$ at resonance $\omega=2\omega_0$. Their number-state
weights are proportional to $\eta_G$ for absorption and $\eta_G+1$ for emission.
The vacuum therefore gives zero upward transition and a nonzero downward
transition. This is ordered, energy-selective information that the commuting
Gaussian arm record of `HC-DU-194` discarded.

That asymmetry has exact excess content over one important rival class:

> A detector channel produced only by an exogenous classical random
> Hamiltonian is a convex mixture of unitary channels and is therefore unital.
> Vacuum relaxation is nonunital. Consequently a verified complete nonunital
> detector channel excludes the classical-random-force-only model.

The converse is false. Energy-population records are reproduced by an ordinary
classical decay process, and the complete amplitude-damping channel is
reproduced by a direct quantum ancilla. Thus downward-without-upward transition
does not identify a gravitational field, a graviton, or field mediation. It
locates the first live channel-structure discriminator and the next attribution
boundary.

The 2024 source also does not yet provide a finite acquired experiment packet.
Its displayed probabilities take the infinite-time limit and contain
$\delta^2(\omega-2\omega_0)$. The paper itself notes that a realistic calculation
needs finite time. A finite switching interval replaces this distributional
object with a bounded sinc-squared response window; apparatus preparation,
readout, competing damping, and finite error remain to be fixed.

This swing earns a scoped Grade-4 random-unitary obstruction, a primary-source
correction to the reopener, and a finite-time contract boundary. It earns no
field-attribution theorem, observed prediction, or active successor.

## Primary-source correction

`HC-DU-194` accurately found that the 2021 Parikh--Wilczek--Zahariade detector
is a pair of free-falling masses and that its proposed output is classicalized.
It was too pessimistic if read as claiming that no follow-on source had supplied
an energy-selective gravitational detector.

The relevant follow-on is:

- Soham Sen, Sunandan Gangopadhyay, and Sukanta Bhattacharyya, *Quantum gravity
  signatures in gravitational wave detectors placed inside a harmonic trap
  potential*, [arXiv:2405.18868](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.18868),
  *Physical Review D* **110**, 026008 (2024).

Starting from the same geodesic-deviation interaction, it adds

\[
L=\frac m2(\dot q^2-\omega^2q^2)
 +\frac{m_0}{2}(\dot\xi^2-\omega_0^2\xi^2)
 -\mathcal G\dot q\dot\xi\xi.
\]

To first order in $\mathcal G$, quantization gives an interaction containing

\[
(a^\dagger-a)\otimes(\chi^{\dagger2}-\chi^2).
\]

The source therefore supplies, rather than fits after the fact:

- a harmonic detector gap $\hbar\omega_0$;
- a two-level transition surface inside the $|0\rangle,|2\rangle$ subspace;
- the selection rule $\Delta n_{\rm detector}=\pm2$;
- the resonance $\omega=2\omega_0$; and
- field-number factors $\eta_G$ and $\eta_G+1$ for absorption and emission.

At $\eta_G=0$, absorption vanishes while emission retains the $+1$ vacuum
term. The paper calls this spontaneous graviton emission and explicitly notes
that local perturbations could imitate it. Its claimed distinctness is only
against the particular semiclassical incoming-wave calculation it compares,
not against every classical dissipative or direct quantum mechanism.

This detector idea is not historically unique. Boughn and Rothman calculated
graviton emission and absorption by atomic hydrogen using semiclassical and
field-theoretic methods and emphasized gauge-invariance subtleties:
[arXiv:gr-qc/0605052](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0605052). The proposed
transition is therefore a serious physical arena, not a new DU mechanism.

## The random-unitary obstruction

### Statement

Let a classical exogenous stochastic force act on a closed quantum detector
only through a realization-dependent Hamiltonian. After averaging over the
classical process, its channel is

\[
\Phi(\rho)=\int d\mu(\lambda)\,
U_\lambda\rho U_\lambda^\dagger.
\]

Then

\[
\Phi(I)=\int d\mu(\lambda)I=I.
\]

Every such random-unitary channel is unital. By contrast, vacuum relaxation on
the effective $|0\rangle,|2\rangle$ detector subspace has amplitude-damping
Kraus operators

\[
K_0=|0\rangle\!\langle0|
 +\sqrt{1-\gamma}|2\rangle\!\langle2|,
\qquad
K_1=\sqrt\gamma|0\rangle\!\langle2|,
\]

so

\[
\mathcal A_\gamma(I)
=(1+\gamma)|0\rangle\!\langle0|
 +(1-\gamma)|2\rangle\!\langle2|\ne I
\]

for $0<\gamma\le1$. Therefore no classical-random-Hamiltonian force by itself
reproduces that complete detector channel.

This amplitude-damping map is the minimal CPTP completion of the source's
one-way $|2\rangle\to|0\rangle$ transition, not a claim that the paper itself
derived a finite complete channel. That missing derivation is part of the
finite-time boundary below.

The same conclusion appears in frequency language. A real stationary
classical scalar process has an even power spectrum,
$S_{\rm cl}(+\Omega)=S_{\rm cl}(-\Omega)$. A ground-state quantum environment
can have unequal ordered weights and hence downward relaxation without upward
excitation. This is the standard quantum-noise-spectroscopy distinction
reviewed by Clerk et al.,
[arXiv:0810.4729](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.4729).

### Scope

The theorem excludes only the explicitly frozen rival:

```text
classical exogenous process -> detector Hamiltonian -> average over unitaries
```

It does not exclude classical damping, feedback, reset, postselection,
unobserved apparatus state, or a non-gravitational quantum environment. Calling
all of those “classical noise” would erase the theorem's type boundary.

## Why the transition still does not identify gravitons

### Population-level classical absorber

If the acquired record is only whether the detector ends in $|0\rangle$ or
$|2\rangle$, the amplitude-damping population law is the column-stochastic
matrix

\[
P=\begin{pmatrix}1&\gamma\\0&1-\gamma\end{pmatrix}.
\]

An ordinary two-state decay process implements exactly this record law. Such a
model is not a random Hamiltonian and therefore does not contradict the
obstruction; it shows why the rival class matters.

### Complete-channel quantum absorber

Prepare an unrelated two-level ancilla in $|0\rangle_E$ and choose a unitary
whose action includes

\[
|2,0\rangle\mapsto
\sqrt{1-\gamma}|2,0\rangle+\sqrt\gamma|0,1\rangle.
\]

Tracing out the ancilla gives the same amplitude-damping channel on every
detector input, including coherences. Complete endpoint process tomography can
therefore exclude the random-unitary rival without deciding whether the
environment was a graviton mode, another quantum field, direct quantum action,
or apparatus cross-talk.

This is the same endpoint-mediation nonidentification already earned in
`HC-DU-192`, now instantiated on a source-pinned gravitational detector.

### Spontaneous emission is a process, not a unique causal explanation

The vacuum/radiation-reaction decomposition is itself delicate. Hsiang and Hu
review why vacuum fluctuation and quantum dissipation must be treated together
in atom--field systems and why classical radiation reaction is not a free
substitute for quantum dissipation:
[arXiv:1910.11527](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.11527). DU therefore should not
claim either that “radiation reaction trivially explains everything” or that a
downward transition alone uniquely reveals a vacuum field. The acquired
multi-time process and its admitted source/deletion controls must do that work.

## The finite-time boundary

The 2024 calculation first defines an adiabatic switching function but then
takes $t_i\to-\infty$, $t_f\to\infty$ and reports a factor
$\delta^2(\omega-2\omega_0)$. A squared Dirac distribution is not a finite
normalized probability. The source acknowledges that a realistic calculation
needs finite limits.

For a rectangular duration $T$, the amplitude window is

\[
\int_0^T e^{i\Delta t}dt
=e^{i\Delta T/2}\frac{2\sin(\Delta T/2)}{\Delta},
\]

and hence

\[
W_T(\Delta)=
\begin{cases}
T^2,&\Delta=0,\\
4\sin^2(\Delta T/2)/\Delta^2,&\Delta\ne0.
\end{cases}
\]

For a continuum and long time this can be converted into a rate with the mode
density and standard golden-rule normalization. Until switching, bandwidth,
mode density, preparation, readout, and nuisance damping are frozen, the paper
does not provide a finite implementation-complete prediction packet.

The two-gap idea nevertheless survives in typed form: two independently fixed
trap frequencies $\omega_{0,1}$ and $\omega_{0,2}$ select two resonances
$2\omega_{0,1}$ and $2\omega_{0,2}$. What remains unearned is a finite no-refit
rate and a physical record that separates the direct/dissipative rivals.
