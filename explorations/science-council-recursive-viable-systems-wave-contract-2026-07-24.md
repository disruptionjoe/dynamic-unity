# Science Council recursive viable-systems wave contract

## Purpose

Run a fourth independent five-persona Science Council wave from the complete
scientific record of the preceding three waves while admitting this new
conditional premise:

> There may be an unknown number of recursively nested viable-system-like
> models that must be discovered to make sense of the dynamics from the
> smallest admitted scales to the largest admitted scales.

Joe's spoken phrase is interpreted as **Viable System Models** in the
Stafford-Beer/cybernetic sense. The physical hypothesis is deliberately named
**recursive viable-system-like organization**, not the literal claim that
atoms, observers, galaxies, or the universe are organizations containing
human managerial departments. Beer's recursion, autonomy, coordination,
regulation, environmental intelligence, and identity functions provide a
candidate structural analogy and source of testable questions. They are not a
physical theorem or a license to label every hierarchy "viable."

The five lenses are:

1. orthodox professor;
2. heterodox professor;
3. commercial scientist;
4. wild-frontier scientist; and
5. philosopher of science.

This is independent parallel work, not deliberation. Each persona must freeze
its contested proposition before reading any same-wave peer output. Persona
agreement is not evidence. Root will independently inspect and rerun every
result used in shared state.

## The admitted premise, typed

The following clauses are the common starting specification, not conclusions.

- **RVS-S0 — unknown recursion depth.** In the admitted premise, the number
  `m` of scientifically useful viable-system levels is not fixed in advance.
  A concrete analysis must compare at least `m=0`, `m=1`, and one `m>1`
  possibility within a declared finite observation window. The conditional
  premise expects one or more levels, but the zero-level rival remains
  mandatory so the analysis can fail locally.
- **RVS-S1 — system boundary.** Every proposed system at level `ell` must
  expose a boundary or partition, internal state, environment, inputs,
  outputs, and persistence criterion. A named particle, observer, era,
  subsystem, or coarse-grained block is not automatically a system.
- **RVS-S2 — operational viability.** "Viable" means that a declared set of
  essential variables remains within a declared admissible region under a
  declared perturbation family for a declared duration or recurrence
  condition. Passive stability, long lifetime, high probability, mere
  existence, and active regulation must be distinguished.
- **RVS-S3 — recursive closure.** A level-`ell` viable unit may participate as
  an operational component of a level-`ell+1` viable unit. Recursion requires
  a shared structural passport or commuting relationship across adjacent
  levels. It does not require identical parameters, literal self-similarity,
  or five anthropomorphic components at every scale.
- **RVS-S4 — functional decomposition.** Any use of Beer's five-system
  structure must operationalize, at minimum:
  1. local operations;
  2. coordination or oscillation suppression;
  3. present-state regulation and resource allocation;
  4. environment-facing anticipation or adaptation; and
  5. persistence/identity constraint selection.
  A persona may replace this with a leaner mathematically defensible
  viability passport, but must state which Beer functions disappear, merge,
  or remain analogy only.
- **RVS-S5 — level discovery.** Scale boundaries and recursion depth must be
  inferred from dynamics, conditional independence, intervention response,
  predictive compression, viability kernels, or another declared criterion.
  They may not be chosen because familiar names such as particle, cell,
  observer, organism, society, galaxy, or universe make a compelling ladder.
- **RVS-S6 — cross-level maps.** Every adjacent pair must expose an upward
  coarse-graining or record map and a downward constraint, control, boundary,
  or constitutive map. If either direction is absent, the work must say
  whether the result is ordinary coarse-graining rather than recursive
  viability.
- **RVS-S7 — covariance and identity.** In a causal-order setting, discovered
  levels and their scores must be invariant under natural relabeling or
  explicitly treat differing labels/update records as physical. A hidden
  fibre, arbitrary partition, scheduler phase, or observer convention may not
  silently select the hierarchy.
- **RVS-S8 — scale and units.** Multiple levels can generate dimensionless
  ratios or timescale separation. They cannot create dimensional units ex
  nihilo. Any physical scale still needs a declared microscopic unit and a
  regulator-stable generated hierarchy.
- **RVS-S9 — record and clock semantics.** A global update opportunity, local
  durable record, copied message, gated miss, post, and macro-level memory are
  distinct objects. A lower-level update does not automatically become one
  upper-level record, and level-specific clocks must expose their
  denominator and comparison map.
- **RVS-S10 — no teleology smuggling.** Persistence under constraints does not
  establish purpose, agency, consciousness, intelligence, or cosmic
  intention. Those stronger interpretations require separate observables.
- **RVS-S11 — no universal-hierarchy assumption.** Nested trees, overlapping
  coalitions, heterarchies, scale-free continua, and no privileged macrolevel
  are live rivals. "Unknown number" must not become permission to add levels
  until a fit succeeds.
- **RVS-S12 — conditional status.** A toy construction, structural analogy,
  or exact finite decomposition does not establish that reality is made of
  recursive viable systems. The work asks what follows, what discriminates
  the premise, and whether it changes Dynamic Unity's best build.

## The recursive-viability passport

Every constructed candidate must fill or explicitly reject these fields for
each proposed level:

```text
level identifier and admitted resolution
microstate and macrostate spaces
boundary / partition / Markov blanket
environment and perturbation family
essential variables and viable region
operational components
coordination channel
regulator / controller / resource constraint
adaptation or environment-model channel
identity / persistence condition
upward coarse-graining or durable-record map
downward constraint or intervention map
level clock / cadence denominator
held-out prediction or survival advantage
relabeling / reslicing / resolution invariance
complexity cost and selected recursion depth
```

At least one quantitative criterion must be used. Examples include:

- viability-kernel volume or controlled invariant-set size;
- survival or recurrence under held-out disturbances;
- predictive information or causal-emergence gain;
- conditional-independence/Markov-blanket stability;
- interventionally sufficient macrostate compression;
- regulator variety versus disturbance variety;
- transfer-operator or timescale separation;
- minimum description length or out-of-sample regret; or
- a rigorously justified alternative.

No single score is presumed sufficient. A persona must state whether its score
tests persistence, control, causal abstraction, compression, boundary
discovery, or recursion.

## Central research question

Can one discover a finite, non-arbitrary sequence of recursively viable
effective systems in a candidate Dynamic Unity dynamics such that each level
has an intervention-stable boundary, a genuine viability advantage, typed
upward and downward maps, and a record/clock semantics—and does that sequence
connect causal microdynamics to observers, physical scales, and cosmological
structure more strongly than ordinary coarse-graining, renormalization, or an
overfit hierarchy?

The useful result may be:

- a constructive multilevel witness;
- a scoped no-go for exact Beer-style recursion in physics;
- an algorithm for discovering recursion depth;
- a distinction between viability, autonomy, causal emergence, and RG;
- a bridge among posts, observer records, and generated scales;
- a counterexample showing arbitrary hierarchy recovery; or
- a sharply factored intermediate object.

## Primary antecedents and scope controls

These are comparison surfaces, not premises:

- [Stafford Beer, *The Viable System Model: Its Provenance, Development,
  Methodology and
  Pathology*](https://www.kybernetik.ch/dwn/Viable_System_Model.pdf)
  describes the organizational/cybernetic VSM and its claim to explain
  independent viability. Beer states that each System One is itself a complete
  viable system and that the topology repeats across recursions. The same
  source also says viable-system boundaries are arbitrary, recursive
  embedding is not unique, and a system participates in multiple recursive
  chains. It therefore does not by itself supply one objectively discoverable
  number of physical levels or a fundamental law of physical scale.
- [W. Ross Ashby, *Requisite Variety and Its Implications for the Control of
  Complex Systems*](https://ashby.info/Ashby-Introduction-to-Cybernetics.pdf)
  gives a quantitative regulation constraint. Requisite variety is a
  necessary bound in its formal setting, not a sufficient certificate of
  viable organization.
- [Kenneth Wilson, *The Renormalization Group: Critical Phenomena and the
  Kondo Problem*](https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.47.773) supplies the
  standard multiscale coarse-graining, flow, fixed-point, and relevant-
  operator comparator. RG recursion alone is not Beer-style viability.
- [Hoel, Albantakis, and Tononi, *Quantifying causal emergence shows that
  macro can beat micro*](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1314922110) supplies a
  concrete effective-information criterion under which a macro description
  can outperform its micro description. That criterion does not by itself
  prove regulation, autonomy, or a unique hierarchy.
- [Karl Friston, *Life as we know it*](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2013.0475)
  supplies a Markov-blanket/self-organization antecedent under declared
  ergodic random-dynamical assumptions. A blanket is not automatically a
  viable controller or a physical observer.
- [Palacios, Razi, Parr, Kirchhoff, and
  Friston, *On Markov blankets and hierarchical
  self-organisation*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2019.110089) gives a
  two-level hierarchical self-organization construction and an in-principle
  recursive/RG reading. Its hierarchy depends on generative-model priors,
  attracting sets, and initial conditions; the authors do not claim that all
  systems organize hierarchically.
- [Jansma and Hoel, *Engineering
  Emergence*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02649) is a recent preprint
  explicitly treating causal contribution across a multiscale hierarchy. It
  is a useful modern comparator for discovering multiple levels, not
  established warrant for this premise.

Every source use must retain its domain, assumptions, dimensionality,
intervention semantics, and definition of system, level, or viability.

## Required order of work

Each persona works in this order:

1. **Reconstruct the research journey.** For Runs 1, 2, and 3, state the
   persona's scientifically distinctive finding, the collective result, and
   which open question logically motivated the next run. Focus on research
   objects and results, not orchestration mechanics.
2. **State the strongest recursive-viability premise.** Define system,
   boundary, viability, recursion, environment, scale, clock, record, and
   depth. State whether Beer's exact five-function form is used, weakened, or
   treated as analogy only.
3. **Build the passport and rival set.** Compare at least:
   - `m=0`, ordinary microdynamics/coarse-graining without privileged viable
     levels;
   - `m=1`, one discovered viable macrolevel; and
   - `m>1`, genuinely recursive levels.
   Include at least one non-tree or arbitrary-partition rival.
4. **Freeze one contested proposition.** Attack a material conclusion,
   inference, or neglected opportunity from the three-wave corpus that the
   recursive premise could actually change. The older finality/VSM mapping is
   an explicit live target; a persona may uphold, factor, narrow, or overturn
   it at the appropriate grade.
5. **Take one bounded swing.** Produce an exact derivation, executable finite
   probe, primary-source-bounded construction, counterexample, or decision
   assay. A verbal systems map alone is insufficient.
6. **Run hard nulls.** Include arbitrary partition, passive persistence,
   hierarchy-depth overfit, relabeling/resolution, held-out perturbation, and
   cross-level-map controls. Add clock, record, post, or preferred-frame
   controls where relevant.
7. **Reconcile by warrant.** Separate derived, conditionally entailed,
   constructively realized, abductively preferred, structural analogy,
   primary-source antecedent, and open conjecture.
8. **Produce a dependency-ordered roadmap.** Each action needs an output,
   falsifier, stop, and exact reopener. Do not restart killed proxy,
   coefficient, scheduler, or generic-enumeration loops.
9. **Produce hypothesis and stop ledgers.** Include possible claims without
   promoting them.
10. **Describe the four-run learning arc in plain English.** Explain what this
    persona learned at each run and what the new premise now changes or fails
    to change.
11. **Finish with an unconstrained divergent pass.** Preserve every genuinely
    noticed path without padding, ranking, commitment, or implied claim.

## Minimum hard nulls

The bounded swing must address:

1. **arbitrary-partition null:** many hand-selected partitions can look
   organized;
2. **passive-stability null:** persistence without regulation;
3. **depth-overfit null:** adding a level cannot earn credit merely by adding
   parameters;
4. **resolution null:** the selected number of levels must not track the
   analyst's cutoff without an explicit continuum interpretation;
5. **relabeling/fibre null:** gauge labels and hidden microfibres cannot select
   the hierarchy;
6. **held-out-disturbance null:** viability must predict beyond the
   perturbations used to discover it;
7. **ordinary-RG null:** self-similar coarse-graining without viability;
8. **clock/record null:** update opportunity and durable record cannot be
   conflated across levels; and
9. **teleology null:** successful regulation does not establish purpose or
   agency.
