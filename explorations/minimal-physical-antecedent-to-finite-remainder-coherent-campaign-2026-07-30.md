# Minimal Physical Antecedent to Finite Remainder

## Ratified direction

Dynamic Unity now asks an upstream question that its earlier North Star left
supplied:

> Determine what target-blind physical antecedents, if any, select material,
> provenance-bearing, observer-indexed record interfaces; then establish their
> observer-accessible reconstruction, operational-duality, and smallest
> finite-remainder boundaries.

The detailed scientific question is:

> What is the minimal target-blind physical antecedent under which lawful
> multi-time dynamics selects a material, observer-indexed,
> provenance-bearing record interface; which observer-accessible time,
> geometry, field or source relations, and capabilities then factor through
> its finite certified records; and what is the smallest physically realizable
> extension of access that exposes any remainder?

This change does **not** ratify a record ontology. It moves interface selection
inside the question. Record-first reconstruction, operational duality, and a
physics-first finite remainder remain coequal outcomes.

The campaign is one conditional funnel:

```text
unselected process description
  -> natural-section existence or obstruction
  -> minimal physical selection frontier
  -> material response-rank discriminator
  -> complete formed-record packet
  -> action-relative record quotient
  -> law-versus-record finite remainder
  -> smallest physically realizable first leak
  -> no-refit cross-platform transfer
  -> layered regional composition
  -> North-Star disposition or dynamics reopener
```

Only Swing 1 is executable. Swings 2--10 are prepared, not preauthorized.
Each activates only from a typed return of the preceding gate. None of the
older parked reopeners is deemed satisfied merely because Joe ratified this
campaign.

## Why this campaign is the coherent choice

Dynamic Unity has repeatedly shown that a state, trajectory, endpoint,
redundancy count, entropy, process representation, or record metric can be
mathematically useful while the apparatus, instrument, sampler, archive,
observer boundary, action class, or nuisance model remains supplied. The old
North Star began after that selection burden.

The highest-value next move is therefore not another candidate metric and not
an immediate experiment. It is to ask whether a complete operational record
interface can be selected naturally from a physical process at all. That gate
has four high-information outcomes:

1. a natural section exists in a scoped arena;
2. no natural section can exist under the declared invariances;
3. extra physical antecedents reduce, but do not eliminate, the interface
   orbit; or
4. the proposed result is already absorbed by known mathematics.

Every outcome determines the next legitimate move. A no-section theorem would
be a real result, not a failed build. A scoped section would identify exactly
what material work should follow. A partial frontier would make the remaining
supplied structure explicit. Prior-art absorption would close a rediscovery
before it consumes a campaign.

This is a charter-authorized work-ordering decision at Grade 0. It is not
evidence that a physical selector exists.

declared equivalence. Finite-resolution work must compare the law-only target

## Swing 1 — instrument no-section admission

**Question.** Does there exist a nontrivial natural assignment

\[
W\longmapsto (I_W,S_W,A_W,P_W,H_W)
\]

from a lawful multi-time process to an instrument, sampler, material archive,
provenance certificate, and observer action class that respects the declared
symmetries, operational equivalences, convex mixtures, and composition?

**Activation.** Immediate.

**Optimized approach.**

1. Scope the object ladder before searching: finite classical processes,
   finite quantum channels/combs, and only then a local-QFT extension.
2. State the category or groupoid of processes and the interface fibre over
   each process.
3. Attempt the smallest constructive section first.
4. In parallel, use automorphism or operational-equivalence orbits to seek a
   two-object counterexample with no invariant choice.
5. Collide the exact statement with known no-programming, measurement

   transformation results.
6. Bank the smallest scope that survives. Do not universalize from one toy.

**Positive.** A natural section exists in a declared arena and names the
minimal structure it uses.

**Cheapest kill.** Two physically and operationally equivalent
representatives related by an admitted automorphism require incompatible
interface choices, with no invariant element in the fibre.

**Durable return.** `NATURAL_SECTION_FOUND`, `NO_NATURAL_SECTION`,
`PARTIAL_SELECTION_FRONTIER`, or `KNOWN_RESULT_ABSORPTION`.
