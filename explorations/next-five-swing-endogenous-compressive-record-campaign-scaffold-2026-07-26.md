# Endogenous compression to held-out physics

## Executive decision

The next five swings should use **staged concentration**:

```text
S1  compare only mechanisms that could satisfy the whole H-CCR-17 conjunction
        |
        v
S2  try to derive the native interface from the winning dynamics
        |
        v
S3  prove an actual write, persistence, and strict physical compression
        |
        v
S4  test no-refit transfer to held-out physics and an enlarged capability class
        |
        v
S5  attack robustness and return the strongest honest North-Star verdict
```

This is deliberately between broad fan-out and immediate commitment. The
first swing is broad enough to avoid spending four swings in the wrong
mechanism. The remaining four stay in one arena so that selection, formation,
compression, and reconstruction cannot be borrowed from different examples
and presented as one result.

`ECR-N5-S1` is now complete as `HC-DU-042`. It returned
`NO_READY_ARENA`: every current candidate selects or supports a stable
algebra, phase, quotient, source, or redundant pattern while leaving the
complete provenance-bearing physical write underselected. The common minimum
missing premise is a write-complete physical antecedent. Matter flow plus
metastability remains the first host, with decoherence/SBS as its mandatory
absorber.

`ECR-N5-S2` is now the sole next executable object, retyped as the
matter-flow metastable write-selector or tokenization-obstruction test.
`ECR-N5-S3` through `ECR-N5-S5` remain conditional branch contracts, not
active runs.

The campaign question is:

> Can independently motivated physical dynamics select and physically form a
> genuinely smaller observer record that still reconstructs independently
> held-out observer physics under a frozen action and resource envelope—or
> can we prove which part of that conjunction is impossible?

That is `H-CCR-17`. The campaign promotes no answer to it.

## 1. Why the problem has changed

`HC-DU-041` separated three receipts:

\[
\ker a\subseteq\ker\xi
\quad\text{(interface selection),}
\]

\[
\ker r\subseteq\ker t
\quad\text{(target reconstruction),}
\]

and

\[
\ker r\subseteq\ker\beta_H
\quad\text{(behavioral sufficiency/finality for capability class \(H\)).}
\]

Existing controls earn these in different places:

- the material gauge and stabilizer-QEC controls strictly compress and
  reconstruct, but their detector or code architectures are supplied;
- Einstein clocks and phase-sensitive repairs reconstruct only after
  injective source tomography;
- localized AQFT selects a realizability family relative to supplied
  coupling data, not one formed archive; and
- public-shadow descent starts only after interfaces have already formed.

The next campaign therefore cannot be another detector variant, another
code, another fitted sensor array, or another public-ledger composition. It
must try to make all of the load-bearing properties coexist in **one natural
physical construction**.

## 2. Why this is not another physical-arena tournament

The prior
[physical arena selection tournament](physical-arena-selection-tournament-2026-07-26.md)
asked which arena could most cheaply combine a lawful source relation, a
formed pointer, exact scoped reconstruction, and a first leak. It correctly
selected a materialized gauge boundary. The later campaign then learned why
that does not close the North Star: the material boundary, detector,
orientation, pointer, decoder, and action class were supplied.

`ECR-N5-S1` asks a stricter question:

> Which mechanism has a credible, locally decidable route to selecting its
> own record interface **and** forming a strict quotient **and** transferring
> to held-out physics?

The gauge and QEC specimens are now controls against which candidates are
tested, not candidates that can win by repeating their existing receipts.

## 3. Typed campaign object

For one frozen lawful realization class \(\Omega\), keep these maps distinct:

\[
a:\Omega\to A
\]

is the independently specified physical antecedent;

\[
\xi:\Omega\to I
\]

is the native interface architecture;

\[
W:(\omega,m_{\varnothing})\mapsto(\omega',m_r)
\]

is an actual physical write from a declared blank memory;

\[
r:\Omega\to R
\]

is the formed record after the write;

\[
t_j:\Omega\to T_j
\]

are independently declared held-out physical targets; and

\[
\beta_H:\Omega\to B_H
\]

is complete admitted behavior under capability/resource envelope \(H\).

A positive `H-CCR-17` specimen must jointly earn:

1. **selection:** \(\ker a\subseteq\ker\xi\), up to a declared physical gauge
   or unavoidable orbit;
2. **formation:** \(W\) physically writes a provenance-bearing, persistent
   record rather than merely transporting supplied data or naming a
   subalgebra;
3. **strict compression:** there are lawful nongauge
   \(\omega_1\ne\omega_2\) with \(r(\omega_1)=r(\omega_2)\);
4. **record attribution:** the lawful pre-record target diameter is nonzero;
5. **held-out reconstruction:** \(\ker r\subseteq\ker t_j\) for at least one
   target not used to select \(\xi\), define \(r\), or fit a decoder;
6. **frozen-envelope sufficiency:** the claimed finality class satisfies
   \(\ker r\subseteq\ker\beta_H\); and
7. **capability honesty:** for a preregistered enlargement \(H\subset H'\),
   either sufficiency persists or one finite first-leak witness is returned.

No single inclusion can stand in for the others.

## 5. Triple Diamond 1 — purpose and opportunity

### Divergence

The lenses considered five broad strategies.

| strategy | strongest case | limiting fact |
|---|---|---|
| Return directly to the material gauge boundary | It already has exact strict compression, reconstruction, and a first leak. | The detector/interface is exactly the supplied ingredient now under question. Repeating it cannot answer `H-CCR-17`. |
| Commit immediately to Einstein--matter | Matter already selects a timelike flow and sometimes a synchronization structure. | The completed ladder found no endogenous write or observer token. Four more swings could merely add a memory by hand. |
| Commit immediately to decoherence/Quantum Darwinism | It has the strongest established account of environment-selected pointer structure and public redundancy. | Full SBS/decoherence may absorb the result; observer boundary, fragment access, and held-out physical target can remain supplied. |
| Prove a universal no-go first | A theorem that no dynamics can select a strict reconstructive record would be profound. | The relevant conditions are not yet sharp enough across metastability, open systems, matter clocks, and topological sectors. A universal statement now risks vacuity. |
| Run a strict mechanism tournament, then concentrate | It compares only candidates capable of the whole conjunction and lets exact failure eliminate bad paths cheaply. | It spends one swing before construction, so the tournament must return a winner, a minimal separator, or a bankable no-ready-arena result. |

### Convergence

Choose the fifth strategy. The campaign objective is not to maximize the
number of ideas touched. It is to find the least artificial host of
endogenous strict compression, then attack that one host until it yields a
theorem, counterexample, or exact obstruction.

The first tournament should compare **mechanism chains**, not evocative
ontologies:

1. **matter-flow to metastable archive** — matter/geometry selects a
   congruence or source variable; nonequilibrium dynamics selects and writes
   a persistent coarse archive;
2. **environment-selected pointer to redundant public record** — an
   independently fixed open-system generator selects a pointer/slow algebra
   and forms redundant fragments;
3. **Hamiltonian-selected topological or logical sector** — local dynamics
   and phase structure select a protected quotient without a supplied check
   matrix;
4. **causal-action or local-QFT source to instrument chain** — a physical
   selection principle narrows the source/configuration before a localized
   process forms a record; and
5. **conservation-law or phase-boundary recorder** — a naturally oriented
   material phase boundary writes a conserved regional distinction without
   inheriting the old supplied gauge detector.

The first chain is the incumbent because it joins the strongest partial
selection result to the strongest plausible formation mechanism. The second
is the strongest known-physics absorber. The third is the strongest
compression control. The fourth has the strongest source-selection ambition
and weakest current record engine. The fifth tests whether a material
boundary can be physically selected rather than merely placed.

No candidate advances merely because it belongs to a more fundamental
theory.

## 6. Triple Diamond 2 — architecture and sequence

### Divergence

Four orchestration shapes were compared.

1. **Five mechanisms in parallel.** Broad but shallow; each can borrow a
   different notion of selection or record.
2. **One arena for all five swings immediately.** Deep but premature; the
   current incumbent has not passed a formation plausibility gate.
3. **Universal theorem, then examples.** Elegant but likely to overgeneralize
   before the right physical hypotheses are known.
4. **One bounded tournament, then four conditional swings in the winner.**
   Preserves divergence once and forces cumulative depth afterward.

### Convergence

Use shape 4. The branch-safe dependency graph is:

```text
ECR-N5-S1  mechanism tournament and arena passport
      |
      +-- winner --------------------+
      |                              v
      +-- near tie -> exact separator -> ECR-N5-S2
      |                              |
      +-- no ready arena -> minimum  |
          missing-premise theorem ---+
                                     v
ECR-N5-S2  native interface selection or obstruction
                                     |
                                     v
ECR-N5-S3  physical write + persistence + strict compression
                                     |
                                     v
ECR-N5-S4  held-out transfer + capability enlargement
                                     |
                                     v
ECR-N5-S5  robustness + North-Star adjudication
```

An adverse result changes the next swing's target but does not end the
campaign. For example, if no point selector exists, Swing 2 returns the
minimal orbit or missing premise and Swing 3 asks whether formation is
well-defined on that orbit. If the only formed record is injective, Swing 3
banks a tomography ceiling and Swing 4 tests whether any physically selected
coarsening preserves a held-out target. If transfer fails, Swing 5 hardens
the smallest counterexample rather than polishing the failed model.

## 7. Five prepared swing cards

### `ECR-N5-S1` — Endogenous Record Mechanism Tournament

**Decision uncertainty.** Does any current mechanism family have a credible
pre-hardware route to all seven typed obligations, and which exact missing
obligation separates the leaders?

**Input.** `HC-DU-033F/G/H`, `HC-DU-035E`, `HC-DU-040C/D/E`,
`HC-DU-041`, `H-CCR-17`, and the known local-model learning gate.

**Method.**

1. Freeze one common arena passport containing the seven obligations.
2. Give each of the five mechanism chains a supplied-versus-selected ledger,
   smallest lawful class, candidate write, strict-quotient witness, two
   possible held-out targets, capability enlargement, and strongest
   absorber.
3. Require one local exact theorem or counterexample that could be obtained
   before hardware. Reject candidates whose first meaningful discriminator
   is empirical access.
4. Compare lexicographically:
   - nonempty target-open lawful class;
   - plausible target-independent interface selection;
   - actual formation and persistence;
   - strict nongauge compression;
   - held-out no-refit target;
   - finite \(H\subset H'\) assay;
   - fewer supplied arrows; and
   - cheapest exact kill.
5. Return one winner, a near tie with one exact separating question, or
   `NO_READY_ARENA` plus the common minimum missing premise.

**Strongest rivals.** The material gauge and QEC specimens can appear
superior because their supplied architectures already close the hard arrows.
Full SBS/decoherence can absorb environment-selected public records.

**Cheapest positive.** One candidate has a typed, primary-source-supported
path to every obligation and a locally decidable next theorem.

**Cheapest kill.** Every candidate either supplies the interface/write,
becomes injective tomography, lets the law close the target, or lacks a
held-out target. The common failure becomes an exact minimum-premise
conjecture or obstruction program.

**Durable output.** An endogenous-record arena passport, supplied/selected
matrix, absorber ledger, and sole next theorem contract.

**Stop.** Do not construct a model if the tournament cannot name the first
decision-changing local theorem or counterexample.

**Maximum grade.** Physical research selection and method preparation; no
scientific claim.

**Computation/hardware.** Primary-source collision and exact analysis first.
No hardware. No simulation unless a minimal computation returns an exact
candidate separator unavailable at equal grade analytically.

### `ECR-N5-S2` — Native Interface Selection or Minimum-Premise Obstruction

**Decision uncertainty.** Does the winning physical antecedent select its
record interface up to declared gauge/orbit without consulting the target?

**Inherited input.** The exact winner or minimum separator from Swing 1.

**Method.**

1. Freeze \(\Omega,a,\xi\), symmetries, representation equivalence, boundary
   data, resource class, and perturbation class.
2. Test \(\ker a\subseteq\ker\xi\) and equivariance under the physical
   symmetry group.
3. Use counterfactual twins with identical antecedents and incompatible
   interfaces as the primary hostile test.
4. Classify the result as a selected point, selected orbit, plural family,
   trivial interface, or empty nontrivial class.
5. If selection fails, derive the smallest additional physical premise and
   whether it is a reference, boundary, state, environment, asymmetry, or
   action restriction.

**Strongest absorbers.** Predictability sieve/pointer-basis selection,
spontaneous symmetry breaking, committor/slow-mode theory, topological phase
selection, and ordinary measurement-apparatus design.

**Cheapest positive.** A uniqueness-up-to-gauge theorem stable over a
declared perturbation class.

**Cheapest kill.** Two equally lawful, symmetry-related interface
architectures survive every frozen antecedent and predict different record
quotients.

**Adverse branch.** Carry the minimal orbit or missing premise—not a
hand-chosen point—into Swing 3 and ask whether the physical write descends to
it.

**Durable output.** A selector theorem, orbit classification, or
minimum-premise obstruction.

**Maximum grade.** Scoped selection/necessity grade only in the frozen class.

**Computation/hardware.** Symbolic or exact finite classification only when
it proves the symmetry/orbit boundary. No hardware.

### `ECR-N5-S3` — Physical Formation, Persistence, and Strict Compression

**Decision uncertainty.** Does the selected interface actually form a
provenance-bearing record, and is that record a genuine physical quotient
rather than a transported initial value or an injective source log?

**Inherited input.** The selected point/orbit or minimum-premise receipt from
Swing 2.

**Method.**

1. Specify the write \(W\), blank memory, source, energy/resource ledger,
   reset, provenance, and finite stability horizon.
2. Separate write formation from passive transport and from post hoc
   declaration of an observable algebra.
3. Prove persistence under a frozen perturbation ball using the mechanism's
   natural tool: spectral gap, metastable basin, large-deviation bound,
   conservation law, topological protection, or local causal factorization.
4. Exhibit two lawful nongauge realizations in one record fibre.
5. Confirm nonzero pre-record target diameter and rule out target-defined
   coarse graining.
6. Determine whether the quotient is autonomous for a preregistered
   capability class \(H\).

**Strongest absorbers.** Landauer accounting, decoherence/SBS, metastability
and sufficient-statistic theory, QEC/noiseless subsystems, ordinary detector
physics, and inaccessible-environment completion.

**Cheapest positive.** A target-independent write theorem with a nontrivial
record fibre and finite persistence/error bound.

**Cheapest kills.**

- no actual write occurs;
- the stable variable is supplied by initial conditions or apparatus;
- the record map is injective modulo gauge;
- the target is already law-fixed; or
- persistence exists only outside the declared action/resource envelope.

**Adverse branch.** Bank `TRANSPORT_ONLY`, `SUPPLIED_INTERFACE`,
`TOMOGRAPHY_CEILING`, or `NO_AUTONOMOUS_QUOTIENT` and pass the exact
obstruction to Swing 4.

**Durable output.** A formation theorem or a minimized formation/compression
counterexample with a machine-checkable kernel certificate where possible.

**Maximum grade.** Scoped physical formation and strict-compression result;
not universal finality or ontology.

**Computation/hardware.** A local model is allowed only if it identifies the
exact fibre, spectral boundary, or counterexample before hardware. Parameter
sweeps without a proof boundary are excluded.

### `ECR-N5-S4` — Held-Out Transfer and Capability-Growth Assay

**Decision uncertainty.** Does the formed strict quotient reconstruct
independently held-out physics without refit, and exactly where does the
claim break when capability grows?

**Inherited input.** The strongest honestly formed quotient or exact adverse
receipt from Swing 3.

**Method.**

1. Predeclare at least two target families—preferably one time/geometry/field
   target and one action/capability target.
2. Freeze calibration sources, held-out sources, interventions, nuisance
   variables, decoder, error tolerance, and resource budget before testing.
3. Report lawful target diameter before record conditioning.
4. Test \(\ker r\subseteq\ker t_j\) on the held-out split with no refit.
5. Test \(\ker r\subseteq\ker\beta_H\) separately.
6. Enlarge capability once, \(H\subset H'\), and either prove continued
   factorization or localize the first same-record separating action.
7. Run law-only, hidden-environment, target-coded, representation, and
   ordinary-sufficient-statistic twins.

**Strongest absorbers.** Blackwell/quantum sufficiency, process tensors,
inverse problems, effective field theory, QEC, and full-environment
completion.

**Cheapest positive.** One frozen noninjective record decoder transfers to a
held-out physical target and one capability class, with a preregistered
finite boundary under \(H'\).

**Cheapest kill.** One lawful same-record pair changes the held-out target at
fixed \(H\), or success disappears after a legitimate nuisance,
environment, or resource completion.

**Adverse branch.** Return a finite target-specific obstruction, an
operational-duality-only result, or a proof that the record's sufficient
target class is narrower than claimed.

**Durable output.** A no-refit transfer certificate plus first-leak witness,
or the smallest exact same-record/different-target counterexample.

**Maximum grade.** Scoped reconstruction, operational duality, or
capability-relative remainder in the declared physical class.

**Computation/hardware.** Local exact computation is allowed when the
held-out test can decide the factorization boundary. Hardware remains
unavailable; any later hardware-only discriminator becomes an awareness
note, not a reason to keep building.

### `ECR-N5-S5` — Robustness and North-Star Adjudication

**Decision uncertainty.** Does the surviving result reflect a physical

**Inherited input.** The selector, formation, compression, transfer, and

2. Recheck provenance and physical joint realizability.
3. Collide the complete result—not isolated components—with its strongest
   standard absorber.
4. State exactly which arrows are selected, orbit-selected, supplied,
   fitted, represented, or merely assumed.
5. Return one global verdict:

```text
ENDOGENOUS_COMPRESSIVE_RECONSTRUCTION
ENDOGENOUS_OPERATIONAL_DUALITY_ONLY
FINITE_CAPABILITY_RELATIVE_REMAINDER
SELECTION_OR_FORMATION_OBSTRUCTION
INJECTIVE_TOMOGRAPHY_ONLY

```

**Cheapest positive.** The same theorem and held-out decoder survive the
frozen benign transformations and strongest absorber without refit.

**Cheapest kill.** A benign representation/refinement change destroys the
selector, quotient, or held-out factorization.

**Durable output.** A scoped theorem package, exact no-go/obstruction, or
minimum-premise research reset. Paper or prediction work may be recommended
only afterward under its own authority.

**Maximum grade.** Whatever the weakest required receipt honestly supports;
never the average or aspiration of the five swings.

**Computation/hardware.** Formal and local robustness tests only. No external
action in this campaign by default.

## 8. Triple Diamond 3 — assurance and adaptation

### Hard attribution stops

Stop and return the named failure rather than repairing silently when:

1. the law already makes the target constant before the record;
2. the interface, basis, observer, code, boundary, pointer, archive, decoder,
   or action class is inserted and then credited as selected;
3. the “record” is only a named algebra, passive transport, or unformed
   environmental correlation;
4. reconstruction uses an injective source log;
5. the quotient is defined from the held-out target;
6. incompatible records are concatenated without physical joint
   realizability;
7. a capability enlargement exposes a fibre while the old finality claim is
   retained;
8. a completion, environment, regulator, resource, or boundary is changed
   without re-adjudicating the contract;
9. a local build cannot produce decision-changing knowledge before external
   hardware; or
10. a component theorem is promoted as a new ontology, universal law, paper,
    or prediction.

### Positive controls

Keep the existing controls fixed:

- material gauge boundary: strict compression plus supplied formation;
- stabilizer QEC: protected quotient plus supplied code/check family;
- Einstein two-clock and two-sensitivity phase cases: injective tomography;
- localized AQFT: causal instrument family without selected formed archive;
- SBS/Quantum Darwinism: environment-induced public-record absorber;
- process tensors and inaccessible-environment completions: omitted-history
  absorbers; and
- authenticated distributed systems: provenance, failure-domain, and
  action-relative finality control.

### Useful outcomes under every branch

| branch | useful scientific return |
|---|---|
| One mechanism passes the tournament | Concentrated theorem target with explicit absorbers. |
| Two candidates tie | One exact discriminating lemma rather than two full builds. |
| No candidate is ready | Common minimum-premise theorem or no-ready-arena result. |
| Interface selection fails | Symmetry orbit, plural family, or minimum external premise. |
| Formation fails | No-write/transport-only obstruction. |
| Compression fails | Injective-tomography ceiling. |
| Held-out transfer fails | Smallest same-record/different-target witness. |
| Transfer holds only at \(H\) | Exact capability-relative finality boundary. |
| Robustness fails | Representation/refinement dependence theorem. |
| Whole chain survives | First complete `H-CCR-17` specimen, still scoped to its frozen class. |

### Convergence

The campaign is worth running because it has no low-information terminal
branch. What it must not do is keep a favored construction alive by supplying
each missing arrow one at a time.
