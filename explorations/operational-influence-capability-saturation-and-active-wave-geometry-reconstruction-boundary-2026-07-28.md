# Operational causal capability reconstruction

## Executive result

`HC-DU-093` established that an unlabelled future-algebra diagram
reconstructs causal order only when the algebra assignment is
order-reflecting. This swing asks whether complete operational response data
automatically supplies that converse.

It does not.

> One complete process determines which influences that process actually
> realizes. Spacetime causal order determines which influences are physically
> possible. Permission does not require use.

The smallest exact witness has only two sites. A constant/replacer process
has the same complete intervention-response law whether the sites are
causally ordered or spacelike-incomparable. Complete process tomography cannot
discover a causal possibility that the process never uses.

The positive condition is exact. For a physically admitted family of
processes and interventions, take the union of all operational influences and
then its transitive closure. This reconstructs the physical causal order
exactly if and only if the family is **causally saturating**: every admitted
causal comparison is realized by a finite chain of distinguishable physical
influences.

This is the operational version of `HC-DU-093`'s order-reflection premise.
It is not automatically guaranteed by:

- one history;
- one process tensor;
- no-signalling;
- algebraic commutation;
- ordinary observational faithfulness;
- all mathematically causal channels; or
- a theoretical list of unimplemented interventions.

The strongest positive control is already known. Lorentzian nonlinear-wave
inverse-problem theorems show that a sufficiently rich source-to-solution map
available in a neighborhood of one timelike worldline—or even restricted
along one timelike curve in a later result—can determine topology,
differential structure, and conformal geometry in the causally accessible
region.

That changes the interpretation of the North-Star problem:

> Geometry is not generally reconstructible from one actual record history.
> It can be reconstructible from certified records of a sufficiently rich,
> independently fixed capability surface: what localized interventions could
> be made and what responses they would produce.

The remaining gap is no longer “find another record scalar.” It is:

```text
physical law selects a realizable source/probe family
  -> bounded interventions produce retained response records
  -> finite certified data approximate the required response operator
  -> held-out causal/conformal targets reconstruct without refit.
```

The audited inverse theorems use an ideal source-to-solution operator over a
continuum of sources and responses. A single timelike curve narrows spatial
access, but it does not make the data one finite record. Recent QFT work also
proves that mathematical causality is weaker than physical operation
realizability. Therefore the active-wave route is a serious positive
candidate, but it does not yet satisfy the finite certified reopener or select
a ready successor.

## 1. Frozen operational object

Let \((V,\prec)\) be a finite strict causal poset of admitted physical sites.
Let \(\mathfrak P\) be a declared family of processes. Each process
\(P\in\mathfrak P\) has:

- a physically typed local intervention family \(\mathcal I_x^P\) at every
  site \(x\);
- a complete context specifying the interventions at the other sites; and
- a complete returned response law at every admitted readout.

For \(x,y\in V\), define

\[
x\rightsquigarrow_P y
\]

when some fixed background context \(c\) and two admitted interventions
\(a,a'\in\mathcal I_x^P\) give distinct response distributions at \(y\):

\[
\left\|
p_y^P(\,\cdot\mid a,c)
-
p_y^P(\,\cdot\mid a',c)
\right\|_{\rm TV}>0.
\]

This is a relation extracted from the complete operational process, not from
one sampled outcome.

Define the family relation

\[
R_{\mathfrak P}
=
\operatorname{TC}
\left(
\bigcup_{P\in\mathfrak P}\rightsquigarrow_P
\right),
\]

where \(\operatorname{TC}\) denotes transitive closure.

Assume **causal soundness**:

\[
x\rightsquigarrow_P y
\Longrightarrow
x\prec y.
\]

This is the operational no-backwards/no-spacelike-signalling direction. It
does not contain the converse.

## 2. Theorem 1 — operational influence is a causal suborder

### Theorem

Under causal soundness,

\[
R_{\mathfrak P}\subseteq\prec.
\]

### Proof

Every edge in every \(\rightsquigarrow_P\) is a causal comparison by
soundness. Transitivity of \(\prec\) contains every finite path of such
comparisons, hence contains their transitive closure. \(\square\)

### Meaning

The complete process can certify positive influence:

```text
response difference under a frozen physical intervention
  -> operational causal influence
  -> one admitted causal comparison.
```

A null response has a different type:

```text
no response difference in the admitted process/action class
  -> no realized influence there
  -/-> spacelike separation
  -/-> absence of a wider causal capability.
```

This is the causal counterpart of a null experiment under a restricted
capability envelope. It identifies operational equivalence for those actions,
not universal physical absence.

## 3. Theorem 2 — the smallest same-process/different-order witness

Let \(V=\{x,y\}\). Compare:

\[
P_{\rm chain}:\quad x\prec y
\]

with:

\[
P_{\rm anti}:\quad x\parallel y.
\]

At both sites admit every local preparation and readout in the declared
finite interface. Let the joint process discard every input and return one
fixed product output:

\[
\mathcal E(\rho_x\otimes\rho_y)
=
\sigma_x\otimes\sigma_y.
\]

Every intervention-response probability is identical in the two physical
causal arenas. In particular,

\[
\rightsquigarrow_{\mathcal E}=\varnothing.
\]

The chain arena permits \(x\)-to-\(y\) influence but this process does not use
it. The antichain forbids that influence. Complete process tomography
therefore cannot distinguish the arenas.

### Corollary — extension ambiguity

Within a permissive operational theory in which the causal order imposes
soundness but does not require every permitted connection to be used, the
response family identifies at most

\[
\left\{
Q:
Q\text{ is a strict partial order on }V,
R_{\mathfrak P}\subseteq Q
\right\}.
\]

Every extra comparison in \(Q\setminus R_{\mathfrak P}\) may remain idle
without changing the process family.

Additional field equations, covariance, locality, spectrum, boundary, or
realizability assumptions can narrow this class. Those are new physical
premises; they are not contained in the response relation alone.

## 4. Theorem 3 — capability-family saturation

Call \((\mathfrak P,\mathcal I)\) **causally saturating** for
\((V,\prec)\) when

\[
R_{\mathfrak P}=\prec.
\]

Equivalently, every causal comparison \(x\prec y\) is joined by a finite path

\[
x=x_0\rightsquigarrow_{P_1}x_1
\rightsquigarrow_{P_2}\cdots
\rightsquigarrow_{P_k}x_k=y
\]

using physically admitted processes and interventions.

### Theorem

Under causal soundness, the unlabelled family of operational influence
relations reconstructs the causal order exactly if and only if the family is
causally saturating.

### Proof

Soundness gives \(R_{\mathfrak P}\subseteq\prec\). Saturation gives the
reverse inclusion, so equality follows.

Conversely, if the response family reconstructs \(\prec\) through its
influence reachability, then every causal comparison must belong to
\(R_{\mathfrak P}\), which is exactly saturation. \(\square\)

This statement is elementary. Its scientific use is the typing:

```text
actual process completeness
  !=
physical capability-family completeness
  !=
causal-order completeness.
```

### Immediate versus reachability reconstruction

The theorem reconstructs the causal **order**. Recovering a particular DAG's
covering edges requires a stronger local condition and intervention coverage.
Parallel causal pathways may cancel observationally, and a total response can
vanish even when individual mechanisms exist.

Mazaheri, Zhang, and Uhler's
[intervention-only causal-discovery result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11816)
is a sharp current positive: hard interventions can tolerate pathway
cancellations that violate ordinary conditional-independence faithfulness.
The result still requires intervention-immediacy faithfulness and explicitly
returns equivalence classes when intervention scope is insufficient.

Thus interventions weaken the old premise; they do not remove every
faithfulness or coverage premise.

## 5. Why commutation cannot supply the missing orientation

Commutation, compatibility, and mutual no-signalling are symmetric
relations. For every relation \(C(x,y)=C(y,x)\), reversing a candidate causal
order leaves \(C\) unchanged.

Therefore a commutation graph by itself cannot orient time.

Microcausality supplies:

\[
x\parallel y
\Longrightarrow
[\mathcal A_x,\mathcal A_y]=0.
\]

It does not, without an additional converse, supply:

\[
[\mathcal A_x,\mathcal A_y]=0
\Longrightarrow
x\parallel y.
\]

Likewise a silent or decoupled process can be mutually no-signalling at
timelike-related sites. Symmetric compatibility may help identify a candidate
incomparability structure only under a converse-microcausality or causal
faithfulness premise. Directed intervention influence is still needed for
orientation.

This yields the minimum relational ladder:

```text
commutation / mutual compatibility
  -> symmetric coexistence information

directed intervention influence
  -> used causal arrows

physically realizable saturating process family
  -> causal possibility order

causal order + continuum hypotheses
  -> conformal geometry.
```

## 6. Quantum causal-model collision

Costa and Shrapnel's
[quantum causal modelling](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.07106) explicitly
imports quantum versions of causal Markov and faithfulness conditions and
admits indefinite causal structures.

Barrett, Lorenz, and Oreshkov's
[quantum causal models](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.10726) use the quantum
process as the central object, derive a DAG causal structure for unitary
quantum circuits, and connect graph properties to operational intervention
statements.

These results absorb the quantum-process components. They also enforce the
correct distinction:

```text
one output state
  < one complete process
  < a physically selected family of processes/interventions
  < a causal or indefinite-causal model class.
```

`HC-DU-073` is the two-site, three-rival minimum-intervention specialization
of this terrain. `HC-DU-094` does not repeat that fixture. It identifies why
even a completely characterized process may fail to reconstruct the wider
causal-possibility order.

## 7. Mathematical causality is not physical realizability

A reconstruction theorem must quantify over operations that the physical
theory actually permits, not every mathematical channel satisfying an
abstract no-signalling constraint.

Fewster and Verch's
[QFT measurement framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.13356) models
measurement through localized dynamical system--probe coupling. The
localization derives causal relations for induced observables and consistent
sequential probabilities.

Mandrysch, Simmons, and Navascués's 2026
[realizability analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12976) sharpens the
boundary in the free scalar field:

- Fewster--Verch-realizable instruments are causal;
- some causal channels cannot be approximated by Fewster--Verch schemes;
- deciding implementability versus distance from implementability is
  uncomputable in their declared channel family; and
- a wide class of random-displacement measurement channels is nevertheless
  realizable.

Therefore:

```text
causal mathematical channel
  !=
QFT-realizable intervention
  !=
bounded observer capability
  !=
recorded attempted process.
```

Using every causal channel to force saturation would silently enlarge the
physical capability class and can manufacture order reflection.

## 8. The serious positive — active nonlinear-wave reconstruction

The operational route is not merely a no-go. A mature inverse-problem
literature supplies the positive shape.

Kurylev, Lassas, and Uhlmann's
[Lorentzian nonlinear-wave inverse problem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.3386)
assumes a known observation neighborhood \(V\) around a timelike geodesic and
the source-to-solution operator

\[
L:
f\longmapsto u|_V
\]

for sources supported in \(V\). In four dimensions, the operator determines
the topological, differentiable, and conformal structure of the maximal
region from which waves can propagate to and return from the observer
neighborhood.

Lassas, Uhlmann, and Wang
[extend the semilinear result](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.06261) and also
recover coefficients of the nonlinearity in its theorem class.

Nursultanov, Oksanen, and Tzou later show that
[the source-to-solution map restricted along one timelike
curve](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06925) can determine conformal structure,
using nonlinear wave interactions.

These results are exceptionally relevant to Dynamic Unity because they:

1. use active local interventions rather than a globally exposed geometry;
2. reconstruct a hidden spacetime region from observer-local responses;
3. recover target geometry without inserting hidden-region coordinates into
   each observation; and
4. explicitly delimit the causally accessible reconstruction domain.

They are conditional Grade-3 reconstruction imports, not Dynamic Unity
discoveries.

### What remains supplied

The theorem contracts supply:

- a globally hyperbolic Lorentzian manifold class;
- a declared nonlinear hyperbolic field equation;
- a known observer neighborhood or timelike curve;
- a source function space and source localization;
- the ideal source-to-solution operator over a continuum of inputs;
- ideal access to the returned field restriction or singularities;
- regularity, smallness, and causal-domain conditions; and
- gauge/conformal equivalence.

The map is a complete counterfactual response surface. It is not one finite
experimental transcript.

“Single timelike curve” means one spatially narrow observation locus, not one
source pulse, one scalar, one time sample, or a finite-bit archive.

## 9. Exact North-Star correction

The result changes the minimum reconstruction ladder:

```text
one passive history
  < one complete actual process
  < a frozen physically realizable intervention family
  < a causally saturating capability surface
  < retained and certified response records for that surface
  < observer-accessible causal order
  < conformal geometry under continuum hypotheses
  < metric scale under additional volume / clock / dynamic data.
```

This does not make capability an intrinsic property of an object. The
capability surface is relational among:

- source;
- operation site;
- process law;
- observation boundary;
- readout;
- resources; and
- target error/horizon.

It also does not define the record using the target geometry. The action and
response contract must be frozen independently, and the geometry remains
held out.

The plain-English conclusion is:

> What happened once may not reveal everything that could have happened.
> Spacetime geometry is partly a structure of possible influence. To recover
> it operationally, an observer needs either a theory that supplies those
> possibilities or records from a sufficiently rich probing program.

Records need not create causal reality for such records to reconstruct it.
They certify the responses of physical capabilities selected elsewhere.
