# Constraint-compatible gravity interventions and radiative reopener

## Result

`HC-DU-197` correctly proved that an interventionally spanning port surface
can reconstruct a relative causal bottleneck. It left one condition implicit:

> the spanning interventions must belong to the physically admissible action
> algebra of the same frozen system.

That condition is load-bearing for gravity. A constrained near field is not in
general an independently replaceable subsystem while its source is held fixed.
Gauss-type constraints, gauge equivalence, boundary conditions, and the field
equations restrict the lawful joint source--field states. An operation that
sets the candidate field to an arbitrary value while leaving the source fixed
may leave the physical completion class rather than interrogate it.

The platform audit therefore changes the next object in four ways:

1. **Replace arbitrary port operations with a declared lawful intervention
   algebra.** An informationally complete family is complete only relative to
   the admissible physical action surface.
2. **Separate the constrained near field from free radiative modes.** The
   former may be fixed by source and boundary data; the latter can, in
   principle, be independently prepared or received.
3. **Treat source/direct equivalence on the constrained surface as an
   operational duality, not merely missing instrumentation.** An off-surface
   cut cannot refute that duality if the cut is not physically realizable.
4. **Reopen the radiative sector only for response-class discrimination.** A
   lawfully varied incoming mode can reject a near-field-only response, but a
   direct presentation supplied with the same radiative input may retain the
   complete response. Ontology still does not follow from response alone.

No reviewed gravitational proposal supplies the complete eight-field packet
left by `HC-DU-197`: source family, independently spanning physical port
operations, receiver, row-level lineage, surgical isolation, no-refit target,
physical port selection, and explicit rivals. An analogue cavity or phonon bus
can supply that packet in its own native domain, but does not transfer
gravitational attribution.

This is a scoped Grade-4 selection/identifiability boundary and platform
audit. It does not prove direct-action gravity, field ontology, quantized
gravity, an experimental impossibility, a Grade-5 remainder, or an active
successor. `CURRENT-RESEARCH.yaml` remains quiescent.

## 1. The missing domain condition

Let

\[
\mathcal C_{\mathrm{law}}
\]

be the frozen lawful completion class and let

\[
\mathfrak A_{\mathrm{law}}
\]

be the interventions physically admitted on that class. A record map is then
not merely a function of a state; it may be the joined family

\[
R_{\mathrm{law}}(c)
=
\{p(o\mid a,c):a\in\mathfrak A_{\mathrm{law}}\}.
\tag{1}
\]

For a target \(T\), reconstruction requires

\[
R_{\mathrm{law}}(c_1)=R_{\mathrm{law}}(c_2)
\Longrightarrow
T(c_1)=T(c_2).
\tag{2}
\]

Adding an operation that does not map lawful completions to lawful
completions does not refine (1). It changes \(\mathcal C_{\mathrm{law}}\), the
physical law, or both. The familiar `do`-operator intuition therefore needs a
typed physical realizability condition before it is transferred to a gauge
field.

### Constraint-compatible intervention principle

> A causal or reconstruction target is identifiable only relative to an
> independently specified lawful intervention algebra. An off-law action may
> be a useful mathematical counterfactual, but its discriminatory power is not
> an empirical discriminator of the original physical system.

This is not new gauge mathematics. The DU contribution is the completion-
fibre consequence: unphysical interventions cannot be counted as added
records, and their absence cannot automatically be diagnosed as incomplete
acquisition.

## 2. Smallest exact constrained counterexample

Let the source, constrained field coordinate, and detector be binary:

\[
S,M,D\in\{0,1\}.
\]

Impose the toy physical constraint

\[
M=S.
\tag{3}
\]

Compare two presentations:

\[
D_{\mathrm{field}}=M,
\qquad
D_{\mathrm{direct}}=S.
\tag{4}
\]

The full mathematical \((S,M)\) cube has four points. The lawful surface (3)
has only

\[
(0,0),\ (1,1).
\]

On that entire surface, both presentations yield the identical response

\[
D=(0,1).
\]

They differ only at \((0,1)\) and \((1,0)\), precisely the two points that
violate (3). Thus an arbitrary replacement of \(M\) would distinguish the
presentations mathematically, but not within the frozen lawful theory.

### Theorem 1 — constrained operational duality

If a mediator coordinate is a fixed function \(M=f(S,B)\) of source and
boundary data throughout the lawful completion class, then a mediated
response \(D=h(M,S)\) and a reduced direct response

\[
\widetilde h(S,B)=h(f(S,B),S)
\]

agree under every lawful source/boundary intervention. No operation outside
the graph of \(f\) is evidence about their relative empirical adequacy unless
the enlarged law independently admits it.

The proof is substitution. Its importance is not difficulty but scope: the
`HC-DU-197` full port cube is an ideal identification object, not a promise
that nature exposes the cube.

## 3. This does not erase every field operation

The conclusion is not “fields cannot be intervened on.” It is sector-relative.

- Constraint-bound longitudinal or near-field variables may be fixed by
  sources and boundary conditions.
- Free radiative modes are independent dynamical data in the relevant
  solution space.
- Gauge-variant local coordinates are not automatically observables, while
  radiative observables can be defined with the necessary gauge treatment.

Khavkine develops gauge-invariant local observables for linearized gravity and
their relation to radiative information
([arXiv:1404.4551](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.4551)). This does not select DU's
record interface, but it blocks the overcorrection that every field-facing
operation is meaningless.

## 4. Exact radiative positive control

Add one independently preparable binary mode \(R\). Compare

\[
D_{\mathrm{rad}}=S\oplus R
\tag{5}
\]

with a near-field-only rival

\[
D_{\mathrm{near}}=S.
\tag{6}
\]

The lawful four-point \((S,R)\) surface separates (5) and (6) whenever
\(R=1\). This is a valid response-class discriminator because varying \(R\)
is part of the physical arena by construction.

But define a direct presentation with the same external input:

\[
D_{\mathrm{direct+rad}}=S\oplus R.
\tag{7}
\]

Equations (5) and (7) agree everywhere. Therefore the positive control earns:

```text
INDEPENDENT_RADIATIVE_RESPONSE = identifiable
NEAR_FIELD_ONLY_RIVAL          = rejectable
FIELD_ONTOLOGY                 = not identified
```

The result is the radiative analogue of the earlier source-action duality:
response structure can be physically consequential without selecting a
unique mediator representation.

### BMV and other near-field entanglement proposals

The Bose--Marletto--Vedral program intentionally infers something about a
total mediator from probe entanglement while avoiding direct quantum control
of gravity
([Marletto and Vedral](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06036)). That makes it an
important theorem-class experiment, but not a realization of an independently
replaceable gravitational port. The direct-action, hidden-component, and
exclusive-mediation boundaries from `HC-DU-190` through `HC-DU-192` still
apply.

### Harmonic trap and massive acoustic resonators

The harmonic-trap proposal supplies an energy-selective receiver and the
vacuum transition asymmetry used in `HC-DU-195`
([Sen et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.18868)). Tobar et al. propose massive
quantum acoustic resonators for stimulated and spontaneous single-graviton
exchange ([arXiv:2308.15440](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.15440)). Shenderov et
al. sharpen a stimulated strategy using correlation with independently
detected gravitational-wave events and outline several tests
([arXiv:2407.11929](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.11929)).

These are serious radiative receiver programs. They do not provide an
informationally complete family of independently prepared incoming
gravitational quantum states, a surgical replacement of the gravitational
port, and complete attempt-level lineage. Their value is therefore not zero;
their typed role is narrower than the `HC-DU-197` packet.

Carney's analysis is the strongest immediate absorber: a click or squeezed
noise signature can be reproduced by a suitable classical gravitational wave
and therefore does not alone establish a graviton
([arXiv:2408.00094](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.00094)). The DU radiative
reopener must consequently predeclare classical-wave, direct-action, material-
damping, and cross-talk rivals rather than call receiver excitation a field-
quantization certificate.

### Controllable material mediator and analogues

A massive material mediator can be coherently controlled in proposals such as
Pedernales, Streltsov, and Plenio
([arXiv:2104.14524](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.14524)). It is useful for
testing mediator-identification logic, but the manipulable port is matter, not
the gravitational field.

A cavity, phonon, or circuit bus can implement the complete native packet and
serves as a positive control for the method. Building it locally would not
advance the gravity target if its only new result were that a controllable bus
is controllable. Under the local-computation rule, do not build this analogue
unless it decides a mathematical or acquisition-design ambiguity before the
gravity boundary.

## 7. The smallest worthwhile physical reopener

The next gravity-facing object, if independently justified later, is not an
arbitrary near-field reset. It is a **lawful radiative response packet**:

1. a predeclared source family or independently characterized incoming
   radiative family;
2. mode, polarization, frequency, timing, and state-resolution operations that
   are physically realizable and gauge compatible;
3. a remote receiver with a frozen instrument and finite-time response;
4. joined source/receiver lineage, including rejected or missing attempts to
   the admitted acquisition grade;
5. source deletion and off-resonant controls;
6. classical-wave, material-damping, cross-talk, direct-action, and generic
   quantum-ancilla rivals;
7. a no-refit transfer target fixed before acquisition; and
8. an explicit statement of which conclusion concerns response class, field
   quantization, or physical ontology.

The positive target should initially be modest and exact:

> A lawful radiative input changes the receiver process in a way excluded by a
> frozen near-field-only and declared classical response class.

That result would still not establish a unique ontology. A stronger result
would need a property constant across the complete lawful realization fibre,
or independently formed field provenance.

> those distinctions become certified?
