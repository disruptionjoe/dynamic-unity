# Cross-repo hardening integration

## Result

The review contained useful controls, but not a missing DU ontology or a new
universal theorem. Dynamic Unity already had the abstract requirements of
target-independent refinements, boundary-relative access, representation
groups, resource ledgers, and factorization-or-witness adjudication.

The useful increment is operational:

1. a source/generativity claim must face one **fixed pre-correlated oracle**
   containing the option set, realized path, and declared counterfactual tree;
2. local record sufficiency must face a **departed environmental support**
   pair whose local interventions agree but whose boundary-crossing
   capabilities differ;
3. a quantum public-fact claim must face a **same-SBS/strong-QD-data**
   absorber;
4. a geometry or informational-distance claim must declare and survive its
   **benign refinement class**;
5. a capability claim must use a **matched executable task, resource, access,
   controller, prior, and error frame**; and
6. P2C's completion constructors become a **baseline battery**, expanded by
   DU-specific fixed-oracle, environment, route, scheduler, decoder, and
   record-instrument rivals.

## The important correction

Temporal Issuance's fixed-oracle result does not imply that physical novelty
must be computationally non-reducible. It proves only that stage-zero
fixedness does not prevent a source from containing a complete
future-correlated disclosure schedule.

Absolute computational non-reducibility would be a poor DU criterion:

- it is relative to a computational representation and admitted oracle;
- every finite observed trace admits a finite encoding;
- it does not distinguish generated computable dynamics from expensive
  lookup; and
- it cannot be established empirically from a finite run.

The correct DU use is an adversarial completion plus independently warranted
causal, future-independence, access, physical-realizability, and resource
conditions.

## Local recheck

- one immutable stage-zero object that replays both a realized path and
  counterfactual finite intervention responses without reselection;
- two global states identical under every deterministic local one-bit
  intervention but separated by one boundary-crossing environmental read;
- an SBS-like closure-key control in which raw fragment count changes while
  audited independent support and the verdict remain fixed;

  hop count;
