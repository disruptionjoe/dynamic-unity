# Concentrated paper-closing program

## Bottom line

The gated-concentration strategy worked, but not by producing the paper that
was initially favored.

`DU-PAPER-003`, **Covariant Records Are Not Free**, now has a tighter exact
theorem package and a much stronger collision map. The general package is
nevertheless absorbed by established complementarity, information-disturbance,
and information-support results. It is not a defensible standalone paper in
its present form.

The bounded hedge produced a reusable finite history-certificate compiler.
The same typed kernel ran on quantum-like and authenticated distributed
fixtures without changing its verdict language. In both fixtures it correctly
found that the apparent physical remainder was omitted formed provenance.
That is a useful negative result and a material advance for `DU-PAPER-007` and
`DU-PAPER-009`, but it is not evidence for record-first ontology or new
physics.

The first reserve, `DU-PAPER-015`, **Is a Global Update Clock Physical or
Gauge?**, also failed its broad novelty gate. Its abstract quotient theorem is
standard trace, refinement, bisimulation, and causal-label-invariance
machinery. A Clock-QCA-specific instrument theorem remains possible, but only
behind a bounded pre-proof.

The next concentrated paper-closing allocation should therefore move to
`DU-PAPER-013`, **Tail Universality Classes in Causal-Set Post
Renormalization**. The most important foundational allocation remains the
physicalization of the `DU-PAPER-007` compiler.

## What was established

### 1. Exact covariant-record controls

For a binary controlled nondemolition recorder with a blank,
source-independent environment and arbitrary fixed archive noise, let

\[
\delta=\tfrac12\|\mathcal N_{|\gamma|}-\mathrm{id}\|_\diamond
       =\frac{1-|\gamma|}{2}
\]

be the induced dephasing distance and let \(D_R\) be the accessible record
distinguishability. Then

\[
D_R^2\leq 4\delta(1-\delta).
\]

The bound is tight for pure pointer states. If the accessible conditional
record factors over independent fragments,

\[
\delta\geq
\frac{1-\prod_k\sqrt{1-D_k^2}}{2}.
\]

For \(N\) equiprobable histories decoded with error \(p\) from a
\(K\)-dimensional archive,

\[
\log_2K\geq \log_2N-h_2(p)-p\log_2(N-1).
\]

The executable receipt passes `12/12` checks twice with byte-identical
artifact SHA-256
`dcc3238ea991d8c1d9543b779567a47fd4d31647f5c1755f6cfbfd6334a64547`.

These equations sharpen the fixture. They do not establish a novel general
record-cost theorem. The two-history relation is an Englert-style
complementarity specialization plus the exact dephasing-channel diamond
distance; the support statement is Holevo-Fano; and the fragment result
requires conditional factorization and fails for correlated fanout.

### 4. Global-clock reserve gate

The broad theorem

> a scheduler is gauge exactly when every admitted behavior and intervention
> factors through the scheduler-forgetting quotient

is mathematically useful but too abstract to distinguish
`DU-PAPER-015`. Its skeleton is standard quotient constancy, trace
equivalence, hiding/stuttering refinement, and bisimulation; the finite
distinguishing-word bound is already the known machinery in `HC-DU-036A`.

One narrow reopener survives:

> Freeze one independently selected Clock-QCA record instrument, its exact
> encoded boost/reslicing maps, the scheduler quotient, and a
> generator-complete intervention/resource algebra. Prove either complete
> instrument-level descent or a tight minimal Lorentz-sensitive
> scheduler-leakage witness.

This is a pre-proof gate, not a full research activation. If the statement
reduces to contextual equivalence or requires target-fitted record semantics,
stop and move the paper-closing allocation to `DU-PAPER-013`.

## Plain-English meaning

We learned three things.

First, a quantum record really is not free in the narrow model: the more
reliably an accessible archive distinguishes two histories, the more it
dephases their coherent superposition. But that fact, including the sharp
curve we proved, belongs to mature quantum-information terrain. The Clock-QCA
is a good host and teaching example; it has not yet made the theorem new.

Second, the compiler did the kind of epistemic work Dynamic Unity most needs.
It took two cases that looked as though “the records miss something physical”
and found the ordinary missing thing in each: a formed result plus the
authenticated route by which it formed. That does not vindicate the record
theory. It prevents the theory from claiming a remainder too early.

Third, a hidden global clock does not become physically interesting merely
because we call it fundamental. If every possible use of the system forgets
the scheduler, it is gauge relative to that use; if something detects it, the
detection must live in a fixed physical model rather than an inserted clock
tag. The general logic is known. Only a tight Clock-QCA-specific leakage
theorem could make that route distinctive.

The practical consequence is clear: stop circling the two broad claims, keep
their exact work, move the next paper-closing swing to the causal-set tail
theorem, and continue the deeper program by replacing the compiler's toy
quantum table with a real controlled process.
