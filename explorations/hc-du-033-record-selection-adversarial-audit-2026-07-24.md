# HC-DU-033A finite record-selection quotient — hostile audit

## Verdict

The finite universal properties survive. Nearly every stronger physical
reading fails.

What remains is a clean classical baseline:

> Given an independently declared finite readout cut, equality constraints,
> and deterministic coalition channels, the finest surviving set-valued fact
> is the appropriate equivalence quotient.

What does not remain is a claim that the quotient forms a physical record,
selects the relevant interface, creates public agreement, or explains the
quantum-to-classical transition.

## Surviving propositions

### Survives without qualification inside `FinSet`

1. The least equivalence generated by the declared maps is the coequalizing
   quotient for Set-valued invariant/durable maps.
2. Factorization through that quotient is necessary and sufficient, and the
   factor is unique.
3. For a physically available deterministic tuple channel, pooling intersects
   kernels.
4. The quotient by the join of coalition kernels is the finest fixed-label
   fact recoverable from every authorized coalition.
5. Joining selection and access relations gives the finest fact satisfying
   both constraint families.
6. Local hardening and later pooling can fail to commute.

### Survives only with explicit labels

- “Finest” means finest set-valued quotient, not necessarily a member of a
  small fixed codomain.
- “Record” means a candidate retained fact at the declared cut, not a
  dynamically formed or irreversible record.
- “Access” means zero-error deterministic decoding from the declared
  coalition channel.
- “Common” means common deterministic factor, not common knowledge.
- “Symmetry” is the supplied action; physical transported outputs require an
  equivariant formulation.
- “Durability” is relative to the supplied map family and readout cadence.

## Accepted scope changes

The wave began by targeting a “universal quotient theorem for fixed-label
durable invariant scalar records” and a “maximal robust public fixed-label
scalar quotient.” The hostile audit forces four wording changes:

1. **Universal object:** use a set-valued quotient; a fixed scalar codomain is
   only a postprocessing target.
2. **Public:** replace “public quotient” with “coalition-common deterministic
   factor.”
3. **Physics:** state that the theorem classifies consequences of supplied
   constraints and does not supply or naturalize those constraints.
4. **Finality:** make adversarial public finality a downstream theorem with
   provenance, locking, incompatibility, and fault assumptions.

These corrections are incorporated in the synthesis, program, Lane state,
and executable artifact.

## Why the hardening-order result still matters

The standard inequality

\[
A\vee(B\wedge C)
\preceq
(A\vee B)\wedge(A\vee C)
\]

has a strict three-state witness. Its value is not mathematical novelty. Its
value is that it defeats one tempting architecture:

```text
observe locally
    -> immediately collapse to a selected fixed-label scalar
    -> gossip/fuse later
```

A coalition can lose a legitimate joint distinction at the first arrow. The
next experiment must compare that architecture against:

```text
observe locally
    -> preserve raw/equivariant fragment plus provenance
    -> compose the authorized coalition channel
    -> finalize the joint fact
```

The second architecture is not automatically superior. It may cost coherence,
memory, latency, privacy, authentication, or trusted joint access. Those costs
must be charged. The current result only proves that the two orders are not
generally information-equivalent.
