# Common causal-response object absorber and typed-duty audit

## Executive result

There is no single established concrete object called “the causal response”
across the audited formalisms.

There is a useful common **schema**:

\[
\text{admitted perturbation or intervention}
\longmapsto
\text{change in a declared observable or outcome law}.
\]

But every serious formalism supplies a different domain, query type, output,
state/boundary contract, and equivalence relation. The shared schema is a
language for comparing them, not a physical host that can be inserted into a
theory.

The closest law-level candidates are:

- Peierls/covariant-phase-space response for classical hyperbolic field
  theories;
- relative Cauchy evolution for locally covariant QFT;
- closed-time-path and \(n\)PI/Schwinger--Dyson hierarchies for causal,
  state-conditioned quantum response and fluctuations; and
- process tensors for operational multi-time response to declared
  interventions.

None selects, from its response object alone:

- a formation stage;
- a realized material carrier;
- a target-bearing retention horizon;
- occurrence provenance;
- an observer/access boundary; or
- a certified record quotient.

The result is therefore not a new response theory. It is an exact admission
boundary:

> A common physical response object cannot be claimed until target-independent
> adapters make its sources, states, queries, outputs, and equivalences
> commute. Even then, lawful response is not a material record selector.

Wave 3 does not activate. No audited host both selects a missing
physical-interface duty and exposes an invariant unabsorbed response-order
question.

## 1. Frozen comparison contract

Each candidate was compared only after declaring:

1. primitive object and category;
2. supplied source, state, contour, boundary, gauge, and renormalization data;
3. selected or calculated output;
4. response semantics;
5. allowed representation equivalence;
6. formation-stage selection;
7. material carrier and persistence selection;
8. provenance, access, and certification selection; and
9. excess over standard theory.

This contract prevents equations, kernels, algebras, and operational processes
from being treated as interchangeable merely because all can answer a
counterfactual question.

## 2. Type-necessity theorem

Let candidate \(i\) provide a response map

\[
R_i:Q_i\longrightarrow O_i,
\]

where \(Q_i\) is its admitted query/perturbation space and \(O_i\) its output
space, both already conditioned on its supplied background data \(B_i\).

### Theorem — common-host prerequisite

A concrete object \(H\) can represent the same physical response in two
candidate formalisms only after there are target-independent adapters

\[
\alpha_i:Q\to Q_i,
\qquad
\beta_i:O_i\to O
\]

such that

\[
\beta_i R_i\alpha_i
\]

agrees for every admitted query and descends through the declared physical
equivalences. If the adapters depend on the held-out target or are absent,
“common response” is only an untyped analogy.

### Proof

Without \(\alpha_i\), the same input has no defined realization in each
formalism. Without \(\beta_i\), their outputs are not elements of a shared
comparison space. Without commutation, the proposed host changes predictions.
Without descent, it changes under transformations declared physically
equivalent. Target-dependent adapters are refits rather than reconstruction.
\(\square\)

The theorem is elementary typed-function mathematics. Grade 4 is attached
only to the necessity of the adapters and the resulting nonimplication. It
does not say that no future physical theory can supply them.

## 4. Primary-source collision

### 4.1 Classical response is already covariant and hyperbolic

Khavkine constructs covariant phase-space and Peierls brackets directly from
the Lagrangian and states the needed constraint, gauge, hyperbolicity, and
global hypotheses. This absorbs the idea that a covariant hyperbolic response
tower is itself a new DU object:

- [Khavkine, *Covariant phase space, constraints, gauge and the Peierls
  formula*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.1282).

It does not select which lawful perturbation becomes a record.

### 4.2 Higher quantum response is already a hierarchy

The closed-time-path master effective action organizes the
Schwinger--Dyson/\(n\)PI hierarchy. Truncation gives finite correlation
dynamics; slaving higher correlations produces familiar dissipation,
fluctuation, and noise:

- [Calzetta and Hu, *Stochastic dynamics of correlations in quantum field
  theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/9903291);
- [Carrington, *The 4PI Effective Action for \(\phi^4\)
  Theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ph/0401123).

Thus “mean equation plus higher response/noise” is occupied mathematics, not
an unclaimed CCRT signature.

### 4.3 The clean low-order matched pair is already stochastic gravity

Semiclassical gravity uses the expectation value of stress energy. Stochastic
gravity adds the stress-tensor noise kernel and Einstein--Langevin response,
derived through influence-functional/CTP methods:

- [Hu and Verdaguer, *Stochastic Gravity: A Primer with
  Applications*](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0211090).

This is the smallest honest low-order/higher-response pair. Its extra
fluctuation layer is standard and state/coarse-graining dependent. It does not
select a material record interface.

### 4.4 Relative Cauchy evolution is the strongest covariant law-response host

Fewster and Schenkel compute relative Cauchy evolution for metric and source
perturbations, identify its derivatives with stress/source response, and show
that the wrong algebraic representation can contain redundancies and fail
composition until quotiented:

- [Fewster and Schenkel, *Locally covariant quantum field theory with external
  sources*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.2436).

This is highly relevant to DU's quotient discipline. It still starts with the
spacetime/source category and algebraic theory and does not choose a state,
instrument, physical archive, or observer index.

### 4.5 Process tensors are the strongest operational host

Process tensors reconstruct arbitrary multi-time non-Markovian response for a
declared sequence of interventions:

- [Pollock et al., *Non-Markovian quantum processes: complete framework and
  efficient characterisation*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.00589).

They close the multi-time operational-description gap but leave the control
slots, system/environment boundary, allowed instruments, material dilation,
and archive externally typed. They are therefore an absorber, not the missing
selector.

### 4.6 Direct action changes ontology, not the interface conclusion

Wheeler--Feynman electrodynamics removes an independently dynamical
electromagnetic field in favor of direct interparticle action subject to
absorber/boundary structure:

- [Wheeler and Feynman, *Interaction with the Absorber as the Mechanism of
  Radiation*](https://journals.aps.org/rmp/abstract/10.1103/RevModPhys.17.157).

This is a serious counterexample to treating a field object as mandatory. It
also shows why CCRT cannot declare a field-valued common host in advance.
Pairwise source relations still do not define which interaction is archived
or accessible to an observer.

### 4.7 Causal action selects a configuration, not a record interface

Causal fermion systems provide a genuine variational selector inside a
supplied causal-action problem:

- [Finster, Kindermann, and Treude, *Causal Fermion Systems: An
  Introduction*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.06450).

That makes CFS the strongest action-selection rival in the matrix. But
selection is generally relative to supplied Hilbert space, spin dimension,
constraints, sector, and regulator structure, and it does not select a DU
instrument, sampler, material archive, provenance relation, or access
boundary.

### 4.8 Parametrization is a real comparison control

Ordinary effective-action components can depend on field parametrization and
gauge choices; the Vilkovisky--DeWitt construction exists specifically to
obtain a parametrization-independent effective action:

- [Panda, Tinwala, and Jadav, *One-particle irreducibility of
  Vilkovisky--DeWitt effective action*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.16105).

Consequently, a higher derivative or vertex cannot be promoted to a physical
first leak until its representation contract is frozen.

## 5. Response-order correction

For a frozen physical source \(J\) and observable \(O\), define the response
hierarchy by derivatives of the declared observable law:

\[
R^{(n)}
=
\left.
\frac{\delta^n\langle O\rangle_J}
     {\delta J^n}
\right|_{J=0},
\]

with retarded/CTP ordering when required.

Two corrections are necessary.

1. A functional derivative order is not a spacetime derivative order in the
   equations of motion.
2. Under a locally invertible source reparameterization with nonsingular
   linear part, the order of the first nonzero response is preserved, but
   higher tensors mix through the chain rule. Changing source type,
   introducing auxiliary fields, integrating out degrees of freedom, or
   changing the observable requires a new adapter.

Therefore `SEED-DU-MPA-20` remains meaningful only for a physically frozen
source--observable pair and representation class. The seed remains open, but
Wave 2 supplies no eligible host on which to execute it.
