# Eighteen technical lenses — hypothesis panel and quadratic vote

## Executive result

Eighteen technical lenses inspected the complete current Dynamic Unity
program inline. Each originated two hypotheses:

- `A`: its strongest near-to-medium-term hypothesis; and
- `B`: its highest-upside frontier hypothesis.

The resulting 36 hypotheses were then subjected to a quadratic vote. Every
lens received exactly 100 credits, could not vote on either hypothesis it
owned, and assigned an explicit integer weight—including zero—to every other
hypothesis.

The top result is a tie in vote units:

1. `HG-B`, **Cycle-Filling Public-Finality Theorem** — 39 vote units from 13
   lenses;
2. `DC-A`, **Loop-Resolution Finality Criterion** — 39 vote units from 8
   lenses.

The stable tie-break favors `HG-B` because its support is broader. `DC-A`
received more quadratic credits—233 versus 165—because fewer voters backed
it more intensely.

These are not independent discoveries. They are two formulations of one
convergence:

> A higher finality layer may do something categorically different from
> collecting more evidence: it can resolve a loop obstruction that prevents
> lower-layer records from supporting one safe public action.

The strongest executable companion is:

3. `FM-A`, **Decidable Refinement-Trichotomy Compiler** — 34 vote units from
   13 lenses.

The strongest approximate/noninvertible mathematical extension is:

4. `QI-B`, **Noisy Public Fixed-Algebra Convergence** — 31 vote units from 10
   lenses.

The ballot therefore points toward one integrated next-attempt candidate:

```text
finite noninvertible record/hypergraph
    -> compute loop syndromes and safe equalizer/fixed algebra
    -> attach a higher certificate that resolves selected syndromes
    -> compute the resulting capability change
    -> return factorization, interface refinement, or class-relative remainder
    -> emit a machine-checkable proof certificate
```

The vote is a curiosity and comparative-priority signal only. It does not
promote an `H-CCR` hypothesis, authorize this swing, establish novelty, or
count as scientific evidence.

## 2. The 36 hypotheses

### `MC` — metastable consensus

#### `MC-A` — Provenance-Weighted Hysteretic Finality

In metastable sampling consensus, irreversible action is controlled by
effective independent provenance rank and the protocol spectral gap,
producing distinct upward and downward finality thresholds after marginal
vote accuracy is fixed.

- **Why:** makes metastability a measurable capability transition.
- **Falsifier:** no hysteresis in matched finite-size experiments, or a smooth
  history-aware confidence model predicts rollback and action equally well.
- **Grade:** conditional empirical hypothesis.

#### `MC-B` — Correlated-Evidence Finality Ceiling

With nonzero common-shock correlation, repeated subsampling cannot drive
conflict risk below a correlation floor unless new independent provenance
enters.

- **Why:** generalizes the existing common-shock control into a protocol stop
  law.
- **Falsifier:** a frozen protocol with no new independent evidence beats the
  derived floor under the complete adversary/resource contract.
- **Grade:** model-class theorem candidate; cross-protocol form open.

### `HG` — hypergraphs and higher-order structure

#### `HG-A` — Higher-Order Record Insufficiency

Some multi-party processes have identical vertices, edges, pairwise overlaps,
and pairwise interventions but differ under one genuine hyperedge
intervention.

- **Why:** would justify higher-order records through a finite necessity
  witness rather than analogy.
- **Falsifier:** every declared higher-order intervention factors through the
  pairwise record structure.
- **Grade:** finite counterexample/necessity theorem candidate.

#### `HG-B` — Cycle-Filling Public-Finality Theorem

An independently certified higher-order constraint fills selected
reconciliation loops: it quotients the loop action by the normal closure of
the certified syndromes, and the resulting fixed algebra is exactly the
enlarged path-independent public fact algebra.

- **Why:** gives layered consensus a precise mathematical job.
- **Falsifier:** the certificate kills the declared loop syndromes but the
  quotient/fixed algebra does not match safe facts or actions.
- **Grade:** exact finite theorem target; physical mapping open.

### `CA` — cellular automata and QCA

#### `CA-A` — Homogeneous Reversible Record-Selection No-Go

A finite translation-invariant reversible CA/QCA starting from an exactly
symmetric blank condition cannot uniquely select a nontrivial local record
algebra without a symmetry-breaking state, boundary, ancilla,
superselection sector, or dissipative/coarse-grained ingredient.

- **Why:** locates the missing structure in the covariant-recorder program.
- **Falsifier:** a fully homogeneous finite reversible rule and symmetric
  state uniquely select one stable nontrivial record algebra.
- **Grade:** scoped no-go candidate.

#### `CA-B` — Local Causal-Cone Witness Bound

For bounded-state, bounded-radius CA/QCA record models, every locally
distinguishable pair has a separating intervention inside a cone whose depth
is bounded by local behavioral rank or realization dimension.

- **Why:** makes the finite witness theorem local and physical.
- **Falsifier:** a finite model first separates beyond every declared
  rank-relative cone bound.
- **Grade:** theorem candidate; quantum extension open.

### `DC` — distributed consensus and BFT

#### `DC-A` — Loop-Resolution Finality Criterion

A stronger finality layer strictly enlarges the bounded-risk action set if
and only if it rejects, repairs, records, or quotients an action-relevant
loop syndrome left unresolved below.

- **Why:** directly formalizes layered hardening as capability change.
- **Falsifier:** capability grows with no resolved action distinction after
  complete cost accounting, or resolving one never changes the action where
  it should.
- **Grade:** conditional necessary-and-sufficient theorem target.

#### `DC-B` — Certified-View Equalizer Theorem

Under a declared fault and view-change class, the maximal fork-safe public
fact is the equalizer of all admissible certified views, not the majority
endpoint.

- **Why:** supplies the noninvertible consensus successor to the holonomy
  theorem.
- **Falsifier:** a fact outside the equalizer remains safe, or an equalizer
  fact authorizes incompatible actions.
- **Grade:** exact protocol-class theorem target.

### `AD` — adversarial distributed systems

#### `AD-A` — Equivocation-Provenance Lower Bound

Preserving an action under adaptive equivocation requires at least as many
authenticated provenance states as the maximum number of action-distinct
adversarial loop sectors inside one endpoint fibre.

- **Why:** extends project-or-lift to Byzantine split views.
- **Falsifier:** a zero-error protocol preserves all sectors with fewer
  authenticated states and no extra trust or assumption.
- **Grade:** finite information/security lower-bound target.

#### `AD-B` — Adaptive Refinement Dichotomy

For a finite adversary class and finite formed-refinement family, either some
refinement coequalizes every honest view or an adaptive schedule yields a
bounded split-view witness.

- **Why:** makes the remainder challenge executable under attack.
- **Falsifier:** a finite frozen instance has neither a sufficient refinement
  nor a bounded split-view witness.
- **Grade:** decidable finite dichotomy target.

### `ZK` — zero-knowledge cryptography

#### `ZK-A` — Certification Without Shared Biography

A proposition can become public, robust, and action-enabling through
zero-knowledge certification while its detailed witness history remains
undisclosed and nonreconstructible to participants.

- **Why:** separates existence, certification, disclosure, and shared
  biography.
- **Falsifier:** every physically realizable action-enabling certificate in
  the declared class requires reconstructing the witness.
- **Grade:** known cryptographic construction; physical-ontology mapping
  open.

#### `ZK-B` — Succinct Proof Does Not Erase Provenance Debt

Succinct proofs compress disclosure and verification, but cannot preserve
\(M\) action-distinct route sectors with fewer than \(M\) total authenticated
support states after proof, reference string, verifier key, trust, and
computational assumptions are charged.

- **Why:** tests whether cryptography evades project-or-lift or relocates it.
- **Falsifier:** a complete protocol preserves \(M\) exact sectors below that
  support with no uncharged assumption.
- **Grade:** resource-aware lower-bound conjecture.

### `DB` — distributed databases and data networking

#### `DB-A` — Serializability-Holonomy Correspondence

For a reversible transaction-comparison class, a global serialization exists

- **Falsifier:** a serializable history has nontrivial action holonomy, or an

- **Grade:** conditional systems hypothesis.

## 6. What the vote is really saying

### A. The top “two” are one theorem family

`HG-B` and `DC-A` should not be counted as two independent wins.

- `HG-B` states the mathematical operation: fill or quotient loop syndromes
  and compute the new fixed algebra.
- `DC-A` states the capability consequence: the higher layer matters only
  when that operation resolves an action-relevant distinction.

Together they propose:

> **Higher-Order Finality Theorem.** A higher certificate changes public
> capability exactly through the action-relevant loop relations it adds,
> removes, or records.

That is not yet proved. The finite reversible special case is within reach;
the noisy, noninvertible, adversarial, and physical forms are open.
