# Physical history-certificate transfer

## Result

The finite history-certificate compiler has crossed its first real
implementation boundary. Its record object is no longer restricted to a
deterministic label supplied by the modeler. The new kernel accepts a
stochastic record channel \(R\) and asks, using exact rational arithmetic,
whether every admitted later response \(P_a\) factors as

\[
P_a = R K_a
\]

for some stochastic decoder \(K_a\). It then combines that test with the
existing path-equalizer and minimum-cost, target-independent refinement
selection.

The same decision contract ran on:

1. a matrix-derived four-qubit quantum process; and
2. a replayable authenticated operation-DAG process with enumerated delivery
   schedules.

Both returned the same scoped answer:

> The noisy public record is insufficient. A formed error/delivery syndrome
> restores exact response factorization, and a certified route/layer tag
> restores path-independent action. If that provenance is outside the
> observer boundary, the compiler returns a candidate remainder; if the
> boundary is enlarged at a declared cost, the remainder disappears.

This is a physical/protocol transfer of the typed method. It is not a claim
that quantum mechanics and distributed consensus are the same physical
system.

## Quantum construction

The quantum fixture uses two Bell-history states, a QND \(X\otimes X\)
stabilizer recorder, a pointer qubit, and an explicit detector-error
environment. Preparation, recording, and error coupling are all matrix
constructed. The probe verifies unitary normalization and that the recorder
preserves the two stabilizer histories.

The accessible pointer is a binary symmetric record with error probability
\(1/5\):

\[
R_{\rm pointer}=
\begin{pmatrix}
4/5&1/5\\
4/5&1/5\\
1/5&4/5\\
1/5&4/5
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Because the two history classes have overlapping pointer support, no
stochastic decoder can recover a deterministic later stabilizer recheck. A
second noisy copy does not repair this. At the enlarged boundary, the joint
pointer/error-syndrome record has disjoint parity sectors and admits the exact
decoder. Certified detector-route provenance is independently required for
the noninvertible path views.

The fixture also executes a signed all-port coherence control. Its declared
alternative has mean \(4/5\), its dephased null has mean zero, and after a
\(1/10\) calibration allowance the finite-model margin is \(7/10\). This is
an ideal finite instrument control, not proper-time certification and not a
laboratory error model.

## Distributed construction

The distributed twin constructs serializable and equivocating operation DAGs
with deterministic content hashes, parent links, sequence numbers, payloads,
signer sets, and fixture MAC tags. It verifies hash integrity, parent closure,
MAC consistency, replay idempotence, and the expected double-sign
equivocation; tampered-payload and tampered-MAC controls are rejected. Five
enumerated delivery schedules create
the same \(1/5\) noisy public audit channel as the quantum pointer. That rate
was deliberately matched as a transfer control; it is not a naturally
selected cross-platform invariant.

The public audit bit cannot determine the later authenticated replay audit.
The joint audit/delivery receipt can: it carries the error syndrome needed for
an exact decoder. An authenticated layer register separately resolves
gossip-versus-hardening path ambiguity. The minimum sufficient refinement has
the same role and cost pattern as the quantum construction.

Hashes, parent checks, and fixture MACs are executable integrity controls here.
They do not provide deployed key management, a forgery reduction, or an
asynchronous BFT safety/liveness proof.

## Exact finite statements

The swing establishes four scoped propositions.

### Stochastic record-sufficiency criterion

For a frozen finite record kernel \(R\) and finite response kernels \(P_a\),
record sufficiency is decidable as exact stochastic-factorization feasibility
\(P_a=RK_a\) for every admitted intervention. The probe returns exact decoder
receipts or an overlapping-support obstruction.

This is a finite Blackwell-style sufficiency specialization, not a novelty
claim.

### Error-syndrome lifting

In both fixtures, a noisy base bit with overlapping support cannot determine a
later deterministic distinction. Adding the physically/procedurally formed
error syndrome makes the relevant sectors disjoint and restores
factorization.

This says that provenance can be prediction-bearing state. It does not say
that every omitted provenance variable is physically accessible or free.

### Boundary-relative remainder

The same frozen process returns `CANDIDATE_REMAINDER` when the syndrome lies
outside the access class and `MINIMAL_REFINEMENT` when a charged boundary
expansion admits it. Therefore the observed remainder is exactly
interface-relative in these fixtures, not irreducible physical structure.

### Typed transfer

The two platforms share the same role signature, stochastic factorization,
path-equalizer, candidate exclusions, and verdict vocabulary. Platform labels
and target actions do not enter candidate construction. This is enough for a
typed transfer control and not enough for physical identity.

# Fresh-record generativity hedge

The hedge keeps the exact finite support result and removes an invalid
inference from it.

- An \(M\)-state exact recorder aliases within \(M+1\) time labels.
- Exact binary prefixes through depth \(T\) require \(2^T\) distinguishable
  global record states, or \(T\) record bits.
- A stage-zero tape can replay one preloaded realized path but fails a
  different held-out intervention path.
- An explicit binary counterfactual response tree through depth eight has 510
  nonroot entries.
- The same entire tree is generated by a two-state fixed parity law with four
  transition entries, while an append-only tape carries the growing exact
  record.

The important distinction is:

> Growing record support and a nonfixed source law are orthogonal resource
> accounts.

Fresh exact records still require support growth, export, weakened semantics,
or modeled coarse-graining/nonunitarity. But that growth does not itself
exclude a compact law fixed at stage zero. Beating a preloaded transcript or
an extensional response table is therefore too weak. The next generativity
test must state the independently justified causal-access or resource
condition that excludes the compressed fixed-law rival without defining
physical novelty as computational non-reducibility.
