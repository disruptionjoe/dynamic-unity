# Conditional-work menu under the unbuilt Z_U and the absent covariant generator

## 0. Why this menu exists, and what it is not

Two named absences currently wall off DU's dynamics-facing lines:

1. **The native Z_U is unbuilt and GU-owned.** GU's 2026-08-03 certification
   redo fixes the state exactly: the first unbuildable arrow of the
   W230-to-FLRW composition is A4 NORM, whose native kinetic form is
   `Z_U = |D_A U|^2`
   (gu-formalization `explorations/de-certification-redo-2026-08-03.md:303-310`);
   "the correct state is **BLOCKED-ON-A4**" (same file, :337); and the
   c_kin question "is not decidable with current objects; it becomes
   decidable exactly when Z_U's" block ratio is built (:338-341). The
   verdict gate adds: "The native record law and the native Z_U remain
   unbuilt regardless of every outcome below" (:13).
2. **The covariant finality generator is absent.** `COVARIANT-FINALITY-DYNAMICS`
   is Grade 0, SEAM ONLY (CURRENT-RESEARCH.yaml:543-545), with stop rule:
   "Do not spend work on another knee, consensus analogy, provider adapter,
   or hardware proposal while the generator is absent"
   (CURRENT-RESEARCH.yaml:574-575) and reopen rule: "Reopen only when one
   explicit local covariant generator with selected coefficient passes
   positivity, no-signalling, energy, and mechanism-specific-discriminator
   checks" (CURRENT-RESEARCH.yaml:577-579).

Joe's direction (2026-08-03): these walls must not become a de facto pause
of DU. This menu enumerates the work that is **legitimately doable now** —
work that violates no standing stop, waits on no GU deliverable, and
converts the walls themselves into typed, testable objects. It is filed in
DU's own conditional-claim style: every item's claim-shape is conditional
with named antecedents, in the manner of TaF's `weakened_conditional`
status ("survives only under an explicitly named added condition,"
time-as-finality `CLAIM-LEDGER.md:112-113` — the H7 precedent) and DU's own
conditional-and-abductive contract
(`lab/process/conditional-and-abductive-research-contract.md`,
`CONDITIONALLY_ENTAILED` warrant).

**What this menu is not.** It is not a selection. The contract is explicit:
`successor_is_selected_after_current_flagship` (CURRENT-RESEARCH.yaml:32);
the current flagship `CCR-MINIMAL-PHYSICAL-ANTECEDENT-TO-FINITE-REMAINDER`
is parked (:97) with `active_scientific_program_id: null` (:80); and
`successor_selection` stands at `no_ready` (:652) under a decision rule that
selects "no successor until both Wave-3 conjuncts or another North-Star
admission contract close" (:671-677). This note is **input** to that future
decision, filed under open, non-WIP intake. Contract mechanics are restated
in §4.

### M1 — THE GENERATOR REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (the recommended lead)

**Deliverable-shape.** One dated exploration, `doc_type: spec`, in the exact
precedent shape of the flip-witness requirements spec
(`explorations/flip-witness-algebra-requirements-2026-07-21.md` — DU's
standing example of a requirements/acceptance rubric written for an object
that does not yet exist, with each requirement tied to the absorber that
kills anything short of it). The spec derives the typed necessary
conditions **any** covariant finality generator must satisfy, from four
already-banked constraint sources:

1. **T110 — the scalar-monotone obstruction.** The only `theorem_backed`
   result attached to a temporal claim in TaF's corpus is negative and
   finite: "in a finite closed reversible state space... a scalar
   nondecreasing score is constant on each permutation orbit"
   (time-as-finality `CLAIM-LEDGER.md:9` for the tier placement; :159 for
   the statement; `tests/T110-finite-permutation-monotone-obstruction.md`
   for the fixture). Derived requirement: the generator **cannot be a
   closed-system scalar finality monotone**; it must be openly open-system,
   and per T110's own failure criteria it must declare its erasure, blank
   capacity, coarse-graining, and hidden-environment resources rather than
   smuggle them (`tests/T110-...md`, Failure Criteria).
2. **H7's open-system condition.** H7 survives at source only as
   `weakened_conditional`: "Finality structure can induce an
   observer-relative temporal direction only under an added D1-monotone
   admissibility, persistence, coarse-graining, constructor-impossibility,
   or open-system resource condition" (`CLAIM-LEDGER.md:67`). Derived
   requirement: the generator must **name its added condition class** from
   that enumerated family (or prove a new class), and its arrow content
   must survive the N11-N14 absorber battery style of matched resource
   accounting.
3. **T588's surviving ledger shapes.** The record-issuance contract fork
   refuted the single observer-readable global monotone ledger (contract B
   cannot represent differential ageing — muon lifetimes, storage rings,
   Hafele-Keating, GPS); per-observer ledgers (A) and regional ledgers
   reconciling at contact (C) survive (`TESTS.md:343`; `CLAIM-LEDGER.md:20-23`).
   Derived requirement: the generator's bookkeeping must be **type A or
   type C, never type B** — its finality ledger must represent
   differential ageing and reconcile regionally at contact, not globally.
4. **The stop rule's own text, read as a requirements source.** The parked
   program already states the acceptance package as obligations:
   "Generator, state space, coefficient, and scale. / Locality,
   no-signalling, positivity, conservation, and resource accounting. /
   Mechanism-specific observable, standard-physics null, and
   existing-bound compatibility" (CURRENT-RESEARCH.yaml:557-559), and the
   reopen rule names the pass gates (:577-579). The spec formalizes each
   as a checkable acceptance clause with its absorber (the program's own
   cheapest kill, :570-572: reproduction by a smooth standard
   unitary/open-system memory model over the frozen intervention class).

DU-internal constraints join the same table: PRED-DU-002's
locality-of-update proviso (finalization must be a local Fewster-Verch
operation; a global Lueders update reintroduces Sorkin's impossible
measurement — `explorations/prediction-register.md:125-135`); HC-DU-057's
fourth-class home (a finality rate could be genuinely new
observer-accessible Lorentz-covariant dynamics — prediction-register
:141-150); and the house stops "Covariance admits; it does not select"
(AGENTS.md:129-131) and no primitive circularity (AGENTS.md:115-116).

**What it converts.** The stop rule stops being a wall and becomes a
**requirements theorem**: a typed statement of what any arriving generator
— GU's Z_U-derived candidate after A4 closes, an external collapse-model
import, or a DU-native construction — must satisfy to be admissible. An
arrival becomes testable on day one instead of triggering a scoping
scramble. A scoped negative ("no generator of class X can satisfy clauses
1+3") is itself a valid Grade-4-shaped result (AGENTS.md:46: "A scoped
no-section theorem is a valid result").

**Cost.** S-M. All four constraint sources are already in custody; the work
is derivation and typing, not construction.

**Does NOT promote.** `COVARIANT-FINALITY-DYNAMICS` stays Grade 0 and
parked; a requirements spec is not a generator and satisfies no clause of
the reopen rule (:577-579). Stop-rule compatibility is exact: the stop bars
four named spends — "another knee, consensus analogy, provider adapter, or
hardware proposal" (:574-575) — and a requirements derivation is none of
them; it is the formalization of the program's own remaining obligations
(:557-559). No mechanism, coefficient, or observable is proposed. The
anti-promotion fence of the Wave 1a note stands verbatim: the kinematic
spine supplies no generator and must not be treated as movement toward one
(absorption note, flagged item 1, :225-233).

**Reopen/consume trigger.** Consumed the moment any candidate generator
arrives from any direction — it is the acceptance rubric that adjudicates
the arrival. Separately feeds successor selection as the specification of
the already-listed candidate class
`covariant-finality-generator-after-finite-residual`
(CURRENT-RESEARCH.yaml:669). If GU's A4 closes with `Z_U` built, the spec's
clauses are the DU-side checklist against which any resulting
finality-dynamics proposal is scored.

### M3 — AUDIT-LADDER FORMALIZATION (the rungs above retained-record, as typed objects)

**Deliverable-shape.** A staged formalization, in the HC-DU-063 style
(definitions plus finite witnesses), of the ladder rungs above

**Cost.** M for the full ladder; the first rung pair is S and can run
standalone. Stages cleanly.

**Does NOT promote.** Formalization is representation (Grade 2 ceiling)
unless a separation witness independently earns a Grade-4 boundary. It
does not assert the audit layer has empirical content — that is exactly
the campaign's open question, and this item is deliberately robust to
either verdict: on a null win the campaign plan itself preserves the
formal boundaries "as scoped formal results" (campaign plan §7, :433-438),
so the typed ladder survives as vocabulary; on a D2 witness it becomes the
banked separation's formal home. No consensus-mechanism claim; the
transducer stop stands (absorption note :247-255).

**Reopen/consume trigger.** Consumes D2's witness machinery when the
campaign lands it; conversely its rung definitions feed D2's frozen
witness forms (campaign plan §6.2 requires target-blind declaration before
data contact — the typed rungs are those declarations' vocabulary).

## 3. Successor-flagship candidacy

**Recommendation: M1 is the successor-flagship candidate from this menu.**
Reasons:

1. It is the only item that directly instantiates a successor candidate
   class already listed in the live authority:
   `covariant-finality-generator-after-finite-residual`
   (CURRENT-RESEARCH.yaml:669).
2. It is theorem-shaped in DU's proven register: requirements/no-go/
   boundary results are where DU's Grade-4 corpus lives, and "a scoped
   no-section theorem is a valid result" (AGENTS.md:46).
3. It compounds three repositories' strongest negative results (T110, the
   H7 conditionalization, the T588 ledger fork, HC-DU-057's fourth-class
   correction, PRED-DU-002's locality proviso) into one acceptance object
   — value that exists whether or not a generator ever arrives.
4. It is the item whose completion most changes DU's posture toward the
   two walls: with M1 banked, "the generator is absent" becomes "the
   generator is specified and awaited," and BLOCKED-ON-A4 becomes a
   testable delivery interface rather than an indefinite dependency.
5. Precedent exists in-house for exactly this move at flagship-adjacent
   scale: the flip-witness requirements spec
   (`explorations/flip-witness-algebra-requirements-2026-07-21.md`).

M2, M4, and M5 are not flagship-shaped (each is a bounded S-or-smaller
deliverable; M2/M5 could route as the single bounded side probe,
CURRENT-RESEARCH.yaml:25, if Joe so directs). M3 could grow into
flagship shape only after the campaign's D2 lands its witness machinery,
at which point it would compete on that day's evidence, not this menu's.
