# Philosopher of science: repository-wide advancement wave

## Verdict

The most consequential move is not another record threshold. It is to correct
what a separating intervention is allowed to establish.

The current Certified Causal Reality discriminator says, roughly:

```text
certified-record factorization
    or
finite physical-remainder witness.
```

That is one step too fast. A finite witness against one candidate record
quotient always establishes **candidate-record insufficiency**. It establishes
an **irreducible physical remainder** only after every independently admissible
physical refinement of the record instrument has also failed.

The missing middle is:

```text
factorization
    or
finite witness against the candidate record
    then
admissible-refinement challenge
    ->
interface incompleteness, class-relative physical remainder,
or incomplete adjudication contract.
```

This is not metaphysical caution added after the science. It is an exact
consequence of finite process representation.

## Frozen finite setting

Let a deterministic controlled process be

\[
M=(S,A,\{T_a\}_{a\in A},o),
\]

where:

- \(S\) is a finite physical state set;
- \(A\) is a frozen admitted intervention alphabet;
- \(T_a:S\to S\) is the response to intervention \(a\);
- \(o:S\to O\) is the independently declared accessible physical outcome;
- \(r:S\to R\) is the candidate certified-record map, fixed before the
  separating intervention is sought.

For a word \(w=a_1\cdots a_k\), write \(T_w\) for the composed transition.
Define full admitted behavioral equivalence

\[
s\equiv_A t
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
o(T_w s)=o(T_w t)\quad\text{for every }w\in A^*.
\]

This definition is used to assess the already-fixed record map. It is **not**
licensed as a physical record-selection rule, because it is constructed from
the entire target experiment family.

The candidate record is interventionally sufficient exactly when

\[
\ker r\subseteq {\equiv_A}.
\]

That is: two states identified by the certified record never differ under an
admitted future experiment.

## Theorem — witness absorption and the limit of ontology

### Statement

For every finite controlled process and every candidate record map \(r\):

1. If \(\ker r\subseteq{\equiv_A}\), every admitted outcome prediction factors
   through the current record.
2. If \(\ker r\not\subseteq{\equiv_A}\), there are \(s,t\in S\) and a finite
   intervention word \(w\) such that
   \[
   r(s)=r(t),\qquad o(T_w s)\ne o(T_w t).
   \]
   This is a finite witness of candidate-record insufficiency.
3. Nevertheless, the refinement
   \[
   r^+(s)=\bigl(r(s),[s]_{\equiv_A}\bigr)
   \]
   is sufficient and preserves the old record as a factor.
4. Therefore no finite separating witness, by itself, establishes that the
   distinguishing structure is ontologically irreducible to records.
   It establishes only that it is absent from the candidate record map.

### Proof

For part 1, equality of records implies behavioral equivalence, so every
function \(s\mapsto o(T_w s)\) is constant on each record fiber. It therefore
has a unique factor through \(r(S)\).

Part 2 is the negation of the inclusion in part 1. Because membership in
\(\equiv_A\) is quantified over finite words in \(A^*\), failure supplies a
finite word.

For part 3, equality under \(r^+\) implies equality of the
\(\equiv_A\)-class. Hence all admitted future outcomes agree. Projection onto
the first coordinate recovers \(r\), so the refinement preserves the old
record.

Part 4 follows: the same process that refutes \(r\)-sufficiency has an
abstract sufficient record refinement. Calling that refinement a *physical
record* would, however, be circular unless it is formed, selected, accessible,
certifiable, and resource-bounded independently of the target outcomes used
to define \(\equiv_A\). \(\square\)

The theorem is elementary finite automata/bisimulation structure. The
important result for Dynamic Unity is the inferential consequence: the
abstract availability of a sufficient encoding and the physical availability
of a selected record are different claims.

## Minimal countermodel

Take four states

```text
u0, u1    latent ready states
v0, v1    revealed terminal states
```

with one intervention `probe`:

```text
probe(u0)=v0
probe(u1)=v1
probe(v0)=v0
probe(v1)=v1
```

Let the accessible output be:

```text
o(u0)=o(u1)=ready
o(v0)=0
o(v1)=1
```

Let the certified-record system report:

```text
r(u0)=r(u1)=READY_FINAL
r(v0)=ZERO_FINAL
r(v1)=ONE_FINAL.
```

The `READY_FINAL` certificate can be perfectly public, unanimous,
authenticated, and idempotently final. None of those properties makes it
sufficient. The one-step probe separates \(u_0\) and \(u_1\).

Now add the abstract refinement

```text
r+(u0)=(READY_FINAL,0)
r+(u1)=(READY_FINAL,1).
```

The witness disappears. But this does not show that the extra bit was already
a physical record. It may be:

- a physically formable but omitted record, making the old interface
  incomplete;
- a latent physical disposition identifiable only through the probe, making
  it a class-relative physical remainder under the pre-probe record contract;
  or
- an artifact of an unfrozen intervention, representation, access, or
  resource class, leaving the comparison incomplete.

The same finite input-output table cannot decide among these without a
record-formation and admissible-refinement contract.

## Exact distinction ledger

This countermodel forces five notions apart:

| Question | What the finite witness establishes |
|---|---|
| Does a distinguishing disposition **exist in the declared model**? | Yes: `u0` and `u1` respond differently. |
| Is it **identifiable**? | Yes, relative to the admitted `probe`. |
| Is it **selected as a record**? | Not shown. The candidate instrument identifies the states. |
| Is some sufficient **encoding** mathematically constructible? | Yes: the behavioral refinement. |
| Is record-first **ontology** licensed? | No. Physical formation, selection, access, and resources remain unproved. |

Conversely, failure of the candidate record does not license physics-first
ontology either. That license requires showing that no admissible physical
record refinement can absorb the witness.

## Admissible-refinement criterion

Let

\[
\mathrm{Ref}_{\Sigma,G,L}(r)
\]

be the record refinements that:

1. preserve the existing record;
2. are produced by a record-formation family \(\Sigma\) fixed independently
   of the target separating outcomes;
3. are equivariant under the declared representation group \(G\);
4. have declared observer access, provenance, and certification semantics;
5. fit the complete resource ledger \(L\); and
6. do not refit the decoder or interface after the witness is known.

Then the ontology discriminator has three honest outcomes:

### A. Interface incompleteness

Some \(r'\in\mathrm{Ref}_{\Sigma,G,L}(r)\) is sufficient.

The witness refuted the old record interface, not record reconstruction as a
class.

### B. Formation-relative physical remainder

\(\mathrm{Ref}_{\Sigma,G,L}(r)\) is nonempty, but no member is sufficient.

The missing structure is irreducible **relative to the declared formation,
symmetry, access, intervention, and resource class**. This is the strongest
ontology-relevant verdict the finite experiment alone licenses.

### C. Incomplete adjudication contract

The refinement class is unfrozen, empty for accidental reasons, refitted
afterward, or contains the target-defined behavioral encoding by fiat.

No ontology verdict follows. An empty class may merely show that the record
selection theory was never supplied. A class containing every behavioral
encoding makes record-first sufficiency tautological.

This criterion converts “irreducible remainder” from a rhetorical label into
a quantified challenge.

## Representation dependence and resource boundary

The theorem is invariant under simultaneous relabeling of states,
interventions, records, and outputs. Its **physical interpretation** is not
representation-free unless \(G\), \(\Sigma\), and \(L\) are fixed.

The abstract refinement may require:

- access to a hidden degree of freedom;
- a new interaction that disturbs the process;
- memory or fresh archive support;
- a trusted decoder or reference frame;
- added latency, energy, entropy export, or coherence loss; or
- an observer boundary enlarged after the fact.

Any of those can make the refinement physically inadmissible even though it
exists as a set-theoretic label. Conversely, refusing every refinement
without a physical selection rule manufactures a remainder.

## Falsifiers and failure scope

The exact theorem would fail if a finite process and record map supplied a
separating admitted word but no sufficient behavioral refinement. The
construction above proves that cannot occur in the declared finite class.

The Dynamic Unity inference would be falsified or narrowed by:

- a target-independent physical selection theorem showing that the canonical
  behavioral refinement is always formed and accessible at the declared
  resource cost;
- a counterexample in a stochastic or quantum process where the proposed
  refinement notion does not preserve the complete process structure;
- a proof that the chosen \(\mathrm{Ref}_{\Sigma,G,L}\) was defined using the
  target outcomes and is therefore circular; or
- a physical positive control showing an admitted refinement that the
  analysis incorrectly excluded.

The result is exact only for finite deterministic controlled processes. It
does not prove a quantum-process, continuum, relativistic, thermodynamic, or
universal ontology theorem.

## Newly visible hypotheses, theorems, and conjectures

1. **Admissible Refinement Dichotomy.** For a nonempty, independently selected
   refinement class, either some bounded refinement is sufficient or a finite
   witness survives every member. This is the correct `HC-DU-036` target.
2. **Selection–Sufficiency Independence Theorem.** Dynamic/equivariant
   selection and intervention sufficiency realize all four truth values.
3. **Refinement Cost Lower Bound.** Absorbing a witness of intervention depth
   \(k\) requires a lower bound on physical support, memory, disturbance, or
   verification resources under a fixed formation family.
4. **Quantum Admissible-Refinement Conjecture.** A process-tensor remainder is
   physical only when no target-independent admissible record instrument or
   supermap makes the rival processes equivalent under the resource bound.
5. **Ontology-License Monotonicity.** Enlarging the intervention family makes
   sufficiency harder; enlarging the admissible record-refinement class makes
   a physical-remainder verdict harder. A robust verdict should state both
   monotonicities.
6. **Public-Finality Nonrepair Theorem.** Finalizing a quotient cannot restore
   information about future action differences already collapsed by that
   quotient; it can only stabilize the insufficient fact.

## Plain-English interpretation

If two situations have the same records but a later experiment makes them
behave differently, we have definitely learned that the records were missing
something. We have not yet learned what kind of thing was missing.

Maybe the missing feature could have been recorded by a better physical
instrument. Maybe it could not be recorded without changing the system,
breaking a symmetry, or exceeding the available resources. Or maybe we never
specified what counts as an allowed record instrument in the first place.

So the decisive question is not merely, “Can we find an experiment that gets
past the records?” It is:

> **After that experiment succeeds, can any independently justified physical
> record system capture the difference without being designed from the answer?**

If yes, we found an incomplete interface. If no, under a serious nonempty
physical and resource contract, we found the strongest defensible kind of
physical remainder. That extra step is what lets Certified Causal Reality
investigate ontology without defining information broadly enough to win
automatically—or declaring physics irreducible merely because the first
record scheme was too coarse.
